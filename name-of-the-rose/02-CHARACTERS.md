## 1. William of Baskerville (HERO)

**Lock block:**

> **WILLIAM OF BASKERVILLE** — English Franciscan friar, age about 50. Tall and gaunt, ~6 feet. Long thin face, prominent cheekbones, sharp light-blue eyes, neatly trimmed reddish-grey beard going silver at the chin, short reddish-grey tonsured hair (crown shaved, ring of hair around the sides). Pale skin weathered by travel. Quick alert expression, faint amusement at the corners of the mouth. Wears the undyed brown wool habit of the Franciscan order, knotted rope belt with three knots (poverty, chastity, obedience), worn leather sandals over wool stockings, a small leather satchel slung crosswise containing a notebook and a riveted-leather case for his eyeglasses. Carries (or wears propped on his nose) **spectacles — a brand-new technology in 1327** — riveted leather frames with two round glass lenses, no earpieces, perched on the bridge of his nose. Often holds them in one hand when not reading. Posture upright, slight forward lean of a man listening attentively. Not a warrior — a scholar's body.

>
> LEFT half: full-body portrait in the brown Franciscan habit and rope belt, standing relaxed three-quarter view, sandals visible, the leather eyeglass case at his hip, holding his spectacles in one hand.
>
> RIGHT half: head-and-shoulders portrait, three-quarter view, holding the riveted-leather spectacles up to his face about to put them on.
>
> [WILLIAM lock block — paste verbatim above.]
>
> NOT a children's book. Serious mature graphic novel, realistic proportions, natural lighting. NO halftones, NO cel shading, NO ink linework. Painted brushwork. No text, no labels, no captions, no border.

## 4. Jorge of Burgos

**Lock block:**

> **JORGE OF BURGOS** — Benedictine monk, age about 85, Spanish (from Castile). Very tall, gaunt, slightly bent at the shoulders. Long thin face, deeply lined, white-bearded down to the chest, white tonsured hair around the sides of a bald crown. **BLIND** — both eyes are pale milky white with no visible pupils, eyelids partly open. Skin parchment-pale, almost translucent over the bones of the face. Wears the plain black Benedictine habit, a wooden rosary at his belt, walks with a long pale ashwood staff. Expression severe, unsmiling, listening with his whole body. The most senior and most feared monk in the abbey. He moves through the dark corridors as if seeing — because he memorized the abbey decades ago, before his sight failed.

## 5. Salvatore of Montferrat

**Lock block:**

> **SALVATORE** — Benedictine lay-brother, age about 40, Italian (from Montferrat). Short, hunched, ugly in a striking way: a wide flat face with a smashed nose, **one milky blue eye and one dark watchful eye**, missing several teeth so his mouth makes a permanent half-grin, sparse scraggly red-brown beard. A pale knife scar across his left cheek. Skin coarse and weathered like a peasant's. Wears a dirty short black Benedictine habit, no rope belt (a knotted strip of leather instead), bare dirty feet much of the year. A small worn copper crucifix at his neck. He is the abbey's hanger-on — Remigio the cellarer protects him. Former follower of the heretic Fra Dolcino, now hiding in plain sight, terrified of being found out. Speaks a comic stew of Latin, Italian, German, French, Spanish all at once.

## 6. Bernard Gui

**Lock block:**

> **BERNARD GUI** — Dominican friar and papal inquisitor, age about 65, French (from Limousin). Tall, lean, very straight-backed, the bearing of a senior judge. Long narrow face, sharp aquiline (hooked) nose, thin pressed lips, pale grey-green eyes that look without blinking, neatly tonsured iron-grey hair. Skin smooth and pale — a man who lives indoors with documents. Wears the **Dominican habit**: white wool tunic with a long white scapular over it, **black wool mantle and hood** over the white, a black leather belt with a small writing case (inkhorn + quills) hanging from it, a heavy silver cross on a black cord. Carries a leather-bound book at his side: his own *Practica Inquisitionis*, the inquisitor's manual he wrote. Expression cold, attentive, intelligent. He is not cruel for cruelty's sake — he is the meticulous administrator of an institution that burns people. The precision is the horror.

## 7. The village girl (unnamed)

**Lock block:**

> **THE VILLAGE GIRL** — peasant from the village below the abbey, about 16 years old, Italian. Small and very thin from hunger, ~5'2", olive skin chapped by cold and wind, large dark brown eyes, long tangled black hair partly hidden under a coarse brown headscarf. A faint scratch on one cheek. Wears: a single layer of coarse undyed brown wool dress patched at the hem and elbows, a frayed darker brown shawl over her shoulders, no shoes — only rags wrapped around her feet against the snow. No jewelry, no ornament. Expression usually frightened, watchful, but with a directness of gaze that does not look away. She has **no name** in the story — Adso never learns it, and that is the point.

## Description-only characters (carried by prose, no ref)

These four monks appear in group scenes anchored by Adso or William and never carry a page alone in Vol 1. Their descriptions go inline in the relevant page prompts.

**MALACHI OF HILDESHEIM** — German librarian, age about 50. Very tall and gaunt, ascetic, long-jawed, hollow-cheeked, dark deep-set eyes, sparse dark hair around the tonsure, thin pressed mouth. Black Benedictine habit. **Heavy iron ring of keys at his belt** (his identifier in any scene). Always watching. Speaks reluctantly. Murdered in Vol 2.

**BERENGAR OF ARUNDEL** — English assistant librarian, age about 32. Soft-featured, plump, pale blond, smooth-cheeked, watery blue eyes that don't meet other eyes. Black Benedictine habit. Nervous fluttering hands. Found drowned in the kitchen tub on P17 — described as half-glimpsed in dim morning light, not graphically.

**SEVERINUS OF SANKT WENDEL** — German herbalist, age about 55. Medium build, weathered hands stained with plant dyes, neat grey beard, kind direct eyes. Wears a leather apron over his black Benedictine habit when working in the herbarium. The friendliest monk in the abbey. Killed in Vol 2.

**REMIGIO OF VARAGINE** — Italian cellarer (head of kitchens and supplies), age about 50. Heavy-set, broad, double-chinned, small shrewd eyes, dark cropped hair around the tonsure. Black Benedictine habit straining at the belt. Used to be a follower of Fra Dolcino — hides it well. Salvatore is his weak point.

## 8. Architectural ref — the Aedificium

**Reference prompt** (generates `refs/ref_aedificium.png`):

> Reference image, 3:2 landscape, painted in oil-painting realism, muted palette, mid-afternoon winter daylight, low grey overcast sky.
>
> A massive Romanesque stone tower — the **Aedificium** of a wealthy Benedictine abbey in the Italian Apennines in 1327. **Octagonal floor plan** with **four square towers** attached at the cardinal corners (north, south, east, west). Three storeys tall. Very thick walls in dressed grey limestone weathered by centuries. Small narrow round-arched windows, only a few per face. Slate roof with snow on it. Built into a cluster of lower abbey buildings — Romanesque chapel with a squat bell-tower, low rectangular cloister, refectory — visible at the base of the great tower. Snow on the ground around it, dark pines on the surrounding mountainside, low grey clouds above.
>
> The tower **dominates** the composition. Looming. Austere. Slightly menacing — this is the building that holds the labyrinth library at its top and will burn at the end of Book Two.
>
> NOT a children's book. NO halftones, NO cel shading. NO modern construction details (no chimneys with pipes, no glass-paned windows — only narrow slit openings; no Gothic flying buttresses; no Renaissance ornament). Painted brushwork. No text, no labels.

## Casting checks (gate before any page generation)

For each of the 8 refs, before passing the gate:

- [ ] Age right? (William 50, Adso 18, Abbot 60, Jorge 85, Salvatore 40, Gui 65, girl 16)
- [ ] Habit color right? (Franciscan brown / Benedictine black / Dominican white+black)
- [ ] Distinctive marker visible? (William's spectacles, Adso's wax tablet, Abbot's garnet cross, Jorge's milky blind eyes, Salvatore's two-color eyes and scar, Gui's *Practica* book, girl's rag-wrapped feet, Aedificium's octagonal+four-corners plan)
- [ ] Register matches Newton / Honda / da Vinci? (oil-painting realism, NOT comic, NOT children's book, NOT digital-painting / concept-art)
- [ ] Period-accurate? (no modern frames on William's glasses, no Renaissance clothing, no Gothic arches, no metal eyeglasses)

If any ref fails, regenerate before proceeding. A drifted ref poisons every page generated from it.
