# The Trial of Socrates — Style Guide

## Style Block (Copy Verbatim to Every Prompt)

```
Art Style: Cinematic photorealistic illustration with warm natural lighting and classical Athenian atmosphere
Color palette: warm honey-gold, marble white, terracotta, shadow blue, olive green
Effects: Mediterranean sunlight, stone texture, atmospheric dust, natural shadow depth
```

---

## Palette Notes

- **Warm honey-gold** — dominant in agora scenes, sunlight, the warmth of intellectual life
- **Marble white** — columns, architecture, the physical structure of Athenian democracy
- **Terracotta** — pottery, Doros's workshop, domestic warmth, the ordinary world
- **Shadow blue** — courtroom shadows, prison stone, the cold machinery of the state
- **Olive green** — the Acropolis hill, trees in the agora, life continuing

**Pages 1–4:** Full warm palette. Golden agora light, terracotta interiors.
**Pages 5–6:** Shift to marble white + shadow blue. Harsh courtroom light.
**Pages 7–9:** Mixed — Socrates in warm light within a cool courtroom.
**Page 10:** Prison: warm lamplight vs. cold stone walls.
**Pages 11–12:** Same prison, but warmer — the conversation is intimate.
**Page 13:** Dawn light. Cool but not cold. Quiet.
**Pages 14–15:** Return to golden agora light, but with emptiness. Something missing.

---

## Panel Rules

- 3–4 panels per page maximum
- Black panel borders, consistent weight
- Clean white gutters
- No decorative borders
- Caption boxes: clean parchment style, dark text
- Speech bubbles: classic white with black outline
- Full-page panels on: Page 5 Panel 1 (courtroom establishing) and Page 14 Panel 1 (empty agora)

---

## Shot Type Reference

| Shot | Use For |
|------|---------|
| Wide/Establishing | Agora, Dikasterion, Acropolis in distance, Kerameikos streets |
| Medium | Conversations, trial speeches, Socrates in dialogue |
| Close-up | Alexis's reactions, Socrates's calm face, Doros's worry |
| Low Angle | Socrates addressing the jury (looking up at him), architecture |
| High Angle | Alexis small in crowd, the vote, vulnerability |

---

## Visual References

**Should look like:** Cinematic, grounded, tactile. Sun on limestone. The feel of ancient Athens as a real, lived-in city — not a museum. Think Ridley Scott's ancient world meets Joann Sfar's graphic novel intimacy.

**Should NOT look like:** Percy Jackson. Cartoon Greece. White marble fantasies (real Athens was painted bright colors). Anything with children's book softness or flat illustration style.
