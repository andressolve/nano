## CHARACTER: ALEXIS (Potter's Son, POV Character)

**Reference sheet prompt:**
```
create image: Subject:
Character reference sheet showing Alexis, a 14-year-old Athenian boy, in two views side by side: close-up portrait on the left, full body on the right.

Character Description:
A lean, angular boy of 14 with the beginnings of adolescent height. Dark brown wavy hair falling just past his ears, olive skin, deep-set dark brown eyes with an earnest, searching quality. High cheekbones beginning to show, straight nose, firm mouth. Clay dust visible on his hands and under his fingernails — he's a potter's son.

Signature identifying marks:
- Clay-stained hands and fingernails
- Dark brown wavy hair past his ears
- Deep-set earnest dark brown eyes

Left panel — Portrait (head and shoulders):
Close-up showing face clearly. Expression: alert, thoughtful, slightly uncertain — a boy thinking hard about something. All facial features visible.

Right panel — Full body:
Standing in 3/4 view. Simple undyed linen chiton belted with a plain cord. Worn leather sandals. No jewelry, no adornment. Clay smudge on his right forearm. Hands at his sides.

Art Style: Cinematic photorealistic illustration with warm natural lighting and classical Athenian atmosphere
Background: Simple warm neutral gradient
Lighting: Soft even lighting from front-left, studio reference style.

General Instruction:
This is an ordinary boy — not handsome, not ugly, not heroic-looking. He's smart and observant but doesn't stand out in a crowd. Both views must show the exact same person. NOT a children's book character. Realistic proportions.

Aspect Ratio: 4:3 (landscape)
```

**Lean lock block:**
```
ALEXIS (14, Athenian boy): Lean, angular, dark brown wavy hair past ears, olive skin, deep-set dark brown eyes. Clay-stained hands. Simple undyed chiton, cord belt, worn sandals. No jewelry.
```

**Which pages:** All pages (1–15)

## CHARACTER: SOCRATES (The Philosopher)

**Reference sheet prompt:**
```
create image: Subject:
Character reference sheet showing Socrates, an ancient Greek philosopher aged 70, in two views side by side: close-up portrait on the left, full body on the right.

Character Description:
A stocky, thick-necked man of 70 with an arresting, famously ugly face. Completely bald on top with a ring of white-gray wiry hair around the sides and back. Broad flat face, wide-set protruding eyes (bright, alert, slightly amused), snub nose (broad and upturned), thick lips. Full short white beard, somewhat scraggly. Deep laugh lines around eyes. Strong, weathered hands.

Signature identifying marks:
- Bald on top, ring of white-gray hair
- Snub nose — broad and upturned
- Wide-set protruding eyes with amused alertness
- Barefoot always

Left panel — Portrait (head and shoulders):
Close-up showing face clearly. Expression: warm, alert, slightly amused — as if he just asked a question he already knows you can't answer. All facial features visible. Snub nose prominent.

Right panel — Full body:
Standing in 3/4 view. Stocky build, thick neck, barrel chest despite age. Wearing a simple, somewhat worn and patched gray-white himation (cloak) draped over left shoulder, leaving right shoulder bare. BAREFOOT — no sandals. Standing relaxed, one hand raised in a conversational gesture.

Art Style: Cinematic photorealistic illustration with warm natural lighting and classical Athenian atmosphere
Background: Simple warm neutral gradient
Lighting: Soft even lighting from front-left, studio reference style.

General Instruction:
Socrates was famously ugly by Athenian standards but magnetically compelling. He should look like a working-class philosopher — not a marble bust, but a real man who walks barefoot through Athens. Both views must show the exact same person.

Aspect Ratio: 4:3 (landscape)
```

**Lean lock block:**
```
SOCRATES (70, Greek philosopher): Stocky, thick-necked. Bald on top, ring of white-gray wiry hair. Broad face, snub nose, wide-set protruding eyes, short white scraggly beard. Worn gray-white himation over left shoulder. Always barefoot.
```

**Which pages:** 1, 3, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13

Art Style: Cinematic photorealistic illustration with warm natural lighting and classical Athenian atmosphere
Background: Simple warm neutral gradient
Lighting: Soft even lighting.

General Instruction:
A working man. His hands and clothing should show his trade. He resembles his son but heavier, wearier. Both views must match.

Aspect Ratio: 4:3 (landscape)

## CHARACTER: MELETUS (The Accuser)

**Reference sheet prompt:**
```
create image: Subject:
Character reference sheet showing Meletus, a young Athenian citizen in his early 30s, in two views side by side: close-up portrait on the left, full body on the right.

Character Description:
A lean, ambitious man in his early 30s with sharp, precise features. Clean-shaven (unusual for Athens — making a statement). Narrow face, high cheekbones, sharp dark eyes with an intense, self-righteous quality. Dark hair carefully styled, swept back from forehead. Thin mouth set in a determined line. Well-groomed eyebrows.

Signature identifying marks:
- Clean-shaven face (distinctive in bearded Athens)
- Sharp, narrow features
- Carefully styled dark hair swept back

Left panel — Portrait (head and shoulders):
Close-up showing face. Expression: self-assured, righteous, the look of a man who believes he is saving his city.

Right panel — Full body:
Standing in 3/4 view, formal posture. Fine white linen chiton with blue-dyed border at hem and neckline — wealthy. Leather sandals with silver clasps. Blue cloak draped over left arm. Clean, manicured hands.

Art Style: Cinematic photorealistic illustration with warm natural lighting and classical Athenian atmosphere
Background: Simple warm neutral gradient
Lighting: Soft even lighting.

General Instruction:
A man of privilege who has appointed himself Athens's conscience. He's not a villain — he genuinely believes Socrates is dangerous. Both views must match.

Aspect Ratio: 4:3 (landscape)
```

**Lean lock block:**
```
MELETUS (early 30s, Athenian): Lean, clean-shaven, narrow face, sharp dark eyes. Dark hair swept back. Fine white chiton with blue border, blue cloak, silver-clasped sandals. Formal bearing.
```
