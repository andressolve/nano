# The Trial of Socrates — Settings

## THE AGORA — Heart of Athens (Pages 1, 3, 14)

SETTING: AGORA
- Open-air marketplace and civic center, colonnaded walkways (stoas)
- Market stalls, fig vendors, potters displaying wares, people in conversation
- Stone steps where people sit to listen to speakers
- The Stoa of Zeus in the background, painted stoa with frescoes
- Plane trees providing shade, pigeons, dogs
- Atmosphere: Alive, warm, full of voices. A city that talks.
- Palette: Full warm — honey-gold sunlight, marble white columns, olive green shade

---

## KERAMEIKOS — Potter's Quarter (Pages 2, 4, 15)

SETTING: KERAMEIKOS
- Workshop district named for its potters (kerameus = potter)
- Narrow streets between stone and mudbrick buildings
- Doros's workshop: open-fronted, pottery wheel, kiln in back yard, shelves of drying pots
- Terracotta dust everywhere, the smell of clay and fire
- Modest family home attached to workshop — flat roof where Alexis can see the Acropolis
- Atmosphere: Domestic, warm, grounded. The real Athens, not the philosophical one.
- Palette: Terracotta dominant, warm gold, earthen brown

---

## THE DIKASTERION — Law Court (Pages 5–9)

SETTING: DIKASTERION
- Large open-air court in the agora precinct
- 501 jurors seated on rising stone benches in a rough semicircle
- Central speaker's platform (bema) — a raised stone block
- Voting urns at the edges — bronze urns for casting disk ballots
- Rope barriers separating public gallery from jury
- No roof — open sky above, harsh midday sun
- Atmosphere: Imposing, formal, relentless. The weight of 501 pairs of eyes.
- Palette shift:
  - Page 5: Establishing — marble white, shadow blue, harsh light
  - Pages 6–7: Interior focus — faces in harsh sun, deep shadows
  - Pages 8–9: Tighter — claustrophobic despite open air, shadow blue dominant

---

## THE PRISON — Near the Agora (Pages 10–13)

SETTING: PRISON
- Small stone building near the southwest corner of the agora
- Socrates' cell: small stone room, a wooden bench, an oil lamp
- Iron gate with gaps wide enough to speak through
- A small window high up, showing sky
- Outside corridor where visitors wait
- Atmosphere: Intimate, quiet, warm lamplight against cold stone. Not a dungeon — a plain room where a man waits.
- Palette: Warm lamplight (amber) vs. cold stone (blue-gray). The contrast is the point.

---

## STREETS OF ATHENS (Pages 14–15)

SETTING: STREETS
- Morning-lit streets between the agora and the city walls
- Stone paths, whitewashed buildings, clay-tiled roofs
- The Acropolis visible above the roofline — Parthenon catching morning light
- Ordinary life: vendors opening shops, women carrying water, dogs sleeping
- The road out through the Dipylon Gate toward the countryside
- Atmosphere: Normal. Unchanged. The city that killed its wisest man didn't pause.
- Palette: Return to warm honey-gold but with emptiness — golden light on empty spaces
