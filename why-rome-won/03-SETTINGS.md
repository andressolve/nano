# 03 — SETTINGS

Recurring locations / visual environments, for consistency of light and palette across the images.

## Italian countryside / Roman road (cover, P1)
Sun-baked hills, cypress and olive, a paved Roman road (basalt slabs), warm golden Mediterranean light,
dust. Distant aqueduct or milestone for scale.

## The marching camp (P2)
Open ground at dusk; a square earthwork fort under construction — fossa (ditch), agger (rampart) topped
with sharpened stakes, gridded leather tents, central command tent. Long low-sun shadows, campfire glow
beginning. Disciplined, orderly, almost architectural.

## The battlefield of Cannae (P5)
Rendered NOT as a landscape but as a tactical schematic on parchment — dusty plain implied. Hot August
light if any landscape shows. Sense of a vast crowded killing-ground compressed into a ring.

## The Roman Forum after Cannae (P6)
Republican-era forum: stone temples, the Curia (senate house), statues; overcast warm light, subdued.
Crowds in mourning, recruits forming up. Dignity under grief — NOT rubble, Rome itself was never sacked.

## The sea off Sicily (P7)
Bright open Mediterranean, whitecaps, spray, strong daylight. Two large galleys locked together. Energy
and motion.

## Parchment / map ground (P4, P5)
Aged cream-to-tan parchment, sepia and iron-gall ink, faint hand-coloring, a compass rose, a subtle
border. The look of a museum exhibit map, not a modern infographic.

## Palette discipline (all settings)
Parchment, sun-bleached stone, bronze, iron-grey, legionary oxblood-red, Mediterranean blue sky. Muted,
warm, period. No neon, no candy colors, no modern tints.
