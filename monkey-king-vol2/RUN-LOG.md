## The health metric — why a page failed, not how often

Count REVISE verdicts by category:

- **Craft** — wrong for a reason a reader would feel: a dropped line, a
  mirrored room, a tail on the wrong mouth. The gate working. Cheap.
- **Gate** — wrong against something the critic *measured* or against a
  criterion that is not on the card. The gate eating the run. **A page that
  costs more than two generations for gate reasons is not a hard page; it is a
  broken gate.** Stop and look at the criterion.
