script gate cleared 2026-10-15 at round 10, APPROVED with findings NONE), never transcribed from it. **Mode**
is blocking at the page gate. **Dominant turn** is the one thing that changed; a
page whose turn will not fit one sentence goes back to
`03-VOLUME-ARCHITECTURE.md`, not to the prompt.

always** — two only when the page's turn *is* the transition.

## Acceptance questions — asked of every page at the script gate

1. **What does each character in this room want from the other person, right
   now?** "To inform them" is not a want and fails the page.
2. **Does anybody say something other than exactly what they mean?**
3. **With the speaker names stripped, can the lines be assigned correctly?**
4. **Is the event dramatized and the meaning left to be inferred** — never the
   reverse?
5. **Is the protagonist's appetite visible,** or have they gone serene?
6. **Why does the reader turn this page?** One sentence, in the script, under
   every page — and it must be true of what is actually on the page.
7. **Can this page physically hold its own text** at the numbers in
   `method/06-TYPOGRAPHY.md`? No balloon over 24 words; a dramatic page under
   ~105 words; a prose page under 140. `tools/script_audit.py` checks this
   mechanically. If a page fails, restage or split it. Never shrink the type.

## Text budget by mode

| Mode | Words | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Dramatic | **45–80**; ~105 only with large protected balloon lanes | reserve lanes before placing faces |
| Illustrated prose | **50–120**, 140 exceptional | compose the prose field first |
| Spectacle / silence | under 15% of visual attention | one short line may anchor scale |
| Chronicle (opt-in) | **60–150**, 170 ceiling, fields + labels | compose the one image the idea needs first |
