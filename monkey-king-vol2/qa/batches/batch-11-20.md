# Sequence gate — pages 11–20

## Uninterrupted read

Six robbers stop Sanzang on the border road and seize at his horse, robe, and
bundle. Wukong eagerly steps between them, turns the needle from his ear into
his staff, and scatters the gang with one blow. When he raises the staff to
kill the fallen leader, Sanzang shields the man. Their disagreement becomes
personal: Sanzang calls Wukong no better than a robber, and the wounded Wukong
abandons him.

Wukong goes to Ao Guang for tea and sympathy. The Dragon King refuses to
validate his grievance, reminds him that he stole the iron staff, and tells a
pointed story about a boy who endured an old man's tests and was rewarded with
his teaching. Wukong rejects the story aloud but accepts its lesson and goes
back.

While Wukong is gone, Guanyin finds the stranded Sanzang and gives him a cloth
cap containing a gold band, together with the words that will make Wukong
stop. Wukong returns of his own accord, and the two reconcile without quite
admitting it. Sanzang then offers the concealed restraint as a present.
Wukong accepts it gladly and wears it west.

At their night camp, the unresolved dispute returns. Sanzang asks Wukong never
to strike another man, even in his defense; Wukong refuses and sweeps the
staff close to him to demonstrate why force is necessary. Frightened, Sanzang
says Guanyin's words. The cap tears away, the gold band remains, and Wukong
falls screaming while Sanzang watches in horror.

## Gate judgment

The pages preserve the material story and the exact scripted text and
attribution across transitions. Sanzang, Wukong, Ao Guang, and Guanyin remain
visually distinct; the bay horse, robe, bundle, staff/needle, cloth cap, gold
band, milestone, border road, Eastern Sea palace, and night camp carry through
where consequential. The focal line stays with the rupture between master and
disciple, including Wukong's hurt, reluctant return, pleasure in the apparent
gift, and the betrayal embodied by the band.

The page turns land their intended questions and consequences. The batch does
not read as compressed summary: page 16 is a deliberate illustrated-prose
intervention rather than a summary stretch. Dramatic pages are broken by the
staff spectacle, the prose encounter with Guanyin, and the full-page tightening
of the band. Consequential beats receive visible aftermath within the run.
