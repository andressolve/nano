# Visual-continuity gate — pages 41–48

## Verdict

APPROVED

Pages 41–48 belong to the same book. The sequence holds the Mineral Ink
register: visible brush-and-ink contours, toothed paper, mineral-pigment
washes, pooled edges, and a consistent warm paper ground. Contrast and black
level stay coherent through the sand-gold daylight, the indigo-black camp
night, and the brighter road morning. The lighting changes read as story-time
progression rather than finish drift.

Wukong, Sanzang, the white horse, Bajie, and Wujing remain recognizable from
their approved locks across changes of scale, angle, expression, and light.
Wujing keeps the gaunt slate build, red hair and beard, ragged sand-brown
clothes, and crescent spade; his skull state changes legibly from necklace to
raft to absent necklace. Bajie remains distinctly round, pink-gray,
black-clad, and rake-bearing. Wukong's gold band, pale face, golden fur, hemp
robe, tiger-skin kilt, red-and-gold staff, and long tail remain stable.

The Sand River retains its defining sand-gold and pale-gray identity with no
material green or blue intrusion. Page 46 enlarges the approved circular
reed-and-skull raft to carry the party, but preserves its perimeter skulls,
bound circular construction, and pale-gold ring, so the transformed object
and story state remain immediately clear. The crossed rake and crescent spade
on page 41 remain readable as two weapons meeting at impact rather than an
identity change.

Dialogue lettering weight and rounded balloon treatment are stable on pages
42–47. The unballooned display lettering on page 41, centered narration on
page 46, and serif closing prose on page 48 are controlled functional changes
and do not make any page feel imported from another book.

No redraw is justified for continuity.
