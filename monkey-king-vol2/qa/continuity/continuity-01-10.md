# Visual-continuity gate — canonical pages 1–10

## Review

Pages 1–10 remain in one coherent Mineral Ink register: visible brush-and-ink contours, toothed-paper texture, flat mineral washes, strong but compatible black levels, and consistent cinematic depth. The spring blue-green Five Elements Mountain sequence holds across pages 1–8, while the warmer ochre road and dusk palette on pages 4 and 9–10 reads as an intentional location and time shift rather than a finish change.

Wukong remains recognizable through the imprisoned, released-armour, and unbanded pilgrim states: the short pale muzzle, wide gold eyes with red rings, swept golden ruff, small wiry build, long tail, and ragged green-gold armour or plain hemp robe track the approved lock and story state. Sanzang retains his young shaven face, saffron robe, red grid kasaya, ringed staff, basket pack, and bay horse. Guanyin retains her white veil and robes, red brow dot, willow, vase, jewelry, and cloud. No recurring character, setting, or consequential object drifts enough to confuse identity or chronology.

Lettering is also continuous: dialogue pages use the same dark hand-lettered weight and cream, black-outlined balloon family; illustrated-prose pages use the same dark serif treatment on warm paper fields. Page 7's painted sound effect is a deliberate spectacle-page accent and remains compatible with the surrounding ink-and-pigment finish.

No page falls outside the visual language of the book. Cosmetic variation in framing, weather, facial expression, and local hue is nonblocking.

## Verdict

APPROVED
