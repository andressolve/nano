## Blind read
The pinned white dragon pleads that it was told to wait for a westbound pilgrim. Wukong confirms that the pilgrim is a monk, then points up the bank to the monk and reveals that the dragon ate his horse. The dominant middle panel belongs to the dragon's dawning horror: it follows Wukong's gesture, sees the monk, and realises its mistake. The final panel turns that shock into miserable explanation while Wukong answers with dry impatience.
