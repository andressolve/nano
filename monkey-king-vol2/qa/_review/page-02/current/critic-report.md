## Blind read
The Monkey King, trapped in a rocky mountainside, brusquely assumes the approaching heavenly woman has come to stare at him. She descends and identifies herself as Guanyin, says she wants something from him, and he answers with bitter skepticism about being asked while helpless. The close, central exchange between Guanyin and the scowling captive unmistakably owns the page. Guanyin changes the situation by giving him a mission—guard an eastward-coming monk on his journey to fetch holy books from the west—and promises that completing it will open the mountain.
