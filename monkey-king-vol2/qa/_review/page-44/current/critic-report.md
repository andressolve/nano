## Blind read
The river ogre confronts the monk by asking whether he is the tenth, then owns the page in the dominant close confession: he ate nine westbound monks and wears their unsinking skulls. He explains that the lady in white told him to wait for the tenth and that, unable to distinguish faces from footsteps under the sand, he mistook the approaching Monkey King for a threat. The page turns from confession to judgment when Wukong puts the ogre's fate in the monk's hands.
