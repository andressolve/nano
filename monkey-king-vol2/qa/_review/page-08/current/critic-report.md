## Blind read
A frightened pilgrim recoils from an explosively arriving monkey and begs not to be eaten. The monkey reassures him that he is his bodyguard. They introduce themselves: the monkey as Sun Wukong, the Great Sage Equal to Heaven, and the pilgrim as Sanzang, bound on foot for the far west to bring back the holy books. Sun Wukong then offers to carry Sanzang there by somersault before supper, but Sanzang refuses because walking every mile is essential to the pilgrimage. Sun Wukong owns the page through his dynamic arrival, extravagant identity, and impatiently magical solution; the turn is Sanzang's principled refusal, after which Sun Wukong realizes this master may be difficult.
