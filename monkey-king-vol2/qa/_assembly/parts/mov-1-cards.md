## Page 1 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
The one caption reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, with no other text anywhere, tail-free over the sky. A string that does not read is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue; the words and their order are what must match. Any writing legible on the seal or elsewhere is extra text and blocking.

### C2 — The face under the mountain does not land
One image owns the page as spectacle: a mountain shaped like five fingers pressed into the earth, and at its base a monkey's face in the rock with its eyes open. If the face is missing, hard to find, eyes shut, or reads as a carving rather than a living monkey trapped in stone, it is blocking.

### C3 — The peach is lost
One peach sits on the rock under his chin and reads as a peach, with a small brown young monkey climbing away and looking back. A peach that cannot be found or read as fruit, or no young monkey, loses the page's tenderness and is blocking; its exact place on the rock is not.

### C4 — Wukong not himself, or out of state
A monkey's face, golden fur matted with moss, gold eyes with a red ring, never a human face. A gold band or cap on his head, or his body drawn free of the rock, is blocking. The young monkey must be small, brown and bare; a second golden or clothed monkey is blocking.

### C5 — The mountain already broken
The ridges stand whole with the small gold seal on the highest one. Split ridges, or the mountain drawn twice as two states, is blocking.

### C5 — Peach continuity
In the first panel the peach has been eaten and its stone is in his teeth. A second whole peach drawn as focal, contradicting the eaten one, is blocking; a stone too small to identify, or missing, is not.

### C4 — The monk not himself
A young beardless man with a fully shaven head, saffron robe and red kasaya, ringed wooden staff, leading a plain brown horse. A white robe, veil, hair, a white or blue-maned horse, or a second monk is blocking.

### C5 — The companions turned back
