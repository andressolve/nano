## Staging notes from the script gate (carry them into the prompts and cards)

- p 1: the peach under his chin must be visible and readable as a peach.
- p 6 panel 3: Sanzang already scrambling down the ridge as the seal lifts.
- p 10: one Wukong far ahead on the hilltop and a gold motion streak racing back
  — make it read as speed, not as two monkeys.
- p 28: protect the balloon lanes for "Thank you too, Wukong…" in the
  transformation panel; it is the movement's heart.
- pp 36–37: stage both pages just outside the farmhouse gate, so that on p 37
  the daughter shuts the gate on the departing travellers.
- p 44: nine balloons; reserve panel 3's four lanes before placing faces and
  the skulls.
- Every page where the monk and Guanyin share a frame (p 16): the separation
  sentence (bare shaven head vs white hood-veil; saffron and red vs pure white;
  ground vs cloud).
- `WUKONG AS BRIDE` is an owner tag: the string belongs to the bride figure's
  mouth. Never letter any owner tag.
