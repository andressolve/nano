## Blind read
The Monkey King eagerly volunteers for a task, describing an expectation of royal payment and assuming he can finish by carrying the man west on his cloud. Guanyin owns the large central panel and changes the terms: the man must travel on the ground for the whole journey, because the journey must belong to him. The Monkey King's shocked question about walking turns the page from confident bravado to recognition of the human cost; Guanyin closes by saying that he will not guard the man alone and that others have also been asked to wait.
