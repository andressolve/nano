## Blind read
Wukong eagerly agrees to help after five hundred years trapped in the mountain and boasts that he can fly the coming traveler west by lunchtime. The large central exchange owns the page: Guanyin calmly explains that the traveler must remove the seal himself and must make the whole journey on foot. Wukong's grin falls when he realizes how slow that will be; Guanyin rises away and reveals that others are waiting too.
