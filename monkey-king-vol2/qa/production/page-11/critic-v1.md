## Findings

### Finding C3
- Observation: The first balloon in panel two (“You want his horse, his bundle, and the robe off his back?”) has no visible tail and sits at the upper left over Sanzang and the horse rather than hanging from or sitting plainly beside Wukong. The following balloon has a clear tail to Wukong, but the first does not.
- Material reader harm: The page does not visually assign both panel-two lines to Wukong as required; his opening challenge floats ambiguously over Sanzang and the horse, weakening the transfer from the monk’s fear to Wukong taking command of the confrontation.
- Redraw justification: Speaker ownership is a release-gate requirement, and the dominant “let me” turn depends on both panel-two lines belonging visibly to Wukong. That ambiguity materially damages the page’s central dramatic relationship enough to risk replacing the complete page.
