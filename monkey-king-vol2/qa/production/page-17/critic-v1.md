## Blind read
The Monkey King returns in a puff of dust, cuts off any welcome, and faces the startled monk. The monk asks why he returned; the Monkey King attributes it to a dragon showing him a picture of a shoe, refuses the rest of the story, and the two resume walking with the horse. The Monkey King owns the page, and his reluctant return turns the relationship back toward companionship, which he immediately undercuts with the claim that he hit nobody and had tea. The bottom walking panel is the strongest single panel.

## Findings

### Finding C1
- Observation: The middle exchange is split into two bordered panels. Standard panel order reads the monk’s question and “I’m glad you came back.” in the left panel before reaching Wukong’s shoe explanation in the right panel.
- Material reader harm: The emotional reply is encountered before the explanation that prompts it, reversing the required causal reading order and weakening the page’s central reconciliation beat.
- Redraw justification: The error is built into the panel structure rather than cosmetic lettering placement; preserving the scripted turn requires replacing the page despite the risk of a complete redraw.

### Finding C4
- Observation: Wukong visibly wears a narrow band across his forehead in every panel.
- Material reader harm: The band contradicts the required object state in which Wukong has no band or cap on his head while Sanzang conceals the cap, prematurely communicating that the controlling object is already on Wukong.
- Redraw justification: This changes a consequential story mechanic and Wukong’s focal identity across the entire page, so it is substantial enough to require replacement.
