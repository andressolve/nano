## Blind read
Wukong questions a white dragon at the stream and learns that its father, the Dragon King of the Eastern Sea, put it there after it burned his best pearl. Wukong recognizes the father, then demands restitution for the eaten horse. The dragon offers to become the monk's horse. The dominant final panel belongs to the white horse emerging in a great spiral of foam with the dragon's pale-blue mane and sea-green eyes, already saddled; the monk reaches to its nose and thanks it, Wukong protests, and the monk's final thanks to Wukong changes the emotional relationship.
