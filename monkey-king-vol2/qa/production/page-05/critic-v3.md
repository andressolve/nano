## Blind read
The monk and his horse arrive below a sealed mountain and a voice from a cave greets the monk as “MASTER.” The central face-to-face reveal owns the page: the speaker is a monkey inside the mountain who has waited five hundred years for a westbound monk. The turn comes when the monkey says a lady in white sent him and orders the wary monk to climb to the summit, remove the gold paper seal, and thereby gain his protection; the monk’s fear is answered with an insistence to climb anyway.
