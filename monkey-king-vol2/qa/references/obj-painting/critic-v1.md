## Mandatory findings

1. The required object lock is absent: this reads as a full-bleed landscape rather than a painting mounted on a hanging silk panel. Rebuild the composition as one isolated vertical or near-square silk panel centered on uninterrupted plain warm-paper ground, with a plainly visible top hanging support and free lower edge; contain the bridge, stream, old man, falling shoe, and reaching boy entirely inside that panel.
2. The plate uses malachite, azurite, cinnabar, and gold throughout, but the painting must be the sea-palace screen's only ink-only flat element. Remove every colored pigment and gold fleck from the image inside the panel and render it solely in black ink and gray washes, with value contrast preserving the high arched bridge, the old man's leaning posture, the detached airborne shoe, and the boy's two raised arms at thumbnail scale.
3. The pavilion, colored mountain panorama, framing pines, and red-leaved trees are unlisted primary scenery that would be learned as part of the recurring painting. Delete those additions rather than translating them into monochrome; retain only the high arched stone bridge over a stream and the three required action markers: old man above, detached shoe falling, boy below reaching.
