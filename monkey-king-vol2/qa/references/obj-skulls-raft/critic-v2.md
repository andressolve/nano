## Mandatory findings

1. The bundled reed in both states reads olive green. Repaint every reed strand and binding in a neutral dark river-reed range of charcoal brown and pale gray, with no green or blue pigment anywhere; retain pale clean bone, sand-gold water, warm paper, and flat late illumination as the only surrounding palette. This no-green/no-blue river palette is a primary location marker and must survive thumbnail reduction and grayscale.
2. The raft is surrounded by several separate thin concentric gold ripples. Replace all of those gold arcs with one broad, continuous pale-gold elliptical band immediately encircling the raft, and leave the remaining localized water strictly sand-gold and pale gray. The single broad ring of light must read as one restrained supernatural accent at thumbnail rather than as multiple decorative water rings.

## Nonblocking observations

- Both required object states are present: one loose open cord and one circular raft.
- Each state contains exactly nine complete, individually countable skulls; none is cropped, overlapped beyond recognition, or submerged.
- The plate has clear negative space, uninterrupted warm-paper ground, and no figures, lettering, labels, watermark, border, blood, or extraneous scenery.
- The skull/reed construction remains legible in profile-like oblique views, grayscale, and at thumbnail; re-entry after absence is carried by the exactly-nine repeated pale skulls.
