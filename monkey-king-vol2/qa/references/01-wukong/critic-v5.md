2. **The ruff violates the Never rule against a swept spiky hair crest.** The head view has long crown spikes and an especially long rearward sweep behind the ear, and the strict profile repeats an elongated, swept-back rear hair mass. Shorten the crown and rear fur tips in every view to no longer than the cheek fur, remove the rearward directional sweep, and close the outer contour into one compact rounded ruff around the pale heart-shaped face.

3. **The stored needle breaks the reserved head silhouette in the head view.** Its red-and-gold end projects beyond the outer edge of the ear and ruff. Reposition and shorten the needle so it lies close and parallel to the right side of the skull and terminates visibly inside the ruff's outer contour, before both the ear edge and the fur edge, in every view where the full staff is absent.
