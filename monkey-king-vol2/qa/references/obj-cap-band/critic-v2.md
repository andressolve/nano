## Mandatory findings

1. Reduce the vertical wall of both the free band and the band inside the cap to approximately half its present height, making both instances one identical finger-width, featureless sheet-gold strip with a light thin edge. Preserve the closed circle, but remove the current collar/cuff mass so the geometry matches the flush band on approved 10-wukong and 12-wukong-bride at thumbnail, in grayscale, and in strict side view.

2. Replace the cap's current high, faceted, rock-like dome with a low pliable skullcap of thin undyed cream cotton: collapse the crown to less than half the present dome height, remove the stone-like angular planes and crack marks, and replace the thick rigid rim with one thin folded cloth brim. The underside must remain clearly visible.

3. Seat the reduced band continuously and flush against the inside face of that thin folded brim, following the same oval without a visible gap. Its primary construction marker must read as a narrow strip sewn into soft cloth, not a separate rigid hoop resting inside a hard cavity.
