## Mandatory findings

1. The ears do not yet hold the locked low macaque structure. In the head view and full-length views, the upper outer rim rises above the brow and turns through a pronounced high point; at thumbnail and in profile this becomes an upright pointed ear rather than the required broad, low cup centered at eye level. Redraw both ears shorter vertically and broader front-to-back, lower their highest point to the brow line or below, and replace the upper point with one continuously rounded blunt semicircular rim. Preserve the cupped inner ear, but give the outer contour no corner or triangular rise in any of the four views.

2. The stored staff marker disappears in view (c). That view has no full staff, yet the strict right-side profile shows no red-and-gold needle behind the exposed right ear. Add one tiny straight red-lacquer-and-gold needle behind that ear, lying flat and parallel to the skull, with only one capped tip visible as a single small tick; do not enlarge it, duplicate it, or let it merge with the ear silhouette.

3. The mended patch migrates from the locked left shoulder onto the visible right shoulder in view (c). Because this is a strict right-side profile, the left-shoulder patch must be hidden rather than repeated on the near sleeve. Remove the patch from view (c) and keep the single neat patch only on Wukong's left shoulder in the views where that shoulder is visible.
