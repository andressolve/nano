## Mandatory findings

1. The ear silhouette is not locked to the required continuously rounded macaque cup. In the three-quarter head, strict profile, and released reaction, the upper outer rim rises to a visible apex instead of turning through one blunt uninterrupted curve; the profile and reaction tests make that apex especially liable to recur as a pointed ear. Redraw the ear in every view with its center at eye level and replace the upper apex with a broad semicircular outer rim whose tangent changes continuously from front attachment to rear attachment. Preserve the cupped inner rim, but give the outer contour no tip, corner, or triangular rise.

2. View (b) shows the full-size staff in Wukong's hand and also retains the red-and-gold stored needle behind his right ear. These are two states of the same primary marker, so the sheet currently teaches a duplicated staff. Remove the needle completely from view (b); show the single behind-the-right-ear needle only in views where the full staff is absent, while leaving the full staff's constant-diameter shaft and end-cap construction unchanged.
