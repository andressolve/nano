## Page intent

## PAGE 3

Dramatic. At the mountain, Wukong's face pushes out of the rock as far as it can go: yes, he will do it, after five hundred years of peaches from his monkeys. Close on both, he grins and promises to put the monk in the west by lunchtime with one somersault; Guanyin, still, tells him the monk must peel off the seal himself, and then gives the rule: the monk goes on the ground, every mile, because flying him there would make it Wukong's journey, not his. ## Exact owner-controlled script

## PAGE 3 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the Mountain of Five Elements.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Wukong's face pushing out of the rock as far as it can go,
eager. Guanyin on the left on her cloud.

> **WUKONG:** "Yes! I'll do it! My monkeys swim the sea every spring to bring their king a peach. Kings get paid."
>
> **WUKONG:** "Five hundred years of it. Get me out before they run out of grandchildren!"

**Panel 2 — dominant, 45%.** Close on both: Guanyin left, still; Wukong right,
grinning, very pleased with himself.

> **GUANYIN:** "When he comes, he must peel off the gold seal himself."
>
> **WUKONG:** "Then I'll put him in the west by lunchtime. One somersault on my cloud."
>
> **GUANYIN:** "No. He goes on the ground, the whole road, every mile. If you fly him there, it's your journey, not his."

**Panel 3 — 30%.** Wukong's face falling. Guanyin rising away on her cloud,
looking back down at him.

> **WUKONG:** "Walk? Do you know how slow people are?"
>
> **GUANYIN:** "I do. And you won't guard him alone. I've asked others to wait for him too."

**Why you turn the page:** a monk is coming, and somebody else is waiting for him.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 3 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
All seven lines read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once each, in order, with no other text. A string that does not read is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue; the words and their order are what must match. A line owned by placement (directly under its speaker's previous balloon, or naming its addressee) is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — The rule does not land
One panel owns the page: both faces close, the monkey grinning at his own plan while Guanyin stays still and says no. In the last beat his grin must visibly fall while she rises away. A face that does not change, or a page that reads as an action page, is blocking.

### C3 — Exchange misowned
Both opening lines, "Then I'll put him in the west by lunchtime..." and "Walk? Do you know how slow people are?" hang from the monkey's mouth; "When he comes...", "No. He goes on the ground..." and "I do. And you won't guard him alone..." hang from Guanyin's. "I do." tailed to the monkey, or any reply readable before the line it answers, is blocking.

### C4 — Identity and state
Guanyin in white with hood-veil, on her cloud, never on the ground. Wukong only a monkey's face in the rock; a gold band or cap, his body free, or his own cloud drawn under him, is blocking.
