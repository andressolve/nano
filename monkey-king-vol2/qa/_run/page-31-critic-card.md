## Page intent

## PAGE 31

Dramatic page. Wukong snaps the lock off the back house and the daughter runs out past him into her father's arms. Inside, Wukong is caught halfway through changing, half monkey and half the young woman, while the worried monk in the doorway asks him not to hurt the pig; Wukong answers that he is going to marry him. Then the bride sits primly on the curtained bed, hands folded, perfect except for the thin gold band at her hairline. The one dominant turn is the girl let out and Wukong becoming the bride, carried by the half-changed figure facing the monk. The real daughter stays small and silent; the old man is a background figure. The joke is the gold band. The reader turns because the bride is now waiting in the dark for a pig.
