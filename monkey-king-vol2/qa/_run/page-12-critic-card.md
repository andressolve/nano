## Page intent

## PAGE 12

A spectacle page, one image, on the border road among boulders. The needle has become the red-and-gold iron staff, swung in one enormous arc: robbers fly off in every direction, cudgels spin into the sky, and the leader lies flat on his back in the dust with his chopper gone. The dominant turn is the staff raised high in both of Wukong's hands over a man who cannot get up. The only word is the sound of the blow. Small behind the horse, the monk's mouth hangs open, silent. The reader turns because the staff is about to come down, and the joy of the fight has tipped into something the monk cannot bear to watch.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 12 — card

### C1 — transcription
The single sound cue reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, along the staff's arc, with no other text anywhere; a missing or misspelled cue is blocking, and the cue is not to be measured. The run length of a repeated letter, letter spacing, and casing style are nonblocking; the word must match. Any balloon, caption, or label is blocking. Also nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, and a regional spelling variant of the same word; the words and their order are what must match.

### C2 — the turn lands: the staff over the fallen man
One image owns the page: the great arc with robbers flying, and above all the staff raised high in Wukong's two hands over the leader flat on his back. Blocking if the raised staff and the helpless man do not read by eye, or the page is split into panels. Exact composition is nonblocking.

### C3 — the staff
The staff is a straight red-lacquered iron rod with gold end caps, longer than Wukong is tall; blocking if it reads as a spear, blade, or the monk's ringed staff, or if it is doubled. Flying cudgels are plain wood.

### C4 — identity and state
Wukong is the small golden monkey with gold red-ringed eyes, a long tail, and the mended hemp robe; a band or cap on him is blocking. The monk, small behind the horse with his mouth open, is the shaven monk in saffron and red and has no balloon. The number of flying robbers is nonblocking. The robbers read as ordinary beardless working men in cloth head-wraps and rough brown and gray, the same men across pages 11 to 13, the leader the tallest with the chopper; any robber reading as a monk (shaven head, robe) or as a man with a long gray moustache is blocking. The exact robber count in a melee and the details of their weapons are nonblocking.

### C5 — focal integrity
Wukong's face and his two hands on the staff are whole and correct; a third hand or malformed grip is blocking. Robber anatomy in the melee is nonblocking.
