## Page intent

## PAGE 14

A dramatic page on the floor of the Eastern Sea, a world of azurite and crystal. Wukong plunges into Ao Guang's palace, scattering shrimp and crab guards, and hails the Dragon King, who freezes on his throne: not you again, the thief of his iron pillar. The dominant turn is the horrified host: the vast dragon-lord pouring tea from a tiny pot with great care, his eyes straying for one wistful moment to a small empty coral seat beside him, while Wukong sprawls with his feet up and says he quit his monk this morning because the monk called him a robber. Ao Guang says he is one. Wukong grins that robbing Ao Guang was different. The reader turns because the one he robbed is the only one he came to tell.

## Exact owner-controlled script

## PAGE 14 · *dramatic*

**Location:** Ao Guang's crystal palace on the floor of the Eastern Sea.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** Wukong plunging down through blue water into a hall of
crystal and coral, shrimp and crab guards scattering. On a coral throne at the
far end, the enormous Dragon King frozen, fingers stopped on the throne's arm.

> **WUKONG:** "Ao Guang! Dragon King of the Eastern Sea! Still got your throne?"
>
> **AO GUANG:** "No. No, no. Not you again. You took my iron pillar that held the sea down, and shrank it to a needle!"
>
> **WUKONG:** "Calm down, old fish. I only want tea."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 45%.** Ao Guang, huge, pouring tea from a tiny pot with
great care, very polite and very frightened. Beside the throne, a small empty
coral seat; for a moment his eyes go to it, wistful. Wukong sprawled on a stool on the right, his feet up.

> **AO GUANG:** "I heard you were under a mountain. Why are you out?"
>
> **WUKONG:** "A monk let me out, so I could guard him to the west. I quit this morning. He called me a robber."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Ao Guang on the left, eyes slitting, fingers drumming again. Wukong on the
right on his stool, grinning, teacup raised.

> **AO GUANG:** "You are a robber. You robbed me of the staff."
>
> **WUKONG:** "That was different. That was you."

**Why you turn the page:** the one he robbed is the only one he has come to tell.

## Numbered blocking criteria

### C1 — transcription
Every listed string reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in causal order, owned by the right mouth by tail and placement, with no extra text; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue are nonblocking; the words and their order must match. A line owned by placement (directly under its speaker's previous balloon, or naming its addressee) is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — the turn lands: the horrified host
The eye must find the vast Dragon King pouring tea from a tiny pot for the sprawled monkey as the page's centre, in a sea-floor world of blue and crystal with no green. Blocking if the tea scene does not read as the main image or the place reads as land. Exact proportion is nonblocking.

### C3 — attribution
In the arrival, the two Wukong lines hang from the plunging monkey and "No. No, no." from Ao Guang; at tea, Ao Guang asks and Wukong answers; in the last exchange Ao Guang speaks first, Wukong replies. Each line must hang from its own speaker's mouth by tail, not merely make sense as his. Blocking if any line is owned by the wrong mouth or a shrimp or crab carries a balloon.

### C4 — identity and scale
Ao Guang is a robed dragon-lord with a long horse-like head, antlers, floating white whiskers, gold slit eyes, enormous beside Wukong; a human face or ordinary-man scale is blocking. Wukong is the small golden monkey in the mended hemp robe; a band or cap on him is blocking.

### C5 — the empty seat
A small empty coral seat beside the throne is visible in the tea panel, with nobody in it; a figure seated in it is blocking. Exact glance direction is nonblocking.

### C6 — focal integrity
Ao Guang's two pouring hands, the tiny pot, and both speaking faces are whole and correct; malformed focal hands are blocking.
