## PAGE 11 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the border road, among boulders.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** Six rough men jumping out from behind boulders with cudgels
and a chopper. The leader grabs the bay horse's bridle; two others tug at the
monk's bundle and at his robe. The robbers say nothing. Sanzang on the left,
palms together, terrified.

> **SANZANG:** "Please! I'm only a monk. I have nothing worth stealing!"

**Panel 2 — dominant, 45%.** Wukong stepping out in front of the monk, grinning
wide, hands on his hips, the robbers on the right, the monk behind the horse
on the left.

> **WUKONG:** "You want his horse, his bundle, and the robe off his back?"
>
> **WUKONG:** "Master, stand behind the horse. It's my first fight as a bodyguard, and I've been looking forward to it."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Close: Wukong pulling a tiny needle from behind his right
ear and holding it up between two fingers at the leader's chopper.

> **WUKONG:** "Never seen a needle like this one? Watch the needle."

**Why you turn the page:** the needle is about to stop being a needle.

## PAGE 12 · *spectacle*

**Location:** the border road, among boulders.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 100%.** The needle grown into a red-and-gold iron staff
twice his height, swung in one great arc: five robbers flying in five
directions, cudgels spinning off into the sky. The leader flat on his back in
the dust, chopper gone. Wukong standing over him, the staff raised high in
both hands to bring it down. Behind the horse, the monk's mouth open.

> **SOUND** *(along the arc of the staff)*
> "WHAM"

**Why you turn the page:** the staff is about to come down on a man who cannot get up.

## PAGE 13 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the border road, among boulders.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Sanzang throwing himself across the fallen robber, arms
out, the staff above them both.

> **SANZANG:** "No! Stop! You'll kill him!"
>
> **WUKONG:** "That's the idea! Move, Master!"

**Panel 2 — dominant, 45%.** Wukong lowering the staff, furious, the robbers
running off behind him. Sanzang kneeling up, shaking, facing him.

> **SANZANG:** "He's a man! You could have frightened him off. You were going to break his head!"
>
> **WUKONG:** "He had a chopper for your head! On this road it's eat or be eaten."
>
> **SANZANG:** "Then you're no better than he is. I can't walk with a robber."

**Panel 3 — 20%.** Close on Wukong's face: the hurt under the anger.

> **WUKONG:** "I waited under a rock for five hundred years for you. Fine. Walk to the west by yourself."

**Panel 4 — 10%.** The empty road. A gold streak shooting up into the sky. The
monk alone by the horse.

**Why you turn the page:** he has left, and the monk is alone on a road that eats monks.

## PAGE 14 · *dramatic*

**Location:** Ao Guang's crystal palace on the floor of the Eastern Sea.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** Wukong plunging down through blue water into a hall of
crystal and coral, shrimp and crab guards scattering. On a coral throne at the
far end, the enormous Dragon King frozen, fingers stopped on the throne's arm.

> **WUKONG:** "Ao Guang! Dragon King of the Eastern Sea! Still got your throne?"
>
> **AO GUANG:** "No. No, no. Not you again. You took my iron pillar that held the sea down, and shrank it to a needle!"
>
> **WUKONG:** "Calm down, old fish. I only want tea."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 45%.** Ao Guang, huge, pouring tea from a tiny pot with
great care, very polite and very frightened. Beside the throne, a small empty
coral seat; for a moment his eyes go to it, wistful. Wukong sprawled on a stool on the right, his feet up.

> **AO GUANG:** "I heard you were under a mountain. Why are you out?"
>
> **WUKONG:** "A monk let me out, so I could guard him to the west. I quit this morning. He called me a robber."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Ao Guang on the left, eyes slitting, fingers drumming again. Wukong on the
right on his stool, grinning, teacup raised.

> **AO GUANG:** "You are a robber. You robbed me of the staff."
>
> **WUKONG:** "That was different. That was you."

**Why you turn the page:** the one he robbed is the only one he has come to tell.

## PAGE 15 · *dramatic*

**Location:** Ao Guang's crystal palace.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Ao Guang on the left on his throne, turning to point at a painted screen
behind him: ink only, an old man on a stone bridge, a shoe falling, a boy
below reaching up. Wukong on the right, looking up at it.

> **WUKONG:** "What's that? An old man throwing his shoe at a boy?"
>
> **AO GUANG:** "He dropped it off a bridge, and the boy fetched it. He dropped it again, and the boy fetched it again. Three times."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** The painting, large, filling the panel: the bridge, the old man, the falling
shoe, the boy. In the foreground, Ao Guang's huge head and shoulder on the
left, leaning in sideways to the monkey, and Wukong small on the right,
teacup in his hand.

> **WUKONG:** "And then?"
>
> **AO GUANG:** "And then the old man taught him everything he knew, and the boy became the greatest man of his time."
>
> **AO GUANG:** "Going back is very admirable. You could go back. Now. Far away."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Wukong on his feet, scowling, tea slopping. Ao Guang behind
him, hopeful.

> **WUKONG:** "That's a terrible story. I hate it."
>
> **AO GUANG:** "You're leaving?"
>
> **WUKONG:** "I'm going back to my monk. Don't tell anyone you were right."

**Why you turn the page:** he is going back, and he does not know what is waiting.

## PAGE 16 · *illustrated prose*

**Location:** the border road.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 70%.** The monk sitting on a stone by the bay horse, his
face wet. Above him, low, Guanyin on her cloud, bending down, putting a plain
cloth cap into his hands. Inside the cap, a thin band of gold glints.

> **PROSE FIELD 1** *(over the empty sky, never over either face)*
> "The robbers had run. The monkey had flown. Sanzang sat on a stone and did not know which way to go.
>
> Then the woman in white came down on her cloud. 'I am Guanyin,' she said. 'I sent the monkey to you.' She put a plain cap into his hands, with a thin band of gold sewn inside it, and taught him three words: Gold, hold fast.
>
> 'He will come back,' she said. 'Give him this. If ever he is about to do a terrible thing, say the words.'
>
> 'What will they do to him?' asked Sanzang.
>
> 'Make him stop.'"

**Why you turn the page:** the monk has a trap in his hands, and the monkey is coming back.

## PAGE 17 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the border road, by a white milestone.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** Wukong landing in a puff of dust beside a white milestone.
Sanzang on his stone, startled, hiding the cap behind his back.

> **WUKONG:** "I'm back. Don't make a speech."
>
> **SANZANG:** "I wasn't going to."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 45%.** The two of them face to face by the milestone,
Sanzang left, Wukong right, arms folded, not quite looking at him.

> **SANZANG:** "Why did you come back?"
>
> **WUKONG:** "A dragon showed me a picture of a shoe. It's a long story, and a stupid one, and I'm not telling it."
>
> **SANZANG:** "I'm glad you came back."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Walking west again: Wukong a step ahead, the monk leading
the horse.

> **WUKONG:** "Don't be glad. Walk. And I didn't hit anybody the whole time I was gone. I had tea."

**Why you turn the page:** he came back on his own, and the monk is still hiding the cap.

## PAGE 18 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the border road, at a resting place.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Sanzang on the left holding out the plain cloth cap.
Wukong turning, surprised.

> **SANZANG:** "I have something for you, to go with the robe."
>
> **WUKONG:** "Another present? For me?"

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** Wukong pulling the cap on with both hands,
delighted, posing for the monk. Inside the brim, the thin gold band, glinting.
Sanzang on the left, palms pressed together, not smiling.

> **WUKONG:** "I had a cap in Heaven with long feathers. Nobody gave me that one either. I took it."
>
> **SANZANG:** "It suits you."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Wukong striding ahead in the cap, looking back, grinning.
The monk behind, eyes down.

> **WUKONG:** "Come on, Master! I'll be good all day today. Maybe all week."

**Why you turn the page:** he has put it on, gladly, and the monk has not told him what it is.

---

## PAGE 19 · *dramatic*

**Location:** a camp by the road at night.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** The fire. Sanzang on the left, earnest. Wukong on the
right in the cap, laughing.

> **SANZANG:** "Wukong, promise me something. Never strike a man again, not even to save me."
>
> **WUKONG:** "Promise that? The next robber takes your head off, and I stand there with my hands folded?"

**Panel 2 — 30%.** Wukong pulling the needle, and the staff growing out of his
fist, swinging it to show off, its end sweeping past the monk's nose. The monk
flinching back.

> **WUKONG:** "This keeps you alive, Master. Not promises."

**Panel 3 — dominant, 45%.** Sanzang, frightened, eyes shut, palms pressed
together hard, saying the words. Wukong behind him, staff still up, one hand
flying to his head.

> **SANZANG:** "Gold, hold fast."
>
> **WUKONG:** "Ow! What's that? What did you do?"

**Why you turn the page:** the monk said the words because he was frightened, and something is closing on the monkey's head.

---

## PAGE 20 · *spectacle*

**Location:** the camp by the road at night.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 100%.** Wukong rolling in the dust of the road, both
hands clutching his head, the staff flung aside, the cloth cap tearing apart
and flying off in pieces. Around his brow, glowing, a thin gold band. In the
firelight on the left, Sanzang with both hands over his mouth, horrified.

> **WUKONG:** "STOP! Make it stop!"

**Why you turn the page:** the cap is gone, and the band is still on him.
