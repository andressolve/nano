## Page intent

## PAGE 22

A dramatic page at the South Sea, above Guanyin's small rocky island with its still bamboo grove. Wukong lands on a rock and shouts up at Guanyin on her cloud that she tricked him, let him out from under one mountain and put another on his head, and she says yes. The dominant turn is the confrontation: she explains calmly why the monk needed a way to stop him, he insists he went back on his own, and she agrees, and still the band stays; it comes off only at the end of the road. The page belongs to the contrast between his fury, jabbing at his brow, and her stillness. He turns away, and she sends him back to the monk, who is frightened and alone. The reader turns because the band stays and the monk is alone in the dark. The sea and bamboo stay calm and subordinate.

## Exact owner-controlled script

## PAGE 22 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the South Sea, above Guanyin's island.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** A rocky island with a still bamboo grove in a pale jade
sea. Wukong landing on a rock below, pointing up at Guanyin on her cloud.

> **WUKONG:** "You tricked me! You let me out from under one mountain and put another one on my head!"
>
> **GUANYIN:** "Yes."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 45%.** Guanyin calm on the left; Wukong on the right,
jabbing at the gold band on his brow.

> **GUANYIN:** "You were about to break a man's head, and the only way your monk could stop you was to lie down under your staff."
>
> **WUKONG:** "I went back to him, on my own, before your hat!"
>
> **GUANYIN:** "I know you did. But now he can stop you with three words, before you do a thing that can't be undone."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Wukong turning away. Guanyin watching him go.

> **WUKONG:** "Then take it off."
>
> **GUANYIN:** "When you reach the west, it will come off by itself. Go back to him. He's frightened, and he's alone."

**Why you turn the page:** the band stays, and the monk is alone in the dark.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 22 — card

### C1 — Transcription or ownership fails
Any of the seven scripted strings is missing, altered, doubled, reordered, or unreadable from the 600 × 900 proof, or extra text appears; or a balloon cannot be assigned by tail and placement. A string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant; a line directly under its speaker's previous balloon is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — The confrontation does not land
A dramatic page whose dominant moment, found first by the eye, is Guanyin calm on her cloud and Wukong jabbing at the band on his brow. Blocking if her stillness against his fury is lost.

### C3 — Attribution
"Yes." must hang from Guanyin after Wukong's accusation. In the dominant exchange her reason, then Wukong's "I went back to him…", then her "I know you did…", in that order, each from its own mouth. "Then take it off." from Wukong, then her final line. Blocking if any line belongs to the wrong mouth.

### C4 — Identity, setting, and band
Guanyin stays on her cloud in her white hood-veil; Wukong wears the thin plain gold band. Blocking: the monk present, no band or a cap or crown on Wukong, or the place not readable as a jade sea with an island.

### C5 — Focal generation integrity
Blocking: a duplicated named figure, gross anatomy on Wukong's jabbing hand or Guanyin's hands, or text over a face. Waves and bamboo detail are nonblocking.
