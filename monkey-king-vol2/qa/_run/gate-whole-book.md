# Whole-book release gate — canonical pages 1–48

Fresh read-only reviewer with no inherited history. This gate sees promoted
pages and owner materials only, never builder prompts or audits.

1. Read all 48 pages once in order at 600 × 900, story material closed.
   Write the retell, the cast, the causal chain, and the ending: what it cost
   and why the final image matters.
2. Then open `08-FULL-SCRIPT.md` and `07-PAGE-CONTRACT.md`. Check every page's
   exact text and attribution against the script, every declared mode against
   what rendered, and the mode balance of the whole book.
3. Answer the acceptance questions for each movement: who acted, what they
   wanted, what choice was made, why, who was affected, what the protagonist
   now believes, and why we went to the next scene. A movement fails if the
   answer depends on information outside the pages.

Write `qa/whole-book.md` and return `APPROVED` or `REVISE`. Every finding
names pages, material reader harm, and redraw justification. Never edit or
promote.
