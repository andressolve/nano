## Materiality threshold

The critic is a release gate, not a defect collector. A visible issue is
blocking only when it materially harms the reader event, exact text or
attribution, named or focal identity, consequential continuity or object state,
focal generation integrity, or the dominant dramatic relationship. Every
correction replaces the complete page. Minor background, cosmetic, tiny-prop,
geometry, colour, texture, and prompt-fidelity observations are nonblocking.
Omit them. Numeric panel shares and lettering sizes are builder construction

Each numbered criterion is also its stable retry signature. Cite it only when

A sound cue whose letters and order match the script reads correctly; the run

## Page 48 — card

### C1 — transcription
Every listed string reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in causal order, owned by the right field: one prose field, four paragraphs, in order, the last one alone, with no extra text anywhere; a string that does not read is blocking, and a string that reads is not to be measured. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue are nonblocking; the words and their order are what must match. Any balloon, caption, or label is extra text and blocking.

### C2 — the prose page and its picture
Illustrated prose: one dominant morning road image with the prose field over open sky. A field over the figures is blocking; field shape and line breaks are nonblocking.

### C3 — he waits
By eye, Wukong stands far ahead on a hilltop, hand shading his eyes, looking back at the others coming up the road. If he looks away west or walks off, the last turn fails and it is blocking.

### C4 — the company and state
The monk rides the white horse; Wujing and Bajie carry one luggage pole between them; Wujing wears no skulls. A missing member or a skull necklace is blocking; Wukong wears the hemp robe and thin gold band, and a cap or no band is blocking.
