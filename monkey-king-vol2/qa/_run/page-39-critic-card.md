## Page intent

## PAGE 39

Spectacle. The travellers reach the Flowing Sand River: water the colour of sand, flat and heavy, so wide its far side is lost in haze, no boats and no waves, and a tall stone tablet on the bank carved with its name and its warning. The dominant turn is the size of the obstacle and the stone's sentence: eight hundred miles, and nothing floats. The monk, the pig, and the monkey are tiny beside the tablet, staring; the river owns the page. The reader turns because there is no bridge and no boat, and the monk cannot fly. What stays subordinate: the reeds and the figures' faces, which need not be read.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 39 — card

### C1 — transcription
Every listed string reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in causal order, owned by the right object: the three lines are carved on the stone tablet, with no extra text anywhere; a string that does not read is blocking, and a string that reads is not to be measured. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue are nonblocking; the words and their order are what must match. The tablet's words are object text, not balloons; any balloon or caption is extra text and blocking.

### C2 — the river owns the page
A spectacle page: the sand-coloured river, so wide its far side is lost, and the tablet carry it, with the travellers tiny beside the stone. A boat, a bridge, or a visible far bank with detail is blocking; reed and haze treatment are nonblocking.

### C3 — a different world
The river and land read sand-gold and pale gray, unlike the green road behind them. Green or blue water is blocking because the river must look like a desert; small tonal variation is nonblocking.

### C4 — three travellers
The monk on a white horse, the pig, and the monkey are present and recognisable as themselves at small scale. A missing traveller is blocking; their faces need not be readable.
