## Page intent

## PAGE 24

Spectacle. Eagle Grief Stream: a deep green mountain stream in a gorge of gray cliffs. Out of the water, in a column of white foam, bursts a long slim white dragon, and its jaws close over the monk's plain bay horse, saddle and all. On the bank Wukong leaps back with the monk tucked safe under one arm; on the water floats the empty bridle. The only word is the sound of the swallow. The dominant turn: the white dragon swallows the horse. The dragon and the swallow own the page; Wukong and the monk are the startled witnesses, and the reader turns because the horse is gone and the dragon is still in the water.

### C1 — Transcription fails
The one sound cue must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, beside the dragon's jaws, with no other text anywhere on the page; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue; the words and their order are what must match. Any balloon is blocking.

### C2 — The swallow does not land
One image must own the page as a spectacle: a white dragon bursting up out of a green stream in foam, its jaws closing over a brown horse, saddle and all, while Wukong leaps back with the monk under one arm and an empty bridle floats on the water. If the reader cannot tell the horse is being swallowed, or the horse also stands whole on the bank, the turn fails.

### C3 — Identity
The dragon: a slim white serpent with sea-green eyes, two short horn spikes, and a pale-blue mane; antlers, whiskers, a fin beard, or robes are blocking. Wukong: a small golden monkey in a hemp robe with a thin gold band on his brow; a missing band, a cap, or armor rags are blocking. The monk: young, shaven, in saffron and red.

### C4 — Integrity
The monk reads as held safe. Exactly one dragon. Foam and rock detail are nonblocking.
