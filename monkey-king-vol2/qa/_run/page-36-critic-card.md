## Page intent

## PAGE 36

Dramatic page. At dawn in Old Gao's courtyard the pig kneels before the monk, forehead in the dust, begging to be taken on. Wukong steps between them, jabbing a finger: this pig locked a girl in a shed. The monk answers calmly that he took Wukong too, and gave him a robe. The one dominant turn is the pig kneeling and Wukong's objection met by the monk's reply, in that exchange. Then Wukong goes quiet, touching the mended patch on his robe's shoulder, and covers it with a joke. Last, the pig turns on his knees to the daughter at the gate and apologizes for locking the door, afraid she would run from his real face. Old Gao and the daughter are silent witnesses. The reader turns because he has said sorry, and she has not answered yet.
