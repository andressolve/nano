## Page intent

## PAGE 27

Dramatic. Close on the pinned dragon's young face, its sea-green eyes wide: it is not just an eel, a lady in white told it to wait here for a pilgrim going west. Wukong asks whether that means a monk going west, and the dragon says yes. Then the great image: Wukong points back up the bank at the monk, who gives a small, weak wave, and the dragon's eyes follow his finger to the man whose horse it ate. Oh no. Last, the dragon miserable with its chin in the water, explaining it was hungry and nobody mentioned a horse; Wukong, unimpressed, has waited five hundred years under a rock. The dominant turn: the monster was waiting for the monk, and ate his horse first. The dragon's dawning horror owns the page.

### C1 — Transcription fails
All seven strings must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in causal order, each owned by the right mouth by tail and placement, with no other text; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue; the words and their order are what must match. A line owned by placement is owned even if its tail wanders.
