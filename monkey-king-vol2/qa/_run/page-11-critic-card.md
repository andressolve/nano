## Page intent

## PAGE 11

A dramatic page on the border road among boulders. Six rough men jump out at the monk: one seizes the bay horse's bridle, two tug at his bundle and his robe, and none of them says a word. Sanzang, terrified, begs that he is only a monk with nothing to steal. The dominant turn is "let me": Wukong steps out in front of his master grinning, hands on hips, delighted that this is his first fight as a bodyguard, and sends the monk behind the horse. The page ends close on two fingers holding up a tiny needle at the leader's chopper, so the reader turns to see what the needle becomes. The robbers stay a silent threat; Wukong's appetite for the fight owns the page.

## Exact owner-controlled script

## PAGE 11 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the border road, among boulders.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** Six rough men jumping out from behind boulders with cudgels
and a chopper. The leader grabs the bay horse's bridle; two others tug at the
monk's bundle and at his robe. The robbers say nothing. Sanzang on the left,
palms together, terrified.

> **SANZANG:** "Please! I'm only a monk. I have nothing worth stealing!"

**Panel 2 — dominant, 45%.** Wukong stepping out in front of the monk, grinning
wide, hands on his hips, the robbers on the right, the monk behind the horse
on the left.

> **WUKONG:** "You want his horse, his bundle, and the robe off his back?"
>
> **WUKONG:** "Master, stand behind the horse. It's my first fight as a bodyguard, and I've been looking forward to it."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Close: Wukong pulling a tiny needle from behind his right
ear and holding it up between two fingers at the leader's chopper.

> **WUKONG:** "Never seen a needle like this one? Watch the needle."

**Why you turn the page:** the needle is about to stop being a needle.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 11 — card

### C1 — transcription
Every listed string reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in causal order, owned by the right mouth by tail and placement, with no extra text; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue are nonblocking; the words and their order must match. A line owned by placement (directly under its speaker's previous balloon, or naming its addressee) is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — the turn lands: "let me"
The eye must find Wukong stepping out in front of the monk, grinning, as the page's centre, with the ambush above as the set-up and the needle close below as the hook. Blocking if the ambush strip outweighs Wukong's arrival or the page is not read as dramatic panels. Exact proportion of the panels is nonblocking.

### C3 — attribution
The monk's plea hangs from Sanzang's mouth; both lines of panel two and the needle line hang from Wukong's. Blocking if any robber carries a balloon, or if the plea could be read as Wukong's. A tail that drifts but sits plainly beside its speaker is nonblocking.

### C4 — identity and state
Wukong is the small golden monkey with gold red-ringed eyes, a long tail, and the mended hemp robe; Sanzang is the young shaven monk in saffron and red. A band or cap on Wukong is blocking. Robbers bearded, or wearing monk's or monkey's identity, is blocking. The robbers read as ordinary beardless working men in cloth head-wraps and rough brown and gray, the same men across pages 11 to 13, the leader the tallest with the chopper; any robber reading as a monk (shaven head, robe) or as a man with a long gray moustache is blocking. The exact robber count in a melee and the details of their weapons are nonblocking.

### C5 — the needle
Panel three shows one tiny needle held up between two fingers, reading as a needle, not a staff and not a knife. Blocking if the needle is missing or already full-length. The robber count in the ambush is nonblocking.

### C6 — focal integrity
Speaking faces and Wukong's needle hand are whole and correct; malformed focal hands or a doubled Wukong is blocking. Background anatomy among the robbers is nonblocking.
