## Page intent

## PAGE 44

Dramatic. The ogre, standing in the shallows, asks the monk if he is the tenth one, and then tells him the worst thing: nine monks came to this river going west, and he ate them; their skulls would not sink, so he wears them. The dominant turn is that confession, close on the ogre with his hand closed over one skull and the monk's face beyond his shoulder. Then the lady in white's instruction, and the honest answer to why he swung: he hears feet, not faces, and he thought the Monkey King had come for him. Last, Wukong, low and tight on his staff, gives the monk the choice. The reader turns because the monk has to answer.

## Exact owner-controlled script

## PAGE 44 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the bank of the Flowing Sand River.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** The ogre standing in the shallows on the right, spade
lowered, facing the monk on the left. Bajie hiding behind the horse.

> **OGRE:** "Are you the tenth one?"
>
> **SANZANG:** "The tenth what?"

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** The ogre, close, one hand closed over a skull on
his necklace. The monk's face beyond his shoulder.

> **OGRE:** "Nine monks came to this river before you, going west. I was hungry, and I was a monster, and I ate them."
>
> **OGRE:** "Their skulls wouldn't sink, so I wear them."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** The ogre on the left, still talking, looking at the monk.
Sanzang on the right, frightened, standing his ground.

> **OGRE:** "Then the lady in white came and told me: wait for the tenth one."
>
> **SANZANG:** "Then why did you swing at me?"
>
> **OGRE:** "Under the sand I hear feet, not faces, and I swing at the first one."
>
> **OGRE:** "Up here I saw the Monkey King, and I thought he'd come for me."

**Panel 4 — 15%.** Close: Wukong, his hand tight on the staff, speaking low to
the monk without taking his eyes off the ogre.

> **WUKONG:** "Master. Say the word and he goes back in the river. Your choice, not mine."

**Why you turn the page:** the monster has told the monk the worst thing, and the monk has to answer.
