## Page intent

## PAGE 7

A spectacle page, near-silent. The five ridges burst apart with a roar, rock and dust thrown into the sky, and out of the middle, riding the blast, the small gold monkey in the rags of his old armor, arms flung wide, tail out, laughing at the sky. Far down the slope, tiny, the monk lies flat on his back beside his horse, looking up; the gold seal flutters away high on the wind. The one dominant turn is the mountain cracking, the monkey rising, and the monk falling. The page belongs to the monkey's freedom; the monk is small and subordinate but must be there, because he did this. One sound and nothing else. The reader turns because the monkey is out and the monk is on his back looking at him.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 7 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
The one sound cue reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, with no other text anywhere. A string that does not read is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue (BOOOM for BOOM is fine); the words and their order are what must match. Any balloon or caption is blocking.

### C2 — The release does not land
One image owns the page: the mountain bursting open and the small gold monkey rising out of it, arms wide, laughing. If the eye cannot find him, or the page reads as panels rather than one spectacle image, it is blocking.

### C3 — The monk is missing
The tiny monk lies on his back far down the slope, beside his horse. If he is absent or reads as debris, the reader loses who did this; that is blocking. His exact pose is not.

### C4 — Released Wukong
A gold monkey in green-gray rags of old armor, round gold eyes, long tail. A hemp robe, a gold band, a cap, or two monkeys is blocking.

### C5 — The mountain state
The mountain is split open, drawn once, with the seal flying away high. A whole mountain beside a split one is blocking.
