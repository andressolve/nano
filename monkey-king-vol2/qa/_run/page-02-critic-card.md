## Page intent

## PAGE 2

Dramatic. At the mountain, a white cloud comes down out of a blue sky with a tall woman in white on it, and the monkey's face in the rock rolls its eyes up at her, a peach stone in his teeth, and tells her to get in line. Close and level with his face, calm on her cloud, she names herself, Guanyin, and says she has come to ask him for something; he scowls that she is asking a rock. Then she bends a little closer and tells him about a monk walking west for the holy books, on a road that eats monks, and the price: guard him the whole way, and the mountain opens. His eyes go narrow and interested. The dominant turn: Guanyin arrives with the offer. The reader turns because the way out has a price and he has not heard all of it. The sky and ridges stay subordinate to the two faces.

## Exact owner-controlled script

## PAGE 2 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the Mountain of Five Elements.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** A white cloud coming down out of a blue sky toward the
cleft. On it, small, a tall woman in white. In the foreground, the monkey's
face in the rock, eyes rolled up toward her, peach stone in his teeth.

> **WUKONG:** "If you've come to stare at me, get in line. Everyone in Heaven already had a turn."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** GUANYIN on her cloud, level with his face, close:
white hood-veil, a willow sprig lifted in her right hand, a white vase in her
left. Calm. The monkey's face on the right, scowling, a head of moss.

> **GUANYIN:** "I'm Guanyin. I haven't come to stare. I've come to ask you for something."
>
> **WUKONG:** "Ask a rock? Lady, I can't even scratch my own nose."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Guanyin bending a little toward him. Wukong's eyes going
narrow, interested.

> **GUANYIN:** "A monk is walking this way from the far east, to fetch the holy books from the far west."
>
> **GUANYIN:** "It's a long road, and it eats monks. Guard him the whole way there, and this mountain will open."

**Why you turn the page:** the way out has a price, and he has not heard all of it yet.

## Materiality threshold

The critic is a release gate, not a defect collector. A visible issue is
blocking only when it materially harms the reader event, exact text or
attribution, named or focal identity, consequential continuity or object state,
focal generation integrity, or the dominant dramatic relationship. Every
correction replaces the complete page. Minor background, cosmetic, tiny-prop,
geometry, colour, texture, and prompt-fidelity observations are nonblocking.
Omit them. Numeric panel shares and lettering sizes are builder construction
guidance, never critic measurements.

Each numbered criterion is also its stable retry signature. Cite it only when
the reader harm and redraw justification are concrete.

A lettered speaker name, label, or tag anywhere on the page is extra text and
blocks under the transcription criterion, whatever else reads correctly:
finished pages carry dialogue, captions, prose, and sound cues only.
A sound cue whose letters and order match the script reads correctly; the run
length of a repeated letter in it is nonblocking and never counted.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 2 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
All five lines read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once each, in order, with no other text. A string that does not read is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue; the words and their order are what must match. A line owned by placement (directly under its speaker's previous balloon, or naming its addressee) is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — The arrival and the offer do not land
One panel owns the page: Guanyin calm on her cloud, level and close with the monkey's scowling face in the rock. If the meeting reads as scenery, or the page is not a dramatic dialogue page, it is blocking.

### C3 — Exchange misowned
"If you've come to stare at me..." and "Ask a rock? Lady..." must hang from the monkey's mouth by tail; "I'm Guanyin..." and both lines about the monk and the long road must hang from Guanyin's. A line tailed to the wrong mouth, or "Ask a rock?" readable before "I'm Guanyin.", is blocking.

### C4 — Identity
Guanyin a serene woman in white with a white hood-veil, willow sprig and vase, on a cloud, never on the ground; a shaven head or saffron on her is blocking. Wukong a monkey's face only, in the rock, never a human face; a gold band or cap on him, or his body free of the rock, is blocking.

### C5 — Peach continuity
In the first panel the peach has been eaten and its stone is in his teeth. A second whole peach drawn as focal, contradicting the eaten one, is blocking; a stone too small to identify, or missing, is not.
