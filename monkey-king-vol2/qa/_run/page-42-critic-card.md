## Page intent

## PAGE 42

Dramatic. The fight goes under. On the bank Wukong hops with his staff, unable to see into sand, while the monk reminds him he fought the dragon underwater; Wukong's answer is that this is soup. The dominant turn is under the surface, in murky gold: Bajie, better in water than anyone guessed, fighting the ogre rake against spade, the nine skulls swinging on their cord.
