## Page intent

## PAGE 21

A dramatic page at the night camp. Wukong, on his knees, pulls at the gold band and it will not move; then rage: he springs up with the staff raised over the monk, who stands his ground, terrified, palms together, and says the words again. The dominant turn is that rage and the staff over the monk, answered by three words. Then Wukong is down again, the staff dropped, crying that he has stopped; the monk, shaking, is sorry; and Wukong asks the question that turns the page: who taught you those words? The lady in white. The page belongs to the two of them in the moment the staff is over the monk; the exchange afterwards must be easy to follow line by line. The reader turns because the monkey knows whose band it is and is going to find her.

## Exact owner-controlled script

## PAGE 21 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the camp by the road at night.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Wukong on his knees, panting, pulling at the gold band with
both hands. It does not move.

> **WUKONG:** "It won't come off. It's grown into my head!"

**Panel 2 — dominant, 45%.** Wukong springing up with the staff raised over the
monk. Sanzang on the left, terrified, standing his ground, palms together.

> **WUKONG:** "You tricked me! You gave me a present, and it was a trap!"
>
> **SANZANG:** "Gold, hold fast."

**Panel 3 — 30%.** Wukong down on his knees again, the staff dropped. Sanzang
stopping, shaking.

> **WUKONG:** "Stop! I've stopped! I put it down!"
>
> **SANZANG:** "I'm sorry. I'm sorry!"
>
> **WUKONG:** "Who taught you those words?"
>
> **SANZANG:** "The lady in white. Guanyin."

**Why you turn the page:** he knows whose band it is, and he is going to find her.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 21 — card

### C1 — Transcription or ownership fails
Any of the seven scripted strings is missing, altered, doubled, reordered, or unreadable from the 600 × 900 proof, or extra text appears; or a balloon cannot be assigned by tail and placement. A string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant; a line directly under its speaker's previous balloon, or naming its addressee, is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — The rage does not land
A dramatic page whose dominant moment, found first by the eye, is Wukong with the staff raised over the monk, who stands his ground. Blocking if the moment reads as a strike landing or the monk fleeing.

### C3 — Attribution across the final exchange
"Stop! I've stopped!…" and "Who taught you those words?" must hang from Wukong's mouth; "I'm sorry. I'm sorry!" and "The lady in white. Guanyin." from the monk's, in that alternating order. In the dominant moment, "You tricked me!…" from Wukong reads before the monk's "Gold, hold fast." Each by its own tail.

### C4 — Identity and band state
Wukong wears the thin plain gold band in every panel. Blocking: no band, a cap on his head, or the band as a crown; the staff still in hand in the last panel.

### C5 — Focal generation integrity
Blocking: a duplicated named figure, gross anatomy on Wukong's two hands at the band or on the staff, or text over a face. Background detail is nonblocking.
