## Page intent

## PAGE 16

An illustrated-prose page on the border road, Wukong absent. The monk sits on a stone by the bay horse, his face wet, not knowing which way to go. Guanyin comes down low on her white cloud, names herself, and puts a plain cloth cap into his hands, a thin band of gold glinting inside it, and teaches him three words to say if the monkey is ever about to do a terrible thing. The dominant turn is the gift passing from her hands into his: a trap the monk now holds. The prose carries what the picture cannot, ending on her answer to what the words will do: make him stop. The reader turns because the monkey is coming back, and the monk has the cap.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 16 — card

### C1 — transcription
Every prose paragraph reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order, in the prose field, with no extra text; a paragraph that does not read is blocking, and the lettering is not to be measured. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue are nonblocking; the words and their order must match. Any balloon is blocking.

### C2 — the turn lands: the cap passes to the monk
One illustration owns the page: Guanyin bending down from her cloud to put a plain cloth cap into the seated monk's hands, a thin gold glint inside it, and the prose sits over empty sky. Blocking if the cap is not readable in his hands, or the prose covers either face or the hands. The declared mode is illustrated prose; a page of panels is blocking.

### C3 — separation of monk and Guanyin
The monk is a young man with a bare shaven head, saffron and red, seated on a stone on the ground, his face wet; Guanyin is a woman in white with a white hood-veil and a red dot between the brows, on a cloud, never on the ground. Blocking if either could be taken for the other or the same figure appears twice.

### C4 — absence and object state
Wukong is not on this page; a monkey anywhere is blocking. There is one cap, plain cloth, and the band is inside it, not on anyone's head; a crown or jeweled band is blocking.

### C5 — focal integrity
Both faces and the hands passing the cap are whole and correct; malformed focal hands are blocking.
