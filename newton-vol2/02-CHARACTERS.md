## Notes on Newton across the four age phases

Continuity markers from age 44 to age 84 (carried forward from Book One):

1. Long narrow face — only deepens with age.
2. Prominent straight nose.
3. Deep-set gray-brown watchful eyes — sharp at every age.
4. Compressed self-contained mouth.
5. Long precise hands.
6. **Hair / wig progression:**
   - Age 44 → natural dark-with-gray hair, NO wig
   - Age 55 → shoulder-length powdered wig in public, hair off in private
   - Age 65 → iconic full-bottom Augustan wig (Kneller portrait)
   - Age 84 → wig off, thin white hair under a nightcap

When generating multi-character pages, **always attach the correct age-phase Newton reference** to the `edit_image` call (or to the prose). Wrong-age refs produce silent drift.
