# The House of Atreus Vol 2 — Style Guide

## Style Block (Copy Verbatim to Every Prompt)

```
Art Style: Cinematic photorealistic illustration with warm natural lighting and Mediterranean atmosphere
Color palette: warm sandstone, olive green, terracotta brown, Aegean blue, golden amber
Effects: Golden hour sunlight, atmospheric haze, natural shadow depth, dusty stone textures
```

### Palette Override for Specific Page Ranges

**Pages 1–2 (Aulis — becalmed fleet):** Replace Effects line with:
```
Effects: Overcast gray sky, flat gray sea, oppressive still air, muted tones
```

**Page 16 (Furies appear):** Replace Effects line with:
```
Effects: Cold blue-black darkness, supernatural distortion, warmth completely absent
```

**Pages 17–18 (Athens trial):** Replace Effects line with:
```
Effects: Clean bright Athenian daylight, clear air, open sky, honest warmth
```

---

## Palette Notes

Same 5 colors as Volume 1, rebalanced:
- **Pages 1–2:** Aegean blue desaturated to gray. Sandstone muted. No amber.
- **Pages 3–4:** Golden amber floods back with Iphigenia. Then dies again at Aulis.
- **Pages 6–9:** Ironic golden warmth — beautiful day for murder. Then blood-red interior.
- **Page 16:** Blue-black dominance. Only cold tones. The Furies bring visual corruption.
- **Pages 17–18:** Clean, bright balance of all five colors. A transformed palette.

---

## Panel Rules

- 3–4 panels per page maximum. **Prefer 3 to keep under 800 words.**
- Black panel borders, consistent weight
- Clean white gutters
- Caption boxes: weathered parchment style, dark text
- Speech bubbles: classic white with black outline
- **Full-page panels on:** Page 5 Panel 2 (sacrifice moment) and Page 16 (Furies descend) — but keep text minimal on these

## Shot Type Reference

| Shot | Use For |
|------|---------|
| Wide/Establishing | Aulis fleet, Mycenae Lion Gate, Areopagus, landscapes |
| Medium | Confrontations — Clytemnestra vs Agamemnon, Orestes vs mother |
| Close-up | Emotional peaks — Iphigenia's face, Clytemnestra's grief, Orestes's tears |
| Low Angle | Clytemnestra on throne, Athena presiding, Apollo's appearance |
| High Angle | Iphigenia small against the fleet, Orestes fleeing Furies |

---

## Visual References

**Should look like:** Same grounded cinematic photorealism as Vol 1. The style is the constant across both volumes — what changes is the light and color.

**Should NOT look like:** Fantasy illustration, children's book, anime, stylized flat art. The Furies should feel WRONG and uncanny, not like fantasy monsters.

## Key Visual Echoes to Volume 1

- **Lion Gate** appears again (Pages 7, 10, 16) — same architecture, different mood
- **The road to Mycenae** (Page 12) — Orestes approaches from the same road Thyestes fled on
- **The feast/murder pattern** — Agamemnon's bath murder echoes the feasts of Vol 1
- **Purple carpet** (Page 7) echoes the sacrificial altar (Page 5) — royalty laid on the ground
