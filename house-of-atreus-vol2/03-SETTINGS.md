# The House of Atreus Vol 2 — Settings

## AULIS — The Greek Camp (Pages 1–5)

SETTING: AULIS
- Hundreds of beached black-hulled warships with curved prows lined along a gray beach
- Vast tent camp between the ships — thousands of idle soldiers, cooking fires
- Sacrificial altar on a rocky promontory above the sea
- Coastal scrub, beach grass, bare rock at water's edge
- Atmosphere: Overcast, windless, oppressive stillness — sea flat as glass, sails limp, everything stalled
- Palette: Muted — gray sky, gray sea, sandstone and brown. NO golden amber until Iphigenia arrives (Page 3)
- When Iphigenia arrives: warm golden light returns on the road, then dies at camp

---

## MYCENAE — Citadel Under New Rule (Pages 6–11, 14–16)

SETTING: MYCENAE (same as Volume 1 but darker)
- Lion Gate with carved lions, cyclopean walls, megaron great hall — all familiar
- But under Clytemnestra/Aegisthus it feels diminished, darker, more oppressive
- Royal bath chamber: large stone room with sunken bath, oil lamps, steam
- Agamemnon's tomb: simple stone grave outside palace walls
- Atmosphere: Heavy, claustrophobic, steeped in old blood
- Palette: Pages 6–7 ironic golden warmth (homecoming). Pages 8–10 shift to torchlight and blood-red. Pages 14–16 cold interior, then blue-black when Furies arrive.

---

## THE ROAD TO MYCENAE (Pages 3, 12)

SETTING: GREEK COUNTRYSIDE
- Rolling hills, olive groves, wildflowers, winding dusty roads
- Connects to Mycenae from the south
- The same road Thyestes fled on in Volume 1 — Orestes walks it in the opposite direction
- Atmosphere: Warm golden when Iphigenia travels it (Page 3). Soft dawn light when Orestes approaches (Page 12).
- Visual echo: deliberately mirror Vol 1 Page 12 (Thyestes leaving) with Vol 2 Page 12 (Orestes arriving)

---

## AREOPAGUS — Athens (Pages 17–18)

SETTING: AREOPAGUS
- Flat-topped rocky hill in Athens, open to the sky
- Stone benches arranged in a semicircle for jurors
- City of Athens visible below — white buildings, other hills
- A single ancient olive tree nearby
- Atmosphere: COMPLETELY different from Mycenae — open, bright, clean air. A new kind of space after claustrophobia.
- Palette: Clean bright daylight. All five colors in honest balance. Not the deceptive warmth of feasts — genuine, clear light.
