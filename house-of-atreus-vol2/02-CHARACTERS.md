# The House of Atreus Vol 2 — Character Definitions

## CRITICAL: How Character Locking Works

The reference image does 80% of the character locking. The text lock block is reinforcement only.

For every page prompt:
1. Upload the reference image(s) for all characters in the scene
2. Paste the SHORT lock block below
3. Include: "featuring the same characters shown in the reference images"

## CHARACTER: AGAMEMNON (King of Mycenae, Commander of the Greek Fleet)

**Reference sheet prompt:**
```
create image: Subject:
Character reference sheet showing Agamemnon, a Greek warrior-king in his mid-40s, in two views side by side: close-up portrait on the left, full body on the right.

Character Description:
Tall, commanding man in his mid-40s. Broad chest, thick arms — a warrior king. Broad face with heavy brow, strong jaw with short-trimmed dark beard. Dark brown hair streaked with gray. Gray-blue eyes, burdened and heavy-lidded. Scar on right cheekbone from battle. Weathered sun-darkened skin. Large rough hands.

Signature identifying marks:
- Scar on right cheekbone
- Gray-blue heavy-lidded eyes (same as his father Atreus)
- Short dark beard streaked with gray

Left panel — Portrait:
Close-up, expression: weary authority, direct gaze. Scar and beard clearly visible.

Right panel — Full body:
Standing in 3/4 view. Bronze breastplate over dark red chiton. Leather pteruges (skirt strips). Bronze greaves. Leather sandals. Bronze sword at left hip. Lion-head gold brooch pinning traveling cloak at right shoulder.

Art Style: Cinematic photorealistic illustration with warm natural lighting and Mediterranean atmosphere
Background: Simple warm neutral gradient
Lighting: Soft even lighting from front-left.

General Instruction:
Reference sheet for consistency. A man who carries power like a burden. Both views must match.

Aspect Ratio: 4:3 (landscape)
```

**Lean lock block:**
```
AGAMEMNON (mid-40s, Greek king): Tall, broad, commanding. Dark brown hair gray-streaked, short dark beard. Gray-blue heavy-lidded eyes, scar on right cheekbone. Bronze breastplate over dark red chiton, lion-head gold brooch.
```

**Outfit changes:**
- Pages 1–5 (Aulis): Bronze armor over dark red chiton, military
- Page 7 (homecoming): Fine purple-bordered chiton, gold crown, traveling cloak

**Which pages:** 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 9

## CHARACTER: THE FURIES / ERINYES (Spirits of Blood Vengeance)

**Reference sheet prompt:**
```
create image: Subject:
Character reference sheet showing the three Furies (Erinyes) from Greek mythology — three terrifying female spirits of vengeance. Show all three standing side by side in a single wide composition.

Character Description:
Three women of indeterminate age — could be ancient, could be young — gaunt, tall, moving in unison. Hollow-cheeked faces with solid black eyes (no whites visible). Mouths often open showing teeth. Long wild black hair moving as if in wind even when air is still. Tears of dark blood stream from their black eyes. Blue-black veins visible under pale gray skin. Tattered black robes dragging on the ground, appearing wet or oily. Serpents twine around their arms.

Signature identifying marks:
- Solid black eyes — no whites
- Blood tears streaming from eyes
- Hair moving as if alive, even in still air
- Pale gray skin with visible blue-black veins

Full composition:
Three figures standing side by side, slightly different heights but unified in posture. Each pointing forward with long pale fingers. Tattered black robes. Bronze-tipped whips in one hand each. Serpents on arms.

Art Style: Cinematic photorealistic illustration with warm natural lighting and Mediterranean atmosphere
Background: Dark, nearly black gradient
Lighting: Cold blue-gray light from above, harsh shadows.

General Instruction:
They should feel WRONG — uncanny valley, almost human but not. Slightly blurred at edges as if eyes cannot focus on them. Genuinely disturbing, not fantasy-monster cartoonish.

Aspect Ratio: 4:3 (landscape)
```

**Lean lock block:**
```
THE FURIES (three, ageless): Gaunt, tall women in unison. Solid black eyes (no whites), blood tears, wild black hair moving unnaturally. Pale gray skin, blue-black veins. Tattered wet black robes, bronze whips, serpents on arms.
```

**Which pages:** 16, 17, 18
