# 03 — SETTINGS

Recurring locations / visual environments, for consistency of light and palette across the images.
Spans six centuries — light and architecture shift from Republican Italy to late-antique Constantinople.

## Italian countryside / Roman road (cover, P1)
Sun-baked hills, cypress and olive, paved Roman roads, aqueducts on the horizon, warm golden
Mediterranean light, dust. The enduring built world of Rome — vast, peaceful, with the first long
shadows of late afternoon.

## The map ground (P2, P4)
Aged cream-to-tan parchment, sepia and iron-gall ink, faint hand-coloring, a compass rose, a subtle
border. Museum-exhibit map, not a modern infographic. P2 = Italy as one Roman whole; P4 = the
Mediterranean empire at its greatest extent.

## The river at dawn (P3)
A shallow Italian river crossing at first light, mist on the water, low golden sun behind armored men —
the Rubicon mood: quiet, fateful, the point of no return. NOT a battlefield.

## A ruined Roman street / field in civil war (P5)
Third-century crisis: a colonnaded street or open ground under a smoke-greyed sky, a toppled imperial
statue, scattered worthless coins. Iron-grey and ash, the warmth draining from the palette — Rome
turned against itself.

## Constantinople on the Bosphorus (P6, P9)
A new great city on the water: massive land walls, domes and basilicas, a busy harbor, the strait
catching gold dawn light. In P6 it is rising and hopeful (the heart moving east); in P9, a thousand
years on, it is the last Rome — older, mosaic-gold, enduring.

## The northern frontier / the Danube (P7)
A wide cold river under a heavy northern sky, far from the Mediterranean warmth — grey-green water,
mud, wagons, crowds crossing. The edge of the Roman world, where the outside is being let in.

## A late-Roman hall (P8)
Interior of a Ravenna-era palace or basilica: marble columns, late-antique mosaic, Latin inscriptions,
cool even light through high windows. Dignified, quiet, institutional — the room where a title lapses
without a battle.

## Palette discipline (all settings)
Parchment, sun-bleached stone, bronze, iron-grey, imperial purple, Mediterranean blue. The palette
deliberately COOLS and greys through Part Two (crisis) and warms back to mosaic-gold in the East (P6,
P9) — light tracks the argument. No neon, no candy colors, no modern tints.
