# Settings — A Wizard of Earthsea

All outdoor scenes carry weather (mist, rain, wind, spray). Palette per style guide: cold maritime flats, gold only for fire/werelight.

## GONT (P1–P4)
A single great mountain a mile high rising from a storm-grey sea, famous for goatherds and wizards.
- **Ten Alders** (P1–P2): high lonely village on the mountain's flank — alder trees, stone-and-timber huts, steep muddy lanes, brambly hillsides, springs, drifting mist. The fog-raid happens in these lanes.
- **Springs of Ar** (P3): a cold mountain stream among rocks and alders; naming ceremony in bare early spring.
- **Re Albi & Ogion's house** (P4, P14): the "Falcon's Nest," a cliff-edge hamlet on the Overfell high above Gont Port and the sea. Ogion's small wooden house: one room, stone hearth (gold light), sleeping alcove, drying herbs, a shelf of great leather lore-books. Rain outside, silence inside.

## ROKE (P5–P9)
The isle of the wise; heart of the Archipelago's magic.
- **Thwil town & harbor** (P5): small crooked-streeted port under grey light; the Great House above it.
- **The Great House** (P5–P7): vast stone school of the wizards — courts, halls and stairways lit by silver werelight and candle-gold; the carved door of horn and ivory.
- **Roke Knoll** (P8): a bare rounded green-black hill outside the walls where the foundations of the world are said to stand. Night scene: starlit, then torn open with cold spell-light.
- **The Isolate Tower** (P7): a lone grey tower on the northmost cape where the Master Namer teaches; cold cells, endless lists of names.

## LOW TORNING (P10)
A humble islet of the Ninety Isles, far west: narrow channels instead of streets, footbridges, fishing boats, net-hung cottages, Ged's bare wizard's hut. Damp, poor, kindly.

## PENDOR (P11)
A ruined pirate-lord island: broken black towers, burnt banks, rubble streets with treasure glinting under ash, a dead harbor. The dragon coiled over the ruined town. Grey sky, yellow dragon-fire eyes.

## OSSKIL & THE COURT OF THE TERRENON (P12–P13)
A grim northern land, raven-cold: bleak snow-swept moors (the Keksemt Moors), a foreign tongue, no trees.
- **The Terrenon keep**: a solitary pale tower-keep alone in the empty moor, enchanted walls, unnaturally cold; silver-bound doors descending to a cellar where the STONE OF TERRENON waits — a rough, unshaped, ancient stone set in the floor, older than the world, imprisoning an Old Power. Its light is wrong: pale, sourceless.

## IFFISH (P17)
East Reach; the harbor town of Ismay under clean snow: warm hearths, feasting, Vetch's comfortable house, Yarrow's kitchen. Deliberately the WARMEST page in the book — gold firelight everywhere.

## THE OPEN SEA & THE DRY LAND (P15–P16, P18–P20)
- **The hunt at sea** (P15–P16): open small-boat ocean, swells, spray, a reef and sand-bar with two ancient castaways in a driftwood hut.
- **Beyond Astowell** (P18–P19): past the last land, the sea under a dim low sky slowly becomes DARK DRY SAND-like ground — dunes of shadow-dark grit, no horizon, the boat *Lookfar* stranded as if on air behind, silence. The uncanniest setting in the book.
- **Lookfar** (P17–P20): Vetch's small open sailing boat, red-brown sail, weather-worn hull with painted eyes at the bow.
