Conventions: every prompt = STYLE BLOCK → SHADOW RULE (if shadow present) → ANTI-DRIFT → character locks (from refs, re-read first) → panel spec → LETTERING block + restrictions. No quotation marks inside bubbles. Refs listed per page. ⚠ = spot-check against the printed book if available; fallback wording noted.

## Quiz (WHY, 5 questions — draft; shuffle answers at reader build)
1. Why did Ogion teach Ged so little magic at first? (Because listening/silence/balance comes before power — Ged mistook restraint for ignorance.)
2. Why did summoning Elfarran loose the shadow? (Real summoning tears the world's balance; the rent let something through — the act done for pride, not need.)
3. Why did Ged refuse the Stone of Terrenon's offer of the shadow's name? (Old Powers don't serve, they use; the offer was the trap that had herded him there.)
4. Why did Ogion's advice — turn and hunt the hunter — change everything? (Hunted, Ged's fear fed it; hunting, he chose the ground; the shadow's power over him was his own running.)
5. Why could Ged defeat the shadow only by speaking his OWN name? (The shadow was the dark half of himself; you cannot kill your shadow, only make yourself whole by owning it.)
