# Style Guide — A Wizard of Earthsea

## STYLE BLOCK — paste verbatim into every prompt, first

> Bold ink-line comic-book art with FLAT muted color. Clean confident black linework, flat color fills, minimal soft rendering, dynamic composition. The palette is cold and maritime: sea-grey, slate blue, fog-white, cold green water, weathered brown wood and sail-cloth, dark wet stone. LOW SATURATION throughout — with ONE exception: warm gold, which appears ONLY as hearth-fire, candlelight, werelight, and spell-glow. Mist, rain, and weather are part of every outdoor scene.

## REGISTER / SHADOW RULE — paste verbatim on every page where the shadow appears

> THE SHADOW is an ABSOLUTE FLAT MATTE BLACK shape — no highlights, no texture, no outline glow, no gradient, no reflected light. It is the one element in the image that light never touches.

## ANTI-DRIFT — paste verbatim into every prompt

> NOT a children's book. Serious mythic tone, realistic heroic proportions, cinematic staging.

## Color logic (the volume's signature)

- **The world:** muted cool flats. Even daylight is silver, overcast, maritime.
- **Warm gold = life and magic.** Hearths, candles, werelight, true spell-glow. Iffish (P17) is the warmest page in the book on purpose.
- **The shadow = flat matte black, always.** Everything else in Earthsea has light in it somewhere; the shadow never does. As the book darkens (P12–P19), black area grows.
- **Skin tones:** the Archipelago is brown. Ged red-brown copper, Vetch black, Ogion dark copper-brown, Jasper light brown. White skin appears exactly twice: the Kargish raiders (P2) and Serret (P13). This inversion is deliberate and canonical.

## Layout rules (narrative mode)

- 3:2 landscape, 1536×1024, quality high.
- 3–4 panels per page, layout stated explicitly (e.g. "THREE panels: two equal panels across the TOP, one WIDE panel across the BOTTOM"), clean solid-black borders, clear white gutters. Single-scene splash pages allowed for hero beats (P8 bottom, P18, P19).
- Generous text: multiple bubbles + caption boxes per page is correct. Keep each individual element legible.
- Caption boxes: weathered parchment tint, dark serif text. Speech bubbles: off-white, dark serif, tails explicitly aimed. NO quotation marks inside bubbles.
- Chapter-place tags: small slate banner top-left corner of P1-panel-1 style pages ("GONT" / "ROKE" / "THE OPEN SEA") — these double as the voyage-strip anchors.

## Lettering block template

Every prompt ends with:

```
LETTERING — verbatim, render exactly:
[element list: panel, type, position, exact string]
RESTRICTIONS: All words spelled correctly. Do not duplicate text. Do not invent extra captions or signage. Do not put quotation marks inside speech bubbles. NO modern objects, NO watermarks.
```
