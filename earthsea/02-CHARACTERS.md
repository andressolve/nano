# Characters — A Wizard of Earthsea

Locks are visual only — never put "Ged" or "Le Guin" in a generation prompt as the lock; the description is the lock. Every 2+-page character gets a ref. Skin tones are canonical and non-negotiable (see brief).

## GED — phase 1: the boy (P1–P8, ages 12–15)
**Ref:** `refs/ref_ged_young.png`
> A wiry boy of about fourteen with RED-BROWN COPPER skin, straight black hair roughly and unevenly cut, dark fierce eyes, sharp hawk-like features, slight but strong build. NO facial scars. Wears a rough undyed wool tunic and a plain dark travel cloak. Proud, quick, hungry-to-prove-himself bearing. Realistic child anatomy. NOT cute, NOT mascot proportions, NOT oversized eyes.

Page 1–2 (Duny, age 12): same lock, goatherd staff, barefoot, mountain village clothing.
Roke pages (P5–P8): same lock, plain grey novice's cloak.

## GED — phase 2: the scarred wizard (P9–P20, ages 18–19)
**Ref:** `refs/ref_ged_scarred.png`
> A lean harsh-faced young man of nineteen with RED-BROWN COPPER skin, straight black hair, hawk-like features, weathered and grim. FOUR PARALLEL WHITISH SCARS rake down the LEFT side of his face, from beside the left eye across the cheek to the jaw, like the mark of a great claw — old, healed, pale against his dark skin. Carries a wizard's staff of dark yew wood as tall as himself. Heavy dark sea-stained cloak. Driven, haunted, resolute bearing.

## OGION THE SILENT (P3, P4, P14)
**Ref:** `refs/ref_ogion.png`
> A tall lean man past middle age, DARK COPPER-BROWN skin, grey hair tied back, lean and tough as an old hound, deep-set calm dark eyes, weathered still face. Plain brown homespun cloak and tunic, tall oaken walking staff. Utterly unhurried; he stands in rain without minding it.

## VETCH / Estarriol (P5, P8, P9, P17–P20)
**Ref:** `refs/ref_vetch.png`
> A heavyset plain-faced young man a few years older than Ged, BLACK skin, short tightly-curled black hair, warm open honest expression, sturdy thick-shouldered build. Plain dark sorcerer's cloak; in later pages (P17 on) carries a mage's staff and wears the easy confidence of a settled island wizard.

## JASPER (P5, P8)
**Ref:** `refs/ref_jasper.png`
> A tall graceful young man of about nineteen, LIGHT-BROWN skin, fine dark hair neatly kept, elegant handsome features, slender build. Fine grey cloak clasped at the throat with a SILVER clasp. An expression of courteous, superior mockery — the smile that never reaches the eyes.

## SERRET (P13; young version P4 in prose)
**Ref:** `refs/ref_serret.png`
> A beautiful woman in her twenties with PALE WHITE skin — the rare Osskilian look — long straight BLACK hair falling loose, grey eyes, slender and still. Robed in white and silver, a silver circlet. Watchful, persuasive, sorrowful beneath the surface.
P4 (girl at Re Albi, ~14): prose only — a sly dark-haired girl in fine clothes, pale, teasing.

## NEMMERLE, Archmage (P5, P8)
**Ref:** `refs/ref_nemmerle.png`
> An immensely old man, his hair and beard and robe all worn WHITE as driftwood, thin as a reed, leaning on a white staff. On his shoulder a great BLACK RAVEN with a dagger-like beak and eyes like pebbles.

## YEVAUD, Dragon of Pendor (P11)
**Ref:** `refs/ref_yevaud.png`
> An enormous ancient dragon, lean as a hound and huge as a hill: scales of GREY-BLACK iron, vast ribbed bat-like wings, a long narrow toothed head, eyes of slow yellow fire — the only warm light on his page. Coiled over broken black towers.

## HOEG the otak (P6–P14)
**Ref:** `refs/ref_otak.png`
> A small sleek wild animal the size of a large rat: broad whiskered face, dark-brown brindled fur, large bright dark eyes, sharp teeth. Rides tucked in Ged's hood or on his shoulder. A wild animal, NOT a cartoon pet.

## Prose-only (single page)
- **Ged's aunt** (P1): a bent village witch, dark copper skin, grey-streaked hair, bone-and-herb charms.
- **Kargish raiders** (P2): WHITE-SKINNED, YELLOW-HAIRED warriors in bronze and leather with plumed helms, swords and torches — the only white crowd in the book.
- **Master Hand** (P6): a gentle grey-haired mage juggling illusion-lights.
- **Kurremkarmerruk** (P7): a dry thin ancient master at a high desk of name-lists.
- **Gensher** (P9): a dark stern middle-aged Archmage.
- **Pechvarry** (P10): a weathered boatwright, dark brown skin, kind worried face.
- **Skiorh / the gebbeth** (P12): a gaunt oarsman in a peaked hood — then the hood turns and THERE IS NO FACE, only flat black.
- **Yarrow** (P17): Vetch's young sister, black skin, quick bright manner, kitchen fire-light.

## Ref-sheet generation prompts

All refs: 3:2 landscape 1536×1024, character sheet with LEFT: portrait bust, RIGHT: full-body standing, neutral warm-grey background, no text, no labels, STYLE BLOCK + ANTI-DRIFT verbatim. Group sheets (Foundation-validated, one gen, labeled panels) allowed for: [Vetch + Jasper + Nemmerle] and [Serret + otak]. Solo sheets mandatory for Ged ×2 and Ogion and Yevaud.
