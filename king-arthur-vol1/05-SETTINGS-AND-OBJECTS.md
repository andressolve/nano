# Settings and Objects

*World palette: a painted golden-age adventure Britain — earth, moss, oak,
iron, snow-light and torchlight, deep blue reserved for Arthur. Each location
is a variation on it, except the lake, which must look like a different
world. The Chronicle pages are not a location: they are vellum, ink, and wash
(see the mandate).*

Each location names one restrained accent that does dramatic work.

---

## Locations

### 1 · THE CLIFF PATH — p 1
Night storm on a sea cliff, a castle's black shape above, rain in sheets, one
small lantern. **Accent:** the lantern on the bundle.

### 2 · UTHER'S CHAMBER — p 2
A dark stone hall, a great bed, crowded lords in shadow, one brazier.
**Accent:** the red dragon on the coverlet.

### 3 · SIR ECTOR'S FARM — pp 3–5
A small timber-and-stone manor in a muddy yard, a stable, a forge-fire, hens,
autumn going to winter. Warm, cramped, loved. **Accent:** the forge glow on
Kay's new armour.

### 4 · LONDON AND THE CHURCHYARD — pp 6, 8–18, 20–26
Old Roman walls with medieval roofs built against them, snow, crowds,
banners. The churchyard: a squat stone church, snow on the graves, and in the
middle the great grey block with the iron anvil and the sword. The great hall
for the crowning and the feast. **Accent:** the gold letters on the stone.

### 5 · BEDEGRAINE — pp 28–34
A forest valley at dawn, frost, mist on a stream, two armies; afterwards the
same valley in grey light. **Accent:** the blaze of the sword from the stone
on p 29 — the only time it shines.

### 6 · CAMELOT — pp 35, 37, 49, 51–58
A castle on a green hill above a river; inside, a high timber-roofed hall
with a great hearth. First a long table, then the round one. **Accent:** the
gold names on the Round Table.

### 7 · THE FOREST AND THE FOUNTAIN — pp 38–43, 48
Old oaks, moonlight, a stone fountain spilling into a pool, Pellinore's
battered green-and-white pavilion, a shield hanging on a tree. **Accent:** the
moon in the fountain's pool.

### 8 · THE LAKE — pp 44–47
A wide lake in white mist, still as glass, reeds, a small boat, far hills
gone pale. Cold silver and grey; no warm colour anywhere except Arthur.
**Accent:** the arm in white silk.
