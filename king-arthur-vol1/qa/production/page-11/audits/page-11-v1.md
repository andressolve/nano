## Intent read

The page reads as the solitary event: an empty snowbound churchyard, Arthur alone and surprised, pulling the sword upward with both hands as snow breaks from the anvil. The church and falling snow establish a quiet, weather-lit spectacle. The caption sits in the upper-left sky, and the image is owned by Arthur and the blade.

## Exact text check

Transcription on the proof: “It came out as easily as a stick out of snow.” It is the only readable page text. The gold carving reads as ornament, not as words.
