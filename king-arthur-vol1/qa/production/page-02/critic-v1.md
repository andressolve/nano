## Blind read
Two winters later, King Uther lies dying before his assembled lords. The large opening panel owns the page: Merlin stands between the anxious court and Uther's deathbed and presses Uther to name who will be king. Uther's fear for his hidden son causes a close, intimate exchange; Merlin's assurance that Arthur is safe turns Uther toward publicly declaring that he has a son and giving him the kingship and his blessing. The closing caption changes the scene into its consequence: Uther dies that night, while none of the lords has ever heard of a son.
