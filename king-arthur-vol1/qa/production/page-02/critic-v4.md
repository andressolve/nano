## Blind read
Two winters later, King Uther lies dying while anxious lords crowd his chamber. A standing white-haired adviser presses him to name the next king. Uther privately asks whether his son Arthur is safe; the adviser says the hidden boy would be killed if the lords found him. Uther owns the page and causes its turn when he then tells the gathered lords that he has a son who will succeed him and gives the son his blessing. Uther dies that night, leaving the lords confronted with a son they had never known existed.
