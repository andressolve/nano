VERDICT: REVISE

## Blind read
An illuminated chronicle page establishes Uther Pendragon as Britain’s ruler, then makes his lack of an heir the threat that could split the realm and revive its wars. Uther owns the page: the prose begins and ends with his kingship and hope, while the genealogy beneath the map turns the fear of succession toward Arthur. The turn is from sorrow and political danger to collective prayer and the promise of a son.

## Visible text

At that time
Uther Pendragon
ruled all Britain,
a king of great
renown, strong
in war and just in peace.
Yet the years brought sorrow
unto him, for he had no heir.

And so the lords of the land
spoke often of the need for
a prince, that the realm might
endure in unity. For without
an heir, they feared, Britain
would again be divided,
and the old wars return.

But Uther would not lose
hope, and the prayers of
many faithful hearts rose
to Heaven, that a son might
be granted unto him in due
season, and that the light
of Britain should not fail.
