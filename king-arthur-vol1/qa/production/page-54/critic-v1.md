VERDICT: REVISE

## Blind read
A single full-page council scene is owned by the standing armored leader at the far side of the round table. The gathered knights' attention establishes a formal decision or command as the page's turn.

## Visible text
NONE. No name is lettered anywhere on the page.

## Findings

### Finding C1
- Observation: The required caption is absent; no visible text appears on the 600 x 900 proof.
- Material reader harm: The reader never receives the appearance of the names or Pellinore's status as the first knight to sit.
- Redraw justification: The page omits its only scripted text and therefore cannot deliver the required story information.

### Finding C2
- Observation: The scene is viewed near table height, each occupied place lacks a visible gold glow, and no single dark gap can be found; the standing leader rather than the ring owns the composition.
- Material reader harm: The intended spectacle of a completed table full of glowing names except for one place does not read.
- Redraw justification: The page's dominant reader event and page-turn cause are replaced by a materially different council scene.

### Finding C3
- Observation: No knight is visibly distinguished in deep blue; the dominant figure stands over the seated ring.
- Material reader harm: Arthur cannot be read as the lone blue figure seated as one among equals.
- Redraw justification: The page loses the consequential visual statement of Arthur's equal place in the fellowship.

### Finding C4
- Observation: The biggest man is standing with both hands on the table rather than sitting with a hand on his heart, and no short, bald, beaming knight is visibly identifiable among the group.
- Material reader harm: Pellinore's first acceptance of the fellowship and Ector's delighted presence are absent.
- Redraw justification: Both required focal identities and their story-defining actions fail to read.

### Finding C5
- Observation: The vacant chair behind the standing leader has a gold marker before it rather than being the one empty place with no gold.
- Material reader harm: The exception that gives the completed table its unresolved meaning is reversed.
- Redraw justification: The page materially misstates the consequential object state that causes the turn.
