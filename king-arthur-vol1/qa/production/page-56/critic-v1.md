## Findings

### Finding C2
- Observation: Arthur holds Excalibur by its blade with the hilt forming a cross above his hands, but the standing knights visibly clasp their sword grips, with pommels above their hands and blades descending; their hilts do not form crosses and they do not share Arthur's oath gesture.
- Material reader harm: The defining visual action of the fellowship's oath is absent from the answering knights, so the page reads as Arthur making the cross-hilt vow while the others merely hold swords rather than all joining the same solemn rite.
- Redraw justification: The failed shared gesture affects the dominant group relationship and the page's central reader event, making it substantial enough to risk replacing the complete page.
