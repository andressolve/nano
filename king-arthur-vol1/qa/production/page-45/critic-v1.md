VERDICT: REVISE

## Blind read
An old bearded man warns a blond boy to be polite as a black-haired woman approaches across the lake. The woman owns the page: her full-height walk over the water is the image the eye finds first and holds longest. She identifies the boy and the sword, then offers the sword in return for a future gift of her choosing. The boy immediately promises; the old man grips his arm too late and protests; the woman tells him to take the boat and retrieve the sword. The turn is the boy binding himself to an unknown future demand.

## Findings

### Finding C4
- Observation: Far behind the central Lady, the sword is held by a visibly complete second white-clad, dark-haired woman rising from the lake, rather than by an arm alone.
- Material reader harm: The second woman duplicates the Lady’s defining black-hair-and-white-gown identity in the same focal tableau, making the singular named Lady and the source of the sword visually ambiguous.
- Redraw justification: The card explicitly makes a second woman an identity blocker; this is a complete extra human figure attached to the consequential sword, not a minor background mark or cosmetic defect, so the central supernatural event cannot be released as drawn.
