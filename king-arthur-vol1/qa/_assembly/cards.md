# Critic cards

## Page 1 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
The two title-card lines and the caption must each read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order, with no other text; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. The title lines are printed title text over the sky, not a balloon. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant; the words and their order must match.

### C2 — The flight does not land
The page must read as one spectacle image: a castle on a storm cliff and one small hooded figure carrying a bundle down the path, the warm lantern light on a tiny baby's hand. If the reader cannot find the bundle or the hand, or the figure reads as walking toward the castle rather than away, the turn fails.

### C3 — The man is not Merlin
A very tall, thin, stooped figure in a dark grey-green hooded robe with a crooked staff taller than himself, face hidden. A pointed hat, stars, a glowing spell, or a short heavy figure is blocking.

### C4 — Extra presence
No other person on the page and no balloon. A second figure, or the baby drawn with a face as a toddler, is blocking; rain, waves and rock texture are nonblocking.
