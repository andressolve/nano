- **Face shape:** Long, heavy, horse-like, with a strongly projecting lantern jaw; it does not drift toward Arthur's soft oval or Merlin's narrow gaunt wedge.
- **Brow and eyes:** The brow is heavy but controlled, and the eyes read small and grey beneath it. They do not acquire Merlin's wild-browed, deep-set intensity.
- **Nose:** Broad, irregular, and visibly broken more than once, including in the strict profile.
- **Mouth and expression:** Closed, grave, and patient rather than sneering or cruel; the face does not imply villainy.
- **Hair mass and line:** Iron-grey and cropped close to the skull in every unhelmeted view.
- **Facial hair:** Short, thick, iron-grey, and square-cut; never long, forked, braided, or red.
- **Build:** The front, profile, and battle silhouettes all read as the tallest and widest man in the current approved system, built like a bear.
- **Posture and gesture:** Views (b) and (c) preserve the very still stance with both hands on the pommel of the huge point-down sword; view (d) converts that stored mass cleanly into action.
- **Costume value pattern:** Old dented black-painted plate-and-mail beneath a faded green surcoat remains legible in colour and grayscale.
- **Accent and primary marker:** Faded green and the white boar are consistent; the shield and raised huge sword in view (d) are listed elements, not continuity-inventing props.
- **Four-view compliance:** The sheet contains the required large three-quarter portrait, full-length front, true ninety-degree profile, and flat-topped-great-helm battle view. The helmet, narrow eye-slit, battered green-and-white shield, and raised sword are correct.
- **Never list:** No long or forked beard, no round bald head, no red hair, and no villainous expression or bearing.
- **Sheet hygiene:** No lettering, labels, watermark, internal borders, or scenery is present.
