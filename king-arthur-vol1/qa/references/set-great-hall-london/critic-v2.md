## Mandatory findings

1. The four tall green wall hangings carry large repeated gold tree devices, reinforced by the dominant tree image on the far-wall tapestry. At thumbnail scale this reads as a coherent heraldic system, directly violating the plate's `no heraldry` requirement and teaching a recurring emblem to later pages. Remove every large isolated tree/tree-of-life device from the side hangings and far-wall tapestry. Replace those fields with low-contrast, unfigured woven cloth in dull green, oak brown, and muted ochre; any retained beam decoration must remain a continuous non-emblematic vine border rather than an isolated badge.

2. A conspicuous fire opening directly beneath the high table imports the great-hearth primary marker assigned to Camelot and risks collapsing the two royal halls after long re-entry. Remove that fireplace opening and continue the far wall and dais in plain stone and oak. Keep London identified by the long stone nave, painted beams, paired side tables, raised high table, open near-end oak doors, rush floor, and iron-bracket torchlight; do not introduce another central architectural fire feature.

## Nonblocking observations

- The plate is the required 1536 × 1024 landscape, unframed, and presents one coherent view.
- It contains no figures, words, pseudo-writing, signature, monogram, watermark, or corner mark.
- The great oak doors stand open at the near end; long trestle tables and benches flank the aisle; the raised high table and tall central carved chair read clearly at the far end; painted beams and floor rushes are present.
- The earth, oak, iron, stone, and torchlight palette fits the world's golden-age register. The small blue night windows read as ambient night rather than a competing deep-blue costume field.
- The many vessels, food settings, candles, and door rings are nonconsequential dressing and do not place a listed object in a wrong state, though later page prompts should not treat their exact arrangement as continuity-critical.
- In grayscale and at thumbnail size, the open-door foreground, bright central aisle, paired table masses, and distant high table remain readable. The candidate contains no human silhouette or face that could collide with an approved character.
