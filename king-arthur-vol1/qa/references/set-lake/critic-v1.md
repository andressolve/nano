## Mandatory findings

1. The foreground reeds, wooden boat, shore, and several rocks carry conspicuous brown, ochre, and olive warmth. This violates the location lock of cold silver, grey, and white only, and would teach later pages to warm the lake independently of Arthur. Repaint every warm foreground mass into a tightly cold range: reeds in desaturated charcoal-grey, boat in weathered silver-grey, and shore and rocks in blue-grey and neutral grey. Preserve the boat's plain construction and the existing reed/boat silhouettes, but remove all readable brown, ochre, yellow, rust, and olive colour groups, including at thumbnail scale.

2. The distinct pale sun-or-moon disk above the left hills creates an unplanned dramatic accent. The lake's specified accent is the arm in white silk, which is deliberately absent from this empty setting plate. Remove the circular disk as a readable object and replace it with diffuse white cloud light of nearly uniform value, leaving the future white-silk arm as the only localized dramatic accent in lake scenes.
