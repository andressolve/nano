## Mandatory findings

1. The shield is in the wrong consequential state. Replace the round, bare-metal bossed shield with the same large kite-shaped shield locked on `06-pellinore`: battered faded green field, white boar device, and no alternate heraldry or shield geometry. Keep that single shield hanging from the oak.
2. The required accent is subordinated to an unplanned campfire and orange-lit pavilion. Remove the campfire, its orange cast light, and the warm interior lamp; light the clearing in cool silver-blue moonlight so the moon's reflection in the fountain pool is the brightest and only dramatic accent. Keep the pavilion readable by moonlight rather than by a second warm focal source.
3. The fountain introduces prominent anthropomorphic masks not specified by the location lock, creating face-like forms beside character inputs and a recurring design the plan does not require. Rebuild the fountain as plain, battered medieval stonework with a nonfigurative central spout; retain the basin and falling water, but remove all carved human, bearded, or beast faces.
