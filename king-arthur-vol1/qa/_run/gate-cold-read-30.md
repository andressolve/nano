# Script-blind cold read — canonical pages 1–30

Fresh read-only reviewer with no inherited history. Receive only the 600 × 900
proofs in `pages/proofs/` for pages 1–30, in order. Never open scripts,
prompts, audits, critic cards, reports, handoffs, references, or candidate
folders. You have not read this book and you do not know the source.

Read once at normal pace. Then write: a retell of the whole story so far; the
cast, described physically and by role; the causal chain; any page that
required backtracking, rereading, or a guess; any text or attribution that
failed at reading pace; and what you expect next. If you misidentified a
character, say so: the reader would have made the same mistake, and that is
blocking even if the script would have confirmed the intent.

Write `qa/cold-reads/cold-read-30.md`. Return `APPROVED` when the story
lands without documentation. `REVISE` findings must name pages and material
reader harm. Omit cosmetic and art-direction observations. Never edit or
promote.
