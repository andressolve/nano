## Page intent

## PAGE 22

Dramatic. Two moments in the great church. First, the crowning: in a tall stone church full of candles and crowds, Arthur kneels before the altar in a new deep-blue surcoat, a thin gold crown just set on his head by churchmen in white, his hand on the sword laid on the altar, and he swears to be a true king and protect all of them. A few lords at the front stand with arms folded, unsmiling. Then, close, the crown a little too big and tipped, sweat on his lip, Arthur turns on Merlin at his side: you know who my father was, tell me. Merlin promises: not here, tonight. The dominant turn: Arthur is king, and tonight he'll learn whose son he is. The reader turns waiting for that night.
