## Page intent

## PAGE 16

Dramatic page. Ector gets up off his knees and puts his big hand on the back of Arthur's neck, the way a father does, and tells him he will always have them. Arthur asks if he can still call him Father; Ector answers, "When nobody's listening, lad. Always." Then Ector glances across at Kay, standing apart and red in the face, and asks the new king one thing: make Kay his steward, because no man alive will fight harder for him. Arthur, watching Kay, agrees, but says first he needs to talk to him. The dominant image is the father's hand and the boy's question. The turn is that Arthur still has a father, and now has to face the brother who tried to take the crown.
