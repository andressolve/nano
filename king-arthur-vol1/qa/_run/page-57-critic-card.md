## Page intent

## PAGE 57

Dramatic. Kay points across the table at the one chair with no name in front of it and offers to sit in it himself, so it will not look as if someone refused Arthur. The page belongs to the empty chair, tall and plain in a pool of light from a high window, with Merlin standing behind it, his hand on its back, telling Kay there will be no name on it yet, because it is kept for someone who has not come. Arthur asks who. Merlin tells the whole table they will know him when he comes, and that anyone else who sits in it will be very sorry. Kay, half-risen, sits back down fast with both hands up. The dominant turn is the table waiting for someone nobody knows. Kay's retreat is comedy, kept small.

### C1 — Transcription or ownership fails
All five strings must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once each, in causal order, owned by the right mouth by tail and placement, with no extra text. A string that does not read is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant; a line naming its addressee is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — The empty chair does not own the page
The page must read as a dramatic page whose dominant image is the empty chair, plain, with no gold before it, Merlin's hand on its back. If the eye finds Kay's comedy before the chair, the turn fails.

### C3 — Attribution
Kay's offer hangs from Kay; Merlin's two lines from Merlin; the one-word question from Arthur, not Merlin or Kay; Kay's last line from Kay. A misplaced "Who?" is blocking.

### C4 — Identity
Merlin thin, stooped, forked white beard, wild white hair; Kay sharp-faced, black-haired, thin moustache, black and yellow; Arthur slight, blond, in deep blue. Arthur and Kay confused is blocking.

### C5 — Consequential object state
Exactly one empty chair with no gold before it, and nobody sits in it. Magic light from Merlin is blocking; the window light is scripted.

### C6 — Kay's steward state
Kay appears in his steward's black: a plain black tunic with a yellow border, no mail and no black-and-yellow surcoat. Kay in his old mail and quartered surcoat is blocking: it sends him back to the tournament and battle pages just when his place at Camelot should read. The exact width of the yellow border or whether the keys show is nonblocking.
