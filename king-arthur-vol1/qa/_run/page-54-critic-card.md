## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 54 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
The one caption must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, with no other text; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. The gold at each place is glow, not text: any legible name or invented word on the table is extra text and blocking. Nonblocking: curly against straight quotes, a comma present or missing, a regional spelling variant.

### C2 — The spectacle does not land
The page must read as a spectacle from high above: the great round table ringed with knights, a gold glow at each seated place, and one gap. If the eye cannot find the one empty chair with no gold, the turn fails.

### C3 — Arthur is not one among equals
Arthur, the only figure in deep blue, sits at a place on the ring no higher than anyone. A throne, a raised seat, or Arthur at a head of the table is blocking.

### C4 — Identity
Pellinore, the biggest man at the table, sits with a hand on his heart; Ector, short, bald and beaming, is among the knights. No background knight wears blue, a forked white beard, or red braids. Exact faces at this distance are nonblocking when bulk, colour and posture read.

### C5 — Consequential object state
Exactly one chair is empty with no gold before it; a second dark place or an empty chair with gold is blocking. The number of chairs and knights is nonblocking.
