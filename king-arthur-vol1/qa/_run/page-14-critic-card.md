## Page intent

## PAGE 14

Spectacle page with a spoken turn. Arthur stands on the stone with the sword drawn a second time, holding it up, astonished at himself. Below him in the snow, Ector and Kay are down on their knees, heads bowed. Arthur begs them to get up; Ector refuses, calls him "my lord", and tells him he is not his father. Then a close view of Arthur's face, the sword forgotten in his hand, and one word: "What?" The kneeling image owns the page. The turn is that the man Arthur has called Father all his life has just told him he isn't. The stone and the snow are setting; the kneeling men and the boy's face are the event.

### C1 — Transcription, order, and ownership
Every one of the 3 listed strings must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once each, in causal order, Arthur's first line from Arthur above, Ector's from Ector's raised face below, and Arthur's one word from his close-up, with no extra text anywhere; a string that does not read, is missing, doubled, reordered, or misowned is blocking. Kay is silent; a balloon tailing to Kay is misownership. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue; the words and their order are what must match. A line placed directly under its speaker's previous balloon, or naming its addressee, is owned even if its tail wanders. A string that reads is not to be measured.

### C2 — The kneeling image
One image owns the page: Arthur on the stone with the sword raised, astonished, and Ector and Kay kneeling in the snow below. If the men are standing or the boy looks triumphant, blocking. The close-up on Arthur's stunned face follows.

### C3 — Ector's line is Ector's
The refusal and "I'm not your father" must hang from Ector, whose mouth is visible, not from Kay or Arthur. Blocking if a reader could assign it to Kay.

### C4 — Identities
Arthur fair-haired in brown wool, no blue; Ector short, bald, clean-shaven, russet; Kay black-haired in black-and-yellow. Confusion is blocking.

### C5 — Object state and integrity
The anvil is empty and the plain iron-grey sword is in Arthur's hand, no glow. Faces and focal hands whole; snow and church detail nonblocking.
