## Page intent

## PAGE 32

Dramatic. Arthur stands on foot in the front rank among smiths and farmers, their spears braced, as Lot charges down at him, mocking him as a farm boy. Arthur shouts to his people to hold. The charge hits and the line does not break, and in the middle of it, among his people, Arthur raises the sword from the stone and it blazes with white light. Lot's horse rears, his riders shield their eyes, and his army turns and runs while Lot shouts back that this isn't over. Arthur owns the page, and the blazing sword raised among ordinary people is its heart. The dominant turn: Lot's army has broken, and Arthur has won. The reader turns to see what winning looks like. The rout stays small and distant, and no one falls on the page.

## Exact owner-controlled script

## PAGE 32 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the battlefield.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** Arthur on foot in the front rank of his own army,
shoulder to shoulder with smiths and farmers, spears and pitchforks braced.
Charging down at them, Lot on a huge horse with his riders behind him, braids
flying, sword up. Arthur on the left, Lot on the right.

> **LOT:** "There you are, farm boy! Now we'll see whose crown it is!"
>
> **ARTHUR:** "Hold together! Hold!"

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** The charge hits the spear line and the line
does not break. In the middle of it, among his people, Arthur raises the
sword from the stone, and the blade blazes with white light, as bright as
thirty torches, lighting up the whole field. Lot's horse rears; Lot's riders
throw their arms up over their eyes.

> **CAPTION** *(top of the panel)*
> "The line held. And when Arthur raised the sword among his people, it blazed like thirty torches."

**Panel 3 — 20%.** Lot's army turning and running up the far slope; Lot
looking back over his shoulder as he rides away.

> **LOT:** "This isn't over, boy! Do you hear me? This isn't over!"

**Why you turn the page:** Lot's army has broken, and Arthur has won.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 32 — card

### C1 — Transcription
All four strings read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order, with no extra text: Lot's taunt, Arthur's command, the caption, Lot's parting shout. A string that does not read is blocking; one that reads is not to be measured. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a comma present or missing inside a line, and a regional spelling variant are nonblocking. Lot's taunt names the farm boy and is owned by that even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — The blaze among his people owns the page
The eye must find the line holding and Arthur, among farmers and smiths, raising the sword as it blazes with white light while Lot's horse rears and riders shield their eyes. If Arthur stands apart from his people, or the light comes from somewhere other than the sword, the turn is wrong. Dramatic mode.

### C3 — Attribution
Both Lot lines hang from Lot's mouth, the second as he flees; "Hold together! Hold!" hangs from Arthur's.

### C4 — The rout
Lot's army must read as turning and running, with Lot looking back. If they seem to still be attacking, the page loses its outcome.

### C5 — Identity and integrity
Lot: copper-red braids, gold torc, bearskin. Arthur: slight blond boy in blue. The sword is plain iron, not gold or jewelled. No blood or bodies.
