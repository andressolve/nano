## Page intent

## PAGE 23

Dramatic. The crowning feast in a long torchlit hall, tables full. At the high table sit Arthur in blue with his thin crown, Kay and Ector beside him, Merlin behind. The doors at the far end are flung open and King Lot strides in with his lords, an open chest at his feet spilling gold cups and silver onto the floor. Arthur, eager, welcomes him and asks if his gifts arrived. Lot owns the rest of the page: he kicks the chest so the cups roll, says they don't take presents from a boy nobody has heard of, and then, grinning, hand on his sword hilt, offers a gift of his own: a sharp sword, coming for Arthur's neck. The dominant turn: the most powerful lord in Britain has just threatened the king at his own feast.
