## Page intent

## PAGE 24

Dramatic. The hall has gone silent. Merlin has stepped forward from behind the high table, staff raised, and he owns the page: he tells Lot to be quiet and listen, and announces to everyone that this boy is the son of King Uther, and that he was there when Uther said he had a son. Lot stands in the middle of the floor, facing him. Behind Merlin, Arthur is half-risen from his chair, hearing it at the same moment as everyone else. Then Lot, at the doors, laughs it off, mocks the wizard's sword tricks, tells the boy to come down from his chair, and leaves with his lords, promising the battlefield. The dominant turn: the whole hall has just heard whose son Arthur is, before Arthur heard it himself. The reader turns to see what Arthur does with that.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 24 — card

### C1 — Transcription or ownership fails
All four lines (two Merlin, two Lot) must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once each, in causal order, each owned by the right mouth by tail and placement, with no extra text. A string that does not read is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured. A line owned by placement (directly under its speaker's previous balloon, or naming its addressee) is owned even if its tail wanders. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word; the words and their order are what must match.

### C2 — Merlin's announcement does not own the page
Merlin, staff raised before a silent hall, must own the page, with Lot facing him and Arthur half-risen behind him at the high table. If Arthur is not visibly there to hear it, the turn (he heard it with everyone else) is lost.

### C3 — Lot's exit does not read
The second image must show Lot at the doors, laughing, leaving with his lords; his two lines hang from Lot. Arthur has no balloon on this page.

### C4 — Identity and magic
Merlin (tall, thin, forked white beard, wild white hair, staff) and Lot (red braids, bearskin, torc) must read as themselves. A glow from Merlin's staff or hands is blocking. Background lords are nonblocking.
