## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 21 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
The caption and the shout must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once each, with no extra text; the shout belongs to the crowd and has no tail to any one mouth, the caption has no tail. The two may read in either order. A string that does not read is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing, a regional spelling variant, the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue; the words and their order are what must match.

### C2 — The people do not own the page
The ordinary people of London, pouring in with fists up and mouths open, must own the page; the lords are small and shocked. If the crowd reads as lords or soldiers, or the page reads as a battle, the turn (the people said yes) is lost.

### C3 — Arthur and the flare do not read
Arthur must be findable on the stone above everyone, the blond boy, with the sword in his hand flaring white and his face startled at it. A missing flare, or a glow on anything besides the sword, is blocking. Crowd counts, repeated background faces, and crowd anatomy are nonblocking.
