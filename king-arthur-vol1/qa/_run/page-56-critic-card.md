## Page intent

## PAGE 56

Dramatic. All the knights stand around the round table holding their swords upright by the blades so the hilts make crosses. Arthur alone kneels at his place, Excalibur held up the same way, and swears first because he needs it most: never to be cruel, to give mercy to anyone who asks, to protect anyone who cannot protect himself, never to fight for a wrong cause. Behind him the doors Lot walked out of stand open on the dark. Ector, Kay, Pellinore and the others listen, and Ector's eyes are wet; then every knight answers together. The dominant turn is that the fellowship is sworn. The page belongs to the kneeling boy king and the faces answering him; the open doors stay quiet background, and nobody draws a sword against anyone.

### C1 — Transcription or ownership fails
Arthur's three lines and the knights' shout must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once each, in order, with no extra text; Arthur's lines hang from Arthur, the shout belongs to the knights together. A string that does not read is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant; a line placed directly under Arthur's previous balloon is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — The oath does not land
The page must read as a dramatic oath: knights standing with swords held upright as crosses, Arthur alone kneeling with Excalibur held up the same way, then the faces answering. If Arthur stands, or another knight kneels with him, the turn fails.

### C3 — Identity
Arthur a slight blond boy in deep blue; Ector short, bald, round-faced, clean-shaven, eyes wet; Kay sharp-faced and black-haired in black and yellow; Pellinore huge with a square grey beard. Ector and Pellinore confused is blocking.

### C4 — Consequential object state
Excalibur is bright with a gold-wired hilt and green stone and held by its blade; a sword pointed at anyone, or blood, is blocking. The open doors behind are scripted. The number of knights is nonblocking.

### C5 — Focal generation failure
Arthur's two hands on Excalibur are focal; a third hand or fused hand is blocking. Background hands and repeated faces are nonblocking.

### C6 — Kay's steward state
Kay appears in his steward's black: a plain black tunic with a yellow border, no mail and no black-and-yellow surcoat. Kay in his old mail and quartered surcoat is blocking: it sends him back to the tournament and battle pages just when his place at Camelot should read. The exact width of the yellow border or whether the keys show is nonblocking.
