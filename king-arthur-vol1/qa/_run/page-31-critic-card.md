## Page intent

## PAGE 31

Dramatic. Arthur stands in the shallow stream with his sword up while enemy riders and spears close in around him. Then Kay bursts into the ring on his black horse, shouting who he is and that this is his brother. Kay hauls Arthur up behind his saddle and wheels back toward their own people, scolding him; Arthur answers that Kay came alone too, and Kay gets the last, dry word. Kay owns the page: the jealous brother rides into an army for the king. The dominant turn: Arthur is back with his own people, and King Lot is coming for him. The reader turns to see Lot's charge. The enemy riders stay faceless and subordinate. No blow lands on the page, and the brothers' exchange is warm and funny under the danger.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 31 — card

### C1 — Transcription
All four strings read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order, with no extra text. A string that does not read is blocking; one that reads is not to be measured. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a comma present or missing inside a line, and a regional spelling variant are nonblocking; the words and their order must match. A line placed directly under its speaker's previous balloon is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — Kay's rescue owns the page
The eye must find Kay bursting on horseback into the ring of enemies around Arthur, who stands in the stream with his sword up. If Kay's arrival does not read as a rescue, the turn fails. Dramatic mode.

### C3 — Attribution of the exchange
Three lines are Kay's ("Get away from him!…", "Back to the line…", "Somebody had to…") and one is Arthur's ("And you came for me alone."), and each must hang from its own speaker's mouth by tail or placement. If Arthur's line reads as Kay's, or Kay's last word as Arthur's, the joke and the warmth are lost.

### C4 — Back to the line
The reader must see Kay carrying Arthur behind his saddle back toward their own side's spears. The exact direction of travel is nonblocking.

### C5 — Identity and integrity
Arthur blond, soft-faced, in blue; Kay black-haired, sharp-faced, in black and yellow. No blow lands; blood or bodies are blocking.
