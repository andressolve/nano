## Page intent

## PAGE 5

Dramatic. Night in the forge shed, the fire down to red coals. Arthur, knees pulled up on a grain sack, asks Ector whether he will ever be a real knight. Ector looks into the coals instead of at him and promises only that Arthur will be whatever he was born to be. Arthur says that isn't an answer; Ector says it's the only one he has and sends him to bed. Last, Ector alone at the dying fire, big hands around his cup, looking at the door the boy went through, unhappy. The dominant turn: Ector knows something about Arthur, and he isn't saying. The first image, the two of them in the firelight, owns the page; the empty-doorway close is the quiet aftershock. The reader turns carrying a secret.

## Exact owner-controlled script

## PAGE 5 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the forge shed, night.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 50%.** Night. The forge fire has burned down to red
coals. Ector sits on the anvil block with a cup, Arthur on a sack of grain
facing him, knees pulled up, firelight on both faces. Arthur on the left,
Ector on the right.

> **ARTHUR:** "Father? Kay was knighted at nineteen. Do you think I'll be a knight one day? A real one?"

**Panel 2 — 25%.** Ector looks into the coals instead of at Arthur.

> **ECTOR:** "When you're grown, lad, you'll be whatever you were born to be. I promise you that."
>
> **ARTHUR:** "That isn't an answer."
>
> **ECTOR:** "It's the only one I've got. Go to bed."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Arthur has gone. Ector sits alone at the dying fire, his
big hands around the cup, looking at the door Arthur went through. He is not
happy.

**Why you turn the page:** Ector knows something about Arthur, and he isn't saying.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 5 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
All four spoken lines must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in causal order, each owned by the right mouth by tail and placement, with no other text; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant; the words and their order must match.

### C2 — The evasion does not land
The reader must see Ector avoiding Arthur's eyes and, at the end, Ector alone looking after the boy, unhappy. If Ector meets Arthur's gaze warmly while answering, or the final image lacks him alone with the empty doorway, the secret is lost and it is blocking.

### C3 — Attribution in the A–B–A exchange
"When you're grown, lad…" and "It's the only one I've got. Go to bed." are Ector's; "Father? Kay was knighted…" and "That isn't an answer." are Arthur's. The reply must not be readable before its question. A swapped or ambiguous owner is blocking.

### C4 — Identity
Arthur: slight, beardless, straw-blond boy in brown. Ector: short, broad, bald-topped, clean-shaven, round ruddy face. A bearded Ector or a dark-haired Arthur is blocking.
