## Page intent

## PAGE 20

Dramatic. The churchyard is packed with lords in furs and mail, and Arthur, still in his patched brown farm tunic, holds the sword up at the stone, drawn for the third time. The lords' faces show anger, not wonder. King Lot owns the page: he sneers that it is the wizard's trick, demands to know whose son the boy is, and calls him a squire from a pigsty. Arthur, red to the ears, shouts back that it was a good farm, and Lot turns it into a joke for the lords: the king of the hens. The dominant turn: the lords are laughing him out of the churchyard. Merlin watches from the church doorway and says nothing. The reader turns because the boy has done the impossible thing three times and the powerful men are still saying no.

**Location:** the churchyard, morning, crowded.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 50%.** The churchyard packed with lords in furs and
mail. Arthur, still in his patched brown tunic, stands at the stone holding

> **LOT:** "Who is he? Whose son? Nobody knows! Am I to kneel to a squire who grew up in a pigsty?"

Lot, left, laughing to the lords.

> **ARTHUR:** "It wasn't a pigsty! It was a good farm!"
>
> **LOT:** "Hear that, my lords? The king of the hens!"

**Why you turn the page:** the lords are laughing him out of the churchyard.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 20 — card

### C1 — Transcription or ownership fails
All four lines (three Lot, one Arthur) must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once each, in causal order, each owned by the right mouth by tail and placement, with no extra text. A string that does not read is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured. A line owned by placement (directly under its speaker's previous balloon, or naming its addressee) is owned even if its tail wanders. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word; the words and their order are what must match.

### C2 — The lords' contempt does not own the page
One image must own the page: the churchyard full of angry lords, Arthur at the stone with the sword held up. If the lords read as amazed or cheering, the turn (they are laughing him out) is lost.

### C3 — The pigsty exchange is misattributed
Arthur's "It wasn't a pigsty!" must hang from Arthur's mouth and "Hear that, my lords?" from Lot's, with Lot's joke clearly after Arthur's shout. A swapped or reversed exchange is blocking.

### C4 — Identity
Lot must read as Lot (copper-red braided beard, bearskin, gold torc, broad square face); Arthur as the blond boy in his brown farm tunic. Merlin, if visible in the doorway, is silent and carries no balloon. Background lords' faces and anatomy are nonblocking.

### C5 — The sword is wrong
The sword is the plain iron sword and does not glow on this page. A bright or jewelled sword is blocking.
