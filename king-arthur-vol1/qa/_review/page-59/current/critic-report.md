## Blind read
Across a snowy hillside, snowballs roll past dated flags and grow larger as the legend of King Arthur accumulates contributions across centuries. The narrator and the visual snowball chronology own the page. The turn comes as successive writers add the great king, Merlin, the Round Table, Camelot, Lancelot, and the Grail; the chronology resolves at the surviving Winchester Round Table, then opens outward again with the promise that storytellers added many more knights.
