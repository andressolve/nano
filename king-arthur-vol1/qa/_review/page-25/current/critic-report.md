## Blind read
In an emptied feast hall, the blond young man in blue confronts the old, white-bearded man across the high table while gripping a crown in his fist. The large middle panel owns the page: the young man accuses the elder of taking him from his father and letting Ector raise him on a lie, and the elder meets his eyes, shows no regret, says the concealment saved him from being murdered, and insists he would do it again. In the final panel the young man's anger gives way to hurt and quiet attention; he asks whether his father ever saw him, and learns that his father saw him once, on the night of his birth, and named him.
