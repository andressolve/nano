## Blind read
An illuminated historical page asks whether King Arthur was real. The narrator and the surviving written evidence own the page: an account attributed to Gildas around 540 describes Badon and names Ambrosius but not Arthur, while the much later History of the Britons names Arthur as the victor of twelve battles, ending at Badon. The turn is the nearly three-hundred-year gap and the later appearance of Arthur's name, changing apparent historical evidence into an unresolved question about whether he was real or remembered into being.
