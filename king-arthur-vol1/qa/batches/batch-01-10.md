## Blind sequence record

On a stormy night, Merlin secretly carries a newborn away from a castle. Two winters later, the dying King Uther asks whether his son Arthur is safe; Merlin confirms that the boy is hidden, and Uther publicly names his unknown son as his heir before dying. With no accepted king, Britain's lords fight for the crown for thirteen years while Arthur grows up far away on Sir Ector's farm, unaware that he is Uther's lost son.

At fifteen, Arthur serves his newly knighted foster brother, Kay. A summons to a great New Year tournament takes Ector, Kay, and Arthur to London. Before they leave, Arthur asks whether he will ever become a knight, and Ector's guarded answer shows that he knows something about Arthur's birth. On the road, Arthur encounters the scale and age of London; a chronicle interlude explains the Roman fabric of Britain, its later political fragmentation, and the boundary between history and Arthurian legend.

In a London churchyard, Arthur, Kay, and Ector watch King Lot fail to pull a sword from a stone declaring that its successful claimant is Britain's true-born king. Lot dismisses the sword as Merlin's trick, while Merlin says it waits for someone else and looks toward Arthur. At the tournament field, Kay discovers that he left his own sword at the inn and sends Arthur to retrieve it. Arthur finds the inn locked and, thinking only of helping Kay avoid humiliation, remembers the unattended sword in the churchyard.
