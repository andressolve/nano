# Character Ledger — 8 locks, hard ceiling

Identity hierarchy, in order of priority:
**1.** face shape and apparent age · **2.** hair and facial-hair mass ·
**3.** body silhouette and habitual posture · **4.** costume value pattern ·
**5.** colour accent, prop, repeated gesture.

> Costume and colour may reinforce a character; they may never be the only
> cues. Separation is built through skull, jaw, nose, eye set, hairline,
> apparent age, body mass and posture — the traits that survive grayscale and
> thumbnail reduction. **Every page prompt names both the positive lock and the
> nearest forbidden lookalike.**

Ages and silhouettes are spread on purpose: one boy, one young man, one
middle-aged lord, three old men built nothing alike, one woman, and a dying
king. Three bearded older men (Merlin, Pellinore, Ector) are the risk; they
are separated by beard *shape*, head *shape*, and body *mass* before costume.

## 1 · ARTHUR — 15

**Anchor:** new.

**Wants (volume):** from Ector, to be his son; from Kay, to be his equal; from
the lords, to be accepted; from the songs, to be the hero in them.
**Wants (secretly):** to know who he is, and to be sure he deserves the sword.
**Moral position:** he believes being king means being the one who wins; he is
actually learning that it means being the one who keeps others safe. No one
ever says this aloud.

**Arc:** the squire who wants in → king and orphan in one afternoon → refused
and terrified → saved by his brother, then proud → broken by his pride →
gives up a fight he could win → builds a table where he sits no higher than
anyone.

**Structural lock:** a boy, not a young man — slight, long-limbed, still
growing into big hands and feet. Oval face with a soft jaw and no beard,
a few freckles across a short straight nose; wide-set grey-green eyes,
straight dark brows; mouth that shows every feeling. Thick straw-blond hair,
uneven, falling over his forehead and ears, roughly cut. Fair skin, reddened
by weather. Posture: leans forward into everything; when he's ashamed, he
pushes his hair back with the heel of his hand.

**Costume:** Movements I–II: undyed brown wool tunic, patched, a squire's
leather belt; III onward: a deep blue surcoat over mail, plain, too big in the
shoulders at first; a thin gold circlet only at the crowning and the feast,
never in battle. **Accent:** the deep blue. Sword from the stone: plain,
long, a cross-guard of dark iron, a round pommel — it breaks on p 40. From
p 46: Excalibur (see `05`).

**Never:** a man's jaw or stubble; Kay's dark hair and sharp face; a
heroic chest; a crown in battle; serene.

**Voice:** eager, blunt, too honest; says the thing out loud before he's
thought about it. "Was that wrong?"

## 2 · MERLIN — very old

**Anchor:** new.

**Wants (volume):** Arthur alive, and Arthur good — in that order, and he
isn't always sure which matters more.
**Wants (secretly):** to be there when the kingdom he foresaw comes true; he
already knows he won't be.
**Moral position:** he believes he is guiding a king; he is also deciding a
boy's whole life without asking him, and he knows it.

**Arc:** the hooded figure with the baby → the wizard at the stone → the
voice that tells the hall the truth → the rescuer and the scold → the teacher
at the lake → the man who warns he won't always be there.

**Structural lock:** tall, very thin, stooped from the shoulders, with a
long neck. A long narrow face, deeply lined; a great hooked nose; deep-set
eyes, bright black, under wild brows. A long, narrow, *forked* white beard to
the chest, and wild white hair that stands away from his head. Big-knuckled
hands, always moving. Posture: leans on a crooked staff taller than himself;
points with two fingers.

**Costume:** a hooded robe of very dark grey-green, frayed at the hem, with
a rope belt; no stars, no pointed hat. **Accent:** a single silver ring with a
green stone.

**Never:** a pointed wizard's hat or stars; Pellinore's bulk; Ector's round
head; a short or square beard.

**Voice:** dry, quick, impatient, funny; asks questions he already knows the
answer to. "Which do you like better?"

## 3 · SIR ECTOR — about 55

**Anchor:** new.

**Wants (volume):** to keep the boy he raised as his son; then, losing that,
to make sure his own son Kay is not forgotten.
**Wants (secretly):** for Arthur to forgive him for fifteen years of a kind
lie.
**Moral position:** he believes he kept a promise to Merlin; he also kept a
boy from knowing his own name, and he has always known that too.

**Arc:** the warm, gruff father → the man who knows → the father who kneels
→ the old man who asks one gift for his son.

**Structural lock:** short and broad, barrel-bodied, thick-necked. A big
round head, bald on top, with a fringe of short grey hair around the sides;
a round, ruddy, clean-shaven face with heavy jowls; small kind brown eyes
under bushy grey brows; a broken nose. Thick forearms. Posture: plants his
feet wide; holds his belt with both thumbs.

**Costume:** a worn russet country-knight's tunic over an old mail shirt, a
heavy brown travelling cloak. **Accent:** russet.

**Never:** a beard; Pellinore's height; Merlin's thinness.

**Voice:** plain, warm, few words, country speech. "Get up, lad. No — I'm the
one who should kneel."

## 4 · SIR KAY — 19

**Anchor:** new.

**Wants (volume):** to be the important one; then, after the stone, to be
needed by the brother who outranks him.
**Wants (secretly):** for his father to look at him the way he looks at
Arthur.
**Moral position:** he believes he's the clever one who sees through
everyone; he is the jealous brother who tells the truth when it counts and
rides into a battle alone for Arthur.

**Arc:** the cocky new knight → the liar at the stone → the one who tells
the truth → the steward who saves the king → the doubter who asks about the
empty seat.

**Structural lock:** tall, lean, hard-edged, a young man. A narrow face with
high sharp cheekbones and a pointed chin; a thin straight nose; narrow dark
eyes, quick and sardonic; a thin mouth with a habitual half-smile on one side.
Straight black hair worn short and neat; a faint first moustache he is proud
of. Olive skin. Posture: chin up, weight on one hip, arms folded.

**Costume:** brand-new mail and a black-and-yellow surcoat (Ector's colours
are russet; Kay chose his own); later the steward's black with a yellow
border. **Accent:** yellow.

**Never:** fair hair; Arthur's round softness; Lot's beard.

**Voice:** sarcastic, fast, funny, always has the last word — until he
doesn't. "Of course it's mine. Who else's would it be?"

## 5 · KING LOT OF ORKNEY — about 40

**Anchor:** new.

**Wants (volume):** the crown, or at least never to kneel to anyone lower
than himself.
**Wants (secretly):** for the boy to fail so he never has to wonder whether
he could have pulled the sword.
**Moral position:** he believes he is defending Britain from a wizard's
puppet; he is defending his own pride.

**Arc:** fails at the stone and laughs → refuses the boy → insults him at the
feast → leads the war → retreats swearing → walks out of the round table.

**Structural lock:** big, heavy-shouldered, a powerful man going thick at
the waist. A broad, square, weathered face; a strong flat nose; pale blue
eyes, hooded and contemptuous; a heavy brow. Long copper-red hair and a long
copper-red beard plaited into two braids, with iron rings in them. Posture:
head back, looks down his nose; laughs with his whole chest.

**Costume:** a sea-king's look from the far north: a black bearskin cloak
over scaled mail, a heavy gold torc at the neck, a plain iron crown. **Accent:**
gold torc and copper hair against black.

**Never:** clean-shaven; Merlin's white; Pellinore's battered grey.

**Voice:** loud, mocking, sure; every sentence is a dare. "Come down from that
chair, boy, before we pull you down."

## 6 · KING PELLINORE — about 50

**Anchor:** new.

**Wants (volume):** a fight worth having; then, after he is spared and
invited, a lord worth serving.
**Wants (secretly):** to stop living in a tent by a fountain.
**Moral position:** he believes guarding the fountain proves knights'
worth; he is a lonely, honest man waiting for someone better than himself.

**Arc:** the champion who beats Arthur → enchanted to sleep → spared and
invited → the first knight to sit at the table.

**Structural lock:** enormous — the tallest and widest man in the book, a
bear. A long, heavy, horse-like face, scarred across one cheek; a big
lantern jaw; small grey eyes; a nose broken more than once. Iron-grey hair
cropped close to the skull; a short, *square-cut* iron-grey beard. Posture:
very still until he moves; stands with both hands on the pommel of a huge
sword planted point-down.

**Costume:** old, dented black-painted armour and mail, a faded green
surcoat with a white boar, a battered pavilion of green-and-white cloth.
**Accent:** the faded green.

**Never:** a long or forked beard (Merlin); a round bald head (Ector);
red hair (Lot); villainy.

**Voice:** slow, gravelly, formal, few words, no insults. "Then you'll fight
like one."

## 7 · THE LADY OF THE LAKE — ageless

**Anchor:** new.

**Wants (volume):** to give the sword to the right king — and one day to ask
a gift of him.
**Wants (secretly):** unknown; the book doesn't tell.
**Moral position:** neither good nor wicked; she is the lake's.

**Arc:** the arm in the mist → the woman on the water → the bargain → gone.

**Structural lock:** tall and still. A long pale oval face, high forehead,
calm grey eyes that don't blink often, a straight narrow nose. Very long
straight black hair, wet, falling to her knees. Pale skin with a faint cool
tint. Posture: perfectly upright; hands folded; walks on the water as if it
were a floor.

**Costume:** a plain long gown of white samite, wet at the hem, a thin
silver circlet. **Accent:** silver-white against dark water.

**Never:** a smile that reveals anything; jewellery beyond the circlet;
coloured hair.

**Voice:** quiet, formal, every word chosen. "One day I will ask you for a
gift."

---

## 8 · KING UTHER — about 60, dying

**Anchor:** new. One page (p 2).

**Wants:** to name his son before he dies.
**Structural lock:** a gaunt, hollow-cheeked old man in a great bed; a
long grey moustache with no beard; sunken eyes; long thin grey hair spread on
the pillow; a heavy red-and-gold coverlet with a dragon. **Accent:** the
red dragon.

**Never:** Merlin's beard; Lot's red.

**Voice:** a whisper, every word costing him. "My son… Arthur."

## Reserved design space

No background or unnamed figure may inherit a complete identity stack:

- fair-haired boy in blue → **Arthur**
- tall thin old man with a long white forked beard → **Merlin**
- short broad bald clean-shaven old man → **Ector**
- dark-haired sharp young man in black and yellow → **Kay**
- copper-red braided beard, bearskin, gold torc → **Lot**
- huge iron-grey man with square beard, green surcoat → **Pellinore**
- black-haired woman in white → **the Lady of the Lake**

Background lords at the stone, the feast, and the table are middle-aged, in
mixed muted surcoats, with brown or grey hair and short beards; no blond
boys, no forked white beards, no red braids, no white gowns.

## Collision matrix — pairs requiring adversarial boards

| Pair | Risk | Carried by |
|---|---|---|
| Merlin / Pellinore | highest | thin vs. enormous; long forked white beard vs. short square grey; wild hair vs. cropped; robe vs. armour |
| Merlin / Ector | high | tall thin stooped vs. short broad planted; long face and beard vs. round clean-shaven face |
| Ector / Pellinore | high | bald round clean-shaven vs. cropped grey hair and square beard; short vs. huge |
| Arthur / Kay | medium | blond, round-faced boy vs. black-haired, sharp-faced young man; blue vs. black-and-yellow |
| Lot / Pellinore | medium | red braids, gold, bearskin vs. grey, green surcoat, dented black armour |
| Merlin / Uther | low (one page) | forked white beard vs. grey moustache only; standing vs. in bed |
