# The House of Atreus Vol 1 — Settings

## MOUNT SIPYLUS — Palace of Tantalus (Pages 1–3)

SETTING: SIPYLUS
- Hilltop palace of rough-cut limestone, open-air courtyard with columns
- Olive groves cascade down slopes, fig trees, wild herbs on rocky terrain
- Fertile valley below, distant mountains, sea glimmering on horizon
- Atmosphere: Warm golden light, deceptively peaceful, beauty concealing rot
- Palette: Full warm — sandstone, olive green, golden amber dominant

---

## PISA — Kingdom of Oenomaus (Pages 4–7)

SETTING: PISA
- Royal gate flanked by stakes bearing thirteen suitors' skulls
- Wide dusty chariot track running along coastal cliffs above the Aegean
- Dry scrubland, wind-bent pines, dust clouds, bleached rock
- Atmosphere: Harsh bright sunlight, exposed, wind-swept, dangerous
- Palette: Warm but harsh — sandstone bleached white, bright blue sky, dust
- Cliff scene (Page 7): Sunset turning blood-red over the sea

---

## MYCENAE — Citadel of the Atreids (Pages 8–15)

SETTING: MYCENAE
- Lion Gate: iconic gateway with two carved lions flanking a column, set in massive cyclopean stone walls (enormous rough-cut boulders fitted together)
- Palace megaron: great hall with four massive wooden columns around central circular hearth, painted frescoes of lions and warriors on walls
- Throne room, corridors, treasure vault
- Dry brown hills, sparse olive trees, scrub grass
- Atmosphere: Imposing, heavy, claustrophobic even outdoors — the weight of stone and power and accumulated sin
- Palette shifts across pages:
  - Pages 8–9: Warm but heavy
  - Pages 10–11: Firelit interior, amber darkening to sickly yellow
  - Page 12: Apocalyptic amber-red (reversed sun)
  - Pages 13–15: Cold moonlight, muted tones

---

## ORACLE'S CAVE (Page 13)

SETTING: ORACLE CAVE
- Dark, dripping cave interior, smoke from ritual fire
- Bones and dried herbs hanging from ceiling
- Ancient withered oracle figure barely visible through haze
- Atmosphere: Claustrophobic, otherworldly, prophetic dread
- Palette: Near-monochrome — smoke gray, fire orange, deep shadow

---

## MOUNTAIN HUT (Page 14)

SETTING: MOUNTAIN EXILE
- Rough shepherd's hut in wild mountainous country
- Sparse, harsh terrain — rocks, scrub, wind
- Training area with wooden practice post
- Atmosphere: Isolated, austere, a place of purpose not comfort
- Palette: Muted — cold stone, gray-green scrub, overcast sky
