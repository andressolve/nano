# The House of Atreus Vol 1 — Character Definitions

## CRITICAL: How Character Locking Works

The reference image does 80% of the character locking. The text lock block is reinforcement only. Keep it short.

For every page prompt:
1. Upload the reference image(s) for all characters in the scene
2. Paste the SHORT lock block below
3. Include: "featuring the same characters shown in the reference images"

## CHARACTER: TANTALUS (King of Sipylus)

**Reference sheet prompt:**
```
create image: Subject:
Character reference sheet showing Tantalus, an ancient Greek king in his 50s, in two views side by side: close-up portrait on the left, full body on the right.

Character Description:
A tall, gaunt man in his 50s with an angular, once-powerful frame. High cheekbones, deep-set dark brown eyes with calculating intensity, sharp aquiline nose, thin lips. Silver-gray hair swept back from high forehead, shoulder length. Deep lines etched from nose to mouth. Prominent veins on forearms.

Signature identifying marks:
- Deep nasolabial lines framing thin mouth
- Prominent forearm veins
- Silver-gray hair swept straight back

Left panel — Portrait (head and shoulders):
Close-up showing face clearly. Expression: calm and focused with an unsettling edge, looking directly at viewer. All facial features visible.

Right panel — Full body:
Standing in 3/4 view. Fine white linen chiton with gold-embroidered border at hem and neckline. Leather sandals with gold buckles. Thin gold circlet crown. Gold ring on right hand. Hands relaxed at sides.

Art Style: Cinematic photorealistic illustration with warm natural lighting and Mediterranean atmosphere
Background: Simple warm neutral gradient
Lighting: Soft even lighting from front-left, studio reference style.

General Instruction:
This is a reference sheet for character consistency. Both views must show the exact same person. Prioritize clarity over atmosphere.

Aspect Ratio: 4:3 (landscape)
```

**Lean lock block:**
```
TANTALUS (50s, Greek king): Tall, gaunt, angular frame. Silver-gray hair swept back, deep-set dark eyes, aquiline nose, deep nasolabial lines. Thin gold circlet, white chiton with gold border.
```

**Which pages:** 1, 2, 3 (Tartarus version: emaciated, chiton torn, no crown)
