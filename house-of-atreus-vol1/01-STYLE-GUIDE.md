# The House of Atreus Vol 1 — Style Guide

## Style Block (Copy Verbatim to Every Prompt)

```
Art Style: Cinematic photorealistic illustration with warm natural lighting and Mediterranean atmosphere
Color palette: warm sandstone, olive green, terracotta brown, Aegean blue, golden amber
Effects: Golden hour sunlight, atmospheric haze, natural shadow depth, dusty stone textures
```

---

## Palette Notes

- **Warm sandstone** — dominant in architecture, ground, skin tones. The world these people built.
- **Olive green** — landscape, vegetation. The natural world framing the human drama.
- **Terracotta brown** — earth, leather, dried blood. Weight and history.
- **Aegean blue** — sea in distance, sky. Freedom, escape, what lies beyond.
- **Golden amber** — sunlight, fire, feasts. Warmth that can be genuine or deceptive.

**Pages 1–8:** Full warm palette. Golden hour dominance.
**Pages 9–10:** Palette darkens. Amber shifts toward sickly yellow in firelit interiors.
**Page 11 (Feast reveal):** Warm palette corrupted — gold becomes bile, amber becomes sinister.
**Page 12 (Reversed sun):** Apocalyptic amber-red. Palette at maximum distortion.
**Pages 13–15:** Cold shift. Moonlight blues, muted tones, darkness.

---

## Panel Rules

- 3–4 panels per page maximum
- Black panel borders, consistent weight
- Clean white gutters
- No decorative borders
- Caption boxes: weathered parchment style, dark text
- Speech bubbles: classic white with black outline
- Full-page panels on: Page 6 Panel 1 (chariot race) and Page 12 Panel 1 (reversed sun)

---

## Shot Type Reference

| Shot | Use For |
|------|---------|
| Wide/Establishing | Sipylus palace, Gate of Skulls, Lion Gate, landscape transitions |
| Medium | Conversations, feast scenes, the bargain |
| Close-up | Emotional moments — Tantalus's madness, Atreus's cold rage, Thyestes's horror |
| Low Angle | Power shots — Zeus standing, Atreus over Thyestes |
| High Angle | Vulnerability — Pelops on slab, Thyestes fleeing |

---

## Visual References

**Should look like:** The reference image provided (boy overlooking Mediterranean village) — cinematic, grounded, tactile, warm natural light. Think Ridley Scott's ancient world films. Think heavy stone architecture with real weight.

**Should NOT look like:** Disney Hercules. Percy Jackson. Stylized flat illustration. Glowing fantasy lighting. Anything with a children's book softness.
