# 01 — STYLE GUIDE

One register, two page modes. NOTE (user, 2026-06-09): deliberately NOT the oil-painting biography register — this volume gets its own look: premium modern aerospace illustration.

## STYLE BLOCK — paste verbatim into every prompt

> Premium modern aerospace illustration — crisp, highly detailed digital painting in the style of a high-end aviation book cover. Vivid but controlled palette: deep high-altitude blues, steel grays, desert tans, blazing flare oranges and afterburner glow used for drama. Dramatic cinematic lighting, volumetric clouds, sharp surface detail on aircraft and missiles with correct proportions.

## REGISTER BLOCK — paste verbatim into every prompt

> Realistic illustration. NOT a comic. NO halftones, NO cel shading, NO cartoon outlines. Painterly-crisp rendering, cinematic lighting.

## ANTI-DRIFT DIRECTIVE — paste verbatim into every prompt

> NOT a children's book. Serious mature illustrated technical book, realistic proportions, natural lighting, cinematic composition.

## Page modes

**SKY mode** (cinematic): full-bleed painted sky scene. Caption bands top/bottom in the Honda formula — "off-white box, dark serif text, readable."

**POSTER mode** (cutaway/diagram): the page is a vintage-engineering-poster spread — warm ivory paper background, central painted object (missile cutaway, gun, guidance diagram), 4–6 callout caption boxes arrayed around it with thin leader lines, dark serif text. Think 1950s cutaway poster, painted not vector. Same painter's hand as SKY pages.

## Text treatment

- Caption boxes: "off-white box, dark serif text, readable" (SKY) / "ivory callout box, dark serif text, thin leader line to the part" (POSTER).
- LETTERING block verbatim, last in prompt. Restrictions block closes every prompt.
- No speech bubbles in this volume except the McLean pages (max 1 short bubble, <12 words).
- All aircraft markings generic: "USAF"-style star roundels okay, no squadron numbers, no invented serials. Say "NO spurious stenciling text" in restrictions when relevant.

## Restrictions block — paste verbatim, closes every prompt

> All words spelled correctly. Do not duplicate text. Do not invent extra captions or labels. NO watermarks, NO modern logos, NO spurious signage or stenciling.
