# 03 — SETTINGS

## HIGH SKY (duel pages 1, 9, 10, 13)
Altitude ~9,000 m. Deep blue above, hazy horizon line low in frame, towering cumulus stacks for scale and drama. Late-afternoon sun, long light. Contrails and missile smoke trails are bright white ribbons. Flares burn blinding white-orange with smoky tails falling in arcs.

## MOJAVE DESERT / CHINA LAKE (pages 3, 4, 5)
1950s. Creosote scrub, pale sand, sawtooth dry mountains on the horizon, enormous clean blue sky. Outdoor test range: wooden observation tower, corrugated metal sheds. Indoor lab: cluttered workbench, vacuum tubes, oscilloscopes, hand tools, blackboard, tungsten light, desert glare through a window.

## IVORY POSTER SHEET (pages 2, 6, 7, 8, 11, 14)
Not a place — the POSTER mode backdrop. Warm ivory paper, faint age tone, clean even light. Central painted object with callout boxes and thin leader lines. Vintage 1950s engineering-poster feel, painted not vector.
