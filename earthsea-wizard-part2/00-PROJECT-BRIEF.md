# A Wizard of Earthsea - Part Two: The School for Wizards

## Purpose

This is the direct continuation of `../earthsea-wizard-part1/`, not `../earthsea-vol2/`. It expands the next bounded arc of *A Wizard of Earthsea* at the same narrative density and in the same finished-page register as Part One.

## Scope

- Source coverage: Chapters 3-4, from Ged's first voyage aboard *Shadow* through his years at Roke, the loosing of the shadow, his recovery, and his departure as a staff-bearing wizard for Low Torning.
- Length: cover + 30 landscape pages.
- Arc: Ged finds the fast teaching he wanted, mistakes excellence for wisdom, lets rivalry isolate him from friendship, and pays for proving that no power is beyond him.
- Ending: Ged passes the school's final threshold by asking rather than mastering, receives his staff, and sails west carrying both Nemmerle's cost and an unnamed enemy.

## Editorial Standard

Each major scene must contain:

1. Orientation: where, when, and who is present.
2. Desire: what Ged or the scene's viewpoint character wants now.
3. Interaction or action.
4. A visible turn.
5. Reaction or consequence.
6. A clear bridge to the next scene.

The Roke curriculum must be dramatized rather than summarized. Vetch becomes Ged's friend before the story asks the reader to feel their separation. Jasper's contempt must work through repeated, escalating encounters rather than one sudden challenge. Nemmerle's death, Ged's long recovery, Gensher's warning, Vetch's true-name gift, and the Doorkeeper's final lesson each receive space on the page.

## Visual Standard

`../earthsea-wizard-part1/pages/` is the visual source of truth. Preserve its etched black line, flat muted maritime color, weathered parchment captions, off-white serif speech bubbles, realistic anatomy, copper- and black-skinned Archipelagans, restrained warm light, and generous 3-4 panel pacing.

Roke broadens the setting without changing the register: wet slate roofs, massive grey stone, moss-black courts, candle-gold interiors, silver werelight, green-black hill country, and cold northern seas. Magic remains physical and restrained. The shadow is absolute flat matte black.

## Production Path

- Subscription-backed built-in Codex image generation only.
- No `OPENAI_API_KEY`, imagegen CLI, or direct API billing.
- Lock new Roke identities before prototypes or pages.
- Attach the relevant identity refs plus at least one approved Part One page as the visual-register reference to every generated page.
- Bake all captions and dialogue into the generated page image.
- Regenerate a full page when identity, text, staging, attribution, reading order, or canvas dimensions fail. No crop-patching or HTML/SVG lettering.

## Source Policy

Story facts are adapted from Chapters 3-4 and cross-checked against the existing legacy project notes and chapter summaries. All production lettering is original adaptation prose except the previously verified line, `To light a candle is to cast a shadow.` No dialogue is presented as a quotation from Le Guin unless it has already been explicitly verified in the project research.
