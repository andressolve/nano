# Character Locks - Part Two

## Ged - Roke student, unscarred

Primary identity source: `../earthsea-wizard-part1/refs/ref_ged_young.png`

Production ref: `refs/ref_ged_roke.png`

A wiry red-brown copper-skinned boy who ages from thirteen to seventeen across the Roke years. Straight black hair roughly and unevenly cut, fierce dark eyes, sharp hawk-like features, slight strong build. No scars before Page 26. He arrives in the rough undyed tunic and dark travel cloak from Part One, then wears a plain charcoal-grey novice cloak, dark leggings, and worn boots. Proud, quick, observant, and increasingly self-contained; never cute, soft-featured, pale, or fashionably ornate.

## Ged - scarred student and young wizard

Identity sources: `refs/ref_ged_roke.png`, `../earthsea/refs/ref_ged_scarred.png`

Production ref: `refs/ref_ged_scarred.png`

The same red-brown copper-skinned face at seventeen to eighteen, thinner after illness. Four parallel pale healed claw scars rake down the LEFT side of his face from beside the left eye across the cheek to the jaw. His speech and expression are initially strained. He wears a plain dark student's cloak; only on Page 30 does he carry a dark yew wizard's staff. No ornate jewelry, no aged adult body, no scars on the wrong side.

## Vetch / Estarriol

Identity source: `../earthsea/refs/ref_group_roke.png`, figure A.

Production ref: `refs/ref_vetch.png`

A broad, sturdy adolescent a little older than Ged with deep black skin, short tightly curled black hair, round plain features, kind dark eyes, and an easy warm smile. Plain dark charcoal student's cloak over practical clothes. His gestures are open and grounded. As a sorcerer he carries a simple wooden staff. Never comic-relief proportions, never pale, never thin, never ornate.

## Jasper

Identity source: `../earthsea/refs/ref_group_roke.png`, figure B.

Production ref: `refs/ref_jasper.png`

A tall graceful adolescent with light-brown skin, fine dark hair kept neatly, elegant handsome features, and a slender build. Fine cool-grey cloak fastened with a round silver clasp. Controlled posture, economical gestures, courteous superior half-smile. His contempt is quiet rather than theatrical. Never white-skinned, blond, bulky, or openly snarling.

## Archmage Nemmerle

Identity source: `../earthsea/refs/ref_group_roke.png`, figure C.

Production ref: `refs/ref_nemmerle.png`

An immensely old, very thin man whose long hair, beard, robe, and weathered staff are worn white as driftwood. Deeply lined warm-brown skin remains visible beneath the whiteness. A large matte-black raven sits on his shoulder or nearby. His presence is frail, lucid, and vast; never a glowing angel, never muscular, never a generic white-bearded king.

## Master Doorkeeper

Production ref: `refs/ref_roke_masters.png`, figure A.

A small, ordinary-looking older man with medium-brown skin, cropped grey hair, clean-shaven face, plain brown wool, and alert amused eyes. No staff and no display of rank. His humility is the test.

## Master Hand

Production ref: `refs/ref_roke_masters.png`, figure B.

A gentle middle-aged mage with dark copper-brown skin, close grey beard, weathered practical hands, and a charcoal robe. Calm teacher's bearing. He demonstrates illusion with a plain pebble and modest silver light.

## Master Namer, Kurremkarmerruk

Production ref: `refs/ref_roke_masters.png`, figure C.

A dry, reed-thin ancient man with brown skin, narrow face, sparse white hair, and layered ink-grey robes. He sits at a high desk among severe books and name lists. Severe concentration, not cruelty.

## Gensher, new Archmage

Production ref: `refs/ref_roke_masters.png`, figure D.

A stern middle-aged man with deep brown skin, close-cropped black-and-grey hair, strong narrow face, and unadorned deep-grey robes. He carries a dark staff. His authority is exact, grave, and suspicious after Nemmerle's death.

## Hoeg, the otak

Identity source: `../earthsea/refs/ref_group_serret_otak.png`, animal B.

Production ref: `refs/ref_hoeg.png`

A small sleek wild mammal about the size of a large rat or tiny cat, with dark-brown brindled fur, broad whiskered face, rounded ears, bright black eyes, compact low body, short legs, and a long furred tail. Alert and fierce, able to curl in Ged's hood. A wild animal, not a mascot, ferret, dog, or cartoon pet.

## Roke students and sailors

Recurring crowd locks, no solo ref. Archipelagans of varied brown and black skin tones in practical wool and leather. Sailors are weathered and work-worn. Students wear simple grey cloaks with no uniforms, crests, hats, or modern school objects.
