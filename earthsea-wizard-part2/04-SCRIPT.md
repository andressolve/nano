# Script - A Wizard of Earthsea, Part Two: The School for Wizards

Every page uses the Style Block and Lettering Restrictions in `01-STYLE-GUIDE.md`, the listed character refs, an approved Part One page as visual-register reference, and the strict attribution rules in `../earthsea-wizard-part1/05-SPEECH-ATTRIBUTION-STUDY.md`. All text below is verbatim production lettering.

## Cover

Single cinematic image. Ged stands outside the small west door of the Great House in rain, one hand near the dark wood. The door's true ivory-and-horn form glimmers faintly through the illusion. Roke Knoll rises behind the school under a divided sky. Vetch is a warm, distant figure in one court; Jasper is a poised silhouette in another. Across the threshold at Ged's feet lies one impossible shape of absolute flat matte black, foreshadowing without touching him.

Lettering:

- Title: `A WIZARD OF EARTHSEA`
- Subtitle: `PART TWO: THE SCHOOL FOR WIZARDS`
- Credit: `after the novel by Ursula K. Le Guin`

Refs: unscarred Ged, Vetch, Jasper. Shadow rule applies.

- Panel 2: a young sailor makes room beside a bread bowl and teases Ged; other sailors work and listen.
- Panel 3: night under the sail; Ged shares food, knots rope badly, and begins to laugh with them before storm cloud gathers ahead.

- Sailor P2, second: `Then earn us reason to remember it.`
- Caption P3: `They shared bread, work, and mockery without malice. By nightfall they were his first companions beyond Gont.`

Attribution map P2: sailor LEFT has exactly two balloons, upper-left and lower-left; Ged RIGHT has exactly one centered-right balloon between them. Reading order sailor, Ged, sailor. All background sailors are silent.

## Page 14 - Candle and Shadow

- Panel 1: Ged holds a dull pebble that appears diamond-bright in silver illusion-light.
