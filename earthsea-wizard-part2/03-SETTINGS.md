# Settings - Part Two

## The ship *Shadow* and the Inmost Sea

A broad, oared trading vessel with a high weathered hull, square dark-red-brown sail, serpent-carved prow, wet rope, barrels, and a crew of roughly seventy. The deck is crowded and communal. The sea journey moves from cold Gont dawn into rain, heavy swells, and a black storm before a single Roke light appears through the scud.

## Thwil

The small port of Roke: steep narrow streets, tall huddled houses, sharp wet slate roofs, mussel stalls, stone gutters, laundry, children, and grey-green harbor light. To Ged it feels enormous. Ordinary people are accustomed to wizardry and answer strangers with practical riddles.

## The Great House of Roke

A vast, old school built of massive grey stone above Thwil. It is never a castle of spectacle. Its scale emerges through linked courts, shadowed passageways, refectory halls, stairs, workrooms, libraries, and quiet doors.

- **The west door:** first appears as small, plain wood. Its true material is ivory from a dragon's tooth, framed in black horn. The Doorkeeper stands beside it without insignia.
- **Inner court:** open to rain and moving leaves, with a fountain, a black tree, and the sense that the world's speech is briefly audible.
- **Long Table:** a deep refectory hall lit by candles and restrained silver werelight; long dark tables, shared bread, no luxurious feast.
- **Teaching courts:** moss-dark stone, basins, pebbles, bowls, plants, and simple objects used for exact demonstrations.
- **Healing chambers:** plain whitewashed stone, narrow beds, winter light, herbs, linen, and silence.

## Roke Knoll and the Immanent Grove

Roke Knoll is a bare rounded green-black hill above the town, open to the whole sky. Early scenes are wet, windy, and youthful. Moon's Night is clear, cold, and starlit. During the summoning the world opens as a vertical rent of cold white light; the shadow that crosses it remains absolute matte black.

The Immanent Grove lies beyond: an ancient green darkness of roots and leaves where the Master Patterner teaches sorcerers. It is seen only from the edge in this volume; prentices do not enter.

## The Isolate Tower

A lone grey tower on Roke's northmost cape, surrounded by heather, black rock, and cold sea. Inside are narrow cells, a high classroom, severe desks, candles, books, and name lists. Winter wind presses against the walls. The visual rhythm is repetition, discipline, and scale without spectacle.

## Roke harbor at departure

Wet stone quay beneath the Great House, working boats, cold western light. Ged leaves with a plain yew staff on a modest vessel bound west. The island remains green and sheltered behind him; the open sea ahead is steel-grey.
