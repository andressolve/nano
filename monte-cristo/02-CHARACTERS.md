# The Count of Monte Cristo — Character Reference Specifications

## Reference Rule

Every recurring character receives an approved 3:2 landscape reference sheet
before appearing on a finished page. Sheets contain no text. Later pages inherit
identity from the approved image rather than redescribing the character from
scratch.

## Edmond Dantès — Marseille, 1815

- Age 19.
- Tall, lean, strong without bodybuilder mass.
- Mediterranean French sailor; sun-warmed olive complexion.
- Raven-black loose curls, black-brown eyes.
- Open, attentive face; calm under physical pressure; emotionally unguarded
  with his father and Mercédès.
- Working clothes: off-white linen shirt, indigo or navy waistcoat, dark
  trousers, faded red-brown sash when aboard ship, practical shoes.
- His capability should be visible before his beauty.

Required views:

1. full-body neutral three-quarter;
2. working pose hauling or controlling rope;
3. head-and-shoulders open expression;
4. head-and-shoulders realizing danger.

## Edmond Dantès — Château d'If

- Same bones and eyes as young Edmond.
- Mid-to-late twenties in the reference phase.
- Gaunt body, hollow cheeks, shoulder-length tangled black hair, rough beard.
- Damp rough linen prison clothes; bare or crudely shod feet.
- Despair has changed his body, but Faria's teaching has returned focus to his
  gaze.
- Hands are worn and capable, with damaged nails from stone and tools.

Required views:

1. full-body before Faria, collapsed posture;
2. full-body after months of learning, alert crouch with improvised tool;
3. close face in despair;
4. close face with the new analytical gaze.

## The Count of Monte Cristo — Paris, 1838

- Late thirties; unmistakably the same man.
- Lean, controlled, almost unnaturally still.
- Pale from prison and cultivated nocturnal presentation, but not corpse-like.
- Black hair carefully arranged; clean-shaven for the principal Count identity.
- Immaculate black tailcoat or evening coat, fitted black waistcoat, high white
  collar, restrained dark cravat, dark trousers, gloves or a single ring used
  selectively.
- Wealth appears through cut, material, servants, timing, and space—not
  superhero ornament.
- Expression range is narrow but meaningful: social courtesy, private pain,
  controlled threat, moral shock.

Required views:

1. full-body doorway posture;
2. seated conversational control;
3. courteous public close-up;
4. private close-up in which Edmond becomes visible.

## Abbé Faria

- Italian priest and political prisoner, late sixties to seventies.
- Thin but not spectral; physically diminished, intellectually electric.
- High forehead, deep lively eyes, long white-gray hair and beard.
- Worn prison clothing adapted with practical repairs.
- Fingers stained by improvised ink and work.
- Warmth, impatience, humor, authority, and sudden illness must all be possible.
- Never a mystical wizard or exposition machine.

Required views:

1. full-body emerging through the tunnel;
2. kneeling teacher with diagram and tool;
3. delighted analytical close-up;
4. physically vulnerable close-up without loss of dignity.

## Gérard de Villefort — Marseille, 1815

- Age roughly 27–30.
- Handsome, controlled, upwardly mobile royal prosecutor.
- Dark neatly arranged hair, clean-shaven, intelligent eyes trained to conceal
  reaction.
- Formal black legal/court dress appropriate to the Restoration, white collar
  and cravat; no modern judge's robe shorthand.
- His danger is not villainous appearance. It is the speed with which charm
  becomes calculation.

Required views:

1. formal standing public posture;
2. seated examining posture;
3. close-up before reading Noirtier's name;
4. close-up after deciding to sacrifice Edmond.

## Mercédès — Marseille, 1815

- Young Catalan woman, approximately Edmond's age.
- Dark hair, warm olive complexion, direct dark eyes.
- Grace and strength without aristocratic polish.
- Simple Catalan/Marseille working dress, shawl, and practical hair arrangement.
- Her love for Edmond is active choice, not decorative devotion.

## Mercédès, Countess de Morcerf — Paris, 1838

- Late thirties; clearly the same woman.
- Composed elegance shaped by survival rather than social vanity.
- Dark hair dressed for 1838 Paris, with restrained jewelry.
- Rich but sober gowns in deep burgundy, brown, black, or muted green.
- Her gaze should cut through the Count's performance before anyone else's.
- She carries grief, intelligence, fear for Albert, and the courage to confront
  Edmond without pleading for Fernand.

Required views:

1. full-body formal salon posture;
2. seated maternal/private posture;
3. public composure close-up;
4. recognition close-up.
