# 03 — SETTINGS

All grounded in the gritty documentary register. Dust, haze, hard light, one hot accent.

- **DUST / SMOKE ADVANCE** (P1, P13) — open broken ground, a tank charging forward throwing up a wall of
  dust and engine smoke, hard low sun or muzzle glow, grit in the air. The hero "charging out of the murk" shot.
- **WAR-WORN FIELD / RUINED GROUND** (P2, P9, P11) — flat cratered farmland, shattered treeline, overcast
  desaturated light, mud and tracks. Human scale on P2.
- **DARK SCHEMATIC VOID** (P3, P5, P7, P12 — POSTER pages) — charcoal/slate technical-plate background, no
  environment, central object lit, amber/steel-blue leader lines. Field-manual look.
- **GUN-LINE / FIRING** (P4, P8) — the tank firing the main gun, a hard muzzle-flash and a flat dust shock
  wave off the ground; or a HEAT round / interceptor flaring against the armor. Smoke and grit.
- **TURRET INTERIOR / SIGHTS** (P6, P10) — cramped armored interior, green/amber sight glow, the gunner's
  thermal screen with a target reticle; the crew at their stations.
- **OPEN HORIZON / SWARM** (P14) — a hazy horizon with small FPV drones diving from above; the tank below
  with its sensors and an interceptor / jammer answering. The race continues. No people harmed.

Reader footer device: a small persistent **"IRON TRIANGLE" strip** — FIREPOWER · PROTECTION · MOBILITY —
that highlights the corner a given page is about (echoes the turret book's KILL-CHAIN strip and Name of the
Rose's prayer-hours strip). Pages map: P4/P5/P6 = FIREPOWER, P7/P8 = PROTECTION, P9 = MOBILITY, the synthesis
pages (P3/P12) light all three. Decide final mapping at reader build.
