- Bottom band: `Hit hard. Survive being hit. Move fast. More gun and more armor make it heavier — and heavier makes it slower. You cannot win all three. Every tank is a careful compromise.`

## P5 — POSTER (TWO WAYS TO KILL ARMOR)
Subjects: two labeled diagrams side by side — the dart and the jet.

- Title band, top: `TWO WAYS THROUGH STEEL`

## P6 — CINEMATIC (FIRE CONTROL: HIT WHILE MOVING)
Subjects: interior — the gunner's thermal sight with a reticle on a distant tank; Marko's hands.
Scene: cramped turret interior, green-amber glow. A thermal screen shows a far-off vehicle inside a bright
reticle with range numbers; the gunner's hands on the controls. The point: hitting while bouncing.
LETTERING:
- Top band: `Hitting something far away while you bounce over rough ground sounds impossible. A computer makes it routine.`
- Label: `LASER — measures the exact range in a blink`
- Label: `THERMAL SIGHT — sees heat, so it works in the dark`
- Label: `STABILIZER — holds the gun steady while the tank pitches`
- Bottom band: `The computer redoes the aiming math more than a hundred times a second, leading the shot so the tank can fire on the move and still hit.`

## P7 — POSTER (PROTECTION: ARMOR)
Subjects: three labeled armor diagrams.
Scene: dark schematic plate. Three small painted cross-sections: (1) a sloped plate with a round
glancing off, (2) a layered sandwich block, (3) an outer plate spaced away from the hull. Amber leader lines.
LETTERING:
- Title band, top: `THREE TRICKS OF ARMOR`
- Label: `SLOPE IT — a tilted plate is thicker to a shot, and can bounce it off`
- Label: `LAYER IT — steel and ceramic sandwiched together break up the metal jet`
- Label: `SPACE IT — an outer plate makes a jet go off early, before the real armor`
- Bottom band: `Modern armor is not just thick — it is clever. The trick is more protection without adding crushing weight.`

## P9 — CINEMATIC (MOBILITY: ENGINE + TRACKS)
Subjects: the tank crossing deep mud, close on the churning tracks.
Scene: low angle, the heavy tank powering through thick mud and a shallow trench, tracks throwing up earth,

## QUIZ (build into reader — WHY questions, length-matched distractors, shuffled answers)
Drafted in the reader-build step per CRITICAL QUIZ RULE. Five questions covering: why a tank is always a
compromise (iron triangle / can't max all three), why armor is sloped (more effective thickness + deflect,
no extra weight), why the gun leads/uses a computer (hit a moving target while moving), why tracks instead
of wheels (spread weight / low ground pressure across mud), why cheap drones threaten tanks (cost asymmetry
+ thin roof / top attack). End interstitial line: `THE RACE GOES ON`.
