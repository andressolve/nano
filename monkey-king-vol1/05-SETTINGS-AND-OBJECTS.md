# Settings and Objects

**World palette:** ink line with mineral-pigment washes, the blue-green
landscape tradition lit like a film. Malachite green and azurite blue for
the living world, cinnabar red for the monkey's things, gold leaf for
Heaven. Each location is a variation on that, except the three that must
look like a different world: the sea floor (azurite and crystal, no green),
Laozi's furnace (cinnabar and black, no blue), and the edge of the world
(white, and nothing else).

Each location names one restrained accent that does dramatic work.

---

## Locations

### 1 · Flower-Fruit Mountain — the peak and the waterfall · pp 1–3, 14, 20, 25–26, 28, 46–47
A green mountain rising out of an eastern sea, fruit trees down its flanks,
a bare stone peak at the top and a waterfall roaring off a cliff into a pool.
Malachite and mist, warm morning light. The place says: nobody owns this, and
it is enough. On pp 46–47 it has a new neighbor, a bare five-ridged mountain
where nothing grows.
**Accent:** the split stone on the peak, which stays split for the whole book.

### 2 · The Water Curtain Cave · pp 4–6
Behind the waterfall: an iron bridge over the pool, and past it a dry stone
house with stone beds, stone bowls, stone stools, a stone hearth, all cut
long ago by nobody the monkeys know. Blue-green light through the water, then
warm firelight inside. The place says: a home that was waiting.
**Accent:** the wall of falling water seen from inside, silver, always moving.

### 3 · The Master's mountain — the Cave of the Slanting Moon · pp 8–13
Far to the west: a courtyard of swept white gravel before a cave mouth in a
gray cliff, a single pine, a bell. Pale, cool, dawn and midnight only. The
place says: nothing here is decorative, everything here is a lesson.
**Accent:** the back door, a small round door in the cliff, shut on every
page but one.

### 4 · The floor of the Eastern Sea — Ao Guang's crystal palace · pp 15–18
Down through green water into blue: a palace of crystal and coral, columns of
white shell, a floor of sand lit from above, fish drifting through the hall.
Azurite and crystal, no green at all. The place says: this is old, this is
rich, and none of it is for you.
**Accent:** the pillar, glowing dull red at the back of the treasury, the
only warm thing in the sea.

### 5 · The South Gate of Heaven · p 21
A gate the size of a city, gold on white cloud, guards the size of towers on
either side, and the palaces of Heaven going up behind it forever. Gold leaf
and cloud-white, noon light from everywhere. The place says: you are small.
**Accent:** the gate is open. Nobody stopped him.

### 6 · The Hall of Jade — the Emperor's throne room and banquet hall · pp 19, 22, 27, 32, 34, 39, 42–43, 45
One enormous hall, and every scene in Heaven that is not a garden or a stable
happens in it. A raised throne at the far end, jade-green pillars, a floor
like still water, and for the banquet the same hall set with long tables.
Pale gold and jade. The place says: decisions are made here, slowly, by
people who do not have to hurry. On pp 41–42 it is broken.
**Accent:** the throne's steps. Who is on which step is the whole politics
of the book.

### 7 · The Heavenly stables · pp 22–24
A long stable of white wood under a cloud roof, a thousand horses of every
colour, a small desk by the door with a ledger on it. Warm hay light. The
place says: honest work, and nobody watching.
**Accent:** the desk. He breaks it on p 24.

### 8 · The peach garden · pp 29–31
Terraces of peach trees on a hillside in Heaven, three orchards in three
colours of blossom, fruit the size of a monkey's head. Pink, green, gold;
late-afternoon light. The place says: this took three thousand years.
**Accent:** peach stones on the ground, more each page.

### 9 · Laozi's laboratory and the furnace · pp 33, 40–41
A round room of black iron and cinnabar red, shelves of gourds and jars, and
at the center the Eight-Trigrams furnace, a bronze vessel taller than a man
with a lid like a temple roof. Firelight only. The place says: things are
made here and unmade here.
**Accent:** the five gourds on the highest shelf, and then the empty shelf.

### 10 · The sky over Flower-Fruit Mountain — the battlefield · pp 26, 34–38
The mountain seen from above, ringed by cloud, and the cloud full of Heaven's
army. Storm light, gold armor against slate. The place says: they brought
everyone.
**Accent:** the banner on the peak, GREAT SAGE EQUAL TO HEAVEN, visible in
every fight.

### 11 · The edge of the world — the five pillars · p 44
White. A flat white nothing, and five pink columns rising out of it into a
white sky, tapering, taller than sight. No line, no texture, no horizon.
The place says: this is as far as anything goes.
**Accent:** the writing, red, on the middle pillar.

### 12 · The Five-Elements Mountain · pp 46–47
Location 1, changed: a new mountain of five bare ridges, like the fingers of
a hand pressed down, with a monkey's face in a cleft at its base and a
golden paper seal on its summit. Snow in the last page.
**Accent:** the seal on the summit, gold, the only writing in the landscape.
