## The specific failure this volume must avoid

**The catalogue of powers and the court of clerks.**

The source is an episode reel: a trick is learned, a realm is raided, a rank is
granted, a fight is won, repeat. Adapted faithfully it becomes two dead
registers at once. On Earth it becomes a *catalogue* — narration listing what
Wukong can now do (seventy-two shapes, the somersault, the hair clones, the
staff's weights and names), which `00-CRAFT-MANDATE.md` §6 forbids outright.
In Heaven it becomes a *court of clerks* — ministers reciting ranks, offices,
banquet seating, and who reports to whom, which is logistics and never drama.

The countermeasure is fixed here and enforced at the script gate:

- **Three tricks, no more.** The cloud somersault, the shape-change, and the
  staff. Each is *learned or taken* in one scene with a cost, and *paid off*
  once at a turn the reader has been waiting for. Nothing else Wukong does in
  the source is a named ability in this book.
- **Heaven speaks through two faces.** The Jade Emperor wants quiet; Laozi
  believes every problem can be managed. Every decision Heaven makes is one of
  those two people wanting something, on the page, in a room. No ministers.
- **Wukong is never above it.** He is loud, greedy, hurt, delighted, and
  insulted, on every page he appears. The moment he reads as serene, the
  engine is gone. The wound under the noise — *you are a rock, you are nobody,
  you were not invited* — must be visible at least once per movement.
