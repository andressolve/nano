## PAGE 40 — the furnace sentence

### Reader turn
Laozi tells the chained monkey the furnace will give back the peaches and the elixir, Wukong says he is made of a rock, and the Emperor says do it.

### Ordered moments
1. Top panel, dominant, about 50% of the page, the largest image on the page by far, close and tall. Laozi has come down to the monkey's level, the two faces filling the panel, quiet, the tray of five empty gourds held in exactly his two hands, no dabbing, no smile. Wukong left, in chains, his two chained hands at his front, silent, mouth closed, no balloon. Laozi right, mouth visible. Exactly two balloons, both Laozi's, stacked on the right beside his mouth, the furnace line above, the peaches line below, both tails to Laozi.
2. Middle strip, about 20%. Wukong alone, close, looking straight ahead at Laozi beyond the frame, flat. One balloon beside his mouth, tail to Wukong.
3. Bottom panel, about 30%. The Emperor seated on the throne at the right, two fingers to his temple, eyes shut. Laozi at the left edge, looking up at him, mouth visible. Exactly two balloons: Laozi's `Then we'll get a rock…` line upper left beside Laozi's mouth, tail to Laozi, its only appearance on the page; the Emperor's answer clearly lower, right, beside the Emperor's mouth, tail to the Emperor.

### Exact strings
LAOZI: `My furnace burns for forty-nine days. Whatever goes in comes out as what it was made of.`
LAOZI: `You are made of my top peaches, that nobody was ever allowed, and my elixir. I want them back.`
WUKONG: `I'm made of a rock.`
LAOZI: `Then we'll get a rock, and I'll get my pills back. Majesty?`
JADE EMPEROR: `Do it. And Laozi. This time it works.`
Render exactly these 5 strings once each, in this order, with clear ownership; no other text anywhere on the page.
