## Mandatory findings

1. The location palette and illumination are wrong for the South Gate of Heaven. Saturated azurite navy and malachite/teal masses dominate the sky and make the plate read as a blue-green living-world landscape, while the shadowed tower sides and gate leaves imply directional light. Redesign the atmospheric field as shell-white and cloud-white banks with pale-gold edges, reducing blue-green to only faint cool shadow traces; light both tower faces and the lintel in the same high-key, everywhere-noon value range. Preserve the fully open central portal as the primary marker and the single deepest spatial value, with the gold-leaf gate against white cloud remaining the dominant thumbnail read.
