## Mandatory findings

1. Required view (a) is missing. The first figure is another full-length action pose with the habitual shading gesture, not the mandated three-quarter head-and-shoulders neutral view. Replace it with a true three-quarter head-and-shoulders study in even light, expression at rest and about to laugh, large enough to lock the short heart-shaped face, low brow, wide-set round amber eyes, wide mouth, small projecting ears, golden ruff, pale muzzle and cheek patches, and needle behind the ear. Keep the habitual shading gesture in a full-body view rather than using it to obscure the brow and face lock in view (a).

2. View (c) does not supply the required strict profile of the whole design: the head turns to profile, but the torso, shoulder mass, hips, and legs remain a rear three-quarter view. Replace the third figure with a true ninety-degree armored profile from brow through boots, preserving the short forward muzzle, low brow, projecting ear, circular golden ruff, visible needle, small wiry build, long arm, long tail, non-square stance, and two separately readable back-curving plumes. This profile must establish the body silhouette as well as the face.

3. The blue-green mountain scene and repeated rock plinths turn the neutral lock sheet into a setting reference that can follow Wukong across the book. Remove the mountain scenery and the rock supports from views (a)–(c), leaving plain warm paper. Repose the full-length armored view in a clean crouch or mid-leap so the required wiry, never-square posture reads without an invented support; retain only the explicitly required split stone under the raised foot in stone-born view (d).
