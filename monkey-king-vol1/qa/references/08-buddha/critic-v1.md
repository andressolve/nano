## Mandatory findings

1. The sheet has blocking internal borders: hard vertical divisions isolate the close-up, full-body, and profile compartments, and a hard horizontal division frames the hand view. Rebuild all four views on one uninterrupted plain warm-paper field; remove every vertical and horizontal dividing edge, architectural frame, and scenic compartment so no view is boxed or bordered.

2. The three-quarter close-up has a smooth gold semicircular disk behind the dark curls. It reads as an unlisted halo or crown ornament, breaks the required hair-mass silhouette, and is inconsistent with the other views. Remove the gold disk completely. Carry tight dark curls continuously from the hairline over the entire cranium, rising only into one small dark curled dome at the crown, with the same dome height and outline in the three-quarter, full-body, and strict-profile views. No halo, smooth gold cap, crown, or other head ornament may remain.

3. The full-body view lowers one open hand toward the floor in an earth-touching gesture. That violates the lock that the Buddha does not gesture and conflicts with the specified still seated pose. Place the visible open hand palm-up, resting without reach or directional action on the knee; keep the shoulders level and the torso motionless. The isolated hand view may retain its slightly curled palm-up fingers.
