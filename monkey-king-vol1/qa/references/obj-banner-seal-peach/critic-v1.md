## Mandatory findings

1. The plate is not on plain warm paper: blue-green mountains, shore, and water occupy both sides and pass behind the objects. This destroys the required neutral isolation and risks propagating a single Flower-Fruit-like location behind the banner, seal, and peach even though they recur in different settings and states. Remove every mountain, shoreline, water, mist, and cast scenic wash; place the same three objects on one uninterrupted plain warm-paper ground, with pigment confined to the object silhouettes and clean negative space completely surrounding each object.

## Nonblocking observations

- The canvas is landscape and contains the required three objects without figures, lettering, labels, watermarks, borders, or a visible support pole.
- The banner is cinnabar-red, whole, unfaded, wind-filled, and has a continuous outer contour.
- The gold-leaf strip is glyph-free, visibly taller than the required 4:1 proportion, and ends in a slight paper curl.
- The peach reads as a single large fruit with one clear cleft.
- The three object silhouettes remain mutually distinct at thumbnail and in grayscale; no four-view character requirement applies to this object plate.
