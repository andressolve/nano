## Mandatory findings

1. The beard violates Laozi's short-square lock and his Never prohibition against Subhuti's narrow pointed beard: views (a), (b), and especially (c) taper into a rounded or pointed goatee, and the geometry changes between views. Redraw the same beard in all four views as a short, jaw-width white block ending no more than two finger-widths below the chin, with vertical sides and a blunt horizontal lower edge; no central taper, point, or dangling profile tuft.
2. The primary gourd marker is not singular or stable: views (b) and (d) show two separately corded round vessels, while view (c) obscures the count. Replace them with one unmistakable dried bottle gourd on one cord at the same hip, with the same two-lobed silhouette, scale, and attachment in every view where the hip is visible. No second vessel may remain to propagate as a recurring costume prop.
3. The default face in view (a) is openly laughing, not the locked warm, worried, one-step-ahead resting expression. Close the tooth-showing mouth into a small closed worried half-smile and raise the inner ends of the short white brows slightly while keeping the small bright eyes alert; retain view (d) as the fully unsmiling reaction state.
