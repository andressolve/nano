## Mandatory findings

1. The banner is in the wrong consequential state: its trailing half has a deep missing wedge and resolves into two separated tails, reading as a split or torn battle flag. Fill the entire wedge, join the tails into one broad cloth body, and redraw one continuous, unbroken wind-pulled outer hem from upper edge through trailing edge to lower edge. Preserve saturated cinnabar across the repaired area and leave no notch, split, tear, hole, fray, or missing corner.

2. The miniature staff is not a uniform reduction of the full-length master: its gold end sections remain nearly full-state length relative to its shorter red shaft, so it reads as an independently designed short baton, and it does not carry the prescribed single visible red tip. Replace it with one uniformly scaled copy of the complete full-length cylinder, preserving exactly the same shaft-to-cap and band positions, parallel constant-diameter shaft, and two blunt ends at every proportion; reduce the whole object as one transform and stage the needle state so one red tip remains visibly legible. Do not introduce a collar, point, finial, or independently enlarged end hardware.

3. The gold seal marker is too broad and reads as a thick rigid plaque: its visible proportion is under 3:1, with hard side edges, a substantial cast shadow, and raised-looking mottling. Narrow it to a glyph-free vertical strip at least four times taller than wide, remove the beveled side and every raised rim or relief cue, flatten the surface to paper-thin gold leaf, and use only a feather-light local shadow or one slight paper curl to show flexibility.

## Nonblocking observations

- The plate has no figures, lettering, labels, watermark, borders, landscape, horizon, or scenery.
- Exactly five gourds are independently countable, retain clear neck-and-bulb silhouettes, and stay legible as five at reduced scale.
- The dull iron ring and peach are isolated and readable; the peach has a clear cleft, a softly unequal contour, and a useful scale relationship to the staff shaft.
- The warm-paper ground, mineral-ink treatment, cinnabar staff and banner, dull iron, gold leaf, and restrained local shadows otherwise fit the specified register and object palette.
