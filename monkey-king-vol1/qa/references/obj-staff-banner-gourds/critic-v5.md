## Mandatory findings

1. The banner's trailing edge has a deep inward notch that divides it into two pointed tails. This is a damaged or swallow-tailed state, not the required intact banner, and it would falsely prefigure the faded p47 state. Rebuild the cloth with one continuous, unbroken wind-pulled outer hem from the upper edge around the trailing edge to the lower edge: fill the missing wedge completely, join the two tails into one broad cloth body, and leave no notch, split, tear, hole, fray, or missing corner. Keep the entire cloth saturated cinnabar red.

2. The full-length staff is approximately column-thick, with a shaft-to-length proportion far heavier than the approved staffs in sheets 01 and 09, while the miniature uses a different apparent cap-and-band proportion. Rebuild both states from one master staff silhouette copied from the approved sheets: a constant-diameter red shaft about 1/35–1/40 of its total length, parallel from cap to cap, with blunt gold end caps and bands in the same relative positions. Produce the miniature by uniformly reducing that complete master geometry, retaining the prescribed single visible red tip; do not independently redesign or simplify either end.

3. The banner support is a second red-and-gold weapon-like upright with a pointed ornamental finial, so at thumbnail it competes with Wukong's staff and approaches Erlang's lance silhouette. Replace it with one plain, slender, neutral dark support of constant diameter, with blunt unornamented ends and no gold bands, collars, blade, barb, spear point, or finial. Keep it clearly separate from the staff states.

4. The gold seal marker reads as a rigid, dark-edged gold plaque rather than a flat paper strip. Rebuild it as a paper-thin vertical gold-leaf strip at least four times taller than wide, with no beveled side, raised rim, hard slab thickness, relief, or glyph-like mottling; use only a feather-light local shadow or slight paper curl to establish that it is flexible paper rather than metal.

## Nonblocking observations

- The plate is figure-free and uses one uninterrupted warm-paper ground with restrained local shadows. It contains no lettering, labels, watermark, border, horizon, or location scenery.
- Exactly five dried bottle gourds are independently countable, retain clear pinched-neck silhouettes, and remain separable in grayscale and at thumbnail.
- The isolated iron ring is closed, dull, and bracelet-like. The peach has a clear cleft and an asymmetric organic outline.
- The four-view, costume-free face, strict-profile, and reaction-expression tests are not applicable to an object plate. Their object equivalents were applied: isolated silhouette, thumbnail, grayscale, nearest-prop opposition, state continuity, and re-entry after a long absence.
