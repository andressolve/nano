## Mandatory findings

1. The staff lock is structurally wrong and the needle state is missing. Remove the banner, ties, spear point, red bead, and ornamental finial from the staff. Rebuild it as an isolated straight red-lacquered iron cylinder with the same blunt gold-capped ends and gold band spacing carried by 01-wukong and 09-wukong-tempered. Add a second, separately isolated miniature state of that same staff as a slender needle with one clearly visible red tip; both states must remain distinguishable from Erlang's pointed lance in grayscale and at thumbnail.

2. The banner is already in a damaged late state: its fly edge breaks into multiple deep torn tongues. Rebuild it as a separate banner on its own plain support, with one continuous clean outer cloth contour, no notches, holes, split streamers, fraying, fading, or writing. Keep a full, wind-filled cinnabar field so the faded p47 state can be introduced only on the page.

3. The golden seal is a broad squat rectangle, which does not preserve the consequential summit marker established by the Five-Elements setting lock. Rebuild it as one thin vertical gold-leaf paper strip, at least three times taller than it is wide, with a flat paper silhouette, no glyphs, and enough warm-paper clearance around it to remain a narrow strip at thumbnail.

4. The peach reads as an ordinary apple-sized fruit beside the gourds and staff. Increase its diameter to approximately a bare Wukong head width relative to the approved staff shaft, and give it one clear peach cleft and softly asymmetric peach contour; keep it isolated so its oversized head-scale silhouette survives grayscale and re-entry on p47.
