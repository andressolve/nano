## Mandatory findings

1. The miniature staff is structurally a staff-to-spear hybrid: a thick red shaft steps down into a long exposed silver-gray blade or spike and terminates in an enlarged pointed red end. Rebuild the entire miniature state as a uniformly scaled-down version of the same uninterrupted straight red-lacquered cylinder established by the full-length staff; remove the silver-gray extension, the stepped spear shoulder, and the enlarged lance-head silhouette, while retaining one clearly visible small red tip with no separate blade, barb, or ornamental point. At thumbnail and in grayscale, it must read as a pin-thin collapsed staff rather than Erlang's pointed lance.

## Nonblocking observations

- The plate is figure-free and has no lettering, labels, watermark, framing border, landscape, horizon, or location scenery.
- The full-length staff is straight and uninterrupted between blunt gold-capped ends, with no intermediate collar, banner, ties, finial, or spear point.
- The banner is a separate, intact, fully unfurled cinnabar field with a continuous clean contour, no writing, and no premature fading or damage.
- Exactly five independently countable dried bottle gourds are present; their outer contours remain separated at thumbnail and in grayscale.
- The dull iron bracelet-sized ring, the isolated glyph-free vertical gold paper strip, and the oversized cleft, softly asymmetric peach are all present in the required states.
- The uninterrupted warm-paper ground, restrained cast shadows, mineral-pigment treatment, cinnabar, gold, dried-gourd ochre, and dull iron are consistent with the system palette and the objects' source locations.
