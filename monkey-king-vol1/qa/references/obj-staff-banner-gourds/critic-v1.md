## Mandatory findings

1. The plate shows eight dried gourds, but the locked consequential state is exactly five. Redesign the gourd group to contain exactly five independently countable dried bottle gourds: remove three, give each a clearly pinched neck-and-bulb silhouette, and separate their outer contours so no overlap can make the count read as four or six at thumbnail or in grayscale.

2. The banner is collapsed on the ground, heavily torn into long streamers, and already weathered. That is the wrong default state for an object first seen raised and then flying through every fight. Redesign the primary banner marker as one intact cinnabar-red cloth field held fully unfurled in a wind-filled flying silhouette, with a clean outer edge, no tears, no fading, and no writing; keep it isolated from terrain so later page art can age it only for the p47 state.

3. The object plate is staged on a detailed mountain overlook with pines, peaks, horizon, and stone terrain instead of the specified plain-paper field. Remove the entire landscape, horizon, trees, mountains, mist, and stone ledge. Recompose only the listed objects on one uninterrupted plain warm-paper ground, retaining restrained local cast shadows but introducing no location scenery or additional prop.
