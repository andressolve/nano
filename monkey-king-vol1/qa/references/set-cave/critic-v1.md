## Mandatory findings

1. Replace every freestanding brown wooden stool or bench with a monolithic stool cut from the same gray cave stone as the beds and platforms. Remove all wood color, grain, thin timber legs, and joinery so the furnishings read as the ancient stone beds, stone bowls, and stone stools required by the location lock.
2. Remove the metallic gold-leaf patches scattered across the ceiling and right-hand vault. Rebuild those surfaces as charcoal stone with malachite and azurite mineral washes under cool blue-green water light; confine the only warm color to the orange-cinnabar fire and its short local spill inside the hearth, leaving the silver falling-water wall as the location's restrained accent.
