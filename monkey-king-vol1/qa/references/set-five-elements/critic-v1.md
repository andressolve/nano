## Mandatory findings

1. The hard horizontal seam between the green and snow states is a blocking border. Redesign the sheet as two unframed landscape vignettes on one continuous warm-paper ground, with open or feathered space separating the states and no ruled, full-width, or edge-to-edge dividing line.

2. The Five-Elements Mountain is required to be a new mountain of five **bare** ridges where nothing grows, but the upper state is densely green and the snow state retains numerous conifers. Remove every tree, shrub, and green groundcover from the five-ridge hand-mountain in both states; retain exposed stone in the first state and snow directly on exposed stone in the last-page state.

3. The manifest requires the hand-mountain **beside the peak**, and the setting ledger defines it as Location 1 changed. Recompose both states to include a separately readable Flower-Fruit peak beside the new five-ridge mass, preserving Flower-Fruit's bare summit split stone and waterfall so returning readers can identify the old location after the long absence.

4. The required cleft at the base of the hand-mountain is not structurally readable. Cut one deep, dark, face-sized cleft into the base of the central ridge, with enough open rock around it to stage Wukong there on the page; keep the reference plate itself figure-free.

5. The two long cinnabar bands crossing the sea are an unlisted, high-salience recurring object and displace the location's restrained accent. Remove both red bands completely from both states; no bridge, ribbon, banner, causeway, or red wake may cross the water on this plate.

6. The summit marker reads as a thick freestanding gold square rather than a golden paper seal. Replace it with one thin, vertical gold-leaf paper strip visibly affixed flat to the summit rock, narrow enough to read as paper rather than architecture and with no legible glyphs on the reference plate.

## Nonblocking observations

- The ink line, paper tooth, pigment pooling, and mineral-wash treatment fit the required Mineral Ink register.
- The living-state malachite and azurite palette and the warm daylight are broadly appropriate once the hand-mountain's prohibited vegetation is removed.
- The five-ridge hand silhouette remains legible at thumbnail and in grayscale, and the snow state usefully provides the required last-page condition.
- The plate contains no figures, labels, watermark, or legible lettering.
