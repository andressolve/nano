## Mandatory findings

1. Remove the entire blue-green mountain landscape, pine, water, mist, and every scenic mark. Rebuild the plate on one uninterrupted plain warm-paper ground with only the full-length staff and its needle state; no location imagery may remain to propagate as part of the object's lock.

2. Rebuild the full-length staff from the approved 01 and 09 geometry: one straight constant-diameter red-lacquered iron cylinder, two blunt gold end caps, and only the flush gold bands at the same relative positions as the approved carried staff. Delete all three oversized raised mid-shaft collars and all bulbous, stepped, or projecting end rings so there is no third staff design.

3. Rebuild the lower state as a true uniform miniature of that same straight cylinder, isolated with clear space: keep both ends blunt, remove the dark tapered projection and any doubled line that reads as a point or blade, and retain exactly one small visible cinnabar-red tip. It must read as a needle-sized staff rather than a pin, spear, or separate gold object.

## Nonblocking observations

- The required full-length and needle states are both present and spatially separated.
- No figures, lettering, labels, watermark, or panel borders are visible.
- Cinnabar red and gold are legible in color, and the full-size staff remains a strong horizontal silhouette in grayscale and at thumbnail size.
