## Blind read
An armored crowned monkey owns the page alone. He enters a round gourd-filled chamber holding a jar, decides that five gourds contain sweets, and eats their glowing contents by the handful. The turn comes when he realizes that he has consumed elixir rather than sweets: his earlier giddy movement gives way to a clear-eyed, seated stillness as he studies his faintly glowing hands and says that he feels wonderful.

## Findings

### Finding C2
- Observation: The sober aftermath panel at the bottom is unmistakably the largest panel; the shelf-feast panel with the pouring gold pills is smaller and therefore does not own the page.
- Material reader harm: The accidental consumption of the five gourds is the causal dramatic turn, but the page gives greater visual weight to the reaction after it, so the required feast beat does not land as the dominant reader event.
- Redraw justification: Correcting the blocking hierarchy requires reallocating the page’s major panel emphasis, a structural change substantial enough to require replacing the complete page.
