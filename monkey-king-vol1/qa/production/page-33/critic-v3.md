## Blind read
An armored monkey enters a storeroom full of gourds, recognizes it as Laozi's place, and decides to sample what he assumes are sweets and take five gourds for Old Ma. The middle panel unmistakably owns the page: he pours the contents of a gourd into his hand and eats them. The turn lands in the bottom panel when, surrounded by emptied gourds, he realizes the contents were Laozi's elixir rather than sweets and feels wonderful.

## Findings

### Finding C7
- Observation: In the dominant middle panel, Wukong has three visible hands: one resting on the pouring gourd, one cupped beneath the falling pills, and one at his mouth. The hand on the gourd is an extra, duplicated focal hand.
- Material reader harm: The impossible anatomy sits at the center of the pour-and-eat action and materially disrupts the visual clarity and integrity of the accident that owns the page.
- Redraw justification: This is a focal hand duplication expressly covered by C7, not a background or cosmetic flaw; it compromises the dominant reader event enough to justify replacing the complete page.
