VERDICT: REVISE

## Blind read
The middle panel unmistakably owns the page: the monkey warrior pours a gourdful of small golden pellets into his palm and eats them. He first enters a storeroom, identifies it as Laozi's place, and decides to investigate what “the peach man” keeps. He mistakes the contents of the gourds for sweets that Old Ma would enjoy and consumes the contents of five gourds. The turn comes when he realizes they were Laozi's elixir rather than sweets; the theft/mistake changes into magical consequence as he says he feels wonderful.

## Findings

### Finding C7
- Observation: In the dominant panel, Wukong has three visible hands: one rests on and controls the pouring gourd, one is cupped beneath the falling pills, and a third is held at his mouth. The focal hand anatomy is duplicated.
- Material reader harm: The anatomical duplication makes the central action—pouring the pills into his palm and eating them by the handful—visibly incoherent at the exact dramatic turn.
- Redraw justification: C7 expressly makes a duplicated focal hand blocking, and the defect occupies the page-owning action rather than a background or cosmetic detail, so its harm is substantial enough to risk replacing the complete page.
