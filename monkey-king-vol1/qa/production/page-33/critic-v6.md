## Findings

### Finding C3
- Observation: Only four empty gourds are visibly countable on the floor in the last panel: two at left, one at bottom center, and one at right. The tied red jar is a separate object, not a fifth gourd.
- Material reader harm: The last panel contradicts Wukong’s “All five gourds” realization and does not show the consequential aftermath of consuming all five, so the accidental total consumption at the center of the turn does not fully land.
- Redraw justification: C3 explicitly makes four gourds blocking; this is a consequential object-state and story-count failure in the payoff panel, substantial enough to risk replacing the complete page.
