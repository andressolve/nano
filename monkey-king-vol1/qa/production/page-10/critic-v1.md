VERDICT: REVISE

## Blind read
In the courtyard, Subhuti questions Wukong, rejects his answer, and taps him three times while the students laugh. The center tapping panel owns the page. Subhuti causes the turn, and Wukong owns the final change by understanding the taps, Subhuti's clasped hands, and his departure as a secret instruction to come to the back door at midnight.

## Visible text
WUKONG: Everything you said, Master. Every word. But not the thing I came for.

SUBHUTI: Wukong. Seven years. What have you learned?

SOUND: TAP TAP TAP

SUBHUTI: Then you have learned nothing.

WUKONG: Three taps. Hands behind his back. He means: midnight, the third watch, and come in by the back door.

## Findings

### Finding C3
- Observation: In Panel 1, Wukong's reply is in the far-left speech balloon and Subhuti's question is in a balloon to its right, so the reply reads before the question.
- Material reader harm: The required question-and-answer exchange is reversed at the page's opening, making Wukong appear to answer before Subhuti asks and breaking the scripted causal reading order.
- Redraw justification: A reply readable before the line that prompts it is explicitly blocking, and the reader must reconstruct the opening exchange rather than receive it in sequence; preserving this reversal would materially damage the dramatic setup despite the risk of replacing the page.
