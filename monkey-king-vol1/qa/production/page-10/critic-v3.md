## Blind read
Subhuti asks Wukong what he has learned after seven years, and Wukong says he learned every word but not the thing he came for. The dominant middle image is owned by Subhuti standing over the seated Wukong and tapping him on the head three times with the whisk handle while the students laugh behind their hands. The apparent rebuke changes into a secret invitation when Subhuti walks away with his hands behind his back and Wukong alone decodes the taps and gesture.

## Visible text
SUBHUTI: Wukong. Seven years. What have you learned?

WUKONG: Everything you said, Master. Every word. But not the thing I came for.

SOUND (on Wukong's head): TAP TAP TAP

SUBHUTI: Then you have learned nothing.

WUKONG: Three taps. Hands behind his back. He means: midnight, the third watch, and come in by the back door.
