VERDICT: REVISE

## Blind read
Subhuti's public test and three taps own the page: Wukong says he learned every word but not what he came for, Subhuti questions him, taps his head three times, and declares that he has learned nothing while the students laugh. The apparent rejection changes into a private success when Subhuti walks away and Wukong alone decodes the taps and the Master's hands-behind-back posture as instructions to come to the back door at midnight in the third watch.

## Visible text
WUKONG: Everything you said, Master. Every word. But not the thing I came for.

SUBHUTI: Wukong. Seven years. What have you learned?

SOUND: TAP TAP TAP

SUBHUTI: Then you have learned nothing.

WUKONG: Three taps. Hands behind his back. He means: midnight, the third watch, and come in by the back door.

## Findings

### Finding C3
- Observation: In the top row, Wukong's answer is in the left image and Subhuti's question is in the right image, so the answer reads before the question that prompts it.
- Material reader harm: The exchange arrives in reverse causal order, making Wukong appear to answer an unseen question before Subhuti asks it and breaking the scripted setup of the public test.
- Redraw justification: This is a blocking reversal of the page's initiating dialogue rather than a cosmetic or geometric issue; restoring the required question-then-answer sequence is substantial enough to risk replacing the complete page.
