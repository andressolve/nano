## Blind read
The golden monkey owns the page through his action. He touches the towering black pillar, discovers that it is warm and responsive, and commands it to shrink. The dominant middle panel shows the smaller pillar amid a cracking, rumbling sea floor while the dragon recoils; the turn is the pillar obeying the monkey. The monkey then calmly claims a staff while the dragon confronts him with the consequence.

## Findings

### Finding C4
- Observation: The last panel shows the monkey raising an apparently empty finger; no needle is visibly held between finger and thumb or tucked behind his ear. The last clearly visible form of the object is still the upright banded rod in the preceding panel.
- Material reader harm: The sea anchor never visibly completes its transformation into the tiny staff the monkey can carry, so the page’s consequential object-state payoff and his final claim do not land.
- Redraw justification: This is the focal magical transformation and the page-ending continuity state, not a tiny-prop detail; leaving it unreadable loses the central event strongly enough to warrant replacing the page.
