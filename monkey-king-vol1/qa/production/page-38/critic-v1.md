## Blind read
The monkey warrior owns the page. He charges toward an armored spearman and a black dog while an old man in a cloud offers up a dark metal bracelet. The turn is the bracelet becoming a huge ring and crashing down onto the monkey warrior, knocking away his staff and leaving him pinned, surrounded, and chained while the armored man disclaims credit for the decisive blow.

## Findings

### Finding C2
- Observation: The Laozi-and-cloud opening is visibly the largest panel, while the huge ring’s strike is confined to a shorter middle strip and does not unmistakably own the page.
- Material reader harm: The decisive ring-from-behind ambush reads as a transitional beat instead of the dominant dramatic turn that reverses Wukong’s charge and enables the hound and chains.
- Redraw justification: This is the page’s foundational dramatic hierarchy, not a cosmetic or exact-geometry issue; preserving the current composition would materially subordinate the scripted climax, so the harm is substantial enough to risk replacing the complete page.
