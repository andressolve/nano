## Findings

### Finding C1
- Observation: The panel 2 left balloon points to Laozi and begins with the lettered `Laozi.` before his confession, so the Emperor does not own that required address. The `Come down off that...` balloon points to the Jade Emperor rather than Wukong.
- Material reader harm: Wukong's throne-taking challenge reads as the Emperor's speech, while the Emperor's turn toward Laozi reads as Laozi labeling or addressing himself. The page therefore reverses ownership in both focal power exchanges.
- Redraw justification: Correct speaker ownership is essential to the page's dominant dramatic relationship; these attribution failures are substantial enough to risk replacing the complete page.

### Finding C3
- Observation: The visible reading order is `Stable boy...`, then `Laozi.` / `I have nothing left...`, then `Come down off that...`; in the final panel, `Who's the Buddha?` appears before `Then send west...`.
- Material reader harm: Laozi's surrender arrives before Wukong's throne challenge, and Wukong asks about the Buddha before the Emperor first calls for him. Both cause-and-response sequences are broken.
- Redraw justification: The page-turn event depends on the Emperor's decision prompting Wukong's question and Laozi's answer; reversing that causal spine is substantial enough to risk replacing the complete page.
