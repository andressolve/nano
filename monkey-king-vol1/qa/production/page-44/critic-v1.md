## Blind read
The Buddha calmly confronts the Monkey King about why he wants the throne. The Monkey King says it will prove he is not nobody and claims the throne belongs to the strongest. The Buddha turns the dispute into a wager: if the Monkey King can jump out of his hand, the throne is his; if not, he must return to Earth and remain there. The Monkey King accepts with a boast about the distance of one somersault, steps onto the Buddha's palm, and the Buddha tells him to go. The Buddha owns the page through his immense scale, calm control, and outstretched hand; the turn is the conversion of the throne dispute into a concrete, consequential test that the Monkey King accepts.
