VERDICT: REVISE

## Blind read
Laozi arrives above the peak on a cloud and offers Heaven's post to the armored Wukong while Old Ma and the other monkeys watch. The dominant upper image owns the page, with Wukong elevated on the split stone between Laozi and Old Ma. Wukong delights in being summoned and dismisses the nature of the post; Old Ma turns the invitation into a warning about his unfinished promise and a personal plea that he come back. He is leaving again, now because Heaven invited him.

## Visible text
- Laozi, by the balloon tail and placement: "Sun Wukong. I am Laozi, from the Jade Emperor, who rules Heaven. He has heard of you. He is offering you a post."
- Wukong, by the balloon tail and placement: "Heaven has heard of me?"
- No valid speaker attribution; the tail points up and right into empty space below Wukong rather than to Laozi: "All of it."
- Wukong, by the balloon tail and placement: "Heaven, Old Ma. They sent for me. Me. Nobody has ever sent for me."
- Old Ma, by the balloon tail and placement: "What is the post?"
- Wukong, by placement beneath him: "Who cares!"
- Old Ma, by the balloon tail and placement: "You said you'd teach us not to die. You haven't yet."
- Wukong, by the balloon tail and placement: "When I'm back. When I'm a lord."
- Old Ma, by the balloon tail and placement: "Come back. That's the whole thing I want from you. Come back."

No speaker name, label, or tag is separately lettered anywhere on the page; the names visible are part of dialogue.

## Findings

### Finding C3
- Observation: The balloon for Laozi's "All of it." has its tail at the upper right, pointing into empty space below Wukong and away from Laozi on the left.
- Material reader harm: The confirmation does not belong to Laozi by tail or placement, so the first exchange loses its required speaker ownership and no longer reads as Laozi answering Wukong's question.
- Redraw justification: This breaks a required causal exchange in the dominant page-owning image; restoring unambiguous attribution is substantial enough to risk replacing the complete page.
