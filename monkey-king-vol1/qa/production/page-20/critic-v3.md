## Blind read
An elderly heavenly messenger arrives on a cloud and tells the armored monkey hero that Heaven has heard of him and is offering him a post. The hero is astonished and thrilled simply to have been summoned; when his elder asks what the post is, he dismisses the question. The elder reminds him that he has not yet taught the troop how not to die and asks him to come back. The hero owns the page: the summons changes him from leader among his waiting troop into an eager departing court aspirant, and he steps onto the messenger’s cloud promising to return as a lord.
