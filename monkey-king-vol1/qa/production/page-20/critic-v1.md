VERDICT: REVISE

## Blind read
The gold-armored monkey owns the page. After setting out to seek greater wisdom, he asks a group of elder apes to accept him as a disciple. Their apparent leader rejects him as too restless but redirects him east, beyond the sea, to Laozi. The monkey journeys there and renews his request before a blue-robed sage; the turn is rejection becoming a new destination and a second appeal for discipleship.

## Findings

### Finding C1
- Observation: None of the required Page 20 dialogue is visible. Instead, the page contains captions and exchanges about Wukong seeking enlightenment, being rejected by Old Ma, traveling to Laozi, and asking Laozi to accept him as a disciple.
- Material reader harm: The exact owner-controlled script is entirely absent, so Heaven's offer, Wukong's blind acceptance, Old Ma's question about the post, and her final plea to come back cannot be read.
- Redraw justification: Every caption and balloon belongs to a different scene; correcting the complete missing script and its attribution requires replacing the full page.

### Finding C2
- Observation: The dominant image is Wukong overlooking a mountain-and-sea landscape. Laozi is not hovering on a cloud to deliver Heaven's offer, and Old Ma is not present beside the split stone for that invitation.
- Material reader harm: The invitation does not occur, so the page's intended dramatic turn—Wukong accepting Heaven's offer without asking what the post is—does not exist.
- Redraw justification: The page is built around a different journey-and-discipleship event, not the required invitation tableau; restoring the intended owner and turn requires a complete redraw.
