## Blind read
The dominant opening panel unmistakably owns the page: a blue-robed heavenly elder arrives on a cloud and offers the armored monkey a post from Heaven while the old gray monkey and a seated group watch. The armored monkey owns the outward action and is thrilled simply to have been noticed; he does not care what the post is and prepares to leave, promising to return as a lord. The emotional ownership shifts to the old gray monkey when he recalls the armored monkey’s unfulfilled promise to teach them not to die. His touch and repeated request to come back turn the heavenly promotion into a tender, uneasy parting.
