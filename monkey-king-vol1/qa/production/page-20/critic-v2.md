VERDICT: REVISE

## Blind read
The large top panel owns the page. A round, bald old human envoy hovers on a cloud with a scroll before a young golden monkey in armor on a split stone, while an old gray monkey and a crowd watch. The envoy brings an offer from Heaven; the armored monkey is thrilled to have been chosen and starts leaving with him without asking what the post is. The old gray monkey questions the offer, reminds him of his unfinished promise, and changes the emotional stake from the honor of the invitation to whether he will come back.

## Visible text
- Young armored golden monkey: “Heaven has heard of me?”
- Round bald old human envoy on the cloud: “Sun Wukong. I am Laozi, from the Jade Emperor, who rules Heaven. He has heard of you. He is offering you a post.”
- Round bald old human envoy on the cloud: “All of it.”
- Young armored golden monkey: “Heaven, Old Ma. They sent for me. Me. Nobody has ever sent for me.”
- Old gray monkey: “What is the post?”
- Young armored golden monkey: “Who cares!”
- Old gray monkey: “You said you’d teach us not to die. You haven’t yet.”
- Young armored golden monkey: “When I’m back. When I’m a lord.”
- Old gray monkey: “Come back. That’s the whole thing I want from you. Come back.”

The names “Sun Wukong,” “Laozi,” “Jade Emperor,” and “Old Ma” are lettered only as dialogue. No name is lettered as a standalone speaker name, label, or tag, and there is no other extra text.

## Findings

### Finding C3
- Observation: In the dominant panel, Wukong’s upper-left “Heaven has heard of me?” reads before Laozi’s upper-right invitation, although Wukong’s line is the reply to that invitation.
- Material reader harm: The page presents Wukong’s astonished reply before the offer that causes it, reversing the central invitation exchange and making the dominant turn land out of causal order.
- Redraw justification: The reversed prompt-and-reply order affects the page-owning exchange and the reason for Wukong’s departure, so it materially disrupts the scripted reader event enough to justify replacing the page.
