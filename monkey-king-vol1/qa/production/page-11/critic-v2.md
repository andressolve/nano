## Blind read
An elderly master owns the page. At third watch, he meets the monkey disciple at the back door and privately teaches him two means of escaping death: bodily transformation and cloud travel. The secret lesson is the central event. The master's final warning that admiration would make him send the disciple away alone changes the mood from eager instruction to alarmed denial.
