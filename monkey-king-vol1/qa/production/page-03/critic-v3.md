VERDICT: REVISE

## Blind read
At a crowded waterfall pool, an old gray monkey in a red shawl says nobody knows where the water comes from and names kingship as the prize for going through it and returning alive. A young gold monkey proposes looking, confirms the prize, and then plunges into the waterfall. The gold monkey owns the page, and the dare changes into immediate action, although the closing balloon reads as belonging to a brown crowd monkey on the right.

## Visible text
Old gray monkey in the red shawl: "Nobody knows where the water comes from. It has been falling since before I was born, and I am very old."

Young gold monkey: "So somebody should go and look."

Old gray monkey in the red shawl: "Somebody should. Whoever goes through and comes back alive, we make king. That was always the rule. Nobody has ever done it."

Young gold monkey: "King. Of all of you?"

Old gray monkey in the red shawl: "Of all of us."

Right-side brown crowd monkey: "I'll go!"

Sound in the water: "SPLASSSH"

## Findings

### Finding C3
- Observation: In the plunge panel, the "I'll go!" balloon sits over the right-side crowd and its tail points into a brown crowd monkey, away from Wukong's gold streak. It reads as the crowd monkey's line rather than Wukong's.
- Material reader harm: The climactic decision is assigned to a different monkey while Wukong is already plunging, so the page can read as a second monkey volunteering instead of Wukong declaring and immediately carrying out the dare. This breaks Wukong's ownership of the causal turn.
- Redraw justification: The defect changes who chooses the dangerous act at the page's closing beat, which is central to the scripted reader event and substantial enough to risk replacing the complete page.
