## Blind read
At the pool below the waterfall, Old Ma explains that nobody knows where the water comes from and names the standing prize: whoever goes through and returns alive will be king. Wukong, the young golden monkey, owns the page. He confirms that the kingship would be over all the monkeys, then changes the scene by volunteering and leaping into the waterfall as the crowd shrieks.

## Visible text
OLD MA: Nobody knows where the water comes from. It has been falling since before I was born, and I am very old.

WUKONG: So somebody should go and look.

OLD MA: Somebody should. Whoever goes through and comes back alive, we make king. That was always the rule. Nobody has ever done it.

WUKONG: King. Of all of you?

OLD MA: Of all of us.

WUKONG: I’ll go!
