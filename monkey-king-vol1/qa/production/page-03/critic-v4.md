VERDICT: REVISE

## Blind read
The monkey troop gathers around a waterfall. Old Ma explains that no one knows its source and that whoever passes through and returns alive will be made king. Wukong confirms the prize, volunteers, and leaps into the falls. Wukong owns the page; the turn is his move from hearing the dare to committing himself and plunging into the water.

## Visible text
OLD MA: “Nobody knows where the water comes from. It has been falling since before I was born, and I am very old.”

WUKONG: “So somebody should go and look.”

OLD MA: “Somebody should. Whoever goes through and comes back alive, we make king. That was always the rule. Nobody has ever done it.”

OLD MA: “King. Of all of you?”

WUKONG: “Of all of us.”

WUKONG: “I’ll go!”

## Findings

### Finding C1
- Observation: The sound cue visibly reads “SPLASSSHH,” with an extra final H, instead of the required “SPLASSSH.”
- Material reader harm: One of the seven owner-controlled strings is not exact, so the released page would alter the script’s required visible text.
- Redraw justification: Exact text is a release requirement, and the mismatch cannot be accepted without releasing a known script error.

### Finding C3
- Observation: In panel 2, the tail of “King. Of all of you?” points to Old Ma, while “Of all of us.” is visually attributed to Wukong, reversing the required speakers.
- Material reader harm: The exchange makes Old Ma question whether the prize is kingship over everyone and makes Wukong confirm it, reversing who checks the terms and who grants the prize.
- Redraw justification: The reversed ownership changes the causal character exchange at the center of the dare, materially misrepresenting both named characters’ roles.
