## Findings

### Finding C2
- Observation: The bottom chase is visibly the largest image and unmistakably owns the page; the temple with its furred, tufted tail-pole is a smaller setup above it.
- Material reader harm: The chase seizes the hierarchy before the temple disguise and exposed tail can land as the dominant joke and turn, reversing the required emphasis of “the temple and the flagpole; he runs.”
- Redraw justification: C2 explicitly blocks a page where the chase is the largest image. Correcting that dominant panel hierarchy is substantial enough to require replacing the complete page.
