VERDICT: REVISE

## Blind read
The large opening confrontation panel owns the page: a monkey warrior crouches on a peak beneath a blank red banner while facing a three-eyed armored warrior and his dog across the clouds. The armored warrior identifies both men and tests the banner’s claim; the monkey answers with a taunt. In the middle panel the armored warrior laughs that others run, and the monkey says he cannot and will not run. The turn is the armored warrior’s “Good.” and their weapons colliding in the final panel, changing the encounter from verbal challenge to combat.

## Findings

### Finding C1
- Observation: In the middle panel, the balloon containing “I don’t run. I’ve got nowhere to run to. This is it.” is placed beneath Wukong, but its tail points right into the empty space between Wukong and Erlang rather than at Wukong’s mouth.
- Material reader harm: Wukong’s required answer is not owned by the right mouth, so the page does not clearly deliver his refusal to run as his reply to Erlang and the defining exchange loses exact attribution.
- Redraw justification: Exact speaker attribution is a release-gate requirement, and a required line whose tail points at nobody is explicitly blocking; preserving this page would leave the scripted reader event materially unclear.
