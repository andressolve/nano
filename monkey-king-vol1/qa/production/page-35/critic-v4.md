VERDICT: REVISE

## Blind read
The large opening tableau owns the page: a small golden monkey warrior waits beneath a flying red banner on a split peak while a young human warrior in silver armor, accompanied by a gray hound, faces him alone from a cloud with Heaven's army held back as scenery. The human introduces himself and the monkey taunts his third eye; the human laughs. The following exchange presents the monkey as saying that everyone else runs and the human as saying that he does not run, after which their weapons meet and the human says "Good." The turn is from poised introduction and amused challenge to an actual clash.

## Findings

### Finding C3
- Observation: The balloon for "Nobody has ever asked me that. Everyone else just runs." points to Wukong, while the balloon for "I don't run. I've got nowhere to run to. This is it." points to Erlang. The script requires the first line to belong to Erlang and the reply to Wukong.
- Material reader harm: The swapped ownership reverses the central exchange: Wukong appears to claim that others flee him, while Erlang rather than Wukong declares that he will not run. This materially changes the dominant dramatic relationship and removes the scripted reason Erlang laughs and welcomes the fight.
- Redraw justification: Both halves of the page's decisive exchange are owned by the wrong mouths, so the required reader event cannot be recovered by interpretation; correcting this central attribution warrants replacing the page.
