VERDICT: REVISE

## Blind read
Erlang confronts Sun Wukong beneath the red banner, identifies himself, and says he has come to test whether the banner's claim is true. Sun Wukong answers the threat by joking about Erlang's three eyes. Erlang laughs because nobody has reacted that way instead of running; Sun Wukong says he cannot run because this place is all he has. Erlang accepts the answer, and they charge into a staff-versus-trident clash. The large final impact panel unmistakably owns the page, with the confrontation jointly focused on Sun Wukong and Erlang; Sun Wukong's refusal to flee causes the turn from verbal testing to combat.

## Findings

### Finding C2
- Observation: The final staff-and-lance strike, driven by the large radiating impact burst, converging figures, and central sound cue, is the page's strongest image by eye; the calmer meeting panel above does not plainly dominate it.
- Material reader harm: The scripted meeting does not own the page. The reader event lands primarily as the spectacular blow rather than Erlang and Wukong first confronting and recognizing one another as equals, weakening the intended dominant dramatic relationship and turn into combat.
- Redraw justification: This is a page-level hierarchy failure involving the two principal events, not a local or cosmetic defect; correcting which event dominates requires risking the complete redraw.
