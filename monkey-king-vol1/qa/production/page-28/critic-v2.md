## Blind read
The large top panel unmistakably owns the page. A heavenly official, seated on a cloud and reading from a scroll, publicly names Sun Wukong “Great Sage Equal to Heaven” and accepts the words on his banner while Sun Wukong and the mountain monkeys witness it. In the second panel, the elder monkey challenges why Sun Wukong would return to Heaven after already winning a mountain and a name; Sun Wukong answers that he wants Heaven itself to say the name he chose. In the final panel, as Sun Wukong prepares to leave with the official, the elder asks him to return and Sun Wukong promises that he always does. The turn is from public recognition and pride to the personal cost of leaving home, resolved by a quiet promise of return.
