## Blind read
Laozi reads Heaven's proclamation to the gathered troop while Wukong sits on the split stone beneath the flying banner and Old Ma watches below. Wukong owns the page, but the dominant event is Laozi's public reading of the name and title Wukong chose for himself. The public triumph turns into a private parting when Old Ma asks why he will go back, then places her hand on his head and asks him to return; Wukong promises that he always does.

## Visible text
Laozi, reading from the scroll: "By order of the Jade Emperor: Sun Wukong is named Great Sage Equal to Heaven, with a house in Heaven and no duties."

Laozi: "Those are your words, Great Sage. I read them off your banner. Heaven agrees to them."

Old Ma: "You beat them. You have a mountain and a name. Why go back up?"

Wukong: "Because they said it. Heaven said my name, the way I wrote it. I want to hear them say it up there."

Old Ma: "Come back."

Wukong: "I always do."
