VERDICT: APPROVED

## Blind read
The armored monkey boasts from the Buddha's open palm that he reached the end of the world and left his name there. The Buddha tells him to look down; the enormous turned hand and its middle finger own the page as the monkey recognizes his red writing and realizes he never left the hand. He rejects the revelation and bolts toward the door while the Buddha's open hand descends above him and orders him to wait.

## Visible text
Wukong (balloon tail points to the monkey): "I went to the end of the world. Five pillars. I wrote my name on the middle one. Send someone to look."

Buddha (balloon tail points to the Buddha): "I don't need to. Look down."

Printed in red on the middle finger: "THE GREAT SAGE EQUAL TO HEAVEN WAS HERE"

Wukong (balloon tail points to the monkey): "That's my writing."

Buddha (balloon tail points to the Buddha): "And that, I think, is the rest of it. You never left my hand."

Wukong (balloon tail points to the monkey): "That's a trick! You cheated, I went, I felt the wind—"

Buddha (balloon tail points to the Buddha): "Yes. Now hold still. Five hundred years should do it. Then someone will come for you. Wait for him."
