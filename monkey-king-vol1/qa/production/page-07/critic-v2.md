## Blind read
The monkey's search owns the page. He leaves by raft, spends years traveling among humans and asking old people about immortals, and his undirected wandering becomes a purposeful journey when an old woman identifies the gray mountain and the Master who lives there.

## Visible text
Narrator: He built a raft out of a dead pine and tied it with vines, and Old Ma stood on the shore and did not tell him to stay, because she had been the one who told him about immortals, and she knew it.

Narrator: The sea took him west for a season. The world of men took him for three years. He learned to walk on two legs so they would not chase him, and to wear a coat so they would not stare, and he asked every old person he met the same question. Most of them laughed.

Narrator: One did not. She pointed at a gray mountain with a single pine, and said:

Old woman: the Master lives there. He has had students for forty years, and he has never once taught any of them what you are asking for.
