## Blind read
Seven years pass within one courtyard scene: the same gold monkey sweeps through the seasons, then sits at the back of the students listening while the Master teaches. The monkey owns the page through his repeated labor and patient attention. What changes is time and his way of learning: although the Master refuses his yearly question seven times, the monkey learns to listen and remember instead of writing.

## Visible text
Narrator: He swept for seven years.

Narrator: He swept the gravel and the steps and the roof, and chopped the wood and carried the water, and at the lessons he sat at the back with the broom across his knees, and listened so hard his ears hurt. The Master talked about the way things are, and the students wrote it down, and the monkey did not write. He remembered instead, which turned out to be better.

Narrator: He did not learn how not to die. He asked, once a year, on the day he arrived. The Master said: not yet. Seven times.
