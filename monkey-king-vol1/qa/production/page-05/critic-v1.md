## Blind read
Wukong bursts back through the waterfall and announces the dry stone home he has found. He then leads the troop across the bridge into the cave, where Old Ma invokes the rule, makes him bow, and places her hand on his head as the troop cheers. Wukong owns the page; Old Ma gives the turn. His successful return changes him from the troop's explorer into the Handsome Monkey King.
