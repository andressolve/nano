The mountain was called the Mountain of Five Elements. It did not move, and neither could he.

Winter came, then spring, then winter. He could turn his head and see his own peak across the water, and the banner on it going pale. He could not reach the needle behind his ear. He learned what a year was, and how slowly five hundred of them go.

Every year, when the peaches came in, an old gray monkey climbed up and set one on the rock where he could reach it, and sat with him a while, and said the Buddha’s words back to him, the ones he had told her the first winter. She did that for as many years as she had. After that a younger monkey did it, who had been told to.
