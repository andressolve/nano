VERDICT: REVISE

## Blind read
An elderly adviser answers a richly robed ruler's disbelief that one monkey with a stick has stopped his whole army. The ruler unmistakably owns the page through his scale, central placement, and final decision. The adviser reframes the conflict as something that can be contained with an impressive but empty title, a house, and no duties; the ruler accepts the logic and orders the words granted while barring the monkey from the hall.

## Findings

### Finding C3
- Observation: In the middle panel, Laozi’s “It’s words. Words cost nothing. Give him the words, a house up here, and no duties, and he’ll sit in it.” balloon is on the left and reads before the Emperor’s “Equal to Heaven. Equal to me.” balloon on the right.
- Material reader harm: Laozi’s reply arrives before the Emperor’s disgust that prompts it, reversing the required causal order of the bargain and weakening the page’s dominant turn.
- Redraw justification: The reversal affects the central exchange that changes Heaven’s decision, so the scripted reader event does not land in sequence and is substantial enough to replace the page.
