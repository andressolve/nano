## Findings

### Finding C4
- Observation: In the close view, the middle pillar continues below the frame, so its foot is not shown. The visible red spray is detached at the far right and reads as splatter associated with the red writing gesture, while the monkey's right hand is still touching the final line; it does not read as a small arc marking the pillar at its foot.
- Material reader harm: The image communicates the signing, but not the distinct second action in “Then, to be sure, he marked it.” That removes the irreverent mark from the scripted name-and-mark turn and weakens the reason to turn the page.
- Redraw justification: The missing second action is one of the page's three dominant beats and a required consequential object state, not a cosmetic splash-shape issue; restoring it is substantial enough to risk replacing the complete page.
