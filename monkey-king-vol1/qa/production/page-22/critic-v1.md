The ruler assigns him to keep the Heavenly Horses. Wukong excitedly mistakes “Heavenly” for a sign of high rank, but the courtier corrects the misunderstanding and leads him away. The top panel unmistakably owns the page; Wukong’s mistaken pride and the courtier’s correction cause the comic turn.
