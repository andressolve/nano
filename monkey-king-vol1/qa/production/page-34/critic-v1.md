## Blind read
An elderly court official carrying a cluster of gourds rushes into a devastated heavenly banquet hall and reports to the enthroned ruler that someone ate all five gourds of elixir, destroying five hundred years of work. The ruler initially answers as though another reward might solve the problem, but the official insists the offender cannot be killed or managed and urges him to send Erlang with everything available. Looking out over massed forces, the ruler hesitates because Erlang never comes to the hall; the official answers that Erlang will come for a fight and is uniquely equipped—and willing—to face the offender. The elderly official owns the page: his escalating report and insistence turn the ruler from outrage and habitual bargaining toward the prospect of calling Erlang.
