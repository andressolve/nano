## Blind read
The young monkey returns to the mountain on a cloud, boasts to Old Ma that he cannot die, and offers to teach the troop. The dominant opening image belongs visually to his homecoming. Old Ma owns the turn: she cuts through his boast by naming his expulsion and altered face, then accepts that he came back. Her touch quiets him. He recovers by claiming a name of his own, recognizes that a king needs a weapon, and is pointed toward the Dragon King’s treasury under the eastern sea.
