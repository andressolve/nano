# Continuity gate — pages 31–40

APPROVED

Pages 31–40 remain in the same book. The Mineral Ink register holds across the run: warm toothed paper, visible ink drawing, pooled mineral washes, malachite/azurite/cinnabar accents, flat gold, and scene-motivated cast light remain coherent. Page 33's deeper blacks and cinnabar heat belong to the locked laboratory, while the broader gold fields on pages 35–38 read as outdoor cloud light rather than a finish change. Contrast, line density, paint treatment, balloon construction, and lettering weight vary only within the established expressive range.

Wukong remains recognizable by his short pale muzzle, wide amber eyes, golden fur, gold scale armor, red tunic, tiger kilt, black cloud-boots, paired back-curving plumes, tail, and red-and-gold staff. Laozi retains his bald round head, square white beard, stout indigo silhouette, and gourd association. The Jade Emperor retains his black beard, beaded crown, yellow dragon robes, and throne posture. Erlang remains clearly distinct from Wukong through his human face, third eye, silver armor, single red plume, lance, and hound.

The recurring settings also hold: page 31 belongs to the peach garden; pages 32, 34, 39, and 40 preserve the Hall of Jade's green columns, reflective water floor, lamps, central stair, and throne; page 33 preserves the laboratory's circular red-black chamber, shelf wall, and iron furnace; and pages 35–38 preserve Flower-Fruit Mountain's blue-green peaks, split stone, waterfall, fruit trees, and cloud-lit gold sky. The banner, peaches, five gourds, and iron ring remain legible in their consequential story states. Minor shifts in prop scale, background detail, and small-figure facial resolution are cosmetic and do not confuse recognition, action, or state.

No page in this range no longer belongs to the same book. No redraw is justified.
