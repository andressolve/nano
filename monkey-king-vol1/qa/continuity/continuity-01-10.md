# Visual-continuity gate — canonical pages 1–10

## Verdict

APPROVED

## Assessment

Pages 1–10 remain visibly part of the same book. The sequence consistently uses visible brush-and-ink contours, toothed paper, mineral-pigment washes, pooled edges, and restrained cinematic depth rather than smooth or glossy rendering. The warmer gold and malachite balance on pages 1–6, the grayer sea journey on page 7, and the cooler azurite courtyard on pages 8–10 follow setting and lighting changes without breaking the register. Interior blacks on pages 4–6 and the lifted snowy values on page 9 remain logically motivated and retain the same line-and-wash finish.

Lettering is coherent: serif text stays with narration, hand lettering stays with dialogue, and the cream balloons retain compatible irregular contours, outlines, tails, and weight. Display effects on pages 3, 5, and 10 vary appropriately without making any page feel imported from another book.

Character recognition remains secure. Wukong keeps his golden-tawny fur, pale muzzle, wide amber eyes, projecting ears, wiry build, long tail, and leaf kilt; his temporary coat on page 7 is story-motivated and leaves his identity clear. Old Ma keeps her gray fur, long white lined muzzle, red shawl, stoop, and stick across pages 2–6. Subhuti keeps his long face, white topknot, pointed beard, pale robe, upright posture, and whisk across pages 8–10. No named character drifts into a lookalike or changes enough to confuse story state.

Recurring settings also hold. The split peak, waterfall, sea, and blue-green mountain mass remain recognizable on pages 1, 3, and 7; the Water Curtain Cave retains its waterfall wall, iron bridge, blue pool, stone terraces, and firelit living space on pages 4–6; and the courtyard preserves its rock face, pine, swept gravel, cave mouth, and round blue door on pages 8–10.

## Revision findings

None. No page falls outside the book’s established continuity, and no redraw is justified.
