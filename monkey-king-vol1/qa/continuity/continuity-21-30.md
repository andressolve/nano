# Visual-continuity gate — canonical pages 21–30

## Verdict

APPROVED

## Findings

NONE.

## Assessment

Pages 21–30 remain one book in the approved Mineral Ink register. Warm toothed-paper grounds, visible ink contours, mineral-pigment washes, flat gold, grounded shadows, and atmospheric blue-green depth persist across the sequence. Page 26 pushes the black level substantially deeper for the descending army, but the retained paper, broken wash edges, gold cloud light, red banner, and ink treatment make that a motivated dramatic extreme rather than a foreign finish. No page falls outside the sequence.

Wukong remains immediately identifiable by his golden fur and pale muzzle, compact wiry build, gold scale armor over red, tiger-skin kilt, black cloud-boots, long tail, paired back-curving plumes, and red staff. Old Ma retains her gray fur, long white lined muzzle, stoop, stick, and worn red shawl on pages 25 and 28. Laozi remains round, bald, indigo-robed, white-bearded, and gourd-marked; the Jade Emperor remains broad, gold-robed, black-bearded, crowned, and bead-screened. Changes of scale, angle, and expression do not confuse identity or story state.

The South Gate, white cloud-roofed stables, reflective green-and-gold Hall of Jade, split Flower-Fruit peak, and terraced peach garden preserve their approved spatial and palette identities. The recurring banner stays a red cloth standard; its unlettered reverse on page 26 and the shift from dark painted words on page 25 to gold words under Heaven's light on page 28 do not impair object continuity. The page-30 peach is the approved head-sized fruit and remains consequential at first glance.

Lettering weight, cream fields, black outlines, and rounded balloon shapes are stable enough to preserve the reading system. Page 27's dialogue uses a more serifed face than the surrounding hand-lettered dialogue, but tails, balloon shapes, contrast, and reading order remain unambiguous. This is cosmetic variation without material reader harm and does not justify redrawing an otherwise continuous page.
