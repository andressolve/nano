# Sequence gate — canonical pages 41–48

## Blind sequence record

Wukong survives Laozi's furnace by abandoning futile resistance, finding the
one thread of cold, and doing the one thing he has never done: waiting. After
forty-nine days he explodes out of the furnace unchanged except for the fire
now fixed in his eyes, sending Heaven running and leaving its rulers out of
answers. He confronts the Jade Emperor from that position of triumph. Laozi
admits he has nothing left, so the Emperor asks the independent Buddha for
help.

The Buddha answers Wukong's demand for the throne with a calm wager: escape
his open hand and the throne is his. Wukong leaps to what he believes is the
end of everything, signs and marks the middle of five pillars, and returns to
claim victory. The writing and mark are revealed on the Buddha's finger;
Wukong never left the hand. His pride turns to disbelief and flight, but the
Buddha's descending hand becomes the Mountain of Five Elements and traps him
beneath it for five hundred years. The final page lets those years be felt:
an old gray monkey brings him a peach and company each year, a younger monkey
eventually inherits the kindness, and Wukong learns to wait for the person
the Buddha promised would come.

The emotional sequence is endurance, violent triumph, swagger, challenge,
humiliation, confinement, and patient hope.

## Script comparison

The visible sequence matches the scripted causal and emotional progression.
Story text and attribution remain exact across the page turns. Wukong, Laozi,
the Jade Emperor, and the Buddha remain distinct and correctly placed. The
furnace, fire-ringed eyes, staff, five pillars, inscription and mark, Buddha's
hand, paper seal, Flower-Fruit Mountain, fading banner, and yearly peach carry
their consequential continuity through the batch. Each page preserves its
focal turn, and the page turns pay off without a missing causal bridge.

Pacing is varied across illustrated prose, spectacle, and dramatic pages. The
two adjacent dramatic exchanges advance different turns rather than creating
a wall of scenes. The last page compresses centuries deliberately while
showing the human-scale aftermath of the sentence, so it does not read as a
summary that skips a required beat.
