## Blind sequence record

On the empty peak of Flower-Fruit Mountain, a stone cracks open and a golden young monkey steps out. The mountain monkeys gather around him; Old Ma accepts him as one of them, and he grows into the quickest and loudest of the troop despite not knowing where he came from.

At the waterfall, Old Ma explains that no one knows where the water comes from and repeats the old rule: whoever passes through it and returns alive will become king. The young monkey volunteers, plunges through, and discovers a dry stone home behind the water with room for the whole troop. He brings the others inside. Old Ma honors the rule, names him the Handsome Monkey King, and he promises that he will never tire of being their king.

Later, beside the fire, the king asks Old Ma what being very old means. She tells him that she has seen three kings die and that only immortals escape the fate awaiting monkeys, kings, and mountains alike. Alarmed, he resolves to find an immortal and learn how to keep himself and the troop from dying.

He builds a raft and leaves while Old Ma watches from shore. After a season at sea and years crossing the world of men, repeatedly asking old people for help, he is directed to a gray mountain where a master with many students lives. The master first dismisses him, but his answer that he came from a rock earns further attention. Because he has no family name, the master gives him one: Sun Wukong.

Wukong then sweeps and serves for seven years through the changing seasons. He listens from the back of the lessons, cannot write, and remembers instead. Once each year he asks to be taught how not to die; seven times the answer is not yet. At last the master asks what he has learned. Wukong answers that he remembers every teaching except the one he crossed the sea to receive. The master says he has learned nothing, taps him three times on the head with his staff, hides his hands behind his back, and leaves. While the other students laugh, Wukong understands the concealed direction: come through the back door at midnight on the third watch.
