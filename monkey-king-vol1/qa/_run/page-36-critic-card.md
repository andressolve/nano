## Page intent

## PAGE 36

Spectacle, nearly silent. The duel of shapes, read top to bottom, from the sky over Flower-Fruit Mountain down to the pool under the waterfall. High up, a gold sparrow dives with a hawk on its tail, and the hawk has an eye in its forehead. In the pool, a gold fish flashes under the surface and a cormorant with a red crest strikes down through the water; this middle image owns the page. In the reeds at the pool's edge, a gold water snake, and a crane with a red plume stepping down onto it, one foot raised. No monkey and no man appear; the two fighters are only their shapes, gold against silver-and-red. One caption over the sky says what the reader is watching. The dominant turn: the duel of shapes, top to bottom. The reader turns because for the first time someone can keep up, and it will not end in the water.

### C1 — transcription
The single caption reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in the top panel, tail-free, with no other text anywhere on the page. A missing, altered, or doubled caption, or any added lettering, is blocking. A caption that reads is not to be measured.

### C2 — spectacle, top to bottom
The page reads as a near-silent spectacle in three stacked images, sky, pool, reeds, read downward, and the pool image with the cormorant striking through the surface plainly owns the page. A page that reads as dialogue, shows a monkey or a man in his own body, or has no single dominant image, blocks.

### C3 — the shapes read as the two fighters
Without being told, the reader can tell the gold sparrow, fish, and water snake are one fighter and the hawk with the third eye, the cormorant with the red crest, and the crane with the red plume are the other, each pair pursuer and pursued in the same image. A missing gold or marked animal in any image, or an unreadable pairing, blocks. Species accuracy does not.

### C4 — continuity of place
This is Flower-Fruit Mountain's waterfall and pool, green and misted, not the sea floor and not Heaven. A page that reads as a different location blocks; exact geography does not.

### C5 — generation integrity
The six animals are whole creatures with the right number of wings, legs, and heads, and the water reads as water. A malformed animal in the dominant image blocks; soft background reeds do not.
