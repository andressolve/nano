## Page intent

## PAGE 43

Dramatic. Dominant turn: the Emperor at his own throne: "send for the Buddha." The Emperor is pressed back against his throne at the top of the steps; Wukong stands three steps below with the staff, armor scorched, plumes singed, eyes gold; Laozi is on the floor among the empty gourds with nothing in his hands; the pillars lean. Wukong lists every idea they had, stable boy, empty house, no invitation, a furnace, says he is still here, and tells the Emperor to come down so he can sit in the throne a while. The Emperor looks to Laozi, who for the first time has no answer. Then the Emperor, eyes closed, like a man giving up a secret, says send west to the Buddha. Wukong asks who that is. Laozi says: the one who owes Heaven nothing, whom Heaven has never asked for anything, until now. The reader turns because the Emperor just sent for that being.

## Exact owner-controlled script

## PAGE 43 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the Hall of Jade.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 50%.** The Emperor pressed back against his own throne
at the top of the steps, right. Wukong three steps below, left, staff in hand,
armor scorched, plumes singed at the tips, eyes gold. Laozi on the floor among
the empty gourds with nothing in his hands. Pillars leaning.

> **WUKONG:** "Stable boy. Empty house. No invitation. A furnace. That's every idea you had, and I'm still here."
>
> **WUKONG:** "Come down off that. I'll sit in it a while. See how it feels."

**Panel 2 — 20%.** The Emperor looking to Laozi. Laozi, for the first time,
with no answer: hands open, empty.

> **JADE EMPEROR:** "Laozi."
>
> **LAOZI:** "I have nothing left, Majesty. Nothing."

**Panel 3 — 30%.** The Emperor, still on the throne, saying it with his eyes
closed, like a man giving up a secret.

> **JADE EMPEROR:** "Then send west. To the Buddha. Tell him Heaven is asking."
>
> **WUKONG:** "Who's the Buddha?"
>
> **LAOZI:** "The one who lives west of everything and owes Heaven nothing. Heaven has never asked him for anything. Until now."

**Why you turn the page:** the Emperor just sent for the one being who owes Heaven nothing, and Laozi had to say so out loud.
