## Page intent

## PAGE 32

Dramatic. The Hall of Jade laid for the banquet and empty of guests: long tables, trays of peaches, jars of wine, covered dishes, the throne bare at the far end. Wukong alone in the doorway, plumes up, decides the food is going to waste. The dominant turn is the eating: he is up on the top table beneath the empty throne, in it to the elbows, a wine jar tipped to his mouth, trays going over, apologizing to a peach for being the one who eats it. Then he weaves out through a side door with a jar under his arm and his cap crooked, into a corridor lit red at the far end, and chooses red. Wukong owns the page alone; no one else is in the hall. The reader turns because he is drunk and has walked toward the one room in Heaven he should not enter. The feast's inventory stays subordinate to one monkey on one table.

### C1 — Dialogue transcription or ownership fails
All four strings, three balloons and the sound cue `GLUG GLUG GLUG`, read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order; every balloon is Wukong's, the sound cue is tail-free beside the jar, no other text. A string that does not read is blocking; one that reads is not to be measured.

### C2 — The turn does not land
The largest panel is not Wukong on the top table beneath an empty throne, in the food to the elbows with the jar tipped up and a peach in hand. The page must read doorway, feast, then the walk out.

### C3 — The hall is not empty
Any guest, servant, soldier, the Emperor, or Laozi is visible. He eats the party alone; one extra person breaks the event. A statue or pillar ornament is nonblocking.

### C4 — Wukong is not himself, or his state is wrong
The monkey does not read as golden, Great Sage armor, plumed cap, tail in frame; or the cap does not read upright in the first panel and crooked in the last. Exact plume angle is nonblocking.

### C5 — The red corridor does not read
The corridor in the last panel does not read as lit red at its far end, so his last line makes no sense. Exact hue is nonblocking when it reads as red light.

### C6 — Focal generation failure
Wukong's face, hands, the jar, or the peach he addresses is malformed or duplicated. Tray contents and pillars do not count.
