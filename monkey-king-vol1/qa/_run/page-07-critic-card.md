## Page intent

## PAGE 7

Illustrated prose, and the one page in this movement with two places, because the turn is the crossing itself. A raft of pine logs on an enormous gray-green sea, one small figure standing on it, not looking back, the green mountain going small behind him. Below, a monkey in a man's coat that is too big for him walks a dirt road on two legs toward a gray mountain with a single pine. The prose field carries the raft, Old Ma on the shore not telling him to stay because she knows she set him going, the season at sea, the years among men, the coat, the question asked of every old person, and the one old woman who points at the mountain where the Master has never taught anyone the thing he is asking for. The dominant turn is the raft, the sea, the years among men, and the Master's mountain. Wukong owns the page, alone. The reader turns because the Master has never taught it and the monkey has never taken no. The road stays subordinate to the small figure on the wide water.

> **PROSE FIELD 1** *(over the flat sea, never over the raft)*
> "He built a raft out of a dead pine and tied it with vines, and Old Ma stood on the shore and did not tell him to stay, because she had been the one who told him about immortals, and she knew it.
>
> The sea took him west for a season. The world of men took him for three years. He learned to walk on two legs so they would not chase him, and to wear a coat so they would not stare, and he asked every old person he met the same question. Most of them laughed.
>
> One did not. She pointed at a gray mountain with a single pine, and said: the Master lives there. He has had students for forty years, and he has never once taught any of them what you are asking for."

**Why you turn the page:** the Master has never taught anyone the one thing the monkey came for, and the monkey has never in his life taken no.

## Materiality threshold

The critic is a release gate, not a defect collector. A visible issue is
blocking only when it materially harms the reader event, exact text or
attribution, named or focal identity, consequential continuity or object state,
focal generation integrity, or the dominant dramatic relationship. Every
correction replaces the complete page. Minor background, cosmetic, tiny-prop,
geometry, colour, texture, and prompt-fidelity observations are nonblocking.
Omit them. Numeric panel shares and lettering sizes are builder construction
guidance, never critic measurements.

Each numbered criterion is also its stable retry signature. Cite it only when
the reader harm and redraw justification are concrete.

A lettered speaker name, label, or tag anywhere on the page is extra text and
blocks under the transcription criterion, whatever else reads correctly:
finished pages carry dialogue, captions, prose, and sound cues only.
A sound cue whose letters and order match the script reads correctly; the run
length of a repeated letter in it is nonblocking and never counted.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 7 — card

### C1 — Transcription
Every paragraph of the prose field reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order, as one continuous field over open water, never over the raft or the figure, and no other text appears. A paragraph that does not read or is out of order is blocking; text that reads is not to be measured.

### C2 — The turn does not land
The page must read as a small figure on a raft on a huge sea with the green mountain going small behind him, then, below, the same monkey in a too-big coat walking a road on two legs toward a gray mountain with a single pine. The raft owns the page by eye. If the road image dominates, or the order is unclear, the page fails.

### C3 — Wukong is not himself, or does not carry across
On the raft, the gold monkey, bare but for leaves, not looking back; on the road, the same gold monkey in a man's coat too big for him, upright, tail showing. A different creature in either image, or a human on the road, blocks. The cut of the coat does not.

### C4 — Old Ma or the Master appears
No Old Ma and no red shawl on the page; no Master, students, courtyard, or cave. The pine mountain stays a distant destination. A speck on a far shore is nonblocking; a readable figure is not.

### C5 — The raft is not a raft, or the mountain is not going small
The raft must read as rough pine logs lashed together, not a boat, and the green mountain behind must read as far away and diminishing. Log count and vine detail are nonblocking.

### C6 — Focal generation failure
A doubled figure in either image, a figure fused into raft or road, or corruption where the eye lands blocks. Texture faults in water, sky, or road do not.
