## Page intent

## PAGE 30

Illustrated prose. The peach garden in late-afternoon light: three terraces of peach trees in three colours of blossom, and at the top, in the fork of the biggest tree, Wukong on his back with a peach the size of his head. The prose does the telling. The bottom trees make you wise, the middle ones make you live forever, and nobody remembers what the top ones do because nobody was ever allowed to eat one.
