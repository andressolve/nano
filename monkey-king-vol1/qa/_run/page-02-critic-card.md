## Page intent

## PAGE 2

Illustrated prose. On the slopes under a fruit tree, a small gold monkey stands in a crowd of brown ones, and the birds have left the tree because of the light in his eyes. Old Ma, gray, stooped, in her faded red shawl, has laid her hand flat on top of his head, and he is looking up at her. The prose field below carries the years: he has no mother, he has a rock that will not close, the troop comes to look, Old Ma says he is here, and he grows, faster and louder than any of them, not knowing what he is. The dominant turn is that he has no mother and the troop takes him, sealed by Old Ma's hand. Old Ma owns the page; Wukong receives it. The reader turns because he does not know what he is, and neither do they. The crowd stays subordinate to the one hand on the one gold head.

> **PROSE FIELD 1** *(over the plain ground below the figures, never over faces)*
> "He was a monkey, more or less. He was gold where the others were brown, and his eyes gave off a light that made the birds leave the tree. He had no mother. He had a rock, and the rock was open now and would not close.
>
> The monkeys of the mountain came to look. The eldest, Old Ma, put her hand flat on his head, the way she did with the new ones, and left it there a while.
>
> 'Well,' she said. 'You're here.'
>
> He stayed. He grew. He was faster than all of them, and louder, and he did not know what he was, and for a long time nobody minded."

**Why you turn the page:** he does not know what he is, and neither do you.

## Materiality threshold

The critic is a release gate, not a defect collector. A visible issue is
blocking only when it materially harms the reader event, exact text or
attribution, named or focal identity, consequential continuity or object state,
focal generation integrity, or the dominant dramatic relationship. Every
correction replaces the complete page. Minor background, cosmetic, tiny-prop,
geometry, colour, texture, and prompt-fidelity observations are nonblocking.
Omit them. Numeric panel shares and lettering sizes are builder construction
guidance, never critic measurements.

Each numbered criterion is also its stable retry signature. Cite it only when
the reader harm and redraw justification are concrete.

A lettered speaker name, label, or tag anywhere on the page is extra text and
blocks under the transcription criterion, whatever else reads correctly:
finished pages carry dialogue, captions, prose, and sound cues only.
A sound cue whose letters and order match the script reads correctly; the run
length of a repeated letter in it is nonblocking and never counted.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 2 — card

### C1 — Transcription
Every paragraph of the prose field reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order, as one continuous field on plain ground, and no other text appears. A paragraph that does not read, is out of order, or lies over a face or hand is blocking; text that reads is not to be measured.

### C2 — The turn does not land
The page must read as one illustrated-prose page whose single image is Old Ma's hand laid flat on the small gold monkey's head inside a ring of brown monkeys under an empty tree, with the prose beneath. If the image splits into panels, or the hand on the head is not what the eye finds, the page fails.

### C3 — Wukong and Old Ma collide or are not themselves
He is small, gold, smooth-faced, round amber eyes, looking up; she is gray, stooped, white-muzzled, half-lidded, on a stick, in a faded red shawl. They must be told apart instantly and neither may have a human face. Slight shade differences are tolerable; two monkeys of one colour, or shawl or stick on the wrong one, are not.

### C4 — The crowd steals an identity
No brown monkey may be gold, gray, shawled, or carry a stick, and no crowd monkey may have a balloon. Repeated or vague crowd faces are nonblocking.

### C5 — Focal generation failure
A doubled named figure, a hand that does not sit on the head, a fused or missing face on either named monkey, or corruption at the point of contact blocks. Faults in tree, slope, or distant monkeys do not.
