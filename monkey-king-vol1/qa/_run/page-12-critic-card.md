## Page intent

## PAGE 12

Spectacle. One image, no panels, almost no words. Dawn high over the western sea: a tiny gold figure upside down in mid-somersault on a puff of white cloud, the curve of the sea far below him, the sun enormous beside him. Far behind and below, the gray mountain with its single pine, already small. The dominant turn is the first somersault, over the sea, at the sun. One short caption over the sea says what this is: one somersault, a hundred thousand miles, and the first one he did just to see. Nothing else competes; the scale of sky and sun against the smallness of the monkey is the whole event. The reader turns because he can go anywhere now, and the first thing he does with that is show off.

### C1 — Transcription
The one caption reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, tail-free, with no balloon and no other text anywhere. A string that does not read is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured.

### C2 — Spectacle, rendered
A single image with no panel borders, and the event unmistakable by eye: a tiny gold monkey upside down in mid-somersault on a puff of cloud, high above the curve of the sea, beside an enormous sun. Tolerance: none for a paneled page; the horizon's placement is not graded.

### C3 — Scale and direction
The monkey is small against sky and sun and plainly upside down and in motion; the reader feels distance, not a portrait. The mountain below is small, far, gray, with a single pine. Tolerance: a faint but present mountain passes.

### C4 — Identity
The figure is Wukong: gold fur, tail, leaf kilt, no armor, cap, or staff. Tolerance: at this scale a readable gold silhouette with a tail passes.

### C5 — Nothing else
No other figure, face, or creature shares the sky, and no second text appears. Tolerance: none for figures; wave and cloud detail is not graded.
