## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 26 — card

### C1 — transcription
The one caption reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, tail-free, with no other text anywhere — no legible words on the banner on this page. A caption that does not read is blocking; one that reads is not to be measured.

### C2 — the army against one monkey owns the page
The page is spectacle: a great spiral of faceless black-armored soldiers coming down the sky and one small gold figure under the banner with a tree-trunk staff. If the monkey is large and the army small, or the page breaks into dialogue panels, the scale is lost — blocking.

### C3 — arrival, then retreat
The bottom strip shows the same army going back up in disorder with helmets falling, and it reads after the descent. A page that shows only the attack, or a retreat that reads as a second attack, undoes the caption — blocking.

### C4 — identity and the faceless army
The one figure is Wukong: gold armor, plumes, tail, the staff enormous. No soldier has a face, a third eye, a standing plume, silver armor, or a crown; no named character leads them. A soldier readable as Erlang or any named face is blocking.

### C5 — the banner flies
The red banner is raised on its pole over the peak in the main image. A missing banner is blocking; its exact placement is not.

### C6 — focal integrity
The gold figure and the staff whole; the spiral of soldiers coherent. Repeated soldier silhouettes are expected and tolerated.
