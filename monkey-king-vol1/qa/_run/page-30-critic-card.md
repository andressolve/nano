## Page intent

## PAGE 30

Illustrated prose. The peach garden in late-afternoon light: three terraces of peach trees in three colours of blossom, and at the top, in the fork of the biggest tree, Wukong on his back with a peach the size of his head. The prose does the telling. The bottom trees make you wise, the middle ones make you live forever, and nobody remembers what the top ones do because nobody was ever allowed to eat one. He started at the top. The dominant turn is that he eats, one a day and then one an hour, and the stones pile up under the tree where the gardeners will find them, and he does not care, because it is his garden and nobody said not to. Wukong owns the page alone. The reader turns because someone is about to come and count. The blossom and the terraces stay subordinate to one monkey in one tree with one peach.

> **PROSE FIELD 1** *(over the plain hillside, never over the tree)*
> "The garden had three thousand six hundred trees. The ones at the bottom ripened every three thousand years, and made you wise. The ones in the middle ripened every six thousand years, and made you live forever. The ones at the top ripened every nine thousand years, and nobody remembered what they did, because nobody had ever been allowed to eat one.
>
> He started at the top.
>
> He ate one a day, then one an hour. He ate them in the tree, and the stones fell where the gardeners would find them, and he did not care. He was the Great Sage Equal to Heaven, and it was his garden, and nobody had said not to."

**Why you turn the page:** he is eating the peaches of Heaven one an hour, and someone is about to come and count them.

## Materiality threshold

The critic is a release gate, not a defect collector. A visible issue is
blocking only when it materially harms the reader event, exact text or
attribution, named or focal identity, consequential continuity or object state,
focal generation integrity, or the dominant dramatic relationship. Every
correction replaces the complete page. Minor background, cosmetic, tiny-prop,
geometry, colour, texture, and prompt-fidelity observations are nonblocking.
Omit them. Numeric panel shares and lettering sizes are builder construction
guidance, never critic measurements.

Each numbered criterion is also its stable retry signature. Cite it only when
the reader harm and redraw justification are concrete.

A lettered speaker name, label, or tag anywhere on the page is extra text and
blocks under the transcription criterion, whatever else reads correctly:
finished pages carry dialogue, captions, prose, and sound cues only.
A sound cue whose letters and order match the script reads correctly; the run
length of a repeated letter in it is nonblocking and never counted.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 30 — card

### C1 — Prose transcription fails
Each of the three prose paragraphs reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order, as one field over plain ground, with no other text on the page. A paragraph that does not read or is split into scattered boxes is blocking; a paragraph that reads is not to be measured.

### C2 — The turn does not land
The page does not read as illustrated prose in which one image owns it: a golden monkey lying in the fork of the topmost tree, eating a peach the size of his head, above three terraces. If the reader cannot tell he is at the top and eating, the page fails.

### C3 — Wukong is not himself
The figure does not read as the golden monkey in Great Sage armor with the plumed cap and a tail. A human face or gray fur is blocking; armor detail is not.

### C4 — The stones do not tell the story
The reader cannot see many peach stones on the ground beneath his tree. Their exact count is nonblocking.

### C5 — Someone else is in the garden
A gardener, an old man, or any figure but Wukong is visible, spoiling the arrival the next page owes.

### C6 — Prose sits over busy art
The field lies across blossom, the tree, or the figure so that reading it means fighting the picture. Field colour is nonblocking when the text reads over calm ground.

### C7 — Focal generation failure
Wukong's face, hands, or the peach is malformed or duplicated. Leaf texture and distant blossom do not count.
