## Page intent

## PAGE 4

Spectacle. From inside the waterfall: the falling water is a wall of silver seen from behind, an iron bridge crosses the pool, and beyond it a whole house is cut into the mountain, stone beds, stone bowls, stone stools, a stone hearth, all dry, all empty, big enough for hundreds. Wukong is tiny on the bridge, dripping, mouth open. One caption over the dry floor names what he has found. The dominant turn is the Water Curtain Cave itself, a stone house behind the fall. The place owns the page; Wukong is its first witness, and his smallness is the measure of its size. The reader turns because there is a whole home behind the waterfall and nobody has ever lived in it. No other monkey, no fire, no fruit, nothing that would make it already lived in; the emptiness is the point.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 4 — card

### C1 — Transcription
The one caption reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, tail-free, over the dry stone floor, and no other text appears. A caption that does not read, or any stray label or second string, is blocking; a caption that reads is not to be measured.

### C2 — The turn does not land
The page must read as one interior seen from behind the waterfall: a silver wall of water, an iron bridge, and a stone house with beds, bowls, stools, and a hearth going back into the mountain, plainly empty and dry. If the page breaks into panels, or the house does not read as a home, the spectacle fails.

### C3 — Wukong is not the small witness
A tiny gold monkey, drenched, mouth open, must be findable on the bridge, and he must be the only living thing. A second monkey or a person inside blocks; his exact size or pose does not.

### C4 — The house is not stone, or is already lived in
Beds, bowls, stools, and hearth must read as cut stone, with no fire, fruit, bedding, or sign of anyone. Wooden furniture, a lit hearth, or signs of occupation block; the count of beds or bowls does not.

### C5 — The waterfall is not seen from inside
The falling water must read as a wall seen from behind, on one side, with light coming through it. A waterfall seen from outside, or a dry doorway with no water, blocks.

### C6 — Focal generation failure
A doubled figure, corrupt geometry where bridge meets house, or a figure fused into the bridge blocks. Texture faults in water or stone do not.
