## Page intent

## PAGE 14

Dramatic. Home in one leap. A cloud comes down onto the peak of Flower-Fruit Mountain beside the split stone, monkeys pour up the slopes, and Old Ma is on the rock with her stick and red shawl and one flat hand over her eyes, his own gesture. Wukong jumps off the cloud before it lands, shouting that he did it, he cannot die, he will teach every one of them. The dominant turn is the homecoming, Old Ma, and the dragon king under the sea. Her hand flat on his head, she says the thing he did not: you were thrown out. She read it in his face. Never mind; he came back. Then he is up on the split stone looking east, hand over his eyes: fine, he will make a name of his own, and a king needs a weapon. Old Ma tells him the old ones spoke of the Dragon King's treasury under the eastern sea, and nobody has ever gone and asked. The reader turns because that sentence gets this monkey moving.

## Exact owner-controlled script

## PAGE 14 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the peak of Flower-Fruit Mountain.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 50%.** A cloud coming down onto the peak beside the split
stone. Monkeys pouring up the slopes. Old Ma on the rock, right, stick, shawl,
and one flat hand up over her eyes, his gesture. Wukong jumping off the cloud,
left, before it has landed.

> **OLD MA:** "Ten years and a season. I counted."
>
> **WUKONG:** "Old Ma! I did it. I can't die. I'm going to teach every one of you."

**Panel 2 — 20%.** Her hand flat on his head. His face crumpling for a second
and recovering.

> **OLD MA:** "You were thrown out."
>
> **WUKONG:** "How can you—"
>
> **OLD MA:** "You came back on a cloud and your face was wrong. Never mind. You came back."

**Panel 3 — 30%.** Wukong up on the split stone, looking east at the sea, hand
over his eyes. Old Ma below him.

> **WUKONG:** "He said never to say I was his. Fine. I'll make a name of my own."
>
> **WUKONG:** "A king with no weapon is just a loud monkey. Where does a king get one?"
>
> **OLD MA:** "The old ones said the Dragon King under the eastern sea has a treasury. Nobody has ever gone and asked."

**Why you turn the page:** nobody has ever gone and asked, which is exactly the kind of sentence that gets this monkey moving.

### C1 — Transcription
The eight listed strings read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in causal order, owned by the right mouth, with no extra text. A string that does not read is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured.

### C2 — The landing owns the page
One image plainly carries the page: the cloud coming down beside the split stone, monkeys pouring up the slope, Old Ma on the rock with a flat hand over her eyes, Wukong leaping off before it lands. Tolerance: an unequal but readable hierarchy passes.

### C3 — Order of the exchange
"Ten years and a season." before "Old Ma! I did it."; "You were thrown out." before "How can you—", and "You came back on a cloud" only after it; "He said never to say I was his." then "A king with no weapon" both before Old Ma's "The old ones said", which is last. Tolerance: awkward tails pass if order and ownership are never in doubt.

### C4 — Two monkeys, separated
Wukong is young, gold, amber-eyed, bare in a leaf kilt; Old Ma is old, gray, white-muzzled, stooped over a stick, in the one red shawl. They are never confusable at any scale, including the close beat of her hand on his head. Tolerance: brown faceless crowd monkeys pass; a gray crowd monkey with a shawl does not.

### C5 — Continuity of the peak
The stone on the peak is split open. Wukong wears no armor or cap and carries no staff or needle. Old Ma's shawl is the only red on the mountain. Tolerance: the exact shape of the split or the cloud is not graded.

### C6 — The gesture and the face
Old Ma's hand lies flat on his head and his face visibly crumples and recovers; in the last beat his flat hand shades his eyes toward the sea. Tolerance: background monkeys' anatomy is not graded.
