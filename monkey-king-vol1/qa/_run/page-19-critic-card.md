## Page intent

## PAGE 19

Dramatic. Heaven, for the first time: the Hall of Jade, a throne room the size of a valley, and the Jade Emperor seated high with two fingers at his temple, wanting only quiet. Laozi, round and busy, stands on the step below with the Dragon King's shell letter, dabbing his brow, and reports that a monkey out of a stone has taken the pillar that holds the sea down, a monkey called Wukong. The Emperor shuts his eyes and orders soldiers; he does not want to hear the name again. Then Laozi turns with an idea, one finger up: give the monkey a small job up here, because a monkey with a title holds still, and titles cost less than soldiers. The Emperor agrees and sends Laozi to fetch him personally. The page belongs to the two men and the step between them. The dominant turn is Heaven deciding to manage the monkey. The hall's grandeur stays subordinate.

## Exact owner-controlled script

## PAGE 19 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the Hall of Jade, in Heaven.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 50%.** A hall the size of a valley: a throne at the top
of a flight of steps, jade-green pillars, a floor like still water. THE JADE
EMPEROR seated, right and high: a broad man in his prime, black beard in three
points, a flat crown with strings of beads before his face, two fingers at his
temple. LAOZI at his elbow on the step below, left: round, bald, white beard
cut square, indigo robe, a gourd at his hip, holding a shell letter and
dabbing his brow with his sleeve.

> **LAOZI:** "From the Dragon King of the Eastern Sea, Majesty. A monkey has taken the pillar that holds the sea down."
>
> **JADE EMPEROR:** "A monkey."
>
> **LAOZI:** "A monkey that came out of a stone, and says he cannot be killed. He's called Wukong."

**Panel 2 — 20%.** The Emperor's eyes shut.

> **JADE EMPEROR:** "Send soldiers. Bring the pillar back. I don't want to hear this name again."

**Panel 3 — 30%.** Laozi one step down, turning with the idea, one finger up.

> **LAOZI:** "Or, Majesty. Give him a job. A small one, up here. A monkey with a title holds still. Soldiers cost more than titles."
>
> **JADE EMPEROR:** "Fine, Laozi. Go and fetch him yourself."

**Why you turn the page:** Heaven has decided to manage him, and the man who thought of it has to go down and fetch him personally.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 19 — card

### C1 — Transcription
All six strings read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once each, in causal order, owned by the right mouth. No other text; the shell letter carries no legible writing. A string that does not read is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured.

### C2 — Heaven decides to manage him
One panel plainly owns the page: the vast Hall of Jade with the Emperor seated high and Laozi on the step below with the letter. The page ends on Laozi being sent to fetch the monkey himself. If it reads as soldiers dispatched with no counter-idea, the turn fails.

### C3 — Attribution and order
The Emperor's "A monkey." must be readable only after Laozi's "From the Dragon King," and Laozi's "A monkey that came out of a stone" must read third. Laozi's "Or, Majesty. Give him a job." must be readable before the Emperor's "Fine, Laozi."

### C4 — Emperor and Laozi are two different men
A broad man in his prime with a black three-point beard and beaded crown, seated, against a round bald old man with a short square white beard, standing, in indigo with a gourd. Either mistakable for the other, or Laozi mistakable for the Master with topknot and whisk, is blocking.

### C5 — The step
Laozi stands below the Emperor's level on the steps in every panel; the Emperor never stands. The number of steps is nonblocking.

### C6 — Generation integrity
Both faces, Laozi's raised finger, and the Emperor's hand at his temple are whole; no duplicate figure.
