## Page intent

## PAGE 15

Spectacle. Wukong walks down into the Eastern Sea as if it were a staircase, upright and unhurried, green water giving way to blue around him, fish parting to let him pass. Far below, on the sand, a palace of crystal and white shell glows in the dark, and at the very back of it burns one dull red point, the only warm thing in the whole sea. The page belongs to Wukong's confidence: the sea does not resist him, and the reader feels the sheer drop between the small monkey and the vast palace that has no idea he is coming. One caption over open water carries the whole idea. The dominant turn is the descent itself, and the discovery of the crystal palace below. The fish, the coral, and the palace's ornament stay subordinate to the single image of a monkey walking downward through water toward a red glow. Nothing is spoken; nobody is met yet.

### C1 — Transcription
The one caption reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, tail-free, over open water, with no other text anywhere on the page. A caption that does not read is blocking; a caption that reads is not to be measured.

### C2 — The descent owns the page
The page reads as one image: a monkey walking downward through the sea as if down a stair, a crystal palace far below him, one red glow at its back. If it reads as a swim, a fall, or a multi-panel page, the spectacle mode has not rendered.

### C3 — Wukong reads as Wukong
The small figure is the golden monkey of the earlier pages, bare in his leaf kilt with a tail, not a human diver and not a gray or brown monkey. Seen from above and behind is fine so long as gold fur and tail read.

### C4 — The palace and the glow
The palace reads as crystal and shell on sand, with one dull red glow at its rear, the only warm point in a blue sea. A missing glow, or several fires, breaks the setup for the next page. Exact architecture is nonblocking.

### C5 — Generation integrity
One whole monkey, no duplicate, no corrupted anatomy. Fish shapes and coral texture are nonblocking.
