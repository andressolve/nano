## Page intent

## PAGE 34

Dramatic. The Hall of Jade wrecked after the banquet: tables over, trays empty, robed guests standing about with nothing to do, the Emperor at the throne. Laozi arrives at a run with five empty gourds and tells the Emperor where he was: counting. The monkey ate the elixir, five hundred years of work. The dominant turn is Laozi's face changing. The Emperor asks what now, another title, a bigger house, and the man who invented every offer says no more offers; he cannot be killed and cannot be managed; send everything, and send Erlang. On the terrace the army pours out of the South Gate like ink, and the Emperor lays a hand on Laozi's shoulder, the first time he has touched anyone. Laozi owns the page; the Emperor's touch is the aftermath. The reader turns to meet the one person in Heaven who will enjoy this. The guests and the army stay subordinate to two faces.

## Exact owner-controlled script

## PAGE 34 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the Hall of Jade, and its terrace.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** The hall wrecked: tables over, trays empty, robed guests
standing about the mess with nothing to do. The Emperor at the throne, right.
Laozi arriving at a run, left, five empty gourds in his arms.

> **JADE EMPEROR:** "My banquet. In front of everyone. Where were you?"
>
> **LAOZI:** "In my laboratory, Majesty. Counting. He ate the elixir. All five gourds. Five hundred years of my work."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** Laozi on the step, the gourds dropped and
rolling. His face has changed: no dabbing, no smile. The Emperor seeing it.

> **JADE EMPEROR:** "And now? Another title? A bigger house?"
>
> **LAOZI:** "No more offers. He can't be killed and he can't be managed. Send Erlang. Send everything, and send Erlang."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** The terrace outside the hall. Below it, the army pouring
out of the South Gate like ink. The Emperor's hand on Laozi's shoulder, the
first time he has touched anyone.

> **JADE EMPEROR:** "Erlang. My nephew. He never comes to this hall."
>
> **LAOZI:** "He'll come for a fight. Three eyes, a hound, and the only one in Heaven who will enjoy this."

**Why you turn the page:** the man who made every offer just said no more offers, and the reader is about to meet the one person in Heaven who enjoys this.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 34 — card

### C1 — Dialogue transcription or ownership fails
All six strings read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in causal order, three owned by the Emperor and three by Laozi, no other text. A string that does not read is blocking; one that reads is not to be measured.

### C2 — Attribution or order breaks the exchange
`In my laboratory, Majesty…` is readable before `Where were you?`; `No more offers…` before `And now? Another title? A bigger house?`; `He'll come for a fight…` before `Erlang. My nephew…`; or a line sits on the wrong man's side.

### C3 — The turn does not land
The largest panel is not Laozi on the throne's step, gourds dropped, face changed, while the seated Emperor sees it.

### C4 — Laozi and the Emperor collide
Laozi must read bald with white side hair, square white beard, indigo, standing; the Emperor black three-point beard, beaded jade crown, yellow-gold, seated until the terrace. Neither may read as a long-faced topknotted man.

### C5 — Laozi's face has not changed
Laozi is still smiling or dabbing his brow. The reader must see a different face from every earlier page, or the line is unearned.

### C6 — Consequential continuity fails
The five empty gourds are missing, the hall is not wrecked, a guest speaks, the army below the terrace is missing, or the Emperor's hand is not on Laozi's shoulder in the last panel. Guest and soldier counts are nonblocking.

### C7 — An absent character appears
A monkey, or a figure with a third eye, silver helmet, standing red plume, hound, or lance, is visible anywhere. The reader would think Wukong or Erlang had arrived.

### C8 — Focal generation failure
Either man's face or hands is malformed or duplicated. Guest faces and soldier ranks do not count.
