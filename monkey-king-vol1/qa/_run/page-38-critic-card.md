## Page intent

## PAGE 38

Dramatic. The peak of Flower-Fruit Mountain. Wukong runs, looking back at Erlang, not up. High in the clouds, Laozi on his own small cloud holds out a dull iron ring the size of a bracelet, unsmiling, and asks forgiveness: he offered everything Heaven had, and this is the last thing he has that is his. The ring, grown to a cartwheel, comes down on the back of Wukong's head. He pitches forward, the staff flies from his hand, the hound is already in the air. Then the aftermath: face-down under his own banner, the ring round his head, the hound's jaws on his leg, Erlang's boot on the staff, soldiers closing with chains, and he is laughing. Erlang wants it understood this was not him; Wukong counts them all, army, Erlang, dog, bracelet, and wants that said in the hall. The dominant turn: the ring from behind; the hound; chains; it took all of you.

## Materiality threshold

The critic is a release gate, not a defect collector. A visible issue is
blocking only when it materially harms the reader event, exact text or
attribution, named or focal identity, consequential continuity or object state,
focal generation integrity, or the dominant dramatic relationship. Every
correction replaces the complete page. Minor background, cosmetic, tiny-prop,
geometry, colour, texture, and prompt-fidelity observations are nonblocking.
Omit them. Numeric panel shares and lettering sizes are builder construction
guidance, never critic measurements.

Each numbered criterion is also its stable retry signature. Cite it only when
the reader harm and redraw justification are concrete.

A lettered speaker name, label, or tag anywhere on the page is extra text and
blocks under the transcription criterion, whatever else reads correctly:
finished pages carry dialogue, captions, prose, and sound cues only.
A sound cue whose letters and order match the script reads correctly; the run
length of a repeated letter in it is nonblocking and never counted.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 38 — card

### C1 — transcription
Every listed string reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in causal order, owned by the right mouth, with no extra text anywhere on the page and no readable characters on the banner. A string that does not read, is missing, doubled, or misattributed is blocking. A string that reads is not to be measured.

### C2 — the ring from behind owns the page
The ring, grown huge, striking the back of Wukong's head plainly owns the page, the staff leaving his hand, the hound in the air. The reader must understand it came from Laozi above while Wukong looked back at Erlang. Judge ownership by the blind-read story, not panel area: if the blind read identifies the strike as the unmistakable causal hinge and understands that Laozi's blow from behind reverses Wukong's escape, C2 passes even when the opening or aftermath occupies more space. Block only when the blow reads as Erlang's, the ambush/reversal is unclear, or another beat actually displaces the strike as the reader event.

### C3 — order and attribution
Laozi's "Forgive me, Great Sage ..." reads first and belongs to the small figure in the clouds. Erlang's "That wasn't me ..." reads before Wukong's "I saw him on the way down ...", and "Say that in the hall when they ask." is Wukong's and last, from a laughing mouth on a face-down monkey. A reply readable before its prompt, or a line that could belong to either, blocks.

### C4 — identity of three named characters
Laozi reads as a round, bald, square-bearded old man in indigo with no smile, never long-faced, topknotted, or pointed-bearded. Erlang reads as the young human with the third eye and single standing plume; Wukong as the small golden monkey in gold armor with two back-curving plumes. Any pair that blends blocks.

### C5 — object state and focal integrity
By the last image the ring is round Wukong's head, the staff is under Erlang's boot, the hound has his leg, chains are arriving, the banner still flies, and he is laughing, not defeated. Wukong's face at the blow and after, and Laozi's hands on the ring, are whole. A missing ring, a staff still in his hand, a defeated expression, or a mangled focal face blocks; the number of soldiers does not.
