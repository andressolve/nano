# Exact script — pages 31–40

Open only after the blind sequence read.

## PAGE 31 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the peach garden.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Laozi coming up the terrace with a basket, gourd at his
hip, bending to the peach stones on the path. His eyes go up the terraces to
the biggest tree at the top, where two gold feet hang down.

> **LAOZI:** "Great Sage. These stones are from the top trees. Nobody in Heaven has ever been allowed one."
>
> **WUKONG:** "They're good. Bit dry."
>
> **LAOZI:** "I need forty of the middle peaches for my elixir and forty more for tonight. Where are the—"
>
> **WUKONG:** "For tonight?"

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** Wukong dropping out of the tree to land in front
of Laozi, left, peach juice on his chin. The basket between them. Laozi, right,
dabbing his brow.

> **LAOZI:** "The banquet. The Emperor's peach banquet. It's tonight, in the hall. Every immortal of rank in Heaven."
>
> **WUKONG:** "Every immortal of rank. I'm Equal to Heaven. That's a rank. Where's my invitation, Laozi?"

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Laozi not answering, bending to pick up a peach stone.
Wukong very still, the way he was still under the three taps, but with no
smile.

> **LAOZI:** "Wukong. The title was so that you would not need one."
>
> **WUKONG:** "Say it plainly. Am I invited?"
>
> **LAOZI:** "No."

**Why you turn the page:** the title was so that he would not need an invitation, and now he knows what that means.

## PAGE 32 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the Hall of Jade, set for the banquet.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** The hall laid with long tables: trays of peaches, jars of
wine, covered dishes. No guests yet. Wukong alone in the doorway, plumes up.

> **WUKONG:** "Every immortal of rank. And the food's just sitting here. That seems like a waste."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** Wukong up on the top table beneath the empty
throne, in it to the elbows: peaches, a whole roast something, a wine jar
tipped to his mouth, trays going over. He is talking to a peach.

> **WUKONG:** "You were going to be eaten by somebody important. Sorry. It's me."
>
> **SOUND** *(the jar)*
> "GLUG GLUG GLUG"

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Wukong weaving out through a side door with a wine jar
under his arm and his cap crooked, into a corridor lit red at the far end.

> **WUKONG:** "Whoever's important can have what's left. Now. Which way is home. This way's red. Red's fine."

**Why you turn the page:** he ate the party, and he is drunk, and he has just walked toward the one room in Heaven he should not be in.

## PAGE 33 · *dramatic*

**Location:** Laozi's laboratory.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** A round room of black iron and cinnabar red, shelves of
gourds and jars, and in the middle a bronze furnace taller than a man with a
lid like a temple roof. Wukong swaying in the doorway with the jar.

> **WUKONG:** "Gourds. Laozi's gourds. This is Laozi's place. Ha. The peach man. Let's see what the peach man keeps."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** Five gourds on the highest shelf, unstoppered,
gold pills spilling into his palm like sweets. His face lit gold from below.
He is eating them by the handful.

> **WUKONG:** "Sweets! He hides sweets up here. Old Ma would love these. Five whole gourds. I'll take her some."
>
> **SOUND** *(the pills)*
> "crunch crunch crunch"

**Panel 3 — 25%.** The five gourds empty on the floor. Wukong sitting among
them, suddenly stone sober, eyes clear, looking at his own hands, which are
giving off a faint light.

> **WUKONG:** "Oh. Oh, no. Those weren't sweets. That was the thing he makes. His elixir. All five gourds. And I feel wonderful."

**Why you turn the page:** he just ate five hundred years of Laozi's work by accident, and it worked.

## PAGE 34 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the Hall of Jade, and its terrace.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** The hall wrecked: tables over, trays empty, robed guests
standing about the mess with nothing to do. The Emperor at the throne, right.
Laozi arriving at a run, left, five empty gourds in his arms.

> **JADE EMPEROR:** "My banquet. In front of everyone. Where were you?"
>
> **LAOZI:** "In my laboratory, Majesty. Counting. He ate the elixir. All five gourds. Five hundred years of my work."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** Laozi on the step, the gourds dropped and
rolling. His face has changed: no dabbing, no smile. The Emperor seeing it.

> **JADE EMPEROR:** "And now? Another title? A bigger house?"
>
> **LAOZI:** "No more offers. He can't be killed and he can't be managed. Send Erlang. Send everything, and send Erlang."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** The terrace outside the hall. Below it, the army pouring
out of the South Gate like ink. The Emperor's hand on Laozi's shoulder, the
first time he has touched anyone.

> **JADE EMPEROR:** "Erlang. My nephew. He never comes to this hall."
>
> **LAOZI:** "He'll come for a fight. Three eyes, a hound, and the only one in Heaven who will enjoy this."

**Why you turn the page:** the man who made every offer just said no more offers, and the reader is about to meet the one person in Heaven who enjoys this.

# MOVEMENT VI — ERLANG · pp 35–38

---

## PAGE 35 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the sky over Flower-Fruit Mountain.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 50%.** Storm light. The army hanging in a ring in the
clouds around the peak, not attacking. In front of it, alone on a cloud,
right, ERLANG: a young man in silver armor with a single red plume standing
up from his helmet, a third eye open in the middle of his forehead, a
three-pointed lance held upright, a lean gray hound at his heel. Wukong on the
peak under his banner, left, staff out, plumes back.

> **ERLANG:** "Sun Wukong. Great Sage Equal to Heaven, it says up there. I'm Erlang. The Emperor's nephew. I came to see if the banner's true."
>
> **WUKONG:** "Three eyes. Which one do I hit first?"

**Panel 2 — 20%.** Erlang laughing, really laughing. The third eye narrowing
while the other two stay still.

> **ERLANG:** "Nobody has ever asked me that. Everyone else just runs."
>
> **WUKONG:** "I don't run. I've got nowhere to run to. This is it."

**Panel 3 — 30%.** The lance and the staff meeting. The shock of it throwing
the ring of cloud outward. The hound flattening itself.

> **SOUND** *(the strike)*
> "KRANNNG"
>
> **ERLANG:** "Good."

**Why you turn the page:** somebody finally spoke to him like an equal, and then hit him, and he liked both.

## PAGE 36 · *spectacle*

**Location:** the sky over Flower-Fruit Mountain, down to the pool.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** High up: a gold sparrow diving, and a hawk with a third eye
on its tail.

**Panel 2 — dominant, 40%.** The pool below the waterfall: a gold fish
flashing under the surface, and a cormorant with a red crest striking down
through it.

**Panel 3 — 30%.** The reeds at the pool's edge: a gold water snake, and a
crane with a red plume stepping down on it, one foot raised.

> **CAPTION** *(in the top panel, over sky)*
> "Shape for shape. He had never met anyone who could keep up."

**Why you turn the page:** for the first time in his life someone can keep up, and it is not going to end in the water.

## PAGE 37 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the slope of Flower-Fruit Mountain, below the peak.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 50%.** A small temple standing on the slope where no
temple was: red doors, a round window, an offering box. Behind it, a flagpole,
furred, with a tuft on the end. Erlang, human again, walking round it with the
lance on his shoulder. The hound sniffing the pole.

> **ERLANG:** "A temple. Very nice. Doors, a window, an offering box."
>
> **ERLANG:** "I've seen a lot of temples, Great Sage. I have never seen one with the flagpole round the back."

**Panel 2 — 20%.** The round window blinking.

> **WUKONG:** "It's a new style."
>
> **ERLANG:** "I'm going to kick the door in. Whatever's inside, I'll hit it."

**Panel 3 — 30%.** The temple bursting back into a monkey mid-leap, away, up
the slope toward the peak. Erlang after him. The hound after both.

> **WUKONG:** "Later! Another day! I'll come and find you!"
>
> **ERLANG:** "You said you don't run!"
>
> **WUKONG:** "That was before the dog!"

**Why you turn the page:** he is running, and he said he never runs, and something above him in the clouds has been waiting for exactly this.

## PAGE 38 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the peak of Flower-Fruit Mountain.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Looking up from the peak: high in the clouds, Laozi on his
own small cloud, holding out a dull iron ring the size of a bracelet. Below,
Wukong running, looking back at Erlang, not up.

> **LAOZI:** "Forgive me, Great Sage. I offered you everything Heaven had. This is the last thing I have that is mine."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** The ring, grown to the size of a cartwheel,
coming down on the back of Wukong's head. Wukong pitching forward. The staff
flying out of his hand. The hound already in the air.

> **SOUND** *(the ring)*
> "KLONNNG"

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Wukong face-down under his own banner, the ring round his
head, the hound's jaws on his leg, Erlang's boot on the staff, soldiers closing
with chains. He is laughing.

> **ERLANG:** "That wasn't me. I want that understood. That was Laozi, from behind."
>
> **WUKONG:** "I saw him on the way down. The army, and you, and the dog, and the old man's bracelet. It took all of you."
>
> **WUKONG:** "Say that in the hall when they ask."

**Why you turn the page:** it took all of them, and he wants Heaven to know it, and Heaven is about to decide what to do with a monkey it cannot kill.

## PAGE 39 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the Hall of Jade.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** The throne axis, seen from far back. Wukong in chains before
the throne, left, the ring off, held by two soldiers, armor scuffed, still
upright. Erlang beside him, lance grounded, the hound sitting. The Emperor
seated, right. Laozi at his elbow. The staff lying on the lowest step where the
soldiers dropped it. A tray of five empty gourds beside the throne.

> **ERLANG:** "It took all of us, uncle. He wanted that said."

**Panel 2 — 25%.** The Emperor, close, on his feet before the throne for the
second time in his life, looking down at the monkey.

> **JADE EMPEROR:** "The Great Sage Equal to Heaven. Kneel."

**Panel 3 — dominant, 50%.** Wukong, close, in chains, looking up at him, chin
up, not kneeling; the soldiers' hands on his shoulders pressing down and getting
nowhere. Erlang watching him, not the Emperor.

> **WUKONG:** "You gave me the title. Equals don't kneel. You had it read out in front of everyone."

**Why you turn the page:** he is in chains in front of the whole court and he has just told the Emperor of Heaven that they are equals; somebody has to answer that.

## PAGE 40 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the Hall of Jade.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 50%.** Laozi coming down to Wukong's level, close,
quiet, no brow-dabbing, the tray of empty gourds in his hands. Wukong in
chains, left; Laozi, right.

> **LAOZI:** "My furnace burns for forty-nine days. Whatever goes in comes out as what it was made of."
>
> **LAOZI:** "You are made of my top peaches, that nobody was ever allowed, and my elixir. I want them back."

**Panel 2 — 20%.** Wukong alone, close, looking straight at Laozi, flat.

> **WUKONG:** "I'm made of a rock."

**Panel 3 — 30%.** The Emperor on the throne, fingers to his temple, eyes
shut. Laozi at the left edge, looking up at him.

> **LAOZI:** "Then we'll get a rock, and I'll get my pills back. Majesty?"
>
> **JADE EMPEROR:** "Do it. And Laozi. This time it works."

**Why you turn the page:** they are going to burn him for forty-nine days to get the pills back, and he just said he is made of a rock as if that helps.
