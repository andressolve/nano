## Page intent

## PAGE 44

Dramatic. Dominant turn: the wager. The Buddha is here, seated, filling the far end of the hall so that the throne sits beside his knee: saffron over one shoulder, a bare curled head, a small smile, one hand open on his knee. Wukong is tiny on the floor before that hand with his staff up. Everyone else has gone to the walls. The Buddha asks what he wants the throne for; Wukong says so they can never say he is nobody, it goes to the strongest, and that is him. The Buddha's hand opens flat, palm up, the size of a courtyard, and he offers a small bet: jump out of the hand and the throne is his; fail, and he goes back to Earth and stays. Wukong climbs onto the palm laughing, looking back at the Emperor: big, but not that big. The Buddha says then go. The reader turns because he is about to leap.

## Exact owner-controlled script

## PAGE 44 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the Hall of Jade.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 55%.** THE BUDDHA, seated, filling the far end of the
hall so that the throne is beside his knee: a saffron robe over one shoulder,
a bare head of tight dark curls, long earlobes, half-closed eyes, a small
smile, one hand open on his knee. Wukong, tiny, on the floor in front of that
hand, staff up. Everyone else has gone to the walls.

> **BUDDHA:** "Heaven asked, so I came. Sit down a moment. What is it you want the throne for?"
>
> **WUKONG:** "So they can't ever say I'm nobody. It goes to the strongest. That's me. Ask them."

**Panel 2 — 20%.** The Buddha's hand opening flat, palm up, the size of a
courtyard.

> **BUDDHA:** "Then a small bet. Jump out of my hand, and it's yours. I'll say so myself."
>
> **BUDDHA:** "If you can't, you go back to Earth, and you stay there."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Wukong climbing onto the palm, laughing, looking back over
his shoulder at the Emperor.

> **WUKONG:** "Out of your hand? One somersault is a hundred thousand miles. You're a very big man, but you're not that big."
>
> **BUDDHA:** "Then go."

**Why you turn the page:** he is standing on the palm of the only being who has ever been polite to him, and he is about to leap.
