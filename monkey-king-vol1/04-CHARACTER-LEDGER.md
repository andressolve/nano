# Character Ledger — 8 locks, hard ceiling

Identity hierarchy, in order of priority:
**1.** face shape and apparent age · **2.** hair and facial-hair mass ·
**3.** body silhouette and habitual posture · **4.** costume value pattern ·
**5.** colour accent, prop, repeated gesture.

> Costume and colour may reinforce a character; they may never be the only
> cues. Separation is built through skull, jaw, nose, eye set, hairline,
> apparent age, body mass and posture — the traits that survive grayscale and
> thumbnail reduction. **Every page prompt names both the positive lock and the
> nearest forbidden lookalike.**

The cast divides into three silhouettes that must never blur: **two monkeys**
(one gold and young, one gray and stooped), **three old men** (one lean and
bearded, one round and bearded, one bare-headed and immense), and **three who
are none of those** (a dragon, a crowned emperor in his prime, a young armored
warrior). The danger pairs are inside the first two groups.

---

## 1 · SUN WUKONG, the Monkey King — apparent age: a youth, ageless

**Anchor:** new.

**Wants (volume):** from everyone, to be counted as somebody. From Subhuti, a
name and the trick that beats death. From Ao Guang, a weapon that is heavy
enough. From Heaven, a real place, not a title. **Wants (secretly):** for
someone to have made him on purpose.

**Moral position:** believes he is claiming what he is owed. Is actually
breaking things until someone says his name with respect. Nobody says this.

**Arc:** born from nothing, crowned → taught, then thrown out → armed →
employed, then insulted → titled, then not invited → unkillable → caught →
tempered → beaten by the one thing that never fights him → waiting.

**Structural lock:** a monkey, not a man. Short heart-shaped face, pale
hairless muzzle and cheek patches against a golden-tawny fur; a wide mouth;
large round amber eyes set wide; low brow; small ears standing out from the
skull; a golden ruff of fur around the head, not hair. Small and wiry, a
head shorter than any human in the book, long arms, a long tail always in
frame. Never stands square: one foot up on something, crouched, mid-leap, or
sitting on a thing meant for sitting under. Habitual gesture: a flat hand
shading his eyes to look far away.

**Costume states (three, all in the reference plan):**
(a) *Stone-born* (pp 1–14): bare, a kilt of leaves, no ornament.
(b) *Great Sage* (pp 18–41): golden scale armor over a red tunic, a
tiger-skin kilt, black cloud-boots, and a phoenix-feather cap with two long
pheasant plumes that curve back over his head. The needle behind his ear;
the staff in his hand: a straight constant-diameter red-lacquered iron
cylinder with an uninterrupted red mid-shaft, blunt gold end caps, and tight
flush gold banding only beside the caps. At needle size, the left end remains
blunt and gold-capped; the right cap is retracted and not visible, exposing one
flat blunt cinnabar-red writing tip immediately beyond the final flush gold
band.
(c) *Furnace-tempered* (pp 42–48): state (b), scorched, with eyes gone gold
and a thin red ring of fire around each iris.

**Never:** gray or silver fur, a stooped back, a shawl, a stick, milky eyes
(Old Ma). A third eye, a human face, a topknot (Erlang). The staff never has
mid-shaft collars, bulbous or projecting end hardware, a tapered point, a
blade, or Erlang's lance silhouette.

**Voice:** says exactly what he thinks to exactly the person he should not,
in short sentences, and laughs in the middle of them.

## 2 · OLD MA, elder of the monkeys — apparent age: very old

**Anchor:** new. Invented; collapses the source's unnamed old monkey and the
named monkey officers into one face.

**Wants (volume):** from Wukong, that he come back. From the troop, that they
stay together. **Wants (secretly):** to see him once more before she dies.

**Moral position:** believes she is keeping him grounded. Is actually the one
who set him going, with one true sentence, and knows it.

**Arc:** first hand on the stone monkey → names the prize → crowns him → tells
him kings die → sends him to the sea → says "come back" → climbs the mountain
with a peach, every year, for as many years as she has; after her, a younger monkey who was told to.

**Structural lock:** a monkey, unmistakably older. Long narrow face, muzzle
gone white, silver-gray fur thinning at the crown, white tufted brows, eyes
pale and half-lidded, deep lines at the mouth. Stooped, small, walks bent
over a peach-wood stick, sits with knees up and arms wrapped around them.
Habitual gesture: a hand laid flat on Wukong's head.

**Costume:** a faded red shawl, the only red on Flower-Fruit Mountain until
the armor arrives. Nothing else.

**Never:** golden fur, amber eyes, armor, a cap, plumes, a tail in motion,
any leap (Wukong). Never any human trait.

**Voice:** short, dry, and kind. Says the hard thing once and does not
repeat it.

## 3 · SUBHUTI, the Master — apparent age: ancient, upright

**Anchor:** new.

**Wants (volume):** from Wukong, that he learn to use what he is given
without being seen using it. **Wants (secretly):** the student he has been
waiting for, and this is him, and he will lose him.

**Moral position:** believes he is protecting the tricks from the world. Is
actually protecting the world from the tricks, and the monkey from the
world, and fails at both.

**Arc:** asks who he is → names him → tests him with three taps → teaches him
at midnight → throws him out for a pine tree → is never seen again.

**Structural lock:** a lean old man, tall, straight as a post. Long narrow
face, high forehead, hooded eyes, a thin high-bridged nose, long white beard
falling in a single narrow point to mid-chest, white hair pulled up into a
topknot with a simple wooden pin. Thin body, no stomach. Stands with hands
folded inside his sleeves. Habitual gesture: a white horsetail whisk flicked
once to end a conversation.

**Costume:** a pale gray-blue robe, one value, no pattern, no gold.

**Never:** a round face, a bald crown, a short square beard, a stomach, a
gourd, anything gold or indigo, a worried expression (Laozi). Never a bare
head, long earlobes, or golden skin (the Buddha).

**Voice:** few words, level, and every one of them a test.

## 4 · AO GUANG, Dragon King of the Eastern Sea — apparent age: old, vast

**Anchor:** new.

**Wants (volume):** from Wukong, to leave. From Heaven, that someone deal
with this. **Wants (secretly):** never to be asked for anything again.

**Moral position:** believes he is being generous to a small guest. Is
actually giving away the sea's anchor to end an awkward conversation.

**Arc:** polite → amused → alarmed → robbed → writing to Heaven.

**Structural lock:** a dragon in the shape of a lord: a long horse-like
dragon head with a scaled blue-green face, two branching antlers, long
white whiskers that float as if underwater, round golden eyes with slit
pupils, a beard of fins. Human body, enormous: seated, he appears about twice
the height of a standing man, and Wukong comes to less than half of him.
Sits on a coral throne and does not get up until the pillar moves. Habitual
gesture: fingers drumming on the throne's arm.

**Costume:** robes of deep sea-green with a pattern of waves, a coral crown,
a pearl at the throat.

**Never:** a human face. Never ordinary-man scale: seated, he must visibly
tower over a standing man, without requiring an exact drafting ratio.

**Voice:** grand, weary, and very polite right up to the edge of panic.

## 5 · THE JADE EMPEROR, ruler of Heaven — apparent age: fifty, in his prime

**Anchor:** new. Absorbs the Queen Mother of the West: the peach banquet is
his.

**Wants (volume):** quiet. Every decision he makes is a way of not having to
think about the monkey any more. **Wants (secretly):** for someone else to
decide.

**Moral position:** believes he is being patient. Is actually being
contemptuous, and never once considers that the monkey might be owed
anything.

**Arc:** reads the letter → offers a job → is defied → grants a title →
throws a party without him → sends an army → sends Erlang → orders the
furnace → is backed against his own throne → says "send for the Buddha."

**Structural lock:** a broad-faced man in his prime, heavy jaw, full cheeks,
black brows, black eyes, a black beard trimmed into three points, black hair
hidden under the crown. Heavy build, seated whenever possible, hands on his
knees. Habitual gesture: two fingers pressed to his temple.

**Costume:** robes of yellow-gold with dragon roundels; the flat-topped jade
crown with strings of beads hanging before his face. The only black beard in
the book.

**Never:** white hair, a white beard, a stoop, a whisk, a gourd (Subhuti,
Laozi). Never standing when he can sit.

**Voice:** short declarative sentences that end conversations, until the one
page where they do not.

## 6 · LAOZI, the Emperor's counselor and alchemist — apparent age: old, round

**Anchor:** new. Absorbs Gold Star of Venus: proposes the job, proposes the
title, then becomes the executioner.

**Wants (volume):** from the Emperor, permission to manage the problem. From
Wukong, that he take the offer. **Wants (secretly):** his elixir back, and to
be right.

**Moral position:** believes every problem can be handled with the correct
offer. Is actually the one who invents the insult (the stable), the empty
title, and the party list, and turns to fire the moment it costs him
personally.

**Arc:** "give him a job" → carries the invitation himself → tells the truth
about the rank → "give him the words" → lets the banquet slip → loses the
elixir → "no more offers" → drops the ring → lights the furnace → has
nothing left.

**Structural lock:** a round old man. Round face, round cheeks, small bright
eyes, a bald shining crown with white hair only at the sides and back, a
short square white beard, a round belly. Short, moves quickly for his age,
stands with his weight forward and both hands busy. Habitual gesture: dabbing
his brow with his sleeve.

**Costume:** deep indigo robes with a gold sash; a gourd on a cord at his hip.

**Never:** a long face, a topknot, a narrow pointed beard, a whisk, a
gray-blue robe, stillness (Subhuti). Never a black beard, a crown, a
seated pose (the Jade Emperor).

**Voice:** warm, quick, reasonable, and always one step ahead, until he isn't.

## 7 · ERLANG, Heaven's champion — apparent age: twenty-five

**Anchor:** new.

**Wants (volume):** a good fight. **Wants (secretly):** to win it on his own,
and he doesn't.

**Moral position:** believes he is Heaven's answer. Is actually the only
person in Heaven who treats the monkey as an equal, and is robbed of his win
by Laozi's ring from behind.

**Arc:** arrives alone → introduces himself → fights shape for shape → laughs
at the flagpole → chases → watches Laozi and the hound finish it.

**Structural lock:** a young man, human, handsome and sharp: a long jaw, high
cheekbones, straight nose, level dark brows, and a third eye set vertically
in the center of his forehead, open and alert. Black hair in a high topknot
under a small silver helmet with a red plume. Tall, athletic, stands square
with his weight even, a three-pointed double-edged lance held upright.
Habitual gesture: the third eye narrowing while the other two stay still.

**Costume:** silver-white armor over a dark tunic, a yellow-gold sash. The
hound at his heel is scenery: a lean gray dog, no balloons.

**Never:** fur, a tail, a muzzle, a ruff (Wukong). Never a beard. Never
plumes that curve back like Wukong's cap; his plume stands up.

**Voice:** amused, direct, sporting. The only one who talks to Wukong the way
Wukong talks to everyone.

## 8 · THE BUDDHA — apparent age: none

**Anchor:** new.

**Wants (volume):** for the monkey to learn to wait. **Wants (secretly):**
nothing. That is the point of him.

**Moral position:** believes nothing needs to be done that cannot be done
sitting down. Is actually the only being in the book who likes Wukong, and
the one who imprisons him.

**Arc:** arrives seated → offers the bet → holds out a hand → turns it over
→ closes it into a mountain → is gone.

**Structural lock:** a serene giant, seated, larger than any room he is in.
Broad smooth face, half-closed eyes, a small smile, long earlobes, golden
skin, a bare head of tight dark curls rising to a small dome at the crown.
Broad shoulders, one hand always visible and open. Habitual gesture: none.
He does not gesture. Everything else in the frame moves instead.

**Costume:** a plain saffron robe over one shoulder. No ornament.

**Never:** a beard, a crown, a topknot, a whisk, a gourd, a standing pose,
a frown. Never small. Never a face that could be Subhuti's with the beard
removed: Subhuti is long and lean; the Buddha is broad and round-shouldered.

**Voice:** slow, plain, and slightly amused. Never a sermon. Asks questions.

## Reserved design space

No background or unnamed figure may inherit a complete identity stack:

- golden fur + tiger-skin kilt + plumed cap → **Wukong**. No other monkey
  wears anything.
- gray fur + red shawl + stick → **Old Ma**. Every other monkey is brown,
  bare, and young.
- long white beard in a point + topknot + gray-blue robe → **Subhuti**
- bald crown + square white beard + gourd → **Laozi**
- black three-point beard + beaded jade crown + yellow robe → **the Jade Emperor**
- third eye + silver helmet with standing red plume → **Erlang**
- bare curled head + saffron over one shoulder + seated giant → **the Buddha**

Heaven's soldiers are scenery: identical black lacquered armor, face-plates
down, no faces. The Master's students are scenery: shaved heads, brown robes,
young, none bearded. Dragon guards are scenery: shrimp and crab soldiers, no
antlers, no whiskers.

## Collision matrix — pairs requiring adversarial boards

| Pair | Risk | Carried by |
|---|---|---|
| Subhuti / Laozi | **highest** | face length (long vs round), skull (topknot vs bald crown), beard (long point vs short square), build (lean vs stout), posture (still, hands in sleeves vs forward, hands busy), robe value (pale vs dark), prop (whisk vs gourd), expression (level vs worried) |
| Wukong / Old Ma | high | fur value (gold vs gray), age (smooth vs lined, white muzzle), posture (mid-leap vs stooped), costume (kilt or armor vs shawl and stick), eyes (round amber vs half-lidded pale) |
| Jade Emperor / Laozi | medium | beard colour (black vs white), head (crown vs bald), age (prime vs old), pose (seated vs standing), robe (yellow vs indigo) |
| Buddha / Subhuti | medium | beard (none vs long), face (broad vs long), head (curled dome vs topknot), scale (giant vs man), robe (saffron vs gray-blue) |
| Wukong / Erlang, in armor | medium | face (muzzle vs human), fur vs skin, third eye, plume direction (back-curving pair vs single standing), armor value (gold vs silver) |
| Wukong (state b) / Wukong (state c) | continuity, not collision | the golden eyes with the red ring must read at proof scale and must not read as a different character; the scorch is on the armor, not the face |

The highest-risk pair is the two old men who share a room in the Hall of
Jade on no page (Subhuti never enters Heaven), but the reader will meet Laozi
six pages after losing Subhuti, and must not think the Master has followed
the monkey to Heaven. That is a re-entry collision, and the adversarial board
tests it as if they stood side by side.
