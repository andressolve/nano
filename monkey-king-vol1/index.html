After the story

Test your understanding

  1. Old Ma has just crowned Wukong king of the Water Curtain Cave, and everyone is happy. Why does he leave the mountain?

    Correct: Old Ma tells him that the three kings before him are dead, and only the immortals don't die. He has just found out that he ends, and his answer is to go and find an immortal and make him teach, so none of them has to die.

    Not quite. He is not bored and nobody drives him out. Old Ma tells him that kings die too, and he decides to find an immortal and make him teach, so that none of them has to die.

  2. Wukong turns himself into a pine tree in the courtyard, and the students clap. Why does the Master throw him out?

    Correct: the Master said the shape is for staying alive and the cloud is for leaving, and that if Wukong used them to be admired he would be sent away alone. Wukong did it anyway, so the Master keeps his word.

    Not quite. The shape was fine and the students were clapping, not complaining. The Master had warned him: use the shape and the cloud to be admired and I will send you away alone. Wukong used them to show off, so the Master does exactly what he said.

  3. Heaven's whole army has just lost to one monkey with a stick. Why does Laozi tell the Jade Emperor to give Wukong a title instead of sending more soldiers?

    Correct: Laozi thinks a title is cheaper than a war. Words cost nothing, a monkey with a title holds still, and soldiers cost more than titles. He never thinks Wukong deserves it; he just wants him to sit quietly in a house.

    Not quite. Laozi does not think Wukong deserves anything, and he is not trying to be his friend. He tells the Emperor that words cost nothing, that a monkey with a title holds still, and that soldiers cost more than titles.

  4. Laozi shuts Wukong in his furnace for forty-nine days. What does Wukong do inside that he has never done before, and why does the furnace not destroy him?

    Correct: he hits the walls for a day, tries the fish and the bird, and they do not work. Then he finds the one corner where the wind comes in and waits, which he has never done for anything. The furnace gives back what a thing is made of, and Wukong is made of a rock, so it cannot burn him away. It only makes him stronger.

    Not quite. The fish did not help, because there was no water, and the lid stayed shut for forty-eight days. What he does is wait, for the first time in his life, curled around one thread of cold air. The furnace gives back what a thing is made of, and he is made of a rock, so it cannot destroy him.

  5. At the end, Wukong is under the Mountain of Five Elements. Why does Old Ma climb up with a peach every year?

    Correct: he cannot move and cannot go home, so Old Ma does what he cannot do and comes to him. She sets a peach where he can reach it, sits with him, and gives him back the words the Buddha gave him: someone will come, wait. It is her way of helping him wait.

    Not quite. The peach is not magic and nobody ordered her. Wukong cannot come back to the mountain, so Old Ma comes to him, every year, and says the Buddha's words back to him: someone will come, wait.
