# Settings — Foundation: The Plan

## 1. Imperial hypership interior (P1 — done)
Observation lounge of a chrome imperial liner. Curved viewport onto space, banks of amber-lit consoles, uniformed crew. Cool steel blues + amber instrument glow. (As rendered on the validated P1.)

## 2. Trantor from orbit (P1 — done; reusable for finale imagery)
The entire planet sheathed in bronze-teal metal: towers, ridges of cityscape instead of continents, traffic of ships. Monumental chrome liner in foreground, red-and-blue livery, "IMPERIAL LINER 88".

## 3. Trantor interior — the metal world (P2)
Vast enclosed avenue-canyons, no sky, layer upon layer of structure receding into teal haze; rivers of moving walkways and lights; crowds tiny against the scale. Cool teal/steel palette, amber artificial suns.

## 4. Seldon's office, Streeling University (P3, P7)
The one district open to real sky. Ivory-toned buildings (not steel grey), warm daylight. Office: spare scholar's room — desk, data-spools, a glowing hand-calculator pad, wide window onto ivory towers and open blue sky. In P7 a small table-projector casts a luminous galaxy spiral.

## 5. Luxor Hotel room / corridor (P4)
Comfortable imperial-modern hotel interior, suddenly hard-edged: uniformed Commission soldiers (scarlet-trimmed grey, sidearms holstered) at the door. Shadows go cold.

## 6. Trial hall of the Commission of Public Safety (P5 — done; P6 anchors on it)
Cathedral-scale dark green-black marble hall. High tribunal bench with five aged commissioners in scarlet-and-gold robes and close-fitting judicial caps; huge gold imperial starship emblem on the wall; dusty amber light shafts from high slots. Seldon stands in a raised dock at right; Advocate declaims at left.

## 7. Terminus — colony vista (P8, P15 window view)
A bare young world: ochre-brown plains, thin pale-blue sky, low white domed-and-terraced colony city with ONE landmark tower (the Encyclopedia Building, a stepped white ziggurat with a bronze emblem). Sparse green cultivation strips. Frontier optimism, small against emptiness.

## 8. Terminus interiors — City Hall / Board room (P9, P10, P12, P15)
Modest by imperial standards: pale plastered walls, functional furniture, slate-blue and warm-wood tones, amber desk lamps. The Board room: long table, five trustee chairs, wall chart of the Encyclopedia's volumes. Hardin's office: cluttered, a window onto the city, a bottle of Vegan tobacco smoke haze optional (skip smoking for kids — keep desks and starmaps).

## 9. Time Vault (P13, P14)
A plain, purpose-built chamber: bare walls, rows of simple chairs for the Board, one empty glass-walled cubicle at the center. Near-dark. When the recording activates, the cubicle fills with the luminous blue-white hologram of an old man in a powered wheelchair — the only light source, casting pale glow on stunned faces.

## 10. Galaxy spiral (P7 projection, P16 finale)
'70s airbrush cosmic: a great spiral galaxy in teal and magenta with a dense amber core (Trantor's heart of empire) and a single hot amber spark at the outermost rim tip (Terminus). On P16 a faint thousand-year arc of light sweeps rim-to-core.
