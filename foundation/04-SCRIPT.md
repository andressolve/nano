# Script — Foundation: Book One — The Plan

16 pages + cover. All lettering verbatim — copy word-for-word into prompts. Speakers staged LEFT-TO-RIGHT in speaking order (lettering rulebook #1). Bubbles ≤ ~8 words unless a signature line owns its panel.

## COVER

Monumental chrome imperial liner (red-and-blue livery) sweeping low over the bronze-teal metal world of Trantor, galaxy spiral rising behind in teal/magenta, one hot amber star at the spiral's rim. '70s SF paperback poster composition with title zone at top.

- Top, huge chrome '70s sci-fi block letters: `FOUNDATION`
- Beneath it, smaller amber serif band: `BOOK ONE · THE PLAN`

## P3 — PSYCHOHISTORY *(PROTOTYPE 1 — dialogue-dense two-character page)*

**Anchor:** `composite_act1_gaal_seldon.png`. **Layout:** two top panels + wide bottom. Setting 4 (Seldon's office, ivory University, open sky in window).

- **Panel 1 (top-left), medium:** SELDON at his desk holding a glowing hand-calculator pad, equations of soft light rising from it.
  - Bubble, tail to the old man: `Psychohistory. The mathematics of human crowds.`
  - Bubble below it, tail to the old man: `One man is unpredictable. Quadrillions are not.`
- **Panel 2 (top-right), close-up:** GAAL's face lit by the pad's glow, awed and troubled.
  - Bubble, tail to the young man: `Your equations say the Empire is dying.`
- **Panel 3 (wide bottom):** both bent over the pad between them — SELDON left, GAAL right; the glowing figures reflect on their faces.

  - Bubble, tail to the young man on the RIGHT: `Within three centuries. Probability 92.5 percent.`
  - Caption, cream band bottom: `The Empire had stood for twelve thousand years. It looked eternal. Only the mathematics could see that it was already falling.`

## P16 — THE PLAN (closing-as-invention)

**Anchor:** none (generate). **Layout:** single full-bleed cosmic image, no panels. Setting 10.

A great spiral galaxy in teal and magenta fills the page; its dense amber core slowly dimming; at the outermost rim tip, a single hot amber spark blazes. A faint luminous arc sweeps from the spark along the rim toward the core — the thousand-year road. Ghosted enormous and faint among the stars behind the spiral: the lined, gently smiling face of an old man (Seldon lock, translucent, starfield showing through).

**LETTERING:**
- Caption, cream band top: `The Empire would take three centuries to die. The darkness after it would last one thousand years — not thirty thousand — because a handful of scientists had been placed on a bare rock at the edge of everything, like a seed, at exactly the right moment.`
- Caption, cream band bottom: `Seldon had promised them a thousand years of crises, each with only one way through. The first was over. The Plan had begun.`
- Small cream tag, bottom right corner: `END OF BOOK ONE`
