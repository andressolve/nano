## Ged / Sparrowhawk

- Eighteen years old, lean and wiry.
- Deep red-brown copper skin; never pale, tan-white, or olive.
- Straight rough black hair, sharp brows, hawk-like nose and gaze.
- Four pale healed scars on the left cheek from beside the eye toward the jaw.
- Plain grey-beige tunic, dark trousers and boots, weathered charcoal cloak, plain yew staff.
- He begins publicly composed and privately exhausted. By Osskil his face is wind-burned, sleepless, and frightened, but never aged into a middle-aged man.

- Hoeg does not speak, glow, cast magic, or attack the gebbeth.

## The shadow and gebbeth law

- There is no separate shadow reference. Use the approved matte-black treatment in Part Three Page 15 and the new dual-state Skiorh sheet.
- The shadow is absence, not black skin or dark clothing. Human dark-skinned characters retain visible features, reflected light, and ink texture; the shadow does not.
- The gebbeth has Skiorh's exterior clothes but none of Skiorh's visible body or identity.
- It has no speech balloon except the explicitly scripted true-name calls.
