Every page uses the Style Block and Lettering Restrictions in `01-STYLE-GUIDE.md`, the listed identity and environment refs, an approved finished Part Three page as visual-register reference, and the attribution rules in `../earthsea-wizard-part1/05-SPEECH-ATTRIBUTION-STUDY.md`.

## Cover - Hunted

One cinematic image with no panel grid. Night on the Osskil moor under sparse snow. Ged runs toward the viewer across dead heather, copper-skinned, scarred, exhausted, one hand gripping a plain yew staff whose tip carries the smallest white-gold defensive ember. Tiny Hoeg is barely visible inside his cloak. Behind him, Skiorh's empty clothing advances with the peaked hood containing absolute flat matte black—no face, eyes, bones, smoke, or texture. Far ahead and above them, the pale Court of the Terrenon stands alone, its open gate a single rectangle of steady warm-white light. The gate must look like possible refuge and possible trap at once.

Lettering:

- Title: `A WIZARD OF EARTHSEA`
- Subtitle: `PART FOUR · HUNTED`
- Credit: `FROM THE NOVEL BY URSULA K. LE GUIN`

No balloons, taglines, runes, page numbers, or extra text.

Refs: scarred Ged, Hoeg, Skiorh/gebbeth, Terrenon exterior. Route: `low-torning`.
