Every page uses the Style Block and Lettering Restrictions in `01-STYLE-GUIDE.md`, the listed identity refs, an approved finished Part Two page as visual-register reference, and the attribution rules in `../earthsea-wizard-part1/05-SPEECH-ATTRIBUTION-STUDY.md`.

## Cover - The Dragon of Pendor

One cinematic image with no panel grid. Ged stands in a tiny narrow boat in the lower foreground, copper-skinned and scarred, yew staff planted for balance. Beyond him, the drowned towers of Pendor rise from iron-grey water. Yevaud is coiled around the highest ruin, so immense that stone and scale merge. Two lean juvenile dragons bank through storm cloud. Across the water behind Ged stretches a flat, impossible matte-black shadow that belongs to no object and has no face or texture. Restrained furnace-gold in Yevaud's eye; all else cold maritime grey.

Lettering:

- Title: `A WIZARD OF EARTHSEA`
- Subtitle: `PART THREE · THE DRAGON OF PENDOR`
- Credit: `FROM THE NOVEL BY URSULA K. LE GUIN`

No balloons, taglines, runes, page numbers, or extra text.

Refs: scarred Ged, Yevaud, juvenile dragons. Route: `ninety-isles`.

## Page 1 - The Salt Roads

Three panels, widening into arrival.

- Panel 1: high wide view over the Ninety Isles at changing tide: low islets, reed beds, mudbanks, footbridges, and dozens of narrow boats using water as roads.
- Panel 2: Ged at the rail of the modest westbound vessel from Part Two, studying how islanders pole and sail between rocks. Hoeg peeks from his cloak.
- Panel 3: the ship leaves him and his bundle at a tiny Low Torning dock. A cluster of dark-skinned islanders waits beside the Head Isle-Man. Pendor is only a bruise on the far western horizon.

Lettering:

- Caption P1: `West of Roke, land and water changed places with every tide. The people of the Ninety Isles traveled salt roads that vanished twice a day.`
- Caption P2: `Ged came with a wizard's staff and no wish for honor. He had learned what honor could cost.`
- Caption P3: `Low Torning had asked Roke for help. It was the poorest charge offered, and the first Ged chose.`

No speech balloons.

Refs: scarred Ged, Hoeg, Low Torning people. Route: `ninety-isles`.

- Panel 3: Ged stands alone inside: narrow bed, hearth, table, dry roof. Hoeg drops from his cloak to sniff the floor.
- Panel 4: close on Ged at the doorway, looking back at the waiting people with softened expression.

## Page 11 - He Goes After Him

- Panel 1: wide sickroom. Ged sits rigid beside Ioeth, eyes closed, staff across his knees. Pechvarry, wife, and witch keep vigil. His physical body remains clearly present.
- Panel 2: extreme close view of Ged's hand loosening on the staff as sound and lamplight seem to recede.
- Panel 3: full-width Dry Land threshold. Color drains away. Spirit-Ged stands on a vast ash-grey slope; far below, a small child figure walks away into starless distance.

Lettering:

- Caption P1: `Ged sent his awareness after the life that was slipping from the room.`

## Page 15 - What Waited

Full-page metaphysical splash. The low wall runs across the middle distance under a starless black sky. Ged stands on the Dry Land side, ash-grey but recognizably copper-skinned beneath the drained palette, one hand on the stones. On the living side, directly across from him, the shadow is absolute flat matte black: scarcely human-shaped, faceless, reaching without motion. Behind it, almost lost, is the warm pinprick of the sickroom lamp. Vast negative space and silence. No monsters, smoke, eyes, or blue glow.

Lettering:

- Caption top: `The shadow had found him.`
- Caption bottom: `Death lay behind him. The hunter waited ahead. Only one way led back to the living.`

No speech balloons.

Refs: scarred Ged. Route: `dry-land`.

- Panel 3: he runs and leaps the stones as the shadow reaches across his path.
- Panel 4: the sickroom explodes back around his body in one hard white-gold blaze from the plain yew staff. The shadow recoils into matte black at the panel edge; every human in the room remains physically present.

Lettering:

- Caption P1: `He could retreat into death and never be taken.`
- Caption P2: `Or return afraid, guilty, and alive.`

## Page 27 - Dragon Against Dragons
