# 03 — SETTINGS

All grounded in the gritty documentary register. Dust, haze, hard light, one hot accent.

- **NIGHT FRONTLINE POSITION** (P1, P13) — a sandbagged/earth-revetment position at the edge of a field or
  treeline, 3 a.m., near-black with the turret's green sensor glow and an orange muzzle flash. Cold, tense.
- **DAYLIGHT FIELD / RUINED GROUND** (P2, P3) — flat war-worn farmland, broken vehicle in the background,
  overcast desaturated light. Human scale (Lena).
- **DARK SCHEMATIC VOID** (P4, P7, P10, P12 — POSTER pages) — charcoal/slate technical-plate background,
  no environment, central object lit, amber/green leader lines. Field-manual look.
- **SENSOR / SCREEN INTERIOR** (P5, P6) — close on the turret's sensor head and on the operator's screen
  view (target boxes, thermal palette). Greens and grays.
- **OPEN SKY ENGAGEMENT** (P8, P9, P14) — pale haze sky, the drone small against it, tracer or jam-wave
  toward it. Dust and grain.
- **NAVY SHIP DECK + KOREAN BORDER** (P11) — split or two-zone: a gray warship deck with the white-domed
  Phalanx Gatling firing; and a fenced border line with a sentry-gun post. Documentary, no people harmed.
