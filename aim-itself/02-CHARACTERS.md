# 02 — CHARACTERS (locked subjects)

Machines are the "characters" — locked with ref sheets exactly like human faces in the bios.
Lock the **visual**, never the name. Re-read the ref PNG before every page (per gemini_thin.md C.4).

---

## 1. THE TURRET — "Sentinel" (HERO)

A ground counter-drone autonomous turret. The protagonist of the book.

**LOCK:**
> A squat robotic gun turret on a heavy tripod / low armored base. A **12.7 mm heavy machine gun** on a
> motorized two-axis gimbal (it can swivel 360° and tilt up at the sky). Above the gun, a **sensor head**:
> a small flat **radar panel**, a gimbaled **electro-optical / infrared camera ball** (a dark glassy
> sphere), and two stub antennas. Matte **olive-drab and gunmetal**, weathered, dust-caked, cabling
> running to an ammo box and a battery. A faint **phosphor-green glow** from the sensor optics. Reads as
> alert and alive — the sensor cluster is its "head." Realistic military hardware, NOT a cartoon robot,
> NOT a Hollywood mech.

Signature marks: the glassy green-lit camera ball; the flat radar panel; the up-tilted heavy barrel.

## 2. THE FPV DRONE — the threat (small)

The cheap first-person-view attack drone.

**LOCK:**
> A small **quadcopter / racing-style FPV drone**, about the size of a dinner plate. Black **carbon-fiber
> X-frame**, four motors with black propellers, a small forward **camera** and a whip **antenna**, and an
> **anti-tank grenade warhead zip-tied underneath** (a stubby cone-tipped charge). Scuffed, taped, field-
> improvised. Fast and menacing. NOT a toy, NOT glossy consumer-drone styling.

## 3. THE SHAHED-STYLE DRONE — the bigger threat (secondary, P2/P11/P14)

**LOCK:**
> A pale gray **delta-wing loitering munition**, about 3.5 m wingspan, blunt nose (warhead), a small
> **pusher propeller at the tail**, simple fixed fins. Cheap, mass-produced look, gray primer finish.
> Drones along like a moped engine. (This is the slow droning threat, distinct from the small FPV.)

## 4. LENA — the operator (HUMAN ANCHOR)

One recurring human so the reader has a face. A counter-drone technician.

**LOCK:**
> A woman counter-drone technician, **about 30**, **olive-green field fatigues** and a plate carrier,
> short dark hair tied back, a headset over one ear, weathered hands. Calm, focused, tired. Holds a
> **ruggedized control tablet**. Realistic adult proportions. (Appears on the human-scale pages: P2, P3,
> P12. The machine works without her on the auto pages.)

Lock the visual, not "a Ukrainian soldier" — describe the fatigues/headset/tablet, never a named army.
