# 01 — STYLE GUIDE

## Register (NEW this volume — deliberately distinct from Sky Duel)

This is **not** the glossy aerospace-poster look of Sky Duel. This volume reads like a **frontline field
manual crossed with conflict photojournalism** — gritty, desaturated, documentary.

### STYLE BLOCK (paste verbatim into every page prompt, block 1)

> Gritty modern military-technology illustration — the look of a frontline field manual crossed with
> conflict photojournalism. Desaturated palette: gunmetal gray, olive drab, concrete, cold steel-blue,
> dust and haze in the air. A single hot accent per image — muzzle-flash orange, tracer line, or
> night-vision / thermal phosphor green. Harsh directional light, deep shadow, grain and grit, handheld
> documentary framing. Painted photographic realism. NOT glossy, NOT a glamour poster, NOT a video-game render.

### REGISTER / ANTI-DRIFT BLOCK (paste verbatim, block 2)

> Serious adult military-technology book. Real hardware, realistic proportions, realistic materials.
> NOT a comic. NO halftones, NO cel shading, NO ink linework, NO clean vector look. NOT a children's book,
> NOT a cartoon, NOT cute.

## Two page modes

**CINEMATIC** (10 pages) — single composed photographic scene. Night frontline, daylight field, ship, border.
Dust, haze, hard light, one hot accent. Caption bands top and/or bottom.

**SCHEMATIC POSTER** (4 pages: P4, P7, P10, P12) — a **dark technical field-manual plate**, NOT warm ivory.
Dark slate / charcoal background, the central object painted in the round, thin **amber or phosphor-green
technical leader lines** to callout boxes. This replaces Sky Duel's warm-ivory engineering poster with a
"military schematic" look that fits the gritty register. Callout boxes: dark, pale bold sans text.

## Caption / lettering formula (hold across the whole volume)

- **Caption bands & boxes:** "dark charcoal caption band, pale bold sans-serif text, high contrast, readable."
  Full-width top/bottom bands on cinematic hero pages (≈30–70 words each). One formula, held every page.
- **On-image labels** (sensor parts, target boxes, method rows): short, ALL-CAPS, pale text on a dark tag.
- **No speech bubbles needed** in most pages (machines, not dialogue). If a human line appears, round dark
  bubble, pale text, under 12 words, NO inner quotation marks.
- Open the lettering block with `LETTERING — verbatim, render exactly:` and close with the restrictions block:
  `All words spelled correctly. Do not duplicate text. Do not invent extra captions. NO modern logos, NO watermarks, NO spurious signage. Every word in plain English with NO accent marks on any letter.`

## Palette / accent

- Base: gunmetal #2b2f33, olive #4b4f3a, concrete #8a8f8c, steel-blue #3a4a5a.
- Hot accents (one per image): muzzle orange #e8772e, tracer/thermal green #5fb86a, thermal white #eef2e8.
- **Reader accent color: phosphor green `#5fb86a`** (Sky Duel was blue, Scattergun brass — this is green,
  the night-vision/thermal signature). Dark flipper reader, same family as the other tech explainers.

## Safety framing (non-negotiable)

The target is always a **drone — a machine**, never a person. No human casualties depicted. Muzzles point
up-range at the sky. The SGR-A1 history beat is framed as "the gun that watches and aims by itself," never by
what it shoots. This framing kept Scattergun at zero safety-filter rejections; hold it here.
