# Salt and Stone — Life Three Settings

## ATHENS — PIRAEUS AND CITY (Pages 1-4)

```
SETTING: ATHENS / PIRAEUS
- The port district and city, 4 years after Salamis. Athens at its most confident and expansionist.
- Piraeus shipyards: busy, loud, prosperous. New triremes being built constantly for League expeditions.
- Nikos's small home: simple whitewashed walls, terracotta roof, courtyard with a fig tree. Modest but stable.
- The Agora: busier than in Life Two. More construction. Political tension visible — Themistocles is gone (ostracized), new leaders rising.
- Palette: bronze gold, warm stone, deep harbor blue — carry from Life Two but slightly warmer, more domestic.
- These pages should feel settled, comfortable — but also slightly claustrophobic. The city is closing in.
```

## THE SEA / STORM (Pages 5-7)

```
SETTING: EASTERN AEGEAN / BLACK SEA APPROACH
- An Athenian trireme on a League expedition. Oar banks, cramped deck, salt spray.
- The storm: massive waves, dark sky splitting with lightning, the ship breaking apart.
- The water: dark, cold, violent. Wreckage. Bodies.
- Palette: storm gray, dark ocean blue, white foam, black sky — brief return to Life Two's darkness.
- This is the LAST time we see the sea. After this, Nikos is landlocked for the rest of his life.
```

## BLACK SEA COAST — LANDING (Pages 8-9)

```
SETTING: BLACK SEA NORTHERN COAST
- A rocky, wild coastline. Not Mediterranean — colder, grayer, wilder.
- Behind the beach: grassland begins almost immediately. No trees on the horizon.
- The transition zone: where the world Nikos knows ends and the steppe begins.
- Palette: muted gray-green coast, pale sky, first hints of steppe gold inland.
```

## THE STEPPE (Pages 10-20)

```
SETTING: PONTIC STEPPE (modern-day Ukraine/southern Russia)
- Vast grassland stretching to every horizon. The sky is enormous — 2/3 of every composition.
- Grass moves in waves like the sea. Golden-green in sun, silver in wind.
- The Scythian camp: a cluster of felt-covered wagons and round tents (yurt-like structures). Horses everywhere.
- Cooking fires, drying hides, children playing, dogs. A functioning community, not a primitive camp.
- Night sky: star-packed, no light pollution. The Milky Way visible as a bright band.
- Seasonal shifts: early pages are late summer (green-gold), later pages shift to autumn (amber, dry gold).
- Palette: steppe green, golden grassland, pale sky blue, earth brown, amber firelight.
- CRITICAL: This should feel like the most beautiful place Nikos has ever been — but also the harshest. Wind never stops. Nights are cold. The ground is hard. Beauty and difficulty coexist.
```

## THE HORIZON (Final Page)

```
SETTING: THE OPEN STEPPE — DAWN
- The final image. Nikos on horseback, riding east into a sunrise over endless grass.
- The horizon is unbroken — no cities, no ships, no walls. Just land and sky.
- Palette: golden sunrise, steppe green below, pale sky above. The warmest, most open image in the entire trilogy.
- This is the visual opposite of Life One's opening (a small island, sea all around). Now: infinite land, no sea at all.
```
