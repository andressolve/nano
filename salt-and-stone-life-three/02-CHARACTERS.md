# Salt and Stone — Life Three Character Definitions

## CRITICAL: How Character Locking Works

The **reference image** does 80% of the character locking. The text lock block below is reinforcement only. Keep it short.

For every page prompt:
1. **Upload the reference image(s)** for all characters in the scene
2. Paste the SHORT lock block below
3. Include the phrase: "featuring the same characters shown in the reference images"

Do NOT pad lock blocks with atmospheric or redundant description.

## CHARACTER: NIKOS (The Man — age 19-20)

**Reference sheet prompt:**
```
create image: Subject:
Character reference sheet showing a 19-20 year old Greek man in two views side by side: close-up portrait on the left, full body on the right.

Character Description:
Young man, 19-20, medium-tall build with broad shoulders and lean muscle from years of physical labor. Mediterranean olive skin, weathered and sun-darkened. Strong jaw with short dark stubble. Dark brown hair, thick, slightly longer than military cut, wind-tousled. Dark brown eyes, deep-set, watchful — the eyes of someone who has seen too much for his age. Strong nose, slightly crooked (broken at some point). A thin scar runs along his left palm. A burn scar on his right forearm. A shell necklace on a leather cord hangs at his throat — worn smooth, never removed.

Left panel — Portrait:
Close-up head and shoulders. Calm, steady expression. Looking directly at viewer. Stubble visible. Shell necklace visible at throat. Weathered skin.

Right panel — Full body:
Standing 3/4 view. Wearing a simple off-white linen chiton (knee-length, belted at waist with leather), leather sandals, leather wrist wraps. Shell necklace visible. Burn scar on right forearm visible. Confident, grounded stance — not heroic, just solid.

Art Style: Cinematic painterly realism with restrained brush texture, historical graphic novel aesthetic
Background: Simple warm neutral gradient
Lighting: Soft even studio reference lighting

General Instruction:
This is a reference sheet for character consistency. Both views must show the exact same person. Prioritize clarity. He should look 19-20, not younger.

Aspect Ratio: 4:3 (landscape)
```

**Lean lock block (paste into page prompts):**
```
NIKOS (19-20, Greek): Broad-shouldered, olive skin, dark stubble, dark brown windswept hair. Shell necklace on leather cord. Scar on left palm. Burn scar on right forearm. Dark brown deep-set eyes.
```

**Outfit changes across the chapter:**
- Pages 1-4 (Athens): Off-white linen chiton, leather belt, sandals
- Pages 5-7 (Sea): Bare-chested or torn chiton, soaked
- Pages 8-10 (Early steppe): Salvaged chiton, barefoot, ragged
- Pages 11-20 (Steppe life): Scythian clothing — leather tunic, wool trousers, fur-lined coat, leather boots. Still wears the shell necklace.

**Which pages:** ALL (1-20, cover, back cover)
