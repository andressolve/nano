# 01 — STYLE GUIDE

## Register
Oil-painting realism, matching the Newton / Gauss / da Vinci / Honda volumes. This is a Gilded-Age / Belle-Époque electrical drama: gaslight giving way to the cold blue-white of the electric arc. The single visual signature of the book is **electric blue-white light cutting through warm period gloom** — sepia rooms, gunmetal machines, and one hot source of arc-light.

## STYLE BLOCK — paste verbatim into every page prompt
> Oil-painting realism, cinematic period illustration. Muted Gilded-Age palette: warm sepia and umber interiors, gunmetal and brass machinery, deep shadow — pierced by cold electric blue-white light (arcs, filaments, sparks) as the single hot accent. Painterly brushwork, dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, film-still composition, shallow depth of field. Late-19th / early-20th-century America and rural Austrian Empire. Visible canvas texture, no photographic sheen.

## REGISTER BLOCK — paste verbatim into every page prompt
> Oil-painting realism. NOT a comic. NO halftones, NO cel shading, NO ink linework. Painted brushwork, cinematic lighting, muted period palette.

## ANTI-DRIFT DIRECTIVE — paste verbatim into every page prompt
> NOT a children's book. Serious mature graphic novel, realistic proportions, natural lighting, cinematic composition.

## Palette
- **Base:** warm sepia, umber, oxblood, aged-paper cream, gaslit amber for pre-electric scenes.
- **Machinery:** gunmetal grey, blackened iron, polished brass, copper.
- **THE ACCENT:** electric blue-white (`#cfe8ff`→white core) for arcs, sparks, filaments, lightning, the glow of AC. Use sparingly and let it dominate the one thing it touches. This is the book's through-line: wherever Tesla's idea is alive, the cold blue light appears.
- **Twilight/hotel scenes (P1, P15–P17):** desaturated, cold grey-blue and dim lamp-amber; the arc-light is gone — only a single warm bulb and the grey city. The *absence* of the electric blue in the late pages is deliberate and emotional.

## Camera language
- Cinematic single-image pages (default). Low angles for the towering Tesla and for machines; intimate eye-level for the hotel/pigeon beats.
- Strong chiaroscuro; rim-light figures against dark labs; silhouette Tesla against arc-light or the Wardenclyffe tower.
- Montage finale (P18): four-to-six zones unified by the rotating-field motif at center.

## Narration treatment (the Honda formula)
- **Caption boxes:** "off-white box, dark serif text, readable." Hold this style across the whole volume.
- **Hero pages (T5):** a top caption band sets the moment, a bottom band closes it; 50–80 words each is fine on gpt-image-2.
- **Speech bubbles:** round, off-white, dark serif, tail explicitly described. Under 15 words. **No quotation marks inside bubbles — the bubble shape IS the quote.**
- **Quote artifacts:** the *Faust* lines (P5), the pigeon line (P16), and the 1927 future-quote (P17) are rendered as caption-band text in the period serif, treated as the page's emotional artwork.
- **Lettering block** opens with `LETTERING — verbatim, render exactly:` and lists each element by position + exact string. Close every prompt with the restrictions block.

## In-image non-English / archaic text → always an English helper
- The *Faust* lines (P5) are English (Bayard Taylor translation) — no helper needed, but caption them as "from Goethe's *Faust*" so a first-time reader knows what the words are.
- Latin/German signage if any gets an English gloss in the caption.

## Restrictions block — close every prompt with this
> All words spelled correctly. Do not duplicate text. Do not invent extra captions. NO modern logos, NO watermarks, NO spurious signage, NO modern cars or electronics in period scenes.

## Period-accuracy guardrails
- 1856–1900 scenes: gaslight, frock coats, oil lamps, horse carts, brick-and-iron labs. The electric light is the *new, miraculous* thing — treat it that way.
- 1900–1943 scenes: early electric New York, the Hotel New Yorker (Art Deco, opened 1930) for the late frame.
- Machinery is brass/iron/copper — Tesla coils, dynamos, the magnifying transmitter, the latticed Wardenclyffe tower (mushroom-domed top).
- NO anachronisms: no modern Tesla-car logo, no LED-blue, no contemporary skylines except as stylized "the world he built" in the P18 montage (kept painterly/timeless, not literal modern brands).

## Anti-drift watchlist
- Tesla must read **tall, gaunt, elegant, formal** — never cute, never heroic-musclebound. Realistic proportions.
- Keep the blue accent disciplined; if the whole page glows blue it loses meaning. One source of arc-light.
- Old Tesla (P1, P16, P17) must look genuinely ~86 and frail — hollow-cheeked, white-haired — not a lightly-aged prime Tesla.
