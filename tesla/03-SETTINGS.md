# 03 — SETTINGS

Recurring locations. Keep them consistent across pages.

## Smiljan village (1856–1860s) — P2
Rural mountain village, Military Frontier of the Austrian Empire (now Croatia). Stone-and-timber house, low Orthodox church nearby, wooded hills, livestock, ox-carts. Warm earth tones, candle/hearth light, big open frontier sky. Pre-industrial, poor, dignified.

## The sickroom (~1873) — P3
A modest bedroom in the family home. Iron bed, a single candle or oil lamp, religious icons on the wall, a frost-grey window. Sickly, hushed, shadowed. The priest-father at the bedside. Warm amber lamp against cold blue window light — the first appearance of the blue/warm tension.

## Graz Polytechnic lecture hall (1875) — P4
A tiered 19th-century European university lecture theatre, dark wood, chalkboard, gaslight. A Gramme dynamo (a brass-and-iron DC machine with a visible commutator throwing small sparks) on the demonstration bench. Rows of students. Authority and ridicule.

## Budapest city park at sunset (Feb 1882) — P5
A formal European city park: gravel paths, bare or budding February trees, wrought-iron benches, the low golden-orange sun. Cold dusk air. The most lyrical setting in the book — warm sunset gold, long shadows, a stick drawing in the dirt path.

## The laboratory (1884–1900) — P6, P7, P9, P12
Tesla's American electrical lab: brick walls, tall windows, workbenches crowded with coils, dynamos, glass tubes, copper and brass apparatus, leather belts to overhead shafts. Dark, cathedral-like, lit by the cold blue-white of arcs and the warm glow of early bulbs. The Colorado Springs station (P12) is a big barn-like wooden shed on the high plains with the giant magnifying transmitter and a 142-ft mast.

## Westinghouse's office / boardroom (1888–1891) — P8, P10
A solid Gilded-Age industrial office: dark paneling, a big desk, rolled drawings, a window onto smokestacks. For P10 (torn contract), add the weight of a financial crisis — ledgers, anxious light, the contract document on the desk.

## Niagara Falls / Adams Power Plant (1896) — P11
The great falls thundering; the powerhouse with its huge Tesla polyphase generators (massive iron-and-copper machines); power lines marching toward the glow of Buffalo on the horizon. Awe, scale, mist, and the cold blue of live current. Triumph.

## Wardenclyffe, Shoreham, Long Island (1901–1917) — P13, P14
A flat, empty Long Island field. The latticed 187-ft tower with its mushroom-domed copper top, the low brick lab beside it. Overcast, lonely, vast sky. For P14 (failure): grey, cold, the tower dark and then being dynamited — dust and collapse.

## The Hotel New Yorker, Room 3327 (1934–1943) — P1, P15, P16, P17
A small, plain upper-floor Art-Deco hotel room. A narrow bed, a desk, a window onto the cold grey Manhattan skyline. Pigeons on the sill. Dim single-bulb lamp-amber against grey city light. **No electric blue here** — the arc-light of his life is gone; only the warm lamp and the grey window. Lonely, hushed, dignified poverty.

## The montage world (P18)
Not a literal place — a unified painterly composition: power lines, lit cities at night, spinning motors, factory floors, a wall socket, a hydro dam — all radiating from the rotating-magnetic-field motif at center, washed in the book's electric blue. Old Tesla and the white pigeon small at the center. Timeless, not modern-branded.
