# 02 — CHARACTERS

**Rule:** lock blocks describe the VISUAL, never the famous name. The name lives in the ref filename and the script, not in the prompt the model sees. Re-Read each ref PNG before writing any page prompt and write a one-line verbatim observation.

All reference sheets: **1536×1024 landscape**, single figure (or object), **plain warm-toned neutral background, neutral expression, no text, no labels, no border.** Oil-painting realism + register block + anti-drift directive prepended.

## TESLA — three age phases

### ref_tesla_young.png  (~20–28: Graz P4, cholera-youth P3, Budapest P5, NY arrival P7)
**Lock:** A very tall, very thin young man of about 24, pale fair skin, lean angular face with high cheekbones and a long straight nose. **Black hair, parted precisely in the exact middle and combed back flat and sleek.** Neat dark mustache. Deep-set, pale, piercing light eyes (grey-blue), intense and serious. Long thin elegant hands. Ramrod-straight, formal posture. Dressed in a modest but immaculate dark frock coat, high stiff collar, dark cravat — slightly threadbare on the arrival page but always meticulously neat. Aristocratic, hungry, fastidious. Realistic adult anatomy, NOT cute.
**Ref prompt:** half-length portrait + a smaller full-length standing figure on the same sheet, three-quarter view, the lean tall young man described above, plain warm-grey background, soft museum lighting, no text.

### ref_tesla_prime.png  (~35–43: P8 Westinghouse, P9 War, P10 torn contract, P11 Niagara, P12 Colorado, P13 Wardenclyffe)

Pale skin, long angular clean-jawed face, high cheekbones, long straight nose. **Jet-black hair parted dead-center and combed sleekly back; trim neat black mustache.** Deep-set pale piercing eyes, magnetic and commanding. Impeccable dark formal three-piece suit, high white wing collar, dark silk cravat or bow, sometimes grey gloves. Upright, theatrical, supremely controlled posture; long expressive hands. Realistic proportions.
**Ref prompt:** half-length portrait + smaller full-length standing figure, the impeccably-dressed tall dark-haired inventor described above, plain warm-grey background, dramatic side light, no text.

### ref_tesla_old.png  (~80–86: P1 frame, P15 lonely years, P16 pigeon, P17 death)
**Lock:** The same man now very old, about 84 — extremely tall and skeletally thin, stooped slightly. Deeply hollowed cheeks, sunken temples, gaunt lined face, prominent cheekbones and long nose now sharp with age. **Thin white-grey hair, still parted in the middle and combed back; clean-shaven or only the faintest grey stubble** (mustache gone). Deep-set, pale, weary but still piercing eyes. Long bony hands. A worn but still-formal dark suit, slightly too big on the shrunken frame, high old-fashioned collar. Frail, dignified, lonely. Realistic elderly anatomy.
**Ref prompt:** half-length portrait + smaller full-length seated figure, the gaunt very old white-haired man described above, plain warm-grey background, soft cold window light, no text.
