# Script - A Wizard of Earthsea, Part One: The Boy of Gont

Every page uses the Style Block and Lettering Restrictions in `01-STYLE-GUIDE.md`, plus the listed character refs. All text below is verbatim production lettering.

## Cover

Single cinematic image. Duny stands on a high wet Gont ridge, small against the mountain and sea. Behind him, mist fills the steep lanes of Ten Alders. Ahead, far below, a dark ship waits in the bay. Ogion is a distant still figure on the path between boy and ship. Cold slate palette with one small forge-gold light in the village.

Lettering:

- Title: `A WIZARD OF EARTHSEA`
- Subtitle: `PART ONE: THE BOY OF GONT`
- Credit: `after the novel by Ursula K. Le Guin`

Refs: Duny, Ogion.

## Page 1 - The Mountain

Three panels: wide top establishing Gont; two equal bottom panels at Ten Alders.

- Panel 1: Gont rising from the sea, its peak in storm cloud; tiny villages on its flanks.
- Panel 2: twelve-year-old Duny drives goats through wet bracken, looking toward the open horizon instead of the animals.
- Panel 3: at the bronze forge, his father pulls him back to the bellows; Duny resents the command but obeys.

Lettering:

- Caption P1: `Gont is one mountain standing in the sea. Its people are poor, stubborn, and famous for two things: goats and wizards.`
- Caption P2: `High on its northern slope lived a bronzesmith's youngest son. His mother had named him Duny.`
- Father P3: `The fire does not care where your thoughts wander. Work the bellows.`
- Duny P3: `I am working.`
- Caption P3: `His hands obeyed. The rest of him was always elsewhere.`

Refs: Duny, Gont family.

## Page 2 - The Goats' Word

Three panels: two top, one wide bottom.

- Panel 1: Duny overhears his aunt mutter to a goat that has climbed onto her roof.
- Panel 2: alone on the hillside, he repeats the sounds experimentally.
- Panel 3: every goat crowds around him in uncanny stillness; his aunt has witnessed it from the path.

Lettering:

- Caption P1: `His father's sister lived above the village. People came to her for fever-charms, love-knots, and curses they would not name aloud.`
- Aunt P1: `Down, fool beast. Down and be still.`
- Duny P2: `Those were words of power.`
- Aunt P3: `Where did you learn that call?`
- Duny P3: `From you.`
- Aunt P3: `Then say it once more. Slowly.`

Refs: Duny, Gont family.

## Page 3 - A Witch's Teaching

Four panels in a measured grid.

- Panel 1: aunt closes her smoky hut and draws a binding circle around Duny.
- Panel 2: she teaches him wind-knots and bird calls using bone, feather, and herb tokens.
- Panel 3: Duny makes a spark leap between his fingers, delighted; the aunt watches with possessive calculation.
- Panel 4: outside at dusk, Duny tests the charm alone and the wind answers too strongly.

Lettering:

- Aunt P1: `Power runs in you. Until you learn its banks, it will flood wherever it pleases.`
- Caption P2: `She knew the small magic of villages: mending, finding, binding, calling. She taught him every word she dared.`
- Duny P3: `What comes after these?`
- Aunt P3: `First learn obedience.`
- Caption P4: `He learned the words. Obedience was harder.`

Refs: Duny, Gont family.

## Page 4 - Sparrowhawk

Three panels: two top, one wide bottom.

- Panel 1: village boys mock Duny beside the goat pens; a sparrowhawk circles above.
- Panel 2: Duny calls the hawk down; it wheels close over his raised arm and the boys fall silent.
- Panel 3: sunset over the distant coast; red fires burn on another headland while villagers gather beneath the great yew.

Lettering:

- Boy P1: `Call your goats, witch-boy.`
- Duny P2: `I call what I choose.`
- Caption P2: `After that they called him Sparrowhawk. It was only a use-name, safe for anyone to speak.`
- Villager P3: `Those are not shepherd fires.`
- Father P3: `Armouth is burning.`
- Caption P3: `Kargish raiders had come to Gont.`

Refs: Duny.

## Page 5 - The Choice to Stay

Four panels: village meeting, intimate reaction, departure, forge.

- Panel 1: frightened villagers argue beneath the yew; smoke stains the distant sky.
- Panel 2: Duny sees his aunt quietly taking food and charms toward the mountain caves.
- Panel 3: his father remains at the forge while others prepare to flee into the forest.
- Panel 4: Duny steps to the bellows beside him.

Lettering:

- Villager P1: `They have ships enough for an army. We have hunting bows.`
- Another villager P1: `Take the children into the forest before dark.`
- Duny P2: `You're leaving?`
- Aunt P2: `I know which battles can be won.`
- Father P3: `I will not leave fifty years of work for strangers to burn.`
- Duny P4: `Then tell me what to do.`

Refs: Duny, Gont family.

## Page 6 - The Forge Night

Three panels progressing through the night.

- Panel 1: villagers hammer rough spearheads while Duny works the great goat-hide bellows.
- Panel 2: father fits hot bronze to hoe and rake handles; Duny's arms tremble.
- Panel 3: near dawn, father gives Duny a crude spear and sees that the boy can barely lift it.

Lettering:

- Caption P1: `All night the forge burned. Farmers brought hoes, hooks, and every length of straight wood they owned.`
- Father P2: `Steady. Metal remembers every careless blow.`
- Duny P2: `So do sons.`
- Father P3: `You have done enough. Go with the younger children.`
- Duny P3: `I am not a child today.`

Refs: Duny, Gont family.

## Page 7 - Waiting in the Fog

Three panels: village geography remains clear.

- Panel 1: dawn fog fills the lane; villagers wait behind fences and walls.
- Panel 2: pale yellow-haired Kargish raiders emerge faintly at the lower end near the tannery.
- Panel 3: Duny lowers the spear his exhausted arms cannot hold and studies the moving fog around the springs.

Lettering:

- Caption P1: `Morning brought the enemy and a common mountain fog.`
- Father P1: `Hold until they enter the lane.`
- Caption P2: `The Kargs had armor, swords, and the certainty of men attacking the helpless.`
- Duny P3: `The fog is already here.`
- Duny P3, second: `It only needs telling where to go.`

Refs: Duny, Gont family.

## Page 8 - The Weaving

Four panels with increasing fog.

- Panel 1: Duny kneels at the spring, gathering mist with both hands and speaking.
- Panel 2: his father angrily pulls him upright as the Kargs approach unseen.
- Panel 3: the fog suddenly thickens until father and son are ghostlike silhouettes.
- Panel 4: Duny directs his father toward a familiar side path.

Lettering:

- Father P2: `Be still! Hide if you cannot fight.`
- Duny P2: `Listen to me.`
- Duny P3: `I have hidden all of us.`
- Duny P4: `Lead them uphill toward High Fall. We know every wall and turning. They know none.`
- Father P4: `Can you hold this?`
- Duny P4: `As long as I can.`

Refs: Duny, Gont family.

## Page 9 - Warriors in the Mist

Three dynamic panels: two top, one wide bottom.

- Panel 1: young villagers dart through fog, taunting and vanishing; Kargs wheel toward false movement.
- Panel 2: Gontish hunters loose arrows from behind a stone wall; Duny strains unseen at the spring.
- Panel 3: fog-shapes like towering villagers surge along the lane, driving the frightened Kargs toward High Fall and away from the homes.

Lettering:

- Karg P1: `There! No, behind us!`
- Village boy P1: `Come catch the mountain, yellow-hair!`
- Caption P2: `Every fence concealed a defender. Every footpath became an escape.`
- Karg P3: `Sorcery! The dead are walking!`
- Caption P3: `The invaders fled from enemies made of mist.`

Refs: Duny.

## Page 10 - The Cost

Three panels after the action.

- Panel 1: wind tears the fog apart, revealing damaged homes, dropped weapons, and exhausted villagers; no gore.
- Panel 2: father finds Duny standing alone beside the yew, eyes open but unresponsive.
- Panel 3: father carries the slight boy into the forge-house while villagers silently make way.

Lettering:

- Caption P1: `By full morning the Kargs were running for their ships. Ten Alders still stood.`
- Father P2: `Duny.`
- Father P2, second: `Boy, look at me.`
- Caption P3: `No blade had touched him. He could not speak, eat, sleep, or hear.`
- Villager P3: `He spent everything he had.`

Refs: Duny, Gont family.

## Page 11 - The Silent Mage

Four panels over five days.

- Panel 1: aunt tries charms over Duny's bed; nothing changes.
- Panel 2: the story of the fog travels down mountain paths with traders and shepherds.
- Panel 3: Ogion walks down from the high forest into Ten Alders, staff in hand.
- Panel 4: he touches Duny's forehead and lips; the boy finally focuses and takes breath.

Lettering:

- Aunt P1: `His power ran beyond my reach.`
- Caption P2: `But the tale of the boy and the fog travelled farther than any plea for help.`
- Villager P3: `A healall has come down from the mountain.`
- Father P4: `You are no village sorcerer.`
- Ogion P4: `Nor will your son be a common man.`

Refs: Duny, Ogion, Gont family.

## Page 12 - What the Boy Needs

Three panels around Duny's bed and forge hearth.

- Panel 1: Duny eats broth, watching Ogion while father and aunt question him.
- Panel 2: aunt recognizes the Mage of Re Albi; father remains guarded.
- Panel 3: Ogion speaks directly to Duny, not over him.

Lettering:

- Aunt P1: `You are Ogion the Silent. The mage who stopped the earthquake.`
- Father P2: `What do you want with my son?`
- Ogion P2: `To give him his name when he comes of age. Then, if he wills it, to teach him.`
- Ogion P3: `Power kept ignorant turns against its bearer.`
- Duny P3: `I will learn.`
- Ogion P3: `First you will choose.`

Refs: Duny, Ogion, Gont family.

## Page 13 - The True Name

Three panels: preparation, passage, naming.

- Panel 1: on Duny's thirteenth birthday, the aunt removes his childhood name before the gathered village.
- Panel 2: nameless and dressed in a plain knee-length passage shift, he walks through the icy Springs of Ar under moving cloud shadows.
- Panel 3: Ogion clasps his arm on the far bank and whispers the true name into his ear.

Lettering:

- Caption P1: `At thirteen a child of Earthsea passes out of childhood. The old name is taken away.`
- Caption P2: `Nameless, he crossed the living water alone.`
- Ogion P3, whisper: `Ged.`
- Caption P3: `His true name belonged to him and to those he trusted with his life. To the world he would be Sparrowhawk.`

Refs: Duny, Ogion, Gont family.

## Page 14 - Leaving Ten Alders

Four panels from feast to road.

- Panel 1: a rough village feast; Ged keeps glancing toward Ogion.
- Panel 2: father gives him a well-made bronze knife without ceremony.
- Panel 3: aunt gives him a charmed alder staff; neither embraces him.
- Panel 4: Ged and Ogion leave through bright autumn forest; Ged looks back once at everyone he knows.

Lettering:

- Father P2: `A traveller should own one thing that will not fail him.`
- Ged P2: `You made this for me?`
- Father P2: `Who else?`
- Aunt P3: `The staff knows the paths above the Vale.`
- Ogion P4: `Come, Sparrowhawk.`
- Caption P4: `The feast continued behind him. His old life did not.`

Refs: Duny, Ogion, Gont family.

## Page 15 - The Apprenticeship Begins

Four panels across several walking days, with consistent clothing and geography.

- Panel 1: Ged imagines himself and Ogion as hawks over the mountain, shown as a pale thought-vignette.
- Panel 2: reality: they walk quietly through wet forest carrying their own packs.
- Panel 3: Ged finally asks when the teaching will begin.
- Panel 4: Ogion stops beside a small seedpod and asks Ged to look at it.

Lettering:

- Caption P1: `Ged expected secret names, transformations, and storms called by a lifted hand.`
- Caption P2: `For seven days they walked. Ogion cast no spell.`
- Ged P3: `When does my apprenticeship begin?`
- Ogion P3: `It began when we left your village.`
- Ogion P4: `What is this?`
- Ged P4: `A seed. What use is it?`

Refs: Duny, Ogion.

## Page 16 - Listening

Three panels in a rainstorm.

- Panel 1: Ogion studies the seedpod and explains; Ged tries to follow.
- Panel 2: hard rain begins; Ogion simply shelters beneath a fir while Ged waits for a weather spell.
- Panel 3: Ged crouches soaked and angry; Ogion rests with eyes closed, entirely at peace.

Lettering:

- Ogion P1: `Know it in root, leaf, flower, scent, seed, and season. Then its name may mean something in your mouth.`
- Ged P1: `But what can it do?`
- Ogion P1: `It is alive. That is not a small thing.`
- Ged P2: `You could send this rain over the sea.`
- Ogion P3: `To hear, one must be silent.`
- Caption P3: `Ged heard only the rain.`

Refs: Duny, Ogion.

## Page 17 - Winter at Re Albi

Four panels showing a stable routine rather than a plot summary.

- Panel 1: arrival at Re Albi under first snow, sea and Great Port far below.
- Panel 2: Ogion shows Ged his sleeping alcove and the shuttered west window.
- Panel 3: Ged studies large runes at the hearth while winter wind shakes the house.
- Panel 4: Ogion returns with firewood; Ged has kept food warm for him. Their quiet companionship is visible.

Lettering:

- Caption P1: `At Sunreturn they reached Re Albi, the Falcon's Nest, high above the sea.`
- Ogion P2: `This is your place while you choose to remain.`
- Caption P3: `All winter Ged learned the runes from which true speech begins.`
- Ged P4: `There is soup.`
- Ogion P4: `Then we are fortunate.`
- Caption P4: `He came to love the silence before he understood it.`

Refs: Duny, Ogion.

## Page 18 - White Hallows

Four panels in one spring encounter.

- Panel 1: Ged gathers rare white flowers in a wet meadow between streams.
- Panel 2: the pale dark-haired daughter of Re Albi's lord approaches and recognizes him as Ogion's apprentice.
- Panel 3: she asks for magic; shy Ged summons a falcon from the cliff.
- Panel 4: the falcon circles but refuses his wrist; the girl watches without offering praise.

Lettering:

- Girl P2: `You are Sparrowhawk, the mage's apprentice.`
- Ged P2: `Who asks?`
- Girl P2: `Someone who has never seen a great spell.`
- Girl P3: `Call me that falcon.`
- Ged P3: `Watch.`
- Girl P4: `It did not come all the way.`
- Caption P4: `He heard the challenge she had not spoken.`

Refs: Duny, young Serret.

## Page 19 - The Boast

Four panels in a second meadow encounter two days later.

- Panel 1: Ged returns with a herb basket, pretending surprise to find the girl waiting.
- Panel 2: they gather flowers together; she asks increasingly dangerous questions.
- Panel 3: close on Ged claiming more knowledge than he possesses.
- Panel 4: she leaves uphill; Ged watches, humiliated by his own uncertainty.

Lettering:

- Girl P1: `I thought a wizard might change his shape.`
- Ged P2: `A wizard might.`
- Girl P2: `And call a spirit back from death?`
- Ged P3: `If he chose.`
- Girl P3: `You speak often of choosing.`
- Girl P3, second: `I wonder what you can do.`
- Caption P4: `He went home determined to answer her.`

Refs: Duny, young Serret.

## Page 20 - The Open Book

Three panels growing darker.

- Panel 1: Ogion is away; Ged opens the oldest lore book beside the hearth in afternoon light.
- Panel 2: he finds the summoning and reads farther than he intends while the fire gutters.
- Panel 3: total night inside the house; by the shut door an absolute matte-black crouching shape reaches toward him.

Lettering:

- Caption P1: `He searched for a changing spell. Pride led him deeper into the book.`
- Ged P1: `I will show her something no village witch could imitate.`
- Caption P2: `The runes described a spirit summoned from the country of the dead.`
- Caption P3: `When he looked up, he had been reading without light.`
- Whisper P3, from shadow: `Ged.`

Refs: Duny. Shadow rule applies.

## Page 21 - At the Door

Three panels, immediate continuation.

- Panel 1: Ged is frozen in the chair as the black shape stretches nearer.
- Panel 2: the door bursts open around Ogion, whose staff burns with clean white-gold light; the shadow vanishes.
- Panel 3: Ogion lights the ordinary hearth and closes the lore book while Ged sits shaken and ashamed.

Lettering:

- Ogion P2: `Away.`
- Caption P2: `One word, spoken with knowledge, drove the darkness out.`
- Ogion P3: `That spell could have cost your power and your life.`
- Ged P3: `I meant to find a different spell.`
- Ogion P3: `Tell me why.`

Refs: Duny, Ogion. Shadow rule applies.

## Page 22 - Two Roads

Four intimate fireside panels with clear alternating speakers.

- Panel 1: Ged admits the girl's goading; Ogion explains that she and her mother serve unknown powers.
- Panel 2: Ged turns shame into anger and accuses Ogion of withholding teaching.
- Panel 3: Ogion answers without anger and offers a real choice.
- Panel 4: Ged looks between Ogion's oaken staff and the dark west window toward the sea.

Lettering:

- Ogion P1: `Power is never a game played for praise. Before an act, a mage must know its price.`
- Ged P2: `How can I know, when you teach me nothing?`
- Ogion P2: `Now you have seen something.`
- Ogion P3: `Stay, and learn what I know in my slow way. Or I will send you to Roke, where the high arts are taught.`
- Ged P4: `I love you, Master.`
- Ged P4, second: `But I choose Roke.`

Refs: Duny, Ogion.

## Page 23 - The Great Port

Three panels during the descent and arrival.

- Panel 1: Ged and Ogion descend fifteen miles from the Overfell; the city and crowded bay grow below them.
- Panel 2: armed guards at the dragon-carved gate kneel to Ogion; Ged stares at his quiet master in new understanding.
- Panel 3: the Great Port overwhelms Ged with ships, merchants, ropes, languages, and the smell of salt.

Lettering:

- Caption P1: `A few days later they walked down from the mountain toward the first city Ged had ever seen.`
- Guard P2: `Honor to the Mage of Re Albi, who stilled the mountain.`
- Ged P2: `You never told me they remembered.`
- Ogion P2: `The mountain remembers. That is enough.`
- Caption P3: `For the first time, Ged stood beside the sea.`

Refs: Duny, Ogion.

## Page 24 - The Ship Called Shadow

Four panels with a deliberate final beat.

- Panel 1: harbourmaster presents a broad trading ship bound east; sailors load barrels beneath its carved serpent prow.
- Panel 2: Ogion gives Ged a sealed letter and warns him not to command sea winds he does not understand.
- Panel 3: brief farewell; Ogion walks away without looking back while Ged stands suddenly alone.
- Panel 4: wide final panel, the ship pulls from the quay at dawn; Ged is small among the rowers, watching Gont and Ogion recede.

Lettering:

- Harbourmaster P1: `The ship sails for the Inmost Sea. Her name is Shadow.`
- Ogion P2: `Give this to the Warder of Roke. Learn before you command.`
- Ged P3: `Master--`
- Ogion P3: `Go with a fair wind, Sparrowhawk.`
- Caption P4: `He had chosen the quicker road. The mountain fell behind him.`
- Final caption P4: `END OF PART ONE`

Refs: Duny, Ogion.
