# A Wizard of Earthsea - Part One: The Boy of Gont

## Purpose

This is an expanded pilot for the Earthsea adaptation. It tests whether the existing graphic register can support a complete, easy-to-follow narrative when the story is given enough pages.

The shipped `../earthsea/` edition remains untouched. This pilot expands the material formerly compressed into its first four pages.

## Scope

- Source coverage: Chapters 1-2 only, from Duny's childhood in Ten Alders through his departure aboard the ship *Shadow*.
- Length: cover + 24 landscape pages.
- Arc: an angry, gifted boy discovers power, saves his village at real cost, receives his true name from Ogion, and then chooses fast mastery over the slower wisdom his teacher offers.
- Ending: Ged watches Ogion leave the quay and steps aboard *Shadow*. The choice is complete; its consequence lies ahead.

## Editorial Standard

Each major scene must contain:

1. Orientation: where, when, and who is present.
2. Desire: what the viewpoint character wants now.
3. Interaction or action.
4. A visible turn.
5. Reaction or consequence.
6. A clear bridge to the next scene.

No caption may resolve an important conflict that the page never dramatized. Named recurring characters are introduced before the plot depends on them. Time compression is allowed only after a stable relationship or routine has been shown.

## Visual Standard

The shipped edition is the visual source of truth. Preserve its etched black line, flat muted maritime color, weathered parchment captions, off-white speech bubbles, realistic anatomy, copper-skinned Archipelagans, and restrained warm light. The pilot changes pacing, not style.

## Source Policy

Story facts were checked against the official Penguin sample of the novel and the existing project research. Lettering is original adaptation prose except for the already verified six-word line, "To hear, one must be silent." Do not invent quotations and present them as Le Guin's text.
