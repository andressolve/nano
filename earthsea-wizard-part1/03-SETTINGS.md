# Settings

## Gont

A single mountain rising a mile from a storm-grey sea. Weather changes rapidly across its slopes. Alder forest, steep paths, springs, mist, exposed cliffs, and distant sea are recurring geography.

## Ten Alders

A poor highland village strung along one muddy rising lane: stone-and-timber huts, the great yew, a tannery, goat pens, Duny's father's open-sided bronze forge, and the cold springs of the Ar above the village. The same landmarks recur during the raid so the reader can follow the action spatially.

## The Road Around Gont

Forests and mountain meadows curving south and west around the peak. The road alternates between tiny villages, exposed ridges, rain-heavy woods, and nights beneath trees. Ged expects spectacle; Ogion attends to roots, weather, and silence.

## Re Albi

The Falcon's Nest on the Overfell: a cliff-edge town above the Great Port, with the sea far below. Ogion's timber house is one large room with a stone hearth, goatshed, sleeping alcove, rune books, drying herbs, and a west window shuttered against winter gales. The white-hallows meadow lies between two rain-filled streams above town.

## Great Port of Gont

Ged's first city and first direct encounter with the sea: dragon-carved landgate, tall stone buildings, wet quays, fur merchants, shouting sailors, barrels, ropes, oars, and ships crowded in the sheltered bay between the Armed Cliffs.
