# Character Locks

## Duny / Ged

Ref: `refs/ref_ged_young.png`

A wiry boy of twelve to thirteen with red-brown copper skin, straight black hair roughly cut, fierce dark eyes, sharp hawk-like features, and a slight strong build. No scars. At Ten Alders he is barefoot in a rough undyed wool tunic with an alder goatherd staff. After the naming he adds a dark travel cloak, leather coat, and bronze knife. Proud, impatient, observant; never cute or soft-featured.

## Ogion

Ref: `refs/ref_ogion.png`

A tall lean man past middle age with dark copper-brown skin, grey hair tied back, deep calm eyes, and a still weathered face. Plain brown homespun cloak and tunic, tall oaken staff. He never hurries or performs authority.

## The Bronzesmith

Refs: `refs/ref_bronzesmith.png` (solo production crop), `refs/ref_gont_family.png` (group source).

Duny's father: a broad, weathered dark copper-brown man in his late fifties, iron-grey beard, scarred forearms, leather forge apron, heavy practical hands. Stern, proud, emotionally inarticulate. His love is expressed through work and useful gifts.

## The Witch of Ten Alders

Refs: `refs/ref_witch_aunt.png` (solo production crop), `refs/ref_gont_family.png` (group source).

Duny's aunt: a lean bent dark copper-skinned woman in her sixties, sharp hooded eyes, grey-streaked braided hair, dark layered village wool, bone and herb charms. Clever, possessive, and limited; not a grotesque caricature.

## The Girl of Re Albi

Ref: `refs/ref_serret_young.png`

A tall pale white-skinned girl of about fourteen with long straight black hair, grey eyes, fine dark brows, and costly but restrained white-and-grey clothing. Watchful, coaxing, and hard to read. Her stillness contrasts with Ged's eagerness.

## Kargish Raiders

Recurring crowd, no solo ref. Pale white skin, long yellow hair, bronze and leather armor, plumed helms, round shields, spears, and torches. Foreign to Gont; never dark-skinned.
