# Character Ledger — 8 locks, hard ceiling

Identity hierarchy, in order of priority:
**1.** face shape and apparent age · **2.** hair and facial-hair mass ·
**3.** body silhouette and habitual posture · **4.** costume value pattern ·
**5.** colour accent, prop, repeated gesture.

> Costume and colour may reinforce a character; they may never be the only
> cues. Separation is built through skull, jaw, nose, eye set, hairline,
> apparent age, body mass and posture — the traits that survive grayscale and
> thumbnail reduction. **Every page prompt names both the positive lock and the
> nearest forbidden lookalike.**

Five humans and three monsters. The humans are spread on purpose: a young
giant, an old thin king, a queen, a lean dark sneerer, a slight boy — and the
young giant grown old. The three monsters are separated by shape before
colour: Grendel a gaunt bald man-shape, his mother a long-jawed
weed-haired water-wolf, the dragon a serpent with wings.

**Beowulf is one character with two locked states.** Young (pp 6–35) and old
(pp 37–55) never share a page. What carries him across fifty years: the
badly-set nose with its flattened mid-bridge break, the white notch through
the left eyebrow, the cleft chin, the pale blue eyes, the long hair tied back
at the nape, the size of his hands, and Wealhtheow's gold neck-ring (from p 22
on).

## 1 · BEOWULF — 19 (young state)

**Anchor:** new.

**Wants (volume):** for the world to know his name; to pay Hrothgar back the
debt of his father's life; for nobody ever to laugh at him again.
**Wants (secretly):** to be sure he is not, after all, the slow boy by the
fire.
**Moral position:** he believes glory is what a man is for; he is actually a
young man whose courage is real and whose boasting is a wound. Nobody says
this aloud.

**Arc:** the boy they laughed at → the stranger who boasts → the bare-handed
victor → the diver into the black lake → the man who returns a failed sword
without a word of blame → the young hero who doesn't hear the warning.

**Structural lock:** very tall and broad in the shoulder but still rangy, a
young man not yet thickened — long arms, enormous hands. A broad open face
with wide cheekbones and a square, cleft chin; clean-shaven; a broad, compact
nose broken once, with one obvious flattened angular bump at mid-bridge and
the lower bridge set slightly toward his right. Pale blue eyes, a little
narrowed when he grins; thick straight brows, the left one split at its outer
third by a bold narrow white scar that removes one complete vertical strip of
brow hair and extends just beyond the brow above and below. No other facial
scars. Long tawny-gold hair pulled back and tied at the nape with a leather
thong, loose strands at the temples. Fair skin, wind-reddened. Posture: chest
forward, chin up, a grin that arrives before his words; when he means
something, he goes very still.

**Costume:** a knee-length mail shirt over a dark wool tunic, a deep sea-blue
wool cloak pinned at the right shoulder with a round bronze brooch, leather
boots and cross-gartered legs; a boar-crested iron helmet carried, rarely
worn. Unarmed and in tunic only for the night of Grendel (pp 16–19).
**Accent:** the sea-blue cloak; from p 22, Wealhtheow's twisted gold neck-ring.
His own sword: a narrow, straight, unpatterned iron-grey blade, brown leather
grip, compact warm-bronze guard, and matching lobed pommel.

**Never:** a beard or stubble (young state); a bodybuilder's bulging
physique; Unferth's dark hair; any second facial scar or scratch; serene or
modest; a horned helmet.

**Voice:** loud, cheerful, cocky, quick with a joke, then suddenly plain and
deadly serious. "Then let me pay it back."

## 1b · BEOWULF — about 70 (old state)

**Structural lock:** the same face fifty years on: the crooked nose, the
notch through the left brow, pale blue eyes now pouched and deep-set, the
cleft chin under a **short, full, close-trimmed white beard**. Long white
hair still pulled back and tied at the nape. Still the biggest man in any
room but heavy now, thick through the chest and neck, shoulders a little
rounded, deep lines from nose to mouth, a weathered, spotted forehead.
Enormous old hands. Posture: stands slowly, then stands like a wall; rubs the
gold neck-ring with his thumb when he is thinking.

**Costume:** old, good mail; a faded blue cloak with the bronze brooch; the
gold neck-ring; the boar-crested helmet; from p 43 the **iron shield** (ordered p 40, forged p 41), a
plain dark round shield all of iron. His sword **Naegling**: long, grey, old,
a narrow worn blade, a silver-banded grip, a broad down-curved warm-gold guard,
and a matching lobed pommel. **Accent:** the faded blue and the gold ring.

**Never:** Hrothgar's long flowing beard or thin frame; a crown; frail or
bedridden before p 49; serene before p 50.

**Voice:** slower, drier, fewer words, the old grin still there. "He was
right. It's all three."

## 2 · HROTHGAR — about 70

**Anchor:** new.

**Wants (volume):** his hall back, and his people safe in it; then, having
it, to give the young man who won it everything — including the warning.
**Wants (secretly):** to have been the one who could have done it.
**Moral position:** he believes a king's greatness is his generosity; he is a
good old man who has had to watch, helpless, for twelve years.

**Arc:** the builder of the hall → the grieving king → the host of a stranger
→ the father who adopts → the friend who loses his friend → the old man who
warns and weeps.

**Structural lock:** tall, very thin, long-boned, slightly stooped. A long
narrow face, high cheekbones, sunken cheeks; deep-set grey eyes, kind and
tired; a long straight nose. **A very long, flowing white beard to the
waist**, and long thin white hair loose to the shoulders under a plain gold
circlet. Long thin hands, heavy gold rings. Posture: sits deep in his high
seat; leans on a carved staff when he stands; reaches out with both hands to
the people he loves.

**Costume:** a long dark crimson robe with a gold-woven border, a grey fur
mantle. **Accent:** the gold circlet and the crimson.

**Never:** a short beard (old Beowulf); bulk; mail; a horned helmet.

**Voice:** slow, warm, formal, a little sad; speaks in full sentences like an
old song. "From now on I'll love you like a son."

## 3 · WEALHTHEOW — about 40

**Anchor:** new.

**Wants (volume):** the hall safe; then, after the victory, to make sure the
hero and her husband remember her small sons.
**Wants (secretly):** for someone to promise her the boys will be safe after
Hrothgar is gone.
**Moral position:** she believes a queen holds the hall together with the
cup; she does.

**Arc:** the cup-bearer in a hall of fear → the queen who thanks the
stranger → the mother who asks the hero a favour and gives him the ring.

**Structural lock:** tall and upright, strong-shouldered. A long oval face,
a strong straight nose, level dark brows, steady hazel eyes, a firm mouth that
rarely smiles. Dark auburn hair in two heavy braids wound with gold thread,
under a fine white linen veil held by a gold band. Posture: perfectly
straight; carries the great gold cup in both hands, high.

**Costume:** a long forest-green gown, a long apron-dress with two oval gold
brooches at the shoulders joined by strings of amber beads. **Accent:** the
great gold cup; then the twisted gold neck-ring she gives away.

**Never:** blue; blonde hair; a crown like Hrothgar's; a fragile pose.

**Voice:** clear, measured, queenly; every sentence has a purpose. "Be kind
to my sons."

## 4 · UNFERTH — about 35

**Anchor:** new.

**Wants (volume):** to stay the cleverest man in the hall; then, after
Grendel, to be forgiven without having to ask.
**Wants (secretly):** to have been brave enough to face Grendel himself.
**Moral position:** he believes he sees through braggarts; he is a jealous
man who turns out to be generous when it costs him most.

**Arc:** the mocker → the shamed → the silent one under the claw → the man
who lends his famous sword → the man handed it back without blame.

**Structural lock:** lean, wiry, medium height, narrow-shouldered. A narrow,
sharp, hollow-cheeked face; a thin high-bridged nose; heavy-lidded dark eyes
under arched black brows; a thin mouth that sneers on one side. **Black hair
cut close, and a short black pointed beard** trimmed to a point at the chin.
Olive skin. Posture: sits at the king's feet on a low stool, one knee up,
elbow on it; leans back to talk.

**Costume:** a black wool tunic with a silver-embroidered collar, a black
cloak, a silver arm-ring. His sword **Hrunting**: a long pattern-welded blade
with wavy grey markings like flowing water, a hilt wrapped in silver wire, a
compact warm-metal guard, and a matching lobed pommel.
**Accent:** silver against black.

**Never:** fair or long hair; a long beard; bulk; a villain's snarl after p
21.

**Voice:** smooth, quick, mocking, fond of a well-made sentence. "It has
never failed anyone who held it."

## 5 · GRENDEL

**Anchor:** new.

**Wants:** silence in the dark; the hall gone.
**Arc:** the listener in the fen → the raider → the hand that meets a grip
→ the one who runs.

**Structural lock:** a man-shape twice the height of a man and wrong in every
proportion: gaunt and stooped, a narrow chest with every rib showing, arms so
long the knuckles nearly drag, huge hooked black claws. **Hairless.** A long
skull with a heavy ridge of brow, deep sockets with eyes that glow like coals,
a wide lipless mouth. Hide the grey-green of wet stone, scaled on the arms and
shoulders. Posture: hunched, head low and forward; moves in sudden lunges.

**Costume:** none. **Accent:** the ember eyes. Always half in shadow; never
seen whole and fully lit.

**Never:** hair; wings; speech; a comic or pitiable face; his mother's long
jaw.

**Voice:** none. No balloons.

## 6 · GRENDEL'S MOTHER

**Anchor:** new.

**Wants:** her son's arm back, and the man who took it.
**Arc:** the shape in the lake → the raider → the guardian of the hall under
the water → the one who falls to the giant's sword.

**Structural lock:** larger and heavier than Grendel, powerful through the
shoulders. A long wolfish jaw full of teeth, a flat nose, pale eyes that shine
green like a fish's in the dark. **A mass of long, matted black-green hair
like river-weed** hanging to her waist. Dark blue-black hide, wet-shining, pale
underneath; webbed, clawed hands. Posture: low and crouched, then rearing
high.

**Costume:** none; a bone-handled long knife at the bottom of the lake.
**Accent:** the green eyes and the weed hair.

**Never:** Grendel's bald head or ember eyes; speech; a human woman's face.

**Voice:** none. No balloons.

## 7 · WIGLAF — about 17

**Anchor:** new.

**Wants (volume):** to be worthy of his family's old king; to know whether
heroes are afraid.
**Wants (secretly):** to be in the songs.
**Moral position:** he believes heroes are born brave; he finds out they
decide to be.

**Arc:** the boy in the ashes → the boy who asks "were you ever afraid?" →
the one who stays → the heir.

**Structural lock:** slight and lanky, still growing, narrow-shouldered.
A narrow face with a long straight nose, big dark brown eyes, dark brows that
lift when he's worried, a wide mouth; beardless. **Short, thick, dark-brown
curly hair.** Light olive skin, freckles across the nose. Posture: stands
too straight when he is frightened; grips his shield rim with both hands.

**Costume:** a too-big mail shirt (his father's), a russet tunic, a plain
iron cap-helmet; a round **yellow linden-wood shield** — it burns on p 48;
an old short sword. From p 52: Beowulf's gold neck-ring and boar helmet.
**Accent:** the yellow shield; then the gold ring.

**Never:** fair or straight hair; young Beowulf's breadth or cleft chin; a
beard; confidence before p 47.

**Voice:** quick, earnest, polite, a little too fast. "Were you ever afraid?"

## 8 · THE DRAGON

**Anchor:** new.

**Wants:** its gold, every piece, counted; the thief.
**Arc:** asleep on the hoard → awake and burning → killed by two men.

**Structural lock:** a long serpent fifty feet from snout to tail, thick as
an oak at the chest, coiling; four short clawed legs; great ragged bat-like
wings. A long armoured head with a ridge of backswept horns and a jaw like a
crocodile's; eyes like molten gold. Scales bronze and black, with seams of
ember glow between them, brightest when it breathes; a crest of spines from
skull to tail.

**Accent:** the ember-seamed scales and the river of fire.

**Never:** speech; four big legs like a lizard-horse; a friendly face; any
rider.

**Voice:** none. No balloons.

## Reserved design space

No background or unnamed figure may inherit a complete identity stack:

- huge young clean-shaven man with tawny hair tied back, blue cloak → **Beowulf (young)**
- huge old man with short white beard, white hair tied back, gold neck-ring → **Beowulf (old)**
- thin old man with waist-length white beard and gold circlet → **Hrothgar**
- tall woman in green with auburn braids and a gold cup → **Wealhtheow**
- lean dark man with a pointed black beard in black and silver → **Unferth**
- slight dark-curly-haired boy with a yellow shield → **Wiglaf**

Background Danes and Geats are men of middle age in brown, grey, rust and
undyed wool, with brown, grey or dark-blond hair and full beards; no
clean-shaven giants, no blue cloaks, no waist-length white beards, no black
pointed beards, no women in green with gold cups. Hrothgar's oldest friend
(pp 2, 4, 22, 24) is an unnamed old warrior with a grey braided beard and a
bald head; he must never resemble Hrothgar.

## Collision matrix — pairs requiring adversarial boards

| Pair | Risk | Carried by |
|---|---|---|
| Young Beowulf / old Beowulf | must **match** | flattened mid-bridge nose break, bold white left-brow notch, cleft chin, pale blue eyes, tied-back hair, the ring — continuity board, not separation |
| Hrothgar / old Beowulf | high (readers may conflate the two old kings) | thin vs massive; waist-length flowing beard vs short trimmed; loose hair and circlet vs tied hair and helmet; crimson robe vs mail |
| Young Beowulf / Wiglaf | medium | broad, tawny, clean jaw with cleft vs slight, dark curly, narrow face, freckles |
| Unferth / Wiglaf | low | black pointed beard, sneer vs beardless boy with curls |
| Grendel / Grendel's mother | high | bald, gaunt, grey-green, ember eyes vs weed-haired, heavy, blue-black, long wolf jaw, green eyes |
| Grendel / dragon | low | man-shape vs serpent with wings |
