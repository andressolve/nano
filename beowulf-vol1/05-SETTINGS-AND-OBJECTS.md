# Settings and Objects

*World palette: a painted northern saga — peat-brown, slate, sea-grey and
pine, lit by hearth-gold and torchlight inside and cold moonlight outside;
deep sea-blue reserved for Beowulf. The mere and the dragon's barrow must look
like different worlds. The Chronicle pages are not a location: they are
vellum, ink and wash (see the mandate).*

Each location names one restrained accent that does dramatic work.

---

## Locations

### 1 · THE FEN AND THE MOOR — pp 1, 17, 20
Black pools, reeds, mist lying in strips, a rising moon; on a far hill the
hall's lit doorway. Cold blue-grey everywhere except that distant gold.
**Accent:** the hall's light, far off.

### 2 · HEOROT — pp 2–5, 9–13, 15–22, 24–25, 33–34
The greatest hall in the world: long, high, timber, with a gold-shingled roof
and carved, curling gable-ends like dragon heads. Inside: a long central
hearth, carved pillars, benches along both walls, shields and spears hung
above them, a high seat at the far end, great double doors with iron bands.
Gold and red by firelight; after the raids, cold and grey by day. After p 20,
Grendel's claw hangs high under the gable above the doors. **Accent:** the
hearth-gold.

### 3 · THE SEA AND THE CLIFFS — pp 6, 8–9, 35, 56
The Geats' harbour of turf-roofed houses and beached ships; the grey-green
sea; Denmark's white cliffs; Beowulf's ship — long, clinker-built, a high
curled prow, a square striped sail, oars. **Accent:** the ship's prow.

### 4 · THE MERE AND THE HALL BENEATH — pp 20, 24, 26–32
A black lake in a cleft of grey rock under dripping trees, fog on the water,
a waterfall, water-snakes on the rocks. Below: murky green-black depths, then
an air-filled cave-hall with a strange pale fire, old giant-work arches,
weapons on the walls. **Accent:** the pale fire, and the giant's sword.

### 5 · BEOWULF'S HALL AND THE GEATS' LAND — pp 37, 39–41
Fifty years on: a smaller, sturdier hall on the Geats' coast, then its burned
black timbers, smoke over the villages, a forge in a stone shed. **Accent:**
the forge glow on the iron shield.

### 6 · THE HEADLAND AND THE BARROW — pp 38, 43–55
A high grassy headland over the sea, an ancient grave mound with a stone
doorway and a stream running out of it, steaming; inside, a stone chamber
heaped with gold, cups, armour and a golden banner. **Accent:** the steam of
the stream; inside, the gold banner's glow.
