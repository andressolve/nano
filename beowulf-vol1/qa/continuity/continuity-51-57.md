# Visual-continuity gate — canonical pages 51–57

## Verdict

APPROVED

No page in this run has drifted out of the book.

## Continuity judgment

- Pages 51–56 retain the Northern Saga register: peat-dark blacks, weighted
  metal and stone, visible painted texture, strong simple staging, and
  lighting that follows the hoard glow, exposed northern coast, funeral fire,
  grey daylight, and sunset without breaking the established material world.
- Wiglaf remains unmistakable on pages 51–55: slight build, narrow freckled
  face, dark curls, russet underlayer, and too-large mail. The inherited gold
  neck-ring and boar-crested helmet preserve the approved forms and clearly
  communicate his changed story state without displacing his identity.
- Old Beowulf on pages 52–53 retains the approved massive frame, weathered
  face, white hair, short white beard, mail, faded sea-blue cloak, and gold
  neck-ring. He remains distinct from Wiglaf and reads consistently through
  the dying state.
- The gold-filled stone chamber and luminous banner on page 51 match the
  approved hoard lock. Pages 52–55 preserve the headland's stone doorway,
  grass, rock, cliffs, and sea; page 56 preserves the approved tarred ship and
  striped sail. Changes of viewpoint, weather, and completion of the mound are
  narrative progression rather than setting drift.
- Page 57 makes the intended clean shift to Vellum Chronicle: warm ivory
  vellum, ruled brown margin, iron-gall palette, and a red-and-lapis interlace
  initial with restrained gold. It contains no story character and remains
  visibly part of the same designed volume.
- Serif narration remains coherent across the sequence. The dialogue pages
  use the same dark hand-lettered weight, warm bone balloons, organic balloon
  contours, and clear tails; no lettering treatment or balloon shape creates
  a continuity break.

There are no revision findings.
