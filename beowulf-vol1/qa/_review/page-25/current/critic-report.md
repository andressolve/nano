## Blind read
Beowulf enters the damaged hall in a hopeful mood and greets the seated king, but the king's grief owns the page, especially in the central close-up. The king reveals that his oldest friend was killed, identifies Grendel's mother as the second attacker, and says her tracks lead to a black lake; the turn is Beowulf taking the king's hands and converting grief into a decision to pursue the killer.
