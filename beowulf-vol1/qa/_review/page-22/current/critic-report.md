## Blind read
At a crowded, torchlit feast, an old harpist sings while an old warrior raises his cup, two boys gaze up at the celebrated man in the blue cloak, and rich gifts lie before him. The feast owns the page. The veiled queen then offers him a twisted gold neck-ring and asks him to be kind to her sons; he wears the ring, rests a hand gently on the smaller boy's head, and promises to be their friend. The page changes from public celebration and reward to a quiet personal bond and protective promise, then resolves with the hall's first safe night.
