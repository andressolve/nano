## Mandatory findings

1. The strong radiant opening in the sky creates warm, near-gold directional illumination and glittering water, contrary to the required grey northern morning and the register's flat grey exterior daylight. Rebuild the entire sky, sea reflections, roof highlights, and shore highlights in cold slate-grey, sea-grey, and bone-white under a continuous overcast; remove the radiant sun opening and every gold-lit cloud or water passage so the light is broad, cold, and directionless.
2. The large high-contrast oxide-red-and-cream sail is the plate's dominant accent, while the location's specified primary marker is the ship's prow. Reduce the sail's value and saturation contrast while retaining its oxide-red and undyed-cream stripes, and shift the sailing ship's framing so its forward high curled prow is isolated against pale open sea or sky and becomes the strongest compact silhouette and highest local contrast on the plate.
