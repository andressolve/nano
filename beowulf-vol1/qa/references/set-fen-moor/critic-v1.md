## Mandatory findings

1. The fen does not preserve the required cold palette and sole warm accent. Ochre, rust, and warm peat-brown spread across the foreground grasses, tussocks, banks, reeds, and stumps, so the distant doorway is not the only warm colour. Redesign the plate so every pool, bank, reed, tussock, stump, mist strip, hill, and exterior surface of the hall is cold slate, silver, or blue-grey; remove the ochre/russet warmth from the terrain and retain warm gold in exactly one place: the distant doorway.
2. The hall's identity marker is diluted by warm light extending through several apertures and along the wall rather than being carried by one lit doorway. Redesign the hall as a cold, near-black timber silhouette with every window, seam, and secondary aperture unlit; preserve one clearly readable open doorway as the single compact gold accent on the far hill.
