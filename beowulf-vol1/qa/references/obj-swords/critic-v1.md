## Mandatory findings

1. The shared physical scale is structurally wrong. The three human swords are approximately four-fifths the total length of the whole giant's sword, so the giant weapon reads mainly as broader, not "far longer than any other here"; the detached giant hilt is also roughly one-third larger than the corresponding hilt on the whole sword. Keep the whole giant sword at its present maximum size, uniformly reduce each of the three human swords until each is about 60–65% of the giant sword's total length, and uniformly reduce the detached hilt until its pommel, grip, guard width, and guard thickness exactly match the whole sword's gold hilt at the same scale. Re-space all five studies with open ground and preserve the required left-to-right order.

2. The post-melt state is wrong: a dark, ragged blade remnant projects below the detached golden cross-guard. Remove that remnant completely. The fourth study must end cleanly at the underside of the gold guard, with uninterrupted peat-toned ground below it, so it reads as the golden hilt alone and cannot regenerate a broken or partially surviving blade after page 31.

## Nonblocking observations

- The plate is unframed and contains no lettering, labels, runes, watermark, signature, hands, figures, or scabbards.
- The warm peat ground, iron-grey blades, silver, leather, brass, and gold stay inside the Northern Saga palette; no reserved deep sea-blue appears.
- Hrunting's wavy pattern-welding and silver-wire grip are immediately readable, and the whole giant sword's broad pitted blade remains distinctive in grayscale and at thumbnail size.
- The five studies are in the required narrative order, and no extra recurring object has been introduced.
