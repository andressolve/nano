## Mandatory findings

1. The four complete swords do not share the mandated physical scale or top alignment. The three human swords are roughly four-fifths of the giant sword's total height rather than 60–65%, and the whole giant sword's pommel crown sits substantially above the other three. Keep the giant sword nearly plate-height; align the pommel crowns of Beowulf's sword, Hrunting, the whole giant sword, and Naegling to one exact horizontal coordinate; then uniformly reduce each human sword so its complete pommel-to-tip length is exactly 60–65% of the giant sword's complete pommel-to-tip length. Do not change any human sword's internal proportions to reach that ratio.

2. The blade-less giant-sword state is not a 1:1 structural repeat of the hilt on the whole giant sword: its guard is narrower and differently curved, and the terminal and ornament geometry do not occupy identical positions. Replace the fourth study with an exact same-scale duplication of the third study's hilt: identical pommel height and three-lobe silhouette, identical grip length, diameter, braid pitch, and collars, and identical guard width, thickness, curvature, round terminals, medallions, and ornament placement. Remove only the blade, leaving uninterrupted peat ground directly below the guard with no blade fragment.

## Nonblocking observations

- The plate is clean and unframed, with no figures, hands, scabbards, lettering, labels, inscriptions, runes, signature, or watermark.
- All five required studies are present in the correct left-to-right state order. The melted state correctly has no blade or blade fragment.
- The warm peat ground, iron-grey blades, restrained warm metal, and absence of deep sea-blue fit the Northern Saga palette. The objects retain the approved painterly material register and no consequential object is added.
- Hrunting's flowing blade pattern remains readable in grayscale; Naegling's down-curved guard remains readable at thumbnail size; the giant sword remains unmistakable by breadth, pitting, and mass. Once the mandated scale and duplicated-hilt geometry are repaired, the plate's object identities should survive re-entry after a long absence.
