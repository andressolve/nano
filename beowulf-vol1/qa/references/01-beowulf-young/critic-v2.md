## Mandatory findings

1. **The required unarmed night-state view is wrong.** View (d) contains a long dark weapon or scabbard descending from the figure's right hand, and that hand reads closed around it rather than empty and ready. Redesign this state marker by removing every weapon, scabbard, helmet, mail element, and cloak from view (d), and show both enormous empty hands visibly open at his sides with the feet planted and the body completely still.

2. **The body reads as a mature power-built fighter rather than a rangy nineteen-year-old who has not yet thickened.** The heavy neck and trapezius, thick upper arms and forearms, and compact trunk survive the thumbnail silhouette and violate both the structural lock and the Never list. Preserve the wide shoulder span and oversized hands, but reduce the neck/trapezius and upper-arm/forearm circumference, narrow the ribcage and waist below the shoulders, and lengthen the visible upper-arm, forearm, thigh, and shin proportions in all full-length views.

3. **The broken nose is not a stable, unmistakable continuity marker.** It reads nearly straight in the large portrait and does not carry a clear healed angular step into the strict profile, so it is unlikely to survive grayscale, reduced scale, or Beowulf's old-state redesign. Give the same broad nose one conspicuous lateral dogleg at mid-bridge in views (a), (b), and (d), all bending to the same anatomical side, and preserve that break as a small angular step in the exposed bridge of view (c); do not lengthen it or turn it into a hook.

4. **The left-brow notch scar is too faint and inconsistent to function as the second permanent age-state marker.** A pale mark can be found in the close portrait, but the break in the brow line is not clearly recoverable in the frontal figures or strict profile at reference-sheet scale. Cut one short white notch fully through the hair of the left brow at one fixed angle and position, and reproduce that same visible interruption in all four views, including the exposed brow in the strict profile.
