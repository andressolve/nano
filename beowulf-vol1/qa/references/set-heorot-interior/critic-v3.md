## Mandatory findings

1. The cold-grey view places breaking surf, a sea inlet, and steep coastal cliffs immediately beyond Heorot's open doors. That changes the locked location from a hall on a rise above the moor into a shoreline hall and will propagate a false geographic marker into every daylight interior. Replace the entire exterior vista beyond the doors with the primary location marker: low, treeless moor in cold flat grey daylight, with no visible surf, beach, sea inlet, or coastal cliffs. Keep the iron-banded double doors and the hall geometry unchanged.

2. The plate's timber, stone floor, shields, and daylight read as high-detail photographic/3D environment rendering, unlike the approved Northern Saga sheets and the required opaque-oil register. Rebuild the surface treatment around a visible warm peat-brown underpainting, broad confident brush shapes in the beams and paving, and restrained impasto confined to hearth, torch, and reflected-gold highlights; remove lens-like microtexture and photographic edge/detail treatment while retaining the current long-hearth silhouette and warm-fire/cold-day value opposition.

## Nonblocking observations

- The plate is correctly figure-free and contains no lettering, labels, runes, signature, watermark, or enclosing border.

- Nothing hangs beneath the gable above the doors, so the plate remains usable before and after the separate claw state is introduced.
- No deep sea-blue intrudes on Beowulf's reserved colour space.
- The loose straw and rough bench dressing can read as raid aftermath and do not establish a consequential recurring object, provided the foreground wooden forms remain benches rather than animal-feed troughs in the revision.
