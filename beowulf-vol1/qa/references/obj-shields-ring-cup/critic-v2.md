## Mandatory findings

1. Remove the black wolf device from Wiglaf's shield completely. Rebuild it to match the approved 08 sheet as a plain yellow-ochre, visibly vertical-planked round linden shield with one central dark iron boss and a simple dark rim; retain only wear, plank grain, and small construction fasteners, with no heraldic animal or other face-wide motif.

2. Replace Wealhtheow's cup's two tall animal-neck handles with two small plain loop handles attached close to the upper sides of the bowl. Remove both animal heads and their projecting necks while retaining the wide deep footed bowl and the single knotwork band. This must match the simple handled cup already carried on the approved 04 sheet.

3. Put all six studies at one shared physical scale. Keep each shield as the dominant form; reduce the torc to a neck-sized outside diameter no wider than the helmet, reduce the great cup to roughly half a shield diameter including its small handles, and keep the hoard goblet smaller again at roughly one quarter of a shield diameter. Do not independently enlarge the torc or cups to fill their cells.

## Nonblocking observations

- All six required objects are present, with no figure, hand, lettering, rune, signature, watermark, frame, or border.
- The revised iron shield succeeds: it is one plain continuous dark forged-iron disc with a simple rim and central boss, without radial seams, wedge divisions, plank-like cracks, ornamental face rivets, or concentric decoration.
- The two cups remain separable at thumbnail and in grayscale by broad footed-bowl versus tall narrow goblet silhouette; the hoard cup's red garnets are a supporting rather than sole cue.
- The intact shield, ring, cup, goblet, and helmet states are appropriate to a permanent multi-state object plate; no burned shield or broken object is mistakenly locked as the default.
- Warm peat ground, iron grey, worn yellow ochre, hearth-gold, garnet red, even reference light, and weighted painted-metal texture fit the Northern Saga register. No reserved deep sea-blue appears. Because this is a cross-location object plate rather than a setting plate, no single location accent is required.
