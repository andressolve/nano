Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes or apostrophes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of any repeated letter; the words and their order are what must match.

### C2 — The quiet does not land
The page must read as a calm illustrated-prose page: an old king alone on the steps of a whole, peaceful hall above the harbour, watching the sea. If the eye finds action, alarm, or a second event instead of the old man and his quiet hall, the turn fails.

### C3 — Not the same man grown old
Beowulf must read as the young hero fifty years on: huge and heavy, white hair tied back, a short white beard, the crooked nose and notched left brow, a faded blue cloak. A long flowing beard, a thin frail body, a crown, or a face with none of his marks is blocking.

### C4 — The ring missing
Wealhtheow's twisted gold ring must be visible at his neck; the prose names it. A missing ring is blocking; its exact twist or scale is nonblocking.

### C5 — The hall already burned
The hall must be whole and peaceful. Fire, ruin, or a dragon on this page is blocking.
