### C1 — Transcription fails
Both captions must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once each, in order, tail-free, with no other text; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue; the words and their order must match.

### C2 — The swing does not own the page
The page must read as spectacle, and the eye must find last and largest Beowulf swinging the enormous sword at her as she rears. If the blow is shown landing, or the knife panel dominates, the turn fails.

### C3 — The sword is not discovered
The reader must see the huge sword on the wall before it is in his hands. If it is never seen on the wall, or appears in his hands before it is found, it is blocking.

### C4 — Death or wound drawn
No blood, cut, or body. Her knife skids on mail and does not wound him. Any wound is blocking.

### C5 — Identity
