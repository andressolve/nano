### C1 — Transcription fails
Both paragraphs of the prose field must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order, with no other text; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes or apostrophes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of any repeated letter; the words and their order are what must match.

### C2 — The burial does not land
The image must own the page with a tall new grassy mound on the highest point of the headland over the sea, riders circling it with cloaks streaming. If the mound reads as the old dragon's barrow with a stone door and steaming stream, or the riders are not circling it, the turn fails. The exact number of riders is nonblocking.

### C3 — Not illustrated prose
The prose field sits over plain sky, clear of faces and riders; prose broken into balloons, or lettering over the figures, is blocking.

### C4 — Identity and state
Wiglaf leads the riders, slight and beardless, wearing the boar-crested helmet and the gold ring. A Wiglaf without the helmet or ring, a second boar helmet, a blue-cloaked rider, or old Beowulf alive on the page is blocking.
