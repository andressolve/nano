## Page 9 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
Both prose paragraphs must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order, with no other text anywhere; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue; the words and their order are what must match.

### C2 — The challenge and the way do not land
The beach confrontation must own the page: a mounted Danish guard with his spear levelled at the strangers, and Beowulf facing him with his helmet under one arm and his other hand open and empty. Then the guard's spear must point up a stone road to a gold-roofed hall on a hill. If Beowulf reads as threatening the guard, or the hall is missing, the turn fails.

### C3 — Identity
Beowulf: huge, young, clean-shaven, tawny-gold hair tied back, crooked nose, white notch through the left eyebrow, cleft chin, deep sea-blue cloak. The guard: a bearded middle-aged Dane, not in blue. A bearded or dark-haired Beowulf, or a guard who resembles him, is blocking.

### C4 — Extra presence and text
No balloons; no king, queen or Unferth; no words on the hall. The exact number of Geats behind Beowulf is nonblocking; minor horse anatomy is nonblocking, Beowulf's hands are not.
