## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 31 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
Both captions must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once each, in order, tail-free, with no other text; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue; the words and their order must match.

### C2 — The melting does not own the page
The page must read as spectacle, and the eye must find Beowulf alone in sudden light holding up a blade that is melting away. If the blade reads as whole, or the light does not change the cave, the turn fails.

### C3 — The hilt and the sword count
In the close image there must be exactly one golden hilt with no blade, and Hrunting in his other hand; in the large image one melting giant's blade and Hrunting on the floor. A whole giant's sword anywhere, a second giant's sword, or a third sword is blocking.

### C4 — Body drawn
Of her only a dark still mass of weed-hair. A body, face, wound, or blood is blocking.

### C5 — Identity
Beowulf recognisable: clean-shaven, cleft chin, broken nose, sea-blue cloak, gold neck-ring. Unrecognisable is blocking.
