### C1 — Transcription fails
Both prose paragraphs must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order, ending on "Denmark.", with no other text anywhere; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue; the words and their order are what must match.

### C2 — The arrival does not land
One image must own the page: a long ship with a striped sail running hard over the sea, Beowulf at the prow grinning into the spray, and a line of white cliffs ahead. If the cliffs are missing, or the ship reads as becalmed or at harbour, the turn fails. The prose field must sit over plain sky, not over Beowulf's face.

### C3 — Beowulf is not Beowulf
Beowulf: a huge, young, clean-shaven man with tawny-gold hair tied back, a crooked nose, a white notch through the left eyebrow, a cleft chin, and the only deep sea-blue cloak on the page; joyful, not solemn. A beard, dark hair, a second figure in blue, or a gold neck-ring is blocking.

### C4 — Integrity
No monster, no hall, no balloon. The number of crew aboard is nonblocking; repeated crew faces and rigging detail are nonblocking.
