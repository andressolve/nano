## Page intent

## PAGE 48

Dramatic. A blast of dragon fire sets Wiglaf's yellow wooden shield alight and he flings it away. Old Beowulf hauls him in behind the iron shield, furious and astonished, shouting that he told him to stay on the hill. Wiglaf, shaking, answers with the old king's own confession: he was afraid every time, and so is Wiglaf, and he came anyway. Then Beowulf swings Naegling with all his strength into the dragon's head, and the old blade breaks in pieces; the caption says his arm was too strong for any sword. The dominant turn: the old king has no sword left. The shattering sword owns the page; the shared shelter behind the iron shield is the human heart of it.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 48 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
All three strings must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in causal order, each owned by the right mouth by tail and placement, with no other text; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes or apostrophes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of any repeated letter; the words and their order are what must match.

### C2 — The broken sword does not land
The reader must see Naegling break in pieces against the dragon's head and know the old king has no sword left. If the blade reads as whole or the strike is unfindable, the turn fails.

### C3 — Attribution
"I told you to stay on the hill!" is Beowulf's; "You told me you were afraid every time…" is Wiglaf's, read after it. Each must hang from its own speaker's mouth by tail; a swap is blocking.

### C4 — Sword count
Exactly one sword in the dominant panel, Naegling, breaking; a second whole sword there, or Naegling also whole elsewhere after it breaks, is blocking.

### C5 — Identity and object state
Old Beowulf huge, white-bearded, boar helmet, iron shield, gold ring; Wiglaf slight, beardless, dark curls, no ring. The yellow shield burns and is flung away. Any of these wrong is blocking.
