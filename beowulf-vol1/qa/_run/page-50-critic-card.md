## Page 50 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
All four spoken lines must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in causal order, each owned by the right mouth by tail and placement, with no other text; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes or apostrophes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of any repeated letter; the words and their order are what must match. A line placed directly under its speaker's previous balloon, or naming its addressee, is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — The bequest does not land
The dying king and the kneeling boy must own the page, and the reader must finish knowing Wiglaf is sent into the barrow. If the dragon or the scenery outweighs the two of them, the turn fails.

### C3 — Attribution
"Its teeth were poisoned…" is Beowulf's; "Don't talk like that, my lord. Please." is Wiglaf's, read after it. "Fifty years I never needed anyone…" and "Now go into the barrow…" are Beowulf's. Each must hang from its own speaker's mouth by tail; a swap is blocking.

### C4 — Identity and state
Old Beowulf: helmet off, white hair loose, white beard, gold ring still at his neck, eyes open. Wiglaf: slight, beardless, dark curls, no ring, no boar helmet. A wound, blood, closed eyes, or the ring on Wiglaf is blocking.
