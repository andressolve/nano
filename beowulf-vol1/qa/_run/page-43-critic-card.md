### C2 — The old man and the sea do not own the page
The page must read as illustrated prose: the old king sitting alone on a rock looking out to sea, the steaming barrow below, the waiting men small behind him. If the men, the runaway, or the barrow own the eye instead of the seated old man, the turn fails.

### C3 — Memories drawn
Hrothgar, Grendel, the claw, the lake, or the young Beowulf drawn as an inset, ghost, or vision is blocking; the memories live in the prose.

### C4 — Not old Beowulf
Huge and heavy, white hair tied back, short white beard, crooked nose, notched left brow, faded blue cloak, the gold ring at his neck. A long flowing beard, a thin body, or a missing ring is blocking.

### C5 — Prose over busy art
A field over the old king's face or hands is blocking. The number of waiting men is nonblocking.
