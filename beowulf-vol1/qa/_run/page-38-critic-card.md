## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 38 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
Both captions must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order, with no other text; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes or apostrophes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of any repeated letter; the words and their order are what must match. Captions carry no tail and belong to no mouth.

### C2 — The sleeping dragon does not own the page
The coiled dragon asleep on its mountain of gold must be what the eye finds; the runaway entering the mound reads first and smaller. If the boy, the headland, or the gold alone owns the page, or the dragon reads as awake, the turn fails.

### C3 — The theft is not legible
The runaway must visibly lift exactly one golden cup at the edge of the hoard. No cup, several cups, or a cup the reader cannot find is blocking; the cup's shape is nonblocking.

### C4 — The dragon reads wrong
A long serpent with folded ragged wings, a horned crocodile-jawed head, bronze-and-black scales with ember seams, eyes shut. Open eyes, fire, a balloon, or a lizard-horse body is blocking.

### C5 — The runaway reads as Wiglaf
The runaway is ragged, unarmed and shieldless. A slight boy with dark curls and a yellow shield is blocking.
