## Materiality threshold

The critic is a release gate, not a defect collector. A visible issue is
blocking only when it materially harms the reader event, exact text or
attribution, named or focal identity, consequential continuity or object state,
focal generation integrity, or the dominant dramatic relationship. Every
correction replaces the complete page. Minor background, cosmetic, tiny-prop,
geometry, colour, texture, and prompt-fidelity observations are nonblocking.
Omit them. Numeric panel shares and lettering sizes are builder construction
guidance, never critic measurements.

Each numbered criterion is also its stable retry signature. Cite it only when
the reader harm and redraw justification are concrete.

A lettered speaker name, label, or tag anywhere on the page is extra text and
blocks under the transcription criterion, whatever else reads correctly:
finished pages carry dialogue, captions, prose, and sound cues only.
A sound cue whose letters and order match the script reads correctly; the run
length of a repeated letter in it is nonblocking and never counted.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 16 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
The three prose paragraphs must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in causal order, each owned by the right mouth or field by tail and placement, with no other text; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue; the words and their order are what must match. A line owned by placement (directly under its speaker's previous balloon, or naming its addressee) is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — The waiting does not land
Illustrated-prose mode: one prose field over the dark rafters and one large image of Beowulf lying awake with empty hands, the shut and barred doors in view. If his eyes read closed, or the doors read open, the turn fails. The prose field must not cover his face or hands.

### C3 — Beowulf armed or in mail
Beowulf must be in his tunic only, with no mail, sword, or helmet on or beside him, his empty hands on his chest. Mail on his body or a sword in reach is blocking; a cloak used as a blanket by a sleeping Geat is nonblocking.

### C4 — Identity
Beowulf: broad clean-shaven face, cleft chin, broken nose, notched left brow, tawny hair tied back. A beard, dark hair, or a face that reads as someone else is blocking; the sleeping Geats' faces are nonblocking.

### C5 — Premature monster
No Grendel, no shadow at the door, nothing hung over the doors. Any of these is blocking.
