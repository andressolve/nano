Spectacle. Years later, a longship under a striped sail rides far out on a gold evening sea. ### C2 — The memory does not land
The page must read as one spectacle: a longship under a striped sail far out on a gold evening sea, a sailor at the prow pointing up at a green mound on a high cliff, the others turning to look. If the mound cannot be found, or the pointing does not lead the eye to it, the turn fails.
