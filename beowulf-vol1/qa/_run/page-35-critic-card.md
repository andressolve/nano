## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 35 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
Both prose paragraphs must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order, on plain ground, no other text; a paragraph that does not read is blocking, text that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue; the words and their order must match. No one speaks; any balloon is extra text.

### C2 — The parting does not own the page
The old king holding Beowulf like a son, weeping, must be what the eye finds first; the harbour is the payoff below. If the embrace is lost among the loading Geats, or reads as a greeting, the turn fails.

### C3 — Two places, one journey
Above, the white-cliffed beach with the ship; below, the Geats' turf-roofed harbour with Beowulf handing gold to a crowd. If the places merge, or the giving-away does not read, the page fails.

### C4 — Identity
Hrothgar: very thin, waist-length white beard, circlet, crimson. Beowulf: young, huge, clean-shaven, tawny hair tied back, sea-blue cloak, gold neck-ring, in both panels. A bearded or ringless Beowulf, or blue on anyone else, is blocking; repeated crowd faces are nonblocking.

### C5 — Focal integrity
The embrace is two people with two faces that read; extra or tangled arms in it are blocking. Crowd hands are nonblocking.
