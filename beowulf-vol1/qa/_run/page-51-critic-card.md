## Page intent

## PAGE 51

Spectacle. Wiglaf goes alone into the dragon's grave. Inside the barrow's stone chamber he stands small, knee-deep in a sea of gold — cups, dishes, rings, old helmets eaten by rust — gathering an armful, and high above the hoard hangs a banner woven all of gold that glows in the dark by its own light, lighting his astonished face. One caption tells it. The dominant turn: he has to carry it out to a dying man. The glow and the boy's face own the page; the gold is a field, not an inventory. No dragon here, nobody else, no voice: the silence is the point. The reader turns because the treasure is only worth something if the old king lives to see it.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 51 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
The one caption must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, with no other text anywhere; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. The banner carries no words. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes or apostrophes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of any repeated letter; the words and their order are what must match.

### C2 — The hoard does not land
The page must read as one spectacle image: a small Wiglaf alone inside a dark stone chamber, knee-deep in gold, gathering an armful, under a golden banner that glows by its own light onto his astonished face. If the banner's glow cannot be found, or the gold reads as a few scattered coins rather than a hoard, the turn fails.

### C3 — Identity
Wiglaf: slight, beardless, short dark curls, freckled narrow face, too-big mail over russet. A fair-haired or bearded figure, or one with Beowulf's breadth, is blocking. A gold ring at his neck, a boar helmet, or a yellow shield is blocking: none is his yet or still.

### C4 — Extra presence
No dragon, no old king, no other person, no balloon. A dragon in the chamber or a body among the gold is blocking; individual cup and helmet shapes in the heap are nonblocking.
