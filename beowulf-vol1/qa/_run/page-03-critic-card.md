## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 3 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
Both prose paragraphs must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order, with no other text; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue; the words and their order must match. The prose field must not sit over the doorway or the shadow.

### C2 — The break-in does not land
One image owns the page: the barred doors bursting inward, the fire bent flat, a huge wrong shadow with ember eyes in the doorway, and the warriors still asleep. If the doors read as closed or merely open, or the sleepers are already up fighting, the turn fails.

### C3 — Harm or the monster drawn
No one is harmed, seized, or bleeding, and no body appears. Grendel is a shadow and two eyes, not a fully lit figure. Any blood, wound, or drawn victim is blocking; a partly visible arm in deep shadow is nonblocking.

### C4 — Not illustrated prose
The page must read as illustrated prose: one parchment prose field and one dominant illustration, with no balloons or captions. A balloon or sound cue on the page is blocking.
