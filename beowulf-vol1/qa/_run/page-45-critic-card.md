The one spoken line must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, owned by Beowulf's mouth by tail and placement, with no other text; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. ### C2 — The fire does not own the page
The dragon bursting from the barrow in a river of fire, with the old king crouched behind his iron shield as the flame splits around it, must be what the eye finds. If the challenge panel outweighs it, or the man cannot be found in the fire, the turn fails.

### C3 — The dragon speaks or reads wrong
Winged serpent, horned crocodile jaw, molten-gold eyes, bronze-and-black ember-seamed scales. Any balloon, tail, or sound word from the dragon, or a lizard-horse body, is blocking.

### C4 — The shield state
The shield he crouches behind is plain dark iron. A wooden, painted, or yellow shield, or a burned or wounded Beowulf, is blocking.
