## Page 13 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
All three of Beowulf's lines must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in causal order, each owned by Beowulf's mouth by tail and placement, with no other text; no shout, laugh or sound word anywhere. A string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue; the words and their order are what must match. A line owned by placement (directly under its speaker's previous balloon, or naming its addressee) is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — The win does not land
Beowulf, grinning and mock-apologetic before the hall while Unferth stops smiling, must own the page; then Beowulf goes still and plain; last the whole hall roars, Hrothgar laughs, and Unferth sits down dark-faced. If the hall's roar is missing, or Unferth reads as still smiling at the end, the turn fails.

### C3 — Attribution
"So that's how I lost the race…", "Here's the truth, Unferth…" and "Tonight the Danes are going to see what a Geat can do." are all Beowulf's. A balloon on Unferth, Hrothgar or any Dane is blocking.

### C4 — Identity and separation

Hrothgar, small: very thin, waist-length white beard, crimson. Beowulf and Unferth reading alike is blocking; small background faces are nonblocking.

### C5 — Continuity
