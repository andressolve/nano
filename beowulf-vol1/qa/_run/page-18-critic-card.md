## Page intent

## PAGE 18

Spectacle. The claw closes on the sleeper, and the sleeper's hand closes on the claw. Close and huge, an enormous human hand clamps Grendel's scaled wrist; Beowulf, rising off the bench in his tunic, grins and asks if the monster was looking for him, and Grendel's coal eyes go wide. ## Materiality threshold

The critic is a release gate, not a defect collector. A visible issue is
blocking only when it materially harms the reader event, exact text or
attribution, named or focal identity, consequential continuity or object state,
focal generation integrity, or the dominant dramatic relationship. Every

Each numbered criterion is also its stable retry signature. Cite it only when

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 18 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
The one spoken line and two captions must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in causal order, each owned by the right mouth or field by tail and placement, with no other text; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue; the words and their order are what must match. A line owned by placement (directly under its speaker's previous balloon, or naming its addressee) is owned even if its tail wanders. "Looking for me?" belongs to Beowulf, never to Grendel.

### C2 — The grip does not land
Spectacle mode: the dominant image is a huge human hand clamped on Grendel's scaled wrist with Beowulf grinning below and Grendel's eyes going wide. If the grip is not the first thing the eye finds, the turn fails.

### C3 — Attribution
"Looking for me?" hangs from Beowulf's grinning mouth. A tail toward Grendel, or any balloon for a monster, is blocking.

### C4 — Swords that cut
The Geats' swords bounce off in sparks. Blood, a wound, or a blade biting into Grendel is blocking.

### C5 — Identity and state
Beowulf: tunic only, no mail, no sword, no neck-ring; cleft chin, broken nose, notched left brow, tawny hair. Grendel: hairless, grey-green, ember eyes, partly in shadow. A weapon in Beowulf's hand or a lit-whole Grendel is blocking. Exactly two hands in the focal grip; extra fingers on a background Geat are nonblocking.
