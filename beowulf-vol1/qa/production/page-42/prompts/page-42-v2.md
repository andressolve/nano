Why does a dragon sleep on gold? In the old stories of the North, dragons guarded treasure hidden in the graves of people who died long ago, and only a hero could win it. The poet at Heorot sang of one: Sigemund. In later stories it was his son, Sigurd, who killed the dragon Fafnir.

And people really did bury treasure with their kings. In 1939, under a mound at Sutton Hoo in England, diggers found the shape of a whole ship packed with a king's gold. In 2009, a man with a metal detector found thousands of pieces of gold war-gear in a field in Staffordshire.
