In the morning, thirty men were gone from Heorot. None of them was ever seen again.
Beside the king stood his oldest friend, who had fought next to him in every battle of his life.
Thirty of my men. Thirty! And I slept through it, in my own house.
Then tonight nobody sleeps, my lord. I'll fill this hall with every sword in Denmark. Let it come back and meet them.
And if swords can't stop it?
