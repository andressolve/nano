## Blind read
An elderly crowned lord and a young warrior sit across a fire while the elder studies an ornate hilt. The elder owns the page visually and verbally: he explains that the hilt predates kings, recalls the giants' destruction in a flood, and turns the history into a warning against pride and a reminder that sickness, violence, fire, or age will eventually claim the young man. The young warrior answers obediently, but the closing prose changes the meaning of that answer by revealing that nineteen-year-old Beowulf is not listening.
