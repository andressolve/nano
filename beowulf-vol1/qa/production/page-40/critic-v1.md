## Findings

### Finding C3
- Observation: Wiglaf's first line, `My lord! The dragon has burned half the coast.`, is in an upper balloon with no tail to his mouth. It is bridged to the lower balloon, and only that lower balloon has a tail pointing to Wiglaf.
- Material reader harm: The first required report does not hang from Wiglaf's own mouth; it relies on a shared tail through the following balloon, which fails the card's explicit attribution requirement.
- Redraw justification: Correct speaker ownership for every spoken line is a release-gate requirement, and C3 expressly makes a shared tail blocking, so preserving the otherwise successful page would leave a required line without its own mouth attribution.
