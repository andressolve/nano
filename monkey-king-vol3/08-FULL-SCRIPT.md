# MOVEMENT I — THE RIDGE · pp 1–5

---

## PAGE 1 · *illustrated prose*

**Location:** the road west over White Tiger Ridge.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 70%.** A narrow pale road along the spine of a high
ridge of white rock, hazy blue valleys falling away on both sides. The monk
Sanzang on the white horse; behind him Wujing and Bajie carrying the luggage
pole between them, Bajie's mouth open, complaining. Far ahead, up on a white
boulder, Wukong in his hemp robe with the thin gold band on his brow, one
hand shading his eyes, looking west.

> **PROSE FIELD 1** *(in the open sky above the ridge)*
> "Months after the river of sand, they were still walking west. The monk Sanzang rode a white horse that had once been a dragon. Bajie the pig and Wujing the river ogre carried the luggage, and Bajie complained about it.
>
> Wukong ran ahead. He could cross the world in one somersault, but he was never allowed to fly the monk: the road was the monk's to walk. On Wukong's head was a gold band that squeezed like a fist whenever the monk said three words: Gold, hold fast. The monk had promised to say them only if he must.
>
> Long ago, in a furnace in Heaven, smoke had got into Wukong's eyes and stayed there. No disguise could fool them now. He saw the real face under any face. His iron staff he kept as a needle behind his ear."

**Why you turn the page:** the ridge is empty, and something on it is watching them.

---

## PAGE 2 · *spectacle*

**Location:** White Tiger Ridge, the demon's crag.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 100%.** From the black mouth of a cave at the top of a
tooth of white rock, looking down: the White Bone Lady in her true shape — a
tall lady of bone, a white skull for a face with two green pinpoints of light
in its eye-hollows, long black hair, ash-gray and violet burial silks
trailing like smoke — drawing one long bone finger slowly across her teeth.
Far below, tiny on the pale road, the monk on the white horse, the pig, the
ogre, and the monkey running ahead.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left)*
> "On the ridge, something had been waiting for the monk."
>
> **WHITE BONE LADY:** "They say one bite of that monk and you live forever."

**Why you turn the page:** she wants to eat the monk, and they are walking straight toward her.

---

## PAGE 3 · *dramatic*

**Location:** White Tiger Ridge.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** The travellers stopped on the bare road. Sanzang on the
left, sliding down off the white horse, one hand on his stomach, looking
round at nothing but rock. Wukong on the right, up on a dead pine stump, hand
shading his eyes.

> **SANZANG:** "Wukong, I'm so hungry I can't see straight. Is there a village anywhere on this ridge?"
>
> **WUKONG:** "Not a village, not a farm, not a goat. But there are peaches on a mountain to the south."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 45%.** Wukong on the right, a puff of cloud already
under his feet, pointing a finger at the monk like a teacher. Sanzang on the
left, sitting down on a flat rock. Wujing behind the monk, standing.

> **WUKONG:** "Sit on that rock, Master, and don't move until I'm back. I can't fly you anywhere, but I can fly peaches."
>
> **WUJING:** "Go. I'll stand at his back."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Wukong gone, a streak of cloud vanishing south. Bajie on
the left, dropping his end of the luggage pole, scowling up at the sky.
Sanzang on the right on his rock.

> **BAJIE:** "'Don't move.' 'Hurry up.' 'Hands off the horse.' Who made the monkey the boss?"
>
> **SANZANG:** "Nobody, Bajie. He's just the fastest."

**Why you turn the page:** the monkey has gone, and the monk is alone on the ridge with two guards and an empty stomach.

---

## PAGE 4 · *dramatic*

**Location:** White Tiger Ridge.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Up on the crag: the White Bone Lady drawing a pretty
girl's face down over her skull like a mask, a peach-pink jacket and a black
plait growing round her bones, a lidded bamboo basket appearing on her arm.

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** On the road, the girl walking up, smiling,
holding out the basket. Sanzang on the left on his rock, rising, palms
together, polite. Bajie behind him, nose twitching at the basket.

> **GIRL:** "Holy master, you look so hungry! I'm taking rice and fried noodles to my father in the fields. Please, eat first."
>
> **SANZANG:** "Rice and noodles, and no meat? Then thank you. I've never let anything die for my supper."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** The girl on the left lifting the basket's lid, smiling;
in her eyes, two tiny green pinpoints. Bajie on the right, leaning in over the
monk's shoulder, drooling. Behind them, high in the sky, a small streak of
cloud coming fast from the south.

> **BAJIE:** "Master, it's rude to say no to a lady. I'll say yes for you."

**Why you turn the page:** the monk is about to eat from the demon's basket, and the sky behind her has a cloud in it.

---

## PAGE 5 · *spectacle*

**Location:** White Tiger Ridge.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 100%.** Wukong dropping out of the sky onto the road,
four red-gold peaches tumbling out of his arms, the red staff already
swinging down from over his head, the red rings of his gold eyes flaring.
Through the smiling girl's face, the reader sees what he sees: the pale ghost
of a skull, and bone fingers under her hands. Sanzang and Bajie on the left,
mouths open, too late. Wujing behind them, reaching.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left, in the sky)*
> "Wukong saw the skull under the smile, and he did not stop to explain."

**Why you turn the page:** the staff is falling.

# MOVEMENT II — THREE FACES · pp 6–19

---

## PAGE 6 · *dramatic*

**Location:** White Tiger Ridge.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** Seen as Wukong sees it, inside the flare of his
red-ringed eyes: the girl lying face-down on the road, flat and hollow like a
skin a cicada leaves behind, and out of it a thin white mist with a skull
face in it, streaming away up toward the crag. Wukong on the right, staff up,
glaring after it.

> **WUKONG:** "She's slipping out! Come back here, you bag of bones!"

**Panel 2 — dominant, 45%.** As the monk sees it: the girl lying still,
face-down, a body, nothing more. Sanzang on the left, on his knees near her,
hands shaking, not daring to touch her. Wukong on the right, pointing the
staff at the basket in the dust.

> **SANZANG:** "You killed her! She brought us food, and you killed her without a word!"
>
> **WUKONG:** "That's not a girl, Master, that's an empty skin. She was a demon. Look in her basket!"

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Close: the basket on its side, the lid off: white maggots
wriggling where the rice was, small green frogs hopping out of the noodles.
Bajie on the right, leaping back.

> **BAJIE:** "Frogs! The noodles are frogs!"

**Why you turn the page:** the basket is full of maggots, and the monk is still looking at the girl.

---

## PAGE 7 · *dramatic*

**Location:** White Tiger Ridge.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** Bajie on the left, at the monk's ear, pointing at
Wukong. Sanzang on the right, still on his knees, staring at the frogs.

> **BAJIE:** "Master, don't fall for it. He changes shapes. Once he turned himself into my bride and fooled me for a whole night!"

**Panel 2 — dominant, 45%.** Bajie on the left, hands spread wide, in full
cry. Sanzang on the right, turning to look at Wukong. Wujing behind them,
silent, watching Wukong.

> **BAJIE:** "If he can turn himself into a bride, he can make rice look like maggots. He's tricking you so you won't say the words!"

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Sanzang on the left, palms pressed hard together, eyes
shut, lips moving. Wukong on the right, mid-step toward him, freezing, both
hands flying up to the gold band.

> **SANZANG:** "Gold, hold fast."
>
> **WUKONG:** "Master, no!"

**Why you turn the page:** the monk has said the words.

## PAGE 8 · *dramatic*

**Location:** White Tiger Ridge.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 50%.** Wukong on the right, down in the white dust,
curled up, both hands clamped on the gold band, teeth clenched, tail lashing.
Sanzang on the left standing over him, palms together, eyes shut.

> **WUKONG:** "Stop! Master, stop! It's squeezing my head like a walnut!"

**Panel 2 — 25%.** Sanzang on the left, opening his eyes, his hands coming
apart, shaking. Wukong on the right, up on his knees, panting.

> **SANZANG:** "I promised I'd only say the words if I must. Today I had to. Don't you ever do that again."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Wukong on the left, getting up, furious and hurt.
Sanzang on the right, turning away to the horse. Behind them Bajie picking
up the luggage pole, looking pleased with himself.

> **WUKONG:** "She was a demon, Master! I saw the face under her face!"
>
> **SANZANG:** "I saw a girl with a basket."

**Why you turn the page:** the monk believes what he saw, and the demon has more faces.

## PAGE 9 · *dramatic*

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** On the road, the old woman hobbling toward them

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Close on Wukong on the right, the red rings of his eyes

the same skull. Sanzang on the left at the edge of the panel, on the horse.

> **WUKONG:** "It's her again, Master. Same skull, under a different face."
>
> **SANZANG:** "Wukong, don't you dare."

**Why you turn the page:** Wukong can see the skull, and the monk has told him not to dare.

# MOVEMENT III — HOME · pp 20–22

---

## PAGE 20 · *illustrated prose*

**Location:** Flower-Fruit Mountain.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 70%.** A green peak on the eastern sea, peach trees,
a waterfall roaring white down its face. Hundreds of young brown monkeys
pouring out from behind the waterfall and up the rocks toward a small golden
figure landing on the peak, beside a split stone and an old flagpole with a
rag of faded pink banner on it.

> **PROSE FIELD 1** *(in the open sky above the peak)*
> "Flower-Fruit Mountain stood on the eastern sea where it always had, green and loud, with the waterfall roaring down in front of the cave where he had once been king.
>
> The monkeys saw him from the trees. They screamed. They came pouring out from behind the waterfall, hundreds of them, the children and grandchildren of the monkeys he had left, who had heard stories about him every night of their lives.
>
> The Monkey King had come home."

**Why you turn the page:** the king is home, and his monkeys want to crown him.

> **WUKONG:** "That's right, your king is home! No monk, no magic words, and no walking. Not ever again!"

> **WUKONG:** "And this thing comes off too. Pull! Pull harder! It won't come off."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** A small monkey on the left tugging at the sleeve of

**Why you turn the page:** he is home and free, and he will not take off the robe.
