## Mandatory findings

1. The nose does not hold the structural lock. In the three-quarter head and especially the strict profile it reads as a long, projecting aquiline nose with a descending tip, violating the required short, broad, flat nose with a wide bridge and level blunt end and the explicit prohibition on a long or hooked nose. Redesign the nose in all four views by shortening its projection, broadening the bridge and base, flattening the ridge, and terminating the profile in a level blunt plane with no downward hook; keep the long face and heavy brow unchanged.

2. The sheet replaces the required plain warm-paper ground with a repeated blue mountain, river, tree, and rock landscape behind and around every view. That scenery becomes an unlisted primary setting marker attached to Wujing and prevents neutral system comparison. Remove the entire landscape field and all scenic rocks, water, mountains, and vegetation; place the same four isolated views on one uninterrupted plain warm-paper ground, with at most neutral contact shadows beneath the full figures.
