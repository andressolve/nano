## Mandatory findings

1. **The living face has the wrong structure.** It is long and gaunt, with hollow cheeks, a strongly projected nose, a long nose-to-chin interval and a narrow pointed lower jaw, especially in strict profile. The lock requires a deeply wrinkled but **round** kind face. Shorten the midface and lower face, reduce the nose projection, widen the cheeks and mandibular contour, and replace the pointed chin with a short blunt rounded chin in all three living views. Preserve the narrow dark eyes, small toothless mouth, tears and deep wrinkles.

2. **View (d) depicts an opaque skeletal replacement rather than the required sight-reveal.** Keep the exact living old-woman head, face surface, tight white bun, hands and bent full-body pose from view (b), then show the approved Bone Lady's long-jawed skull and needle-like bone fingers as a pale, translucent, precisely aligned ghost visible through that living face and those living hands, like a figure through thin paper. Do not remove the living skin or substitute a solid skull and bare skeletal hands.

3. **The sheet ground violates the locked presentation and weakens silhouette testing.** Remove every mountain, pine, rock and landscape mark behind and beneath the four views. Place all four views on one uninterrupted plain warm-paper ground, with no scenic horizon or localized vignette background.
