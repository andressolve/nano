## Stage 1: the sheet against the approved system

**Sheet hygiene.** The sheet has no lettering, labels, watermark, border or scenery. It sits on a plain warm-paper ground, landscape, with four depictions.

**Views (a) to (c).**
- **(a)** Three-quarter head and shoulders. She is weeping, with bare fingertips at the cheek and no handkerchief.
- **(b)** Full length, bent over a bamboo cane.
- **(c)** Profile head on a three-quarter-back body. The head reads as profile and shows the nose, blunt chin, tight bun, wooden pin and bent back.

All three views required by the amended lock are present and correct.

**Adversarial tests on (a) to (c).**
- **Face with the costume removed:** an elderly, deeply wrinkled, broad-cheeked woman's face with narrow eyes, a low rounded nose, a rounded chin and a small toothless mouth. No other approved sheet has an old human face, so with the clothes gone she still collides with nothing.
- **Thumbnail silhouette:** a small, deeply bent mass with a knob of bun at the crown and a vertical cane line in front. No other approved silhouette has this. Wujing stoops too, but he is tall and gaunt, with flaring hair and a spade. Her silhouette is the most compact on the sheet set.
- **Grayscale:** a dark-brown garment mass under a white head. This is the opposite value pattern to the girl, who has a black head over a mid-light jacket. Sanzang reads as a light robe under a pale dome. The separation holds.
- **Strict profile:** the head in (c) keeps the same compact face, low nose, rounded chin, bun and pin. It matches (a) and (b).
- **Reaction state:** the weeping face in (a) is the script's state. Grief does not stretch the face into anyone else's.
- **Against the nearest lookalike in the same panel:** the nearest approved lookalike is the girl (`12-bone-girl`), on purpose, because they are the same demon in two disguises. They separate on age, hair (white bun with pin against a black plait with a red string), garment value (dark brown against peach-pink), prop (cane against basket) and posture (bent against upright). The green pinpoints are shared by design and are the reader's clue, not a collision.
- **Re-entry after a long absence:** she appears only on pp 9–10, so re-entry risk is minimal. Her reserved stack (white bun, brown padded jacket, bamboo cane) is complete and recognisable at small size.

## Nonblocking observations

1. **View (d) breaks the Never list but is out of scope.** It shows a skull showing through the right half of her living face, with lit green eye-hollows. That breaks the ledger's Never clause ("never puts a skull inside or over her living face … never adds … extra green lights") and the plan's "only green" rule. The relock ruling explicitly makes view (d) out of scope and nonblocking, so it does not gate this sheet. The risk is that a locked file carrying a skull-in-face image can still teach that composition if the whole sheet is attached to a page generation. Recommended protection (not a condition of this approval): every page prompt that attaches `13-bone-old-woman` should say that only the three left-hand views are the reference, and that the p10 reveal is drawn from `11-bone-lady` plus words, as the ruling already requires. The page critic card for pp 9–10 should name the "no skull inside her face" lane.
2. **Eye pinpoints.** In (a) the green reads a little larger and more glow-like than the "one tiny pinpoint" wording. It is still clearly a pinpoint and helps the reader. Not worth a reroll.
3. **Profile view.** In (c) the head is in profile but the body is turned three-quarter-back. Face, nose, chin, bun, pin and bent back all read, so the profile purpose is met.
4. **Undercollar.** A pale undercollar shows at the neckline in all views. It is a small light accent, not a white robe, and gives no Guanyin signal.
5. **System gaps for the whole-system gate.** The old woman / old man lane and the `adversarial-disguises` and `adversarial-silhouettes-b` boards cannot be judged until `14-bone-old-man` is approved. The reference gate must test them then; this approval says nothing about them.
