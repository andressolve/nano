- Brow and eyes: light juvenile brows and round dark eyes; no gold irises or red rings.
- Nose and mouth: short macaque muzzles and pinkish-brown noses; the fifth monkey's open chattering mouth supplies the required reaction while remaining unlike Wukong's broad adult mouth.
- Hair mass and line: short brown fur throughout; no golden mass and no swept golden crown/rear ruff. The fourth monkey has a close rounded skull-following cap in both appearances.
- Facial hair: no beard, moustache, or human hair treatment.
- Build: the required five builds are legible and individually repeatable: lanky, plump, tiny, wiry, and round/compact.
- Posture and gesture: scrambling on all fours, holding the peach, reaching upward to tug, hanging by one arm, and crouching to chatter are all present and distinct.
- Costume value pattern and accent: every monkey is bare, with no robe, kilt, band, crown, staff, clothing, or gold. The single peach and single supporting branch are the two expressly permitted action props.

- View requirement: the sheet uses the ensemble-specific format specified in the plan—five full-length action figures above and the same five three-quarter heads below, in matching order—rather than the generic four-view single-character format.
