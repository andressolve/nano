# Batch gate — pages 21–30 — sequence

## Blind sequence read

Wukong declares himself happily home and lets the young monkeys celebrate him, but he cannot remove the gold band and will not surrender the robe his monk repaired. His performance of freedom gives way to longing: every evening he climbs the peak and looks west while claiming he is only watching the weather.

On the quieter road west, Bajie has gone begging and Sanzang sends Wujing after him, insisting that he can wait alone. Hunger draws Sanzang into an open golden pagoda, where the Yellow Robe is waiting. The demon captures and binds him. Bajie and Wujing find the monk's staff at the gate and confront the demon, but their admission that Wukong was sent away exposes the party's weakness; the Yellow Robe defeats them in a single breath, Bajie escapes, and Wujing is taken.

Bound beside Sanzang, Wujing blames himself for leaving the monk's back. Sanzang instead accepts that he sent away the one protector who could have kept him safe and admits that he ignored Wukong's warning three times. The Yellow Robe answers the monk's claim to gentleness by turning him into a tiger and tempting him with meat. Caged with a tiger's hunger, Sanzang refuses it, preserving the moral identity the demon meant to disprove. The demon closes the high shutter before sleeping so no light from the sky can reach him.
