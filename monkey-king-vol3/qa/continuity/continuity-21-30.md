# Visual-continuity gate — pages 21–30

## Verdict

APPROVED

Pages 21–30 belong to the same book. The run keeps the Mineral Ink register: visible brush-and-ink contours, toothed paper, pooled mineral washes, and a consistent finished-page density. Palette and lighting move coherently from Flower-Fruit Mountain's wet malachite and orange evening through the cool pine road into the pagoda's black stone, warm lamp points, and deep shadow. Black levels rise appropriately inside the pagoda without changing the rendering language.

Character recognition and state remain stable. Wukong is the small golden, banded pilgrim in his flower crown; the mountain monkeys remain young, brown, bare, and distinct from him. Sanzang retains his shaven young face, saffron robe, red-and-gold kasaya, and ringed staff. Wujing stays gaunt and slate blue-gray with wild red hair, no skulls, and the crescent spade; Bajie remains the round pig-headed figure in black with his rake. The White Horse keeps its white value, pale-blue mane, tack, and sea-green eye. Yellow Robe remains the huge indigo, tusked, black-armoured demon in the mustard star robe. Sanzang's tiger state preserves the monk's dark gentle eyes, torn red-and-gold kasaya, upright posture, and joined forepaws.

The recurring settings and consequential object also remain legible. Flower-Fruit Mountain carries the waterfall, wet green rock, peach growth, and faded pink banner; the pagoda remains a gold-roofed tower in black-green pines and resolves into the same black-stone, firelit hall. The shut tall iron cage and untouched joint of meat on page 30 preserve the object lock and the intended story state.

Nonblocking variation: page 23 presents the pagoda entrance more frontally and with more visible timber than the exterior plate, while pages 26–27 restore the locked black gate and tower silhouette. Page 30 gives the cage stronger warm lamp reflections than the isolated object plate. Neither variation confuses place, object identity, chronology, or reading, so neither justifies a redraw.

Lettering weight, caption treatment, balloon shape, tails, and page finish remain consistent across the sequence. No page breaks the book's visual continuity.
