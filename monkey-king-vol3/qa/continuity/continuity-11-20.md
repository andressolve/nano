# Visual continuity — pages 11–20

APPROVED

No page in this span falls out of the same book.

The White Tiger Ridge pages keep the approved chalk-white, bone-and-ash ground,
hazy azurite distance, visible ink line, paper tooth, and mineral-wash finish.
Their shift from cool daylight into long amber afternoon is motivated by the
sequence rather than a palette or lighting discontinuity. Pages 19–20 then make
the intended location change cleanly: Flower-Fruit Mountain returns to wet
malachite rock, white water, the split peak and cave, the faded pink banner, and
an orange western evening.

Wukong, Sanzang, Bajie, Wujing, the white horse, the old-man disguise, the White
Bone Lady, the bone heap, and the young brown monkeys remain recognizable from
their approved locks. Costume and object state also carry: Wukong's thin gold
band, patched hemp robe, tiger-skin kilt and red-and-gold staff; Sanzang's
saffron and red robes and ringed staff; Wujing's bare neck; the old man's gray
gown, white fringe and beard, and prayer beads; and the skull's carved brow band
all remain legible. The larger monkey crowd on page 20 stays brown, bare, young,
and subordinate to Wukong, so its natural crowd variation does not confuse the
reserved identity.

Contrast, black level, brush-and-ink treatment, balloon shape, lettering
weight, and overall finish remain continuous. The isolated narration pages and
the warmer page 15 climax vary composition and temperature without changing
the book's visual language.

Revision findings: none.
