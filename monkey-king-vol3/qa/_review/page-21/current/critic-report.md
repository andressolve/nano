## Blind read
The Monkey King returns to a waterfall grotto and boasts to the gathered monkeys that he is finally free of the monk, magic words, and walking. He owns the page, especially the large middle panel. His triumphant rejection turns into frustration when he cannot remove the gold band beneath the flower crown; then, when a young monkey tugs at his patched robe, he protects the robe because his monk sewed the patch. The turn is his involuntary attachment to the monk surfacing immediately after he declares his separation final.
