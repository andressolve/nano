## Findings

### Finding C2
- Observation: In the dominant middle panel, the old woman is already lying face-down and still while Wukong holds the staff raised on a steep diagonal; the staff is not coming down on her and no second impact is visible.
- Material reader harm: The required causal turn—the second blow that drops the old woman before the mist escapes—does not read. The panel instead reads as Wukong standing armed over an already fallen body, changing the decisive action at the center of the page.
- Redraw justification: The missing blow is the page's dominant dramatic event, not a cosmetic action-detail issue; without it, the reader cannot see what happened, so preserving the complete page would preserve a materially different story beat.
