## Blind read
The hungry monk asks Wukong whether there is a village on the snowy ridge, and Wukong spots peaches to the south instead. Wukong and the middle panel own the page: already on his cloud, he points at the seated monk, orders him to stay put, and leaves Wujing guarding him. By the final panel Wukong has become a distant streak in the sky, turning the halt into a departure and prompting Bajie to grumble about his authority.

## Findings

### Finding C5
- Observation: In the dominant middle panel, Wujing visibly wears a thick braided cord around his waist.
- Material reader harm: The cord is a specifically forbidden identity element under C5 and makes Wujing’s focal, dominant-panel design materially inconsistent with the required cord-free identity.
- Redraw justification: The prohibited element is large, clear, and attached to a named focal figure in the page-owning panel, so it cannot be dismissed as a tiny prop or background cosmetic issue; correcting the identity error is substantial enough to risk replacing the complete page.
