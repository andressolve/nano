## Blind read
The bowed monkey asks to bow once before leaving, but the monk turns away and refuses to look. The large middle panel owns the page: the monkey pulls three hairs and creates three additional bowing monkeys, surrounding the monk so that wherever he turns he must face a bow. In the final panel, the monkey quietly instructs the blue-skinned staff-bearer to walk behind the monk and keep the pig from influencing him; the staff-bearer agrees. The turn is the monkey's multiplication, which defeats the monk's attempt to refuse the farewell gesture.
