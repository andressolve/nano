VERDICT: REVISE

## Blind read
A pig-like man tumbles into a forest stream and recoils when a white horse speaks. The page belongs to the pig-like man's emotional turn in the dominant middle panel: after the horse reports that its master and Wujing are captive and neither of them can defeat the demon, the pig-like man sends the horse for their big brother but admits that he lied about the bones and expects that lie to keep the brother away. The horse changes despair into a course of action by telling him to confess the truth so Wukong will return for the master.

## Findings

### Finding C2
- Observation: In the dominant middle panel, the balloon containing `Go to Flower-Fruit Mountain and bring back our big brother.` is placed over Bajie and its tail rises toward Bajie's mouth, so the line reads as Bajie's rather than the horse's.
- Material reader harm: The exchange's causal ownership is reversed: Bajie appears to issue the instruction himself instead of receiving the horse's counsel, making the confession that follows read as a self-contradictory continuation rather than the response that drives the page's turn.
- Redraw justification: This is a blocking misattribution of one of the six required lines at the centre of the page, materially damaging the dominant dramatic relationship and the scripted reason Bajie resolves to seek Wukong; correcting it warrants replacing the complete page.
