## Blind read
Wukong owns the page. He frees the bowed, chained prisoner, who confesses that he left the monk alone while searching for the pig. Wukong then finds his Master transformed into a tiger inside a cage and reassures him that he recognizes him beneath any face. The untouched meat becomes the proof and the turn: even after three days hungry and trapped in a predator's body, the monk has kept his rule.
