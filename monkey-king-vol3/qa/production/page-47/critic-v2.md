## Blind read
In the torchlit prison, Wukong faces his kneeling master and says that, in the cage, the master alone saw the self inside the beast; this explains the hurt behind what Wukong did. The middle panel owns the page: the master bows with hands pressed together, admits he should have believed Wukong, apologizes, and says sending him away saved his life. In the final panel Wukong breaks the solemnity, tells his master to rise before the pig sees him bowing to a monkey, then quietly confirms that he heard the apology the first time. The turn is the master's remorse and apology being met by Wukong's dry mercy and acceptance.

## Findings

### Finding C1
- Observation: The first balloon’s tail points toward Wukong, so the page attributes Sanzang’s first scripted line to Wukong.
- Material reader harm: The wrong mouth owns the confession, reversing who admits having been a beast in the cage and breaking the causal setup for Sanzang’s apology.
- Redraw justification: Speaker ownership is central to the reconciliation and is explicitly blocking; the page cannot deliver the scripted emotional turn while Wukong appears to make Sanzang’s confession.
