## Page intent

## PAGE 35

Illustrated prose. Bajie owns the page alone: the worst flyer in the sky, going back. He sits on a small, lumpy, gray cloud that sags in the middle like an old mattress, high over a wide sea, eyes squeezed shut, rake clutched, ears flapping, and far ahead a green peak rises on the coast. The dominant turn is the flight itself: a pig who has never gone back for anybody and never told the truth when a lie would do is flying east to do both, rehearsing a truth that sounds terrible every time. The prose carries the comedy and the shame together; the picture carries the scale of the sea and how small and miserable he is on it. The reader turns because he has reached the monkey's mountain. Nobody else appears.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 35 — card

### C1 — Transcription
All three prose paragraphs read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once each, in order, with no other text on the page: no balloon, caption or sound cue. A paragraph that does not read, is missing, doubled or reworded is blocking. A paragraph that reads is never measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly or straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word (practised/practiced), the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue, and the number of waves, clouds or background props. The words and their order are what must match. No object on this page carries scripted words.

### C2 — The page is the flight
Illustrated prose, one dominant illustration: Bajie small and miserable on his cloud over a wide sea, a green peak far ahead. Blocking if the prose sits over his face, hands or cloud so the picture's joke is lost, or if the page reads as a dramatic exchange. Judge by what the eye finds.

### C3 — Bajie and his cloud
Bajie must read as the pig: pig head, long snout, floppy ears, huge belly, black tunic, rake. His cloud must read as small, lumpy, gray and sagging. Blocking: a bright, white or golden cloud, a large smooth cloud, or a figure who is not plainly Bajie. Ear angle, eyes open or shut, and how he holds the rake are nonblocking.

### C4 — He is alone
No other named character appears: no Wukong, no monk, no Wujing, no horse, no monkeys. A second figure is blocking because it breaks "he had never gone back for anybody".
