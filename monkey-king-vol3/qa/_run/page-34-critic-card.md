## Page intent

## PAGE 34

Dramatic. At the stream, the horse tells Bajie what has happened and what must be done. Bajie falls backward into the water at hearing him talk; the horse says only when it matters, that the master is a tiger in a cage and Wujing is tied, and that neither of them can beat the demon. Face to face, the horse tells him to bring back their big brother from Flower-Fruit Mountain, and Bajie confesses that he lied about the bones. The dominant turn is that confession, answered by the horse: then tell Wukong that, tell him the truth. The reader turns because the pig must go back for the monkey he sent away. Wukong is absent and only named. The stream and the woods stay subordinate to two faces.

## Materiality threshold

The critic is a release gate, not a defect collector. A visible issue is
blocking only when it materially harms the reader event, exact text or
attribution, named or focal identity, consequential continuity or object state,
focal generation integrity, or the dominant dramatic relationship. Every
correction replaces the complete page. Minor background, cosmetic, tiny-prop,
geometry, colour, texture, and prompt-fidelity observations are nonblocking.
Omit them. Numeric panel shares and lettering sizes are builder construction
guidance, never critic measurements.

Each numbered criterion is also its stable retry signature. Cite it only when
the reader harm and redraw justification are concrete.

A lettered speaker name, label, or tag anywhere on the page is extra text and
blocks under the transcription criterion, whatever else reads correctly:
finished pages carry dialogue, captions, prose, and sound cues only.
A sound cue whose letters and order match the script reads correctly; the run
length of a repeated letter in it is nonblocking and never counted.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 34 — card

### C1 — Transcription or ownership fails
Any of the six strings is missing, altered, duplicated, reordered, or does not read from the 600 × 900 proof, or extra text appears; a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, the capitals in "TALK" rendered as emphasis; the words and their order are what must match. Also nonblocking: the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue, and incidental prop counts. No object carries scripted words. A line placed directly under its speaker's previous balloon, or naming its addressee, is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — Attribution across the exchange fails
"You can TALK?" and "He won't come…" must hang from Bajie's mouth; the other four lines from the horse's mouth, with each speaker in frame when he speaks. A horse balloon whose tail points at Bajie, or a Bajie balloon whose tail points at the horse, is blocking. The horse's two opening lines must read before the instruction, and Bajie's confession before the horse's last line.

### C3 — The confession and its answer do not land
The eye does not find the close face-to-face of horse and pig as the page's centre, so the confession and the horse's reply lose their weight; or Bajie's ears are not down in the last beat.

### C4 — Identity or wound state fails
Bajie loses the pig head, floppy ears, belly or black tunic; the horse loses the pale-blue mane or sea-green eyes; the right-foreleg wound is absent, bandaged or bloody.

### C5 — Wrong figures or generation failure
Wukong appears (he is only named), or the monk, Wujing, the dragon or the Yellow Robe; a duplicated figure within one panel; gross focal anatomy on either face. Splash and stone counts are nonblocking.
