## Materiality threshold

The critic is a release gate, not a defect collector. A visible issue is
blocking only when it materially harms the reader event, exact text or
attribution, named or focal identity, consequential continuity or object state,
focal generation integrity, or the dominant dramatic relationship. Every
correction replaces the complete page. Minor background, cosmetic, tiny-prop,
geometry, colour, texture, and prompt-fidelity observations are nonblocking.
Omit them. Numeric panel shares and lettering sizes are builder construction

Each numbered criterion is also its stable retry signature. Cite it only when

### C1 — Caption transcription fails
Either scripted caption is missing, altered, duplicated, out of order, or does not read from the 600 × 900 proof, or any other text appears; a caption that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word; the words and their order are what must match. Also nonblocking: the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue, and incidental prop counts. No object carries scripted words.

### C2 — The failed rescue does not read
The single image does not show, by eye, the dragon at the cage trying to tear it open and the Yellow Robe's blade catching his foreleg; the reader cannot tell the dragon is hurt and losing.

### C3 — Identity or the Wujing / Yellow Robe pair fails
The tiger lacks the torn red kasaya at the throat or the monk's dark eyes; the dragon lacks the sea-green eyes and pale-blue mane; or Wujing and the Yellow Robe cannot be told apart (gaunt slate-gray red-haired ogre in sand-brown vs huge indigo-faced black-topknotted demon in mustard-ochre over black armour), or one of them is drawn twice.

### C4 — Wound or cage in the wrong state
The wound shows blood, or is not on the dragon's foreleg; the cage is open or the tiger is outside it; the window still shuttered with no moonlight. Bar count and lamp count are nonblocking.

### C5 — Wrong figures or generation failure
Wukong or the white horse appears; a skull or necklace on Wujing; a bright-gold scimitar; a duplicated named figure; gross focal anatomy on the dragon, the tiger or the Yellow Robe's sword hand. Background texture and the fur bed are nonblocking.
