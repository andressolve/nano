# Exact script — pages 21–30

Open only after the blind sequence read.

## PAGE 21 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the Water Curtain Cave behind the waterfall.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Inside the cave behind the falling water: young brown
monkeys putting a crown of flowers and leaves on Wukong's head, on top of the
gold band. He sits on a stone throne, one foot up.

> **WUKONG:** "That's right, your king is home! No monk, no magic words, and no walking. Not ever again!"

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** Wukong straining with both hands to drag the
gold band up off his head; it will not move. A chain of monkeys pulling on
him from behind, like a tug-of-war.

> **WUKONG:** "And this thing comes off too. Pull! Pull harder! It won't come off."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** A small monkey on the left tugging at the sleeve of
Wukong's hemp robe to take it off him. Wukong on the right, snatching the
sleeve back, fierce, a hand over the mended patch on his shoulder.

> **WUKONG:** "Leave the robe alone. My monk sewed that patch himself."

**Why you turn the page:** he is home and free, and he will not take off the robe.

## PAGE 22 · *illustrated prose*

**Location:** Flower-Fruit Mountain, the peak.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 70%.** Sunset. Wukong alone on the peak under the
rag of faded pink banner, the flower crown slipping over the gold band, one
hand shading his eyes, looking west over the hills. Below him, monkeys
asleep in the peach trees.

> **PROSE FIELD 1** *(in the open sky to the west, never over his face)*
> "He was happy at home. He told everybody so.
>
> He ate peaches until he was sick, and swam in the pool, and sat on his throne with his foot up, and nobody told him to hurry, or to wait, or to put the staff away. Nobody said three words to him.
>
> And every evening, when the sun went down over the hills, he climbed to the top of the peak, shaded his eyes, and looked west for a long time. He told the monkeys he was looking at the weather."

**Why you turn the page:** far to the west, the monk has nobody at the front.

# MOVEMENT IV — THE YELLOW ROBE · pp 23–34

---

## PAGE 23 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the edge of a pine wood.

**Panel 1 — 35%.** A road running along the edge of a dark pine wood.
Wujing on the right, leaning on his crescent spade, torn, looking back down
the empty road. Sanzang on the left beside the white horse. No Bajie anywhere.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left)*
> "Without Wukong, the road was quieter, and slower, and the monk was hungrier."
>
> **WUJING:** "Wukong told me to walk at your back, Master. But Bajie's been gone an hour with the begging bowl."
>
> **SANZANG:** "Go and find him, Wujing. I'm not a child. I can sit by a horse."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 65%.** Sanzang alone, his ringed staff in his hand,
tying the white horse to a pine. A short way up a path, just inside the
trees, a tall pagoda with gleaming gold roofs, its heavy black-iron front
door standing open.

> **SANZANG:** "A pagoda! Where there's a pagoda, there are monks. And where there are monks, there's supper."

**Why you turn the page:** the monk is walking up the path to the open door alone.

---

## PAGE 24 · *spectacle*

**Location:** inside the golden pagoda.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 100%.** A great dark hall of black stone, lit by a few
iron lamps. On a wide stone bed heaped with furs, the Yellow Robe asleep:
huge, a deep indigo face with two white tusks, a black topknot, a
mustard-ochre robe stitched with silver stars over black armour, a curved
scimitar across his knees. In the open black-iron front door, small, the monk
backing away, his ringed staff slipping out of his hand onto the threshold. One of the demon's pale silver eyes is
open, looking at him.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left)*
> "There were no monks in the pagoda. There was only the demon they called the Yellow Robe."

**Why you turn the page:** the demon is awake.

---

## PAGE 25 · *dramatic*

**Location:** inside the golden pagoda.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** The Yellow Robe on the right, his huge hand closing on
the monk's shoulder. Sanzang on the left, frozen.

> **YELLOW ROBE:** "Well, well. The little monk going west. Everybody on this road wants to eat you."
>
> **YELLOW ROBE:** "And you walked into my house, where nobody ever comes looking for me."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** Sanzang on the left, tied to a thick black
pillar, frightened, chin up. The Yellow Robe on the right, sprawled back on
his stone bed, tossing the scimitar from hand to hand.

> **SANZANG:** "I've never eaten meat, and I've never hurt anyone. Eat me if you must. But you may not hurt my friends."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** The Yellow Robe on the right, laughing, head thrown back.
The monk small on the left at his pillar.

> **YELLOW ROBE:** "May not! A monk tied to my pillar tells me what I may not do!"

**Why you turn the page:** the demon thinks the monk is a joke.

## PAGE 26 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the gate of the golden pagoda.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** Outside the pagoda, at its heavy black-iron front door,
shut tight now: Bajie on the left, banging on it with his rake; Wujing on the
right, his crescent spade raised. The monk's ringed staff lying on the ground
in front of the door. The white horse tied to a pine behind them.

> **BAJIE:** "Hey! You in the gold house! Give back our master, or I'll rake your roof off!"
>
> **WUJING:** "His staff is lying at your door. We know he's in there."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 45%.** The door swinging open: the Yellow Robe
filling the doorway, scimitar on his shoulder, delighted. Bajie and Wujing
small in front of him.

> **YELLOW ROBE:** "A pig and an ogre. Is this the monk's famous guard? Where's the monkey everybody talks about?"

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Bajie on the left, glancing sideways at Wujing. Wujing on
the right, answering, flat.

> **BAJIE:** "He's, um, busy."
>
> **WUJING:** "He was sent away."

**Why you turn the page:** the demon knows the monkey is gone.

## PAGE 27 · *spectacle*

**Location:** the gate of the golden pagoda.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 100%.** The fight in the clearing among the pines:
the Yellow Robe's scimitar sweeping in a silver arc; Bajie's rake spinning
out of his hands; Wujing on his back under the demon's boot, his crescent
spade knocked away; Bajie running for the reeds by the stream, ears flying.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left)*
> "The pig and the ogre fought him together. It took him one breath to beat them both."

**Why you turn the page:** the pig has run, and the ogre is caught.

## PAGE 28 · *dramatic*

**Location:** inside the golden pagoda.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Wujing on the left, tied to the black pillar beside the
monk, bruised, head down. Sanzang on the right, tied to the same pillar.

> **WUJING:** "Wukong told me to walk at your back, Master. I went looking for the pig instead. I didn't keep you safe."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** Close on Sanzang at the pillar, on the right,
turning his face to Wujing, saying it for the first time. Wujing on the left.

> **SANZANG:** "No, Wujing. I sent away the one who could have kept me."
>
> **SANZANG:** "I saw a girl and was sure she was a girl. He told me three times, and three times I said the words."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Silent. The monk tied to the pillar, looking at nothing,
his eyes wet. Wujing beside him, looking at him. The dark hall around them.

**Why you turn the page:** the monk knows what he did, and the demon is coming back.

## PAGE 29 · *dramatic*

**Location:** inside the golden pagoda.

**Panel 1 — 35%.** The Yellow Robe on the right, crouched in front of the
monk, a cup of water in his hand, grinning. Sanzang on the left at the
pillar, Wujing tied beside him.

> **YELLOW ROBE:** "A monk who won't hurt anyone! Let's see how gentle you are with claws and teeth."
>
> **YELLOW ROBE:** "Everybody's a beast underneath, monk. When you eat that meat, you'll know it too. Then I'll eat you."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 65%.** A fine silver mist spraying from the demon's
mouth over the monk; the monk's shape swelling, black stripes rising through
his saffron robe, his face turning into a tiger's, the red kasaya tearing at
his shoulder; the ropes falling slack. Wujing straining away from it at the
pillar.

> **SOUND** *(in the spray)*
> "PFFFT"

**Why you turn the page:** the monk is a tiger.

## PAGE 30 · *illustrated prose*

**Location:** inside the golden pagoda.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 70%.** An iron cage lit by one iron lamp. Inside it a
thin tiger with a torn red kasaya knotted at its throat, sitting upright with
its front paws together, its face turned to the wall. On the cage floor, an
untouched joint of meat on a bone. High above, a shutter closed tight over
the hall's one window. Wujing tied to the pillar in the dark, watching.

> **PROSE FIELD 1** *(over the black stone wall, never over the cage)*
> "The Yellow Robe put the tiger in an iron cage and threw in a joint of meat.
>
> The monk had never eaten meat in his life. Now he had a tiger's hunger, which is enormous, and a tiger's claws and teeth.
>
> He turned his face to the wall and sat with his front paws together.
>
> All day the meat lay there. When the demon went to bed, it was still there, and he did not laugh about it quite so loudly. Before he lay down, he pulled the shutter across the high window, as he did every night, so that no light from the sky could fall on him."

**Why you turn the page:** outside, in the dark, somebody else has been watching.
