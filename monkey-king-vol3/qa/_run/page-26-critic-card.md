### C2 — The demon fills the doorway
The dominant image is the Yellow Robe filling the opened doorway over two small fighters. If he does not dominate by eye, the threat fails: blocking. Dramatic mode.

### C3 — Attribution of the last exchange
"He's, um, busy." must hang from Bajie's mouth and "He was sent away." from Wujing's, Bajie's first. These two short lines are easily swapped: either owned by the wrong mouth is blocking, because the flat truth must be Wujing's.

### C4 — Door and staff
In the first image the black-iron door is shut and the monk's ringed staff lies on the ground before it, visible: Wujing's line points at it. A missing staff, the staff in someone's hand, or the door open there is blocking.

### C5 — Identity and separation
