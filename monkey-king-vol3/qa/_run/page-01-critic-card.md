## Page intent

## PAGE 1

Illustrated prose. The book opens on the road west along the spine of a white, empty ridge: the monk on his white horse, the pig and the river ogre sharing the luggage pole, the pig complaining, and far ahead on a boulder the monkey with the gold band on his brow, shading his eyes. The prose owns the page and carries the turn: who these travellers are, the band and its three words, and the monkey's eyes that see the real face under any face. The picture is wide, bright and drained to bone; nobody speaks. What changes is that the reader now holds the two rules the whole book turns on, the band and the eyes. The reader turns because the ridge is too empty, and something on it is watching.

## Exact owner-controlled script

## PAGE 1 · *illustrated prose*

**Location:** the road west over White Tiger Ridge.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 70%.** A narrow pale road along the spine of a high
ridge of white rock, hazy blue valleys falling away on both sides. The monk
Sanzang on the white horse; behind him Wujing and Bajie carrying the luggage
pole between them, Bajie's mouth open, complaining. Far ahead, up on a white
boulder, Wukong in his hemp robe with the thin gold band on his brow, one
hand shading his eyes, looking west.

> **PROSE FIELD 1** *(in the open sky above the ridge)*
> "Months after the river of sand, they were still walking west. The monk Sanzang rode a white horse that had once been a dragon. Bajie the pig and Wujing the river ogre carried the luggage, and Bajie complained about it.
>
> Wukong ran ahead. He could cross the world in one somersault, but he was never allowed to fly the monk: the road was the monk's to walk. On Wukong's head was a gold band that squeezed like a fist whenever the monk said three words: Gold, hold fast. The monk had promised to say them only if he must.
>
> Long ago, in a furnace in Heaven, smoke had got into Wukong's eyes and stayed there. No disguise could fool them now. He saw the real face under any face. His iron staff he kept as a needle behind his ear."

**Why you turn the page:** the ridge is empty, and something on it is watching them.

## Materiality threshold

The critic is a release gate, not a defect collector. A visible issue is
blocking only when it materially harms the reader event, exact text or
attribution, named or focal identity, consequential continuity or object state,
focal generation integrity, or the dominant dramatic relationship. Every
correction replaces the complete page. Minor background, cosmetic, tiny-prop,
geometry, colour, texture, and prompt-fidelity observations are nonblocking.
Omit them. Numeric panel shares and lettering sizes are builder construction
guidance, never critic measurements.

Each numbered criterion is also its stable retry signature. Cite it only when
the reader harm and redraw justification are concrete.

A lettered speaker name, label, or tag anywhere on the page is extra text and
blocks under the transcription criterion, whatever else reads correctly:
finished pages carry dialogue, captions, prose, and sound cues only.
A sound cue whose letters and order match the script reads correctly; the run
length of a repeated letter in it is nonblocking and never counted.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 1 — card

### C1 — Prose transcription fails
All three prose paragraphs must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order, in one prose field, with no extra text on the page; a paragraph or sentence that does not read, is altered, dropped, doubled, or reordered is blocking, and a paragraph that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes or apostrophes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word; the words and their order are what must match. The number of bundles on the pole or rings on the monk's staff is never a transcription or count matter.

### C2 — The mode or the opening does not land
The page must read as one illustrated-prose page: the prose field owns the reading, over one picture of the company on an empty white ridge, the monk riding, the pig and the ogre sharing the pole, the monkey far ahead looking west. A balloon, a caption, or a sound anywhere is blocking. Exact field placement is nonblocking if the field never covers a figure.

### C3 — Band on
The thin gold band on Wukong's brow in every panel he is in. A Wukong with a bare brow, a crown, or a jeweled or wide band is blocking; at this distance a thin gold line at the brow is enough, and its finish is nonblocking.

### C4 — Identity and separation
Wukong reads as the small golden monkey in the hemp robe; the monk as the shaven young man in saffron and red on a slim white horse with a pale-blue mane; the pig and the ogre as clearly two different creatures. A skull, necklace, or neck cord on Wujing is blocking (his sash of river reeds at the waist is correct). Exact saddle hardware is nonblocking.

### C5 — Generation integrity
A duplicated named figure, a second monkey, a demon or extra person, the monk's wooden ringed staff drawn as a red-and-gold staff, or gross anatomy on a focal figure is blocking. Background rock and pine texture are not.
