## Page intent

## PAGE 3

Dramatic. The travellers stop on the bare ridge. The monk is so hungry he can't see straight; Wukong, up on a dead stump, finds no village but knows of peaches to the south. The dominant moment is Wukong on a puff of cloud, pointing at the seated monk like a teacher: sit on that rock and don't move, I can't fly you but I can fly peaches, while Wujing promises to stand at the monk's back. Then Wukong is gone, a streak south, and Bajie throws down his end of the pole and grumbles about who made the monkey the boss; the monk answers mildly. The turn: Wukong flies for peaches and leaves an order behind. The grumble stays light. The reader turns because the monk is alone on the ridge with two guards and an empty stomach.

## Exact owner-controlled script

## PAGE 3 · *dramatic*

**Location:** White Tiger Ridge.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** The travellers stopped on the bare road. Sanzang on the
left, sliding down off the white horse, one hand on his stomach, looking
round at nothing but rock. Wukong on the right, up on a dead pine stump, hand
shading his eyes.

> **SANZANG:** "Wukong, I'm so hungry I can't see straight. Is there a village anywhere on this ridge?"
>
> **WUKONG:** "Not a village, not a farm, not a goat. But there are peaches on a mountain to the south."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 45%.** Wukong on the right, a puff of cloud already
under his feet, pointing a finger at the monk like a teacher. Sanzang on the
left, sitting down on a flat rock. Wujing behind the monk, standing.

> **WUKONG:** "Sit on that rock, Master, and don't move until I'm back. I can't fly you anywhere, but I can fly peaches."
>
> **WUJING:** "Go. I'll stand at his back."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Wukong gone, a streak of cloud vanishing south. Bajie on
the left, dropping his end of the luggage pole, scowling up at the sky.
Sanzang on the right on his rock.

> **BAJIE:** "'Don't move.' 'Hurry up.' 'Hands off the horse.' Who made the monkey the boss?"
>
> **SANZANG:** "Nobody, Bajie. He's just the fastest."

**Why you turn the page:** the monkey has gone, and the monk is alone on the ridge with two guards and an empty stomach.

## Materiality threshold

The critic is a release gate, not a defect collector. A visible issue is
blocking only when it materially harms the reader event, exact text or
attribution, named or focal identity, consequential continuity or object state,
focal generation integrity, or the dominant dramatic relationship. Every
correction replaces the complete page. Minor background, cosmetic, tiny-prop,
geometry, colour, texture, and prompt-fidelity observations are nonblocking.
Omit them. Numeric panel shares and lettering sizes are builder construction
guidance, never critic measurements.

Each numbered criterion is also its stable retry signature. Cite it only when
the reader harm and redraw justification are concrete.

blocks under the transcription criterion, whatever else reads correctly:
finished pages carry dialogue, captions, prose, and sound cues only.
A sound cue whose letters and order match the script reads correctly; the run

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 3 — card

### C1 — Dialogue transcription or ownership fails
All six strings must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order, each hanging from its own speaker's mouth by tail and placement, with no extra text; a string that does not read is blocking, and a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes (including the single quotes in the pig's line), a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word; the words and their order are what must match. A line placed directly under its speaker's previous balloon, or naming its addressee, is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — Attribution of the three exchanges
Panel one: the monk asks, Wukong answers, each tail to his own mouth, the answer lower. Panel two: Wukong's order first, then Wujing's promise hanging from Wujing's mouth, never from the seated monk's. Panel three: the pig's grumble first, the monk's reply lower, from the monk. Each reply's speaker must be in frame with mouth visible; a reply that reads before its prompt or tails to the wrong figure is blocking.

### C3 — The turn does not land
The panel of Wukong on his cloud, pointing at the seated monk, owns the page; by panel three he is gone, a streak south. Judge by what the eye finds first. If Wukong is still standing on the road in panel three, the departure fails; blocking.

### C4 — Band on
The thin gold band on Wukong's brow in every panel he is in. A bare brow, a crown, or a jeweled or wide band in any panel showing his head is blocking; its finish is nonblocking.

### C5 — Identity and separation
The monk shaven in saffron and red; the pig round and black-clad; the ogre gaunt, blue-gray, red-bearded, in sand-brown. A skull, necklace, or neck cord on Wujing is blocking (his sash of river reeds at the waist is correct). Pole and bundle details are nonblocking.

### C6 — Generation integrity
A duplicated named figure, a balloon on a silent figure, malformed speaking faces or the pointing hand, or the monk's ringed staff drawn as Wukong's red-and-gold staff is blocking. Background hands and texture are not.
