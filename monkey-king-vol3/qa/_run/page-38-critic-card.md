## Page intent

## PAGE 38

Dramatic. Bajie stops being funny. Sitting back on his heels, ears down, he confesses: he lied about the bones, he knew it was her, her name was right there on the skull. The dominant turn is the close on his face, small eyes wet, saying why: Wukong was always the fastest, the bossiest, the master's favourite, and Bajie wanted to be the one he liked best. Then, looking up, he tells what happened since: a demon in a yellow robe, big as a door, has made the monk a tiger, Wujing is tied up, the horse got cut and then talked, and sent him here. Wukong only listens, very still, the peach forgotten in his hand. The reader turns because Wukong has heard the truth and has to decide.
