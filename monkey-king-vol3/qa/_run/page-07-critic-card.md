## Page intent

## PAGE 7

Dramatic page. The turn: Bajie tells the monk Wukong once fooled him with a face, and the monk says the words. Bajie owns the first two beats, whispering at the monk's ear and then in full cry, hands wide: if Wukong can become a bride, he can make rice look like maggots. The monk, still on his knees, turns from the frogs to look at Wukong, and Wujing watches Wukong in silence. The dominant beat is Bajie's argument landing on the monk's face. Then the monk presses his palms together, shuts his eyes and says "Gold, hold fast," and Wukong, stepping toward him, freezes with both hands flying up to the gold band. The reader turns because the words have been said and the pain is coming.

## Page 7 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
All four lines read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order: Bajie's "bride" line, Bajie's "rice look like maggots" line, the monk's "Gold, hold fast.", Wukong's "Master, no!", each owned by its speaker's mouth by tail and placement, with no extra text. A string that does not read is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue, and prop counts; the words and their order are what must match. A line owned by placement (directly under its speaker's previous balloon, or naming its addressee) is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — Bajie's argument does not land
One panel owns the page: Bajie in full cry, hands wide, and the monk turning to look at Wukong. If the eye cannot find the monk's face changing, the reader misses why he says the words; that is blocking.

### C3 — The words are misattributed
"Gold, hold fast." hangs from the monk's mouth, palms together, eyes shut; "Master, no!" hangs from Wukong's and reads after it. Both Bajie lines hang from Bajie. A balloon tailed to the wrong mouth, or Wukong's cry readable first, is blocking.

### C4 — Band on, and Wukong's reaction
The thin gold band on Wukong's brow in every panel he is in (band on); missing is blocking. In the last panel both his hands fly to the band; if the reader cannot tell the words hit him, that is blocking. Exact hand pose is not.

### C5 — Wujing and identity
A skull or necklace on Wujing is blocking. Bajie pig-headed in black, the monk shaven in saffron and red, Wujing gaunt slate blue-gray with a red beard; a swapped or merged identity is blocking.

### C6 — No blood, no ghost
Any blood, or any skull-mist or ghost on this page, is blocking.
