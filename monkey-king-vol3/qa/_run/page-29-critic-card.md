## Page intent

## PAGE 29

Dramatic. The Yellow Robe crouches grinning in front of the monk at the pillar, a cup of water in his hand, and makes his bet aloud: a monk who will not hurt anyone, given claws and teeth; everyone is a beast underneath, and when the monk eats meat, the demon will eat him. Then he sprays a fine silver mist from his mouth over the monk, and the monk changes: swelling, black stripes rising through the saffron robe, the face becoming a tiger's with the monk's own dark eyes, the red kasaya tearing at the shoulder, the ropes falling slack, while Wujing strains away at the pillar. The dominant turn: the demon's bet, the spell of water, the monk becomes a tiger. The transformation owns the page; the bet sets it up. The reader turns because the monk is a tiger.
