## Page intent

## PAGE 22

Illustrated prose. The days at home, and the evenings. At sunset Wukong stands alone on the peak under the rag of faded pink banner, the flower crown slipping over the gold band, one flat hand shading his eyes, looking west over the hills, while below him the monkeys sleep in the peach trees. The prose, set in the open western sky and never over his face, tells the reader he was happy, said so, ate and swam and lounged, and that nobody told him to hurry or wait or said three words to him — and that every evening he climbed up here to look west and called it the weather. The dominant turn: happy, he said; every evening, west. The page is quiet and lonely and lets the reader see what he will not say. The reader turns because, far to the west, the monk has nobody at the front.

blocking only when it materially harms the reader event, exact text or

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 22 — card

### C1 — Transcription
All three prose paragraphs read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once each, in script order, in one prose field, with no extra text anywhere. A paragraph that does not read, is missing, doubled, or reordered is blocking; a paragraph that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word; the words and their order are what must match. The number of peaches or sleeping monkeys, if any, is never counted. The run length of a repeated letter in any sound cue is nonblocking (none is scripted), and the count of background props or crowd figures is never a transcription matter.

### C2 — Alone, looking west
Illustrated-prose mode; one image owns the page: Wukong alone on the peak at sunset, one hand shading his eyes, looking west. Blocking if he is not plainly looking into the distance, if he reads as cheerful company rather than alone, or if the prose field covers his face or hand.

### C3 — Wukong and the band
Wukong is recognizable: golden monkey, hemp robe with the shoulder patch, flower crown slipping. The thin gold band on Wukong's brow in every panel he is in is blocking: band on, showing under the crown. A missing band or Wukong drawn twice is blocking.

### C4 — Place and integrity
Sunset light over the western hills; the faded pink banner rag above him (its exact shape nonblocking; a bright red flag or writing on it is blocking). Sleeping monkeys below are brown, bare, young, no clothing or gold. No absent companion appears; no blood.
