## Page intent

## PAGE 11

Spectacle page. The turn: the words, over and over, and Wukong rolling in the dust. One image carries the page. Wukong tumbles over and over in the white dust, both hands clamped on the gold band, tail thrashing, howling. Above him the monk stands with his palms pressed together and his eyes shut, saying the three words again and again, and then the last warning: strike one more person and you are not my disciple. Behind the horse, Bajie and Wujing stand back; Wujing looks away. At the road's edge the old woman lies still, face-down, her cane beside her. The monk's voice is the only text. The reader turns because this warning is the last one.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 11 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
Both lines read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order: "Gold, hold fast." three times, then "If you strike one more person…", both owned by the monk's mouth by tail and placement, with no extra text (no lettered howl). A string that does not read is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue, and prop counts; the words and their order are what must match. A line owned by placement (directly under its speaker's previous balloon) is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — The spectacle does not land
One image carries the page: Wukong rolling in the dust clutching his head while the monk stands over him, eyes shut, saying the words. If the eye cannot find both and what is happening between them, that is blocking.

### C3 — Band on
The thin gold band on Wukong's brow in every panel he is in (band on), showing under or between his fingers; a missing band is blocking.

### C4 — The monk's balloons belong to someone else
Both balloons must hang from the monk; a balloon tailed to Wukong, Bajie, Wujing or the horse is blocking.

### C5 — Identity and continuity
A skull or necklace on Wujing is blocking. The old woman lies face-down and still at the road's edge with her cane; her absence loses the reason for the punishment and is blocking; her exact place is not. Any blood is blocking.
