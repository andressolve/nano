## Page intent

## PAGE 40

Spectacle. One image owns the whole page: two clouds racing west over the sea toward the setting sun. In front, Wukong's bright cloud, Wukong crouched forward, the red staff in his fist, the gold band on his brow, the flower crown gone; behind him, Bajie's small lumpy gray cloud sagging and struggling to keep up, Bajie calling for him to wait. The page is speed, sky and a joke about friendship. The reader turns because they are flying back to the pagoda.

## Materiality threshold

The critic is a release gate, not a defect collector. A visible issue is
blocking only when it materially harms the reader event, exact text or
attribution, named or focal identity, consequential continuity or object state,
focal generation integrity, or the dominant dramatic relationship. Every
correction replaces the complete page. Minor background, cosmetic, tiny-prop,
geometry, colour, texture, and prompt-fidelity observations are nonblocking.
Omit them. Numeric panel shares and lettering sizes are builder construction
guidance, never critic measurements.

Each numbered criterion is also its stable retry signature. Cite it only when
the reader harm and redraw justification are concrete.

A lettered speaker name, label, or tag anywhere on the page is extra text and
blocks under the transcription criterion, whatever else reads correctly:
finished pages carry dialogue, captions, prose, and sound cues only.
A sound cue whose letters and order match the script reads correctly; the run
length of a repeated letter in it is nonblocking and never counted.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 40 — card

### C1 — Transcription
The caption and Bajie's line read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once each, in order, the caption tail-free and the line owned by Bajie by tail and placement, with no extra text. A string that does not read, is missing, doubled, reworded or hangs from Wukong is blocking; a string that reads is never measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly or straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue, and the number of clouds, waves or background props. The words and their order are what must match. No object carries scripted words.

### C2 — Two clouds racing west
Spectacle: one image owns the page, the two of them flying over the sea toward a low sun, Wukong ahead and Bajie behind. Blocking if the page splits into panels, or if the order of the two is reversed.

### C3 — The two clouds read differently
Wukong's cloud is bright and fast; Bajie's is small, lumpy, gray and sagging. Blocking if the two clouds look alike, because the joke and the waiting depend on it.

### C4 — Band on, crown gone
The thin gold band on Wukong's brow. A missing band is blocking. A flower crown back on his head is blocking because it fell on the page before. The red staff in his fist must read as a straight red staff; its exact fittings are nonblocking.

### C5 — Identities and focal integrity
Wukong reads as the small golden monkey in the hemp robe with the tail; Bajie as the pig, face and mouth readable. Exactly two figures; a third figure is blocking. The hand on the staff must read as one hand gripping; small background anatomy is nonblocking.
