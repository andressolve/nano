## Page intent

## PAGE 30

Illustrated prose. The monk is a tiger now, shut in an iron cage in the black hall, and the page belongs to his refusal. One iron lamp shows a thin tiger in a torn red kasaya sitting upright with his front paws together, his face turned to the wall, an untouched joint of meat on the cage floor. Wujing, tied to the pillar in the dark, watches. The prose tells the reader what the picture holds: a monk who never ate meat now has a tiger's hunger, and he will not eat. The dominant turn is the tiger turning his face from the meat while the shutter is pulled tight against the sky. The reader turns because someone outside has been watching. Wukong is far away and absent. The demon's bed, the lamps and the stonework stay subordinate; the quiet tiger is everything.

### C1 — Prose transcription fails
Any of the four scripted prose paragraphs is missing, altered, duplicated, reordered, or does not read from the 600 × 900 proof, or any other text appears on the page; a paragraph that reads is not to be measured. ### C2 — The refusal does not land, or the mode is wrong
The eye does not find the caged tiger sitting with his face turned from an untouched joint of meat as the page's one image, or the page does not read as illustrated prose: one dominant illustration and a prose field that sits over plain wall, not over the tiger, the meat or a face.

### C3 — The tiger is not the monk
The tiger lacks the torn red kasaya with its gold grid at the throat, or has yellow tiger eyes rather than dark almond ones, or snarls, eats, or wears a human face, so the reader cannot see the monk inside him.

### C4 — Cage, meat or shutter in the wrong state
The cage is open, the meat is eaten, torn or missing, or the high window is open with sky or moonlight falling into the hall rather than shuttered. The exact number of lamps, bars or stones is nonblocking.

### C5 — Wrong figures or generation failure
Wukong, the Yellow Robe, the horse or the dragon appears; a skull or necklace on Wujing; blood; a second tiger; or gross focal anatomy on the tiger. Wujing small and dim in shadow is correct; minor background texture is nonblocking.
