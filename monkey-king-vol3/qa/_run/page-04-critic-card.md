## Page intent

## PAGE 4

Dramatic. Up on the crag, the White Bone Lady draws a pretty girl's face down over her skull like a mask; a peach-pink jacket, a black plait and a lidded basket grow onto her. On the road the girl walks up smiling and offers the hungry monk rice and noodles; he rises, palms together, glad there is no meat, because he has never let anything die for his supper. The girl and the monk own the page; the offer is the turn. Then the girl lifts the lid, two tiny green pinpoints in her eyes for the reader alone, while Bajie leans over the monk's shoulder and accepts for him. Nobody on the road suspects anything. High behind them, a small streak of cloud comes fast from the south. The reader turns because the monk is about to eat from the demon's basket.

## Exact owner-controlled script

## PAGE 4 · *dramatic*

**Location:** White Tiger Ridge.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Up on the crag: the White Bone Lady drawing a pretty
girl's face down over her skull like a mask, a peach-pink jacket and a black
plait growing round her bones, a lidded bamboo basket appearing on her arm.

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** On the road, the girl walking up, smiling,
holding out the basket. Sanzang on the left on his rock, rising, palms
together, polite. Bajie behind him, nose twitching at the basket.

> **GIRL:** "Holy master, you look so hungry! I'm taking rice and fried noodles to my father in the fields. Please, eat first."
>
> **SANZANG:** "Rice and noodles, and no meat? Then thank you. I've never let anything die for my supper."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** The girl on the left lifting the basket's lid, smiling;
in her eyes, two tiny green pinpoints. Bajie on the right, leaning in over the
monk's shoulder, drooling. Behind them, high in the sky, a small streak of
cloud coming fast from the south.

> **BAJIE:** "Master, it's rude to say no to a lady. I'll say yes for you."

**Why you turn the page:** the monk is about to eat from the demon's basket, and the sky behind her has a cloud in it.

## Materiality threshold

The critic is a release gate, not a defect collector. A visible issue is
blocking only when it materially harms the reader event, exact text or
attribution, named or focal identity, consequential continuity or object state,
focal generation integrity, or the dominant dramatic relationship. Every
correction replaces the complete page. Minor background, cosmetic, tiny-prop,
geometry, colour, texture, and prompt-fidelity observations are nonblocking.
Omit them. Numeric panel shares and lettering sizes are builder construction
guidance, never critic measurements.

Each numbered criterion is also its stable retry signature. Cite it only when
the reader harm and redraw justification are concrete.

A lettered speaker name, label, or tag anywhere on the page is extra text and
blocks under the transcription criterion, whatever else reads correctly:
finished pages carry dialogue, captions, prose, and sound cues only.
A sound cue whose letters and order match the script reads correctly; the run
length of a repeated letter in it is nonblocking and never counted.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 4 — card

### C1 — Dialogue transcription or ownership fails
All three strings must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order, each hanging from its own speaker's mouth by tail and placement, with no extra text; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word. A line naming its addressee is owned even if its tail wanders. The amount of rice or noodles drawn is never a count matter.

### C2 — Attribution
Panel two: the girl offers first, the monk replies lower, each tail to the right mouth. Panel three: the pig's line hangs from the pig's snout, never from the monk under him or the girl. The girl in panel one and panel three, and the pig in panel two, are silent; a balloon on any of them is blocking.

### C3 — The turn does not land
The offer owns the page: the girl holding out the basket to the polite monk is the image the eye finds. Panel one must read as the bone lady putting on the girl's face, so the reader knows the girl is the demon.

### C4 — Basket and tell
The basket is closed, then open on rice and noodles; grubs, frogs, or meat in it is blocking, because the monk would be accepting what he can see is wrong. The tiny green pinpoints in the girl's eyes in panel three are the reader's tell; a full skull showing through her face is blocking.

### C5 — Identity and separation
The girl reads as a young village girl, black plait, peach-pink jacket; never in saffron, never with gold. The monk shaven in saffron and red; the pig huge and black-clad. If the girl and the monk blur by face or colour it is blocking.

### C6 — Generation integrity
Blood or flesh in the mask image; Wukong drawn readable on the cloud; a duplicated named figure; or malformed speaking faces or the hand holding the basket is blocking. Background texture is not.
