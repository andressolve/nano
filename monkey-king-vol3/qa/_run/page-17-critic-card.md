## Page intent

## PAGE 17

Dramatic. Wukong gets up out of the dust holding his head. The monk stands very straight, his face wet, and names the three kind people and the three raised staffs. The dominant turn is the monk alone, close, saying the hardest thing he has ever said: go back to your mountain; you are not my disciple anymore. Then Wukong, small, answers that the monk would be dead three times without him and asks him to take the band off; the monk, half turned away, says he knows no words for that. The reader turns because Wukong has been sent away and the band is staying on. The monk's grief must be real, not cold; the bones behind them stay small and quiet.
