## Page intent

## PAGE 15

Spectacle. One image, seen as Wukong sees it through the flare of his red-ringed eyes. The red staff comes down, the old man's shape bursts apart, and in the middle of the burst, for Wukong and the reader only, the White Bone Lady's skull face shrieks, caught, her mist with nowhere to go; white bones fall onto the road and prayer beads fly. Wukong hangs in mid-air above it, both hands on the staff. At the side the monk has buried his face in the white horse's neck with his eyes shut: he sees none of it. The dominant turn is the third blow, faster than the mist, leaving a heap of white bones. The page is triumph and dread at once; nothing is gory, and the monk's blindness is as important as the blow.
