# Exact script — pages 31–40

Open only after the blind sequence read.

## PAGE 31 · *spectacle*

**Location:** the pine wood outside the golden pagoda.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Night. The white horse tied to a pine, looking at the
dark pagoda, its gold roofs dull under the moon.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left)*
> "Tied to his pine, the horse had seen the pig run and the ogre dragged inside."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** The white horse uncoiling into a slim white
dragon, sea-green eyes, pale-blue mane, the empty bridle falling away.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left)*
> "Before he was the monk's horse, he was a dragon."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** The white dragon tearing the shutter off the pagoda's
high window and sliding in, moonlight pouring in behind him.

**Why you turn the page:** the dragon has gone in after the monk.

---

## PAGE 32 · *spectacle*

**Location:** inside the golden pagoda.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 100%.** The dark hall. The white dragon coiled round
the iron cage, wrenching at the bars, the tiger inside looking up. The Yellow
Robe risen from his bed, the silver scimitar flashing, catching the dragon
across the right foreleg. Wujing at the pillar, straining at his ropes.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left)*
> "He tried to tear the cage open."
>
> **CAPTION** *(lower right)*
> "The Yellow Robe woke, and his blade drove the dragon out through the high window and down into the stream."

**Why you turn the page:** the dragon is hurt, and the cage is still shut.

## PAGE 33 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the stream by the pine wood.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Dawn. Bajie crawling out of the reeds on his belly,
muddy, peering round.

> **BAJIE:** "Is he gone? Is anybody still alive? Hello?"

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** The white horse lying in the shallow stream,
a clean dark gash on its right foreleg, its bridle broken. Bajie on the left,
splashing down on his knees beside it.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left)*
> "By morning he was a horse again. A hurt dragon can't hold his shape."

> **BAJIE:** "Horse! What happened to your leg? Did the demon do this to you?"

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Close: the horse lifting its head toward Bajie, sea-green
eyes steady, mouth barely open.

> **HORSE:** "Bajie. Listen to me."

**Why you turn the page:** the horse has spoken.

## PAGE 34 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the stream by the pine wood.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Bajie on the left, falling over backward into the
water. The horse on the right, head up.

> **BAJIE:** "You can TALK?"
>
> **HORSE:** "Only when it matters. The master is a tiger in a cage, and Wujing is tied up."
>
> **HORSE:** "I couldn't beat that demon. Neither can you."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** The horse on the right and Bajie on the left,
close, eye to eye.

> **HORSE:** "Go to Flower-Fruit Mountain and bring back our big brother."
>
> **BAJIE:** "He won't come. I told Master the bones were a trick, and I knew it was a lie."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** The horse on the right, looking at him steadily. Bajie on
the left, ears down.

> **HORSE:** "Then tell Wukong that. Tell him the truth, and he'll come for the master."

**Why you turn the page:** the pig has to go back for the monkey he sent away.

# MOVEMENT V — GOING BACK · pp 35–40

## PAGE 35 · *illustrated prose*

**Location:** the sky over the eastern sea.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 70%.** Bajie on a small lumpy gray cloud that sags in
the middle, over a wide sea, clutching his rake, eyes squeezed shut, ears
flapping. Far ahead, a green peak on the coast.

> **PROSE FIELD 1** *(in the open sky above the cloud)*
> "Bajie could fly, a little, badly. He had not tried since he was thrown out of Heaven for being drunk at a party, and his cloud sagged in the middle like an old mattress.
>
> He flew east all day, over the hills and then over the sea, with his eyes shut. He had never in his life gone back for anybody. He had never told the truth when a lie would do.
>
> He practised the truth all the way there. It sounded terrible every time."

**Why you turn the page:** he has reached the monkey's mountain.

---

## PAGE 36 · *dramatic*

**Location:** Flower-Fruit Mountain, the Water Curtain Cave.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Bajie landing on the peak and a swarm of young brown
monkeys leaping onto him from every side, grabbing his ears and his rake.

> **BAJIE:** "Ow! Let go of my ears! I've come to see the Monkey King!"

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** Inside the cave behind the waterfall: monkeys
dragging Bajie before the stone throne. Wukong on the right, lounging on the
throne, the flower crown on top of the gold band, one foot up, eating a peach,
not looking at him.

> **WUKONG:** "Who's this? I don't know any pigs. Throw him in the waterfall."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Bajie on the left, flat on the floor with monkeys sitting
on him. Wukong on the right, still not looking.

> **BAJIE:** "Big brother! It's me, Bajie! You know me!"
>
> **WUKONG:** "I knew a Bajie once. He talked a monk into sending me away."

**Why you turn the page:** Wukong knows exactly who he is.

---

## PAGE 37 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the Water Curtain Cave.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Bajie on the left, up on his knees, putting on a huge
smile, hands spread. Wukong on the right on the throne.

> **BAJIE:** "That's all forgotten! Master misses you terribly. He cries every night. He sent me to bring you home."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** Wukong on the right, leaning forward on the
throne, close, grinning without any fun in it, looking at Bajie on the left.

> **WUKONG:** "Bajie, I saw through three faces of a demon. I don't need magic eyes to see through you."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Bajie on the left, the smile sliding off his face. Wukong
on the right, sitting back.

> **WUKONG:** "Try again, Bajie. The true one this time."

**Why you turn the page:** the pig has to tell the truth.

## PAGE 38 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the Water Curtain Cave.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Bajie on the left, sitting back on his heels, ears down,
not funny anymore. Wukong on the right, listening.

> **BAJIE:** "I lied about the bones. I knew it was her. Her name was right there on the skull."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** Close on Bajie, his small eyes wet.

> **BAJIE:** "You were always the fastest, and the bossiest, and his favourite."
>
> **BAJIE:** "I wanted to be the one he liked best. So I lied, and he sent you away."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Bajie on the left, looking up. Wukong on the right, very
still, the peach forgotten in his hand.

> **BAJIE:** "Now a demon in a yellow robe, big as a door, has turned him into a tiger, and Wujing is tied up."
>
> **BAJIE:** "The horse got cut, and then the horse talked. He sent me for you."

**Why you turn the page:** Wukong has heard the truth, and has to decide.

---

## PAGE 39 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the Water Curtain Cave.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Wukong on the right, standing up off the throne, the
flower crown slipping. Bajie on the left.

> **WUKONG:** "He sent me away, Bajie. He said the words three times in one day. Why should I go back for him?"

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** Bajie on the left, on his feet, shouting,
honest. Wukong on the right.

> **BAJIE:** "I don't know! I hate flying, and I hate you bossing me, and I came all the way back for you anyway!"

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Close on Wukong, his hand on the mended patch at the
shoulder of his hemp robe, the flower crown falling away behind him, the gold
band bright on his brow.

> **WUKONG:** "A pig came all the way back for a monkey. On a cloud like yours."
>
> **WUKONG:** "Fine. Where is this pagoda, Bajie?"
>
> **BAJIE:** "I'll show you. You'll have to go slow."

**Why you turn the page:** he is going back.

## PAGE 40 · *spectacle*

**Location:** the sky over the eastern sea.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 100%.** Two clouds racing west over the sea toward the
setting sun: Wukong's bright cloud in front, Wukong crouched forward, the red
staff in his fist, the gold band on his brow; behind him, Bajie's lumpy gray
cloud struggling to keep up.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left)*
> "One somersault would have been faster, but only the pig knew the way. He waited for the pig all night and all the next day."
>
> **BAJIE:** "Big brother! Wait for me!"

**Why you turn the page:** they are flying back to the pagoda.
