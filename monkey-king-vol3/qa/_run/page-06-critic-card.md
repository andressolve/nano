## Page intent

## PAGE 6

Dramatic page. The turn: the demon's mist escapes, the monk cries "you killed her!", and Wukong's answer, "look in her basket," turns up maggots and frogs. The page is built on two ways of seeing the same road. First, through Wukong's flaring eyes, the girl is an empty skin and a thin white mist with a skull face in it streams away toward the crag. Then the monk's view owns the page: a girl lying still, face-down, nothing more, and the monk on his knees beside her, shaking, accusing. Wukong points his staff at the basket. Last, the basket itself, tipped over and crawling, and Bajie jumping back. The reader knows Wukong is right; the monk has not looked at the basket, only at the girl. No blood anywhere. The frogs are gross and funny, never horror.

## Exact owner-controlled script

## PAGE 6 · *dramatic*

**Location:** White Tiger Ridge.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** Seen as Wukong sees it, inside the flare of his
red-ringed eyes: the girl lying face-down on the road, flat and hollow like a
skin a cicada leaves behind, and out of it a thin white mist with a skull
face in it, streaming away up toward the crag. Wukong on the right, staff up,
glaring after it.

> **WUKONG:** "She's slipping out! Come back here, you bag of bones!"

**Panel 2 — dominant, 45%.** As the monk sees it: the girl lying still,
face-down, a body, nothing more. Sanzang on the left, on his knees near her,
hands shaking, not daring to touch her. Wukong on the right, pointing the
staff at the basket in the dust.

> **SANZANG:** "You killed her! She brought us food, and you killed her without a word!"
>
> **WUKONG:** "That's not a girl, Master, that's an empty skin. She was a demon. Look in her basket!"

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Close: the basket on its side, the lid off: white maggots
wriggling where the rice was, small green frogs hopping out of the noodles.
Bajie on the right, leaping back.

> **BAJIE:** "Frogs! The noodles are frogs!"

**Why you turn the page:** the basket is full of maggots, and the monk is still looking at the girl.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 6 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
All four lines read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order: Wukong's "slipping out", the monk's "You killed her!", Wukong's "Look in her basket!", Bajie's "Frogs!", each owned by its speaker's mouth by tail and placement, with no extra text. A string that does not read is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue, and the number of frogs or peaches; the words and their order are what must match. A line owned by placement (directly under its speaker's previous balloon, or naming its addressee) is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — The monk's view does not own the page
One panel plainly owns the page: the monk on his knees beside a girl lying still, accusing Wukong, who points at the basket. If the eye lands first on the mist or the basket instead, the reader misses that the monk is looking at the girl, not the basket; that is blocking.

### C3 — The seeing rule breaks
The hollow skin and the skull-mist appear only in the panel framed as Wukong's view (tinted by his sight, or faint and translucent). In the monk's panel the girl is a still body face-down, no mist, no ghost, no face shown. A mist or skull in the monk's view, or the monk reacting to one, is blocking.

### C4 — The exchange is misattributed
"You killed her!" hangs from the monk's mouth; "That's not a girl, Master…" hangs from Wukong's and reads after it. Each must hang from its own speaker by tail, not merely make sense as his; a reply readable first or tailed to the wrong mouth is blocking.

### C5 — Band on, no blood, the basket
The thin gold band on Wukong's brow in every panel he is in (band on); a missing band is blocking. Any blood or wound anywhere is blocking. The basket lies open with maggots and green frogs, gross and comic; a closed basket or rice still in it is blocking.

### C6 — Identity
Wukong a small golden monkey in the hemp robe; the monk shaven, saffron and red; Bajie a pig-headed man in black. A swapped or merged identity is blocking; hand and background finish are not.
