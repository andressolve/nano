## Page intent

## PAGE 28

Dramatic. In the black hall, Wujing, bruised and bound, is tied to the same black pillar as the monk, and he says the worst thing about himself first: he was told to walk at the monk's back, he went looking for the pig, he did not keep him safe. The monk turns his face to him and, for the first time, says what he did: he sent away the one who could have kept him; he was sure the girl was a girl, he was told three times, and three times he said the words. Then silence: the monk staring at nothing with wet eyes, Wujing watching him. The dominant turn: the monk answers "I sent away the one who could have kept me". The close on the monk's face owns the page. The reader turns because the monk knows what he did, and the demon is coming back.
