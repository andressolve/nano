## Page 24 — card

### C1 — Transcription
The single caption reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, tail-free, with no other text anywhere. A caption that does not read, is doubled, or has extra lettering beside it is blocking; a caption that reads is not to be measured. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing, and a regional spelling variant are nonblocking; the words and their order must match. The run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue is nonblocking. Prop counts (silver stars, lamps, staff rings, pagoda storeys, ropes, furs, trees) are nonblocking unless a later criterion on this card names a count.

### C2 — The open eye lands
One full-page spectacle image: a huge demon asleep on a fur-heaped stone bed in a black hall, the monk tiny in the doorway backing away, and one pale silver eye open and plainly looking at him. If the open eye does not read, or reads as both eyes wide awake in a fight-ready pose, the turn ("the demon is awake") is lost: blocking. The scale gap between demon and monk must carry by eye; judge by what the eye finds.

### C3 — Identity and the two yellows
The demon reads as the Yellow Robe: indigo square face, tusks, black topknot, mustard-ochre robe with silver stars over black armour, scimitar across his knees. The monk is shaven, in saffron under a red kasaya. If the two read as the same figure or the monk wears the demon's colours, blocking. **Any monkey on the page is blocking.**

### C4 — Door and staff
The black-iron front door is open with the monk in it, and his ringed staff is falling onto or lying on the threshold. A staff still firmly in his hand or the door shut is blocking, because the next pages depend on the staff left at the door.

### C5 — Integrity
No blood. Monk's face and hands coherent at small scale; demon's face and tusks clean.
