# Critic cards

Reader-facing blocking criteria only. Three to eight per page. Criterion numbers
are stable defect signatures for retry routing. No prompt, audit, reference
manifest, version, history, panel-share rule, or type measurement ever appears
here. A finding is valid only when it states material reader harm and justifies
risking a complete-page redraw.

Format — header exactly `## Page N — card`, criteria `### Cn — title`:

## Page 1 — card

### C1 — Prose transcription fails
All three prose paragraphs must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order, in one prose field, with no extra text on the page; a paragraph or sentence that does not read, is altered, dropped, doubled, or reordered is blocking, and a paragraph that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes or apostrophes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word; the words and their order are what must match. The number of bundles on the pole or rings on the monk's staff is never a transcription or count matter.

### C2 — The mode or the opening does not land
The page must read as one illustrated-prose page: the prose field owns the reading, over one picture of the company on an empty white ridge, the monk riding, the pig and the ogre sharing the pole, the monkey far ahead looking west. A balloon, a caption, or a sound anywhere is blocking. Exact field placement is nonblocking if the field never covers a figure.

### C3 — Band on
The thin gold band on Wukong's brow in every panel he is in. A Wukong with a bare brow, a crown, or a jeweled or wide band is blocking; at this distance a thin gold line at the brow is enough, and its finish is nonblocking.

### C4 — Identity and separation
Wukong reads as the small golden monkey in the hemp robe; the monk as the shaven young man in saffron and red on a slim white horse with a pale-blue mane; the pig and the ogre as clearly two different creatures. A skull, necklace, or neck cord on Wujing is blocking (his sash of river reeds at the waist is correct). Exact saddle hardware is nonblocking.

### C5 — Generation integrity
A duplicated named figure, a second monkey, a demon or extra person, the monk's wooden ringed staff drawn as a red-and-gold staff, or gross anatomy on a focal figure is blocking. Background rock and pine texture are not.

## Page 2 — card

### C2 — The turn does not land
One spectacle image owns the page: the bone lady large on her crag, the travellers tiny and far below, so the reader feels she is watching them and wants the monk. If the travellers are not findable on the road, or she does not dominate, it is blocking.

### C3 — The lady reads wrong
She must read as a lady of bone: skull face with green pinpoints, long loose black hair, gray-violet burial silk. If she reads as a pale lady in white (white robes, a hood or veil, a cloud, a vase, gold), the reader confuses her with the lady in white; blocking.

### C4 — Band on
The thin gold band on Wukong's brow in every panel he is in. At this distance a gold glint at the small monkey's brow is enough; a clearly bare brow where his head is legible is blocking, and the band's width and finish are nonblocking.

### C5 — Generation integrity
Blood, gore, or bones strewn about; a second lady; a disguise or mist; or her balloon's tail running into her finger or into the travellers below is blocking. Rock texture and silk detail are not.
