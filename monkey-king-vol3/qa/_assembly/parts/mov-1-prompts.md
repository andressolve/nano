## PAGE 1 — The road over the ridge

### Reader turn
On an empty white ridge the reader meets the company walking west, and learns the two rules the book turns on: the gold band and its three words, and the monkey's eyes that see the real face under any face.

### Ordered moments
1. One illustrated-prose page; the picture is the dominant image, about 70% of the page. A narrow pale road along the spine of a high ridge of chalk-white rock and bleached grass, a few dead pines, hazy blue valleys falling away on both sides, hard midday light. Middle ground, LEFT of centre: Sanzang riding the white horse, ringed staff upright, straw hat on his back. Behind him, lower left, Bajie and Wujing carrying one luggage pole between them; Bajie at the front, mouth open, complaining; Wujing at the back, silent. Far ahead on the RIGHT, up on a white boulder, Wukong small but clear, one foot up, a flat hand shading his eyes, looking west, the gold band on his brow. No balloons anywhere.
2. The prose field: one matte pale parchment field across the open sky in the upper page, above the ridge, left-aligned, three paragraphs with clear space between; never over a figure, the horse, or the boulder.

### Exact strings
PROSE FIELD 1: `Months after the river of sand, they were still walking west. The monk Sanzang rode a white horse that had once been a dragon. Bajie the pig and Wujing the river ogre carried the luggage, and Bajie complained about it.`
PROSE FIELD 1: `Wukong ran ahead. He could cross the world in one somersault, but he was never allowed to fly the monk: the road was the monk's to walk. On Wukong's head was a gold band that squeezed like a fist whenever the monk said three words: Gold, hold fast. The monk had promised to say them only if he must.`
PROSE FIELD 1: `Long ago, in a furnace in Heaven, smoke had got into Wukong's eyes and stayed there. No disguise could fool them now. He saw the real face under any face. His iron staff he kept as a needle behind his ear.`

Render exactly these 3 strings once each, in this order, as three paragraphs of one prose field; no other text anywhere on the page.

### Character locks
Wukong, from the attached sheet (banded pilgrim, pp 1–19): small wiry monkey, heart-shaped face, gold eyes with a thin red ring, small upright cupped ears, swept-back golden ruff; hemp robe with the mended left shoulder over the tiger-skin kilt; the thin plain gold band on his brow; needle behind his right ear; long tail. Sanzang, from the attached sheet: slender, upright, long oval beardless face, fully shaven head, saffron robe under the red kasaya with gold grid. Wujing, from the attached sheet: gaunt hunched slate blue-gray ogre, wild red hair and beard, sand-brown sleeveless tunic, reed sash, crescent spade. Bajie, not attached, drawn from words: big round pig-headed man, huge floppy ears, black tunic open over the belly, blue sash, rake on the pole. The horse, not attached, drawn from words: slim white, pale-blue mane and tail, sea-green eyes, dark-red saddle, legs sound. Bajie vs Wujing: round pink-gray pig in black vs gaunt blue-gray ogre with red beard.

### Consequential exclusions
No skull, necklace, or cord on Wujing. Staff away. No flower crown; the sheet's fourth view is not drawn. No demon, no other person or monkey. The monk's staff is wooden with a pewter ring-loop, never red and gold. No balloons, captions, sound, or labels.

## PAGE 2 — The lady on the crag

### Reader turn
The White Bone Lady watches the tiny travellers from her crag and wants the monk, because one bite of him means she will never die.

### Ordered moments
1. One full-page spectacle image, the whole page. Foreground, large, on the LEFT and filling the upper two-thirds: at the black mouth of a cave at the top of a tooth of white rock, the White Bone Lady in her true shape, three-quarter view turned toward the drop, her skull face clearly visible, one long bone finger drawn slowly across her lower teeth from her left side, her burial silks trailing like smoke off the rock. Lower RIGHT, far below and very small, the pale road along the ridge: the monk on the white horse, the pig and the ogre with the luggage pole between them, and the monkey running ahead, all tiny and unaware. Hazy blue distance. The caption sits upper left in the dark of the cave-mouth or the sky, tail-free, never over her face or hand. Her balloon sits upper right in open sky on the side of her face away from the finger, tail ending beside her teeth, its path clear of the hand.

### Exact strings
CAPTION: `On the ridge, something had been waiting for the monk.`
WHITE BONE LADY: `They say one bite of that monk and you live forever.`

Render exactly these 2 strings once each, in this order, with clear ownership; no other text anywhere on the page.

### Character locks
The White Bone Lady, from the attached sheet (true shape): tall gaunt lady of bone, a smooth white skull face with a lady's high cheekbones and long jaw, deep eye-hollows with two pinpoints of cold green light, no lips; long straight black hair loose to the waist; long white bone hands with needle-thin fingers; tattered ash-gray and faded violet burial silks. Never Guanyin: no white robes, no hood or veil, no cloud, no willow, no vase, no gold, no red dot. The tiny travellers, drawn from words: the monk in saffron and red on the white horse; the black-clad pig and the gaunt blue-gray red-bearded ogre at the two ends of one pole; the small golden monkey in a pale hemp robe, a glint of gold band at his brow.

### Consequential exclusions
No mist, no disguise, no girl, no basket; the sheet's fourth view is not drawn. The cave holds no other figure. No blood, no bones lying about, no gore. The travellers carry no balloons. No other text.

## PAGE 3 — I can fly peaches

### Reader turn
The hungry monk is told to sit and not move while Wukong flies off for peaches, and the pig grumbles about who made the monkey the boss.

### Ordered moments
1. Panel one, about 30%. The bare ridge road. Sanzang on the LEFT sliding down off the white horse, one hand on his stomach, looking round at rock. Wukong on the RIGHT, crouched up on a dead pine stump, hand shading his eyes. Sanzang's balloon upper left, tail to his mouth; Wukong's reply right, clearly lower, tail to his mouth.
2. Panel two, the dominant panel, about 45% of the page. Wukong on the RIGHT, rising, a puff of white cloud already under his feet, pointing one finger down at the monk like a teacher, mouth open. Sanzang on the LEFT, sitting on a flat white rock, palms together. Wujing standing tall behind the monk, his head well above the monk's, mouth visible. Wukong's balloon at the very top of the panel on the right, tail to his mouth; Wujing's balloon middle left beside Wujing's mouth, clearly lower, tail to Wujing, never toward the monk.
3. Panel three, about 25%. A streak of cloud vanishing into the southern sky. Bajie on the LEFT dropping his end of the luggage pole, scowling up at the sky, mouth open. Sanzang on the RIGHT on his rock, turning to him mildly. Bajie's balloon upper left, tail to his snout; Sanzang's reply right, lower, tail to his mouth.

### Exact strings
SANZANG: `Wukong, I'm so hungry I can't see straight. Is there a village anywhere on this ridge?`
WUKONG: `Not a village, not a farm, not a goat. But there are peaches on a mountain to the south.`
WUKONG: `Sit on that rock, Master, and don't move until I'm back. I can't fly you anywhere, but I can fly peaches.`
WUJING: `Go. I'll stand at his back.`
BAJIE: `'Don't move.' 'Hurry up.' 'Hands off the horse.' Who made the monkey the boss?`
SANZANG: `Nobody, Bajie. He's just the fastest.`

Render exactly these 6 strings once each, in this order, with clear ownership; no other text anywhere on the page.

### Character locks
Wukong, from the attached sheet (banded pilgrim): small wiry monkey, heart-shaped face, gold eyes with a thin red ring, small upright cupped ears, swept golden ruff, hemp robe with the mended left shoulder over the tiger-skin kilt, needle behind the right ear, long tail; the thin plain gold band on his brow in panels one and two. Sanzang, from the attached sheet: long oval beardless face, fully shaven head, saffron robe under the red kasaya with gold grid, hat on his back, ringed wooden staff. Wujing, from the attached sheet: gaunt hunched slate blue-gray ogre, wild red hair and beard, sand-brown tunic, crescent spade; no skulls. Bajie, not attached, drawn from words: huge pig-headed man, floppy ears, black tunic open over the belly, blue sash, rake. The horse, not attached, drawn from words: slim white, pale-blue mane, sea-green eyes, dark-red saddle. Bajie vs Wujing: round pink-gray pig in black vs gaunt blue-gray red-bearded ogre in sand-brown.

### Consequential exclusions
Staff away; no peaches yet. No skull or necklace on Wujing. No demon, no girl, no other figure. Panel three shows no Wukong, only the cloud streak. No flower crown. No captions, sound, or labels.

## PAGE 4 — The girl with the basket

### Reader turn
The demon puts on a girl's face and offers the hungry monk her basket, and he accepts.

### Ordered moments
1. Panel one, about 25%. Up on the white crag at the cave-mouth: the White Bone Lady in her true shape drawing a pretty girl's face down over her skull like a pale silk mask, half on; a peach-pink jacket and a black plait forming around her bones; a lidded bamboo basket appearing on her arm. Silent; no balloon.
2. Panel two, the dominant panel, about 50% of the page. On the road: the girl on the RIGHT walking up, smiling, holding the closed basket out. Sanzang on the LEFT, rising from his rock, palms together, polite. Bajie behind the monk, nose twitching toward the basket, silent. The girl's balloon upper right, tail to her mouth; Sanzang's reply middle left, clearly lower, tail to his mouth.
3. Panel three, about 25%. The girl on the LEFT lifting the basket's lid on white rice and noodles, smiling, two tiny green pinpoints in her eyes. Sanzang seated in the middle. Bajie on the RIGHT, leaning in high over the monk's shoulder, drooling, mouth open. Bajie's balloon upper right beside his snout, tail to his mouth. High in the sky behind, a small streak of cloud coming fast from the south.

### Exact strings
GIRL: `Holy master, you look so hungry! I'm taking rice and fried noodles to my father in the fields. Please, eat first.`
SANZANG: `Rice and noodles, and no meat? Then thank you. I've never let anything die for my supper.`
BAJIE: `Master, it's rude to say no to a lady. I'll say yes for you.`

Render exactly these 3 strings once each, in this order, with clear ownership; no other text anywhere on the page.

### Character locks
The girl, from the attached sheet: about eighteen, round soft face, rosy cheeks, black hair in a single long plait over one shoulder with a red string, peach-pink cotton jacket, dark blue trousers, round lidded bamboo basket, a great deal of smile. The White Bone Lady (panel one), drawn from words: skull face with green pinpoints, long loose black hair, ash-gray and violet burial silk. Sanzang, from the attached sheet: long oval beardless face, fully shaven head, saffron robe under red kasaya with gold grid, hat on his back. Bajie, from the attached sheet: huge pig-headed man, floppy ears, black tunic, blue sash. The girl vs the monk: black plait and round face in peach-pink vs shaven head and long oval face in saffron and red.

### Consequential exclusions
Wukong is absent: only the cloud streak, no figure readable on it. Wujing is not in these panels. The basket holds only rice and noodles; never grubs, frogs, or meat. The girl's sheet's fourth view (a skull showing through) is never drawn here; only the tiny green pinpoints in panel three. No blood, no flesh in panel one: the mask is smooth. No captions, sound, or labels.

## PAGE 5 — The staff falls

### Reader turn
Wukong drops back out of the sky, sees the skull under the girl's smile, and swings without a word.

### Ordered moments
1. One full-page spectacle image, the whole page. Upper RIGHT, Wukong dropping out of the sky onto the road, four red-gold peaches tumbling out of his arms, the red-and-gold iron staff swinging down from over his head in both hands, the red rings of his gold eyes flaring bright. Centre right, below him, the girl, still smiling, basket on her arm. Framed as his view: a faint warm haze of his sight around her, and through her face the pale translucent ghost of a skull with green pinpoints, bone fingers faintly under her hands; her girl's face stays visible under the ghost. LEFT: Sanzang and Bajie, mouths open, too late, staring at the monkey and the falling staff; Wujing behind them, reaching. The staff has not landed. The caption sits upper left in the sky, tail-free.

### Exact strings
CAPTION: `Wukong saw the skull under the smile, and he did not stop to explain.`

Render exactly this 1 string once; no other text anywhere on the page.

### Character locks
Wukong, from the attached sheet (banded pilgrim): small wiry monkey, heart-shaped face, gold eyes with the red rings flaring (never pure red, never beams), small upright cupped ears, swept golden ruff, hemp robe with mended left shoulder over the tiger-skin kilt, long tail; the thin plain gold band on his brow. The girl, from the attached sheet: round soft face, black plait with red string, peach-pink jacket, lidded basket; the sheet's fourth view guides the ghost only. Sanzang, from the attached sheet: shaven head, long oval face, saffron and red kasaya; horrified, watching a monkey attack a girl. Bajie, not attached, drawn from words: huge pig-headed man, floppy ears, black tunic, blue sash. Wujing, not attached, drawn from words: gaunt slate blue-gray ogre, wild red hair and red beard, sand-brown tunic, crescent spade; no skulls.

### Consequential exclusions
Only Wukong's sight shows the skull; the monk and the pig look at Wukong, never pointing at or recoiling from a skull. Exactly one Wukong. No needle behind his ear: the staff is out. The staff has not struck: no impact, no wound, no blood. No skull or necklace on Wujing. The monk's ringed staff never reads as Wukong's. No balloons, sound, or labels.
