## Page 1 — card

### C1 — Prose transcription fails
All three prose paragraphs must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order, in one prose field, with no extra text on the page; a paragraph or sentence that does not read, is altered, dropped, doubled, or reordered is blocking, and a paragraph that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes or apostrophes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word; the words and their order are what must match. The number of bundles on the pole or rings on the monk's staff is never a transcription or count matter.

### C2 — The mode or the opening does not land
The page must read as one illustrated-prose page: the prose field owns the reading, over one picture of the company on an empty white ridge, the monk riding, the pig and the ogre sharing the pole, the monkey far ahead looking west. A balloon, a caption, or a sound anywhere is blocking. Exact field placement is nonblocking if the field never covers a figure.

### C3 — Band on
The thin gold band on Wukong's brow in every panel he is in. A Wukong with a bare brow, a crown, or a jeweled or wide band is blocking; at this distance a thin gold line at the brow is enough, and its finish is nonblocking.

### C4 — Identity and separation
Wukong reads as the small golden monkey in the hemp robe; the monk as the shaven young man in saffron and red on a slim white horse with a pale-blue mane; the pig and the ogre as clearly two different creatures. A skull, necklace, or cord on Wujing is blocking. Exact saddle hardware is nonblocking.

### C5 — Generation integrity
A duplicated named figure, a second monkey, a demon or extra person, the monk's wooden ringed staff drawn as a red-and-gold staff, or gross anatomy on a focal figure is blocking. Background rock and pine texture are not.

## Page 2 — card

### C1 — Transcription or ownership fails
Both strings must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order: the caption first, tail-free; then her line, owned by its tail to the skull's mouth. A string that does not read, is altered, doubled, or carries extra text is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word. The number of tiny travellers or bundles is nonblocking provided the company reads.

### C2 — The turn does not land
One spectacle image owns the page: the bone lady large on her crag, the travellers tiny and far below, so the reader feels she is watching them and wants the monk. If the travellers are not findable on the road, or she does not dominate, it is blocking.

### C3 — The lady reads wrong
She must read as a lady of bone: skull face with green pinpoints, long loose black hair, gray-violet burial silk. If she reads as a pale lady in white (white robes, a hood or veil, a cloud, a vase, gold), the reader confuses her with the lady in white; blocking.

### C4 — Band on
The thin gold band on Wukong's brow in every panel he is in. At this distance a gold glint at the small monkey's brow is enough; a clearly bare brow where his head is legible is blocking, and the band's width and finish are nonblocking.

### C5 — Generation integrity
Blood, gore, or bones strewn about; a second lady; a disguise or mist; or her balloon's tail running into her finger or into the travellers below is blocking. Rock texture and silk detail are not.

## Page 3 — card

### C1 — Dialogue transcription or ownership fails
All six strings must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order, each hanging from its own speaker's mouth by tail and placement, with no extra text; a string that does not read is blocking, and a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes (including the single quotes in the pig's line), a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word; the words and their order are what must match. A line placed directly under its speaker's previous balloon, or naming its addressee, is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — Attribution of the three exchanges
Panel one: the monk asks, Wukong answers, each tail to his own mouth, the answer lower. Panel two: Wukong's order first, then Wujing's promise hanging from Wujing's mouth, never from the seated monk's. Panel three: the pig's grumble first, the monk's reply lower, from the monk. Each reply's speaker must be in frame with mouth visible; a reply that reads before its prompt or tails to the wrong figure is blocking.

### C3 — The turn does not land
The panel of Wukong on his cloud, pointing at the seated monk, owns the page; by panel three he is gone, a streak south. Judge by what the eye finds first. If Wukong is still standing on the road in panel three, the departure fails; blocking.

### C4 — Band on
The thin gold band on Wukong's brow in every panel he is in. A bare brow, a crown, or a jeweled or wide band in any panel showing his head is blocking; its finish is nonblocking.

### C5 — Identity and separation
The monk shaven in saffron and red; the pig round and black-clad; the ogre gaunt, blue-gray, red-bearded, in sand-brown. A skull, necklace, or cord on Wujing is blocking. Pole and bundle details are nonblocking.

### C6 — Generation integrity
A duplicated named figure, a balloon on a silent figure, malformed speaking faces or the pointing hand, or the monk's ringed staff drawn as Wukong's red-and-gold staff is blocking. Background hands and texture are not.

## Page 4 — card

### C1 — Dialogue transcription or ownership fails
All three strings must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order, each hanging from its own speaker's mouth by tail and placement, with no extra text; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word. A line naming its addressee is owned even if its tail wanders. The amount of rice or noodles drawn is never a count matter.

### C2 — Attribution
Panel two: the girl offers first, the monk replies lower, each tail to the right mouth. Panel three: the pig's line hangs from the pig's snout, never from the monk under him or the girl. The girl in panel one and panel three, and the pig in panel two, are silent; a balloon on any of them is blocking.

### C3 — The turn does not land
The offer owns the page: the girl holding out the basket to the polite monk is the image the eye finds. Panel one must read as the bone lady putting on the girl's face, so the reader knows the girl is the demon.

### C4 — Basket and tell
The basket is closed, then open on rice and noodles; grubs, frogs, or meat in it is blocking, because the monk would be accepting what he can see is wrong. The tiny green pinpoints in the girl's eyes in panel three are the reader's tell; a full skull showing through her face is blocking.

### C5 — Identity and separation
The girl reads as a young village girl, black plait, peach-pink jacket; never in saffron, never with gold. The monk shaven in saffron and red; the pig huge and black-clad. If the girl and the monk blur by face or colour it is blocking.

### C6 — Generation integrity
Blood or flesh in the mask image; Wukong drawn readable on the cloud; a duplicated named figure; or malformed speaking faces or the hand holding the basket is blocking. Background texture is not.

## Page 5 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
The one caption must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, tail-free, with no other text on the page; if it does not read, or any balloon, sound, or extra text appears, it is blocking, and a caption that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word. The peaches are four; four or five passes, and their count never blocks.

### C2 — The seeing rule
The skull is shown only as Wukong's view: a faint, translucent ghost over the still-visible smiling girl, his eyes flaring. If the girl is drawn openly as a solid skull or monster for all to see, or if the monk or the pig point at or recoil from a skull, the reader loses why the monk will be horrified; blocking.

### C3 — The turn does not land
One spectacle image owns the page: Wukong dropping from the sky, staff coming down over the girl, the monk and the pig too late. If the staff has already struck, or if the strike reads as a wound or blood, it is blocking.

### C4 — Band on
The thin gold band on Wukong's brow in every panel he is in. A bare brow, a crown, or a jeweled or wide band is blocking; its finish is nonblocking.

### C5 — Identity and separation
Wukong the small golden monkey in hemp with the red-and-gold iron staff; the girl in peach-pink with her plait and basket; the monk shaven in saffron and red; the pig in black; the ogre gaunt and blue-gray with red beard. A skull, necklace, or cord on Wujing is blocking.

### C6 — Generation integrity
A second Wukong, the eyes as pure red or beams, malformed hands on the staff, or the monk's ringed staff drawn as Wukong's staff is blocking. Background anatomy and texture are not.
