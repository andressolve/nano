# 02 — CHARACTERS

Lock the VISUAL, never the mythic name (the model drifts to stock art from names). Names live in the script and ref filenames only. Prefix every recurring-character block in a page prompt with *"keep IDENTICAL in every panel."* Build ref sheets for cross-PAGE continuity even though intra-page continuity holds from prose.

Match the exemplar renderings in `refs/page_daedalus_icarus_v1.png` for Daedalus and Icarus.

---

## DAEDALUS — `refs/ref_daedalus.png`

**Lock:** A lean, weathered man in his mid-50s. Mediterranean olive skin, deeply lined face, high cheekbones. Iron-grey hair swept back, a full but neatly-kept grey beard. Sharp intelligent dark eyes, heavy grey brows. Wiry craftsman's build, strong forearms and calloused hands. Wears a terracotta-brown knee-length tunic, a leather worker's belt with small tools, a leather cord at the neck. Posture forward-leaning, watchful, deliberate. A maker's hands — always doing something.

*Realistic adult heroic proportions. NOT cute.*

**Ref-sheet prompt:** register B (Style Block + anti-drift), landscape 4:3-ish on a 1536×1024 canvas, plain warm-tan neutral background. Character reference sheet: the man above, TWO views on one canvas — a waist-up front portrait (neutral, watchful expression) on the left, a full-body standing figure on the right. Even flat lighting. NO text, NO labels, NO panel borders.

## ICARUS — `refs/ref_icarus.png`

**Lock:** A boy of about 12. Warm olive Mediterranean skin, a lithe, energetic build — a runner's body, not a child's roundness. A tumble of dark brown curly hair. Bright, eager dark eyes, an open expressive face, quick smile. Wears a simple cream/off-white short tunic tied at one shoulder, bare arms, leather sandals. Posture kinetic — always leaning into motion, arms ready to move.

*Realistic child/adolescent anatomy. NOT cute, NOT mascot, NO oversized eyes.*

**Ref-sheet prompt:** register B (Style Block + anti-drift), 1536×1024 canvas, plain warm-tan neutral background. Character reference sheet: the boy above, TWO views on one canvas — a waist-up front portrait (eager, open expression) on the left, a full-body standing figure mid-motion on the right. Even flat lighting. NO text, NO labels, NO panel borders.

## KING MINOS — `refs/ref_minos.png`

**Lock:** The tyrant of Crete, a powerful man in his late 40s. Olive skin, a broad hard face, black beard streaked with grey, cold heavy-lidded eyes. Thick black hair bound by a plain gold circlet crown. Broad-shouldered, imposing. Wears a deep crimson-red robe with a gold-worked border over a darker tunic, heavy gold arm-rings. Carries himself with still, controlling authority — a man used to being obeyed. Often one hand closed into a fist.

*Realistic adult heroic proportions.*

**Ref-sheet prompt:** register B (Style Block + anti-drift), 1536×1024 canvas, plain warm-tan neutral background. Character reference sheet: the king above, TWO views on one canvas — a waist-up front portrait (cold, commanding) on the left, a full-body standing figure on the right. Even flat lighting. NO text, NO labels, NO panel borders.

## THE MINOTAUR — `refs/ref_minotaur.png`

**Lock:** A monstrous man-bull. The muscular body of a huge man, deep bronze-brown skin, but the head of a black bull — heavy curved horns, snorting muzzle, small furious eyes. Powerful shoulders and chest, coarse dark hair along the spine and forearms. A tattered dark loincloth. Shown in shadow and half-light, hulking and dangerous, never comic. Mid-motion: lunging, or rising from darkness.

*Serious mythic monster, menacing, NOT a cartoon cow. Kept in shadow — menace over gore.*

**Ref-sheet prompt:** register B (Style Block + anti-drift), 1536×1024 canvas, dark stone-shadow background (this creature lives in the Labyrinth). Character reference sheet: the man-bull above, TWO views on one canvas — a front view rising/looming on the left, a full-body lunging figure on the right. Dramatic low light. NO text, NO labels, NO panel borders.

## THESEUS & ARIADNE — no ref sheet (single appearance, P3)

Handled in prose on P3 only:
- **Theseus:** a young Greek hero, early 20s, strong athletic build, short dark hair, bronze skin, a short white tunic and a bronze sword, a fierce set jaw. Holds a ball of red thread.
- **Ariadne:** a young Cretan princess, ~18, long dark hair braided with gold, a pale blue-and-white gown, holding the other end of the red thread. Watchful, brave.

The red thread is the visual key of the page — keep it vivid and continuous.
