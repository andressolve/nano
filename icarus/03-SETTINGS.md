# 03 — SETTINGS

## Crete / Knossos (P1–P3)

Bronze-Age Cretan palace: sun-bleached cream and terracotta stone, red-painted tapering columns (broader at top), bright frescoes (dolphins, spirals, bulls) on plaster walls, open sunlit courtyards. Warm, opulent, Minoan. Mediterranean light, hard shadows.

## The Labyrinth (P2–P3)

An underground maze of close cream-stone corridors, torch-lit, deep shadows, no sky. Claustrophobic — walls pressing in, passages forking. The Minotaur's domain: darker, scattered bones suggested (not gory), a sense of being watched. This is Daedalus's cleverness made stone — beautiful and terrible.

## The Tower Prison (P4–P8)

A high stone tower on a cliff at the edge of Crete, overlooking the open sea. Sun-bleached blocks, one barred window, a flat rooftop open to the sky. Below and around: blue Aegean stretching to the horizon, Minos's ships patrolling the water (small dark sails). The point of the setting: **the sea is guarded, the land is guarded — only the sky is free.** Gulls wheel around the tower (they give Daedalus the idea).

## The Open Sky / The Aegean (P8–P13)

The flight setting: vast high sky, scattered white clouds, the great golden sun. Far below, the deep-blue sea dotted with tiny islands, a fishing boat, a shepherd on a green headland looking up. As the story climbs (P9→P12) the sun grows larger and hotter, floods the top of the frame with hot yellow-white; the cool sea-blue shrinks to a thin band at the bottom. On the fall/grief pages the sky is bleached near-white and empty; the sea below is dark and wide.

## Icaria & the way to Sicily (P14)

A small rocky island rising from the sea (the island later called Icaria), a lone fresh grave-mound. Beyond it, the horizon and a far coastline (Sicily) under a calmer, lower sun — evening light, the heat gone out of the day. Quiet, elegiac.
