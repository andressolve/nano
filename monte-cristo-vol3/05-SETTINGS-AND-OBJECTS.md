# Settings and Objects

Paris, autumn into winter 1838: Volume II's palette — candle gold, gas-yellow,
wet black stone, deep interiors — with one shift. **Villefort's house is cold:
grey-blue walls, white marble, frost light, the colour of a closed file.**
The Count's house is black and gold. Auteuil is night-blue and lantern. The
court is dark oak and red.

Each location names one restrained accent that does dramatic work.

---

## Locations

### 1 · The house at Auteuil and its garden
A shuttered old country house in the village of Auteuil outside Paris, bought
by the Count. The garden: high stone walls, gravel walk, overgrown beds, and
**one old plane tree** with a wide pale trunk, the ground beneath it bare.
Interior: a long dining room opening on the garden by glass doors, candles.
Night-blue and lantern-gold.
**Accent:** the **lantern's gold** on the bare earth under the tree; later the
raw dark **hole** Villefort digs there.

### 2 · Villefort's house, Faubourg Saint-Honoré — the drawing room and stair
Grey-blue panelled walls, white marble chimneypiece, a cold crystal
chandelier, a straight white marble stair with an iron rail. Order, polish,
no warmth.
**Accent:** the **white stair**, where Madame de Villefort falls.

### 3 · Noirtier's room (ground floor of the same house)
An old man's room kept in 1815: dark green walls, an Empire clock, a framed
eagle, books; his high-backed wheeled armchair by the window; a small table
with a **carafe and glass of lemon-water** — his medicine — beside him.
**Accent:** the **forest green** of his walls and coat.

### 4 · Valentine's room (upper floor of the same house)
White and dove-grey; a narrow bed with white curtains; a night table with a
**glass of water and a carafe**; a tall wardrobe against the party wall. On p
29 onward, the wardrobe hides a door cut through the wall from the empty house
next door.
**Accent:** the **glass by the bed**, which catches the only light.

### 5 · The garden gate (bottom of Villefort's garden)
A tall iron gate in a high wall, ivy, a lane beyond; Valentine inside,
Maximilien outside, their hands through the bars.
**Accent:** **ivy green** against black iron.

### 6 · The Count's house, 30 Champs-Élysées
Inherited from Volume II (`refs/parents/17-set-count-house.png`): black and
gold, deep shadow, the Count's study; on pp 37 and 54 an upstairs room with a
bed where Valentine sleeps, Haydée beside her.
**Accent:** **gold** candlelight on black.

### 7 · The Morrel house, rue Meslay
The one warm house: a modest Paris apartment, wood and books and a fire; on a
side table, **a small red silk purse under a glass dome**.
**Accent:** the **red purse**.

### 8 · The Assize court, Palais de Justice
Dark oak benches and panelling, a raised bench, the prisoner's dock with an
oak rail, a public gallery packed above; tall windows of grey winter light.
Judges in red behind; Villefort at the prosecutor's desk in black.
**Accent:** the **red robes** of the bench behind Villefort.

### 9 · Père-Lachaise, the Villefort tomb
Rain, black wet trees, white stone; a family vault with an iron door. At night
on p 36, lantern light inside.
**Accent:** the **white** of the stone in rain.

### 10 · Minor, drawn from words (no plate)
The cellar tavern (p 4): low vaulted ceiling, smoke, candle stubs, card table.
The road down the hill at Auteuil (p 7): a steep lane, the Count's gate, a
wall. The prison cell (p 38): stone, a barred window, a bench. The street
outside Villefort's house (p 51): night, gas lamps, wet cobble.
