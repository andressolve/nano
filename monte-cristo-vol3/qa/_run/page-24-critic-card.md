## Page intent

## PAGE 24

A dramatic page in Noirtier's dark green room. Villefort shuts the door and questions his father, who can answer only with his eyes: one long blink for yes, many quick blinks for no, and Villefort says each answer aloud himself. Yes, someone in the house did it; no, it is not Valentine. As the old man's eyes begin to name the poisoner, Villefort straightens and holds up one hand between himself and those eyes to stop them. The dominant turn: Villefort knows there is a poisoner and chooses not to know who. The raised hand and the furious eyes behind it own the page. Noirtier never speaks. The reader turns because the King's Attorney has chosen not to know.

## Exact owner-controlled script

## PAGE 24 · *dramatic*

**Location:** Noirtier's room.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Villefort shutting the door behind him, grey-faced, still
in black.

> **VILLEFORT:** "I have prosecuted four poisoners, Father. I know the face I saw on that pillow this morning."
>
> **VILLEFORT:** "Someone in this house did it. Do you know who? Close your eyes once for yes."

**Panel 2 — 13%.** Noirtier's eyes: one long close.

> **VILLEFORT:** "Yes."

**Panel 3 — 20%.** Villefort leaning down over the chair, hard.

> **VILLEFORT:** "Is it Valentine? Everything her grandmother had is hers now."

**Panel 4 — 12%.** Noirtier's eyes: furious rapid blinking.

> **VILLEFORT:** "No. Then who —"

**Panel 5 — dominant, 30%.** Villefort straightening and holding up one hand
between himself and his father's eyes, to stop them.

> **VILLEFORT:** "Stop. Don't tell me. If I know, I must prosecute, and the name of Villefort will be in every newspaper in France."

**Why you turn the page:** the King's Attorney has chosen not to know who the
poisoner is.
