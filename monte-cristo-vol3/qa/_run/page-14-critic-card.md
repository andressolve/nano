> **PROSE FIELD 1** *(over the warm wall above the fire)*
> "There was one house in Paris where the Count wanted nothing from anyone.
>
> Maximilien Morrel was a captain of cavalry, home from Africa, and the son of Pierre Morrel, the shipowner of Marseille. Nine years ago, on the morning old Morrel meant to shoot himself rather than go bankrupt, a stranger paid every debt he owed, sailed his lost ship back into harbour, and vanished.
>
> The stranger was Edmond Dantès. The Morrels never learned it. They kept the red purse he left behind under glass."

**Why you turn the page:** the Count is in the one house where he is loved,
and nobody in it knows why.

## Materiality threshold

blocking only when it materially harms the reader event, exact text or

focal generation integrity, or the dominant dramatic relationship. Every

### C1 — Transcription fails
All three prose paragraphs must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order, with no other text and no balloons; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue; the words and their order are what must match. Prose over a face, the hands, or the purse is blocking only if it stops a string or the purse from reading.

### C2 — The warm house does not land
One warm firelit image must own the page: a young moustached man laughing as he takes both the Count's hands, and behind them a small red purse under a glass dome. If the purse is missing or unreadable as a purse under glass, the prose's last line has nothing to land on and it is blocking.

### C3 — Identity
Maximilien: square sun-browned face, short cropped dark hair, neat dark moustache, plain brown coat. A blue tunic with gold braid is blocking on this page. The Count: clean-shaven, pale, swept-back black hair, unrelieved black. Hands in the clasp must be whole and plausible; extra fingers or a third hand are blocking.
