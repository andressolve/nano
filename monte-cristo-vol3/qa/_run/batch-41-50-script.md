# Exact script — pages 41–50

Open only after the blind sequence read.

## PAGE 41 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the hall of Villefort's house, at the foot of the white stair,
morning.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Villefort in his black magistrate's robe and cap at the foot
of the white marble stair. Madame de Villefort stands halfway down in violet,
her son beside her with his tin hussar.

> **VILLEFORT:** "Héloïse, I know what you did to Valentine and to her grandmother. My father told me."
>
> **VILLEFORT:** "I have waited three days for you to spare me this."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 45%.** Villefort's cold face, close.

> **VILLEFORT:** "When I come home tonight, if you are still alive, I will arrest you myself, and you will go to the scaffold."
>
> **VILLEFORT:** "You have your poison. Use it."

**Panel 3 — 15%.** Villefort bending to kiss the boy's gold curls; the boy
holding up his tin hussar to be admired. His father's voice is gentle, as it is only
ever for this child.

> **VILLEFORT:** "Be good for your mother. I'll be home tonight."

**Panel 4 — 15%.** The front door closing on Villefort. Madame de Villefort on
the stair, her hand on her son's head, whispering after him.

> **HÉLOÏSE:** "And my son? What will become of my son?"

**Why you turn the page:** he has told his wife to die, and left the boy with
her.

## PAGE 42 · *spectacle*

**Location:** the Assize court, day.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 80%.** The court packed to the walls: the public gallery
crammed, red-robed judges on the high bench, gendarmes in blue. Benedetto in
the dock in his grey smock, grinning at the crowd. Villefort rising at the
prosecutor's desk in his black robe and white bands. And high in the gallery,
the Count.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left)*
> "All Paris had come to see the false prince tried. The King's Attorney rose to question him himself."

**Why you turn the page:** Villefort is about to question the son he does not
know is his.

## PAGE 43 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the Assize court.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** Villefort, cold, a paper in his hand.

> **VILLEFORT:** "Prisoner, you have called yourself a prince. Tell the court your real name."
>
> **BENEDETTO:** "Benedetto, monsieur. It means 'blessed'. I've always thought that was funny."

**Panel 2 — 25%.** Villefort, not looking up.

> **VILLEFORT:** "Your age?"
>
> **BENEDETTO:** "Twenty-one. I was born at midnight on the twenty-seventh of September, 1817."

**Panel 3 — dominant, 45%.** Villefort's pen has stopped. He looks up, slowly.

> **VILLEFORT:** "Where?"
>
> **BENEDETTO:** "At Auteuil, monsieur. In a house with a garden, and a very big tree."

**Why you turn the page:** Villefort knows that garden.

## PAGE 44 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the Assize court.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Villefort steadying himself with both hands on the desk.

> **VILLEFORT:** "And your father, prisoner? Or do you not know his name either?"

**Panel 2 — 20%.** Benedetto leaning on the rail of the dock, delighted.

> **BENEDETTO:** "Oh, I know it, monsieur. My father is the King's Attorney."

**Panel 3 — dominant, 55%.** Benedetto pointing straight across the court; the
whole court turning, every face toward Villefort, alone at his desk in black.

> **BENEDETTO:** "Monsieur, my father is you."

**Why you turn the page:** the whole court is looking at Villefort.

---

## PAGE 45 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the Assize court.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Villefort on his feet, white.

> **VILLEFORT:** "This man is a convict and a liar —"

**Panel 2 — dominant, 45%.** Benedetto, quiet now, the grin gone hard.

> **BENEDETTO:** "That night you put me in a box and buried me under the tree. A man was watching. He dug me up again."
>
> **BENEDETTO:** "You buried me alive, Father."

**Panel 3 — 30%.** Villefort, and the words escaping before he can stop them.

> **VILLEFORT:** "It wasn't breathing. I thought it was dead —"
>
> **BENEDETTO:** "You didn't look."

**Why you turn the page:** Villefort has just confessed without meaning to.

## PAGE 46 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the Assize court.

**Panel 1 — 20%.** Total silence: the court, every face on Villefort — except
one. In the dock, between two gendarmes, Benedetto is grinning up at the
gallery.

**Panel 2 — dominant, 55%.** Villefort standing alone, his cap fallen, his robe
hanging off one shoulder. He looks up past all of them — to the gallery.

> **VILLEFORT:** "Gentlemen, do not trouble to look for proof. It is true. Everything he said is true."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** High in the gallery, the Count, looking down at him. The
left corner of his mouth lifts.

> **CAPTION** *(beside the Count)*
> "He had waited twenty-three years for that face."

**Why you turn the page:** the King's Attorney has confessed in open court,
and the Count has what he came for.

## PAGE 47 · *illustrated prose*

**Location:** inside Villefort's carriage, racing through Paris.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 70%.** Villefort alone in a closed carriage, still in his
robe, his face in his hands; rain streaming past the window; behind, through
the little back window, the lamps of a second carriage, black, following.

> **PROSE FIELD 1** *(over the rain)*
> "He walked out through a court that parted for him like water. Nobody tried to stop him. Behind him the gendarmes were taking Benedetto down, still grinning, the most famous prisoner in France.
>
> In the carriage he thought of nothing for a long time. Then he remembered what he had said to his wife that morning, on the stair. And then he remembered that his son was in the house with her.
>
> “Faster,” he shouted. “For God's sake, faster!”
>
> He did not notice the black carriage behind his, keeping pace all the way."

**Why you turn the page:** he told her to die, the boy is with her, and the
Count is following.

## PAGE 48 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the hall and white stair of Villefort's house.

**Panel 1 — 22%.** Villefort bursting through the front door into the silent
hall.

> **VILLEFORT:** "Héloïse! Where is the boy? Héloïse!"

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** Madame de Villefort at the top of the white
marble stair in violet, holding the iron rail, swaying; the empty cut-crystal
vial — the Count's vial — slipping from her other hand and falling down the
white steps.

> **HÉLOÏSE:** "I did what you told me, monsieur."

**Panel 3 — 28%.** She sinks down onto the top stair, her eyes closing.

> **VILLEFORT:** "Where is my son?"
>
> **HÉLOÏSE:** "I couldn't leave him behind. Not in a house with your name on it."

**Why you turn the page:** she has taken the boy with her.

## PAGE 49 · *spectacle*

**Location:** the upstairs corridor of Villefort's house.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 80%.** A long cold corridor in grey light. At its far
end, a closed white door. On the floor before it, alone, the tin hussar on its
rearing horse, red and gold. Villefort, small in the distance, running toward
it.

**Panel 2 — 20%.** Close: Villefort's hand on the white door handle.

**Why you turn the page:** the door is closed, and his son's soldier is lying
outside it.

## PAGE 50 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the top of the white stair and the upstairs corridor, outside
the boy's door.

**Panel 1 — 20%.** The top of the white marble stair. Madame de Villefort lies
fallen on the top step in her violet silk, eyes closed, with dignity, one hand
still on the iron rail and the other hanging open over the edge of the step.
The Count, black, is coming up the stair; a few steps below her he has stooped
and lifted the empty cut-crystal vial from the step where it fell — his own,
come back to him — and his eyes are on her face above him.

**Panel 2 — 20%.** Down the corridor: Villefort on his knees in the open
doorway with his back to us, his head down. The Count at the corridor's end
behind him.

> **VILLEFORT:** "Who's there?"
>
> **COUNT:** "You dug under my tree at two in the morning, monsieur, and never once asked who I was. I've come to tell you."

**Panel 3 — dominant, 40%.** The Count's face, full in the frame, every mask
off.

> **COUNT:** "Remember the Château d'If. Remember a sailor named Edmond Dantès, who carried your father a letter."
>
> **COUNT:** "I am Edmond Dantès."

**Panel 4 — 20%.** Villefort up off his knees, seizing the Count by the coat
with both hands, dragging him toward the open door.

> **VILLEFORT:** "Dantès. Then come and look. Come and see what you have done!"

**Why you turn the page:** Villefort is dragging him in to see.
