## Page intent

## PAGE 37

A dramatic page at Père-Lachaise in the rain, the day of Valentine's funeral. The dominant turn: Maximilien accuses the Count at the tomb, and the Count says nothing of what he could say. The page belongs to the wide wet scene of the vault door closing on her, the mourners under umbrellas, Villefort at the front, Maximilien bareheaded in his blue uniform at the very door, and the Count at the back of the crowd. Then Maximilien turns away from the tomb and faces the Count: you gave me your word. The Count, rain on his face, tells him to come away because the house is watching, and Maximilien says it again: you promised me. The reader turns because the Count is letting the man he loves believe she is dead. The other mourners stay subordinate.
