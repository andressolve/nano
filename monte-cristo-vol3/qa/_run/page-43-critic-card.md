## Page intent

## PAGE 43

Dramatic. Villefort questions the prisoner coldly, from a paper, barely looking at him: his real name, his age. Benedetto answers with insolent ease and gives the exact birth date the Count taught him. Then Villefort asks where, and Benedetto says Auteuil, a house with a garden and a very big tree. The page's one dominant turn: Benedetto names Auteuil and the garden, and Villefort's pen stops. Villefort owns the dominant panel: his pen still, his face lifting slowly toward the dock. Benedetto is the voice that does it. The reader turns because Villefort knows that garden. The courtroom, the judges and the crowd stay subordinate behind the two of them.

## Exact owner-controlled script

## PAGE 43 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the Assize court.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** Villefort, cold, a paper in his hand.

> **VILLEFORT:** "Prisoner, you have called yourself a prince. Tell the court your real name."
>
> **BENEDETTO:** "Benedetto, monsieur. It means 'blessed'. I've always thought that was funny."

> **VILLEFORT:** "Your age?"

> **VILLEFORT:** "Where?"
>
> **BENEDETTO:** "At Auteuil, monsieur. In a house with a garden, and a very big tree."
