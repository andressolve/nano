## Page intent

## PAGE 52

Dramatic page. At dusk, in the small walled garden behind his own house, Villefort in shirtsleeves, his robe flung on the gravel, collar torn open, hair fallen across his brow, digs furiously in the frozen flower beds with a spade while every window of the house above him is lit. The Count at the garden door tells him, quietly, that this is his own garden and the tree is at Auteuil; Villefort answers that he will dig up every garden in Paris, and keeps digging. The dominant turn: Villefort digs in the wrong garden while the Count watches. The digging madman owns the page; the Count is a still witness, not an actor. This is not the Auteuil garden: no plane tree, no hole from earlier, no box.

### C1 — Transcription or ownership fails
Any of the 3 strings is missing, altered, duplicated, reordered, unreadable from the 600 × 900 proof (a string that reads is never measured), or not owned by the right mouth by tail and placement; extra text appears. Villefort owns "He's here…" and "Then I'll dig up every garden in Paris."; the Count owns "Monsieur. This is your own garden…". Nonblocking, the words and their order being what must match: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, the run length of a repeated letter. A line directly under its speaker's previous balloon, or naming its addressee, is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — The turn does not land
Villefort digging furiously in his own small garden under the lit house must own this dramatic page, with the Count a still watcher. Blocking if the Count dominates, or the digging does not read as madness.

### C3 — Wrong garden
This is a small city garden, not Auteuil. A large plane tree, a box, or a dug hole beneath a tree reads as Auteuil and is blocking.

### C4 — Identity and separation
Villefort in white shirtsleeves with grey hair fallen loose, the Count in unrelieved black with swept-back black hair. Blocking if either reads as someone else.

### C5 — Generation integrity
Broken hands on the spade, a duplicated figure, or a balloon hiding a line is blocking. Flying earth and gravel are not.
