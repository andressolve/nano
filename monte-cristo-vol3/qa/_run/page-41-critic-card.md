## Page intent

## PAGE 41

Dramatic. Morning, at the foot of the white stair in Villefort's house. Villefort, dressed for court, tells his wife he knows she poisoned Valentine and her grandmother, and orders her to use her poison on herself before he comes home tonight. Then, gently, he kisses his small son's curls and leaves for court. The page's one dominant turn: Villefort orders his wife to poison herself and kisses his son goodbye. Villefort owns the page: his cold face, close, giving the sentence. The last beat belongs to Madame de Villefort on the stair with her hand on the boy's head, asking what will become of him. The reader turns because Villefort has told his wife to die and left the boy alone with her. The hall's marble and the door stay subordinate.

## Exact owner-controlled script

## PAGE 41 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the hall of Villefort's house, at the foot of the white stair,
morning.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Villefort in his black magistrate's robe and cap at the foot
of the white marble stair. Madame de Villefort stands halfway down in violet,
her son beside her with his tin hussar.

> **VILLEFORT:** "Héloïse, I know what you did to Valentine and to her grandmother. My father told me."
>
> **VILLEFORT:** "I have waited three days for you to spare me this."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 45%.** Villefort's cold face, close.

> **VILLEFORT:** "When I come home tonight, if you are still alive, I will arrest you myself, and you will go to the scaffold."
>
> **VILLEFORT:** "You have your poison. Use it."

**Panel 3 — 15%.** Villefort bending to kiss the boy's gold curls; the boy
holding up his tin hussar to be admired. His father's voice is gentle, as it is only
ever for this child.

> **VILLEFORT:** "Be good for your mother. I'll be home tonight."

**Panel 4 — 15%.** The front door closing on Villefort. Madame de Villefort on
the stair, her hand on her son's head, whispering after him.

> **HÉLOÏSE:** "And my son? What will become of my son?"

**Why you turn the page:** he has told his wife to die, and left the boy with
her.
