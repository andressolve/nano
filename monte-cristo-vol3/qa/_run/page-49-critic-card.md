## Page intent

## PAGE 49

Spectacle page, wordless. A long cold grey corridor; at its far end a closed white door; on the floor before it, alone, the boy's tin hussar on its rearing horse, red and gold. Villefort, small in the distance in his black robe, runs toward it. A close beat follows: his hand on the white door handle. The dominant turn: the tin hussar lies outside a closed door. The corridor and the toy own the page; the reader understands before Villefort does. The reader turns because the door is about to open. The boy is never seen, nor anything behind the door; Villefort's face is not the subject here, the distance and the small bright toy are.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 49 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
The page carries no strings: transcribing the 600 × 900 proof must find no text at all. Any lettered word, balloon, caption, label or sound cue is blocking; marks that are plainly texture, not letters, are never measured and do not count. The standing tolerances (spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant, the run length of a repeated letter) and ownership by placement have nothing to apply to here.

### C2 — The turn does not land
This is a spectacle page: the long corridor, the closed white door and the small bright toy before it must own the page, with Villefort small and running toward it. Blocking if the toy is not findable by eye, the door is open, or the page reads as a close-up drama rather than the empty corridor.

### C3 — Object state fails
Exactly one tin hussar, red and gold, alone on the floor before the door. A second toy, or the boy himself, or anything seen behind the door, is blocking. Exact toy detail is nonblocking when it reads as the boy's soldier on a horse.

### C4 — Identity fails
The running man must read as Villefort in his black robe with grey hair, not a servant or the Count. Small scale is expected; blocking only if he reads as someone else.

### C5 — Generation integrity
The close hand on the handle has broken anatomy, or a duplicated figure appears. Panelling and floor texture are nonblocking.
