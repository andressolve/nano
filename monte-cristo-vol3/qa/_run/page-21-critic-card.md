## Page intent

## PAGE 21

A dramatic page in the garden at Auteuil after dinner, guests with candles under the one old plane tree. The Count stops on the bare ground and tells a ghost story about a child born and buried here one night long ago; Madame de Villefort is thrilled. The dominant turn: the Count's ghost story under the tree sends Villefort away white. The tableau owns the page — Villefort white as paper gripping the trunk, his wife delighted, and Benedetto, his grin gone, looking at the ground under his own feet and then up at the Count. Villefort turns stiffly for the house with a curt good night. The reader turns because the King's Attorney is fleeing a tree, and the false prince has understood whose garden this is.

### C4 — Identity and separation
