## Page intent

## PAGE 53

Illustrated-prose page. The Count stands alone in a black street under a gas lamp as snow begins, beneath Villefort's lit windows, the window he once counted from across the roofs on the night he said "One." The prose tells what he cannot say: two men are finished, and he cannot say the next word, "Two"; his hand is closed round a tin soldier in his pocket; he keeps seeing a red bottle in a woman's hand where he put it himself; for the first time since the Château d'If he does not know whether God is on his side. The dominant turn: the Count cannot say "Two." The lone figure in snow owns the image; the street, the lamp and the house stay quiet and subordinate.
