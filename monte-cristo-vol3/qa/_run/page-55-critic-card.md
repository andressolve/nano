## Page intent

## PAGE 55

Dramatic page. In the Count's study at night, Maximilien, very calm in a plain brown coat, comes to say goodbye, his father's dark pistol case under his arm: his father nearly used it once, at eleven in the morning. The Count rises and says a stranger came at five minutes to eleven; Maximilien asks how he could know that. The Count lays his hand flat on the pistol case between them, faces close: give me one month; if you still want to die then, I'll load it myself. Maximilien agrees. The dominant turn: Maximilien agrees to wait one month. The hand on the case and the two faces own the page; the study furniture stays dark and subordinate. The case stays shut.

## Exact owner-controlled script

## PAGE 55 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the Count's study, Champs-Élysées, night.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** Maximilien in a plain brown coat in the doorway, very calm,
a dark wooden pistol case with brass corners under his arm.

> **MAXIMILIEN:** "I came to say goodbye, monsieur. This was my father's. He nearly used it once, at eleven o'clock in the morning."

**Panel 2 — 22%.** The Count rising from his desk.

> **COUNT:** "And at five minutes to eleven, a stranger came."
>
> **MAXIMILIEN:** "How do you know it was five to eleven?"

**Panel 3 — dominant, 48%.** The Count's hand flat on the pistol case between
them; their faces close.

> **COUNT:** "I know a great many things. Give me one month. If you still want to die then, I'll load it myself."
>
> **MAXIMILIEN:** "One month."

**Why you turn the page:** Maximilien has agreed to wait, and the reader knows
what the month might hold.

### C1 — Transcription or ownership fails
Any of the 5 strings is missing, altered, duplicated, reordered, unreadable from the 600 × 900 proof (a string that reads is never measured), or not owned by the right mouth by tail and placement; extra text appears. Maximilien owns the goodbye, the question "How do you know it was five to eleven?" and "One month."; the Count owns "And at five minutes to eleven…" and "I know a great many things…". Each short line must hang from its own speaker's mouth by tail, not merely make sense as his. Nonblocking, the words and their order being what must match: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, the run length of a repeated letter. A line directly under its speaker's previous balloon, or naming its addressee, is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — The turn does not land
The Count's hand flat on the closed pistol case, faces close, must own this dramatic page, and "One month." must read as Maximilien's agreement. Blocking if the reader cannot tell he agrees to wait.

### C3 — Identity
Maximilien in a plain brown coat with moustache and sun-browned square face; the Count in black, pale, clean-shaven. Blocking if either reads as another principal.

### C4 — The pistol case
One closed dark case with brass corners. An open case or a visible pistol is blocking.

### C5 — Generation integrity
Broken focal hands on the case, or a duplicated figure, is blocking.
