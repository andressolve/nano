## Costume states

- **Count**: always unrelieved black evening dress (sheet default); outdoors at night the long black cloak (pp 1, 2–3, 7, 22 window, 37–38, 53).
- **Villefort**: rigid black frock coat + high white stock (default) on pp 10, 20–21, 24, 36; **court state** — black magistrate's robe, white bands, black cylindrical cap (cap lost from p46 on) — on pp 6, 41 (morning, leaving for court), 42–46, 47 (carriage), 48–51 (still in the robe); **shirtsleeves digging** on p22 (coat flung off) and p52 (robe flung off, collar torn open, earth on hands).
- **Benedetto**: cellar state (mustard-brown jacket, grey neckerchief, coarse grey shirt — never white) pp 4–5; **prince state** (bottle-green velvet coat, cream embroidered waistcoat, diamond pin) pp 20–21, 27; **prisoner** (grey smock) pp 40, 42–46.
- **Maximilien**: plain brown coat over uniform shirt pp 14–16 (at home); Spahi dark-blue tunic with gold frogging pp 19, 29, 37 (bareheaded in rain); plain brown coat p55.
- **Valentine**: dove-grey and white with pale-blue ribbon pp 10–12, 19, 28, 30 (pale, on a daybed); **white nightdress, hair loose** pp 33–34 (in bed), 35 (lying "dead", white sheet), 38 (white dress in the coffin — use the nightdress/white state), 39, 56 (asleep in white bed).
- **Héloïse**: amethyst-violet silk, honey-gold high coils pp 7–8 (coils coming loose), 10, 21, 23, 25, 41, 48, 50; **violet dressing-gown** p32 (night).
- **Noirtier**: always the wheeled armchair, forest-green coat, rug — pp 11–13, 24, 28, 30, 35 (carried up to Valentine's bedside), 36, 54.
- **Haydée**: crimson-and-gold Epirote, always — pp 2–3, 9, 17, 26, 39 (bedside, sleeves pushed back), 56 (bedside).
- **Villefort's son** (unnamed, no balloons, never named): violet velvet skeleton suit, white collar, gold curls, tin hussar — pp 7, 8, 10, 25, 41. Never drawn after p41. On pp 49–51 only the hussar.

## Consequential objects (exactly one of each where shown)

- **Vial** (cut crystal, red liquid, gold stopper): p8 the Count's hand → given to Héloïse; p32 her hand pouring red drops into Valentine's glass (full); p48 empty, slipping from her hand, falling down the white steps; p50 the Count stoops and lifts it from the step where it fell, empty; p51 held up empty in his open hand. Mentioned, not drawn, elsewhere. Never two vials.
- **Tin hussar** (toy soldier on a rearing horse, red and gold): pp 7 (limp hand over the carriage side), 8, 10, 25 (pressed into the Count's gloved hand, given back), 41; p49 alone on the floor outside the closed white door; p50 Villefort staring at it; p51 the Count's hand picking it up; p56 in the Count's gloved hand in the doorway.
- **Red purse under a glass dome**: pp 14–15 only.
- **Pastille box** (small gold box, one white pastille): pp 34 (open in his palm; she takes it).
- **Pistol case** (dark wood, brass corners, closed): p55.
- **Valentine's glass of water**: p32 red drops poured in; p33 held up to the candle with red threads in it.
- **Noirtier's glass of lemon-water** (his medicine): pp 11–13 on his table, p30 the Count lifts and smells it. Valentine's poisoned drink on p28 is a **cup of chocolate**; the grandmother's (p23) a cup of warm milk.
- **Hole under the plane tree**: bare undisturbed earth pp 1–3, 21; dug open and empty p22 (the plate shows bare earth: on p22 say the hole is dug, from words). On p52 Villefort digs his own small city garden behind his house — NOT Auteuil, no plane tree.
- **Invitation cards** p18: three, plain cream, seen edge-on, no visible writing.
- **The visiting card** p5: printed object text "Prince Andrea Cavalcanti" (object text, on the card only).
- **Noirtier's spelled note** p36: printed object text "H É L O Ï S E" on paper in Villefort's hand.
