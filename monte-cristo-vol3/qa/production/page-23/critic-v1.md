Valentine’s grandmother arrived on Tuesday, with a great deal of luggage and a list
of young men. She was old, and tired from the road, and very fond of her granddaughter.

On the third night Madame de Villefort carried her a cup of warm milk herself. On the way
she stopped in Noirtier’s room, where nobody ever looked, and tipped something red into the cup
in front of the one man in the house who could not tell. The old lady drank it and did not wake.

By the next afternoon the whole house knew two things: that the old lady was dead, and that
everything she had owned, which was a great deal, now belonged to Valentine, who was suddenly
very rich. The young men on the list went home.
