## Blind read
In a candlelit cellar tavern, a ragged young man asks how rich a man is and demands his name. The dark-clad man refuses, then creates a better identity for the young man: an Italian prince supplied with a carriage, a tailor, and money. The dominant middle panel owns the page as the dark-clad man sets the transformation before him in the form of a white name card, purse, and signet ring. In the final panel the young man admires the ring and asks the price; the dark-clad man directs him to dine with the King's Attorney, and the young man treats the prospect as a joke. The turn is the young man's transformation into Prince Andrea Cavalcanti and his binding to the dark-clad man's plan.
