## Findings

### Finding C2
- Observation: The Count is the focal presence in all three panels, including an isolated central close-up and the centered final reply with the folded letter, while Haydée disappears from the middle panel; by eye, his answer and plan own the page rather than her straight confrontation over the dead woman and the milk.
- Material reader harm: The required dominant relationship is reversed. Haydée's accusation reads as setup for the Count's strategic reveal instead of the moral challenge controlling the page, so the pressure of her question is materially subordinated.
- Redraw justification: This is a page-wide hierarchy and focal-composition failure, not a local cosmetic issue; restoring Haydée's ownership would require changing the complete page's dramatic emphasis, and the current emphasis substantially changes the scripted reader event.
