APPROVED

contrast and black level, matte gouache/opaque-watercolor treatment, visible
brushwork, lighting logic, lettering weight, balloon construction, and overall
finish read as one book throughout the sequence.

The Count, Valentine, Héloïse, Villefort, Noirtier, Maximilien, Haydée, and
Benedetto remain recognizable against their approved locks without confusing
collisions. Valentine’s room, Noirtier’s room, the Père-Lachaise vault, and the
Count’s black-and-gold interior retain their locked visual identities and story
state. The red poison and the gold pastille box also remain consequentially
clear.

Maximilien’s uniform braid arrangement on page 37 and the precise dressing of
the Count’s interior on page 39 vary from their locks, but only cosmetically;
neither changes recognition, setting identity, or reading.

No page in this range falls outside the visual continuity of the book. No
redraw is justified.
