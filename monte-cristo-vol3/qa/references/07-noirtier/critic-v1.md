## Structural-lock audit

- **Face shape:** broad and leonine but visibly gaunt, with sunken cheeks; it does not inherit Villefort's narrow inverted triangle.
- **Brow and eyes:** heavy white brows over deep-set dark eyes. The open-eye and long-blink studies make the fierce, glittering eyes the only moving or living feature.
- **Nose and mouth:** the strong projecting nose holds in three-quarter and strict profile; the mouth stays closed and motionless beneath the full cavalry moustache.
- **Hair mass and line:** a long, pure-white mane sweeps back from the forehead to the collar in every view, with no hard side part and no grey-black remnant.
- **Facial hair:** the full white cavalry moustache is consistent across the portrait, seated view, profile, and reaction studies.
- **Build, posture, and gesture:** the body remains seated and utterly still in the high-backed wheeled armchair; the head is upright and both hands lie motionless on the rug. No bodily gesture competes with the eyes.
- **Costume value pattern and accent:** the faded forest-green Empire coat, dark waistcoat and stock, and dark tartan rug match the planned value stack and reserved green accent. The chair and rug are listed continuity elements, not invented props.
- **Never list:** no narrow Villefort face, hard grey side part, frail or pitiable affect, standing pose, open mouth, or movement outside the eyes appears.
- **Required views and artifact check:** the sheet contains the neutral three-quarter portrait, full seated figure with chair and wheels, strict ninety-degree profile, and the two planned eye states. It has no lettering, labels, watermark, signature, drawn frame, or border.
