## Mandatory findings

1. The white stair is not unequivocally the brightest primary marker: the large exterior window at left is at least as bright, while warm brown parquet and furniture plus multiple gold frames and ornaments soften the required grey-blue, white-marble, frost-light austerity. Redesign the value and colour hierarchy so the stair beyond the open double doors is the single brightest and most dominant light form; lower the exterior window to cold mid-grey daylight, cool/desaturate the floor and furniture, and reduce the gold fittings to near-neutral subdued metal so the room reads as “the colour of a closed file.”

2. Several unlisted closed rectangular boxes/cases appear on the central round table and right-side furniture, with further loose rectangular objects on the foreground desk. These can propagate through every drawing-room page and collide with the consequential dark-wood pistol case. Remove every closed rectangular box/case and loose book from the central table, foreground desk, and right-side furniture; leave those surfaces bare rather than substituting another portable prop.

## Nonblocking observations

- The plate is a single continuous 1536 × 1024 landscape view with the required grey-blue panelling, white marble chimneypiece, cold crystal chandelier, open double doors, straight stair, and black iron rail.
- The grate is cold, the stair is clear of objects, and there are no flowers or reserved costume colours on the plate.
- The architecture offers workable light-value zones for same-panel opposition, especially the pale window and stair-hall openings, once the stair is given the required dominant value.
