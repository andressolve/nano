## Mandatory findings

1. **The rendering register is wrong for the locked system.** The candidate reads as smooth photoreal cinematic concept art: plaster, floorboards, bedding, curtains, rug, glass, and furniture are modeled with continuous photographic texture and soft tonal gradation rather than broad visible matte-gouache/opaque-watercolor strokes, simplified interlocking colour shapes, and bold shadow masses. **Structural redesign requirement:** rerender the same room as a plainly painted plate: reduce the walls, bed, curtains, wardrobe, and floor to large interlocking dove-grey, cold grey-blue, and chalk-white shapes; make broad dry matte brushstrokes visible across every surface; consolidate the present soft gradients into two or three decisive shadow masses; reserve hard edges for the bed, closed wardrobe, and water glass/carafe only; remove photographic lens realism and polished 3-D surface rendering.

2. **Unlisted props have been promoted into durable continuity markers.** The large locked trunk at the foot of the bed, the second cropped trunk at right, the ornate box and books on the desk, and the vase on the sill are prominent enough that a page model will repeat them as if they matter. The two strong closed containers are especially consequential-looking beside a story mechanism built around a hidden passage. **Structural redesign requirement:** remove both trunks, the ornate box, the books, and the vase; simplify the left foreground to an unadorned partial writing table and plain chair, or crop them out; keep the room's primary marker stack limited to the narrow white-curtained bed, one night table with one clear glass and one clear-water carafe, and the tall closed wardrobe flush against an unbroken party wall.

- The plate is the required single continuous landscape view; the four-view character-sheet test does not apply to a setting plate.
- It has no figure, face, lettering, label, watermark, signature, inscription, internal panel border, or garment left on furniture.
- The location geometry is correct: narrow bed, white curtains, bedside table, and tall wardrobe against the wall.
- The consequential state is correct: the wardrobe is closed, the wall has no visible hidden-door seam or opening, and the glass/carafe contain clear water with no red drops.
- The cold white and dove-grey palette and nocturnal frost light are correct. The glass and carafe catch a strong highlight, though the rerender should preserve them as the room's restrained accent rather than allowing any remaining furnishing to compete.
- Grayscale and thumbnail reduction preserve the bed/wardrobe/window organization. Profile, reaction-expression, costume-free face, and re-entry identity tests are inapplicable because the plate contains no character.
