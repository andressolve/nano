# Salt and Stone — Life Four Character Definitions

## CRITICAL: How Character Locking Works

The **reference image** does 80% of the character locking. The text lock block below is reinforcement only. Keep it short.

For every page prompt:
1. **Upload the reference image(s)** for all characters in the scene
2. Paste the SHORT lock block below
3. Include the phrase: "featuring the same characters shown in the reference images"

Do NOT pad lock blocks with atmospheric or redundant description.

## CHARACTER: NIKOS (The Father — age 25-26)

**Reference sheet prompt:**
```
create image: Subject:
Character reference sheet showing a 25-26 year old man in two views side by side: close-up portrait on the left, full body on the right.

Character Description:
Man, 25-26, medium-tall with broad shoulders and a strong, weathered build. Mediterranean olive skin, deeply tanned and wind-roughened from years on the steppe. Strong jaw with a full but short dark brown beard (not long — trimmed short in Scythian style). Dark brown hair, thick, grown out past his ears, often windswept. Dark brown eyes, deep-set, calm — a man who has finally stopped running. Strong nose, slightly crooked. Thin scar on left palm. Burn scar on right forearm. A shell necklace on a worn leather cord at his throat — smooth from decades of wear. He wears Scythian clothing: a fitted leather tunic over a wool shirt, leather trousers tucked into knee-high riding boots, a heavy wool and fur coat. A leather belt with a simple bronze buckle.

Left panel — Portrait:
Close-up head and shoulders. Calm, steady, warm expression. Short beard visible. Shell necklace at throat. Weathered skin, crow's feet beginning at eyes. He looks like a man at peace.

Right panel — Full body:
Standing 3/4 view. Full Scythian outfit — leather tunic, fur coat, trousers, boots. Shell necklace visible. Burn scar on right forearm visible where sleeve is pushed up. Grounded, relaxed stance.

Art Style: Cinematic painterly realism with restrained brush texture, historical graphic novel aesthetic
Background: Simple warm neutral gradient
Lighting: Soft even studio reference lighting

General Instruction:
This is a reference sheet for character consistency. Both views must show the exact same person. He should look 25-26 — older than Life Three's 19-20. More weathered, more settled. A father, not a wanderer. Upload the Life Three Nikos reference for facial continuity.

Aspect Ratio: 4:3 (landscape)
```

**Lean lock block (paste into page prompts):**
```
NIKOS (25-26, Greek-born Scythian): Broad-shouldered, olive skin, short dark beard, dark brown windswept hair past ears. Shell necklace on leather cord. Scar on left palm. Burn scar on right forearm. Scythian leather and fur clothing.
```

**Which pages:** ALL (1-20, cover, back cover)
