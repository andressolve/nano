- **Pages 7-8 (Traders arrive)** — the palette collision can go "fantasy bazaar" instead of grounded trade scene

- **Page 16 (Burial full page)** — communal grief can go theatrical or sentimental
- **Pages 18-19 (Stories/Necklace)** — the emotional climax is at highest risk of sentimentality
- **Page 20 (Final ride)** — can drift toward "inspirational poster" aesthetic
