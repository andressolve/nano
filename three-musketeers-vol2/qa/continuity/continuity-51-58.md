# Visual-continuity gate — pages 51–58

VERDICT: APPROVED

Pages 51–58 remain in the **Lantern and Steel** register and belong to the
same book. The sequence keeps the approved lamp-black line, warm laid-paper
ground, opaque ochre/umber/Prussian-blue/vermilion palette, visible painted
texture, strong but readable black level, and motivated lantern/candle or
rain-grey light. The sharp shift from the black storm on page 51 to the muted
brown-grey dawn on page 52 matches the approved ferry-night/ferry-dawn states;
the later return to warm interiors and evening Paris is similarly motivated,
not palette drift.

The named cast remains recognizable against the approved locks. D'Artagnan's
black hair, ochre clothes, broken-feather grey hat, long sword, yellow horse,
and later too-large blue cassock remain stable. Athos keeps his long dark
face, moustache and pointed beard, black underdress, white plume, and grave
bearing. Porthos keeps his broad build, red curls and plumes, curled moustache,
crimson, and gold baldric; his apparent bulk varies modestly with pose on pages
51–53 and 56, but never threatens recognition. Aramis remains the fine-boned
blond with the small moustache and lace. Rochefort retains black dress, red
feather, moustache, and left-cheek scar. The Cardinal retains his gaunt face,
grey pointed beard, skullcap, red, chair, and grey cat across pages 54–55 and
the page-58 coda.

The consequential settings and objects also hold: the ferry landing, rope,
weir, raft/lantern night state and empty dawn state match their approved
plates; the Cardinal's red-hung, one-candle study remains distinct; the tavern,
attic/street lantern, Paris masonry, blank folded pardon, sealed commission,
and buttercup-yellow horse remain legible and consistent. Balloon shape,
cream fill, lettering weight, narration typography, paper borders, and overall
finish remain coherent across the sequence.

No page no longer belongs to the book. No redraw is justified.
