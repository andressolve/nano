### Stage 1: the image alone

There are three vignettes, softened at the edges into one warm laid-paper ground. There are no rules, frames or borders.

1. **Street door at night, in rain.** A narrow arched wooden door is set in a stone wall. One lit lantern hangs on an iron bracket beside it. The cobbles are wet, a lane of tall houses recedes into blue-grey rain, and a small empty wooden handcart stands on the left.
2. **The steep stair, seen from the bottom.** It is a long, narrow, steep timber stair between plank walls, with a rail on the right. One candle burns at the top landing.
3. **The attic.** It has sloping roof beams, one window with small panes looking onto night rooftops, and one narrow bed with rumpled bedding. There is one bare wooden table with one candle in a holder. One iron nail juts from a high beam (upper right).

Checks:
- **Lettering, labels, watermarks, signatures, borders:** none.
- **Figures:** none in any vignette. I checked the receding street, the stair landing and the attic window for small figures and silhouettes. None are present.
- **Register:** this fits Lantern and Steel. It has brush-ink line, matte warm umber and ochre washes, and Prussian-grey rain outdoors. Each interior is lit by a single candle with real cast shadow, and the paper grain is visible. Nothing in it is glossy, rendered like 3D, engraved or soft.
- **Confusability with other plates:** the attic is warm umber with timber and a candle. Athos's room, as the plan describes it, is whitewash in grey window light with no flame. The two will not be mistaken for each other, even at thumbnail size or in grayscale. The bed and table are the only shared furniture, and wall tone and the light source separate the rooms.

### Nonblocking observations

1. **The handcart.** An empty handcart stands in the street-door vignette. It is not in the ledger, and it will tend to reappear on pp 7–8 whenever the street-door study is attached. That is harmless on p 7. On p 8 the page has "a coach at the door", and a stray cart next to the coach could clutter the one beat that matters. Suggested page-packet line for p 8 (not a plate change): "the coach stands at the door; no handcart in the street."
2. **The candle at the top of the stair.** It is lit on the plate. pp 7–8 should set any change in the stair's light from words, especially the dawn on p 8.
3. **The future Red Dovecote stair.** `set-dovecote-common-room` also includes "the dark stair to a door". When that plate is generated, its prompt should keep that stair short and set in a smoky brown common room with the hearth in view. This stops it echoing this tall, narrow, timber-walled stair, which is the house's accent.
4. **The nail.** It is small and sits high in the corner of the attic vignette. Any page whose beat uses it should name it in words. Nothing in the current ledger requires that.
