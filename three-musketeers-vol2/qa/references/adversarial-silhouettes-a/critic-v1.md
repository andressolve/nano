## Mandatory findings

1. **Restore the Athos/Rochefort height lock in both rows.** The rendered figures currently read at effectively the same anatomical height, and the silhouette row supplies much less than the ledger's required full-head difference. Rebuild the shared-baseline lineup so Athos's anatomical crown is one complete head-height above Rochefort's anatomical crown in both the black silhouettes and the rendered figures. Do not obtain the difference through hat or feather height: keep Athos tall, vertically still, and hands-folded, while Rochefort remains middle-height, wiry, chin-up, and hip-cocked.

## Nonblocking observations

- All five figures otherwise read as the same identities as their approved sheets. Athos retains his full beard; Rochefort retains a clean chin and his correct unscarred right-cheek view.
- The face, hair, beard, build, and posture cues survive grayscale and thumbnail reduction. Porthos and d'Artagnan are especially immediate on re-entry; Aramis remains separable from both d'Artagnan and Milady by ordered blond hair length and facial hair.
- No lettering, labels, watermark, enclosing border, or continuity-expanding unlisted prop appears on the board.
