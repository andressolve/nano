## Mandatory findings

Views (a)–(c) use loose bends and wavy locks at the cheeks, neck and shoulder, and view (d) introduces wet strands curling and hanging forward over the forehead and face. Redraw the full hair mass in every view as dark-brown, straight shoulder-length sections with a clean pushed-back front hairline; in view (d), flatten those same straight sections against the skull, neck and shoulders, with no wave, curl, or face-crossing fringe. Preserve the high forehead as the primary face marker.

2. View (d) draws freestanding water droplets around the figure and a puddled wet-ground mark under the boots, although the required state is the soaked figure alone on the plain sheet ground. Remove every suspended drip, falling droplet and puddle/ground-water mark. Carry the river-state marker only through the flattened wet hair, dark heavy cassock and black doublet, while preserving the straight extended right arm and open palm.
