## Mandatory findings

1. The consequential rope is in the wrong, ambiguous state. At the landing it remains visibly wrapped around the large post and runs diagonally toward another post, while several dark branch-or-rope lines cross the water; it does not resolve as the single cut ferry rope hanging slack from the landing post with its severed end submerged. Remove every connecting rope segment between landing posts and every rope-like line or branch across the river. Keep one heavy rope only: bind its landward end to the principal thick post, show a plainly severed free length sagging vertically from that post into the near water, and hide its cut end below the surface. This primary marker must read immediately at thumbnail size.

2. The plate does not yet carry the specified quiet grey-dawn light and downstream weir state. The broad gold reflection and peach opening in the sky make the scene read as warm sunrise, while the large bright falling lip of the weir still reads as an active roaring cascade. Replace the gold and peach illumination with diffuse cool grey first light over low grey cloud, retaining brown calm water without a warm reflective band. Reduce the weir to a low, distant, quiet pale line with no prominent vertical fall or bright foaming foreground edge.

## Nonblocking observations

- The plate is one continuous landscape with no figures, horses, raft, lantern, lettering, labels, watermark, or borders.
- The black wet timber landing, brown water, wet grass, and far bank establish the intended location clearly.
- No approved character face or silhouette can be confused with this setting plate; costume-free face, strict-profile, reaction-expression, and re-entry tests are not applicable to a figure-free plate.
