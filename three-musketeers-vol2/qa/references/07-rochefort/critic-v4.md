## Mandatory findings

1. **The scar is placed on the wrong anatomical side in views (a)–(c).** Most decisively, view (c) is a strict profile with the nose pointing screen-left while the scar remains visible; under the plan's fixed convention, that is the anatomical right cheek, which must be entirely clean. Redraw view (c) as a true ninety-degree anatomical-left profile with the nose pointing screen-right and the single scar continuous from the left temple across the left cheekbone to the left mouth corner. Apply the same directional convention to views (a)–(c): whenever the scarred left plane is exposed, the nose must point screen-right; every right-cheek view must contain no scar or scar-like mark. Preserve only one scar and keep its endpoints identical in all four views.

2. **The face remains long and tapered instead of short and square.** Shorten the lower facial third in all four views, widen both mandibular angles until the jaw sides read nearly parallel, and terminate them in a broad, flatter chin. Carry the same shortened square skull through the strict profile and both full-length heads. This change is mandatory for the highest-risk Athos/Rochefort collision: Rochefort cannot share Athos's long-face read when costume, color, or the scar is obscured.

3. **The body reads tall and rangy rather than compact and muscular.** Reduce the apparent leg-to-torso elongation and broaden the shoulder girdle, chest, forearms, and thighs enough to produce a compact middle-height fighting build, while remaining far narrower than Porthos. Preserve weight back and chin up in views (b)–(d); do not solve this with a bulkier cloak alone.

## Nonblocking observations

- The sheet has four required views, including a full-length strict profile and a bareheaded hat-in-hand state. View (d) correctly holds the feathered hat in his right hand and keeps the other hand at the hilt.
- No lettering, labels, watermark, framing border, sling, blue cassock, white cross, red robe, or unlisted recurring prop appears. The sword is listed continuity.
- The thin moustache/no-beard rule is substantially present. Keep the chin and area below the lower lip as uninterrupted sallow skin in the redraw; do not let the existing dark modeling resolve into a lip tuft, goatee, or stubble marker.
- The black costume fittings stay neutral-to-black, and the red feather remains the only chromatic accent and a strong thumbnail marker.
- The reaction-expression test does not reveal an identity collapse: the heavy brows, moustache, scar, and raised-chin attitude remain usable. The four sheet poses offer limited emotional range, but the required view list does not demand a dedicated reaction pose.
