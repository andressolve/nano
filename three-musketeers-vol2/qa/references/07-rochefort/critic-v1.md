## Mandatory findings

1. **View (c) is the wrong anatomical profile.** It faces screen-left, exposing Rochefort's anatomical right facial plane, while still carrying the scar. Replace it with a true ninety-degree profile of his anatomical **left** side, nose pointing screen-right, with the scar continuous from the left temple across the visible left cheekbone to the left corner of the mouth. The right cheek must remain entirely unmarked in every right-cheek view.

2. **The facial-hair lock is not moustache-only.** A dark tuft/shadow is designed beneath the lower lip, especially legible in the large portrait, and the moustache reads heavier than the specified thin line. Remove every designed hair mark below the lower lip and on the chin, leaving a thin black moustache only. This is required both by Rochefort's lock and by the Athos and Aramis collision separators.

3. **The hair mass is too wavy and extends toward the shoulder.** Redesign it as straight black hair ending cleanly at the collar in all four views; remove the loose S-waves and any strands extending below the collar. This preserves the written separation from Athos's longer shoulder-length mass on re-entry and in costume-free crops.

4. **The red feather is not the only colour accent.** The sword guard, cloak clasp, chest fastenings, and belt hardware read as bright gold/brass. Render all hardware and the sword furniture as blackened steel or neutral near-black so the single red feather is clearly the only chromatic accent, as required by the costume value lock.
