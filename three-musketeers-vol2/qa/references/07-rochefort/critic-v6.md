## Mandatory findings

Redesign view (d) so Rochefort turns toward sheet-left and exposes his anatomical left cheek to the viewer; place the single scar only from the left temple across the left cheekbone to the left corner of the mouth, and leave the anatomical right cheek completely clean. Preserve the correct scar placement in the other views.

Recolour all of that hardware to matte near-black blackened steel in every view; retain no gold or bright-metal highlight.
