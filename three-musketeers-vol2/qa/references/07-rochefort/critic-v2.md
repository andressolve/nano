## Mandatory findings

This teaches both cheeks as scarred and violates the explicit clean-right-cheek Never. Preserve one continuous scar only, from left temple across left cheekbone to the left mouth corner.

Shorten the lower face and widen both mandibular angles so the jaw sides remain nearly parallel from cheek to jaw, then terminate in a broad, flatter chin rather than a point. Cut the back mass to a single clean collar-length terminal line, straighten the strands, and remove every lock that falls below the collar.
