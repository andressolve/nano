## Mandatory findings

1. **The strict profile is the wrong side and makes the scar anatomically wrong.** View (c) points the nose screen-left while displaying the scar on the exposed cheek; the required anatomical-left profile points the nose screen-right. Redesign view (c) as a strict ninety-degree profile with the nose pointing screen-right and the single scar continuous from the exposed left temple across the left cheekbone to the left mouth corner. Remove every scar or scar-like mark from the anatomical right cheek in all views, and keep the scar's endpoints identical wherever the left facial plane is visible.

2. **The face is too long and tapered for the structural lock.** Shorten the skull and especially the lower facial third; widen the jaw until its sides read nearly parallel; replace the current narrowing chin with a broad, flat chin; retain heavy black brows and black eyes. The result must be a short, broad, square hard face in the costume-free crop, not an elongated romantic face.

3. **The facial hair violates the clean-chin lock.** Remove the dark lip tuft/soul patch and every beard or chin-stubble mark. Reduce the moustache to one thin black line without the current heavy curled mass; the entire area below the lower lip and across the chin must be visibly clean skin in all four views.

4. **The hair mass is wavy and extends in loose locks instead of ending at the collar.** Straighten the black hair and terminate it in one blunt horizontal line at the collar. Remove waves, curls, feathered ends, and every lock falling below the collar, including in the bareheaded view.

5. **The full-length construction is too tall, narrow, and long-legged.** Shorten the apparent leg and torso proportions to a compact middle-height build; broaden the shoulders, chest, forearms, and thighs while keeping him far narrower than Porthos. Set the default stance unmistakably weight-back with chest open and chin raised, rather than a tall upright swordsman's read.

6. **Bright metal breaks the all-black value mass and the Never list.** Repaint every button, cloak clasp, buckle, belt fitting, sword guard, and other hardware as blackened steel or neutral near-black. The single red feather must remain the only colour accent; no gold or bright brass may survive.
