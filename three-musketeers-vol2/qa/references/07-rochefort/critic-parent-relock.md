## Nonblocking observations

1. **The mark under the lip in view (a).** It is a faint dark mark, not a goatee, and it does not weaken the beard-against-no-full-beard carrier against Athos's full beard. Because the image model draws what it is shown, the clause "no beard, no goatee, no lip tuft, no chin stubble" should travel verbatim into every page prompt with Rochefort in it. The ledger's "Never" line already provides it. The page critic should treat any tuft that grows toward a pointed beard on a page as an Athos-collision defect.

2. **View (d) cannot be checked for scar side.** In the soaked view there are pale streaks on the cheek, and I cannot tell rain from scar. View (d) is excluded from the lock and is never drawn in this volume. Since the whole sheet is still a generation input, any page showing his right cheek should have the scar side checked on its card. The ledger's "right-cheek profile is clean" clause covers this.

3. **The brief's checklist is out of date against the ledger.** The brief lists "square hard face" and "straight black hair to the collar." Ledger §7, which is the authority and which I judged against, says "high-cheekboned face of middle length" and "wavy, falling past the collar to the top of the shoulders." The sheet matches the ledger. Ledger line 24 also calls the Athos/Rochefort pair "dark, bearded, grave," which contradicts Rochefort's clean chin. Fixing that wording would keep it from leaking into prompts.

4. **The drawing style differs from the other sheets.** This sheet is darker and more textured, closer to painterly oil sketch, while the seven approved sheets are pen and wash. This is not an identity problem. Page prompts should keep the book's register paragraph controlling, so that his texture does not spread to other figures in shared panels.

5. **No height reference.** The sheet cannot show his height against Athos. That carrier lives only in the prompt text ("Athos a head taller"), and it must be stated in every page prompt where they share a panel.
