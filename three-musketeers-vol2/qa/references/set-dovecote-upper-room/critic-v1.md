## Mandatory findings

1. The primary story marker is in the wrong state: the iron stovepipe is rendered as a completely intact, closed tube running continuously from floor to ceiling, so the plate does not establish the broken acoustic passage through which the upstairs conversation reaches the room below. Redesign the pipe while keeping it as the dominant vertical marker: retain its entry through the plank floor, but show one unmistakably broken or disconnected open joint within the upper room, with a visible open mouth/gap large enough to read at thumbnail; do not represent the break as another closed collar or seam.

2. The view through the window shows the silhouette of nearby roofs or a large building with several lit windows, which makes the upper room read as part of a settled street or town rather than a lonely inn on a heath. Replace the exterior view with an empty blue-black night heath and distant low horizon, with no neighbouring roof mass, tower, or lit windows; keep the window itself dark so the candle remains the room's sole light accent.
