## Nonblocking observations

1. Parts of the street and gate studies use fine pen hatching in the stonework and timber. It is loose, not engraved, and this is the plate Volume I approved, so it stays. If any page leans toward engraving-style cross-hatching, the fix is the register paragraph in that page's prompt, not this plate.
2. The plate shows overcast, wet weather. The summer pages (1, 5–6) need "hazy, warm, dry" stated firmly in the packet. Otherwise the wet cobbles and rain-grey sky are likely to bleed into a summer page. This is already a per-plate caution and is noted here only to stress its importance.
3. The gate study shows daylight. P 52 is evening, drawn from words, as the caution says.
