# Critic cards

Reader-facing blocking criteria only. Three to eight per page. Criterion numbers
are stable defect signatures for retry routing. No prompt, audit, reference
manifest, version, history, panel-share rule, or type measurement ever appears
here. A finding is valid only when it states material reader harm and justifies
risking a complete-page redraw.

Format — header exactly `## Page N — card`, criteria `### Cn — title`:

## Page 1 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
The two title lines and the caption must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order (title, then caption), tail-free, with no extra text; a string that does not read is blocking, and a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes or apostrophes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue; the words and their order are what must match. Any balloon on the page is blocking.

### C2 — The turn does not land by eye
One spectacle image owns the page: four friends crossing a Paris bridge toward the reader. The eye must find three blue cassocks with white crosses and one shorter figure in ochre without blue; if the boy wears blue, or the four do not read as four, the caption's point is lost and it is blocking.

### C3 — Identity and separation
The boy reads as the beardless black-haired youth in ochre and a grey hat with a broken feather, leading a buttercup-yellow horse; the three read as a tall still bearded man, an enormous red-moustached man with red plumes, and a slim blond man. A boy with facial hair, a horse of another colour, or two Musketeers that read as the same man is blocking. Exact plume count and costume trim are nonblocking.

### C4 — Generation integrity
A duplicated friend, a fifth companion, a second yellow horse, a drawn sword, blood, or gross anatomy on a focal face or hand is blocking. Background crowd faces and house detail are not.
