numbers here are the approved script's (58 pages). `09-REFERENCE-PLAN.md`'s
"first appearance" column predates a one-page split at p26: trust the script.

**Two or at most three image inputs per page, never more.** The focal
characters of the dominant panel, plus a plate only when the place or the
object is the turn. Everyone else is drawn from words with lock and nearest
lookalike named.

**Porthos and Aramis appear on more than a third of the pages.** On every
page where either is drawn from words, his lock clause carries his three
structural carriers: Porthos — enormous barrel body, red-brown curls and a
huge waxed red-brown moustache, three red ostrich plumes; Aramis — slim,
blond curls to the collar, small blond moustache with a tiny tuft, white lace
collar. Athos drawn from words: tallest, long pale face, short dark pointed
beard, shoulder-length dark hair, one white plume, still.

## Staging rules from the script gate (carry them into prompts and cards)

- **No blood, no drawn wound, nobody shown hit, anywhere.** Blocking on every
  card that has a fight (pp 13, 31–33, 47–51). The bastion attackers run; the
  wall comes down on nobody (p 33 caption says so).
- **The lily (p 13):** a small dark fleur-de-lis brand on the back of her
  LEFT shoulder, seen where the left sleeve has torn open at the shoulder
  seam; the dress otherwise whole. Never on her sheet; from words only.
- **Athos's memory (p 16):** a young lord small and from behind riding away
  from a dark wood; one bare branch high in the foreground. **Never a rope,
  never a body, never a figure hanging.** Blocking.
- **The poisoned grass (p 20):** the wine darkens and blackens the grass where
  it falls; no smoke cloud, no dead animal.
- **The Red Dovecote (pp 22–23):** the upstairs room is shown as what Athos
  hears: a separate panel framed above, the stovepipe running up through both
  rooms so the reader sees the connection.
- **Constance's death (p 43):** storybook terms: her head on his shoulder,
  eyes closing, peaceful; the glass slipping onto the grass unbroken. No pain,
  no convulsion, no mark, no tears streaming. P 44: silent grief, the nuns at
  a distance.
- **The river (pp 47–51):** Milady is never shown in the water; she kneels on
  the raft. The chain of four (p 49): Athos waist-deep reaching, the boy
  gripping Athos's belt with both hands, Porthos gripping the boy, Aramis
  with one arm round the landing post gripping Porthos. P 51: the raft small,
  spinning toward the white line of the weir, one lantern point.
- **The arrest (p 53):** Rochefort's hand on the boy's shoulder; no chains, no
  drawn swords on the boy.
- **Safety filter (method/07 watch):** captivity, poison, death and fear are
  written in storybook words from the start ("closes her eyes", "goes very
  still"); never "dying", "poisoned body", "corpse", "bruised".
- **Two rings never confused:** the boy's (round black stone, right hand) and
  Milady's (flat square violet-black stone, left hand).
- **Milady vs Constance (pp 37–41):** Milady a head taller, pale gold
  ringlets bound up under a pushed-back black veil, dark widow's dress, ice-blue
  eyes; Constance small, round freckled face, dark curls under a white cap,
  dove grey with a white collar. Separation sentence on every page.
- **Milady vs Aramis (pp 47–51):** she has long ringlets far past the
  shoulder, no facial hair, a slate-grey cloak over plum; he has curls to the
  collar, a moustache, the blue cassock.
- **d'Artagnan vs Aramis in blue (pp 57–58):** black unruly hair in the eyes,
  beardless, grey hat with a broken feather vs blond curls, moustache, black
  hat with a white plume.
- **Athos vs Rochefort (pp 4–5 never together; p 53 together):** pointed beard
  and a head taller vs thin moustache, scar on the left cheek, black with one
  red feather.
- **Register emphasis (method/07, standing):** every page set in a dark or
  quiet interior carries a `### Register emphasis` section — the Cardinal's
  study (pp 4, 54, 55), Athos's room at night (pp 15–17), the Red Dovecote
  rooms (pp 22–24), the attic (pp 2, 57), the tavern (pp 11, 56), Milady's
  gold salon (pp 9, 12, 13 — Volume I lesson 10, gold rooms drift
  photographic), the cloister (pp 37–45), the tent (pp 26–27): brush-and-ink
  line on warm laid paper, opaque matte gouache, bold flat shadow masses, the
  location's locked light, no glossy surfaces, nothing gilded the plate does
  not show.
- **Owner tags** `TITLE CARD`, `CAPTION`, `PROSE FIELD 1`, `SOUND`, `PRINTED
  PAPER` and every speaker name are never lettered.
- **No counted story object above five.** The four friends are four; the
  "forty" attackers are a crowd (count nonblocking); the bottles, glasses and
  rings are one or two each.
- **p53 panel 3:** Athos puts the folded paper into the boy's open hand (staging carried from the script gate; no added text).
- **Speaker tags:** `TITLE CARD` on pp 1 and 58; `PRINTED PAPER` on p 30 is object text on the paper in Athos's hands — the one place the Cardinal's words are lettered on the paper itself.
