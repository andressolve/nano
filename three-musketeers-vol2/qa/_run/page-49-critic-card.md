## Page intent

## PAGE 49

A spectacle page, one great image. The black flood at night, lit by lightning: the loose ferry raft swings and spins out on the current, its lantern swaying, Milady kneeling on it. Athos stands waist-deep in the river, hatless, reaching toward her with his hand open. Behind him the boy grips Athos's belt with both hands; behind the boy Porthos grips the boy; and at the edge of the landing Aramis has one arm round the post and holds Porthos. A chain of four men reaching into the river. One caption across the storm says how they held on. The dominant turn is Athos wading in, held by a chain of his three friends. The reader turns because Athos's hand is out and she is close enough to take it.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 49 — card

### C1 — Transcription

The caption reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, with no extra text and no balloon; a caption that does not read is blocking, a caption that reads is not to be measured. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, and a regional spelling variant of the same word are nonblocking; the words and their order must match. The run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue is nonblocking.

### C2 — One spectacle image

A single image in the spectacle mode, where the eye finds Athos's open hand reaching toward the raft. Blocking if the page is cut into panels or the hand is lost.

### C3 — The chain of four

Exactly four men linked in the caption's order: Athos in the water, the boy holding his belt with both hands, Porthos holding the boy, Aramis holding Porthos and the post. The count carries the turn. Blocking if the chain breaks, the order is wrong, or a fifth man joins it.

### C4 — Identity in the flood

Athos hatless with the pointed beard; the boy beardless, black-haired, in ochre; Porthos enormous; Aramis slim and blond. Blocking if two of them read as the same man.

### C5 — Milady on the raft, nobody harmed

Milady stays kneeling on the raft, never in the water; nobody falls, is hurt, or bleeds. Blocking on any of these.
