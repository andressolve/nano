## Page intent

## PAGE 45

A dramatic page. The dominant turn: Athos takes the blame, the boy forgives him, and they ride. Athos crouches beside the boy, who says it was Milady and that he told her himself; Athos answers that he let her go at the Red Dovecote, and to blame him. In the large middle panel the boy looks at him a long moment and says Athos did not pour the wine either; Athos's hand is on his shoulder as he says she did, tells him to get up, and gives the road she took. Last, the boy lays Constance gently on the bench, stands and draws his sword; Athos turns it into a chase. The reader turns because they are riding after her. Subordinate: the bench and the cloister.

blocking only when it materially harms the reader event, exact text or

## Page 45 — card

### C1 — Dialogue transcription or ownership fails
All seven strings must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in causal order, each owned by the right mouth by tail and placement, with no extra text; a string that does not read is blocking, and a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue; the words and their order are what must match. A line placed directly under its speaker's previous balloon, or naming its addressee, is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — The turn does not land
The middle panel must own the page: the boy looking at Athos, Athos's hand on his shoulder; then the boy rising and drawing his sword. If the hand on the shoulder or the drawn sword is missing, it is blocking.

### C3 — Attribution of the three exchanges
The boy's confession and Athos's two lines; the boy's forgiveness and Athos's answer; the boy's threat and Athos's "Ride." Athos's lines read as the boy's, or the reverse, is blocking.

### C4 — Identity
The boy beardless, black hair in his eyes, ochre; Athos tall, long pale face, dark pointed beard, blue cassock. Constance on the bench still and peaceful, with no mark; a mark or a second drawn sword is blocking.

### C5 — Generation integrity
A duplicated principal, gross anatomy on a focal hand, or a broken sword is blocking. Background detail is not.
