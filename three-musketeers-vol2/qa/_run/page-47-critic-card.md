## Page intent

## PAGE 47

A dramatic page at the ferry landing in the rain. Milady stands on the flat ferry raft, hood back, a knife at its rope, and warns the four arriving on the black timbers not to take one step. The page belongs to the moment the boy, face wild and sword out, strides at her and Athos's arm across his chest stops him: Athos refuses to let him do this in the dark, alone, in a rage, because he once did the same and it came back. The boy shakes, his sword point drops, and he asks what they do with her; Athos answers that first she will hear what she did. The dominant turn is Milady on the ferry raft; Athos stops the boy's sword. The reader turns because four men are about to judge her and she has a knife on the rope.

**Location:** the ferry landing.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** The ferry landing in the rain: a flat ferry raft on its
rope, a lantern on a post. MILADY on the raft, hood back, a knife in her
hand at the rope. The four arriving on the black timbers of the landing.

> **MILADY:** "Stay where you are. The ferryman has run. Come one step onto this raft and I cut the rope."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** D'ARTAGNAN striding toward her, sword out, face
wild; ATHOS's arm across the boy's chest, stopping him.

> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "She killed Constance! Let me go!"

> **ATHOS:** "Not like this. In the dark, alone, in a rage. I did it like this once, and look what came back."

**Panel 3 — 20%.** The boy shaking, the sword point lowering.

> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "Then what do we do with her?"
>
> **ATHOS:** "First she hears what she did. Out loud. All of it."

**Why you turn the page:** four men are about to judge her, and she has a knife on the rope.

### C1 — Transcription

Every listed string reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in causal order, owned by the right mouth by tail and placement, with no extra text; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, and a regional spelling variant of the same word are nonblocking; the words and their order must match. The run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue is nonblocking. A line owned by placement, directly under its speaker's previous balloon or naming its addressee, is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — Athos stops the boy

The dramatic mode must land on the moment Athos's arm bars the boy's charge; the eye must find that moment as the page's centre. Blocking if the two read as fighting each other or the stop is not visible.

### C3 — Attribution across the exchange

Milady owns the warning; the boy owns "She killed Constance!" and the question; Athos owns both answers. Each must hang from its own speaker's mouth by tail. Blocking if Athos's lines could be read as the boy's or the reverse.

### C4 — Identity and Milady against Aramis

d'Artagnan beardless, black-haired, ochre, no cloak; Athos tallest with the pointed beard, hatless; Milady pale-gold ringlets, grey cloak over plum. Blocking if Milady could be taken for Aramis or for a man, or if she wears the widow's dress and veil.

### C5 — No harm, raft still tied

The knife is at the rope only; nobody is cut or hit, no blood. Blocking if any blood or wound appears or the rope is already cut on this page.
