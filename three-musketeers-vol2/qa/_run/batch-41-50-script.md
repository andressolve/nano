# Exact script — pages 41–50

Open only after the blind sequence read.

## PAGE 41 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the cloister at Béthune.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** A table in the cloister with a jug and two plain glasses.
MILADY pouring; CONSTANCE beside her looking toward the gate.

> **MILADY:** "Your Gascon is a lucky boy. To him."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** Close, from behind Milady: as she hands a glass
across, her thumb flicks open the dark stone of her ring, and a few grey
grains fall into the wine. Constance's face is turned to the gate.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left, over the stone)*
> "Constance did not see the ring open. She was watching the gate."

**Panel 3 — 20%.** Constance drinking; Milady already walking away toward the
back of the cloister.

> **CONSTANCE:** "To d'Artagnan."
>
> **MILADY:** "I'll go and meet them at the gate. Wait for me here."

**Why you turn the page:** the glass is empty, and Milady is not going to the gate.

## PAGE 42 · *spectacle*

**Location:** the gate of the convent at Béthune.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 100%.** The four bursting in through the convent's
front gate on horseback, the yellow horse first, two nuns stepping back
against the wall. Through the cloister arches beyond, far away and small, a
black carriage pulling out of the back gate.

> **CAPTION** *(lower edge, over the cobbles)*
> "They came through the gate at nine in the morning. At the back of the convent, a carriage was already going."

**Why you turn the page:** they are here, and someone has just left.

## PAGE 43 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the cloister at Béthune.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** D'ARTAGNAN running into the cloister. CONSTANCE on the
bench by the table, an empty glass in her lap, smiling at him, very pale.

> **CONSTANCE:** "I knew your horse. I told her nobody else would ride a horse that colour."
>
> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "Told who? Constance, who was here?"

**Panel 2 — dominant, 55%.** He is on his knees holding her; her head on his
shoulder; the glass slipping from her fingers onto the grass, unbroken.

> **CONSTANCE:** "A lady. She was hiding too. She was so kind to me. She had such pale hair."
>
> **CONSTANCE:** "You came. You promised, and you came."

**Panel 3 — 20%.** Her eyes closing, peaceful; his face. Behind them, in the
arch, Athos taking off his hat.

> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "Constance?"

**Why you turn the page:** she is gone, and the boy doesn't understand yet.

## PAGE 44 · *illustrated prose*

**Location:** the cloister at Béthune.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 70%.** A wide, silent picture: the boy kneeling in the
rose garden with Constance in his arms; Porthos standing with his hat
crushed in his two hands; Aramis on his knees with his small book open;
Athos in the arch, hat off. Two nuns far off along the cloister.

**Panel 2 — 30%.** Aramis's hand making the sign of the cross; the empty
glass on the grass.

> **PROSE FIELD 1** *(over the pale cloister wall, never over the figures)*
> "Nobody said anything for a long time.
>
> Porthos, who had something to say about everything, had nothing. Aramis, who was always about to become a priest and never did, knelt and opened his little book and said the prayer for the dead, very quietly, all the way to the end, without one mistake.
>
> d'Artagnan held her and did not let go. He was thinking that he had told a lady with pale hair about a convent in the north, because she had asked, and because she had let him in by the front door.
>
> The roses smelled of nothing at all."

**Why you turn the page:** someone has to say what happens now.

## PAGE 45 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the cloister at Béthune.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** ATHOS crouching beside the boy.

> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "It was Milady. And I told her, Athos. On the very first day. A convent in the north."
>
> **ATHOS:** "And I had her at the end of my sword at the Red Dovecote, and let her go."
>
> **ATHOS:** "If you need someone to blame, blame me."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 45%.** The boy looking at him for a long moment;
Athos's hand on the boy's shoulder.

> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "You didn't pour the wine either."
>
> **ATHOS:** "No. She did. Get up. The sisters saw her carriage take the north road, toward the river."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** The boy laying Constance gently down on the bench and
standing, drawing his sword.

> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "I'm going to kill her."
>
> **ATHOS:** "We're going to catch her. Ride."

**Why you turn the page:** they are riding after her, and the boy's sword is out.

## PAGE 46 · *illustrated prose*

**Location:** two — the north road in a storm, and the river bank.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 65%.** Night, lashing rain, lightning: four riders flat
out along a flooded road, the yellow horse in front.

**Panel 2 — 35%.** A black carriage tipped in a ditch by a river bank, its
door hanging open, empty; beyond it, through the rain, a lantern on a ferry
landing.

> **PROSE FIELD 1** *(over the storm sky, never over the riders)*
> "The storm came down at dusk and did not stop. They rode through it with their hats gone and the water running off them, following the marks of the carriage wheels until the marks became rivers too.
>
> An hour before midnight they found the carriage tipped in a ditch, its horses gone, its door open, nobody inside. The coachman had run.
>
> Ahead, through the rain, a lantern was swinging on a ferry by the river, and downstream, in the dark, a weir was roaring, where the whole river fell over a wall of stones."

**Why you turn the page:** she is at the river, and there is only one ferry.

## PAGE 47 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the ferry landing.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** The ferry landing in the rain: a flat ferry raft on its
rope, a lantern on a post. MILADY on the raft, hood back, a knife in her
hand at the rope. The four arriving on the black timbers of the landing.

> **MILADY:** "Stay where you are. The ferryman has run. Come one step onto this raft and I cut the rope."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** D'ARTAGNAN striding toward her, sword out, face
wild; ATHOS's arm across the boy's chest, stopping him.

> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "She killed Constance! Let me go!"
>
> **ATHOS:** "Not like this. In the dark, alone, in a rage. I did it like this once, and look what came back."

**Panel 3 — 20%.** The boy shaking, the sword point lowering.

> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "Then what do we do with her?"
>
> **ATHOS:** "First she hears what she did. Out loud. All of it."

**Why you turn the page:** four men are about to judge her, and she has a knife on the rope.

## PAGE 48 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the ferry landing.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Three of them in a row in the rain at the edge of the
landing, left to right: the boy, Porthos, Aramis.

> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "You killed Constance with a glass of wine, and smiled while she drank it."
>
> **PORTHOS:** "You sent poison to a boy, and wrote our names on it."
>
> **ARAMIS:** "You asked the Cardinal for a girl's life as your price, and he gave it to you."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** Athos alone, a step in front of the others,
quiet, rain running off his face; behind him Porthos and Aramis glancing at
each other.

> **ATHOS:** "And you lied to me about everything, the day I married you. That one is mine to forgive. The others aren't."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Milady, smiling, slashing the rope; the rope parting;
the raft lurching out into the flood with her on it.

> **MILADY:** "Then judge me from the bank, gentlemen."

**Why you turn the page:** the raft is loose, and the weir is roaring.

## PAGE 49 · *spectacle*

**Location:** the river.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 100%.** The black flood at night, lit by lightning. The
raft swinging out, spinning, its lantern swaying. Athos waist-deep in the
water, hatless, reaching toward it with his hand open. Behind him the boy,
gripping Athos's belt with both hands; behind the boy Porthos, gripping the
boy; and on the edge of the landing Aramis, one arm round the post, gripping
Porthos. A chain of four men reaching into the river.

> **CAPTION** *(upper edge, over the storm)*
> "Athos went into the river. d'Artagnan took his belt. Porthos took the boy. Aramis took Porthos and the post. Nobody let go."

**Why you turn the page:** Athos's hand is out, and she is close enough to take it.

---

## PAGE 50 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the river.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Athos's open hand, an arm's length from the edge of the
spinning raft, rain streaming off it.

> **ATHOS:** "Take my hand. You'll have a judge and a trial, in daylight. Not a rope in the dark."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 55%.** Milady kneeling on the raft beside the lantern,
looking at his hand; then up at his face.

> **MILADY:** "You hanged me once, and I lived. I won't owe you my life now."
>
> **MILADY:** "Let go of his belt, boy, and he comes with me. He'd drown for me. He always would."

**Panel 3 — 20%.** The boy, chest-deep behind Athos, gripping the belt
harder, his face set.

> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "Never."

**Why you turn the page:** she has refused the hand, and the boy has refused her.
