## Page intent

## PAGE 22

A dramatic page in the Red Dovecote, in two rooms joined by one broken stovepipe. Downstairs by the hearth, Porthos and Aramis play dice while Athos stands at the wall with his ear to the pipe and tells them to be quiet. The dominant turn: through the stovepipe Athos hears Milady's price, the boy and the girl at Béthune. The largest image is the room above, by one candle: the Cardinal in a plain cloak over his red, writing at a small table, and Milady across from him in a grey travelling cloak. He sends her to England; she names her price; he gives her the girl and tells her where the girl is. Then, downstairs again, Athos's still face beside the pipe and Porthos's dice stopped in his fist. The reader turns because Athos now knows where Constance is, and so does Milady.

## PAGE 22 · *dramatic*

**Location:** two — the common room of the Red Dovecote, and the room above it.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Downstairs by the hearth: Porthos and Aramis at dice;
Athos standing by the wall where a broken iron stovepipe runs up through the
ceiling, his ear near it.

> **ATHOS:** "Quiet. This stovepipe is broken. You can hear every word in the room upstairs."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** Upstairs, by one candle: THE CARDINAL at a
small table, a plain cloak over his red, writing; MILADY across from him in
a slate-grey hooded travelling cloak, hood back.

> **CARDINAL:** "You'll take ship for England from the north coast within the week, and do the work we spoke of."
>
> **MILADY:** "And my price, Eminence? The Gascon. And the girl he loves."
>
> **CARDINAL:** "The girl is shut away from the Queen, and no use to me now. She is in the convent at Béthune. Take her."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Downstairs: Athos's face beside the stovepipe; Porthos
with the dice stopped in his fist.

**Why you turn the page:** Athos now knows where Constance is, and so does Milady.
