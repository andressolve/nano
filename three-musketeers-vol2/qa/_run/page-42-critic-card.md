### C1 — Caption transcription fails
The one caption must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, tail-free, with no other text on the page; a caption that does not read is blocking, and a caption that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue; the words and their order are what must match.

### C2 — The turn does not land
One spectacle picture: the riders bursting in through an open front gate, the yellow horse first, and, small and far away through the arches, a black carriage leaving by the back. If the carriage cannot be found, or the yellow horse is not leading, it is blocking. The nuns' exact count is nonblocking.

### C3 — Identity of the riders
The boy reads as the beardless black-haired youth in ochre on the only yellow horse; the three behind read as three different Musketeers in blue (tall and bearded, huge with red plumes, slim and blond). A second yellow horse, or the boy in blue, is blocking.

### C4 — Generation integrity
A duplicated principal, gross anatomy on a focal horse or rider, or a balloon anywhere is blocking. Hoof and harness detail are not.
