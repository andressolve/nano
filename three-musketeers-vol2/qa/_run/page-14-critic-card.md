### C1 — Caption transcription fails
The one caption reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, with no extra text anywhere (no sign on the cart or the house). ### C2 — The escape does not land
One image: the boy in mid-air out of the tall window, tangled in plum curtain, falling toward a hay cart, owning the page; the mode is spectacle. If he reads as standing, already landed, or too small to find, the turn fails.

### C3 — Identity
The falling figure is the beardless black-haired boy in ochre with the long sword. At the window, small, a man in black with a red feather and a pale gold head behind him. A blond or bearded falling figure is blocking.

### C4 — Focal integrity
The boy's body, face and sword hand are whole; nobody is shown hurt. Carter and square detail are nonblocking.
