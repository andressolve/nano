> "Nobody said anything for a long time.
>
> Porthos, who had something to say about everything, had nothing. Aramis, who was always about to become a priest and never did, knelt and opened his little book and said the prayer for the dead, very quietly, all the way to the end, without one mistake.
>
> d'Artagnan held her and did not let go. He was thinking that he had told a lady with pale hair about a convent in the north, because she had asked, and because she had let him in by the front door.

## Materiality threshold

The critic is a release gate, not a defect collector. A visible issue is
blocking only when it materially harms the reader event, exact text or
attribution, named or focal identity, consequential continuity or object state,
focal generation integrity, or the dominant dramatic relationship. Every
correction replaces the complete page. Minor background, cosmetic, tiny-prop,
geometry, colour, texture, and prompt-fidelity observations are nonblocking.
Omit them. Numeric panel shares and lettering sizes are builder construction
guidance, never critic measurements.

Each numbered criterion is also its stable retry signature. Cite it only when
the reader harm and redraw justification are concrete.

A lettered speaker name, label, or tag anywhere on the page is extra text and
blocks under the transcription criterion, whatever else reads correctly:
finished pages carry dialogue, captions, prose, and sound cues only.
A sound cue whose letters and order match the script reads correctly; the run
length of a repeated letter in it is nonblocking and never counted.

### C1 — Prose transcription fails
All four prose paragraphs must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order, in one prose field, with no balloon, caption, or other text; a sentence that does not read, is altered, dropped, doubled, or reordered is blocking, and a paragraph that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes or apostrophes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue; the words and their order are what must match.

### C2 — The mode or the turn does not land
Illustrated prose over one wide, silent picture: the boy holding her, Porthos crushing his hat, Aramis kneeling with the open book, Athos bareheaded in the arch; a small panel of the sign of the cross and the empty glass. If any of the four friends is missing or the field covers a figure, it is blocking.

### C3 — Identity of the four
Boy beardless and black-haired in ochre; Porthos enormous with the red-brown moustache; Aramis slim, blond, small moustache; Athos tallest with a dark pointed beard. Two friends who cannot be told apart is blocking.

### C4 — Tone
A mark on Constance, tears streaming, or a broken glass is blocking. Distant nuns are nonblocking in number.

### C5 — Generation integrity
A duplicated principal or gross anatomy on a focal face or hand is blocking. Roses and stone are not.
