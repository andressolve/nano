## Page intent

## PAGE 10

Illustrated prose. Time passes in one page: the boy goes back to Milady's house every day, and the prose tells what he gave her — his stories, his father, his sword, his horse, his three friends — because nobody grand had ever wanted to hear them. The dominant image is the boy at evening running up the steps of her tall house with a bunch of flowers while the footman is already opening the door: he is happy, and he is let in. Below, smaller and quieter, the tavern table where three Musketeers sit beside an empty fourth chair and no longer look at the door. The dominant turn is that he goes back every day, and at the tavern there is an empty chair. The reader turns because his friends have stopped waiting for him. The footman is scenery; nobody speaks.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 10 — card

### C1 — Prose transcription fails
All three paragraphs of the prose field read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once each, in order, with no extra text anywhere (no balloon, no label, no sign). A paragraph that does not read is blocking; text that reads is not to be measured. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue are nonblocking; the words and their order are what must match. No object on this page carries scripted words.

### C2 — The turn does not land
The eye must find the boy, glad, running up the steps of the tall house with flowers as the door opens for him, and then the empty chair at the tavern table beside three friends. If the empty chair cannot be found, or the friends read as waiting eagerly at the door, the turn fails. The mode is illustrated prose: the prose field sits over plain sky, never over the boy.

### C3 — Identity
The boy is the beardless black-haired youth in ochre with no cloak. The three at the table read as three distinct Musketeers in blue: one tall with a dark pointed beard, one enormous with a red-brown waxed moustache, one slim and blond. A fourth seated Musketeer, or the boy in the tavern, is blocking.

### C4 — Focal integrity
The boy's face and the hand with the flowers are whole; a duplicated boy or a fused footman is blocking. Square and tavern texture are not.
