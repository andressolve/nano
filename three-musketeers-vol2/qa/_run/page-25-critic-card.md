## Materiality threshold

The critic is a release gate, not a defect collector. A visible issue is
blocking only when it materially harms the reader event, exact text or
attribution, named or focal identity, consequential continuity or object state,
focal generation integrity, or the dominant dramatic relationship. Every
correction replaces the complete page. Minor background, cosmetic, tiny-prop,
geometry, colour, texture, and prompt-fidelity observations are nonblocking.
Omit them. Numeric panel shares and lettering sizes are builder construction
guidance, never critic measurements.

Each numbered criterion is also its stable retry signature. Cite it only when
the reader harm and redraw justification are concrete.

A lettered speaker name, label, or tag anywhere on the page is extra text and
blocks under the transcription criterion, whatever else reads correctly:
finished pages carry dialogue, captions, prose, and sound cues only.
A sound cue whose letters and order match the script reads correctly; the run
length of a repeated letter in it is nonblocking and never counted.

### C1 — Transcription fails
Any of the 4 prose paragraphs is missing, altered, duplicated, reordered, or unreadable from the 600 × 900 proof, or extra text or balloon appears. A string that reads is never measured. Nonblocking, since words and order are what must match: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant, the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue. A line directly under its speaker's previous balloon, or naming its addressee, is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — The carriage going north does not land
One image must plainly own the page by eye: the small black carriage far up the straight road at dawn and Athos on the rise watching, the paper in his hand. It must read as illustrated prose, the field in the sky, not over the carriage or Athos.

### C3 — The boy is ready
The second image must show the boy on his feet buckling on his sword with Athos in the tent, alone together. The boy sitting or idle blocks.

### C4 — Identity and absences
Athos tallest, dark pointed beard, blue; the boy beardless, black hair, ochre, no cloak. No yellow horse, and no Milady, Cardinal, Porthos or Aramis; any present blocks.
