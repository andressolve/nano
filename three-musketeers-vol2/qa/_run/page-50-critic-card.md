## Page intent

## PAGE 50

A dramatic page on the river. Athos's open hand reaches toward the edge of the spinning raft as he offers her a judge and a trial in daylight. The page belongs to Milady kneeling on the raft beside the lantern, looking at his hand and then up into his face, refusing it: he hanged her once and she lived, and she will not owe him her life. Then she turns on the boy and tells him to let go of the belt so that Athos goes with her. Last, the boy, chest-deep behind Athos, grips the belt harder, face set, and says never. The dominant turn is Milady refusing Athos's hand; the boy refusing to let go. The reader turns because she has refused the hand and the boy has refused her.

### C1 — Transcription

Every listed string reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in causal order, owned by the right mouth by tail and placement, with no extra text; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, and a regional spelling variant of the same word are nonblocking; the words and their order must match. The run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue is nonblocking. A line owned by placement, directly under its speaker's previous balloon or naming its addressee, is owned even if its tail wanders; Milady's second line, which names "boy", is owned by placement.

### C2 — Milady refuses the hand

Milady kneeling by the lantern, looking from the hand to his face, must be the image the eye finds, in the dramatic mode. Blocking if the hand panel or the boy panel owns the page.

### C3 — Attribution

Athos owns the offer, Milady both refusals, the boy "Never." Each must hang from its own speaker's mouth by tail. Blocking if a Milady line could be read as Athos's or if "Never." floats unowned.

### C4 — Identity

Milady with pale ice-blue eyes, pale-gold ringlets and a grey cloak over plum; Athos hatless with the pointed beard; the boy beardless and black-haired, gripping the belt. Blocking if Milady drifts toward Aramis or any face is malformed.

### C5 — Milady on the raft, nobody harmed

She kneels on the raft, never in the water; no noose and no hanging rope shown, no blood. Blocking on any of these. The ferry's own rope (cut and trailing, or coiled on the raft) is the story's rope and passes.
