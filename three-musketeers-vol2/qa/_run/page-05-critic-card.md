### C1 — Transcription fails
The caption must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, tail-free, with no other text on the page; a caption that does not read is blocking, and one that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes or apostrophes (including the single quotes round 'his friends'), a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue; the words and their order are what must match. Any balloon is blocking.

### C2 — The turn lands by eye
One spectacle image owns the page: the boy on the yellow horse leaning out to seize the bolting carriage horse's bridle, the black carriage with plum curtains, the crowd scattering. Rochefort must be findable, small, watching from a balcony, so the reader sees it was arranged; if he is missing or the rescue does not read as the dominant action, it is blocking.

### C3 — Identity
The boy beardless in ochre without blue; the horse buttercup-yellow; the carriage horses dark; Rochefort in black with a red feather. Rochefort reading as one of the crowd, or the boy's horse not yellow, is blocking. Which cheek of Rochefort shows at this distance is nonblocking unless a scar plainly sits on the right.

### C4 — Generation integrity
Nobody hit or trampled, no blood. A visible Milady at the window, a duplicated boy, or a gross anatomy failure on the boy's hands at the bridle is blocking. Crowd faces and spilled fruit are not.
