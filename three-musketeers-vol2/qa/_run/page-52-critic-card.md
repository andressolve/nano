## Page intent

## PAGE 52

An illustrated-prose page at grey dawn. The page belongs to the wide, quiet image: the river brown and calm, the cut ferry rope hanging slack into the water with nothing on its end, and the four sitting on the bank in their wet clothes with the horses behind them, the yellow horse among them. A smaller panel shows Athos alone at the water's edge, looking downstream. The prose field sits in the grey sky and carries the morning: the empty river, the boy's cold and tiredness and wish for Constance, her burial under the convent roses, and Athos's one word, which the boy at last understands. The dominant turn is dawn: the empty river, Constance buried, Athos's "Good." The reader turns because they have to go back to Paris, where the Cardinal is waiting.

## Materiality threshold

The critic is a release gate, not a defect collector. A visible issue is
blocking only when it materially harms the reader event, exact text or
attribution, named or focal identity, consequential continuity or object state,
focal generation integrity, or the dominant dramatic relationship. Every
correction replaces the complete page. Minor background, cosmetic, tiny-prop,
geometry, colour, texture, and prompt-fidelity observations are nonblocking.
Omit them. Numeric panel shares and lettering sizes are builder construction
guidance, never critic measurements.

Each numbered criterion is also its stable retry signature. Cite it only when
the reader harm and redraw justification are concrete.

A lettered speaker name, label, or tag anywhere on the page is extra text and
blocks under the transcription criterion, whatever else reads correctly:
finished pages carry dialogue, captions, prose, and sound cues only.
A sound cue whose letters and order match the script reads correctly; the run
length of a repeated letter in it is nonblocking and never counted.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 52 — card

### C1 — Transcription

Every scripted prose paragraph reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order, with no extra text and no balloon; a paragraph that does not read is blocking, a paragraph that reads is not to be measured. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes or apostrophes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, and a regional spelling variant of the same word are nonblocking; the words and their order must match. The run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue is nonblocking.

### C2 — The quiet dawn owns the page

The wide dawn image must be the image the eye finds, in the illustrated-prose mode, with the prose field in the sky and never across the four. Blocking if the prose covers the figures or the page reads as night or storm.

### C3 — The empty river

The cut rope hangs slack into calm brown water; no raft, no lantern, no Milady. Blocking if any of them appears.

### C4 — The four and the yellow horse

Four friends sit on the bank with the yellow horse among the horses; Athos stands alone at the water in the second panel. Blocking if the yellow horse is missing or Athos cannot be told from the others; the exact number of horses is nonblocking.
