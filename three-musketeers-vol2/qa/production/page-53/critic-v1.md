The dominant middle panel belongs to the charged confrontation between the young rider and the black-clad envoy: the envoy recites their prior encounters and declares that this sixth meeting comes with an order. The page turns when the young rider accepts the solo reckoning and asks the blue-tabarded, white-feathered companion for the Cardinal's paper; the companion hands it over as something always meant for the young rider to finish.
