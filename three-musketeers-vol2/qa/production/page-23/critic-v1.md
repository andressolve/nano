## Findings

### Finding C4
- Observation: In the last panel, Athos is alone at the foot of the dark stair and looking up at the light under the door, but neither hand is on his sword hilt; the hilt hangs untouched at his left hip.
- Material reader harm: The missing hand-on-hilt state removes the scripted physical cue that his secret ascent is an armed, dangerous confrontation, so the final beat reads as watchful movement rather than the intended threat toward the woman he once hanged.
- Redraw justification: This is a required focal state in the final turn and its sole visible cue of Athos’ imminent armed intent, making the harm substantial enough to risk replacing the complete page.
