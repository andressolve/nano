## Findings

### Finding C1
- Observation: The first balloon visibly reads “D’Artagnan, a whole month, and nobody in Paris has tried to kill me. Not once!” The required opening string begins “Aramis, a whole month...” instead.
- Material reader harm: The boy's first line addresses himself rather than the swordsman he is lunging at, making the opening exchange semantically confused and failing the exact scripted text at the page's dominant focal moment.
- Redraw justification: This is a clear, fully legible wrong word that changes the named addressee and the meaning of the first exchange; it materially harms focal identity and exact dialogue, so accepting it would preserve a conspicuous story error.
