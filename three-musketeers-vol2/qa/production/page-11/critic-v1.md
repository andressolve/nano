## Findings

### Finding C1
- Observation: In the dominant closing panel, “I don’t know that name.” is placed above and to the left of “Lady de Winter.” and therefore reads before the name it answers; “Her name.” also sits to the left with its top above the description balloon, so the five-line exchange does not present the required causal order.
- Material reader harm: The central reveal is reversed: Athos appears to deny knowing an unnamed woman before D'Artagnan identifies her, breaking the interrogation's cause and effect and the page-turn beat.
- Redraw justification: The defect is embedded in the balloon layout of the page's dominant dramatic panel and disrupts the page's decisive story event, making the risk of replacing the complete page proportionate.
