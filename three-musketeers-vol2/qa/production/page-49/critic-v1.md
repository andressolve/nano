VERDICT: REVISE

## Blind read
A storm-driven river rescue fills the page. A central dark-haired man stands in the flood and reaches toward a woman kneeling on a loose landing or raft. Behind him, a dark-haired boy in ochre grips him; farther back, a large red-haired man wraps an arm around a post while clasping the hand of a blond man on the near dock. The central reach across the water owns the page. The turn is the attempt to extend a chain from the dock to the stranded woman, but the visible handholds do not make the narrated chain continuous.

## Visible text

## Findings

### Finding C3
- Observation: The enormous red-haired man holds the post and clasps the blond man's hand, while no grip connects him to the boy; the blond man holds the red-haired man but does not hold the post. The rear links therefore do not show Porthos holding the boy and Aramis holding both Porthos and the post.
- Material reader harm: The visible chain breaks between the boy and Porthos and assigns the anchoring hold to the wrong man, so the caption's causal order and the page's central rescue mechanism do not land.
- Redraw justification: The four-man chain is the dominant dramatic relationship and carries the turn; losing one link and reversing the anchoring action materially changes the reader event, outweighing the risk of replacing the page.
