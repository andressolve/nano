## Blind read
In rain on the river, a hatless, pointed-bearded man reaches from the water toward the raft and offers the kneeling blonde woman a lawful trial instead of a secret hanging. The dominant middle panel belongs unmistakably to the woman beside the lantern: she refuses his hand because he once hanged her, then orders the black-haired boy behind him to release his grip so the man will drown with her. In the final panel, the boy grips the man harder and refuses. The page turns from the woman's rejection of rescue to the boy's defiance of her command.
