## Blind read
Across the river, the Cardinal receives Rochefort in his red study after Rochefort has failed four times to stop the boy. Rochefort asks for ten men, but the Cardinal refuses and turns instead to Milady. Milady owns the page: she dominates the central panel as the Cardinal orders her to discover where the girl goes and remove the boy, then she causes the turn by arranging for her horses to bolt on the river road at noon so the boy will be drawn into stopping them.
