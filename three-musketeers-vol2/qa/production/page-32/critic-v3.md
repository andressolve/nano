## Blind read
An ochre-clad youth at the parapet warns that forty attackers are approaching. The enormous red-clad, red-plumed musketeer turns the cracked wall into their answer, and the dominant lower panel belongs to him and the three companions straining together as the still-upright wall begins to break and tip outward. The change is from sighting the incoming force to setting the wall in motion against it.

## Visible text
"There are forty of them this time!" — spoken by the ochre-clad youth at upper left; the balloon tail points to his mouth. No name is lettered.

"Then that wall is in their way. Help me give it to them." — spoken by the enormous red-clad, red-plumed musketeer at upper right; the balloon tail points to his mouth. No name is lettered.

"All for one – and push!" — spoken by the enormous red-clad, red-plumed musketeer pushing the wall in the lower panel; the balloon tail points down toward him. No name is lettered.
