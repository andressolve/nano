## Blind read
Athos has arranged a discreet meeting in which D’Artagnan and his companions receive a blonde woman whose brother died in battle. The woman owns the page emotionally, especially in the final panel: the men offer honor, remembrance, and further service, but her final response turns the exchange from mourning and obligation into solace because their presence proves that her brother was not alone when he died.
