## Blind read
On a rain-lashed dock, a woman holding the raft rope warns the four pursuing men that the ferryman has fled and that she will cut them off if they step onto the raft. The page is owned by the older, mustached man in the blue cloak: when the furious young swordsman tries to rush the woman over Constance's death, he physically stops him and refuses an isolated, rage-driven killing because he has already lived through its consequences. The turn is from immediate vengeance to deliberate reckoning: the young man asks what they should do, and the older man decides that the woman must first hear all of her deeds spoken aloud.
