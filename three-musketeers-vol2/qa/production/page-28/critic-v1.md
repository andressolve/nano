## Blind read
Porthos, enormous atop a barrel, publicly challenges the surrounding army to bet against the four eating breakfast for an hour in the enemy bastion. His challenge owns the page. Coins then rain into an upturned helmet as the whole army takes the bet. Below, the boy recognizes that nobody can listen to them out there, and Athos reveals the larger turn: everybody will see the act, so the King can reward what the army witnesses.
