## Blind read
Two mounted men overlook a large encampment and a distant fortified city. The black-clad rider reports that the Three Musketeers and the Gascon are moving again. The cardinal owns the page: he explains that the tall one may have overheard him name Béthune and concludes that the King will give the four men leave to ride there. When the black-clad rider says the boy would sooner die than join the cardinal, the cardinal turns the discovery into action by ordering Milady to arrange matters and sending the rider north to Rochefort with warning that four men are coming.
