## Retell of the story so far

D’Artagnan is a poor eighteen-year-old Gascon who came to Paris on a yellow horse and became the friend of the three Musketeers Athos, Porthos, and Aramis. The four saved the Queen from a scheme involving the Cardinal, and the Queen rewarded D’Artagnan with a ring. He also loves Constance, the Queen’s servant. The Cardinal has learned his name and remembers him.

A month later, D’Artagnan still longs for a blue Musketeer cloak. His three friends tease him but are also waiting for him to join them. Across Paris, the Cardinal refuses to let Rochefort make a fifth attempt against D’Artagnan. He gives the problem to Milady instead: she is to use Constance and the Queen’s secrets to remove the boy. Milady arranges for a runaway carriage on the river road, counting on D’Artagnan to intervene.

A false note from his friends draws D’Artagnan to the road. Rochefort releases the carriage horses, and D’Artagnan alone moves to stop them. The beautiful passenger introduces herself as Lady de Winter, called Milady in Paris, and invites him to visit her on Thursday by the kitchen door.

That night Constance tells D’Artagnan she must flee at once because the Cardinal’s people are asking about the Queen’s letters. The Queen is hiding her in an unspecified convent in the north. D’Artagnan wants to follow, but Constance makes him promise to wait until she sends word that she is safe. When he mentions Milady, Constance warns him that everyone knows Milady’s carriage and that nobody knows who pays for it; ladies who behave kindly may have dangerous motives.

D’Artagnan nevertheless visits Milady on Thursday. She flatters him and draws out the story of the Queen’s diamonds, then asks whether there is a girl in his life. He admits there is and reveals that she has gone to a northern convent, though he does not know which one. He returns on Friday, Saturday, and every day afterward. Milady listens to him in a way no grand person ever has, and he becomes absorbed in her. He skips training and meals with his friends. By the end, he is bringing Milady flowers while Athos, Porthos, and Aramis sit beside his empty chair and have stopped expecting him.

## Cast

- **D’Artagnan:** A lean, dark-haired eighteen-year-old in a battered feathered hat and patched mustard-brown clothes, usually carrying a sword. He is the eager Gascon newcomer, the fourth friend of the Musketeers, Constance’s sweetheart, and now Milady’s target. He is brave and quick to help, but pride, romance, and the wish to be taken seriously make him easy to steer.
- **Athos:** A tall, dark-haired, bearded Musketeer in a blue cloak over black clothing. He is the grave, restrained member of the trio and the one D’Artagnan asks to tell the others that he will not wait forever for his cloak.
- **Porthos:** A large, jovial, red-haired and red-feathered Musketeer in blue and red. He boasts that the King gave him his cloak after he did something no one in France can forget.
- **Aramis:** A slim, blond Musketeer with a pale feather in his black hat. He spars with D’Artagnan while thinking about God and appears the most refined and contemplative of the friends.
- **Constance:** A young, curly brown-haired woman in a servant’s cap and plain gray-white dress. She serves the Queen, loves D’Artagnan, carries sensitive knowledge, and is sent into hiding because the Cardinal’s people are looking for the Queen’s letters.
- **The Cardinal:** An older, thin-faced man in red robes and a skullcap, shown with a gray cat. He is the powerful antagonist pursuing the Queen’s secrets and trying to remove D’Artagnan.
- **Rochefort:** A lean, dark-haired man with a narrow mustache, dressed in black. He is the Cardinal’s operative, has already tried four times to stop D’Artagnan, and executes the runaway-carriage setup for Milady.
- **Milady / Lady de Winter:** A beautiful, fair-skinned blonde with long curls, a purple gown, and a dark ring. She is an English noblewoman in Paris and the Cardinal’s chosen agent. She uses apparent gratitude, intimacy, and sustained attention to win D’Artagnan’s confidence and extract information.
- **The Queen:** Not shown directly in these pages. She is the ruler whom the four friends previously saved, Constance’s mistress, and the person whose secrets the Cardinal is pursuing.

## Causal chain

The four friends foil the Cardinal and save the Queen → the Cardinal learns and remembers D’Artagnan’s name → Rochefort fails four times to stop him → the Cardinal assigns Milady to exploit Constance and the Queen’s secrets → Milady and Rochefort stage a runaway carriage, using a forged note to place D’Artagnan in its path → D’Artagnan’s courage makes him rescue Milady and accept her invitation → Constance is forced into hiding and warns him about Milady → D’Artagnan visits anyway → Milady’s flattery gets him to reveal the northern-convent lead → repeated visits pull him away from training and from the three Musketeers → his friends stop waiting for him while Milady gains influence over him.

## What I expect next

Milady will use D’Artagnan’s northern-convent clue to hunt for Constance and the Queen’s secrets. D’Artagnan will continue mistaking manipulation for affection until the danger to Constance, or Milady’s connection to the Cardinal, becomes visible.
