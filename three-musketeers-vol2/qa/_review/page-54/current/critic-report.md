## Blind read
D'Artagnan confronts the cardinal across his desk. The cardinal reports the death of a woman from his service and the witnesses who saw four men at the scene; D'Artagnan answers by naming the cardinal's responsibility for Constance's poisoning, rejects the cardinal's attempts to distance himself from the killing, and finally lays a signed authorization on the desk between them. D'Artagnan owns the page, especially its dominant final panel. The turn is his refusal to use the cardinal's paper as lifelong protection: instead of hiding behind the authorization, he returns it and makes their responsibility explicit.
