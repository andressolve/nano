## Blind read
At night outside a doorway, a young swordsman excitedly tells a maid that a great lady invited him to her house by the front door. The maid reveals that she is leaving at first light on the Queen's orders because the Cardinal's people are hunting the carrier of the Queen's letters; she will be hidden in an unspecified northern convent and deliberately cannot tell him where. He promises to come if she names it, but she explains that not knowing protects her. The maid then asks which lady invited him. When he identifies Lady de Winter—Milady—the exchange turns into her warning that the apparently kind lady may have hidden motives. The large middle panel owns the page through the maid's protective secrecy and their joined hands; the bottom-panel identification of Milady triggers the final warning.
