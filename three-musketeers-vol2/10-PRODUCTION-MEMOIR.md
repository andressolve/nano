## Part I — The decisions that made the book

**The engine: the woman nobody suspects, and the friend who knew.** Milady
enters through the boy's want from Volume I — to be let in — and the front
door is the trap. The reader knows who she is from p4; the boy doesn't until
p13. Athos's secret is the second engine: "Which shoulder?" / "Then she isn't
dead." Every critic named that turn.

**Phase 0 story rulings (delegated authority).** Constance dies, quietly, in
the boy's arms ("You promised, and you came.") — the one person who suspected
Milady from the start, killed with the help of the boy's own careless talk.
Milady is not executed: at a flooded ferry she cuts herself loose, and Athos,
held by a chain of his three friends, offers his hand and a trial in daylight;
she refuses, and the river takes her. Athos's hanging of his wife is told
once, in his voice, and never drawn in use. The ending is the blue cloak,
earned by giving back the Cardinal's blank pardon unasked.

**Cuts that paid.** Milady's whole English half (de Winter, Felton,
Buckingham's death), Kitty, de Wardes, the executioner of Lille and the night
trial, every servant, the King, the Queen and Tréville. Buckingham did not
return. Eight locks, seven of them returning.

## Part II — What the script critics taught (7 rounds)

Round 1 (16 findings, "alive"): the three Musketeers had to be named to their
faces on p3; Aramis needed a want (a great lady's front door is why he is
going into the Church) instead of a tic; Rochefort named on p4; the Cardinal's
want made consistent; Athos's silence about the carriage challenged; the
bastion had to carry the boy's urgency; the paper's leverage enacted, and the
boy's return of it made his own choice. Round 2 (8): **a new p26** — the boy
confronts Athos for letting Milady go ("Hate me on the road, if you like"),
which made "You didn't pour the wine either" at Béthune a payoff; the
Cardinal's study had to carry Constance. Round 3 (1): the commission had to be
named as an officer's rank. Round 4 (2): a quotation nobody said; two friends
appearing and vanishing across a page turn. Round 5 approved; three of its
notes were taken as storytelling gains (the boy's broken promise to wait for
the Queen's word made felt; a hollow "Good."; "half a day ahead"), which
reopened the gate — and round 6 found that the added "Good." had pushed the
boy's choice to stay off the page. Round 7 approved. **Lesson: a note taken
after approval is a new line, and new lines carry new gaps; take them only
when they are worth a round.**

## Part V — For Volume III

Threads this volume leaves:

- **The boy is a Musketeer**, an officer, in blue — the four are equals now,
  and the Cardinal signed the paper.
- **The Cardinal** has lost his second hand and knows the boy cannot be
  bought; he would rather have him "in France than under it".
- **Rochefort** has counted six. He arrested the boy with an order and lost
  anyway.
- **Athos** said "Good." at the river, and the boy finally understood it.
- **Constance** is buried at Béthune under the roses; the boy lights her lamp
  himself.
- Source ground (Dumas's *Twenty Years After*, as evidence only): the four
  grown and apart, Mordaunt (Milady's son), the English civil war, the
  Fronde. Milady's son hunting the four who stood on the bank is the natural
  engine.
