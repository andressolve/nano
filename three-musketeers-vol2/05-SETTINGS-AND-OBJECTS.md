# Settings and Objects

**World palette:** Volume I's, unchanged — brush-ink line with warm gouache
washes, lit like a lantern film. Two colours still own the book: **Musketeer
blue** and **the Cardinal's red**. d'Artagnan's ochre-yellow belongs to him
and his horse. This volume adds a fourth: **Milady's plum** — deep violet
silk with silver, which appears nowhere she is not. Each location is a
variation on the palette except three that must look like different worlds:
Milady's salon (gold candlelight and plum, too beautiful), the camp at La
Rochelle (dun earth, smoke and grey sea), and the river (black, white water,
one lantern).

Each location names one restrained accent that does dramatic work.

---

## Locations

### 1 · Paris — streets, the river road, the bridge · pp 1, 5, 6, 53, 58
Volume I's Paris (`refs/parents/set-paris.png`): grey-blue slate roofs, the
brown river, bridges with houses on them, mud, crowds. Summer light this
time, hazy and warm. On p 53, the city gate at evening.
**Accent:** blue cloaks and red cloaks in the same street, never touching.

### 2 · The field behind the Carmelite convent · p 3
Volume I's (`refs/parents/set-carmelite-field.png`): the long grass, the
yellow wall, the big tree, the trough. Late afternoon.
**Accent:** the trough, where the yellow horse drinks.

### 3 · The Cardinal's study · pp 4, 54–55
Volume I's (`refs/parents/set-cardinal-study.png`): red hangings, a black
desk, one candle, maps, one chair, the grey cat. No window, no daylight.
**Accent:** the cat; the hand on it never stops, until p 55.

### 4 · The house — Constance's door, the stair, the attic · pp 2, 7–8, 57
Volume I's (`refs/parents/set-house.png`): the street door with the lantern
bracket, the steep stair, the attic with one window, a bed, a table, a
candle, a nail. On p 8, dawn, a coach at the door. On p 57, empty downstairs.
**Accent:** the stair. Every scene here is still played across it.

### 5 · Milady's house · pp 9, 11–14
A tall stone house on a square: a front door with a footman, a salon in gold
candlelight with plum hangings, a gilt mirror, a harpsichord, tall windows
on the square, and behind a half-drawn curtain a small cabinet where she
talks business. Warm, gold, beautiful, and slightly too much. The place says:
you have been let in.
**Accent:** the tall window over the square — the way in that he goes out by.

### 6 · Athos's room · pp 15–17
Volume I's (`refs/parents/set-athos-room.png`): whitewash, one chair, one
table, one bed, the sword on the wall. Night, one candle.
**Accent:** the empty chair the boy is not invited to take; Athos sits in it.

### 7 · The camp before La Rochelle · pp 18–20, 25–27, 34–35
A ring of trenches and earthworks on dun ground, tents, smoke, cannon, the
grey sea beyond, and the walled city on its harbour. A hill behind the lines
where the Cardinal sits his horse. Wind, salt light, flags.
**Accent:** the city's walls on the skyline, always in view.

### 8 · The Red Dovecote · pp 21–24
A lonely inn on a heath at night: a lit window, a yard, a low common room
with a big hearth and a **broken stovepipe** running up through the ceiling
into the room above. Upstairs: a small room, a table, one candle, a chair, a
window. Brown, smoky, one fire below, one candle above.
**Accent:** the stovepipe, through which every word comes down.

### 9 · The bastion Saint-Gervais · pp 28–34
A ruined earthwork fort halfway between the lines and the city: broken
walls of stone and earth, a cracked standing wall leaning outward, gun
embrasures, rubble, a flagless pike in the earth. Behind, the army's lines;
ahead, the city walls and a slope the attackers climb. Morning, bright, smoky.
**Accent:** the cracked wall — standing on pp 28–31, coming down on p 33.

### 10 · The north road · pp 36, 46
Long straight roads through flat country, poplars, post inns, rain coming in.
**Accent:** four riders, always four, the yellow horse first.

### 11 · The convent at Béthune · pp 37–45
A small convent of pale stone: a cloister with a square garden and a well,
a gate on the road, an upstairs room with a window on the road, and a back
gate where a carriage can wait. Soft grey northern light, roses, quiet.
**Accent:** the window on the road, where Constance sees the yellow horse.

### 12 · The river ferry · pp 47–52
A flooded river at night in a storm: a ferry landing of black timbers, a
flat ferry raft on a rope across the river, a lantern on a post, the far
bank lost in rain, and downstream the white roar of a weir. On p 52, grey
dawn, the rope hanging slack, the water brown and calm.
**Accent:** the lantern on the raft — the last light, and it goes out.
