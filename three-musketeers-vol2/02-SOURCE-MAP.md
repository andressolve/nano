## The cut list — named, unapologetic, famous material included

### Whole sequences cut

- **Milady's English half.** Her imprisonment by Lord de Winter, the seduction
  of Felton, the assassination of Buckingham. The most famous suspense in the
  book, and not one of the four is in it.
- **The night of the disguise** (d'Artagnan as de Wardes in the dark) and
  Kitty's dress escape. The escape survives as a window and a hay cart.
- **The executioner of Lille and the night trial.** The four do not judge and
  kill a woman; the river does.
- **The Musketeers' equipment chapters** (Porthos's procurator's wife, Aramis's
  mysterious letters, Athos's horse wagered away).
- **The Duchess de Chevreuse and the Queen's letters** as a second plot.
- **Buckingham's fleet at Ré**, the dike, the King's arrival at camp.

### Characters cut

- Kitty, de Wardes, Lord de Winter, Felton, Buckingham (does not return), the
  executioner of Lille, Brisemont, the hired soldiers, Planchet, Grimaud,
  Mousqueton, Bazin, Tréville, the King and Queen (scenery, unseen), the
  abbess of Béthune (scenery, no line).

### Characters merged

- **Kitty + de Wardes → the boy's own ears.** He overhears; he does not need a
  maid or a rival.
- **Brisemont + the hired soldiers → one bottle.** Milady's first attempt on
  the boy is the wine that plants the poison for Béthune.
- **The executioner + the night trial + Lord de Winter → the river.** Each of
  the four names one charge on the bank; Athos offers a trial; the river
  judges.
- **Tréville + the King at the bastion → one caption** granting leave.

### Why the biggest cut pays for the volume

Cutting Milady's English chapters removes three new faces (de Winter, Felton,
Buckingham's death), two new countries' worth of settings, and a third of the
source's length in which the four do nothing but wait. It turns her into what
the engine needs: a woman who is dangerous because she is always just where
the four can almost see her. And it lets the clock run straight: from the Red
Dovecote to Béthune is one road, and she is one day ahead on it.

## Sensitive material and its treatment

- **Constance's death** — on the page, quiet: a glass of wine, her head on his
  shoulder, eyes closing. No pain, no convulsion, no mark. One full page of
  silence after (p 43).
- **Athos hanged his wife** — told once, in his words, in the past, on an
  illustrated-prose page. Drawn as a young lord riding away from a dark wood;
  later, an empty branch. Never a rope in use, never a body.
- **The brand** — a small dark lily-shaped mark on the back of her left
  shoulder, seen where a sleeve has torn at the seam; the shoulder shown, the
  dress otherwise whole.
- **Poison** — a ring with a dark stone that opens; a few grains of powder;
  wine. Never a skull, never a label.
- **Milady's end** — the ferry turning into the dark toward the weir; the
  lantern going out. She is never shown in the water.
- **The bastion** — attackers come with ladders and are driven off by musket
  smoke, steel, and a falling wall; they run. Nobody is drawn hit.
- **Captivity and arrest** — the boy's arrest is a hand on his shoulder and a
  walk, not chains (the safety-filter watch, `method/07`).
