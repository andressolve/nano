# Epic Library Brainstorm — 2026-07-24

## Status

Durable notes from the July 24, 2026 library-planning conversation.

No title has entered production yet. The five candidates below are explicitly retained for future consideration. The most likely next move is to begin **The Count of Monte Cristo** and **The Tale of the Heike** as two parallel projects.

## Governing Creative Direction

The selection criterion is **an absolutely epic tale or story-world**.

Do not turn any one example from the conversation into a restrictive template. In particular:

- *The House of Atreus* is evidence that the family enjoys sophisticated, consequential storytelling. It is not a requirement that the next project be a dynastic curse, tragedy, Greek myth, or structurally similar series.
- Interest in *Assassin's Creed Shadows* and *Ghost of Tsushima* is evidence of enjoyment of immersive historical worlds, exploration, mastery, stealth, combat, and difficult choices. It does not mean the next project must be Japanese.
- Interest in *Civilization VI* is evidence that the boys can enjoy systems, leaders, strategy, technology, diplomacy, war, and civilization-scale cause and effect. It does not mean the next project must be a history explainer or empire-building story.
- Interest in *Blood of Zeus*, George O'Connor's *Olympians* and *Asgardians*, and the new *Odyssey* film is evidence that mythic scale, active supernatural forces, spectacle, and interconnected lore work well. It does not restrict the field to mythology.
- Andres's childhood memory of Chrétien de Troyes's *El cuento del Grial* opened a useful chivalry exploration, but a Perceval/Grail project did not produce enough excitement and is not currently retained.

Treat all of these as **taste anchors, not fences**.

## Audience Signals

- Francisco turned 10 on July 23, 2026.
- Sebastian is 7.
- Both can follow dense plots, recurring characters, moral ambiguity, and stories that do not talk down to them.
- They respond to:
  - scale and spectacle;
  - explorable worlds with surrounding stories;
  - gods, monsters, warriors, factions, and legendary objects;
  - tactical intelligence as well as physical courage;
  - meaningful consequences;
  - strategic and systemic cause and effect;
  - strong visual identities and cinematic set pieces.
- Humor, wonder, adventure, and discovery remain welcome. Do not assume that "epic" must mean uniformly grim.

## Explicitly Retained Candidates

### 1. Romance of the Three Kingdoms

**Status:** Retain for future development.

Potential strengths:

- The collapse of the Han and the emergence of three rival states provides true civilization-scale movement.
- Combines commanders, warriors, advisers, spies, diplomacy, logistics, duels, betrayals, and competing ideas of legitimate rule.
- Strong bridge between character drama and the strategic/systemic appeal of *Civilization VI*.
- Large enough for a substantial multi-volume series, but any adaptation will require a disciplined character spine rather than attempting to preserve the original's full cast.

Session research:

- University of California Press describes it as China's national epic and emphasizes its treatment of power, diplomacy, and the planning and conduct of war:
  https://www.ucpress.edu/flyer/books/three-kingdoms-a-historical-novel/paper

### 2. The Count of Monte Cristo

**Status:** Retain; likely first-wave project.

Potential strengths:

- A fully human epic: betrayal, false imprisonment, education, escape, treasure, reinvention, secret identities, long-range strategy, revenge, and the eventual moral cost of revenge.
- Mission-like episodes and intricate cause-and-effect should connect naturally with the boys' enjoyment of games without imitating game plots.
- Demonstrates that the library's "epic" direction is not confined to mythology or ancient history.

Tentative architecture discussed, not approved:

1. **The Prisoner of Château d'If**
2. **The Treasure**
3. **The Count**
4. **The Price of Vengeance**

Do not treat those titles or the four-volume count as locked. A research and structure pass must determine how to preserve the novel's important families, reversals, and moral landing without turning the adaptation into a plot summary.

### 3. The Tale of the Heike

**Status:** Retain; likely first-wave project.

Potential strengths:

- The fall of the Heike/Taira and the Genpei War provide battlefield action, imperial politics, intimate loss, the emergence of the samurai order, and a powerful Buddhist meditation on impermanence.
- The story gives equal reason to care about victors and losers.
- Visually rich: court and battlefield, red and white clan identities, cavalry, archery, ships, temples, mountains, and the sea.
- The boys' enjoyment of *Ghost of Tsushima* and *Assassin's Creed Shadows* may provide an immediate entry point, but the project should be selected and shaped on the strength of *Heike* itself.

Session research:

- Columbia University Press describes the work as an epic of the Genpei War that combines battlefield chaos, imperial intrigue, samurai lives, women's stories, and Buddhist themes of impermanence, karma, attachment, and renunciation:
  https://cup.columbia.edu/book/the-tales-of-the-heike/9780231510837/

Editorial cautions for a future research pass:

- Decide whether the primary spine is the fall of the Heike, the larger ensemble, or a carefully bounded Yoshitsune continuation.
- Do not reduce the work to "samurai action."
- Distinguish literary tradition, later legend, and recoverable history where that distinction matters.

### 4. The Divine Comedy

**Status:** Retain for future development.

Potential strengths:

- A complete cosmic architecture with a natural three-volume form: *Inferno*, *Purgatorio*, *Paradiso*.
- Each realm offers new visual laws, historical encounters, moral questions, creatures, landscapes, and systems.
- Could exploit the library's improved narrative and explanatory abilities: part graphic novel, part philosophical journey, part map of a universe.

Primary challenge:

- *Inferno* is immediately dramatic; *Purgatorio* and especially *Paradiso* require equally strong narrative and visual strategies rather than becoming illustrated lectures.

No production architecture is approved.

### 5. Nansō Satomi Hakkenden

**Working English label:** *Hakkenden* / *The Eight Dog Warriors*

**Status:** Retain for future development.

Potential strengths:

- Eight spiritually linked warriors, born separately, marked by beads associated with virtues, gradually discover one another and assemble around the Satomi cause.
- Combines supernatural origin, warrior codes, recognition objects, travel across Japan, recruitment, battles, castles, and a large final convergence.
- Naturally supports an ensemble "party assembly" structure without being derived from a video game.

Primary challenge:

- The original is enormous and intricate. A future adaptation must choose a strong emotional and narrative spine rather than merely collecting eight introductions.

Session research:

- Tateyama City describes it as a grand-scale, 106-volume classical novel about eight samurai associated with Confucian virtues:
  https://www.city.tateyama.chiba.jp/hakubutukan/page000656.html
- Josai International University's museum describes it as an epic fantasy novel set near the end of the Muromachi period:
  https://www.jiu.ac.jp/eng/museum/19nanso/english.html

## Likely Parallel Starters

The current leading intention is:

1. **The Count of Monte Cristo**
2. **The Tale of the Heike**

Develop them **in parallel**, not as a forced competition where one must be abandoned for the other.

This is a future production intention, not authorization to begin generating assets immediately. When Andres resumes the work, the first phase should be parallel research and architecture:

- identify authoritative source editions and supporting scholarship;
- define what each adaptation is fundamentally about;
- propose viable volume counts and breakpoints;
- build character/faction maps;
- identify recurring objects and visual throughlines;
- identify the hardest page types to prototype;
- test whether each volume has its own satisfying arc while advancing the larger epic.

The two projects should not be forced into the same format, page count, tone, or visual system merely because they are developed together.
