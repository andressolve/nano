## CICERO — 3 age phases (protagonist)

### ref_cicero_young.png — the rising advocate (~26–36, P2–P3)
Roman man, late 20s–30s, Mediterranean fair-olive skin, **clean-shaven**, short dark-brown hair combed forward,
lean intelligent face, high forehead, alert grey-brown eyes, slightly long nose, mobile expressive mouth (a
speaker's face). Plain off-white wool toga over a simple tunic. Upright, energetic posture, one hand poised as
if about to make a point.

### ref_cicero_consul.png — the consul at his height (~43, P4–P11)
Same man, early–mid 40s, fuller in the face, first lines at the eyes, hair receding slightly at the temples,
short and dark with a little grey. **Clean-shaven.** **Toga praetexta** — off-white wool with a single
**purple-red border stripe** along the curved edge. Commanding, confident, faintly vain bearing; chin up. The
public orator. Right hand raised in the classic oratorical gesture (palm out, fingers extended).

### ref_cicero_old.png — the proscribed old man (63, P13–P17)
Same man, early 60s, grey thinning hair, gaunt and weathered, deep lines, heavy-lidded tired eyes, sorrow in the
face but dignity intact. **Clean-shaven.** Plain undyed off-white wool toga, no purple border now (a private man,
not a magistrate), slightly travel-worn. Stooped but not broken.

> CICERO DISCIPLINE: clean-shaven on every page; use the AGE-CORRECT ref for the page's date. Purple-bordered
> toga ONLY on the consul/magistrate pages (P4–P9). Plain toga otherwise. The raised oratorical hand is his
> signature gesture — recurs and pays off (hands motif).

## SECONDARY CAST (Strategy 1 — lock the harder face, prose Cicero)

### ref_terentia.png — Cicero's wife (P7)
Roman matron, ~30s–40s, dignified, dark hair bound up in a matron's style with a *vitta* (woolen fillet), pale
*stola* (long dress) over a tunic, a *palla* (mantle) draped. Strong, composed, managing face.

### ref_tullia.png — Cicero's beloved daughter (P7, P13)
Young Roman woman, early–mid 20s, gentle warm face, dark hair simply dressed, soft-coloured stola. Warmth and
intelligence — Cicero adored her. (P13 she has died; she appears in memory/portrait only, NOT as a corpse.)

### ref_atticus.png — Titus Pomponius Atticus, lifelong friend (P7, P8)
Roman man, ~40s–50s, calm shrewd banker/scholar's face, neat short grey-brown hair, clean-shaven, plain fine
toga, an unflappable steady expression — the safe confidant. Often shown with a scroll/letter.

### ref_quintus.png — Quintus Cicero, brother (P10, optional)
Roman man, ~40s, resembles Cicero but blunter, more soldierly (he served as a legate), short hair, clean-shaven,
plainer toga or a hint of military tunic.

### ref_catiline.png — Lucius Sergius Catilina (P5, P6)
Patrician Roman man, ~40s, tall, powerfully built, handsome-but-dissipated face, dark hair, clean-shaven, intense
hooded dangerous eyes, a proud bitter mouth. Senator's tunic (broad purple stripe) + toga. Charismatic menace,
NOT a cartoon villain — a dangerous man riding real grievances.

### ref_hortensius.png — Quintus Hortensius Hortalus, rival orator (P3)
Roman man, ~50s, elegant, vain, beautifully groomed, fastidious, fine toga worn just so, a polished
self-satisfied expression — "the king of the courts" Cicero displaced.

### ref_caesar.png — Gaius Julius Caesar (P6 debate, P11–P12)
Roman man, ~50s, lean, balding (high domed forehead, hair combed forward), clean-shaven, piercing dark eyes,
sharp ascetic intelligent face, faint smile of total self-possession. Senator's toga; later a general's red
cloak / laurel hint when dictator. Magnetic, dangerous calm.

### ref_pompey.png — Gnaeus Pompeius Magnus (P11, optional)
Roman general, ~50s, broad solid face, thick wavy hair swept up off the forehead (his signature *anastole*),
clean-shaven, muscled cuirass + red *paludamentum* cloak, fading grandeur.

### ref_cato.png — Marcus Porcius Cato, the rigid moralist (P6)
Roman man, ~40s, austere, deliberately old-fashioned, plain dark tunic (no purple, affecting old-Roman
simplicity), short hair, a **short austere beard** (the one bearded figure), stern unbending face, barefoot/plain
sandals. Inflexible virtue.

### ref_clodius.png — Publius Clodius Pulcher, enemy tribune (P9, optional — can be prose-only)
Patrician Roman man, ~30s, handsome, sneering, fashionable, dark curled hair, clean-shaven, a demagogue's
crowd-pleasing arrogance. Tribune's plain toga but worn with swagger.

### ref_antony.png — Marcus Antonius (Mark Antony) (P15, P16)
Roman man, ~40s, big, thick-necked, brawny, soldier's build, curly dark hair, **clean-shaven**, heavy jaw,
coarse handsome bullish face, muscled cuirass + red cloak (the warlord) OR senator's toga worn carelessly. Brute
force in human form — the army the word cannot beat.

### ref_octavian.png — young Octavian (the future Augustus) (P16)
Very young Roman man, **19**, slight, pale, cold, smooth boyish face, neat light-brown hair, clean-shaven,
calm calculating pale eyes, plain fine toga. Unnervingly composed for his age. (Tie-in: the Augustus the Rome
essays end on — here still the cold young heir.)
