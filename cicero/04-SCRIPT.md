## QUIZ (in reader, NOT an image) — 5 WHY questions, answers shuffled, length-matched distractors
1. **Why was Cicero's rise to consul so extraordinary?** → Because he reached Rome's highest office on oratory
   alone, with no famous family name and no army behind him. (distractors: he was the richest man in Rome; he
   won great battles; he inherited the office.)
2. **Why did crushing the Catiline conspiracy later destroy him?** → Because he had the conspirators executed
   *without trial*, which broke Roman law and gave his enemies the charge that drove him into exile. (distractors
   about bribery, cowardice in battle, insulting Caesar.)
3. **Why did Cicero refuse to join Caesar, Pompey, and Crassus?** → Because joining them meant trading the
   Republic's freedom for a share of their power, and he would not. (distractors: he hated them personally; he
   feared assassination; he was too old.)
4. **Why does Cicero's philosophy still matter today?** → Because he carried Greek thought into Latin and
   invented the words for it — "quality," "humanity," "moral," "science" — words we still use. (distractors: he
   discovered a planet; he wrote Rome's laws; he founded a school in Athens.)
5. **Why couldn't Cicero's words save him in the end?** → Because in a Rome ruled by armies, no speech could
   stop legions; Antony's soldiers ended the man even as his writing survived. (distractors about running out of
   money, losing his voice, being outvoted.)
