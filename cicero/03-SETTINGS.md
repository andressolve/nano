# 03 — SETTINGS

Late-Republican Rome and Italy, ~80–43 BC. Austere, stony, lamplit — NOT gilded imperial marble. Recurring
locations so prompts stay consistent.

### Arpinum (P1)
Cicero's hill town ~100 km SE of Rome. Modest stone-and-stucco country house, olive terraces, blue Apennine
foothills, warm Italian light. Provincial, honest, far from the great city. The "nobody from nowhere" world.

### The Forum Romanum (P2, P3, P5-exterior, P17, P18)
The civic heart of Republican Rome: open paved square, the Curia (Senate house, plain brick-faced rectangular
hall), temples (Saturn, Castor), the **Rostra** — the speaker's platform faced with bronze ships' rams/prows
(*rostra*), where orators address the crowd. Travertine, painted stucco, red tiles, throngs of toga-clad
citizens. NO Colosseum, NO imperial arches. This is the stage of his voice — and his death.

### The Curia / Senate house interior (P4, P6)
Rectangular hall, rising tiers of benches along the long walls, magistrates' curule chairs at the end, tall
shuttered windows, oil-lamp and daylight, a brazier. Rows of toga-clad senators. The Temple of Jupiter Stator
(P5) is similar but a temple cella — columns, a cult statue, lamplight.

### A Roman law court (P2, P3)
Open-air tribunal in the Forum — a raised magistrate's bench, jurors on benches, a crowd pressing close, the
accused, the advocate standing to speak. Tense public theatre.

### Cicero's study / tablinum (P7, P14)
A scholar's room: shelves and pigeonholes (*nidi*) stuffed with papyrus scrolls, a writing table with wax
tablets, reed pens, ink pots, an oil lamp casting warm gold light, a window to a colonnaded garden
(*peristyle*). The warm private heart of the book — where the word is written, not shouted.

### The road into exile / Thessalonica (P9)
Cold, grey, far from Rome. A lonely shore or bleak provincial room, a single guttering lamp, the man slumped
with a half-written letter. Gold light DIM. Behind/in memory: his Palatine house being torn down, a small shrine
rising on the rubble.

### Pharsalus / the war (P10, P11)
Macedonian plain, military camp, tents, standards, distant battle-haze. Soldiers in mail, red cloaks, generals'
cuirasses. Cicero in a plain toga, conspicuously a civilian among armed men — the toga lost in a field of armour.
The visual thesis: the word has no place here.

### Astura / Tusculum villa (P13, P14)
A seaside/country villa, colonnades, cypress and pine, the sea or hills beyond, cool quiet light. Place of grief
(P13) and then of the philosophy (P14). Solitary.

### Formiae — the coast (P17)
Cicero's villa near the sea south of Rome; a litter on a wooded coastal track, pines, grey winter dawn light, the
sea glimpsed beyond. The place of the murder. Dignified, cold, hushed — NOT a battlefield.

> Lighting rule across all settings: the **lamp-gold accent** is the warm light of the voice/word. It is FULL in
> the Forum-at-his-height, the court triumphs, and the study; it DIMS in exile, the war, and grief; it is GONE
> (cold grey) at the death; it returns in the SCROLLS in the finale.
