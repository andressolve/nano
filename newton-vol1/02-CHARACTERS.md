# The Invisible Forces — Characters

## Isaac Newton — Young Scholar / Investigator (Primary A, Pages 7–18)

This is the central Newton of Book One: Trinity student, plague-year investigator, optics and gravity thinker. If only one Newton reference is perfect, it should be this one.

### Character Lock Block (COPY VERBATIM)

```
CHARACTER ISAAC NEWTON — YOUNG SCHOLAR (must appear identical in all panels):
- Age/build: 22-27, medium height, lean and tightly held, not physically imposing but wiry
- Face: Long narrow face, high forehead, prominent straight nose, pale English complexion, controlled expression
- Hair: Dark brown, thick, natural, falling to just below the ears or collar, not wigged
- Eyes: Deep-set gray-brown eyes, watchful and intensely inward
- Facial hair: Clean-shaven
- Mouth: Thin controlled mouth, rarely smiling broadly
- Outfit: Black scholar's gown over a dark wool doublet, white linen collar, dark breeches, plain shoes or boots
- Accessories: Prism, notebook, quill, compass, telescope components, loose papers with diagrams
```

## Notes On Newton Across Ages

Key continuity markers from child to mature professor:

1. High forehead
2. Long, narrowing face
3. Deep-set watchful eyes
4. Straight prominent nose
5. Controlled mouth
6. Dark natural hair rather than later public wig iconography
7. A bodily sense of inwardness — shoulders slightly closed, energy directed into hands and eyes

## Minor Characters Without Dedicated References

The following can remain background or loosely locked unless later drafts prove they need reference sheets:

- Barnabas Smith
- Schoolmaster Henry Stokes
- Grantham classmates / rival boys
- Royal Society members other than Halley
- servants, townspeople, printers, booksellers

Keep the cast visually legible by resisting unnecessary character proliferation.
