# Settings - Foundation, Part Two

## 1. Terminus Fifty Years Later

**Pages:** Cover, 1-4, 28.

Terminus City occupies one compact patch of an immense ochre plain beneath a
thin pale-blue sky. Low ivory ceramic/concrete blocks, teal-painted structural
steel, a few radio masts, compact transit lines, and amber windows surround the
taller stepped Encyclopedia Building. Wind carries rust-colored dust. Beyond
the city there is almost nothing: no forests, mountains, oceans, mine works, or
native ruins.

Page 1 must connect visually to Part One Page 24: chrome exile ships become old
landing monuments, spare hulls, and the first generation of city structures.
The city is stable and industrious, not a desperate camp, but it remains tiny
against the planet.

## 2. Freight Port and Resource Ledger

**Pages:** 2-3.

A modest landing apron at the city's edge with low cargo sheds, compact cranes,
and marked spaces where metal ingots should be stacked. Several racks are empty.
One Foundation reactor-powered tug is clean and compact; an older incoming
freighter is scarred and bulky. Hardin and Lee can overlook the port from a
glass-fronted municipal operations room containing route lights and physical
freight tokens.

The loss of supply is visible in absence: empty racks, dark route lamps, work
orders waiting for metal. Do not cover screens in fake writing.

## 3. Encyclopedia Building and Pirenne's Office

**Pages:** 3-4, 6, 11-12.

The Encyclopedia Building is Terminus's tallest structure: a stepped ivory tower
with teal ribs and narrow amber glazing. Inside, tall data-stack machinery,
indexing reels, archive cylinders, proof tables, and careful scholars create a
cathedral of organized knowledge.

Pirenne's office stands above the archive floor. It has a high window toward
the empty plain, a midnight-blue desk, a coin-safe edge where Hardin waits, and
an immense working diagram for the first Encyclopedia volume. The room faces
inward toward the archive more than outward toward the sky.

**New ref:** `refs/ref_encyclopedia_building.png`.

## 4. Four Kingdoms Strategic Model

**Pages:** Cover, 3-4, 10, 17-20, 25, 27-28.

A physical tabletop projector used by Hardin, not a magical galaxy. Terminus is
a small bright amber-teal point at the center. The former Imperial connection
is a broken pale-gold route toward the core. Anacreon is the nearest and largest
maroon region; Smyrno is slate blue, Konom dark green, and Daribow burnt orange.

The model has three readable states:

1. Empire receded: four separate kingdoms and one isolated Terminus.
2. Anacreon claims Terminus: one maroon vector encloses the central light and
   gains the only active atomic symbol.
3. Balance: the other three vectors converge against exclusive Anacreonian
   control; Terminus remains separate at the center.

Names belong in captions or dialogue, not as unreliable map text.

**New ref:** `refs/ref_four_kingdoms_model.png`.

## 5. Anacreonian Arrival and State Dinner

**Pages:** 5-9.

Rodric arrives in a heavy maroon-and-brass cutter whose hull descends from old
Imperial design but carries visible repairs and chemical exhaust. A formal guard
and brass banners contrast with Terminus's plain apron.

The reception route moves from the port, through the Encyclopedia archive, to a
state dining room/veranda with wide windows over the ochre plain. The table is
Terminus-simple; Rodric's portable plate, medals, and attendants supply most of
the decoration.

**New ref:** `refs/ref_anacreon_arrival.png`.

## 6. Terminus Atomic Plant / Lost Atomics Comparison

**Pages:** 9-10, 15, 27-28.

Terminus's plant is compact, clean, and quietly luminous: smooth enamel housings,
teal maintenance panels, a small amber-white core, tools stored for actual
repair, and engineers who understand the system. It is advanced enough that
plutonium is obsolete.

The contrasting Anacreonian/Imperial-loss imagery shows a huge old reactor or
warship power compartment: dark core, brass valves, patched conduits, oil/coal
auxiliaries, technicians copying inherited procedures without understanding the
atomic system. A separate inset may show an Imperial accident site sealed behind
warning barriers while officials restrict access instead of training repair
staff.

**New ref:** `refs/ref_atomic_contrast.png`.

## 7. Board Chamber / City Hall

**Pages:** 11-12, 16-22, 26-27.

The Board chamber is formal and inward-facing: five curved midnight-blue seats,
an ivory wall embossed with a circular archive motif, a long table, and no
public gallery. The strategic-model projector sits unused at one end until
Hardin activates it.

City Hall is lower, broader, and outward-facing, with windows onto the city,
route and freight boards, communications consoles, public-news reels, and a
practical dark-teal council table. During the coup, civic wardens calmly occupy
the communications room, transit control, and the municipal seal station.

**New ref:** `refs/ref_board_and_city_hall.png`.

## 8. Dorwin Reception and Research Room

**Pages:** 13-16.

Dorwin arrives in a slim immaculate Imperial courier with polished gold and
lavender enamel. His reception is held in a bright ivory guest salon prepared
with ceremonial drapery that Terminus rarely uses. He displays ancient books,
facsimiles, and star charts on a polished table while the real ochre plain is
visible through the window.

For the five-day speech analysis, Hardin's City Hall office contains audio reels,
paper logic tape, a compact symbolic analyzer, and a wall projection that turns
ornate diplomatic phrases into connected statement blocks and finally an empty
center. Use caption text to explain the result; do not rely on generated formula
writing.

**New ref:** `refs/ref_dorwin_and_logic_room.png`.

## 9. Ultimatum and Temporary Base

**Pages:** 17-18, 22, 26-28.

The ultimatum appears as a scarlet official transmission in the Board chamber.
Its content is carried by story captions and balloons, not fake on-screen text.

Anacreonian troop transports land beyond Terminus City on empty ochre ground.
Crews raise prefabricated black-and-maroon barracks, a brass sunburst mast, fuel
tanks, and a heavy sensor tower. The city remains visible in the distance and is
not occupied. No firefight occurs. On Page 28 the same structures are dismantled
and the banner lowered.

**New ref:** `refs/ref_anacreon_base.png`.

## 10. Time Vault Chamber

**Pages:** 23-25.

A spacious, nearly dark room beneath or beside the civic center. Low simple
chairs face a central glass cubicle containing a mechanical projector and empty
floor. At the appointed moment, the lights fade and Seldon's complete seated
recording appears inside the cubicle as the only pale blue-white light.

The room is a timed recording theater, not an ancient magical artifact. It has
no exterior monolith, null field, floating shapes, psychic force, or interactive
interface. The Board and Hardin remain outside the glass.

**New ref:** `refs/ref_time_vault.png`.

## 11. Three-Kingdom Communications / Balance Resolution

**Pages:** 27-28.

Hardin's strategic room links three distinct remote envoys or royal chambers to
the Four Kingdoms model. Each rival sees the same projection: Anacreon enclosing
Terminus and inheriting the only active atomic symbol. Their joint ultimatum is
visualized as three separate communications converging on the Anacreonian base.

The final image returns outdoors. The temporary base is leaving; four foreign
ships remain separated around the field while a small Foundation technical
delegation stands at the center. This is balance, not friendship, empire, or
religion.
