# Characters - Foundation, Part Two

## Identity Policy

Every recurring character and recurring uniformed group receives a locked
1536x1024 reference before dependent page production. The compressed edition's
refs may guide broad continuity, but Part Two earns a new coherent set matched
to the finished expanded Part One register.

Character sheets use a neutral warm-grey/ochre background, no text, no labels,
and no panel-border layout unless a declared group sheet needs separate visual
zones. Each solo sheet includes a large portrait and a full-body standing view.

## Salvor Hardin

**Pages:** Cover, 1-28.

**New ref:** `refs/ref_hardin.png`.

**Lock:**

> Salvor Hardin, man in his mid-thirties, medium olive-brown skin, lean angular face, short dark-brown hair brushed back by the dry wind, grey-green watchful eyes, clean-shaven, compact wiry build. Wears a practical charcoal-teal frontier jacket with a soft open collar over an ivory work shirt, dark straight trousers, worn black boots, and a narrow utility belt; no tie, medals, armor, hat, or weapon. His expression is alert, wry, and self-controlled rather than swaggering. Realistic anatomy.

Hardin listens before he speaks. He handles a coin on Pages 3-4 as a quiet
thinking habit, not a magician's trick. His authority grows physically: at first
he waits in Pirenne's office; by the last pages he stands at the center of the
strategic map.

## Lewis Pirenne

**Pages:** 3-4, 6-9, 11-12, 16-20, 23-26.

**New ref:** `refs/ref_pirenne.png`.

**Lock:**

> Lewis Pirenne, elderly white man in his early seventies, high forehead, swept-back silver hair, neat pointed white beard, thin lips, round wire spectacles, tall narrow upright build. Wears a severe long midnight-blue scholar's coat with a high ivory inner collar and a small restrained brass clasp. Precise hands, proud posture, tired intelligent eyes. Never villainous, foolish, or physically feeble.

Pirenne's belief in the Encyclopedia is sincere. His composure breaks only when
Seldon reveals the cover, and even then the page must show grief for a life's
misunderstood purpose rather than comic humiliation.

## Anselm haut Rodric

**Pages:** 5-10.

**New ref:** `refs/ref_rodric.png`.

**Lock:**

> Anselm haut Rodric, Anacreonian officer and envoy in his late forties, broad-shouldered, weathered tan skin, thick black hair touched with grey, strong hooked nose, waxed dark mustache, proud heavy-lidded eyes. Wears a maroon high-collared military coat with black leather shoulder pieces, faded gold braid, old Imperial medals, brass sunburst clasp, dark breeches, polished knee boots, and a ceremonial sidearm. Impressive but slightly overdecorated; no helmet.

Rodric is intelligent inside a military-feudal worldview. His surprise at
atomics must be visible before he regains diplomatic control.

## Lord Dorwin

**Pages:** 13-16.

**New ref:** `refs/ref_dorwin.png`.

**Lock:**

> Lord Dorwin, Imperial Chancellor in his late fifties, pale skin, carefully curled blond-grey hair, long blond sideburns, soft aristocratic face, amused blue eyes, elegant narrow build. Wears an immaculate lavender-and-cream brocade coat with polished gold embroidery, high cream collar, fitted trousers, jeweled shoes, rings, and carries a small gold-and-enamel snuff box. Refined, graceful, and politically shrewd beneath a foppish surface.

Dorwin's affected speech is represented in the scripted spelling, but the art
must not turn him into a grotesque. His danger is that he is excellent at
producing reassurance without obligation.

## Jord Fara

**Pages:** 12, 18-20, 23-26.

**New ref:** `refs/ref_fara.png`.

**Lock:**

> Jord Fara, senior trustee in his late sixties, bald crown with a thin white fringe, heavy brows, broad nose, deep-set anxious brown eyes, compact square build. Wears a russet-brown high-collared Foundation scholar's suit with subtle woven pattern and a small brass double-ring clasp. Earnest and stubborn, never comic. Visually distinct from Pirenne and Seldon.

Fara embodies dependence on Seldon's authority. He is the Board member most
likely to speak after Pirenne and should remain consistent across the dialogue
prototype and Vault pages.

## Yohan Lee

**Pages:** 2, 11, 16-17 background, 20-22, 26-28.

**New ref:** `refs/ref_yohan_lee.png`.

**Lock:**

> Yohan Lee, Terminus civic organizer in his early thirties, East Asian man with warm light-brown skin, close-cropped straight black hair, square jaw, dark steady eyes, athletic compact build. Wears a practical rust-red overshirt beneath a short dark-teal civic jacket, charcoal trousers, and work boots. No armor, medals, hat, or visible weapon. Direct, grounded, and loyal without becoming a bodyguard stereotype.

Lee executes the coup through civic institutions. He is not a soldier and must
not drift into an Anacreonian uniform.

## Hari Seldon Recording

**Pages:** Cover, 23-25.

**New ref:** `refs/ref_seldon_recording.png`.

**Identity source:** `../foundation-expanded-part1/refs/ref_seldon.png`.

**Lock:**

> The exact elderly Hari Seldon identity from expanded Part One, now recorded near the end of his life: balding crown, wispy white temple hair, deeply lined gentle face, calm faint smile, plain pale-grey scholar's robe. He sits upright in a practical dark powered chair with visible wheels and arm controls, a closed undecorated book on his lap. His complete figure is rendered as a translucent pale blue-white projection with subtle scan lines and a crisp mechanical light source. No halo, no magic, no standing version, no Apple Vault effects.

The recording speaks on a timer and never looks as though it can see or answer
the Trustees.

## The Board of Trustees

**Pages:** 11-12, 16-20, 23-26.

**New group ref:** `refs/ref_board.png`.

The group contains Pirenne and Fara plus three recurring silent trustees:

- **Sutt:** long dark-grey hair, narrow face, muted plum scholar's coat;
- **Fulham:** broad Black man with close silver curls, deep teal coat;
- **Bort:** older woman with warm brown skin, cropped white hair, ivory-and-grey
  scholar's coat.

The five must have distinct faces, ages, silhouettes, and costume colors. The
group ref is an identity source, not a fixed seating layout. Pirenne normally
chairs from center; Fara sits nearest him.

## Terminus Civic Wardens

**Pages:** 21-22, 26-28 background.

**New group ref:** `refs/ref_civic_wardens.png`.

> Three diverse men and women in practical dark-teal civic jackets with narrow ivory piping, plain trousers, work boots, and small circular municipal badges. No helmets, military medals, rifles, or riot armor. They carry communication pads and sealed authority packets; one may have a holstered defensive sidearm that remains untouched.

Their calm presence is how the coup becomes visible without turning into a
gunfight.

## Anacreonian Troops and Delegates

**Pages:** 5 background, 17, 26-28.

**New group ref:** `refs/ref_anacreon_group.png`.

> Anacreonian personnel share maroon, black leather, and faded brass design language derived from Rodric. Troops wear heavy old Imperial-style coats and practical dark helmets; officers wear sunburst clasps. Their gear is bulky, repaired, and chemically powered. The same group sheet also includes one unarmored diplomatic aide in maroon and gold. Distinct faces; no clones; no Apple-series Anacreon styling.

## Three Kingdom Envoys

**Pages:** 27-28.

**New group ref:** `refs/ref_three_kingdom_envoys.png`.

One group sheet with three clearly separated delegates:

- Smyrno: slate blue and copper, severe naval silhouette;
- Konom: dark green and silver, tall administrative silhouette;
- Daribow: burnt orange and cream, compact mercantile silhouette.

They are political rivals, not a friendly team. No labels are baked into the
sheet; page composition and captions identify their role.

## Non-Recurring Figures

No separate identity refs are needed for:

- second-generation Terminus families, freight workers, engineers, and
  Encyclopedia staff on Pages 1-3;
- Dorwin's two silent Imperial attendants on Page 13;
- background citizens and message operators;
- unnamed Anacreonian construction crews at the temporary base.

These figures must not resemble Hardin, Lee, Pirenne, Rodric, Dorwin, Fara, or
Seldon.

## Reference Attachment Rule

Attach every visible recurring identity ref to each page generation, plus the
relevant environment/object ref and a finished expanded Part One page as the
register source. State that character and group sheets are identity/design
sources, not layouts to reproduce.
