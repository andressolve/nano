# Script - Foundation, Part Two: The Encyclopedists

Cover + 28 story pages. Every caption and balloon below is production
lettering and must be rendered verbatim. Dialogue is original condensed
adaptation prose except for short signature phrases already verified in the
shipped Foundation project.

Use the fixed style block, lettering rules, identity refs, environment refs,
and object refs defined in `01-STYLE-GUIDE.md`, `02-CHARACTERS.md`, and
`03-SETTINGS.md`.

## Cover

Single full-bleed poster image, no panels. Terminus City lies small on an
immense ochre plain beneath a pale sky. Salvor Hardin stands in the foreground,
plain frontier jacket moving in the wind, one hand resting on the glowing Four
Kingdoms projector. Lewis Pirenne stands behind him beside the stepped
Encyclopedia Building. Above them, Anacreon's largest maroon vector reaches
toward the planet while Smyrno, Konom, and Daribow press inward from three
directions. Hari Seldon's pale blue-white seated recording appears faintly in a
glass cubicle against deep space. Preserve generous title space and keep the
image political, not military.

Lettering:

- Large chrome science-fiction title at top: `FOUNDATION`
- Amber serif subtitle beneath: `PART TWO · THE ENCYCLOPEDISTS`
- Small cream credit box at bottom right: `after the novel by ISAAC ASIMOV`

Refs: Hardin, Pirenne, Seldon recording, Terminus city, Four Kingdoms model,
expanded Part One Page 24 as register and continuity source.
