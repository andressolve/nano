# Ratio: The Seeker — Characters (Vol 1)

**Lock discipline:** lock the VISUAL, never the name. The model must never see "Pythagoras." The visual description is the lock; the name lives only in the script and the ref filename. Re-Read every ref PNG before writing any page prompt and echo what the ref actually shows. Use the correct age-phase ref per page — never feed the wrong age into edit_image.

Three age-phases of the protagonist plus three mentors/foreigners. The protagonist is the only recurring face across most pages, so his three refs are the most load-bearing images in the volume after the cover.

## Multi-character pages in Vol 1 (plan ahead)
Most pages are protagonist-solo or protagonist + one mentor — handle with the **lock-the-harder-face + describe-protagonist-in-prose** strategy (protagonist appears on most pages, so prose triangulates him). Pages with the protagonist + a foreign mentor (P6/P7 Egyptian, P10/P11 Magus) anchor edit_image on the FOREIGN mentor ref (more distinctive costume) and describe the age-correct protagonist in prose, OR anchor on the protagonist and describe the mentor. No page in Vol 1 has 3+ named cast members in one frame, so **no composite plate is required** for Vol 1. (Vol 2's Brotherhood pages will need composites — note for later.)

## Casting-gate checklist (apply to every ref before passing the gate)
- Age right? (the boy looks ~10, the man looks ~40 not 30-with-grey)
- Era right? (Ionian trailing chiton, not Roman toga, not fantasy robe; Egyptian/Babylonian period-distinct)
- Realistic, not cartoon? (no oversized eyes, no pastel softness)
- Distinctive? (could you pick this face out of a lineup)
- Three phases read as ONE person aging? (same face genetics)
- Register matches oil-painting realism? (no comic linework, no concept-art gloss)
