# Settings — Foundation: Book Two — The Priests

## S1 — Terminus City, 80 F.E. (P1, P6 exterior beats)

Vol 1's frontier colony thirty years on: the low white architecture has grown into confident mid-rise white towers with amber window-light, air traffic lanes, green belts between districts; ochre plains and thin pale sky beyond the city edge. Still recognizably the same world as Vol 1 P8 — matured, not replaced.

## S2 — Hardin's office, City Hall (P1 f3, P2, P3, P6 f1–f2)

The Vol 1 office aged with its owner: same tall window overlooking the city (the P8/P15 window view, now denser skyline), muted slate walls, warm amber lamplight, a broad plain desk, a small table-projector. No pomp.

## S3 — Temple of the Galactic Spirit (P1 f2, P3 f2; kingdom-world interior)

Soaring dim cathedral interior in heavy Viceroy-era stone; rows of worshippers; gold-vested priests on a raised altar platform — and the altar is a REACTOR: a sleek Foundation atomic power core radiating cool blue-white light (the volume's "light of science," same family as the Seldon hologram). Cables and conduits leave the altar like temple columns.

## S4 — Anacreon royal palace (P5, P8 f3, P9, P12, P13)

Maroon-and-gold Viceroy-era grandeur at planetary scale: vaulted halls, banners with Anacreon's gold sunburst, torch-warm sconces beside dying Imperial-era fittings. Wienis's tower chamber (P9, P12, P13): high private room, tall windows over the glittering capital, a huge ultrawave wall screen, guards in maroon.

## S5 — The flagship *Wienis* (P4, P7, P10)

A salvaged Imperial battlecruiser — '70s chrome hero hardware: kilometer-long cold steel-teal hull, engine bells like cathedrals. Bridge interior: cathedral-scale, tiered consoles, banks of indicator light. In P10 the light dies bank by bank to near-black with single cream emergency accents.

## S6 — Coronation arena (P8 f1–f2, P11 f3 exterior)

A vast circular cathedral-stadium open to the night sky, a hundred thousand subjects in tiers, processional gold everywhere; at the center the floating throne dais. Lepold's coronation: the boy king wrapped in a luminous pale aura, throne rising on invisible force — Foundation machines staged as miracle.

## S7 — Anacreon capital at midnight (P11)

The city from above as the interdict lands: districts of light dying in sweeps, temple glows extinguished, until the capital is deep blue-black — then rivers of TORCH FIRE (warm orange, the only light) flooding the avenues toward the palace.

## S8 — The Time Vault (P15)

Identical to Vol 1 P13–P14: near-dark neutral chamber, rows of simple chairs, the glass cubicle; the hologram is the only light source. Continuity: anchor on `../foundation/refs/ref_seldon_hologram.png`; if the chamber itself drifts, crop the validated Vol 1 P13 chamber pixels as a set ref.

## S9 — Galaxy cosmic (P16 finale)

Full '70s cosmic: the galaxy rim; Terminus as a radiant amber-gold LAMP; four small cold crowned sparks circling close around it like moths. Teal/magenta spiral behind.
