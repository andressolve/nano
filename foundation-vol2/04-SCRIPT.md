## WHY-quiz seeds (drafted at reader stage; positions shuffled)

1. Why did Hardin agree to repair the battlecruiser for his enemy? (He was arming his own trap — the buried relay + priest-crewed ship made the "gift" a hostage.)
2. Why did the Foundation wrap its science in a religion at all? (Control without armies: the kingdoms depend on power only priests — trained on Terminus — can run.)
3. Why did the flagship's crew mutiny against their own admiral? (They believed the Spirit had cursed the ship — the religion made the kill switch read as divine judgment.)
4. Why did the mob march on Wienis instead of on the Foundation? (The interdict was framed as the Spirit judging the Regent; Verisof timed and aimed the fury.)
5. Why does Seldon warn that spiritual power "cannot attack in turn"? (Religion defends but can't expand — neighboring rulers now resist the priesthood; the next crisis needs a new tool — trade.)
