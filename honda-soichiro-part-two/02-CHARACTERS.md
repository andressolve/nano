## Older Founder

### Character Lock Block

```text
CHARACTER OLDER FOUNDER (must remain consistent):
- Age/build: late 50s to late 60s, compact Japanese man, sturdy but no longer young, energetic posture that leans toward machines
- Face: weathered square-oval face, deep lines at eyes and mouth, broad practical forehead, sun-browned skin, intense alert eyes
- Hair: thinning dark-gray hair, receding hairline, sometimes hidden by a white work headband in factory scenes
- Facial hair: clean-shaven
- Outfit: practical engineer-founder clothing, white work shirt or light shop coat, dark trousers, sometimes rolled sleeves; never polished executive glamour
- Signature marks: oil under fingernails, direct hands-on touch with engines, quick impatient stance, happiest beside working machines
```
