# The Machine That Learned to Breathe - Project Brief

Biographical graphic novel about Soichiro Honda, Part Two of the Honda story.

## Status

No existing Part Two production plan was found in this repo. Part One (`honda-soichiro/`) is complete and ends with global motorcycle proof plus a reader interstitial teasing Part Two. This folder is the new production volume.

## Window

This volume covers Honda from first automobile entry pressure in 1962-1963 through the Civic/CVCC proof and the founders' 1973 retirement, with a brief coda to the Civic CVCC's early U.S. proof.

## Working Title

**The Machine That Learned to Breathe**

Subtitle: **Soichiro Honda - Part Two**

## Thesis

Part One asked how a boy who chased engines built machines for ordinary people. Part Two asks whether that success can survive its founder. The answer is not a faster motorcycle or a heroic car. It is a company learning to listen to customers, younger engineers, polluted air, and the future.

## Target

- 20 story pages + cover + 5-question quiz.
- 3:2 landscape images, same dark page-flipping reader pattern as Part One.
- Image-native lettering only. HTML displays finished pages and handles navigation/quiz.
- Built-in image generation tool only. Do not call image APIs or CLI image scripts.
- Prompt and model assumption: ChatGPT image generation path using `gpt-image-2` behavior, oil-painting realism, Honda formula caption boxes.

## Key Arc

1. Honda enters cars because the industry door may close.
2. A car is not a motorcycle with four wheels; it needs far more parts, service, and customers.
3. Racing proves speed, but family cars require balance.
4. Honda's air-cooled confidence becomes the 1300's painful lesson.
5. Younger engineers push back and the company changes from one-genius development to team development.
6. Pollution and the Clean Air Act make the next problem moral, not just technical.
7. CVCC and Civic prove the company can answer reality without being trapped inside the founder's first instincts.
8. Honda and Fujisawa step back together.

## Deliberate Chronology Choices

The volume opens with 1962-1963 car entry even though Part One's finale reaches the early 1960s. That overlap is intentional: Part One closes the motorcycle proof; Part Two reopens the same years from the automobile problem.

The emotional close is 1973, when Honda and Fujisawa step back. The final page includes the Civic CVCC's immediate proof after that moment because the whole question of the volume is whether the company can still answer reality when the founders are no longer at the wheel.

## Production Discipline

- Generate reference sheets before pages.
- For recurring people, use visual lock language rather than relying on famous names.
- Read every existing or generated reference before using it.
- Prototype three pages first: Page 1 (cinematic), Page 13 (technical CVCC), Page 18 (retirement/partnership).
- If page text fails, repair that page before bulk generation.
- Do not overwrite accepted pages. Use versioned siblings during repair, then copy finals to canonical names.
