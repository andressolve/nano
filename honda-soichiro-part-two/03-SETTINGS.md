# The Machine That Learned to Breathe - Settings

## Suzuka Circuit and Early Car Reveals

Early 1960s Japan. Concrete grandstands, unfinished track edges, banners and display areas, small prototype cars under public attention. Bright daytime light, optimism mixed with pressure. Avoid modern safety barriers and modern crowd clothing.

## Honda Automobile Factory

1960s-early 1970s production space: fluorescent lights, white-painted columns, welding stations, assembly jigs, car bodies, carts of small parts, workers in white caps and shop coats. It should feel busy but not futuristic.

## Engineering Test Cells

Engine stands, dynamometers, gauges, exhaust hoses, water jackets, cutaway parts, test charts. Harsh practical lighting. The viewer should feel heat, noise, and evidence.

## Honda R&D Office

Drafting tables, blueprint rolls, green desk lamps, ashtrays, slide rules, wall charts, large hand-drawn car sections. Young engineers work in teams. The room should contrast with the founder's earlier lone-workbench world.

## Los Angeles and Air Pollution Context

Late-1960s / early-1970s street light, yellow-brown smog, freeway lanes, city signs kept generic. Use this as a moral problem, not a disaster spectacle.

## EPA / Ann Arbor Test Context

Industrial laboratory, chassis dynamometer, test car strapped down, engineers in white shirts and ties, clipboards, early computer printouts. No modern screens. The scene should be procedural and tense.

## Retirement Press / Board Context

1973 Japanese corporate setting, modest press room or boardroom, microphones, tables, dark suits. No luxury sheen. The emotion is controlled: two founders stepping back because the company has become larger than either of them.
