# Settings — Foundation: Book Three — The Merchants

## S1 — Terminus City, 155 F.E. (P4 exterior, P10 crowd exterior)

Vol 2's city grown into a true trade capital, seventy-five years on: dense white towers with amber window-light, elevated freight lanes, cargo ships rising from ring-shaped port pads on golden drives, green belts, ochre plains at the edge. Recognizably the same world as Vol 1 P8 / Vol 2 P1, two generations richer.

## S2 — Sutt's office, City Hall (P4)

A bureaucrat's room: slate walls, cold white strip-light (deliberately cooler than Hardin's warm office), data-films in racks, a narrow window on the towers. Order without warmth.

## S3 — The *Far Star* (P5 airlock/deck, P13 bridge)

Mallow's trade ship: compact chrome-and-copper trader, warm cabin light, cargo webbing, a broad airlock ramp. Bridge: two-seat, close, functional — a working ship, not a warship. Exterior: candy-copper hull with teal running lights.

## S4 — Korell (P5 spaceport, P6 Commdor's residence, P7 foundry, P14 city/home/factory)

Deliberate drabness: olive and dust-grey, faded brick, weak yellowed daylight, empty wide streets. The spaceport (P5): a cracked landing apron far from any city, wire fences, one control tower. The Commdor's residence (P6): austere long hall, bare stone, thin carpets — poverty worn as principle; the Commdora's private apartment the one over-rich room. The foundry (P7): vast dim shed, sparks, rows of grey-uniformed guards. P14: a Korellian kitchen, a factory floor, a night street — Foundation gadget-glow dying out of each.

## S5 — Siwenna (P8 Barr's estate, P9 Imperial power plant)

Dead Imperial grandeur. Barr's estate (P8): a once-great villa gone to ruin — cracked marble colonnade, dry fountain, dusty violet dusk, one lit room with a brazier. The power plant (P9): a cathedral of gunmetal — turbine halls a hundred meters high, sparse cream indicator lights, catwalks, the Spaceship-and-Sun in tarnished relief over the great doors; everything monumental, nothing new.

## S6 — Terminus council chamber / courtroom (P10, P11, P13)

The Vol 2 council chamber lineage: tiered white seats packed with delegates, a central floor, slate-and-amber palette. For the trial (P10–P11): a raised tribunal bench, a witness floor, and a huge public VISUAL RECORD screen dominating the wall above.

## S7 — The Mayor's office (P12, P15)

Hardin's old room, continuity deliberate: the same tall window over the city (Vol 2 P1/P14 window view, skyline again denser), muted slate walls, warm amber lamplight, the broad plain desk. A new occupant's touches: cargo manifests, a small chrome model of a trade ship on the desk.

## S8 — The Time Vault (P1 flashback panel)

Identical to Vol 1 P13–P14 / Vol 2 P15: near-dark neutral chamber, the glass cubicle, the hologram the only light. Anchor on `../foundation/refs/ref_seldon_hologram.png`; framed as a replay of Vol 2's scene.

## S9 — Askone (P2 council hall + cell, P3 ship's hold / gold demonstration)

A cold austere world: ice-blue exterior light, stone council hall in slate and bone-white with braziers, Elders in saffron-and-white. Gorov's cell: a light-barrier alcove. P3: the cramped hold of Ponyets' ship — workbench, coils, the crude transmuter rig — then the Elders' treasury table under the warm impossible glow of new-poured gold.

## S10 — Galaxy cosmic (P16 finale)

Full '70s cosmic: the galaxy rim threaded with an amber-gold WEB of trade routes, tiny ship-sparks strung along the lines like beads; Terminus the brightest node at the rim. Far across the spiral, the Imperial core glows dim and reddened. Teal/magenta space.
