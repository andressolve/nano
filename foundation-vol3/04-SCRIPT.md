## WHY-quiz seeds (drafted at reader stage; positions shuffled)

1. Why did the religion of science stop working as the Foundation's tool for growth? (The rim's rulers had seen Anacreon — priests meant control from Terminus — so they banned missionaries; Seldon's warning made real.)
2. Why did Mallow hand the "missionary" back to Korell? (The setup was impossible — an empty port far from any city, yet man and mob arrived in minutes; a rescue was the war-pretext Korell wanted; and he recorded everything.)
3. What did Mallow learn inside the Siwenna power plant, and why did it matter? (The Empire's tech-men can run but not repair their machines — Imperial science is fossilized, so in a long contest of goods the Foundation must win.)
4. Why did Mallow refuse to send a fleet against Korell? (A Seldon crisis is won by historic forces, not heroics — Korell was dependent on Foundation machines, so stopping trade let Korell defeat itself.)
5. Why is trade a stronger weapon than religion by the end of the book? (Religion requires belief and can be banned at the border; trade rides self-interest — everyone wants the goods — and creates dependence without conversion.)
