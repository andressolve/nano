## Review protocol

The final gate used two separate passes:

1. **Gate A — script/image fidelity:** full-resolution candidates against the
   approved 55-page script, identity ledger, readability mandates, panel maps,
   speaker lanes, tail endpoints, silent roles, decisive props, and exact
   1024 × 1536 format.
2. **Gate B — cold-read/visual:** the 390 × 585 sequence as a normal reader
   experience, followed by full-resolution inspection for lettering,
   attribution, anatomy, hands, faces, bodies, duplication, objects, borders,
   continuity, and same-illustrator visual coherence.

The passes did not treat an inference as a success. A missing line, remote or
ambiguous tail, identity collision, decisive-prop error, malformed body,
unreadable mobile field, or broken causal transition would have blocked
promotion.
