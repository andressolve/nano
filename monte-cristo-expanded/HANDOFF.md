## Non-Negotiable Format

- Expanded-edition page and cover format is **1024 × 1536, 2:3 portrait**.
- The three monochrome format proofs passed on 2026-07-26.
- Never generate or approve landscape or square expanded-edition pages.
- Velvet Cinema remains the visual language.
- Every reader-facing page is a finished flattened image.
- The reader supplies presentation controls such as navigation, contents,
  zoom/fullscreen, and any quiz; it does not letter or alter story pages.
- Dialogue pages require integrated native balloons with exact wording and
  unambiguous attribution.
- Illustrated-prose pages use intentionally designed prose fields; they are not
  blank captions patched into comic art.

## Governing Readability Test

At every page turn, the reader should understand:

- where and when the scene occurs;
- who is present;
- what each important person wants;
- what choice was made;
- why it was made;
- what changed;
- why the next scene follows.

Mystery is welcome. Missing connective logic is not.

## Text Production Principle

The hybrid edition should distinguish forms honestly:

- Speech belongs in speech balloons integrated into dramatic art.
- Narrative prose belongs in stable editorial fields composed for reading.
- Silent spectacle should not be filled merely because other pages contain
  text.

The final 1024 × 1536 image may combine illustration and typography, but the
reader must never assemble the story through an HTML/SVG overlay.
