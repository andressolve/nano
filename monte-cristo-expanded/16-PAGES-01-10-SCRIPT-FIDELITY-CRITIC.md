## Severity rule

- **BLOCKER:** wrong, missing, extra, duplicated, or unreadable text; reversed
  dialogue; wrong speaker; a tail captured by another or silent character;
  missing causal action or indispensable object; identity confusion; broken
  panel sequence; wrong canvas.
- **NONBLOCKING:** a visible departure from the production record that does not
  alter wording, speaker ownership, identity, causality, reading order, or
  comprehension.

## Cross-page typography finding

ten-page run.** Pages 1–2 and 6 use a more visibly serifed literary balloon
face; Pages 3–5 and 7–10 use a narrower, more hand-lettered sans face. Page 9's
crowd chorus shifts again to a bold upright sans. Every line is legible and no
letterform changes a word, so this does not fail the present fidelity gate.

across all 52 pages.

**Exact repair prescription:** Lock one speech face before Pages 11–52. Use the
predominant Pages 3–5 and 7–10 face, or formally select the serifed alternative,
but do not continue alternating. If any Page 1–10 image is later regenerated
for a blocking reason, bring that entire page into the selected family; never
replace lettering on the existing flattened art.
