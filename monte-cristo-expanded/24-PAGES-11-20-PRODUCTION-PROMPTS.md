## Common production block

Use case: `illustration-story`.

Create a complete new finished historical graphic-novel page at exactly
**1024 × 1536 portrait, 2:3**. Every input image is an identity, setting,
object, palette, lettering, or continuity reference—not an edit target.

Use the locked **Velvet Cinema** finish: mature historical graphic-novel
realism painted in layered matte gouache and opaque watercolor over sparse
charcoal and ink construction; broad visible brushstrokes; simplified
interlocking color shapes; bold shadow masses; tactile cloth, wood, paper,
wax, stone, iron, flame, and water; expressive anatomically credible faces;
selective hard edges at eyes, mouths, hands, and decisive objects. Avoid glossy
game art, steampunk, anime, children's-book softness, pirate fantasy,
photographic lens effects, generic grimdark, and dense engraved cross-
hatching.

Keep one unmistakable top-to-bottom reading path, 64 px safe outer margins,
at least 24 px calm gutters, and one dominant panel or image. No title, page
number, speaker label, production note, signature, or watermark.

Bake every word below into the finished image **verbatim, exactly once, and in
the numbered order**. No other text. Match the predominant lettering family
of accepted Pages 3–5 and 7–10: large narrow upright hand-lettered mixed-case
speech, never the serif balloon face of the historical Page 18 prototype.
Speech balloons are warm ivory with restrained charcoal-brown painted
outlines. Prose uses a dark literary serif on one stable matte parchment
field. Keep every balloon on the named speaker's side. Each tail ends in open
space immediately beside that speaker's mouth. Silent characters receive no
balloons and no tail fragments. No text crosses a panel border or covers a
face, hand, paper, flame, key, cup, door, or other decisive object.

The identity sheets define casting, not composition. Preserve every named
character's face, apparent age, hair mass, silhouette, costume value pattern,
and habitual posture. State and enforce the nearest forbidden lookalike.
Background characters must not inherit a lead character's complete identity
stack. Never patch a failed result; regenerate the full page.

## Batch rejection rule

Reject and regenerate the complete page if any text, letterform, punctuation,
accent, attribution, tail, face, apparent age, costume, dimension, panel order,
dominant turn, or causal beat fails. Never patch a balloon, face, tail, word,
or panel crop into an otherwise accepted page.

After all ten full-size pages pass, create 390 × 585 reader proofs and audit
the sequence without script knowledge. The reader must understand the arrest's
aftermath, Villefort's social stakes, Edmond's innocence, the Noirtier turn,
Villefort's deliberate betrayal, the transfer, institutional erasure, failed
appeal, and the loved ones' unsuccessful rescue efforts in one normal read.
