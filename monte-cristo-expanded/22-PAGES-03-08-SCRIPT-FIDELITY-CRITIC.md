## Mandatory fix — Page 5, Panel 4

The final three-balloon toast is supposed to be:

1. Edmond, upper-left: `To tomorrow.`
2. Mercédès, upper-right: `Tomorrow.`
3. Louis, lower-left: `Tomorrow.`

The third balloon is positioned at lower-left, but its pointer aims into
Edmond’s forearm/body area. It does not end in open space beside Louis’s mouth.
At mobile size it therefore reads as a second Edmond line, leaving Louis with
no securely owned toast. This is exactly the kind of attribution error the
speaker map forbids.

**Required regeneration:** Regenerate the complete Page 5. Preserve the exact
10 lines and their order. In Panel 4, keep Edmond left, Mercédès right, and
Louis visibly separate, with Louis’s face and mouth unobstructed. Reserve a
clear lower-left-to-center lane for Louis’s `Tomorrow.` and make its tail end
in open space immediately beside **Louis’s mouth**—not at Edmond’s arm, hand,
torso, cup, or the table. Fernand must remain distant and silent. Preserve the
visible family-place logic of Louis joining the table. Do not patch the
flattened page.

## Mandatory fix — Page 7, Panel 4

The final balloon is Caderousse’s line:

> That would be a lie. Put the pen down.

The wording is exact, but the lower-right pointer aims toward Caderousse’s
extended hand/forearm and the paper area instead of ending beside his mouth.
The production contract explicitly rejects a tail that stops at a torso or
prop. The error is especially risky because Danglars and silent Fernand are
also visible in the panel while the page’s decisive object sits between them.

**Required regeneration:** Regenerate the complete Page 7. In Panel 4, stage
Danglars unmistakably left and Caderousse unmistakably right with both mouths
visible. Keep the blank sheet and pen dominant in the lower foreground, but
reserve an upper-right speaker lane for Caderousse’s final balloon. Its tail
must end in open space immediately beside **Caderousse’s mouth**, never at his
hand, pen, paper, torso, or Fernand. Keep Danglars’s long explanation first and
on his left-side lane. If silent Fernand makes the two live lanes unsafe,
remove him from Panel 4. Preserve all 9 exact lines, all prior panel sides, and
the distinct approved identities. Do not patch the flattened page.
