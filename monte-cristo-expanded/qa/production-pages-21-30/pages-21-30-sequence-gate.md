## Sequence findings

### Story continuity and emotional rhythm

- Page 21 makes Edmond's refusal of food the exhausted end of years of failed hope rather than a sudden gesture.
- Page 22 interrupts that chosen disappearance with the smallest credible sign of another mind; Page 23 converts the sign into dangerous physical contact; Page 24 pays it off with Faria's unexpected, dryly funny arrival.
- Pages 25–26 move naturally from the mistaken tunnel's geography to shared work, limited food, guard risk, disagreement, and earned trust. The partnership is practical before it becomes affectionate.
- Page 27 turns trust into playful education. Page 28 then expands that single lesson across years and lands on a credible father-son bond without erasing the prison or the tunnel work.
- Page 29 darkens the pleasure of mastery by exposing Edmond's instinct for leverage. Page 30 follows causally by having Faria impose fact-first method on Edmond's anger. The movement ends with a clear forward question—who possessed the information, and who gained—rather than premature certainty.
- No page drops a required bridge, contradicts the preceding exit state, or reveals a later fact before Edmond can know it.

### Identity, setting, and object continuity

- Edmond's Page 21 continuous-time deterioration resolves into the gaunt, dark-haired, rough-bearded prisoner carried through Pages 22–30. His later posture becomes more alert and controlled without changing his identity.
- Faria's high forehead, wild white-gray hair, long beard, thin animated build, expressive hands, and increasing frailty remain distinct and stable from his Page 24 emergence through Page 30.
- The damp mineral cell, closed iron door, floor passage/loose slab, crude tools, rope/thread, pages, and amber lamp evolve coherently. The lamp arrives with Faria, then becomes the visual center of work, teaching, care, and analysis. The opening is exposed when the story needs it and credibly concealed during later school scenes.
- Page 28's repeated figures read as successive years, and Page 27's repeated closing positions read as successive dialogue instants. Neither creates an accidental extra actor across the sequence.

### Attribution and generation integrity

- Page 22's tail-free scrape becomes Page 23's unseen voice at the seam/opening, then Page 24's visible Faria; the source transition is clear across all three page turns.
- Live Edmond/Faria lanes remain readable through the working, teaching, and analytical pages. Page 29's symbolic banker/guard/salon insets and Page 30's memory objects remain silent and visually separate from live dialogue.
- No page turn exposes an obvious extra, missing, duplicated, or fused live actor or limb. The intentional continuous-time repetitions, tunnel hand reveal, tools, food, diagram objects, and inset figures remain materially coherent in sequence.
