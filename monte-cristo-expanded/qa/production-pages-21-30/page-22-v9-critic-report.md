1. `Who is there?`

the single Panel 3 scrape, with the double scrape answering that request. The
page still hints at contact, but it changes the exact dramatic beat and makes
the reader reconstruct the intended exchange.

**Required complete-page regeneration:** Preserve all exact text and the
four-panel structure, but give Panel 4 three unmistakable vertical tiers:
`Who is there?` upper-left with a mouth-adjacent tail; `scrape · scrape`
clearly next at middle-right on the wall, tail-free; `Again.` clearly below it
at lower-left with its own mouth-adjacent tail. The answer must be encountered
before `Again.` at normal desktop and tablet size. Regenerate the complete
1024 × 1536 page; do not move, patch, or reletter only the Panel 4 elements.
