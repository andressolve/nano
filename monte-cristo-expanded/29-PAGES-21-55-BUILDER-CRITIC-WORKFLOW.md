**Typography-gate correction — 2026-08-07:** Andres explicitly directed the
critic not to reject otherwise comfortable pages over rigid native-pixel type
measurements. Source-size targets remain useful builder guidance, but they are
not an independent mandatory veto. Typography blocks approval only when the
actual desktop or tablet proof requires zoom, feels cramped or tiring, contains
malformed/cropped text, weakens attribution or reading order, or otherwise
fails comfortable first-read comprehension. A line measuring below a nominal
40 px source target is nonblocking when the native lettering is exact and the
normal desktop/tablet proofs are clearly comfortable.

**Reasonable essentials gate — 2026-08-07:** Andres explicitly simplified the
remaining-page critic. This is a graphic novel, not an engineering tolerance
exercise. Mandatory review should concentrate on:

1. **faithfulness to the approved script** — exact required text, correct story
   beat/order, no consequential omission, invention, premature reveal, or
   contradiction;
2. **clear speech/source attribution** — each balloon or sound plainly belongs
   to the intended visible speaker or unseen source on a normal read;
3. **generation integrity** — no extra, missing, duplicated, or fused limbs,
   digits, faces, bodies, or actors, and no malformed decisive object that
   changes the action.

Character continuity, period credibility, and visual style remain useful
checks when a discrepancy is obvious and consequential. They are not licenses
to hunt for microscopic deviations. The following are nonblocking when the
page reads naturally at normal desktop/tablet size: exact pixel font height,
one-to-several-pixel safe-margin misses, exact tail-to-lip distance, exact panel
percentages, minor balloon geometry, phone-size performance, tiny palette or
texture variation, and prompt-coordinate misses that do not alter the visible
story. The builder should submit a plausible page to the independent critic
instead of generating dozens of self-rejected variants over these details.

### Builder discipline

- Run one practical self-check for the three essentials: script fidelity,
  attribution, and obvious generation/anatomy integrity.
- If those essentials pass, submit the candidate to the critic. Do not keep
  regenerating privately for polish.
- The builder does not simulate the critic, invent stricter numeric rules, or
  reject a readable page because a prompt coordinate, font target, panel share,
  margin, or tail gap is a few pixels off.
- Only an independent critic's essential `REVISE` finding should normally
  trigger another full-page generation.
- When the critic approves, move on. Perfectionism is not the production goal;
  clear, faithful, intact storytelling is.

Every remaining page is generated by a builder and independently judged by a
critic. The builder cannot approve its own work. A candidate reaches the
canonical `pages/` directory only after the critic returns an unconditional
`APPROVED` verdict and the production lead verifies the promoted bytes.

All image generation uses the built-in, subscription-backed Codex ImageGen
path. Do not use `OPENAI_API_KEY`, the bundled image-generation CLI, or another
API-billed route without Andres's explicit approval in the same conversation.

## Builder Role

For one page at a time, the builder must:

1. read the complete authoritative page record and relevant continuity files;
2. attach every required character reference plus the minimum useful accepted
   style/continuity anchors;
3. write a production prompt containing the exact text verbatim, the complete
   panel map, all speaker lanes and tail endpoints, silent roles, identity
   locks and anti-locks, typography rules, and the 1024 × 1536 portrait lock;
4. generate a complete flattened page with native lettering;
5. save it non-destructively under the applicable QA production directory as
   `page-NN-vN.png` and save the prompt beside it;
6. self-check dimensions, exact text, panel order, attribution, identity,
   style, anatomy, objects, and source-size readability;
7. submit the candidate to the critic without promoting it.

An obvious builder-side failure may be replaced before critic review, but the
discarded candidate remains in QA with a `rejected` name or a rejection note.

## Critic Role

The critic receives the candidate path and the locked authorities, not the
builder's desired verdict. It reviews:

- exact text, punctuation, accents, omissions, duplications, and invented text;
- panel count, size hierarchy, causal order, dominant turn, and page ending;
- balloon order, visible speaker ownership, side, tail endpoint, and silence;
- character face, age, hair, body, costume, posture, nearest-lookalike
  avoidance, and continuity with the prior accepted page;
- setting, period objects, motifs, and decisive-prop legibility;
- Velvet Cinema matte-gouache/opaque-watercolor finish rather than smooth oil,
  glossy concept art, photographic realism, or generic digital painting;
- anatomy, hands, faces, bodies, borders, cropping, and accidental duplication;
- 1024 × 1536 RGB output, safe margins, protected text fields, and actual
  desktop/tablet type comfort; source-pixel measurements are diagnostic rather
  than a standalone rejection rule;
- normal desktop-reader comfort without zooming or production notes, followed
  by a secondary tablet-size check;
- whether a cold reader can say what happened, why, what changed, and why the
  next page follows after one normal read.

Apply that list through the reasonable essentials gate above. A finding is
mandatory only when it creates a visible script-fidelity, attribution,
generation-integrity, or genuinely uncomfortable-reading failure. Do not turn
nominal measurements or prompt targets into independent acceptance criteria.

The verdict is exactly one of:

- `APPROVED` — no mandatory fix remains;
- `REVISE` — one or more mandatory findings block promotion.

Mandatory findings and nonblocking observations must be separated. A page is
never approved because a defect is merely recoverable from context.

## Iteration and Promotion

When the critic returns `REVISE`:

1. preserve the failed candidate and report;
2. send the mandatory findings to the builder;
3. change the staging or prompt as needed;
4. regenerate the **complete page** as the next version;
5. run a fresh critic review on the complete replacement.

Never patch, crop-repair, repaint, reletter, tail-swap, or composite part of a
failed page.

When the critic returns `APPROVED`:

1. copy the approved bytes to `pages/page-NN.png`;
2. verify the canonical file is byte-identical to the approved candidate;
3. create a tablet proof when the page's density or layout warrants it;
4. update the QA version map, reader, and handoff;
5. use the new canonical page as the immediate continuity anchor for Page
   `NN+1`.

## Milestone Gates

After Pages 21–30, 31–40, 41–50, and 51–55 are individually approved, run a
fresh sequence-level gate for:

- story continuity and emotional rhythm;
- cross-page identity, costume, setting, object, and style continuity;
- comfortable desktop reading and a secondary tablet check in uninterrupted
  order;
- regressions that are invisible when pages are reviewed alone.

The milestone gate may require full-page regeneration even when an individual
page previously passed.
