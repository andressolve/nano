## Page 01 — DRAMATIC

Entering state: Mara opens her workshop during an ordinary quiet dawn.
Dominant event: An unseen visitor rings and answers without identifying themself.
Exiting state: Mara is alert to a wary invitation from outside.
Reason to turn: The visitor's identity and purpose remain unanswered.
Panel count: 2

### Panel 1

- Purpose: Establish calm, interrupt it, and make Mara the reacting owner.
- Setting/time: Mara's workshop doorway at dawn.
- Visible characters: Mara; the visitor remains off-panel and silent except for the bell.
- Action: Mara opens the workshop; the bell rings outside; she turns toward it.
- Framing/reader order: Read the ordinary opening first, then the bell, then Mara's reaction.
- Continuity: The closed exterior side of the doorway hides the visitor.
- Reader inference: Someone has arrived deliberately, but Mara does not know who.

#### Exact text

- SOUND | BELL | RING

### Panel 2

- Purpose: Convert interruption into a direct but unresolved invitation.
- Setting/time: The same workshop doorway, seconds later.
- Visible characters: Mara; the visitor remains off-panel.
- Action: Mara questions the visitor and receives a deliberately incomplete answer.
- Framing/reader order: Mara's question reads before the off-panel answer.
- Continuity: Mara stays inside; the visitor stays outside and unseen.
- Reader inference: The caller expects trust without offering proof.

#### Exact text

- DIALOGUE | MARA | Who is there?
- DIALOGUE | OFF-PANEL VISITOR | A friend.
