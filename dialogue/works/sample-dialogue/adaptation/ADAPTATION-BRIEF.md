# Adaptation brief

Status: LOCKED

## Governing story

Mara's ordinary morning changes when an unanswered bell asks her to act.

## Story engine

Curiosity presses against caution and creates the page turn.

## Viewpoint and agency

Mara owns the event and must decide whether to answer.

## Adaptation liberties

The unseen caller and setting detail are deliberately omitted.

## Protected claims

The bell, Mara's reaction, and the wary invitation remain.

## Exclusions

No explanation, decorative subplot, or additional character is retained.
