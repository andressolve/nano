# Default Dialogue Studio audience

Unless the owner explicitly names a different audience, the primary readers are
Francisco and Sebastian. As recorded in July 2026, Francisco is 10 and Sebastian
is 7. They can follow dense plots, recurring characters, moral ambiguity, and
stories that do not talk down to them. That capability is not permission for
heavy reading, compressed plot summary, premature proper-noun loads, or
academic explanation.

They respond strongly to:

- scale, spectacle, discovery, wonder, humor, and cinematic set pieces;
- explorable worlds with meaningful surrounding stories;
- tactical intelligence, strategy, stealth, mastery, and physical courage;
- gods, monsters, warriors, factions, legendary objects, and secret identities;
- difficult choices, consequences, reversals, and characters worth caring
  about;
- visual identities and geography clear enough to follow on a first read.

Ultra-premium storytelling for them means:

- immediate emotional access and a clear reason to care;
- sophisticated events told with clarity rather than simplification;
- complexity introduced only after desire, relationship, or action gives it
  meaning;
- scenes dramatized at the duration they deserve, with room for reaction,
  humor, silence, dread, and awe;
- comfortable native lettering and no exposition wall;
- momentum strong enough that the next page is wanted, not merely understood;
- no prestige solemnity, historical lecture, or fidelity burden substituting
  for pleasure.

Each project still writes its own `adaptation/AUDIENCE-PROMISE.md`. It may
refine or override this profile only through an explicit owner decision.
