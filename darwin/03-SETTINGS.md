# 03 — SETTINGS

Recurring locations. Keep them visually consistent across pages.

## Shrewsbury / The Mount (P1)
Darwin's prosperous family home and its gardens, English Midlands, ~1818. Brick Georgian house, walled
garden, a river (the Severn) nearby, English greenery. The boy's world of hedgerows, ponds, and
collecting jars. Soft overcast English light, green-gold foliage.

## Edinburgh operating theatre (P2)
A grim early-1820s surgical lecture theatre: a steep wooden amphitheatre of student benches around a
central wooden operating table, gaslight/candlelight, dark wood, a sense of dread. (Show NO explicit
gore — see script; anchor on Darwin's recoil, the body implied/draped, the crowd above.)

## Christ's College, Cambridge (P3)
Honey-colored Gothic college courts and stone cloisters, ~1830. A study-room with beetle cabinets,
cork specimen trays of pinned beetles, books, a window onto the green court. Warm academic light.

## HMS Beagle — deck & the sea (P4, P5, P6, P12 inset)
The cramped weathered deck of the small survey ship; rigging against sky; the captain's small chart
cabin; the wide grey-green ocean and distant exotic coasts. Brass instruments, coiled rope, sea-light.
The branching motif lives here in the rigging.

## South America — Patagonia / Andes / Chilean coast (P5)
Fossil cliffs with the great bones of extinct mammals; the high Andes with marine seashells embedded
in rock; the raw lifted shoreline after the 1835 earthquake. Vast, geologic, deep-time scale. Dust,
stone, pale cordillera light.

## Galápagos (P6)
Black volcanic lava fields, sparse dry scrub, giant tortoises, marine iguanas on the rocks, a hard
equatorial sun, the Pacific beyond. Strange, stark, primeval.

## Zoological Society, London — specimen room (P7)
A Victorian museum work-room, 1837: glass cabinets, mounted birds, drawers of specimens, a long table
under tall windows. Gould and Darwin over a tray of small birds. Cool north light.

## Darwin's study — Down House (P8, P9, P10, P11, P14, P17)
The patient heart of the book. Darwin's ground-floor study at Down House (and earlier London lodgings
for the 1837–38 beats): a worn armchair, a wall of books, a microscope and dissecting tools, glass jars,
papers and notebooks heaped on a baize-covered table, a fire, a tall window onto an English garden.
Brown, settled, scholarly, lamplit. The world where the evidence was assembled over decades.

## Down House garden / grounds (P11, P17)
The "Sandwalk" gravel path Darwin paced daily, hedgerows, greenhouse, garden beds — his outdoor
laboratory. Soft English greens, patient domestic nature.

## Linnean Society meeting room (P13)
A formal Georgian/Victorian society room, 1 July 1858: a long table, rows of seated gentlemen-naturalists
in dark coats, a reader standing with papers, oil-lamp and window light, portraits on the walls. Sober,
institutional, historic.

## "The world re-seen" / response (P16)
A montage register: Victorian lecture halls and the press storm — caricatures, debate, naturalists at
work in the field seeing the living world differently. Mixed light.

## The Tree of Life (P18 — closing-as-invention)
NOT a literal place — the branching Tree of Life as the structure of the whole page, its boughs holding
small luminous vignettes of Darwin's life (the boy, the Beagle, Galápagos, the notebook, the book),
roots in deep rock and fossil, crown in green-gold living light. The book's visual motif made whole.
