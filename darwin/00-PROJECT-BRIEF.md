## The window (one sentence)
*This bio covers Charles Darwin from a beetle-collecting boy in Shrewsbury (1809) to the publication of* On the Origin of Species *(1859) and his burial beside Newton — the life of the man who, instead of imposing a design on nature, was patient enough to let the evidence of living things declare its own logic.*

## Editorial north star (do NOT violate)
This is one of the great turns in intellectual history: the shift from **deducing nature from design or first principles** to **induction from overwhelming evidence — letting nature speak for itself.** The book renders the **real intellectual milestones and the real people who reached them.** It does **NOT** contort the life into a manufactured emotional "spine."

The honest spine is Darwin's own character as a thinker: **the patience to get it right rather than the haste to get it first.** That patience IS the method — and the method is the milestone.

## The milestones the book is built on (all verified — see `research/RESEARCH-NOTES.md`)
1. The **Beagle voyage** (1831–36): the great accumulation of evidence — fossils, the lifted Chilean coast, Andean seashells. Earth and life are not fixed.
2. **Galápagos, honestly told** (1835): NO epiphany on the islands. He collects; he does not yet see it.
3. **Gould's analysis** (Jan–Mar 1837): the pattern emerges from *expert reading of the specimens* — the facts, carefully read, speak. The book's central methodological beat.
4. **Notebook B, "I think"** (1837): the first branching tree — common descent.
5. **Malthus** (1838): the mechanism — natural selection. The engine of the tree.
6. The **long patient work** (Down House, the 1844 essay withheld, eight years of barnacles): overwhelming the question before answering it.
7. **Wallace's letter** (1858): the same idea, reached independently — proof it was *in the facts.*
8. The **joint Linnean paper** (1 July 1858) and the **Origin** (24 Nov 1859, sold out in a day).
9. **"Endless forms most beautiful"** — the closing grandeur; nature, allowed to speak.

## Deliberate editorial choices (traceable)
- **Galápagos is told WITHOUT the lightning-bolt myth.** The honest version (the pattern came later, from Gould's analysis) is not a weakness to paper over — it is the whole point of the book. (Page 6 → Page 7.)
- **The finches are demoted to their true role.** Mockingbirds + tortoises drove the early doubt; Gould's finch analysis is the turn. We do not stage a finch epiphany on the islands.
- **Wallace gets real co-credit.** The convergence is a milestone, not a footnote — it demonstrates the idea lived in the evidence.
- **No strict-chronology rigidity.** The body is chronological; the closing tree-of-life page (P18) is the closing-as-invention beat, not a dated event.
- **Annie's death (1851)** appears as a restrained true beat inside the barnacle years (P11), NOT as a manufactured emotional spine.
- **Survival of the fittest / human descent / deathbed conversion** are myths — explicitly avoided (see research notes §myths).
