# 02 — CHARACTERS

**Rule:** lock blocks describe the VISUAL, never the famous name. The name lives in the ref filename
and the script, not in the prompt the model sees. Re-Read each ref PNG before writing any page prompt
and write a one-line verbatim observation.

All reference sheets: **1536×1024 landscape**, single figure (or object), **plain warm-toned neutral
background, neutral expression, no text, no labels, no border.** Oil-painting realism + register block
+ anti-drift directive prepended.

**BEARD DISCIPLINE:** young & mid Darwin are CLEAN-SHAVEN. Only the OLD phase has the famous white
beard. State it in every page prompt (see style guide).

## DARWIN — four age phases

### ref_darwin_boy.png  (~9: Shrewsbury collector, P1)
**Lock:** A slight English boy of about nine, fair skin, **straight light-brown hair** cut in a simple early-1820s style, a round open thoughtful face, wide observant grey eyes, a slightly serious mouth. Plain Georgian boy's clothing: a dark short jacket, high collar, breeches. Holding nothing in the ref. Curious, watchful, a born collector. Realistic child anatomy, NOT cute, NOT big-eyed.
**Ref prompt:** half-length portrait + a smaller full-length standing figure on the same sheet, three-quarter view, the watchful light-brown-haired Georgian boy described above, plain warm-grey background, soft window light, no text.

### ref_darwin_young.png  (~22–27: Edinburgh/Cambridge/Beagle, P2–P10)
**Lock:** A slim fresh-faced young Englishman of about twenty-three, fair skin, **CLEAN-SHAVEN, NO beard, NO mustache** (perhaps faint side-whiskers only). **Wavy mid-brown hair**, fairly full, swept back from a broad forehead. A strong straight nose, level grey eyes under fairly heavy brows, a calm earnest expression. Slim athletic build. Early-Victorian gentleman-naturalist's dress: dark tailcoat or frock coat, high white collar, dark stock/cravat, waistcoat. Healthy, outdoorsy, modest. Realistic adult anatomy.
**Ref prompt:** half-length portrait + smaller full-length standing figure, the slim clean-shaven brown-haired young gentleman described above, plain warm-grey background, soft museum light, no text.

### ref_darwin_mid.png  (~45–50: barnacles / Wallace / Origin era, 1850s, P11–P14)
**Lock:** The same man now about forty-eight, **STILL CLEAN-SHAVEN — side-whiskers only, NO full beard.** **Balding at the crown** with a high domed forehead, mid-brown hair going grey at the temples and over the ears, heavier **overhanging brow ridge** giving deep-set tired thoughtful grey eyes, a long face, slightly stooped scholar's posture. Sober dark Victorian frock coat, high collar, dark cravat, waistcoat. The look of a careful, ailing, patient gentleman-scientist. Realistic proportions.
**Ref prompt:** half-length portrait + smaller full-length standing figure, the balding clean-shaven side-whiskered middle-aged Victorian scholar described above, plain warm-grey background, soft study light, no text.

### ref_darwin_old.png  (~70: cover, P16–P18 — the ICONIC Darwin)
**Lock:** The same man now about seventy — the famous portrait. **Completely bald on the crown** with grey hair at the sides, an enormous **heavy overhanging brow**, deep-set grey eyes, and a **very long, full, flowing WHITE beard** covering the chest. A long lined grave face, large prominent forehead. Dark heavy caped overcoat or dark frock coat, sometimes a broad-brimmed black hat in his hand. Patriarchal, weary, wise, monumental. Realistic elderly anatomy.
**Ref prompt:** half-length portrait + smaller full-length seated figure, the bald, heavy-browed, long-white-bearded old man described above, plain warm-grey background, soft window light, no text.

**Ref prompt:** the oil-realism naturalist's plate of a giant tortoise, a black marine iguana, a mockingbird, and several finches with different beak shapes on black volcanic rock described above, plain warm-grey edges, no text, no labels.
