## The window (one sentence)
*This bio covers Gregor Mendel from a peasant boy grafting trees in a Silesian orchard (1822) through the eight patient years of pea-counting in the monastery garden of Brno, the two lectures the world ignored in 1865, and his death in obscurity in 1884 — to the moment in 1900 when three botanists rediscovered the laws he had found first, and the quiet monk became the father of genetics.*

## Editorial north star (do NOT violate)
This is the **companion to Darwin** — the *mechanism* behind the *pattern*. Darwin showed that life **branches**; Mendel found **how the branching is passed on**, the hidden arithmetic inside the tree. The book renders the **real intellectual milestones and the real people**, told **milestone-first.** It does **NOT** contort the life into a manufactured emotional "spine." (Per [[feedback-let-the-subject-be]].)

The honest human through-line is Mendel's own character as a thinker: **where everyone else described heredity, he COUNTED it.** He turned a mystery into arithmetic — and the patience that let him grow 28,000 plants over eight years is the same patience that let him die without ever seeing the world catch up. The method (counting, ratios, the discrete "factor") IS the milestone. The tragedy (right answer, 35 years too early) is true, not staged.

## The milestones the book is built on (all verified — see `research/RESEARCH-NOTES.md`)
1. **Boyhood on the farm** (Heinzendorf, 1820s–30s): a gardener's eye, learned grafting from his father; deep poverty after his father is crippled.
2. **The hard climb to learning** (Olomouc): hunger, tutoring, illness from overwork; **sister Theresia gives up her dowry** so he can study.
3. **The monastery as refuge** (St. Thomas, Brno, 1843): takes the name Gregor; **Abbot Napp** runs the abbey as a house of science. Failed at pastoral work → redirected to his real gift.
4. **Vienna** (1851–53): **Doppler's physics** teaches him to see nature in **numbers and combinations** — the methodological seed.
5. **The failed exam** (twice, 1850 & 1856): the man who would rewrite biology fails his biology certification — which frees him for the garden.
6. **The question & the method**: heredity was believed to be **blending** (offspring = the average of the parents). Mendel suspected something **discrete**. He chose the pea for its clean either/or traits; spent **two years just making pure lines**; tracked **seven traits** across **~28,000 plants**, and he **counted**.
7. **The vanishing-and-returning trait**: tall × short → all tall; the short *disappears*… then returns in **1 of 4** of the next generation.
8. **The 3:1 / the breakthrough** (**5474 round : 1850 wrinkled ≈ 3:1**): heredity is not blending — it is arithmetic. Hidden **"factors,"** one from each parent, that separate and recombine. (Hero page — the pea square.)
9. **Darwin's missing piece**: Mendel **reads** Darwin's *Origin*; he holds the mechanism Darwin's tree needs. They live at the same time and never connect. (Verified: Mendel read Darwin; Darwin never read Mendel.)
10. **The two lectures** (8 Feb & 8 Mar 1865, Brünn society) → **the 1866 paper** (primary-source artifact): read to a polite, silent room; published; ignored.
11. **The silence & Nägeli**: the famous botanist doesn't understand; steers him to **hawkweed**, which cheats his method. Years lost.
12. **Abbot & the long quiet** (1868–1884): administration and a tax fight swallow the science; bees, weather, sunspots; *"My time will come"* (traditionally attributed); death in obscurity, 1884; papers burned.
13. **The rediscovery** (1900): de Vries, Correns, Tschermak independently find the laws — and find the monk got there first. Vindication.
14. **Closing-as-invention**: the pea-square / hidden arithmetic becomes the structure of the page — Mendel's factors are the gears inside Darwin's tree of life; from peas to all of genetics. His time came.

## Deliberate editorial choices (traceable)
- **The Darwin–Mendel beat is told from the TRUE, poignant side** (Mendel read Darwin; Darwin never read Mendel) — the "uncut Mendel paper in Darwin's library" legend is a MYTH and is avoided. (Research notes §myths.)
- **The "lucky unlettered monk" framing is rejected** — the book shows the Vienna mathematical training as the real engine of the method.
- **Fisher's "too good to be true" statistical critique is deliberately OMITTED** — not a milestone, a nuanced controversy that would muddy a triumphant-but-honest arc; modern scholarship rejects deliberate fraud. (Traceable per bio.md §2.5.)
- **"My time will come" is framed as traditionally attributed** ("he is said to have…"), never as a hard quote. The burning of his papers is framed as reported/traditional.
- **No strict-chronology rigidity**: the body is chronological; the closing pea-square page (P17) is the closing-as-invention beat, not a dated event.
- **Heredity-as-blending is established early** as the wrong idea the world held, so the discreteness of Mendel's "factors" lands as the real revelation.
