# 02 — CHARACTERS

**Rule:** lock blocks describe the VISUAL, never the famous name. The name lives in the ref filename
and the script, not in the prompt the model sees. Re-Read each ref PNG before writing any page prompt
and write a one-line verbatim observation.

All reference sheets: **1536×1024 landscape**, single figure (or object), **plain warm-toned neutral
background, neutral expression, no text, no labels, no border.** Oil-painting realism + register block
+ anti-drift directive prepended.

**MENDEL DISCIPLINE:** clean-shaven in every phase (NO beard ever). The iconic phase is stout,
round-faced, gold oval spectacles, black Augustinian habit. The abbot phase adds a pectoral cross.
State the phase in every page prompt (see style guide).

**Multi-character strategy:** every page is designed to hold **≤2 named faces** (Strategy 1) — lock the
secondary face via `edit_image`, prose Mendel from his lock block. NO composite plate needed.

## MENDEL — four age phases

### ref_mendel_boy.png  (~14: Heinzendorf farm boy, P1–P2)
**Lock:** A sturdy Central-European peasant boy of about fourteen, fair skin a little weathered by
outdoor work, **straight mid-brown hair** cut plainly, a round open earnest face, calm attentive
grey-blue eyes, a serious thoughtful mouth. Plain early-19th-century country clothing: a coarse
linen shirt, a simple dark waistcoat, rough trousers. NO glasses. A careful, watchful boy with a
gardener's hands. Realistic child/adolescent anatomy, NOT cute, NOT big-eyed.
**Ref prompt:** half-length portrait + a smaller full-length standing figure on the same sheet,
three-quarter view, the sturdy brown-haired peasant boy described above, plain warm-grey background,
soft daylight, no text.

### ref_mendel_young.png  (~23: Olomouc student → young friar, P3–P6)
**Lock:** A slim young Central-European man of about twenty-three, fair skin, **clean-shaven, NO beard,
NO mustache**. Short dark-brown hair, a broad forehead, a round-ish earnest face — recognizably the
same person who will grow stout — level grey-blue eyes, an attentive humble expression. Slim, not yet
heavy. Plain dark clothing of a poor student, transitioning to the **long black Augustinian friar's
habit** (a simple black cassock, high collar). Thin gold oval spectacles only just appearing / often
absent. Realistic adult anatomy.
**Ref prompt:** half-length portrait + smaller full-length standing figure, the slim clean-shaven
dark-haired young man in a plain black cassock described above, plain warm-grey background, soft
window light, no text.

### ref_mendel_mid.png  (~38: THE ICONIC experimenter monk, 1856–66, P7–P13) — MOST USED
**Lock:** A **stout, full-figured** Central-European man of about thirty-eight, fair skin,
**clean-shaven, NO beard, NO mustache**, a **round full face**, a high forehead with **short dark hair
receding at the temples**, calm intelligent grey-blue eyes behind **gold oval wire-rimmed
spectacles** — the friendly, heavy, bespectacled look. He wears the **long black Augustinian friar's
habit** (a black cassock buttoned to the neck, high black collar). Patient, mild, sharp-eyed, the
hands of a gardener. Realistic proportions, genuinely heavy-set, NOT gaunt.
**Ref prompt:** half-length portrait + smaller full-length standing figure, the stout round-faced
clean-shaven bespectacled monk in a black cassock described above, plain warm-grey background, soft
study light, no text.

### ref_mendel_abbot.png  (~52: abbot & late life, P14–P17 + cover — the iconic face + regalia)
**Lock:** The same stout round-faced **clean-shaven** man now about fifty-two, **heavier and greyer**,
the same high receding forehead and **gold oval wire-rimmed spectacles**, calm tired kindly grey-blue
eyes, jowls a little fuller. He wears the **black Augustinian habit of an abbot** with a **gold/silver
pectoral cross hanging on a chain over the chest**, a high black collar; a black biretta or skull-cap
may rest nearby. Dignified, weary, mild, monumental — a heavy old monk. Realistic elderly-middle-aged
anatomy.
**Ref prompt:** half-length portrait + smaller full-length seated figure, the heavy grey clean-shaven
bespectacled abbot in a black habit with a pectoral cross on a chain described above, plain warm-grey
background, soft window light, no text.

**Ref prompt:** half-length + small full-length, the plain center-parted dark-haired young peasant

**Ref prompt:** half-length + small full-length, the lean dark-haired clean-shaven Austrian professor

## OBJECTS (recurring — refs keep them consistent)

### ref_peas.png  (P7, P8, P9, P10, P17 — the KEY object ref, reused most)
**Lock:** A naturalist's study-plate of **garden pea plants and their seven either/or traits** in oil
realism: tall climbing pea vines beside short dwarf ones; **round smooth peas beside wrinkled
shrivelled peas**; **yellow peas beside green peas**; open pea pods (one plump and inflated, one
pinched and constricted); **violet/purple pea blossoms beside white pea blossoms**. Green leaves,
tendrils, a few pods split to show the peas in a row inside. Arranged like a careful field study, the
contrasting pairs clearly visible. Warm daylight, painterly.
**Ref prompt:** the oil-realism naturalist's study-plate of garden pea plants showing tall vs short
vines, round vs wrinkled peas, yellow vs green peas, inflated vs pinched pods, and violet vs white
blossoms described above, plain warm-grey edges, no text, no labels.

### ref_garden.png  (P7, P8, P9 — the monastery garden & greenhouse where the peas grew)
**Lock:** A walled **monastery kitchen-garden** in mid-19th-century Moravia: ordered rectangular beds,
ruled rows of pea plants climbing up sticks and string, gravel paths, a lean-to **glass greenhouse**
against an old stone wall, terracotta pots, the grey-stone bulk of the abbey rising behind, soft warm
daylight. Green and orderly, patient, enclosed. No figures.
**Ref prompt:** the walled monastery kitchen-garden with ordered rows of staked pea plants, a lean-to
glass greenhouse against an old stone wall, and the grey abbey behind described above, soft daylight,
painterly, no text, no figures.

## REF GENERATION ORDER
1. `ref_mendel_mid.png` first (most-used, the iconic phase — establishes the face).
2. Then the other three Mendel phases (boy / young / abbot) — check they read as the same person aging.
3. Then the cast (napp, theresia, doppler, nageli).
4. Then objects (peas, garden).

**10 refs total.** Review every one against its lock block before any page is generated.
