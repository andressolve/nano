## QUIZ (5 questions, WHY not WHAT, shuffled answers, length-matched distractors) — see index.html
Planned correct-answer positions: b / a / c / b / a (no length tells; distractors carry period detail).
1. Why did Mendel choose garden peas for his experiments? → (b) because they came in clear either/or kinds and he could control exactly which plant pollinated which.
2. Why was Mendel's COUNTING the key to his discovery, when other naturalists had looked at heredity for centuries? → (a) because the answer only showed up as a ratio across thousands of plants — patient counting revealed the hidden 3:1 pattern that a few plants would hide.
3. Why did the discovery that all the first children were tall (and none medium) overturn the old idea of heredity? → (c) because if traits truly blended, a tall and short parent should give a medium plant — instead one trait won and the other vanished, proving traits stay separate.
4. Why did Mendel's work sit ignored for over thirty years? → (b) because he presented tables of numbers and a strange new arithmetic of life to audiences and experts who had no way to grasp what it meant.
5. Why are Mendel and Darwin remembered as two halves of one story? → (a) because Darwin showed that life branches and changes, while Mendel found the hidden rules that pass traits down each branch — the mechanism Darwin's theory was missing.
