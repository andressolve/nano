# Characters — The Tombs of Atuan · Part One

Locks are visual only — never put "Tenar," "Ged," or "Le Guin" in a generation prompt; the description is the lock. Every 2+-page character gets a ref. Skin tones are canonical and non-negotiable (see brief / style guide).

## TENAR — phase 1: the child (P1–P4, ages 5–6)
**Ref:** `refs/ref_tenar_child.png`
> A small girl of about six with PALE WHITE skin, straight BLACK hair cut short, large dark watchful eyes, solemn round face, tiny slight build. P1: a rough undyed homespun shift, barefoot, running in an orchard. P2–P4: first a plain straight white shift, then a black robe and hood far too solemn for her size. Realistic child anatomy. NOT cute, NOT mascot proportions, NOT oversized eyes.

## ARHA — phase 2: the young priestess (P5–P20, ages 12–15; P5–P7 twelve, P8 on fifteen)
**Ref:** `refs/ref_arha.png`
> A girl of about fifteen with PALE WHITE skin, straight BLACK hair in a single braid down her back, dark fierce watchful eyes, a still proud face that rarely smiles, slight wiry build. Rough black homespun robes, black hood often up; at her belt an iron ring with thirteen keys and a small dagger. Bearing: contained, imperious, and very alone. Realistic proportions, NOT oversized eyes.

P5–P7 (age twelve): same lock, slightly younger and smaller, no key-ring.

## GED / SPARROWHAWK (P15–P20, mid-twenties)
**Ref:** REUSE `../earthsea/refs/ref_ged_scarred.png` — copy into `refs/` before production.
> A lean harsh-faced young man with DEEP RED-BROWN COPPER skin — NOT pale, NOT olive, NOT light tan — straight black hair, hawk-like features, weathered and calm. FOUR PARALLEL WHITISH SCARS rake down the LEFT side of his face, from beside the left eye across the cheek to the jaw, CLEARLY VISIBLE against his dark skin. Carries a wizard's staff of dark yew wood as tall as himself; at its tip, when lit, a soft silver-violet werelight. Winter traveler's clothes: short heavy cloak, leather tunic, wool leggings, laced sandals; a small pack, a leather water bottle, a heavy chain at his neck bearing a rough crescent of dark metal. P18–P20: gaunt, dust-caked, exhausted — but the same face.

## MANAN (P4–P11, P14, P19 — Arha's warden)
**Ref:** `refs/ref_manan.png`
> A big heavy eunuch past middle age with YELLOWISH-PALE skin, completely hairless head like a peeled potato, tiny brown eyes in folds of flesh, great flat slabs of cheek, a lipless slit mouth, thick burly build gone fat. Worn black robes, torn at the hem and too short for him. His whole bearing is stolid, careful sadness; his devotion to the girl he guards is the one warm thing in him.

## KOSSIL — High Priestess of the Godking (P7, P9–P11, P18)
**Ref:** `refs/ref_group_priestesses.png` (group sheet, left panel)
> A tall HEAVY woman of middle age, PALE WHITE skin, a broad heavy cold face with no expression, small hard eyes. Bulky black winter robes and black cowl that make her a mass of darkness with a pale face. Heavy deliberate tread. Cruelty without heat: she holds nothing sacred but power.

## THAR — High Priestess of the Twin Gods (P5, P7, P13; dies before P14)
**Ref:** `refs/ref_group_priestesses.png` (group sheet, center panel)
> A tall woman grown old, PALE WHITE skin, dry and lean and thin as the legbone of a deer, a stern narrow deeply-lined face, grey hair bound tight. Plain black robes. Rigid, taciturn, iron-lawed — but never cruel. Beside Kossil she reads as blade-thin verticality against bulk.

## PENTHE (P6, P12 — novice, Arha's only friend)
**Ref:** `refs/ref_group_priestesses.png` (group sheet, right panel)
> A girl of Arha's age with PALE WHITE skin that flushes cherry-pink, a soft round comfortable face, ready smile, plump build. Black novice's robe always looking slightly rumpled. Warm, easy, unafraid of pleasure — the one splash of ordinary human happiness in the Place.

## Prose-only (single page or background)
- **Tenar's mother** (P1): a young peasant woman, white skin, long FAIR hair the color of firelight, warm tired face, homespun dress.
- **Tenar's father** (P1): a lean weathered peasant, white skin, bitter grieving face, bare feet, earth-clotted hoe.
- **The sacrificer and the stayer** (P2): a figure in a belted WHITE wool gown, face masked white, holding a five-foot polished sword; and a figure all in BLACK, faceless, slender arms staying the sacrificer's raised arms. Both dancer-like, frozen in balance.
- **Priestesses / novices** (P2–P3, P5, background): women and girls in black robes and hoods, white-skinned, torchlit, walking four by four.
- **The three prisoners** (P10): three huddled shapes chained to a rock wall in torch-smoke, faces hidden by matted hair and beard shadow — barely legible as men. Never detailed.
- **Ged's werelight** (P15 on): not a character but lock it like one — a soft silver-VIOLET wisp at the staff tip, smokeless, unconsuming, haloing everything near it in cool light.
