# Settings — The Tombs of Atuan · Part One

Every location below maps to a descent-strip anchor (reader footer). Depth increases through the volume.

## THE ORCHARD (P1) — depth 0
An apple orchard in a deep green valley of Atuan, twilight, early spring. Trees on the eve of blossoming — here and there one early flower, rose and white, like a faint star. Thick new wet grass. A one-room peasant hut with firelight in the doorway. Over the high hills, the evening star, piercing clear. **The only green page in the book.**

## THE PLACE (P3, P5–P7, P12 exteriors) — depth 1
The Place of the Tombs of Atuan: a walled temple compound on a dry hill against desert mountains. Everything tawny, dust-ochre, bone. Three temples: the gold-roofed Temple of the Twin Gods (a windowless white cube), the newer colonnaded Temple of the Godking (thick white columns, painted capitals), and above them all, tawny and ruinous as the desert itself, the huge low HALL OF THE THRONE — patched walls, flattish crumbling dome. The Big House and the Small House; the "Men's Wall" of fitted rock, ten feet, climbable; beyond it the flat plains going on and on. Desert light: harsh, white, shadowless at noon; violet-grey dusk; hard stars.

## THE HALL OF THE THRONE, interior (P2–P3, P16) — depth 2
A vast ruined columned hall. Gaps in the roof where masonry has collapsed; unsteady shafts of sunlight aslant through dust; frost-rimmed weeds between marble paving-tiles. Double rows of thick pale columns like the calves of immense legs. The EMPTY THRONE on its platform up seven broad steps of red-veined marble: huge, black, glinting dully with gold and jewels glazed in dust, cobwebbed, streaked with owl droppings — curtained on each side with webs of blackness that may be curtains or may be shadow. The three highest steps have never been climbed: one slope of grey unstirred dust. Owls nest in the dome.

## THE TOMBSTONES (P8, cover) — depth 2
Inside the loop of the great mortarless Tomb Wall on the hillcrest behind the Hall: NINE black standing stones, eighteen to twenty feet, like huge fingers out of the earth. One stands straight, others lean, two have fallen. Crusted grey and orange lichen — except one, naked and black with a dull gloss. Vague carvings under the lichen, shapes with no meaning left. Cold even at desert noon. Full of meaning, and no saying what they mean.

## THE UNDERTOMB (P9, P11, P15–P16) — depth 3
The great vaulted cavern directly beneath the Tombstones. **Before P15 it is rendered in ABSOLUTE darkness** — it has never been seen, only felt: echoes, cold damp air, walls "rough as lace here, smooth as brass there." **On P15 the werelight reveals it:** jeweled with crystals, pinnacles and filigrees of white limestone, glittering roof and walls, a palace of diamonds, a house of amethyst and crystal. Entered by the red-rock Prisoners' Door (a rough four-foot doorway in a lava outcrop, opened by a long iron key, opening only inward) or the trapdoor behind the Throne. Light is forbidden here. The three prisoners lie in shallow graves in its floor.

## THE ROOM OF CHAINS (P10) — depth 3
A large low room off the lesser maze, hewn stone, lit by ONE fuming torch on a chain — the smoke has no outlet, the air is foul haze. Great iron rings driven into the rock wall; manacles; three huddled shapes. A small wooden trapdoor square in the ceiling for lowering food. The torchlight is reddish, thick, horrible — the only lit room under the hill, and the worst.

## THE LABYRINTH (P14, P17–P20) — depth 4
The great maze under the whole Place: miles of tunnels, five feet wide, twelve to fifteen high, walls of close-fitted dressed stone leaning inward to a vault, floors of rough rock, dead air. All tunnels alike, crossing and recrossing; navigation only by counted turnings. Entered through the IRON DOOR (opens only from outside; a rounded iron bar-handle drops with a grinding clash and blue sparks). Light is permitted here — a single candle-lantern pool moving through black. Spy holes pierce down into it from every building of the Place, hidden under floor tiles.

## THE PAINTED ROOM (P18–P20) — depth 5
A room in the Labyrinth with a lockable door and a spy hole in its ceiling. On its four walls, ancient wall-drawings that leap out of the dark at candle-gleam: human forms with long drooping wings and great eyes, serene and morose — the spirits of the damned who are not reborn. They watch everything that happens in the room with sad, blind eyes.

## THE SMALL HOUSE (P4, P12, P17) — depth 1
The House of the One Priestess, where Arha sleeps alone: a windowless room higher than it is long, a porch with steps where Penthe sits, a locked closet at the hall's end with a hidden spy hole under a floor tile. Rushlight and starlight only.
