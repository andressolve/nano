## Coarse beat map (NOT close-read — verify everything)

- **Ch 7–8:** Arha keeps Ged alive in the Painted Room; visits, questions, food and water in secret; Kossil's suspicion grows. Ged tells her of the outer world and of names. Thar's backstory (told earlier to Arha) becomes load-bearing here: the lost half of the ring of Erreth-Akbe — Thoreg of Hupun, Ensar, Anthil.
- **Ch 9:** The ring — the two halves; the Bond-Rune; the joining. Ged names her: **Tenar**. The center of the whole volume.
- **Ch 10:** Kossil's open move (she digs in the false grave, proves the "sacrifice" lives); Arha's crisis between the dark and the light; the decision to flee. **Manan dies** — he follows to protect her and Ged casts him into the pit ("He died because he loved me, and was faithful. He thought he was protecting me. He held the sword above my neck." — quote candidate, verify context/line).
- **Ch 11:** Escape through the Undertomb as the Nameless Ones wake; **the earthquake — "The Tombstones fell on their graves"**; flight across the western mountains; Tenar's guilt and the knife scene; Ged: **"You were the vessel of evil. The evil is poured out... you were made to hold light, as a lamp burning holds and gives its light"** (verify verbatim). Also: **"I would come from my grave if you called me, Tenar!"** (speaker/context to verify — likely Manan earlier; confirm).
- **Ch 12:** *Lookfar* (painted eyes, mage-wind), the open sea, the plan to take Tenar to **Ogion on Gont**; arrival at **Havnor**, the ring flashing on her wrist; final line: **"Gravely she walked beside him up the white streets of Havnor, holding his hand, like a child coming home."**

## Register continuity + deltas

- Same desert-bone register, THREE LIGHTS, DARKNESS RULE, skin-tone law as Vol 2 (`01-STYLE-GUIDE.md` here is the source). Werelight silver-violet `#9c8ad6` remains Ged's signature.
- **New light event:** the earthquake and the escape into DAYLIGHT — the first sun Arha/Tenar sees as a free person. Plan the palette turn deliberately (bone/black → real sky and sea color) the way Vol 2 planned P15's werelight reveal.
- **New setting types with no Vol 2 precedent:** the Great Treasury (dust, chests, the half-ring), the western mountains in winter, the open sea aboard Lookfar, Havnor harbor (white streets, bridges, towers). Sea pages can lean on Vol 1's maritime vocabulary (`../earthsea/01-STYLE-GUIDE.md`) — that's the register these two inversions share.
- **Descent-strip inversion:** Vol 2's footer descends depth 0→5. Vol 3's natural strip is the *ascent/escape* — consider LABYRINTH · UNDERTOMB · THE FALLEN STONES · THE MOUNTAINS · THE SEA · HAVNOR. Label idea: "The way out of the dark."
