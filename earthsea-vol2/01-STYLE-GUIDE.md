# Style Guide — The Tombs of Atuan · Part One

Same ink-line family as Vol 1 (*A Wizard of Earthsea*), inverted palette: Vol 1 was cold sea-mist; Vol 2 is bleached desert above and absolute dark below.

## STYLE BLOCK — paste verbatim into every prompt, first

> Bold ink-line comic-book art with FLAT muted color. Clean confident black linework, flat color fills, minimal soft rendering, dynamic composition. The palette is sun-bleached desert and darkness: bone-white, dust-ochre, tawny sand, dry sage-grey, black robes, deep charcoal stone. LOW SATURATION throughout. Underground scenes are near-black, lit only by small warm pools of candle or torch flame — except werelight, which is a soft silver-VIOLET glow, the only cool light in the book. Dust, heat-haze, and enormous empty sky are part of every outdoor scene.

## THE THREE LIGHTS — the volume's signature, paste the relevant line on every page

1. **Surface light:** harsh, bleached, shadowless desert sun (or thin cold starlight). It flattens; it does not warm.
2. **Permitted flame:** candle, rushlight, torch, hearth — small WARM ORANGE pools swallowed by darkness at their edge. The only warmth underground.
3. **WERELIGHT (Ged's magic only):** soft silver-VIOLET marshlight glow, smokeless, unconsuming. It appears on NO page before P15. When it appears, it is the most beautiful thing in the image.

Green and blossom-rose exist ONLY on P1 (the apple orchard — the life Tenar loses) and as Penthe's yellow apples on P12. Nowhere else.

## DARKNESS RULE — paste verbatim on every underground page (P9–P11, P14–P20)

> The darkness is ABSOLUTE FLAT MATTE BLACK — no ambient fill light, no blue wash, no visible detail outside the lit pool. Figures at the edge of light dissolve into black. The black is the largest shape on the page.

(This is Vol 1's SHADOW rule transferred to the Tombs themselves: the Nameless Ones are never drawn; the black IS their presence.)

## ANTI-DRIFT — paste verbatim into every prompt

> NOT a children's book. Serious mythic tone, realistic proportions, cinematic staging.

For every page with child-Tenar (P1–P4) add: `Realistic child anatomy. NOT cute, NOT mascot proportions, NOT oversized eyes.`

## Skin-tone law — first visual law, cross-check every gen

- **Kargs are WHITE.** Tenar/Arha pale white skin, black hair. Manan yellowish-pale. Kossil, Thar, Penthe, all priestesses, novices, guards: white-skinned. Fair/yellow hair common in background Kargs (Tenar's mother is fair).
- **Ged is DEEP RED-BROWN COPPER** — NOT pale, NOT olive, NOT light tan, NOT ambiguous. Reuse Vol 1's `ref_ged_scarred.png`. Four parallel whitish scars on the LEFT cheek, CLEARLY VISIBLE.
- This inversion (white protagonist among white people; the one dark face is the wizard from the sorcerers' islands) is canonical and deliberate. If a ref or page drifts, regenerate with intensified unambiguous language — do not pass the gate on "looks okay."

## Layout rules (narrative mode, unchanged from Vol 1)

- 3:2 landscape, 1536×1024, quality high.
- 3–4 panels per page, layout stated explicitly ("THREE panels: two equal panels across the TOP, one WIDE panel across the BOTTOM"), clean solid-black borders, clear white gutters. Single-scene splash allowed for hero beats (P2, P8, P15).
- Generous text: multiple bubbles + caption boxes per page is correct. Keep each element legible.
- Caption boxes: weathered parchment tint, dark serif text. Speech bubbles: off-white, dark serif, tails explicitly aimed. NO quotation marks inside bubbles.
- Chapter-place tags: small dust-ochre banner, top-left of the page's first panel (THE ORCHARD / THE PLACE / THE UNDERTOMB / THE LABYRINTH…) — these double as descent-strip anchors.
- Underground panels may use WHITE-ON-BLACK caption boxes (charcoal box, pale text) when the page is mostly dark — prototype this on P9 before trusting it.

## Lettering block template

Every prompt ends with:

```
LETTERING — verbatim, render exactly:
[element list: panel, type, position, exact string]
RESTRICTIONS: All words spelled correctly. Do not duplicate text. Do not invent extra captions or signage. Do not put quotation marks inside speech bubbles. NO modern objects, NO watermarks.
```
