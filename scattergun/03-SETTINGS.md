# 03 — SETTINGS

## The trap field (P1, P12, P13, cover sky)

Modern clay-shooting range at dawn: mowed green grass field falling away from a concrete shooting line, a low pale-green wooden trap house half-sunken downrange, golden low sun, long shadows, autumn trees far at the field's edge, big warm sky with scattered clouds. Shooters wear earmuffs and shooting vests, always seen from behind or three-quarter rear.

## The Ogden gun shop (P8)

Frontier gunsmith's workshop, Utah, late 1800s: rough plank walls, racks of long guns in wooden cradles, a heavy scarred workbench with a leg vise, hand files and chisels and a small forge glow, oil lamps, wood shavings on the floor, bright dry desert light through one small window.

## The ivory poster sheet (P2–P4, P6, P7, P9–P11, P14)

Warm ivory aged-paper background, faint plate-edge border, painted central object(s), 4–6 ivory callout boxes with thin dark leader lines, dark serif lettering. 1900s sporting-catalog engineering-poster feel, painted not vector. Same painter's hand as FIELD pages.

## The pattern range (P5)

Plain test range: a string of white paper sheets on wooden frames at increasing distances down a grass lane, each sheet peppered with a wider, thinner cluster of holes; distance markers as small painted wooden posts. Overcast neutral light so the paper reads clearly.
