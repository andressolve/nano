# 01 — STYLE GUIDE

One register, two page modes — same architecture as Sky Duel, new palette. Deliberately NOT the oil-painting biography register and NOT Sky Duel's aerospace blues: this volume gets a warm field-sports look.

## STYLE BLOCK — paste verbatim into every prompt

> Premium sporting-arms illustration — crisp, highly detailed digital painting in the style of a high-end field-sports book plate. Warm controlled palette: oiled walnut browns, blued-steel gray-black, polished brass gold, hunter-orange clay targets, golden field light, big dawn skies. Dramatic cinematic lighting, sharp material detail on wood grain and machined steel, correct mechanical proportions.

## REGISTER BLOCK — paste verbatim into every prompt

> Realistic illustration. NOT a comic. NO halftones, NO cel shading, NO cartoon outlines. Painterly-crisp rendering, cinematic lighting.

## ANTI-DRIFT DIRECTIVE — paste verbatim into every prompt

> NOT a children's book. Serious mature illustrated technical book, realistic proportions, natural lighting, cinematic composition.

## Page modes

**FIELD mode** (cinematic): full-bleed painted scene — trap field at dawn, workshop interior, pattern range. Caption bands top/bottom in the Honda formula — "off-white box, dark serif text, readable."

**POSTER mode** (cutaway/diagram): warm ivory vintage-engineering-poster spread — central painted object (shell cutaway, action mechanism, choke section), 4–6 callout caption boxes arrayed around it with thin leader lines, dark serif text. Think 1900s sporting-catalog cutaway poster, painted not vector. Same painter's hand as FIELD pages.

## Text treatment

- Caption boxes: "off-white box, dark serif text, readable" (FIELD) / "ivory callout box, dark serif text, thin leader line to the part" (POSTER).
- `LETTERING — verbatim, render exactly:` block last in every prompt. Restrictions block closes every prompt.
- No speech bubbles in this volume (Browning's pages are caption-only; his one sourced quote renders as a caption).
- No brand names painted on guns: NO "Winchester", NO "Browning" lettering on metal or wood. Engraving is scrollwork only.

## Safety composition rules (every prompt with a firearm)

- Muzzle always pointing up-range, away from viewer, or held open/at rest. Never toward camera, never toward a person.
- Human shooters rendered from behind or three-quarter rear, range setting, ear and eye protection on modern-day figures.

## Restrictions block — paste verbatim, closes every prompt

> All words spelled correctly. Do not duplicate text. Do not invent extra captions or labels. NO watermarks, NO modern logos, NO brand names, NO spurious signage or stenciling.
