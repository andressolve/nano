# 03 — SETTINGS

All 1570s–1630s Germany/Austria/Bohemia. Muted palette per Style Block; candle-gold interiors, cold blue
nights. War appears only as distant glow/smoke/passing soldiers.

## S1 — Weil der Stadt, night hilltop (P1)
Small Swabian free city below: half-timbered gables, town wall, church spire, snow-dusted rooftops, 1577.
Vast cold night sky dominating the frame; the Great Comet with a long curved tail. Lantern-warm windows.

## S2 — Tübingen lecture room (P2)
1590s university hall: dark oak benches, leaded windows on the Neckar, candle sconces, a wooden armillary
sphere (nested rings model of the heavens), chalked diagrams on a dark wooden board. Scholars' gowns.

## S3 — Graz classroom / Stiftsschule (P3)
Protestant school hall, 1595: rows of plain desks, tall windows with summer light, large dark wooden board
on an easel with chalk geometry (triangle inscribed between two circles). Renaissance arcaded courtyard
glimpsed outside.

## S4 — Graz house / city gate (P4)
Modest prosperous burgher rooms; then the stone city gate with a loaded wagon, family bundles, posted edict
on the gate wall. Styrian hills beyond.

## S5 — Benátky castle (P5–P6)
Tycho's Renaissance château above the Jizera river: sgraffito-patterned facades. Interior: a great
observation hall with brass instruments — a huge mural quadrant arc on one wall, celestial globes, sextants,
leather-bound observation logs stacked like treasure. Servants, assistants in the background.

## S6 — Tycho's bedchamber, Prague (P7)
October 1601: heavy curtained bed, candles, dark wood, the gold elephant-medallion chain on the bedside
table, grieving assistants in shadow.

## S7 — Kepler's Prague study (P8–P12)
Karlova street rooms: small, cramped, warm candlelight against blue night in the leaded window. Desk buried
in handwritten calculation sheets (columns of arithmetic), inkpots, dividers, Tycho's log-books. Through the
window: Prague spires (St. Vitus, bridge tower) and the night sky with ONE small red-orange point — Mars.

## S8 — Prague, dark year (P13)
The same rooms drained of warmth: cold grey light, an empty small bed in shadow, packed trunks. Outside,
soldiers on the Charles Bridge, banners. NO Mars-red on this page.

## S9 — Linz (P14–P15)
Danube town under a castle hill. Interiors: plainer than Prague — whitewashed walls, smaller desk, the same
spreading calculation sheets. P14 left zone: stone church door. P14 right zone: wedding table with wine
barrels. P15: night study with sheets of ruled musical staves and geometric diagrams; through the window a
far-off dull red glow on the horizon (the war beginning — the page's only red).

## S10 — Güglingen council chamber (P16)
Bare stone room, 1621: heavy table with documents and seals, two magistrates and a clerk (unnamed, dark
robes), high small window with one shaft of light falling on a very small upright old woman. Her son stands
beside her with a thick written defense. NO instruments, NO violence — papers, stone, light.

## S11 — Ulm print shop (P17)
Timber-framed workshop: wooden hand-press, ink balls, drying pages on lines, type cases; the huge unfinished
Minster tower through the window. Fresh-printed star tables in the foreground.

## S12 — Regensburg sickroom (P18)
November 1630: small inn room, narrow bed, a single candle, account papers on the coverlet, a frost-pale
window with the stone bridge and towers beyond — and one small red-orange point low in the winter sky.

## S13 — The finale sky (P19)
A vast deep-blue starfield filling the page. A great thin GOLDEN ELLIPSE spans it, the Sun a small warm
blaze at one focus (lower left), Mars a small red-orange point riding the ellipse line. Small painted
vignettes stationed like medallions along the orbit line. At bottom center, a tiny dark silhouette of a man
looking up.
