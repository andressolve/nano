# 01 — STYLE GUIDE

## Style Block (paste verbatim into EVERY prompt)
> Oil-painting realism in the tradition of 17th-century German and Dutch masters. Muted period palette:
> deep cold night blues, warm candle-gold interiors, umber browns, half-timber and stone grey. Chiaroscuro
> candlelight; vast starfields over small human figures. Painted brushwork, cinematic composition, natural
> lighting.

## Register block (paste verbatim into EVERY prompt)
> Oil-painting realism. NOT a comic. NO halftones, NO cel shading, NO ink linework. Painted brushwork,
> cinematic lighting, muted period palette.

## Anti-drift directive (paste verbatim into EVERY prompt)
> NOT a children's book. Serious mature graphic novel, realistic proportions, natural lighting, cinematic
> composition.

For the child-Kepler page add: `Realistic child anatomy. NOT cute, NOT mascot proportions, NOT oversized eyes.`

## The Mars-red accent (the volume's signature)
- ONE accent color: **Mars-red / ember red-orange** (`#d1603d` family). It appears as the small burning
  point of Mars in night skies, a wax seal, an ember, the distant glow of war on the horizon (P15).
- It is the light that refused to obey. Use it small and deliberate — a point, not a wash.
- **P13 (the disaster year): NO Mars-red anywhere.** The accent's absence is the emotional turn
  (same device as Tesla's late hotel pages).
- Everything else stays in the muted night-blue/candle-gold/umber register. No bright saturated colors.

## Caption + lettering rules
- Caption boxes: "off-white parchment box, dark serif text, readable" — repeat verbatim per caption.
- Full-width top/bottom bands on hero pages (50–80 words each max).
- Speech bubbles: round, off-white, dark serif, tail described explicitly, under 15 words, NO quotation
  marks inside bubbles.
- Every prompt: lettering section opens `LETTERING — verbatim, render exactly:` and closes with
  `All words spelled correctly. Do not duplicate text. Do not invent extra captions. NO modern logos,
  NO watermarks, NO spurious signage.`
- **Latin always gets an English helper** in the same panel (epitaph, Ne frustra vixisse videar,
  O me ridiculum, territio verbalis gloss).
- Period terms glossed inline on first use: arcminute (P5), Imperial Mathematician (P5), Counter-
  Reformation pressure via plain words (P4), territio verbalis (P16), ellipse (P10).

## Recurring visual grammar
- Night skies are characters: comet (P1), Mars point (P5–P11, P18), supernova (P12), the ellipse (P19).
- Kepler is a small figure under large skies or a close figure inside small candlelit rooms — alternate.
- Calculation sheets: dense handwritten arithmetic columns, never modern notation beyond simple symbols.
- War exists as distant glow, soldiers passing, smoke on horizons — never combat, never bodies.
- The witch-trial page: NO torture instruments depicted, NO violence. Stone room, documents, the small
  upright old woman, officials with papers. Dignity throughout.
