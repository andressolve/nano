## The one-sentence window
*A sickly, half-blind boy from a broken home built the most accurate theory of the heavens ever made —
and then killed it himself over a discrepancy of eight-sixtieths of one degree, because the data would not
allow it; out of that refusal came the ellipse, the laws of the planets, and the birth of modern science.*
Birth at Weil der Stadt (27 December 1571) → death at Regensburg (15 November 1630), age 58, plus a
vindication coda (Gassendi's Mercury transit 1631, Somnium 1634, Newton 1687).

## Spine (milestone-first, let-the-subject-be)
**Data over beauty.** Real milestones, no manufactured arc:
1. **The wrong idea that asked the right question** — the Platonic-solids Mysterium (1596) is false, but it is
   the first time anyone asked WHY the planets are six and WHY at those distances. It opens Tycho's door.
2. **The eight minutes** — the book's engine. His circle-theory matched Tycho to 2′ (best ever built); forced
   to honor distances too, it missed by 8′. Every astronomer alive would have shipped it. He tore it down →
   area law (1602) → ovals → ellipse (spring 1605, "O me ridiculum") → Astronomia Nova (1609).
3. **The harmony and the war** — third law confirmed 15 May 1618, Defenestration of Prague 23 May 1618.
   The mystic and the measurer are the same man, and the measurer always gets the last word (this inversion
   is the book's thesis — see myth list). Golden-vessels defiance.
4. **The cost and the payoff** — the mother's witch trial fought with forensic error analysis (the 8′ integrity
   as a family trait: Katharina refuses to confess under territio); Rudolphine Tables after 30 years and a
   burned print shop; death chasing 11,817 gulden; vindicated within a year (Mercury transit, to the hour).

## Honest editorial discipline (from RESEARCH.md — do not violate)

- **Brass nose, not silver** (2012 analysis).
- **Anti-myth thesis:** Kepler is NOT a mystic who stumbled onto laws. The mysticism supplied motivation,
  never evidence he accepted against data. Say it explicitly in the finale band.
