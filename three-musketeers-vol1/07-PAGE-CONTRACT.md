## Acceptance questions — asked of every page at the script gate

1. **What does each character in this room want from the other person, right now?** "To inform them" is not a want and fails the page.
2. **Does anybody say something other than exactly what they mean?**
3. **With the speaker names stripped, can the lines be assigned correctly?**
4. **Is the event dramatized and the meaning left to be inferred** — never the reverse?
5. **Is the protagonist's appetite visible,** or have they gone serene?
6. **Why does the reader turn this page?** One sentence, in the script, under every page — and it must be true of what is actually on the page.
7. **Can this page physically hold its own text** at the numbers in `method/06-TYPOGRAPHY.md`? No balloon over 24 words; a dramatic page under ~105 words; a prose page under 140. `tools/script_audit.py` checks this mechanically. If a page fails, restage or split it. Never shrink the type.
