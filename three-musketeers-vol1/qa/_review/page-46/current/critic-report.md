VERDICT: APPROVED

## Blind read
The girl in the cap and white collar owns the page. She meets the muddy boy in a candlelit gallery and confirms that his public success was noticed by everyone. The dominant close-up turns that triumph into intimate proof: she closes his hand over a dark-stoned gold ring sent by a woman who saw him and counted to twelve. At the rail, they watch a red-robed figure leave below with a slung black figure following, and the boy realizes that the Cardinal has read his name from the letter he has pursued. Recognition becomes a direct personal threat.
