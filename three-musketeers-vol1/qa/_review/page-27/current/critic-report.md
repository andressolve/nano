He then climbs toward his room while she remains behind watching and wondering about him. ## Findings

### Finding C1
- Observation: The required two-paragraph prose field is absent. The page instead contains three unrelated captions and five dialogue balloons from an inn-arrival exchange.
- Material reader harm: None of the scripted account of the concealed letter, the four riders leaving through the Saint-Denis gate, the replacement horse, or Constance watching the emptied road reaches the reader; the visible text establishes a different event.
- Redraw justification: The full page’s lettering and narrative content belong to another scene, so the scripted Page 27 cannot be recovered without replacing the complete page.

### Finding C2
- Observation: The page is a multi-panel comic-strip sequence dominated by D’Artagnan meeting the innkeeper’s daughter; there is no dominant sunrise image of four mounted riders departing through the gate and no secondary sewing image.
- Material reader harm: The required departure turn does not occur, and the eye cannot find either of the two scripted events in the intended hierarchy.

### Finding C3
- Observation: No mounted group appears. The page shows D’Artagnan alone and on foot, with no bay, red-plumed Porthos, white-plumed Aramis, or bearded Athos.
- Material reader harm: The defining revelation that the journey begins with four identifiable riders is entirely missing.
- Redraw justification: All four mounted focal figures and their identity cues would have to replace the current scene, which requires replacing the complete page.

### Finding C4
- Observation: The innkeeper’s daughter never sews a small blue-sealed letter into an ochre coat; she is shown greeting D’Artagnan, handing him a key, and watching after him.
- Material reader harm: The concealed letter and Constance’s intimate farewell action—the consequential object state and emotional first half of the page—are absent.
- Redraw justification: The required sewing action, letter, coat interaction, and relationship beat do not exist anywhere in the artwork, so they cannot be restored without a complete redraw.
