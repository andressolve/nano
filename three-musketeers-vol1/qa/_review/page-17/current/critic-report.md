## Blind read
The young swordsman arrives with his yellow horse and asks the maid in the white cap about Athos's attic. He cannot pay yet and boasts that he will be famous; she answers dryly. The stair scene owns the page: she looks down at him by candlelight while the crown on her apron reveals a connection to the Queen, changing his interest into complete eagerness. In the attic he hangs his sword on the nail, offers double, asks her name, and she gives it before saying goodnight.
