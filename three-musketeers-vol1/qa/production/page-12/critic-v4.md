## Blind read
The boy arrives hatless to meet the tall, dark, one-armed man under the tree. The man observes that the boy came; the boy answers that he promised to and notes the man's friends are absent, but the man says they are present. Three successive portraits reveal that the large red-moustached man, the slim blond man, and the slung man each expected to meet the same boy at a different hour. The dominant lower scene belongs to the convergence and interruption: the three companions recognize the coincidence and laugh while the boy remains serious, then a scarred man in black and five red-cloaked guards appear behind them. The black-clad man forbids the duel, claims their swords, and personally claims the Gascon, turning the comic collision into an armed threat.
