## Blind read
In a rain-soaked street, D’Artagnan notices a gentleman in a blue cloak standing in a doorway. The blue-cloaked gentleman unmistakably owns the page: he greets D’Artagnan, identifies him as being from Gascony, judges him brave, and gives him the maxim that one needs either friends or a good sword. D’Artagnan answers respectfully and is left reflecting after the gentleman departs. The turn is the stranger’s pointed advice, which changes a cautious chance encounter into a consequential lesson D’Artagnan carries forward.
