## Findings

### Finding C1
- Observation: The balloon reading `Aramis! There are six of them!` has a long tail that ends below the rear of the left departing rider's horse, not at the tan-clad boy's mouth.
- Material reader harm: The required warning is not visibly owned by D'Artagnan; the tail attributes it to nobody, breaking the scripted exchange between the boy riding away and Aramis staying behind.
- Redraw justification: Speaker ownership is a release-critical part of the page's causal farewell, and the visible tail prevents the exact required attribution from reading correctly.
