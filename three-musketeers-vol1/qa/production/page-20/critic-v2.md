## Findings

### Finding C5
- Observation: The lantern remains visibly suspended from the wall bracket by its chain and is still burning above the street; it is not falling free toward the puddle.
- Material reader harm: The focal object state contradicts the caption and prevents the dominant turn—the lantern entering the puddle and taking away the attackers' sight—from landing.
- Redraw justification: The lantern's fall and extinction are the page's central causal action and page-turn mechanism, so preserving the complete page would leave the story beat materially unresolved.
