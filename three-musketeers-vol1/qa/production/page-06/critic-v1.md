## Findings

### Finding C1
- Observation: The required single caption does not appear. Instead, the page contains six narration blocks with different wording plus the lettered name `D’Artagnan.`
- Material reader harm: The exact scripted account of his astonishment and the crowd ignoring him is absent, while the forbidden name label directly defeats the intended fact that nobody here knows his name.
- Redraw justification: Both the only required text and the page-turn premise are replaced by materially different prose and extra text; preserving this page would release the wrong narrative event.

### Finding C2
- Observation: The page is split into a city vista, two large views of the boy beside the horse, and two departure panels. The boy is the dominant large figure, while Paris functions as a backdrop; there is no small boy on the yellow horse, hat off, looking down over the whole city.
- Material reader harm: The intended spectacle of Paris overwhelming a solitary newcomer does not land because the character portrait and departure sequence own the page instead of the city.
- Redraw justification: This is a different composition and dramatic relationship, so the required city-dominant reader event cannot be restored without replacing the complete page.

### Finding C3
- Observation: The focal boy is not a small mounted rider with his grey hat in his hand; he stands beside the horse while wearing or touching the hat, and the lower views show him walking beside the horse.
- Material reader harm: The required focal identity and consequential hat state are visibly contradicted at the moment of arrival.
- Redraw justification: The central figure’s action and object state are wrong across the page’s dominant imagery, making the continuity failure substantial enough to require a complete redraw.
