VERDICT: REVISE

## Blind read
Four swords meet over a candle as three uniformed swordsmen and a younger swordsman form a shared oath. The converging blades and candlelit group unmistakably own the page. The group’s “One for all!” reads first, followed by one standing swordsman’s “All for one.”; the closing narration marks the exchange as unprecedented and thereafter sufficient.

## Visible text
“One for all!” — top-left/center group speech balloon above the four meeting blades, with multiple pointer strokes extending among the group and swords.

“All for one.” — upper-right speech balloon; its tail points to the right standing swordsman.

“Nobody had said it before. After that, nobody had to.” — bottom narration.

No name is lettered anywhere on the page. No other visible text.

## Findings

### Finding C1
- Observation: The upper-left/center group answer, “One for all!”, is encountered before the upper-right Athos line, “All for one.”
- Material reader harm: The reply is readable before the line that prompts it, reversing the scripted call-and-response and breaking the oath’s causal turn.
- Redraw justification: The page’s defining reader event is the correctly ordered oath; reversing its prompt and answer materially changes that event and is substantial enough to require replacement of the complete page.
