## Findings

### Finding C3
- Observation: The scarred man is shown in right profile, with the scar running down the visible right side of his face; C3 requires the scar on the left side.
- Material reader harm: The scar's specified side is part of Rochefort's named focal identity, and reversing it breaks that identity requirement in both appearances where his face is prominent.
- Redraw justification: This is a repeated focal-character identity error in the page's dominant confrontation, not a minor background or cosmetic discrepancy, so it is substantial enough to risk replacing the complete page.
