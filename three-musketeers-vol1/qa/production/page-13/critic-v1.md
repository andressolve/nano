- Observation: None of the seven required dialogue lines appears. The proof instead contains a caption and five different dialogue lines, including a balloon owned by the silent scarred black-cloaked opponent.

### Finding C2
- Observation: The largest image shows all five figures already facing one another, while the boy’s sword is already drawn in his close-up; no dominant image shows him drawing and stepping into the Musketeers’ line, and Athos never asks his name or assigns the man on the left.

### Finding C5
- Observation: The opposing side is represented by one black-cloaked scarred swordsman; five red-cassocked guards with faces away are absent, and the blue-clad bearded Musketeer has no visible left-arm sling.
- Material reader harm: The numerical disadvantage and Athos’s injury—the stakes that make the boy’s choice consequential—are missing.

### Finding C6
- Observation: The boy holds his drawn sword with one visible hand rather than exactly two.
- Material reader harm: The required focal action of drawing his father’s sword is not depicted with the mandated hand ownership, weakening the physical clarity of the choice.
- Redraw justification: Because the boy and his sword are the focal mechanism of the page’s dramatic turn, the failed hand state is substantial enough to require replacement.
