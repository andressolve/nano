## Findings

### Finding C6
- Observation: Six distinct red-cassocked guards are visible in the confrontation line: five grouped to the left of the black-clad officer and another at the far right edge.
- Material reader harm: The extra guard directly contradicts the spoken count of “five of them” and breaks the consequential visual arithmetic behind the boy becoming the fourth man on the outnumbered side.
- Redraw justification: A sixth guard is an explicitly blocking focal generation failure, and here it falsifies the page’s stated count and central story mechanic rather than presenting a cosmetic background discrepancy.
