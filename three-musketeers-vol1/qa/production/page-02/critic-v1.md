## Findings

### Finding C5
- Observation: The father has a raised hand holding the sealed letter and a hand visibly connected to his other arm resting on the horse’s face, while an additional flesh-toned hand rests lower on the horse’s neck without a visibly coherent arm, creating a duplicated/disembodied focal hand.
- Material reader harm: The extra hand makes the central father–son handoff anatomically incoherent at the exact visual focus where the father’s touch on the horse and the sealed letter are meant to define the departure.
- Redraw justification: This is a conspicuous focal generation failure in the dominant image, not a background or tiny-prop defect; preserving it materially damages the page’s defining dramatic relationship enough to warrant replacement.
