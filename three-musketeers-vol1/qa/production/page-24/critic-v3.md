## Blind read

The boy volunteers; when she names the danger and says she has no right to ask, he answers the ask she will not make, takes up his sword, and invokes three friends. She then holds the letter out and promises him a nightly refuge at the Louvre. The dominant face-to-face middle panel owns the page, and the turn is his firm acceptance of her unspoken request.
