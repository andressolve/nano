### Finding C1
- Observation: None of the seven required dialogue lines appears. The page instead contains one different balloon—“You have a letter from the queen!”—and six unscripted prose captions.

### Finding C2
- Observation: The page shows Constance handing over a letter, D’Artagnan following her upstairs, and the discovery that the letter was previously opened. It never shows the face-to-face negotiation over the letter, D’Artagnan’s “That isn’t no” answer, or his taking down the sword.

### Finding C3
- Observation: In the focal reading panel and final panel, the cream letter has red wax; the final panel also shows a separate blue-sealed envelope.

### Finding C5
- Observation: By the last panel, the sword lies on the table rather than being in D’Artagnan’s hands, and the letter lies open on the table rather than being held out to him by Constance.
- Material reader harm: The final required commitments—his ownership of the sword and her offering of the letter—are absent, so the visual acceptance of the mission does not read.
- Redraw justification: Both consequential end-state objects contradict the scripted turn, a structural failure substantial enough to require a complete redraw.
