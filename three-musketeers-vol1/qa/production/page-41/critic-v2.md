## Blind read
A rain-soaked young man returns to a young woman waiting in a doorway and brings her a blue jewel case. The large middle panel unmistakably owns the page: she opens the case, counts the jeweled pieces, and he explains that two are new because two were cut out at the Duke's ball and the Duke had two replacements made, indistinguishable even to him. The turn is from anxious reunion to proof that the damaged set has been secretly restored. In the final panel, she takes the case and sends the man to stand where the woman dressing upstairs can see him; he recognizes that this also exposes him to the Cardinal, and she confirms that is intended.
