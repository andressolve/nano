## Findings

### Finding C1
- Observation: The balloon containing `Not even the Duke could.` is placed beneath the young man's prior line, but its visible tail points up-right toward the casket/empty space rather than hanging from the muddy boy.
- Material reader harm: One of the boy's four required lines is not visibly owned by his mouth, so the final beat of his explanation becomes disembodied instead of reading as his direct assurance to Constance.
- Redraw justification: Correct speaker ownership is mandatory to the scripted exchange, and this visible tail contradicts it on the release proof; that dialogue-attribution failure is substantial enough to replace the page.
