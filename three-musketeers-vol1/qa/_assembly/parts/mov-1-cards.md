## Page 1 — card

### C1 — Title card and caption transcription
The two-line title card and the one caption read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once each, tail-free, in that order top to bottom, with no other text anywhere on the page. A string that does not read from the 600 × 900 proof is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured. Any extra text anywhere on the page (a name, a numeral, a label, a stray word on a wall or paper) is blocking. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue are nonblocking: the words and their order are what must match.

### C2 — The page is not a boy on a yellow horse coming at you
The eye must find one image that is plainly a boy on a bright yellow horse riding toward the reader on a road out of hills, chin up. If the boy is small in a landscape, or the horse is not unmistakably yellow, or the page reads as several panels, the turn has not landed. Landscape detail and sky are nonblocking.

### C3 — d'Artagnan does not read as d'Artagnan
The rider must be a clean-shaven boy with black hair in his eyes, an ochre doublet, a grey hat with a broken feather, and a sword visibly too long for him. A beard, a moustache, a cloak, or a man's age is blocking. Exact patching and boot detail are nonblocking.

### C4 — The horse is the wrong horse
The horse must be old, calm, and buttercup yellow, the only such animal in the book. A brown, white, grey, or cream horse is blocking. Tack detail is nonblocking.

### C5 — Focal generation failure
A duplicated rider, a horse with the wrong number of legs, gross anatomy on the boy's face or hands, or text colliding with the figure is blocking. Distant poplars, farmhouse, and road texture are not.
