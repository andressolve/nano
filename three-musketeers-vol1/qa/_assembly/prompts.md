## PAGE 1 — the yellow horse

### Reader turn
A boy on a ridiculous yellow horse rides straight at the reader with his chin up, and the reader wants to know where he thinks he is going.

### Ordered moments
1. One image, the whole page, about 100%: a white road between ochre hills, a line of poplars, a hot morning sky filling the upper third. The boy and the yellow horse ride toward the viewer in the lower half, large, the horse's buttercup coat and the boy's ochre doublet the warmest things in the frame, the too-long sword hanging past his knee, the broken feather in the grey hat, his chin up and his hair in his eyes. Very small behind them on the road, a stone farmhouse and a gate. The boy's shadow stretches ahead of the horse toward the reader.
2. The title card sits in the open sky at the top, two lines, large and calm, no box. The caption sits lower left over the road, a small matte field, tail-free. Nothing else is lettered.
