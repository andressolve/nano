## Materiality threshold

The critic is a release gate, not a defect collector. A visible issue is
blocking only when it materially harms the reader event, exact text or
attribution, named or focal identity, consequential continuity or object state,
focal generation integrity, or the dominant dramatic relationship. Every
correction replaces the complete page. Minor background, cosmetic, tiny-prop,
geometry, colour, texture, and prompt-fidelity observations are nonblocking.
Omit them. Numeric panel shares and lettering sizes are builder construction
guidance, never critic measurements.

Each numbered criterion is also its stable retry signature. Cite it only when
the reader harm and redraw justification are concrete.

A lettered speaker name, label, or tag anywhere on the page is extra text and
blocks under the transcription criterion, whatever else reads correctly:
finished pages carry dialogue, captions, prose, and sound cues only.
A sound cue whose letters and order match the script reads correctly; the run
length of a repeated letter in it is nonblocking and never counted.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 48 — card

### C1 — Prose transcription
The prose field reads exactly, all three paragraphs in order, once, from the 600 × 900 proof, over plain ground and never over the four or the horse, with no other text on the page. A string that does not read from the 600 × 900 proof is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured. Any extra text anywhere on the page (a name, a numeral, a label, a stray word on a wall or paper) is blocking. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word (duelling for dueling, colour for color), and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue are nonblocking: the words and their order are what must match.

### C2 — The breakfast does not own the page
The eye must find four men at ease on the grass by a yellow wall with a yellow horse drinking from a trough, and second a hand with a ring holding bread. If the hand competes, or the page reads as panels, the turn has not landed.

### C3 — The four or the horse fail identity
The clean-shaven boy, the tall bearded Athos without a sling, the huge red-moustached Porthos, the slim blond Aramis, and a buttercup-yellow horse. A fifth person, a missing one, or a wrong-coloured horse is blocking.

### C4 — The ring fails
A gold ring with one dark stone on the boy's hand in the close image; no ring is blocking.

### C5 — Focal generation failure
Gross anatomy on the hand with the bread, a duplicated Musketeer, or the prose field over a face is blocking. Grass and dew are not.
