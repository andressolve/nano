## Page intent

## PAGE 46

Dramatic. The gallery of the Louvre. Constance running its length; the boy coming up the gallery stair; they stop a step apart. You did it, she counted, the Cardinal counted, everybody counted; I told you I'd be famous. She takes a ring from a small purse at her waist, puts it in his hand, and closes his fingers over it with both of hers: from the Queen's own hand, taken off when she saw him in the door, and Constance sent running; she said tell him I counted to twelve too; she saw me? everybody saw you, you're wearing two countries. ### C1 — Dialogue transcription or ownership fails
All nine lines read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once each, in causal order; Constance's five hang from the girl in the cap and white collar, the boy's four from the muddy boy; each reply sits after the line it answers. A string that does not read from the 600 × 900 proof is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured. Any extra text anywhere on the page (a name, a numeral, a label, a stray word on a wall or paper) is blocking. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word (duelling for dueling, colour for color), and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue are nonblocking: the words and their order are what must match.

### C2 — The turn does not land
The page must read in order as: they meet in the gallery, she closes his hand over a ring from the Queen, they watch the red robe leave below. If her hands closing his over the ring is not the largest image the eye finds, the turn has not landed.

### C3 — The ring fails
A gold ring with one dark stone must be visible passing into the boy's hand; no ring, or a diamond stud instead, is blocking. Exact size is nonblocking.

### C4 — Constance or d'Artagnan fails identity
The small freckled girl in grey with cap and white collar, no apron; the boy clean-shaven in muddy ochre. Blocking if contradicted.

### C5 — The figures below fail
A red-robed figure going out by a side door with a slung black figure after it must be visible below in the last panel; the Cardinal still seated by the dais here is blocking.

### C6 — Focal generation failure
Her two hands and his one on the ring, no extra fingers; a duplicated girl, or a balloon across a face is blocking.
