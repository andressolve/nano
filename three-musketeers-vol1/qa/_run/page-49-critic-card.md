## Materiality threshold

The critic is a release gate, not a defect collector. A visible issue is
blocking only when it materially harms the reader event, exact text or
attribution, named or focal identity, consequential continuity or object state,
focal generation integrity, or the dominant dramatic relationship. Every
correction replaces the complete page. Minor background, cosmetic, tiny-prop,
geometry, colour, texture, and prompt-fidelity observations are nonblocking.
Omit them. Numeric panel shares and lettering sizes are builder construction
guidance, never critic measurements.

Each numbered criterion is also its stable retry signature. Cite it only when
the reader harm and redraw justification are concrete.

A lettered speaker name, label, or tag anywhere on the page is extra text and
blocks under the transcription criterion, whatever else reads correctly:
finished pages carry dialogue, captions, prose, and sound cues only.
A sound cue whose letters and order match the script reads correctly; the run
length of a repeated letter in it is nonblocking and never counted.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 49 — card

### C1 — Caption and end card transcription
The caption and the end card read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once each, tail-free, the end card small below the caption, with no other text on the page. A string that does not read from the 600 × 900 proof is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured. Any extra text anywhere on the page (a name, a numeral, a label, a stray word on a wall or paper) is blocking. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word (duelling for dueling, colour for color), and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue are nonblocking: the words and their order are what must match.

### C2 — The page is not four walking away under a red man's window
The eye must find a seated red figure with a cat at a window in the near foreground and, below, four men walking away four abreast down a street. If the Cardinal is absent, or the four face the viewer, or the page reads as panels, the turn has not landed.

### C3 — The Cardinal fails identity or stands
Thin, grey pointed beard, red robe and skullcap, seated, a grey cat on the sill under his hand. Standing, or no cat, is blocking.

### C4 — The four fail as a silhouette group
Exactly four walking abreast: huge with red plumes, small in ochre in the middle, slim with a white plume, tall with a white plume on the outside. Three or five, or the boy not in the middle, is blocking.

### C5 — Focal generation failure
A duplicated Cardinal, two cats, a fifth walker, or text colliding with a figure is blocking. Rooftop and cobble texture are not.
