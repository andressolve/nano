The eye must find first an old man at a bench with a loupe and tongs and the Duke standing over him, and second the box passing at the water stair. If the stair competes, or the page reads as a comic strip, the turn has not landed.

### C3 — Buckingham or d'Artagnan fails identity
The Duke golden, bearded, in white satin, standing; the boy clean-shaven in muddy ochre, asleep with his hat over his face in the first image and awake with the box in the second. Blocking if contradicted.

### C4 — Object state fails
The casket open with two studs in the tongs beside it in the workroom; closed in the boy's hands at the stair. An open box at the stair is blocking.
