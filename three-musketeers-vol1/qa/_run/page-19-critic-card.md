## Page intent

## PAGE 19

Dramatic, two locations because the jump is the turn: the attic window, and the street below. Night, rain. Above, the boy at the open window in his shirt, cleaning the sword. Below, a cart, a lantern on a bracket, and four men in plain cloaks closing on Constance at her own door, one hand reaching for her mouth, the basket falling; she shouts for help and gets half his name out. He goes out of the window, not the stair: one hand on the sill, the sword in the other, dropping two floors through the rain onto the cart roof with a bang. He lands between the men and the door, sword out, hair in his eyes, and across the street in a dark doorway a fifth figure in black with a red feather stands with his arms folded and does not move. The dominant turn is the men taking Constance and the boy going out of the window. The reader turns because it is one boy, four men, one lantern, and rain.

### C1 — Dialogue transcription or ownership fails
Constance's cut-off cry, the boy's shout, the sound cue, and the boy's challenge read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once each, in causal order; the cry hangs from the girl at the door, the shout and the challenge from the boy; the four men and the man in the doorway carry no balloon. A string that does not read from the 600 × 900 proof is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured. Any extra text anywhere on the page (a name, a numeral, a label, a stray word on a wall or paper) is blocking. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word (duelling for dueling, colour for color), and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue are nonblocking: the words and their order are what must match.

### C2 — The turn does not land
The page must read in order as: men seize the girl below while the boy watches from the window, the boy drops out of the window onto the cart, he lands between the men and the door. If the boy in mid-drop is not the largest image the eye finds, the turn has not landed.

### C3 — Rochefort is missing or wrong
A figure in black with a red feather and a scar must stand in a doorway across the street with folded arms in the last panel, not fighting. A missing watcher, or the watcher drawing a sword, is blocking.

### C4 — Constance or d'Artagnan fails identity
The girl small in grey with the white cap and falling basket; the boy clean-shaven, black-haired, in a white shirt with no doublet. A doublet or hat on the boy here, or a bearded boy, is blocking.

### C5 — Bloodless or count fails
Exactly four attackers; no blood; nobody struck. Five attackers or a wounded figure is blocking.

### C6 — Focal generation failure
A duplicated boy at window and ground in the same panel, gross anatomy on the hand on the sill, or a balloon across a face is blocking. Rain texture is not.
