### C1 — Dialogue transcription or ownership fails
All seven lines read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once each, in causal order; Athos's three from the tall slung man, Porthos's one from the huge man, the boy's three from the boy; in the name exchange the reply sits after the question. Rochefort and the guards carry no balloon. A string that does not read from the 600 × 900 proof is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured. Any extra text anywhere on the page (a name, a numeral, a label, a stray word on a wall or paper) is blocking. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word (duelling for dueling, colour for color), and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue are nonblocking: the words and their order are what must match.

### C2 — The turn does not land
The page must read in order as: the three draw and tell the boy to stand back, the boy draws and steps into the line, Athos asks his name and gives him the man on the left. If the boy stepping into the line is not the largest image the eye finds, the turn has not landed.

### C3 — The Musketeers collide or Athos and Rochefort collide
Athos's face must be unmarked: a scar on Athos, or on anyone but the man in black, is blocking. As on the page before: three separable Musketeers; Rochefort scarred, black, red feather; Athos bearded, blue, sling. Blocking if any pair merges.

### C4 — d'Artagnan fails identity
Clean-shaven, black-haired, ochre, no cloak, drawing a plain swept-hilt rapier too long for him. A moustache or a blue cassock on the boy is blocking.

### C5 — Guards, sling, or bloodless fail
Five red-cassocked guards, faces away, no feather; Athos's sling on the LEFT arm; no blood, no contact.

### C6 — Focal generation failure
Exactly two hands on the boy's drawn sword; a duplicated boy, or gross anatomy on Athos's face looking down is blocking; the number of guards is nonblocking.
