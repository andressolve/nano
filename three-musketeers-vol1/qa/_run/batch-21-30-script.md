# Exact script — pages 21–30

Open only after the blind sequence read.

## PAGE 21 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the street outside the house.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** The four men gone. Rain. D'Artagnan, left, breathing hard,
sword lowered. ROCHEFORT, right, stepping out of the doorway into what light
there is, hands empty, the scar, the red feather.

> **ROCHEFORT:** "Three times you've drawn on me, Gascon. And I'm still not staying."
>
> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "I said the third time you'd stay. Draw!"
>
> **ROCHEFORT:** "Not in the rain, and not for a seamstress. She isn't what I was sent for. She only carries it."

**Panel 2 — 20%.** Rochefort walking away up the street, unhurried, into the
dark. The boy shouting after him.

> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "Carries what?"

**Panel 3 — dominant, 50%.** The doorway. Constance, shaking, picking up the
basket; d'Artagnan beside her, wet through.

> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "What did he mean? What do you carry?"
>
> **CONSTANCE:** "The Queen's letters. I carry them for her. Where, and to whom, I can't tell you. It isn't mine to tell."
>
> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "Then don't tell me. I'll be upstairs. Tonight and every night, until they stop coming."
>
> **CONSTANCE:** "They won't stop."
>
> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "Then neither will I."

**Why you turn the page:** the men were sent for the Queen's letters, and the girl will not say where they go.

## PAGE 22 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the Cardinal's study.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 50%.** A red room with one candle and no window. THE
CARDINAL, right, in a chair: a thin man in a red robe and a red skullcap, a
short pointed grey beard, a grey cat on his lap, one long hand stroking it.
On the black desk in front of him, a folded cream letter with a broken red
seal. ROCHEFORT, left, standing, wet from the rain, hat in his hands.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left, over the red hangings)*
> "The Cardinal, in his red room. The man with the scar answered to him. So did every red cloak in France."
>
> **ROCHEFORT:** "The girl got away, Eminence. A boy stopped us. The Gascon—"
>
> **CARDINAL:** "I have his father's letter. I don't need tonight's. I have read the Queen's letters for a year, and I know what she sent."

**Panel 2 — 25%.** Close on the Cardinal's face, pale grey eyes that do not
blink, and the hand on the cat, which does not stop.

> **CARDINAL:** "Twelve diamond studs. The King's own gift to her, on the Duke of Buckingham's shoulder."
>
> **CARDINAL:** "Go to London. Bring me two of them. Only two."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Rochefort at the door, turning back.

> **ROCHEFORT:** "Why not all twelve?"
>
> **CARDINAL:** "He would miss twelve. Nobody counts to two."
>
> **ROCHEFORT:** "And then?"
>
> **CARDINAL:** "And then the King will give a ball."

**Why you turn the page:** the Cardinal wants two diamonds, and he knows what a ball has to do with it, and you do not yet.

# MOVEMENT IV — ALL FOR ONE · pp 23–27

---

## PAGE 23 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the attic.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Night. CONSTANCE, right, at the top of the stair in the
attic doorway, still in her Louvre grey, white as paper. D'ARTAGNAN, left, up
off the bed.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left, over the dark beam)*
> "Six nights, and nobody came for her. On the seventh she came up the stair."
>
> **CONSTANCE:** "The King gives a ball on Monday. The Queen is to wear her twelve diamond studs."
>
> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "Then she wears them."
>
> **CONSTANCE:** "She can't. She gave them away."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 45%.** Constance sitting on the edge of the table, her
hands pressed together; the boy crouched in front of her.

> **CONSTANCE:** "They were the King's present. She gave them to the Duke of Buckingham, in London."
>
> **CONSTANCE:** "If she comes without them, the court will know. And the Cardinal smiled when the King asked her to wear them."

**Panel 3 — 30%.** The boy on his feet. She has not moved.

> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "Then someone rides to London and brings them back before Monday."
>
> **CONSTANCE:** "Someone the Cardinal doesn't own. There isn't anyone."

**Why you turn the page:** the Queen is going to be caught on Monday, the Cardinal arranged it, and there is nobody to send.

## PAGE 24 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the attic.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** Constance takes a small letter sealed in blue wax with a
crown out of her bodice and holds it in both hands, not yet offering it.

> **CONSTANCE:** "She wrote to the Duke tonight. Find someone to carry it to London, she said. There isn't anyone."
>
> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "There's me. Are you asking?"

**Panel 2 — dominant, 45%.** Face to face, a step apart, the letter between
them. She is looking at him properly for the first time.

> **CONSTANCE:** "It's two days' hard riding to the sea, and the Cardinal owns every inn. I've no right to ask."
>
> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "That isn't no."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** He is taking his father's sword down off the nail. She is
holding the letter out to him now.

> **CONSTANCE:** "One boy against the Cardinal?"
>
> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "One boy and three friends. You haven't met them."
>
> **CONSTANCE:** "The kitchen door at the back of the Louvre. I'll be in it every night until you come."

**Why you turn the page:** she has asked without asking, and he has said yes.

## PAGE 25 · *dramatic*

**Location:** Athos's room.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** A bare whitewashed room: one chair, one table, one bed, a
single sword hung on the wall. ATHOS in the chair, right. PORTHOS filling the
window. ARAMIS on the edge of the table with his book. D'ARTAGNAN standing in
the middle, holding up the small letter sealed in blue wax with a crown.

> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "Two days to the sea, a day to London, and back by Monday. It can be done. The Cardinal will try to stop me."
>
> **ATHOS:** "What does the letter say?"
>
> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "I don't know."
>
> **ATHOS:** "Good. Then nobody can make us tell."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 40%.** Porthos turning from the window, delighted;
Aramis closing his book.

> **PORTHOS:** "London! I have never once beaten an Englishman. I've always wanted to."
>
> **ARAMIS:** "In theory, four riders are more than one. In practice, the Cardinal will not let four through."
>
> **ATHOS:** "That's why it takes four. So that one arrives."

**Panel 3 — 30%.** The boy looking from one face to the next.

> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "You haven't asked me why. Not one of you."
>
> **PORTHOS:** "Why would we? You're going. That's the why."

**Why you turn the page:** three men have just decided to ride against the Cardinal for a reason none of them knows, and they are about to say the thing.

## PAGE 26 · *spectacle*

**Location:** Athos's room.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 100%.** Four sword points meeting above the table, four
hands on four hilts, four faces lit from below by the one candle: Athos grave,
Porthos huge and grinning, Aramis with one eyebrow up, and the boy looking as
if he might cry and would rather die first.

> **ATHOS:** "All for one."
>
> **ALL FOUR:** "One for all!"

> **CAPTION** *(lower edge, under the candlelight)*
> "Nobody had said it before. After that, nobody had to."

**Why you turn the page:** they have said it, and the sun comes up in an hour.

## PAGE 27 · *illustrated prose*

**Location:** two — the attic before dawn, and the Saint-Denis gate.

**Panel 1 — 40%.** The attic by candlelight. Constance sewing the small blue-
sealed letter into the lining of his ochre coat with tiny stitches. He is
watching her hands.

**Panel 2 — dominant, 60%.** The Saint-Denis gate at sunrise: four riders
going through it at a canter, the boy in ochre in the middle on a tall bay,
Porthos's red plumes, Aramis's white one, Athos on the outside. Above the
street behind them, small, a girl in a white cap at an attic window.

> **PROSE FIELD 1** *(over the dawn sky above the gate, never over the riders)*
> "Before it was light she sewed the letter into the lining of his coat, with small stitches, so that it could not fall out and could not be found. He watched her hands and did not talk. When she had bitten the thread she put both her hands flat on his chest, over the place where it was, and did not say come back, because she did not want to hear him promise.
>
> The four of them went out through the Saint-Denis gate as the sun came up. The yellow horse stayed in the stable: Athos had found the boy something faster that nobody laughed at. Behind them, in a window over a narrow street, a girl in a white cap watched the road until there was nothing left on it."

**Why you turn the page:** four riders, one letter, two days to the sea, and the Cardinal owns every inn on the road.

# MOVEMENT V — THE ROAD · pp 28–35

---

## PAGE 28 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the inn at Chantilly.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** A bright inn room at noon, a long table, the four eating
standing up, cloaks still on. At the far end of the table a stranger in a
plain coat has risen with his cup raised toward Porthos, and is looking at
him, and waiting.

> **PORTHOS:** "That man is drinking the Cardinal's health, and he's waiting for me to drink it too."
>
> **ARAMIS:** "Then drink it, Porthos. We are in a hurry."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 45%.** Porthos on his feet, enormous, his own cup up.

> **PORTHOS:** "I drink to the Queen. And to nobody else in this room."
>
> **SOUND** *(the stranger's cup on the floor)*
> "KRSSH"

**Panel 3 — 25%.** The stranger's sword out. Porthos throwing off his cloak,
smoothing the front of his baldric. The other three already at the door,
d'Artagnan looking back.

> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "Porthos, we can't wait for you—"
>
> **PORTHOS:** "Nobody asked you to. Go. He's mine. I'll be an hour behind you, and I'll have his hat."

**Why you turn the page:** three riders leave the inn, and the loudest man in Paris is not one of them.

## PAGE 29 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the bridge at Beauvais.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** Dusk. A stone bridge over a brown river. At the far end,
men with picks, and a line of stones dragged across the road. The three riders
slowing.

> **ARAMIS:** "Road workers. At dusk. With their picks pointed at us. As the Fathers tell us: no."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 45%.** The horses driving through the gap in the
stones. The men swing. Aramis's horse goes down on the planks and Aramis
rolls clear onto his feet, sword out, the lace handkerchief already wound
round his hand. The other two horses are through and beyond. Six men closing
on the one in blue.

> **SOUND** *(the horse on the planks)*
> "KRAK"
>
> **ARAMIS:** "Go on! Both of you! Don't turn around!"
>
> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "Aramis! There are six of them!"

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Aramis with his back to the bridge rail, six in front of
him, calm, two fingers at his moustache.

> **ARAMIS:** "Six. And a bridge is narrow, so they'll have to come at me one at a time. I'll hold the road. Go!"

**Why you turn the page:** Aramis is holding a bridge alone against six, and you are riding away from him.

## PAGE 30 · *spectacle*

**Location:** the bridge at Beauvais.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 100%.** From behind the two departing riders, looking
back down the road: the bridge at dusk, small, and on it one slim figure in
blue with his back to the rail and six men in front of him, one already
sitting in the road holding his arm, and the first star over the river.

> **CAPTION** *(lower edge, over the road)*
> "He held the road. He was still holding it when it got dark. The horse got up and trotted home; Aramis did not go with it."

**Why you turn the page:** two riders are left, and the Cardinal has not run out of inns.
