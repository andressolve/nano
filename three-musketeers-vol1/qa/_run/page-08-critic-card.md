## Page intent

## PAGE 8

Dramatic. A Paris street corner. d'Artagnan, at a dead run after the scar, goes straight into the sling of a very tall Musketeer, and the tall man goes white. This is Athos: very tall, very still, grey eyes, his good hand holding his bad shoulder, while the boy, already two steps past, looks back over his own shoulder for the man who has vanished. The tall man observes that the shoulder was hurt before the boy arrived; the boy apologises and says he cannot stop; the tall man says he has stopped. Face to face, the boy offers satisfaction and never refuses a fight; the tall man names the field behind the Carmelite convent at noon and tells him to bring a friend to stand with him; the boy has none. The dominant turn is Athos's shoulder and the appointment at noon. Athos owns the stillness, d'Artagnan the heat. The reader turns because the boy has a duel at noon with the tallest man in Paris and has lost the scar. The street is scenery; the sling and the two faces are the page.

### C1 — Dialogue transcription or ownership fails
The sound cue and all six lines read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once each, in causal order; each of Athos's three lines hangs by its tail from the tall man in the sling and each of the boy's three from the boy; in both three-line exchanges the reply sits clearly after the line it answers. The run length of the sound cue's letters is nonblocking. A string that does not read from the 600 × 900 proof is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured. Any extra text anywhere on the page (a name, a numeral, a label, a stray word on a wall or paper) is blocking. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word (duelling for dueling, colour for color), and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue are nonblocking: the words and their order are what must match.

### C2 — The turn does not land
The page must read in order as: the boy runs into a slung shoulder, the tall man stops him with two words, the duel is set for noon and the boy has no friend. If the eye does not find the tall still man holding his shoulder as the page's image, the turn has not landed.

### C3 — Athos fails identity
Tall, dark shoulder-length hair, short pointed beard, blue cassock with the white cross, white plume, LEFT arm in a black sling. A scar, a red feather, black clothes, no beard, or the sling on the right arm is blocking.

### C4 — d'Artagnan fails identity
Clean-shaven, black hair in his eyes, ochre doublet, grey hat, too-long sword sheathed. A moustache or a cloak is blocking.

### C5 — Bloodless fails
No blood, no visible wound; the sling is clean cloth over a healed hurt.

### C6 — Focal generation failure
A duplicated Athos or boy, an extra slung arm, gross anatomy on Athos's hand at the shoulder, or a balloon across a face is blocking. Street texture and passers-by are not.
