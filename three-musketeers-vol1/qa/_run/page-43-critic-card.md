## Materiality threshold

The critic is a release gate, not a defect collector. A visible issue is
blocking only when it materially harms the reader event, exact text or
attribution, named or focal identity, consequential continuity or object state,
focal generation integrity, or the dominant dramatic relationship. Every

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 43 — card

### C1 — Caption transcription
The one caption reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, tail-free, with no other text on the page. A string that does not read from the 600 × 900 proof is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured. Any extra text anywhere on the page (a name, a numeral, a label, a stray word on a wall or paper) is blocking. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word (duelling for dueling, colour for color), and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue are nonblocking: the words and their order are what must match.

### C2 — The entrance does not own the page
The eye must find, from above and behind, a woman in white walking down a golden hall through a bowing court with a blaze of diamonds on her left shoulder, with a girl's shoulder and cap in the foreground and a small red figure at the far end. If the Queen's face is shown close, or the diamonds are not the brightest thing, or the page reads as panels, the turn has not landed.

### C3 — The Queen fails as scenery
A close face, a balloon, or a Queen in any colour but white and silver is blocking; the diamonds must be on her LEFT shoulder.

### C4 — The far figures fail
The red figure at the far end must be seated and leaning forward with a slung black figure behind; a standing Cardinal here is blocking.

### C5 — Register drift
The page must read as the same drawn, painted book as the pages around it: visible brush line, paper grain, matte gouache. A glossy, near-photographic render (mirror floor, satin sheen, lens glow) that reads as a different medium is blocking. Ordinary variation in light and palette is nonblocking.

### C6 — Focal generation failure
A duplicated Queen, the diamonds rendered as a countable row of separate pegs, or text colliding with a figure is blocking. Court repetition is not.
