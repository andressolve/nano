### C2 — The lesson does not own the page
The eye must find first the tall man teaching the boy with crossed blades at dawn, then the small warm stair with the girl looking up. If the stair competes or the page reads as a comic strip, the turn has not landed.

### C3 — Athos fails identity or wears the sling
Tall, dark shoulder-length hair, pointed beard, blue cassock, white plume, both arms free. A sling, a scar, or black clothes is blocking.

### C4 — Constance or d'Artagnan fails identity
The girl small in grey and white with cap, keys, and basket looking up; the boy clean-shaven and black-haired at the top. Blocking if either is contradicted.

### C5 — Focal generation failure
