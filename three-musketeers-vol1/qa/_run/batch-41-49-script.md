## PAGE 41 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the back stair of the Louvre.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** A service door at the back of the Louvre, torchlight,
kitchen noise behind it. CONSTANCE, right, in the doorway in her grey, the
apron off. D'ARTAGNAN, left, coming up out of the dark with mud to the waist
and the closed casket under his arm.

> **CONSTANCE:** "You're here. Every night since you left I've stood in this door. Is it—"
>
> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "Twelve. Count them."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** The casket open between them in the torchlight,
her lips moving over the double row; his eyes on her face, not on the box.

> **CONSTANCE:** "Ten. Eleven. Twelve. Twelve."
>
> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "Two of them are new. The scar cut two out at the Duke's ball, and the Duke had two made. Nobody can tell which."
>
> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "Not even the Duke could."

**Panel 3 — 20%.** The box shut and held against her chest, she is already
turning up the stair. He has sat down on the bottom step.

> **CONSTANCE:** "She's dressing. She has ten minutes. Go round to the front doors and stand where she can see you."
>
> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "Where the Cardinal can see me."
>
> **CONSTANCE:** "Yes. That too."

**Why you turn the page:** the box has changed hands, and there are ten minutes.

## PAGE 42 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the Louvre ballroom.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 45%.** A hall of gold and a thousand candles, a court
in silk, and at the far end a dais with two thrones, a crowned figure seated
on one and the other empty. Beside the dais, one column of red: THE CARDINAL,
seated in a chair set there for him, and on the arm of the chair under his
hand a folded cream letter with a broken red seal, as if he had been reading
it while he waited. Behind the chair, ROCHEFORT, his right arm in a white
sling. Above, in the gallery, CONSTANCE in her grey, without
the apron, both hands on the rail, watching the long shut doors.

> **ROCHEFORT:** "And the boy, Eminence? The Gascon reached the boat."
>
> **CARDINAL:** "A boy reached a boat. I have his father's letter; I know what he is. The Duke has ten. Ten is ten."

**Panel 2 — 25%.** Close: the Cardinal's long hand drawing a small pouch half
out of his red sleeve, and pushing it back.

> **CARDINAL:** "She will come in wearing ten, because ten is what she has."
>
> **CARDINAL:** "And I will go to her with the two she lost, in front of everyone, and the King will count."

**Panel 3 — 30%.** The gallery. Constance's hands white on the rail. Below
her, the long doors, all shut, and a herald's staff rising to strike the floor.

> **CONSTANCE:** "Come on. Come on. Come on."

**Why you turn the page:** the doors are opening, and the Cardinal has two diamonds in his sleeve.

## PAGE 43 · *spectacle*

**Location:** the Louvre ballroom.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 100%.** The Queen enters: a figure in white and silver,
seen from the gallery over Constance's shoulder, from behind and above,
walking the length of the hall through a court that bows like wheat. On her
left shoulder, a blaze of diamonds in two rows, drawn large and bright. At the
far end, small, the red chair, and the man in it leaning forward.

> **CAPTION** *(lower edge, over the polished floor)*
> "The Queen came in. Nobody looked at her face. Everyone looked at her shoulder."

**Why you turn the page:** the Cardinal is counting.

## PAGE 44 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the Louvre ballroom.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** The Cardinal's face, close, his lips moving. His hand on
the pouch in his sleeve has stopped.

> **CARDINAL:** "Ten. Eleven. Twelve."
>
> **CARDINAL:** "Twelve."

**Panel 2 — 25%.** Rochefort bending to his ear, sweating.

> **ROCHEFORT:** "I cut two, Eminence. I swear it. They're in your sleeve."
>
> **CARDINAL:** "Then someone made two."

**Panel 3 — dominant, 50%.** From beside the red chair, looking down the
hall: the Queen has reached the dais and the crowned figure's hand is taking
hers, the twelve blazing on her shoulder, the court coming up out of its bow
like wheat standing after wind. In the near foreground, large, the Cardinal's
long hand pushing the pouch all the way back into the red sleeve. A small
inset: Constance in the gallery, both hands coming off the rail.

> **CAPTION** *(lower edge, over the polished floor)*
> "The King took her hand. The court came up out of its bow. And the two diamonds in the red sleeve stayed where they were."

**Why you turn the page:** the trick has failed, and the man who never stands is getting up.

## PAGE 45 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the Louvre ballroom.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 55%.** The Cardinal standing, for the only time in the
book, and turning toward the long doors. In the last of them, a boy with mud
to the waist, hat in hand, hair in his eyes, looking straight back at him
across the whole ballroom.

> **CARDINAL:** "Who is that boy?"
>
> **ROCHEFORT:** "The Gascon. From Meung."

**Panel 2 — 45%.** The Cardinal's hand taking a folded cream letter with a
broken red seal from the arm of the chair beside him, unfolding it, and
reading the name at the bottom.

> **CARDINAL:** "Ah. The letter. A father asking a favour for a son."
>
> **CARDINAL:** "d'Artagnan."

**Why you turn the page:** the most powerful man in France has just learned his name, and said it quietly.

## PAGE 46 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the gallery of the Louvre.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** The gallery. CONSTANCE, right, running the length of it.
D'ARTAGNAN, left, coming up the gallery stair. They stop a step apart.

> **CONSTANCE:** "You did it. She counted, and the Cardinal counted, and everybody counted."
>
> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "I told you I'd be famous."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 45%.** She takes a ring from a small purse at her
waist, puts it in his hand, and closes his fingers over it with both of hers.

> **CONSTANCE:** "This is from her own hand. She took it off when she saw you in the door, and sent me running."
>
> **CONSTANCE:** "She said: tell him I counted to twelve too."
>
> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "She saw me?"
>
> **CONSTANCE:** "Everybody saw you. You're wearing two countries."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** The two of them at the rail, looking down. Below, the red
robe going out by a side door, and after it, the sling.

> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "He knows my name now. The Cardinal. He looked straight at me and opened a letter. Cream, with a red seal."
>
> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "I've been chasing that letter since Meung. He's read my name off it."
>
> **CONSTANCE:** "Then he knows the name of the boy who beat him. Let him."

**Why you turn the page:** there are three men who do not know yet that he arrived.

## PAGE 47 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the attic.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Late night. The attic. D'ARTAGNAN on the bed with his
boots still on and the ring on his hand; Constance a step inside, by the
window. Boots on the stair, and PORTHOS's head coming through the door with a second,
stranger's hat crammed on top of his own.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left, over the dark beam)*
> "They came home that night in the order they had stopped."
>
> **PORTHOS:** "I won. I ate the inn. I rode after you, never caught you, rode back. Did anyone see me? Did you arrive?"
>
> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "I arrived."

**Panel 2 — 25%.** ARAMIS behind him, limping, immaculate, the handkerchief
still round his hand.

> **ARAMIS:** "I held the bridge till dark. Then, as the Fathers tell us, I walked. It's a long way on one leg. Did you arrive?"
>
> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "I arrived."

**Panel 3 — dominant, 50%.** ATHOS, last, filling the doorway, hat in hand,
looking at the boy. Porthos's face beside him says what Athos smells like.

> **ATHOS:** "Did you arrive?"
>
> **D'ARTAGNAN:** "I arrived."
>
> **ATHOS:** "Good."
>
> **PORTHOS:** "Athos. You smell like a hundred barrels."
>
> **ATHOS:** "A hundred and six. I counted them. It was a long week."

**Why you turn the page:** all four are in one attic, and it is nearly morning.

## PAGE 48 · *illustrated prose*

**Location:** the field behind the Carmelite convent.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 65%.** Morning, dew. The four on the grass by the yellow
wall with bread and a bottle, Constance's open basket between them. The
yellow horse drinking from the trough.

**Panel 2 — 35%.** The boy's hand, the ring on it, holding a piece of bread.

> **PROSE FIELD 1** *(over the pale wall, never over the four)*
> "In the morning they ate breakfast on the grass behind the Carmelites, where they had first met, where Porthos had put a man in the trough. The yellow horse drank out of the trough. Constance had sent bread in the basket.
>
> d'Artagnan counted what he had, the way he had on the bench by the river a month ago. The yellow horse, which nobody would laugh at again, because of who had ridden it. His father's sword. A queen's ring, which he could never sell and never explain. Three friends who had each stopped to hold a door so that he could go through it. And a man in red, somewhere in Paris, who knew his name.
>
> He had come to Paris with a letter. He decided it was a fair trade."

**Why you turn the page:** there is a man in red somewhere in Paris who knows his name, and one page left to see him.

## PAGE 49 · *spectacle*

**Location:** a Paris street.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 100%.** From above and behind: four men walking away
from the reader down a narrow Paris street, four abreast, hats on. The small
one in ochre in the middle with a hand on his hilt; the huge one with the red
plumes; the slim one; the tall one on the outside. In the foreground, at the
top of the frame, a window with a thin figure in red in it, watching them go,
one hand stroking a grey cat on the sill.

> **CAPTION** *(lower edge, over the cobbles)*
> "All for one, and one for all. And at a window above the street, a man in red who knew the boy's name."

> **TITLE CARD** *(bottom, small)*
> "End of Volume I"

**Why you turn the page:** there is no next page; the man in red will be there when there is.
