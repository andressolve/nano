### C1 — Dialogue transcription or ownership fails
The caption and all nine lines read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once each, in causal order; each of the three questions hangs from a different man and each of the boy's three answers from the boy; Athos's single word Good sits alone on his side; Constance carries no balloon. A string that does not read from the 600 × 900 proof is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured. Any extra text anywhere on the page (a name, a numeral, a label, a stray word on a wall or paper) is blocking. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word (duelling for dueling, colour for color), and the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue are nonblocking: the words and their order are what must match.

### C2 — The turn does not land
The page must read in order as: Porthos comes through the door, then Aramis limping, then Athos filling the doorway, and each asks the boy whether he arrived. If Athos in the doorway with the boy answering is not the largest image the eye finds, the turn has not landed. A second hat is a comic prop, not a separate story beat: if Porthos and his arrival read clearly, that hat being absent or indistinct is nonblocking.

### C3 — The three Musketeers collide
Porthos huge with red curls and a waxed moustache; Aramis slim, blond, limping, handkerchief on his hand; Athos tall, dark, pointed beard, no sling. Any two merging is blocking. Porthos's second hat being absent or indistinct is nonblocking when his mass, red curls, moustache, and first arrival separate him from Aramis and Athos.

### C4 — Constance is missing
The small girl in grey must be visible inside the attic by the window on the page; her absence is blocking.

### C5 — d'Artagnan or the ring fails
Clean-shaven, muddy ochre, boots on, a gold ring with a dark stone on his hand. Blocking if contradicted.

### C6 — Focal generation failure
A fifth companion, a duplicated Musketeer, gross anatomy on Athos's hand with the hat, or a balloon across a face is blocking.
