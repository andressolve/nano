### C2 — The turn does not land

If the red seated figure beside the dais with the slung man behind is not the largest image the eye finds, the turn has not landed.

### C3 — The Cardinal fails identity or stands
Thin, grey pointed beard, red robe and skullcap, seated, no cat, the cream letter with the broken seal on the chair arm. Standing, dark-haired, or a sword is blocking.

### C4 — Rochefort fails identity or the sling is wrong
One cheek scar, thin moustache, black, red feather, RIGHT arm in a white sling. The left arm slung or no sling is blocking.

### C5 — Constance or the Queen fails
Constance in the gallery in grey with a white collar and no apron; the Queen absent and the doors shut. The Queen already in the hall is blocking.

### C6 — Focal generation failure
A duplicated Cardinal, a cat, an open pouch with visible stones, gross anatomy on the hand at the sleeve, or a balloon across a face is blocking. Crowd repetition and candle flare are not.
