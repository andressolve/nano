## Continuity judgment

keeps the warm laid-paper ground, line-led brush-and-ink treatment, matte
gouache colour, readable paper grain, and scene-appropriate cast depth. Daylight

lightly moustached. Rochefort's scar, black clothing, red plume, and backward

dark curls under a linen cap, grey-and-white dress, crown apron, basket, and
keys. The yellow horse remains immediately recognizable across its appearances.

yellow-stone wall, wall gap, trough, large tree, grass, and directional outdoor

steep wooden stair, warm candlelight, attic beams, narrow bed, table, window,
and isolated wall nail. Paris broadens from river view to street view without
confusing the setting or disrupting the register.
