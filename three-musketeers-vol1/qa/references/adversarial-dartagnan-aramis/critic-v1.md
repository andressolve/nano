## Mandatory findings

1. **Aramis has drifted from his approved face.** Redesign only the board's Aramis face to restore the approved short, softly oval facial envelope: shorten the midface and chin, narrow the jaw to a tapered chin, replace the low heavy brow with the approved fine high arch, and restore the small sharply upturned moustache plus centered soul patch. Keep his slim build and collar-length blond curls. Do not use the blue cassock or cross as the repair.

2. **The board does not prove the pair in strict profile or under a reaction expression.** The four figures are one neutral three-quarter/front pair repeated in grayscale. Recompose the four-test board to include: the pair together in neutral colour, the same pair together in grayscale, a same-scale strict-profile pair, and a same-scale shared reaction-expression pair. In profile, lock d'Artagnan to the longer forward-projecting nose, narrow jaw, and jagged black fringe, while Aramis carries the shorter straighter nose, tapered chin, visible blond hairline/collar curls, and upturned moustache with soul patch. Preserve those face lengths and brow structures through the reaction view rather than relying on costume.
