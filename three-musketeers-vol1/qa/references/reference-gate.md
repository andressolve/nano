## Mandatory findings

1. **Recurring-role coverage passes.** All eight causally important recurring roles have approved permanent sheets: d'Artagnan, Athos, Porthos, Aramis, Constance, the Cardinal, Rochefort, and Buckingham. The Queen, King, guards, jeweler, innkeepers, workers, sailors, and court remain deliberately faceless scenery without balloons rather than unlocked recurring identities. **Structural redesign requirement: none.**

2. **Nearest-lookalike registration and separation pass.** Every character sheet has a named nearest lookalike in the reference plan, and every named collision is carried first by face, age, hair/facial-hair mass, body mass, or posture rather than by colour or costume alone. The four emphasized adversarial pairs pass costume-free, profile, reaction, grayscale, and reduced-scale inspection. **Structural redesign requirement: none.**

3. **Reserved identity space passes.** The plan explicitly forbids every complete principal identity stack from background figures and supplies safe alternatives for guards, other Musketeers, innkeepers, sailors, the court, the Queen, and the King. No principal stack remains available for an unnamed figure. **Structural redesign requirement: none.**

4. **Sheet lettering passes.** None of the eight approved character sheets contains a name, label, caption, border text, watermark, or other lettering. **Structural redesign requirement: none.**

5. **Consequential continuity passes.** The approved setting and object plates lock only story-bearing locations, spatial markers, and stateful objects. The horse, sword, two distinct letters, pass, sling, baldric, handkerchief, pouch, casket states, studs, ring, and cat are accounted for; no unlisted decorative prop is positioned to become a repeated forty-page continuity obligation. **Structural redesign requirement: none.**

6. **Adversarial system coverage passes.** The head board, silhouette/grayscale board, four focused collision plates, and Athos/Rochefort live-pair plate collectively cover re-entry, same-light comparison, profiles, reactions, costume removal, grayscale, thumbnail silhouette, and same-panel opposition. **Structural redesign requirement: none.**

## Nonblocking observations

- The head and silhouette boards make the broad cast hierarchy immediately legible: boy; three differently built Musketeers; young woman; seated red prelate; compact scarred man in black; broad golden duke.
- Athos/Rochefort carries the strongest redundancy in the system: lower-face construction, scar topology, height, posture, value pattern, plume, and opposed sling arms. The face construction remains sufficient when costume and colour disappear.
- Aramis/Buckingham remains safe without relying on their never sharing a page; jaw width, beard mass, curl length, and shoulder breadth survive the adversarial reductions.
- The casket plate makes the ten-to-twelve change readable through two oversized adjacent empty slots, while the letters plate distinguishes the father's and Queen's letters by packet proportion and seal structure even without colour.
- Setting plates repeat only dramatic spatial markers—window, stair, trough, gap, cellar steps, gangplank, dais/gallery, and the palace's white-gold architectural lane—rather than incidental dressing.
