## Mandatory findings

1. The plate has only three views, and all three show the horse fully saddled and bridled; it therefore omits the manifest's required bare state and fails the four-view lock. Redesign the plate as four distinct views of the same old gelding: retain a large three-quarter head study, retain a strict full-length ninety-degree side profile with the established saddle and bridle, add a full-length bare three-quarter view with saddle, blanket, bridle, reins, stirrups, and every other piece of tack completely absent, and add a bare rear view. Preserve the same long, slightly downturned head, modest withers, heavy calm posture, pale mane and tail, and unmistakable buttercup-yellow coat in all four views.

2. The rear/action view binds the horse to a stone trough and depicts the required final re-entry state under full tack. Remove the trough, masonry, water pump, wet paving, and all other scenery from the object plate. Make both bare views read as the horse's late re-entry state through the tack-free silhouette itself; the page may supply the Carmelite-field trough separately from its setting lock.
