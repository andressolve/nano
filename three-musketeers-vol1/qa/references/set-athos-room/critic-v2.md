## Mandatory findings

1. The plate violates the book-wide visual register: it reads as smooth photorealistic monochrome rather than brush-and-ink on warm laid paper with opaque matte gouache. Redesign the entire plate as line-led brush-and-ink architecture on visibly warm laid paper, with raw-umber and restrained grey gouache washes, visible grain, and chalky wash edges; eliminate photographic surface rendering and smooth prestige-oil/3D realism while retaining grey window light and real cast depth.
2. The room's primary marker is structurally wrong. Replace the broad, double-edged cruciform sword and round pommel with the same narrow-bladed seventeenth-century rapier carried by approved Athos: a slender blade, compact swept metal hilt, straight grip, and matching pommel proportions. Keep that single rapier hanging on the wall in the present state so it can leave the wall for Athos's hand on p. 26 without creating a second weapon identity.

## Nonblocking observations

- No figures, lettering, labels, watermark, or internal panel borders are present.
- The required inventory is otherwise exact and spare: one chair, one table, one bed, whitewashed walls, and one wall-hung weapon.
- The grey daylight enters clearly from the single window, and the wall weapon remains the sole dramatic accent.
- The pillow and plain blanket read as ordinary parts of the specified bed, not as consequential continuity props.
- Character-sheet four-view, face-crop, profile, expression, and re-entry tests are not applicable to this set plate; at thumbnail and in grayscale it remains legible as a bare one-window room.
