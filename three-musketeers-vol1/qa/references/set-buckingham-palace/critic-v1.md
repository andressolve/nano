## Mandatory findings

1. The straight vertical split between the exterior and interiors and the straight horizontal split between bedroom and workroom read as panel borders, which are blocking. Redesign the plate as one unframed 1536 x 1024 landscape ground: carry warm laid paper to all four edges and feather the irregular outer contours of all three vignettes into open negative space, with no straight seam, rule, frame, or rectangular division anywhere.

2. The palace does not carry its locked light identity. The charcoal exterior sky, dark brown bedroom floor and bed recess, and nearly black workroom create extensive shadow, while the location is specified as white, gold, and mirror with no shadow anywhere. Relight all three vignettes in the same high-key, diffuse white-gold illumination: replace the charcoal sky with luminous river-grey, lift the bedroom floor and canopy recess to pale white, warm grey, and restrained gold, and open the workroom's black wall and bench masses to readable pale umber line over white-gold paper. Keep depth through line weight and overlapping planes, not black cast-shadow pools.

3. The closed ornate box on the bedroom table can become a false state for the consequential velvet casket, whose ten-stud/two-empty and later twelve-stud states belong to its object lock. Rebuild the small bedside table as a bare white-and-gold primary marker and remove the box, goblet, bowl, and every other portable tabletop object; do not replace them with another container or jewel prop.

4. The workroom contains both a tall lit lantern and an open brazier flame, turning the specified small fire into two competing flame markers. Remove the lantern, its flame, and its glow completely. Retain exactly one small bench fire as the sole flame, and arrange one loupe and one pair of tongs in isolated clear shapes beside it so that bench, loupe, tongs, and single small fire survive thumbnail and grayscale reduction without the surrounding tool clutter becoming a second primary marker.

## Nonblocking observations

- All three manifest views are present: water stair and white river front with masts; white-and-gold bedroom with mirrors and a small bedside table; jeweler's workroom with bench and tools. A fourth view is not required for this setting plate.
- No figures, lettering, labels, or watermark are visible.
- The exterior's broad stair, the bedroom's canopy bed and mirrors, and the workroom's bench are strong re-entry silhouettes once the border and value failures are corrected.
- The visible brush line, laid-paper ground, muted gouache texture, white facade, gold accents, and river of masts otherwise fit the intended register.
