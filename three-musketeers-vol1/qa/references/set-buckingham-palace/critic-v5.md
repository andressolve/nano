## Mandatory findings

1. The required hall of mirrors is missing. The four visible studies are the water stair/white front, the bedroom with casket, a wide workroom, and a close-up of loose jeweler's tools. Replace the bottom foreground tool close-up with a wide hall-of-mirrors study whose primary marker is a repeated rhythm of full-height mirrors in white-and-gold wall bays, clearly distinct from the bedroom; retain the water stair, bedroom/casket, and jeweler's workroom as the other three views.

2. The lighting does not meet the location's explicit no-shadow rule. Cast shadows remain beneath and beside the exterior stair, bed, casket table, workbench, and foreground tools, while the workroom fireplace creates a locally dark recess. Re-light every view with high-key, directionless white illumination and remove those cast-shadow shapes; preserve depth through ink contour, marble joints, mirror edges, and restrained gold accents rather than dark tonal masses. The small workroom fire may remain visible, but it must not become a shadow-casting key light.
