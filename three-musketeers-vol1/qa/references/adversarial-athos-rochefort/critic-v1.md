## Mandatory findings

1. **The paired thumbnail silhouettes collapse the ledger's strongest structural distinction.** Athos and Rochefort are rendered at essentially the same height and with nearly the same narrow, cloaked outline. Redesign every same-scale paired view on a common floor line so that Athos's bare crown is one full head above Rochefort's bare crown, excluding hats and plumes. Keep Athos vertically still and straight; keep Rochefort compact, weight back, with chin raised. The difference must survive grayscale at the smallest row's size.

2. **Athos has inherited Rochefort's identity stack.** The board puts Athos in all black with a red feather, contradicting Athos's approved sheet and both characters' `Never` rules. Restore Athos's blue cloak/cassock with its white cross and single white plume in every clothed board view; reserve the all-black cloak and red feather exclusively for Rochefort. Do not use duplicated costume silhouettes as the adversarial neutralizer: the face crops themselves must prove costume-free separation.

3. **Rochefort's primary facial marker is absent or too faint to read consistently.** On every Rochefort view, carry one continuous pale scar from the left temple across the cheekbone to the corner of the mouth. Preserve that exact path through three-quarter, strict-profile, and reaction views, and give it sufficient value contrast and line weight to remain visible in the board's smallest grayscale rendering.

4. **The board does not test identity through a reaction expression.** Replace one redundant neutral thumbnail pair with a matched, same-scale reaction pair. Hold Athos to a long gaunt face, long nose, heavy-lidded eyes, and pointed beard; hold Rochefort to a shorter square face, heavy brow, strong jaw, thin moustache without beard, and the locked scar path. The two identities must remain readable when the shared reaction changes their brows and mouths.

## Nonblocking observations

- The strict profiles are the board's strongest evidence: Athos's longer facial plane and pointed beard separate well from Rochefort's square clean chin and moustache.
- The sheet contains no lettering, labels, watermark, framing border, or stray recurring prop.
- In the large three-quarter views, both faces are recognizably derived from their approved sheets; the failure is the loss of redundant separation under the required adversarial reductions, not wholesale face replacement.
