## Mandatory findings

1. The hanging glazed lantern contains a second visible flame and forms the plate's largest dark-warm mass, violating both the exactly-one-flame lock and the high-key no-black-mass lighting design. Remove the hanging lantern and every flame or glowing wick except the one small, shallow fire on the bench; keep that bench fire as the sole concentrated dark-warm accent without adding a cast-shadow pool.
2. The foreground loupe reads as a deep cylindrical cup or open vessel, one of the specifically forbidden silhouettes, and its lens is not legible. Replace it with a compact, shallow circular loupe in a thin metal housing, rotated to side-three-quarter so a transparent lens opening is unmistakable; give it no vertical wall, cavity, crown, brim, cup, or hat silhouette, and place it immediately beside the tongs for scale.
3. The foreground hinged tool has long pointed legs and reads closer to dividers than to a single unmistakable pair of jeweler's tongs. Redesign it as exactly one pair of compact pivoted tongs with two opposed gripping jaws and short handles; remove or simplify any secondary bench or rack tools whose paired jaws could read as additional tongs, leaving the remaining tools faint and subordinate.

## Nonblocking observations

- The white, cream, and very light umber palette, pale bench fronts, pale recess, and directionless high-key illumination otherwise carry Buckingham's shadowless white-and-gold world.
- The plate has no figures, casket, lettering, labels, borders, or watermark.
- A single unframed landscape view is correct for this set plate; the four-view test applies to character locks, not this setting entry.
