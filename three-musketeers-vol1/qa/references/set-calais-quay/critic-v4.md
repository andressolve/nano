## Mandatory findings

1. The foreground post carries a conspicuous pale notice with multiple lines of pseudo-lettering. Lettering on any reference sheet is blocking, and this paper also creates a wrong fixed/display state that can compete with the consequential Cardinal's pass, which must remain a folded paper carried and shown by a character. Structurally redesign the foreground control point as a clearly hinged, waist-high **blank timber barrier spanning the quay approach beside the foot of the gangplank**: remove the notice and every paper-like rectangle from the setting, retain no marks or writing on the barrier, and leave clear negative space beside it for the later sergeant and hand-held pass.

## Nonblocking observations

- The grey sea and sky, wet stone, wind-driven spray, gulls, ropes, and harbour architecture carry the specified Calais morning atmosphere and the book's rain-grey/river-grey outdoor register.
- The gangplank is large, central, and strongly legible in colour, grayscale, and at thumbnail scale, so the location's dramatic accent is usable for the duel.
- The restrained lantern warmth against the cold harbour is compatible with the lantern-film register and does not displace the grey location palette.
- No watermark or panel border is visible, and no human figure is clearly present.
