## Mandatory findings

1. The barrier currently reads as a permanent chest-high quay-edge railing parallel to the water, not the pass-controlled barrier across the quay approach. Replace that foreground fence with one bare timber checkpoint gate crossing the landward walking route: set its long rail perpendicular to the quay edge, leave a clearly readable route from the town-side stones through the gate to the foot of the gangplank, and keep every timber face completely bare—no notice, paper, placard, inscription, or substitute symbol.

2. The lantern is the plate's brightest, warmest accent and the massive black barrier is its strongest silhouette, so the mandated gangplank is demoted at both thumbnail and grayscale. Remove the foreground lantern, flame, and warm spill completely; suppress the warm window pinlights; relight the plate as diffuse grey morning; reduce the barrier gate's silhouette to secondary weight; and carry one uninterrupted, light-edged wet gangplank diagonally from the open stone quay to the boat so that the gangplank is the first high-contrast marker after the harbour silhouette and remains clear enough to stage the duel.
