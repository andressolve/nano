# Character Ledger — 8 locks, hard ceiling

Identity hierarchy, in order of priority:
**1.** face shape and apparent age · **2.** hair and facial-hair mass ·
**3.** body silhouette and habitual posture · **4.** costume value pattern ·
**5.** colour accent, prop, repeated gesture.

> Costume and colour may reinforce a character; they may never be the only
> cues. Separation is built through skull, jaw, nose, eye set, hairline,
> apparent age, body mass and posture — the traits that survive grayscale and
> thumbnail reduction. **Every page prompt names both the positive lock and the
> nearest forbidden lookalike.**

The cast divides into four silhouettes that must never blur: **the boy** (one
beardless youth, the only one in the book); **three Musketeers in the same
blue** (tall and still, huge and loud, slim and neat: three different bodies
under one cassock, separated by mass, hair colour, and facial hair before
anything else); **two dark men in the Cardinal's service** (one seated in
red, one standing in black with a scar); and **two who are none of those** (a
young woman in grey and white; a golden duke in white satin). The danger
pairs are Athos/Rochefort (dark, bearded, grave, the same age to a child's
eye) and Aramis/Buckingham (fair, elegant, fine-featured).

## 1 · D'ARTAGNAN — eighteen

**Anchor:** new.

**Wants (volume):** from everyone, to be let in the door. From the scar, the
fight he was denied at Meung. From the three, to be one of them. From
Constance, to be asked. **Wants (secretly):** a family, because his father
sent him away with a horse and a sentence.

**Moral position:** believes he is fighting for his honour. Is actually
fighting to be let in, and gets in the only way that works: by being the one
who does not leave when the others are outnumbered. Nobody says this.

**Arc:** sent away with a letter → robbed of it → shut out → challenges three
men → picks a side → given a name by the three → given a room and a girl →
asked → rides → left alone → beats the scar → arrives → comes home → named by
the Cardinal.

**Structural lock:** a boy, not a man: long narrow face, sharp chin, a long
straight Gascon nose, dark brows, dark brown eyes set close and quick,
sun-brown skin. **No beard, no moustache — the only clean-shaven male face in
the book.** Black hair, thick, unruly, falling into his eyes, cut roughly to
the jaw. Lean, all elbows and knees, narrow shoulders, a head shorter than
Athos and half the width of Porthos. Never stands still: weight forward on
the balls of his feet, one hand always on the hilt. Habitual gesture: pushing
his hair out of his eyes with the back of his sword hand.

**Costume:** a doublet of Gascon ochre-yellow wool, faded and patched, with
brown breeches and worn brown riding boots; a grey felt hat with a broken
feather; his father's sword, an old long rapier with a plain swept hilt, too
long for him. No cloak, ever: he is not a Musketeer. In Movements V–VI, mud
to the waist. He keeps the same clothes for the whole book.

**Never:** a beard or moustache of any kind (every other man). Blue (the
three). Black clothes, a scar (Rochefort). Blond hair (Aramis, Buckingham).
Broad shoulders (Porthos).

**Voice:** says the biggest thing first and the reason after, if at all. Asks
questions he already knows the answer to. Never more than two sentences
before he moves.

## 2 · ATHOS — thirty, looks older

**Anchor:** new.

**Wants (volume):** from d'Artagnan, nothing, which is why the boy gives him
everything. From the world, to be left alone with a sword and a chair.
**Wants (secretly):** a reason to care about anything, and this boy is one,
and he will not say so.

**Moral position:** believes he is teaching a boy to survive Paris. Is
actually letting a boy remind him of what he was before whatever happened,
which this book never names and never will.

**Arc:** collided with, in pain → sets the duel → laughs when the boy has
challenged all three → fights one-handed → asks the boy's name → teaches him
at dawn → says "good" when nobody knows what is in the letter → holds the
cellar → comes home smelling of barrels → "Did you arrive?"

**Structural lock:** tall, straight as a post, the tallest man in the book by
a hand. Long pale face, high forehead, straight nose, grey eyes, hooded and
level, deep vertical lines beside the mouth. Dark brown hair to the shoulder,
straight, pushed back; a short pointed beard and a neat moustache, dark.
Lean, broad-shouldered, no stomach. Stands with both hands folded on the
pommel of his sword, point on the ground, and does not shift his weight.
Habitual gesture: stillness; the one man in any panel who is not moving.

**Costume:** the Musketeer's blue cassock with the white cross, worn open over
a black doublet; black hat, one white plume; a plain steel-hilted sword. Left
arm in a black sling, pp 8–16 only; gone from p 17.

**Never:** a scar, black clothes without the blue, a thin moustache with no
beard, a red feather (Rochefort). A stomach, red hair, a gold baldric
(Porthos). Fair hair, a book (Aramis). Never seen running.

**Voice:** the fewest words on any page he is on. Says "good" when things are
bad. Never explains; repeats only an order, once, alone in its own balloon ("Ride.").

## 3 · PORTHOS — thirty-five

**Anchor:** new.

**Wants (volume):** from everyone, to be looked at. From d'Artagnan, at first,
satisfaction for having seen the back of the baldric; then an audience.
**Wants (secretly):** to be loved for the man under the baldric, which he
would be embarrassed to know he already is.

**Moral position:** believes he is the bravest of the four. Is actually the
kindest, and the first to stop.

**Arc:** the cloak comes off → "one o'clock" → the trough → four abreast →
stories at the table → "I've never beaten an Englishman" → the toast at
Chantilly → "Go, I'll be an hour" → home, having eaten the inn.

**Structural lock:** enormous: the widest man in the book by half, a head
taller than d'Artagnan, chest like a barrel, big hands. Round ruddy face,
heavy jaw, small bright blue eyes, a big nose. Red-brown hair in thick curls
to the collar, a great red-brown moustache waxed out to points, no beard.
Stands chest out, fists on hips, feet planted wide. Habitual gesture: a hand
smoothing the front of the baldric.

**Costume:** the blue cassock with the white cross, strained across the
chest, over a doublet of crimson; a wide shoulder-baldric embroidered with
gold in front and plain buff leather behind (seen once, p 9); a black hat
with the largest plume in the book, three ostrich feathers, red. A heavy
sword with a gilt hilt.

**Never:** lean, still, pale, quiet (Athos). A beard. Anything neat (Aramis).
Never seen without the baldric after p 9.

**Voice:** the loudest on any page; puts himself in every sentence, and is
generous in the same breath: "I would have eaten it myself, but you're
thinner."

## 4 · ARAMIS — twenty-four

**Anchor:** new.

**Wants (volume):** from everyone, to be believed when he says he is leaving
for the Church. From d'Artagnan, at first, the handkerchief back, and for
nobody to have seen it. **Wants (secretly):** never to leave the three, and
he is the one who ends up on the bridge.

**Moral position:** believes he is the clever one who will get out of all
this. Is actually the one who stays.

**Arc:** the handkerchief → "two o'clock" → pins a hat to the wall → Latin
at the table → "the Cardinal will not let four through" → the horse goes
down at Beauvais → "Go, I'll hold the road" → home, limping, smiling.

**Structural lock:** slim, of middle height, a hand shorter than Athos, half
the width of Porthos. Fine oval face, smooth high forehead, straight small
nose, large dark eyes with long lashes, pale clear skin, a small mouth.
Blond hair, fair and fine, curled to the collar and always in order; a
small neat blond moustache and a tiny tuft under the lip, no beard. Stands
with his weight on one hip, one hand at his moustache. Habitual gesture: two
fingers touching the moustache before he speaks.

**Costume:** the blue cassock with the white cross, immaculate, over a
doublet of dove grey with a white lace collar; black hat with one white
plume; a slim sword with a silver hilt; a small book in a strap at his belt;
a white lace handkerchief with a small embroidered monogram (p 10, p 29, p 47).

**Never:** a beard, dark hair, height, stillness (Athos). Bulk, red, a
baldric (Porthos). Pearls, white satin, curls to the shoulder, a beard
(Buckingham). Never dishevelled, even on the bridge.

**Voice:** soft, polite, and sly; says "as the Fathers tell us" and "in
theory"; says the opposite of what he is about to do.

## 5 · CONSTANCE — twenty

**Anchor:** new. Unmarried; the house is hers. Absorbs the Bonacieux
household entire.

**Wants (volume):** from the Queen, to be trusted. From d'Artagnan, someone
who is not a courtier, and then more than that. **Wants (secretly):** him,
which she says with a window and never with a word.

**Moral position:** believes she is protecting the Queen. Is actually sending
a boy against the Cardinal's whole machine, knows it, and asks anyway,
because there is nobody else.

**Arc:** lets the attic → looks up the stair → is taken in the street → is
freed → cannot say why → comes up the stair white as paper and asks → sews
the letter into his coat → watches the doors at the ball → gives him the
ring.

**Structural lock:** small, quick, a head shorter than d'Artagnan. Round face,
soft chin, short straight nose, wide-set hazel eyes, dark brows, a scatter of
freckles. Dark brown hair in curls, pinned up under a white linen cap with
strands always escaping. Slight, straight-backed, hands never idle. Habitual
gesture: looking up the stair.

**Costume:** a plain dress of dove grey with a white apron and white cuffs;
the Queen's livery only as a small gold-embroidered crown on the apron's bib;
a ring of keys at her waist; a covered basket. At the ball, the same grey
with the apron off and a white collar.

**Never:** finery, jewels, a train, a crown, a fan, white satin (the Queen,
who is scenery). Never seen at the Louvre except at the back stair (p 41) and in the gallery at the ball.

**Voice:** practical, fast, Parisian, and truthful about everything except
herself.

## 6 · THE CARDINAL — forty-five

**Anchor:** new. Absorbs the King's demand at the ball; the King is scenery.

**Wants (volume):** from Rochefort, two diamonds and nothing else. From the
Queen, to be shamed in front of the court on Monday, so that after Monday
nobody in France outranks him. **Wants (secretly):** never to have to raise
his voice, and he never does.

**Moral position:** believes he is protecting France from a queen who gives
away the King's gifts. Is actually removing the last person the King listens
to over him. Nobody says either thing.

**Arc:** reads the stolen letter → does not care about the girl → orders two
diamonds → arranges the ball → waits with the pouch in his sleeve → counts
to twelve → counts again → reads the name off the letter.

**Structural lock:** a thin ascetic man, seated whenever possible: long
narrow face, hollow cheeks, high forehead, a thin high-bridged nose, pale
grey eyes, hooded, that do not blink; pale skin. Grey hair cropped close
under the cap; a short pointed grey beard and a thin grey moustache, both
precise. Narrow-shouldered, long-fingered. Habitual gesture: one hand
stroking a grey cat on his lap; the hand never stops.

**Costume:** a cardinal's robe and cape of one saturated red, a red skullcap,
a small gold cross on a chain. The only red robe in the book. At the ball,
the same red in a sea of gold.

**Never:** black clothes, a scar, a sword, standing, a hat with a feather
(Rochefort). Dark hair (Athos). Never seen with a raised hand or a raised
voice. Never seen outside a chair except once, turning toward the ballroom
doors on p 45; at the window on p 49 he is seated, the cat on the sill.

**Voice:** quiet, complete sentences; never asks a question he does not
already know the answer to, except one.

## 7 · ROCHEFORT, the man with the scar — forty

**Anchor:** new. Absorbs Milady's theft, de Wardes's pass, and Jussac's
guards.

**Wants (volume):** from the Cardinal, to be the hand. From d'Artagnan, to
erase Meung: a boy drew on him in an inn yard and has kept turning up since.
**Wants (secretly):** to be feared by someone who is not paid to fear him.

**Moral position:** believes he is serving France through the Cardinal. Is
actually a proud man being beaten by a boy three times in a month, and
knows it by Calais.

**Arc:** laughs at the horse → pockets the letter → "The Gascon" at the
Carmelites → retreats → watches in the alley: "Three times you've drawn on me" → sent to London →
"This is the fourth" → into the harbour → a sling at the ball.

**Structural lock:** a dark man of middle height, taller than d'Artagnan,
shorter than Athos: a square hard face, heavy black brows, black eyes, a
strong jaw, sallow skin, and **a pale scar anatomically fixed to the left
side, running from the left temple down across the cheekbone to the corner
of the mouth**. It is visible only when the left facial plane is exposed;
his right-cheek profile is clean and must never inherit or mirror the scar.
Black hair, straight, to the collar; a thin black moustache, no
beard. Compact, muscular, sits a horse sideways, stands with his weight back
and his chin up. Habitual gesture: a laugh through the nose.

**Costume:** black from hat to boot: a black doublet, black cloak, black hat
with a single red feather; a plain black-hilted sword. Never the guards' red
cassock: he commands them, he is not one of them. Right arm in a white sling,
pp 42–46 (from the fall into the harbour, p 35).

**Never:** blue, a white cross, a pointed beard, a sling on the left arm,
stillness with hands on a pommel (Athos). Red robes (the Cardinal). A scar
anywhere but the left side of the face, including any mark on the right
cheek in a right-cheek profile.

**Voice:** clipped and mocking, and always one word too many, which is how
the boy knows he is not calm.

## 8 · THE DUKE OF BUCKINGHAM — thirty-five

**Anchor:** new.

**Wants (volume):** from d'Artagnan, to know she is safe. From the Cardinal,
to be beaten at his own trick. **Wants (secretly):** an excuse to be near her,
and the boy is the closest he will get.

**Moral position:** believes he is keeping a gift. Is actually keeping the
evidence that can ruin her, and understands this the moment he counts ten.

**Arc:** comes down the stairs himself → reads → counts ten → understands the
trap → sends for the jeweler → does not sit for two days → cannot finish
"Tell her."

**Structural lock:** a broad-shouldered man in his prime, as tall as Athos:
a handsome square-jawed face, straight nose, blue eyes, high colour, a wide
mouth. Golden hair in long loose curls past the shoulder; a short golden
beard and a full moustache. Athletic, moves fast, takes stairs two at a time.
Habitual gesture: pulling off one glove.

**Costume:** white satin doublet and breeches slashed with gold, a wide
collar of white lace, a rope of pearls, a blue silk sash. The only man in
the book dressed for a party at every hour. No cloak, no cassock.

**Never:** slim, blond and fine-featured, a small moustache, a book, dove
grey, blue serge (Aramis). Never seen in London's mud; the boy brings the
mud in.

**Voice:** warm, grand, and fast to decide; the one man in the book who
talks as quickly as d'Artagnan.

## Scenery with a signature (unlocked, no balloons)

- **The Queen.** A figure in white and silver across the ballroom, pp 43–44,
  seen from a distance or from behind, face never close. Twelve diamond
  studs in two rows on the left shoulder. Never named; only "the Queen."
- **The King.** A crowned figure seated on the dais, pp 42–44, no face.
- **The Cardinal's guards.** Red cassocks with a white cross, five at the
  Carmelites, four in the alley (in plain cloaks), a handful at Amiens. No
  faces, no names, no balloons.
- **The jeweler.** An old man at a bench with a loupe, p 39, no balloon.
- **Innkeepers, road workers, sailors, the court.** No faces.

## Reserved design space

No background or unnamed figure may inherit a complete identity stack:

- beardless youth + black hair in the eyes + ochre doublet → **d'Artagnan**
- tall + dark pointed beard + blue cassock + sling → **Athos**
- huge + red moustache + gold baldric + red plumes → **Porthos**
- slim + blond curls + small moustache + blue cassock → **Aramis**
- grey dress + white cap + basket + keys → **Constance**
- red robe + grey beard + cat + chair → **the Cardinal**
- black clothes + scar + red feather → **Rochefort**
- gold curls + white satin + pearls → **Buckingham**

## Collision matrix — risk, and what carries the separation

Adversarial boards are built for every pair at medium risk or above. Low-risk pairs are proved on the head and silhouette boards and get no board of their own; the Queen is scenery with no face and no board may invent one.

| Pair | Risk | Carried by |
|---|---|---|
| Athos / Rochefort | highest | beard shape (pointed beard vs thin moustache only); Rochefort's anatomically fixed left-cheek scar and clean right cheek; height (Athos a head taller); cloak value (blue with white cross vs all black); posture (stillness vs weight back, chin up); the sling on opposite arms in their shared pages |
| Aramis / Buckingham | high | mass (slim vs broad); hair length and curl (to the collar, fine vs past the shoulder, heavy); beard (none vs short golden beard); costume value (blue serge vs white satin); pearls; the two are never on the same page |
| d'Artagnan / Aramis | medium | hair colour (black vs blond); beard (none vs small moustache); costume (ochre, no cloak vs blue cassock); posture (weight forward vs weight on one hip) |
| Athos / the Cardinal | medium | age (thirty vs forty-five, grey); colour (blue vs red); posture (standing vs seated); hair mass (to the shoulder vs cropped under a cap) |
| Porthos / Buckingham | low (no board) | hair colour (red-brown vs gold); face (round and ruddy vs square and handsome); costume (blue and crimson vs white satin) |
| Constance / the Queen (scenery) | low (no board; the Queen has no face) | the Queen is never close; grey and white vs white and silver; a cap vs a crown |
