# 03 — SETTINGS · "The Pea Square"

This is a diagrammatic explainer, so there is essentially **one "setting": the plate itself** — the aged-cream notebook page. There is no monastery, no garden scene to stage (the bio did that); here the "world" is the paper, the ink, and the pencil grid. These are the recurring surfaces and layouts so pages stay shelf-consistent.

## SURFACE A — The cream plate (every page)
The ground for every page: **aged cream / ivory laid paper**, visible grain, soft foxing at the edges, faint pencil-ruled margin. Calm even daylight, no dramatic shadow. This is the constant — the book reads as one notebook opened to successive pages. Soft sepia ink, warm graphite-pencil grey, chalk white. Living accents (pea-green, blossom-violet) sit *on* the cream like specimens pinned to a study sheet; the cream is mostly bare — don't flood it with colour.

## LAYOUT 1 — The teaching plate (default, most pages)
One central diagram (a pair of plants, a row of peas, a 2×2 grid, a tally) with **few, short caption boxes** (≤3 text zones + the diagram). Caption boxes = aged-cream insets with soft sepia serif text, like notebook annotations. Diagram labels = handwritten-pencil, short, with thin painted leader-lines pointing to what they name. Title strip optional at top.

## LAYOUT 2 — The POSTER / cutaway plate (P5 & P11 only)
A single **big central diagram** (the grid machine) on the cream plate, with **3–5 short labelled callouts** pointing to its parts via thin pencil leader-lines, a **title strip** across the top, one **summary line** at the bottom. P5 = the 2×2 machine explained (how to draw it, parents on the edges, how a cell is filled). P11 = the scaled-up 4×4 dihybrid grid → 9:3:3:1. These are the geometry/text-dense pages — prototype first. No dark schematic background — keep it cream, pencil leader-lines (re-skin of the mil-tech POSTER scaffold to the botanical register).

## LAYOUT 3 — The frame plate (P1 & P14)
The two pages that carry the small Mendel cameo. P1: the unexplained **3 : 1** sitting on the plate as a posed question, a hand/half-figure of Mendel at the edge. P14: the "DO IT YOURSELF" invitation — paper peas / coin to flip toward 3:1, Mendel's cameo closing the bridge to the biography. Slightly warmer, more "notebook-in-use" than the pure teaching plates.

## FOOTER STRIP — the concept-strip (every page)
A persistent thin strip showing **the grid growing up**: `factor → 2×2 → 3:1 → 1:2:1 → 4×4 → 9:3:3:1`, with the current page's stage **highlighted**. Rendered in the same pencil/cream register (NOT a modern progress bar in the image — the *reader* HTML has its own; this in-image strip is the botanical-plate version, like the KILL-CHAIN / IRON-TRIANGLE footer devices). Reinforces "one machine, built up."

## Palette discipline (repeat of style guide, load-bearing here)
- Base: cream paper, sepia ink, graphite grey, chalk white — **mostly bare paper**.
- Accents only where life/colour is the point: **pea-green** (leaves/vines/pods), **blossom-violet `#9176c4`** (the flower + reader accent), **gold/green** where the seed-colour trait is shown.
- The grid is **always** hand-ruled pencil/chalk, never a printed table.
