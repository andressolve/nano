## P2 — THE FACTOR (pairs, one from each parent)
**Stage:** factor
**Visual:** One pea plant in the centre. Two factor-tiles sit over it as a **pair**. Thin leader-lines: one tile labelled "from the mother plant," one "from the father plant." Below, a small diagram: parent → passes one tile → offspring.
**LETTERING:**
- Title: **EVERY PLANT CARRIES A PAIR**
- Caption: "Each plant carries its traits as a **pair of factors** — one inherited from each parent. (A factor is just one instruction for a trait; today we call one version an **allele**.)"
- Two tile-labels: **one from each parent**
- Caption: "So far the factors are a black box. We don't need to know what they're made of — only that they come in twos, and split one-to-each when the plant has offspring."

## P3 — DOMINANT & RECESSIVE (what shows vs what hides)
**Stage:** factor
**Visual:** A single plant carrying a **T** tile and a **t** tile (the pair). The plant is **tall**. Leader-lines: T → "the version that shows," t → "the version that hides, but is still carried." A faint ghosted short plant shows what t *would* look like alone.
**LETTERING:**
- Title: **ONE VERSION WINS — THE OTHER HIDES**
- Glossed labels: "**T** = the tall factor (capital). **t** = the short factor (small letter)."
- Caption: "When a plant carries both, it grows **tall**. The version that shows is **dominant**; the one that hides but is still carried is **recessive**. The short trait isn't gone — it's waiting."
**Footer strip:** `factor` highlighted.

## P4 — PURE LINES → ALL HYBRIDS (the vanishing trait)
**Stage:** 2×2 (building)
**Visual:** Left: a **pure tall** parent (two **T** tiles, **TT**) — a tall vine. Right: a **pure short** parent (two **t** tiles, **tt**) — a short bush. A painted arrow crosses them. Below: a **row of offspring, all tall**, each carrying one **T** and one **t** (**Tt**). The short trait has *vanished* from view.
**LETTERING:**
- Title: **CROSS PURE TALL × PURE SHORT**
- Parent labels: **TT (pure tall)** · **tt (pure short)**
- Offspring label: **all Tt — all tall**
- Caption: "Breed a pure-tall plant with a pure-short one and **every** child is tall. The short trait seems to disappear. It hasn't — each child quietly carries one **t**."
**Footer strip:** `2×2` highlighted.

## P7 — THE HIDDEN 1:2:1 (look inside the three)
**Stage:** 1:2:1
**Visual:** The same four cells from P6, but now sorted by **what they carry** (genotype), not just how they look. **TT** (1) · **Tt Tt** (2) · **tt** (1). A bracket groups the three tall ones to show two of them secretly carry a short factor.
**LETTERING:**

- The ratio (hero): **1 : 2 : 1** — "one **TT** : two **Tt** : one **tt**"
- Caption: "The simple 3 : 1 hides a tidier truth. Of the three tall plants, **two** secretly carry a short factor. The grid shows both stories at once."
**Footer strip:** `1:2:1` highlighted.

## P8 — PROBABILITY = COUNTING SQUARES (coin-flip re-enactment)
**Stage:** 3:1 (revisited)
**Visual:** Two coins / two-sided tokens, each side a tile (**T** / **t**), being "flipped." A small tally sheet pairs the outcomes: TT, Tt, tT, tt — mapping onto the four grid cells. The point: a Punnett square is just counting equally-likely flips.
**LETTERING:**
- Title: **IT'S JUST COUNTING THE CHANCES**
- Caption: "Each parent gives one factor at random — like flipping a coin for **T** or **t**. Two flips, four equally-likely results. That's exactly the four boxes."
- Tally label: "**T-T · T-t · t-T · t-t** → 3 tall : 1 short"
- Caption: "Probability here isn't hard — it's counting outcomes. Flip enough times and you drift toward 3 : 1 yourself."
**Footer strip:** `3:1` highlighted.

## P9 — THE TEST CROSS (TT or Tt? cross and count)
**Stage:** 2×2 (applied)
**Visual:** A **mystery tall plant** (is it TT or Tt? — a "?" tile beside a T). It's crossed with a **pure short** (**tt**) test plant. Two small grids show the two possible answers: if the mystery is **TT** → all offspring tall; if **Tt** → half tall, half short. The offspring tell you the hidden factor.
**LETTERING:**
- Title: **A TRICK TO READ THE HIDDEN FACTOR**
- Caption: "A tall plant might be **TT** or **Tt** — you can't tell by looking. So cross it with a pure-short **tt** plant and count the children."
- Two outcome labels: "**all tall → it was TT**" · "**half short → it was Tt**"
- Caption: "The hidden factor can't hide from the count. This is the **test cross**."
**Footer strip:** `2×2` highlighted.

- Caption: "Track two traits at once. Each pair of factors splits on its own — a seed's **shape** is decided independently of its **colour**. Any combination is possible."
