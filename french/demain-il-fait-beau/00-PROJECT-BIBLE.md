# DEMAIN, IL FAIT BEAU ! — two-page French comic

## Status

Approved by the user on 2026-08-13 for production.

## Purpose

Create one short, enjoyable two-page comic using French Starter language the
children have completed through Lesson 11. The story should feel like a real,
pleasant comic rather than a worksheet or vocabulary display.

## Reader promise

Rain interrupts Hugo and Léo's football plans, so they stop at a café. While
they have something warm, Hugo invites Léo to play the next day. Sunshine in
the final panel completes the plan.

The story is deliberately linear:

```text
rain → café stop → order → invitation → sunny next day
```

## Locked script

Two portrait pages. Each page has exactly four large panels in a clean 2×2
grid, read left-to-right and top-to-bottom.

### Page 1 — Rain and the café

#### Panel 1 — The rain

Outside in a contemporary neighborhood, Hugo and Léo have been heading to play
football when steady rain begins. They are dressed for wet weather. The plain
black-and-white football is already visible under Hugo's arm. Hugo looks up at
the rain.

HUGO: `Il pleut.`

#### Panel 2 — A warm stop

Inside a small welcoming neighborhood café. Hugo and Léo arrive at the counter
with damp coats; Hugo still carries the same football. A friendly café worker
stands behind the counter. Hugo orders.

HUGO: `Bonjour ! Je voudrais un chocolat chaud, s’il vous plaît.`

#### Panel 3 — The follow-up

The café worker looks toward both boys and makes a small open-hand gesture
toward the pastry display.

SERVER: `Et avec ça ?`

#### Panel 4 — The croissant

Léo indicates one clearly visible croissant in the unlabelled display. Hugo,
the server, and the football remain consistent with the preceding panels.

LÉO: `Un croissant, s’il vous plaît.`

### Page 2 — Tomorrow's plan

#### Panel 1 — Finish the order

At the same counter, the worker hands over one hot chocolate and one croissant.
Hugo politely closes the order. The football remains visible.

HUGO: `C’est tout, merci.`

#### Panel 2 — The invitation

Hugo and Léo sit together at a small café table. The hot chocolate and
croissant are on the table. The same football rests clearly beside Hugo's
chair. Hugo indicates the football and asks Léo about tomorrow.

HUGO: `Tu veux jouer demain ?`

#### Panel 3 — The answer

Reverse angle at the same table. Léo smiles and accepts. The food, drink, table,
clothes, and football remain coherent.

LÉO: `Oui, avec plaisir !`

#### Panel 4 — Sunshine

A clear next-day transition. Bright sun, blue sky, and dry ground in a local
park. Hugo and Léo are now actively playing football in lighter versions of
their established everyday clothing. Léo speaks while running toward Hugo.

LÉO: `Il fait beau !`

## Language lock

Every balloon comes directly from language taught in French Starter Lessons 8,
10, and 11:

- `Il pleut.`
- `Bonjour ! Je voudrais un chocolat chaud, s’il vous plaît.`
- `Et avec ça ?`
- `Un croissant, s’il vous plaît.`
- `C’est tout, merci.`
- `Tu veux jouer demain ?`
- `Oui, avec plaisir !`
- `Il fait beau !`

Do not add dialogue, captions, translations, labels, prices, menus, signs,
sound effects, or other visible text.

## Character locks

### Hugo

Ten years old; pale olive skin; tousled dark-brown hair; green overshirt over an
orange T-shirt; charcoal trousers; red canvas trainers. Curious, friendly, and
initially a little reserved. In the rain he wears a practical navy waterproof
jacket over the established outfit. The orange T-shirt may show at the collar.

### Léo

Eleven years old; deep brown skin; short tight black curls; mustard-yellow
hoodie; dark navy jeans; white trainers. Relaxed, welcoming, and naturally
expressive. In the rain he wears a practical teal waterproof shell over the
hoodie and dark rain boots. A little mustard yellow may show at the collar and
cuffs.

### Café worker

A friendly adult woman in her thirties with medium-brown skin, dark hair tied
in a low bun, a burgundy long-sleeve shirt, and a plain cream apron. Natural,
professional expression. She appears only in the café and has exactly one
balloon, on Page 1 Panel 3.

## Prop and setting locks

- One generic black-and-white football, first visible in Page 1 Panel 1 and
  continuous through Page 2 Panel 3.
- One small neighborhood café with warm ochre walls, a dark teal counter,
  simple wood furniture, and one unlabelled pastry display.
- One plain white cup of hot chocolate on a saucer and one croissant.
- No logos, brands, menu lettering, price labels, cash-register text, signs,
  jersey numbers, or decorative pseudo-writing.
- The next-day park is ordinary, green, and unmistakably dry and sunny.

## Visual register

Original all-ages Franco-Belgian clear-line adventure-comic illustration:
clean confident black contours, flat warm colors, precise readable shapes,
lively natural acting, and enough environmental detail to feel like a real
place. Match the established `Les jeux vidéo` page. The boys look ten and
eleven—not toddlers, mascots, or teenagers. Do not imitate an existing
copyrighted comic, character, artist, or page.

## Page production rules

- Generate a supplemental cast/prop/setting reference before either page.
- Generate each complete page as one cohesive image; do not assemble separately
  generated panels.
- Exactly four panels and four speech balloons per page.
- Exactly one visible speaker and one speech balloon in every panel.
- Each tail must terminate clearly at the intended speaker's mouth.
- Dialogue must be baked into the page image.
- No post-hoc HTML, SVG, canvas, or image-edit lettering.
- Approve Page 1 before generating Page 2.
- Page 2 must use Page 1 plus the minimum accepted references for continuity.
- After both pages pass individually, review the two pages uninterrupted before
  building the reader.

## Essentials gate

A page fails if it has wrong or missing text, ambiguous attribution, incorrect
panel order, identity drift, a broken football/food/weather transition, obvious
anatomy or generation damage, unreadable lettering at the actual reader size,
or extra visible text. Cosmetic differences that do not harm the story are
nonblocking.
