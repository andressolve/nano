## The health metric — why a page failed, not how often

Count REVISE verdicts by category:

- **Craft** — wrong for a reason a reader would feel: a dropped line, a
  mirrored room, a tail on the wrong mouth. The gate working. Cheap.
- **Gate** — wrong against something the critic *measured* or against a
  criterion that is not on the card. The gate eating the run. **A page that
  costs more than two generations for gate reasons is not a hard page; it is a
  broken gate.** Stop and look at the criterion.

**Failure modes the gates did not catch, or that cost time:**
- **A long multi-paragraph prose field drops a clause, the same clause, every time** (old p 7, four candidates, "was Third Brother,"). Restaging inside the page did not cure it; splitting the material into a dramatic page plus a two-paragraph prose page did, first candidate each. Watchlist.
- **Multi-view setting plates attached together produce a montage** (p 31: two plates, each with two or three studies, came back as four panels of different times of day, no prose). One plate plus an explicit one-continuous-scene instruction fixed it; p 35 was fixed pre-emptively. This is the Musketeers collage failure again, with plates instead of sheets → promoted to `method/07-FAILURE-MODES.md`.
- **Landscape canvas from the page builder** (old p 8, p 34): the builder did not delete and regenerate as its brief says; the driver stopped. Seen twice → promoted.
- **Model capacity errors** ("Selected model is at capacity") stopped the driver twice (p 23 critic, p 28 builder). Rerunning the same step by hand or after a pause cleared it.
- **The ceiling has no recorded per-page exception.** An owner-authorized edit beyond v4 (p 20) needed `book.toml` changed for one run and a rejected pair moved to an archive so `verify.py` would pass. Tools need a recorded exception (method note).
- **Reference locks that the generator cannot hold** (Liu Bei's long arms and lip tuft; Guan Yu's beard-bag view, used on no page): resolved by advisor rulings that kept separation in face, height, earlobes, and costume, and by replacing an unused state view with one the script needs. Packets written before the rulings had to be swept for stale lock wording.
- **Lowercase sentence start** inside a balloon (p 20, twice): a generator habit on a mid-balloon sentence. Watchlist.
