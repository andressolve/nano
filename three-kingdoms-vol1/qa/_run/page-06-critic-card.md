## Page intent

## PAGE 6

Spectacle. Morning in the walled peach garden in full pink-and-white bloom, petals falling through slanting light: the only soft colour in the book. Under the largest tree, three cups of wine and smoking incense on a small table; the three men kneel side by side, cups raised, faces solemn, even Zhang Fei's. Liu Bei speaks the oath for all three: to stand together, protect the people, serve the empire, and, not born on the same day, to die on the same day. The dominant turn is the oath itself. The garden and the three kneeling men are one image; nothing else competes. The reader turns because they have just promised to die together and now must live up to it. The table, incense and cups are small and plain.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 6 — card

### C1 — Transcription or ownership fails
The two oath lines must each read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order, both owned by Liu Bei's mouth, with no extra text; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing, a regional spelling variant.

### C2 — The oath does not land
One image owns the page as spectacle: three men kneeling side by side under blossoming peach trees, cups raised, solemn. If it reads as a party, a meal, or a crowd scene, or the blossom does not make it a different world from the dusty pages before, it fails.

### C3 — The order and identity of the three fail
Guan Yu left, Liu Bei centre and speaking, Zhang Fei right, each recognisable: red face and long beard; pale, broad-faced, long-eared, moustache only; broad bristling whiskers. A balloon hanging from either other man is blocking.

### C4 — The three cups
Three men, each with one cup raised; a missing cup, a fourth man, or a duplicated brother is blocking. Exact cup shape and the table's details are nonblocking.
