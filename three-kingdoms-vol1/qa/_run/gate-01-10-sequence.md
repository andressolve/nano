# Sequence gate — canonical pages 1–10

Fresh read-only reviewer with no inherited history. Do not open generation
prompts, builder audits, candidate folders, prior reports, or the full script.

1. Read `pages/page-01.png` through `pages/page-10.png` once in order
   at 600 × 900 (`pages/proofs/`), story material closed. Each page was
   approved on its own; that does not approve the run.
2. Record the uninterrupted causal and emotional sequence in plain language.
3. Only then open `qa/_run/batch-01-10-script.md` and compare.
4. Judge material story, exact text/attribution across transitions, named
   identity, consequential object and setting continuity across page turns,
   focal integrity, and page-turn failures. Also judge pacing across the
   batch: does any stretch read as compressed summary? Is there variation of
   page mode, or a wall of dramatic scenes? Does any consequential beat lack
   its aftermath?
5. Write `qa/batches/batch-01-10.md` and return `APPROVED` or `REVISE`.

Every `REVISE` finding names pages, the visible observation, the material
reader harm, and why a complete-page redraw is justified. Omit minor and
cosmetic observations. Never edit or promote.
