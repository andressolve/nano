## Page intent

## PAGE 7

Dramatic. Under the blossom, straight after the oath, the three rise from their knees beside the empty cups, and Zhang Fei counts on his thick fingers who is oldest: Liu Bei is Big Brother, Red Face is Second, and he, the youngest, is Third, which sounds terrible. Then Liu Bei bows to them both, moved and hungry at once, and names what brothers need, weapons, soldiers, money, against his twenty pairs of sandals; Guan Yu, stroking his beard, offers a horse and a cart. The dominant turn is Liu Bei's bow and the plain admission of how little they have. Zhang Fei answers it by throwing his arms wide toward his farmhouse: tomorrow he sells everything but the house and this garden. The reader turns because Zhang Fei has just given away all he owns. The table and cups stay small.

## PAGE 7 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the peach garden.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** Under the blossom, the three rising from their knees,
the cups empty on the little table. Zhang Fei, right, counting on his thick
fingers. Liu Bei, left, and Guan Yu, centre, watching him.

> **ZHANG FEI:** "Now, who's oldest? You are, Liu Bei. So you're Big Brother."
>
> **ZHANG FEI:** "Red Face is Second Brother. I'm the youngest, so I'm Third Brother. Third! It sounds terrible."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 45%.** Liu Bei, left, bowing to them both with folded
hands, moved and hungry at once. Guan Yu, right, stroking his beard.

> **LIU BEI:** "Brothers need weapons, and soldiers, and money. I have twenty pairs of sandals."
>
> **GUAN YU:** "And I have a horse and a cart."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Zhang Fei throwing his arms wide toward the farmhouse
behind the garden wall, grinning.

> **ZHANG FEI:** "And I have a shop, fields, and cattle. Tomorrow I sell all of it. Everything but the house and this garden."

**Why you turn the page:** Zhang Fei has just given away everything he owns, and now they can buy weapons.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 7 — card

### C1 — Transcription or ownership fails
The five lines must each read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in causal order, each owned by the right mouth by tail and placement, with no extra text; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant. A line placed directly under its speaker's previous balloon or naming its addressee is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — The turn does not land
One panel must own the page by eye: Liu Bei bowing, moved and hungry, setting what brothers need against his twenty pairs of sandals. The page must end on Zhang Fei throwing his arms wide toward his farmhouse, so his giving everything away reads as the answer.

### C3 — Attribution across the exchanges
Zhang Fei owns both counting lines and the closing offer; Liu Bei owns the weapons and sandals line; Guan Yu owns the horse and cart. Each must hang from its own speaker's mouth by tail, and Guan Yu's reply must read after Liu Bei's line.

### C4 — Identity of the three fails
Liu Bei pale, broad-faced, long-eared, moustache only, in hemp; Guan Yu red-faced with a long loose beard in green, no armor; Zhang Fei broad and whiskered in a butcher's apron. Brothers confused or duplicated is blocking.

### C5 — Focal generation integrity
Garbled speaking faces, or Zhang Fei's wide-flung arms with extra or missing hands, are blocking. Blossom detail and the cups' exact shape are not.
