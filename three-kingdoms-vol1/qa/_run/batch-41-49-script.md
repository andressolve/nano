# Exact script — pages 41–49

Open only after the blind sequence read.

## PAGE 41 · *spectacle*

**Location:** the plain before Hulao Gate.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 100%.** Zhang Fei and Lü Bu at the centre of the
plain, the snake spear and the halberd locked, the black horse and the red
horse rearing chest to chest, dust boiling up around them. Far off on both
sides, two whole armies watching, motionless.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left)*
> "Fifty times they came together, and neither gave a single step."

**Why you turn the page:** fifty times, and Zhang Fei is tiring.

---

## PAGE 42 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the plain before Hulao Gate.

**Panel 1 — 20%.** Zhang Fei, right, soaked in sweat, his arms heavy, his
horse staggering. Lü Bu, left, laughing, not tired at all.

> **LÜ BU:** "Is that all a butcher has?"

**Panel 2 — 25%.** Guan Yu galloping in from the left, the Green Dragon
blade swinging up, his beard flying.

> **GUAN YU:** "Third Brother! I am here!"

**Panel 3 — dominant, 40%.** Two against one: Guan Yu and Zhang Fei on
either side of Lü Bu, blade and spear coming in together, and Lü Bu
catching both on the shaft of his halberd, still laughing.

> **LÜ BU:** "Two of you? Good. Now it's almost a fight."

> **CAPTION** *(lower left)*
> "Thirty more times. And still Lü Bu did not give a step."

**Panel 4 — 15%.** Far off on the hill under the gold banner, Liu Bei on his
horse, gripping the reins, not moving.

**Why you turn the page:** even two of the brothers can't beat him, and Liu Bei is still on the hill.

---

## PAGE 43 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the hill under the gold banner.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** On the hill, Liu Bei on his horse, gripping the reins,
watching. Beside him, Yuan Shao, hatless and muddy, recovered his seat.

> **YUAN SHAO:** "Your butcher had no business getting in Lü Bu's way. Now your brothers will die down there."
>
> **YUAN SHAO:** "You stand under my banner, sandal-seller, so you take my orders. Stay back, or you will die with them."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 50%.** Liu Bei drawing his two swords, one in each
hand, crossing out of their sheaths; his face fiercer than we have ever
seen it.

> **LIU BEI:** "My brothers have fought eighty times while I sat here. In a peach garden, we swore to die on the same day."
>
> **LIU BEI:** "If today is that day, I will not spend it standing on a hill."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Liu Bei galloping down the slope toward the fight, both
swords out, his white cloak flying. Yuan Shao staring after him.

**Why you turn the page:** the worst fighter of the three is riding at Lü Bu.

---

## PAGE 44 · *spectacle*

**Location:** the plain before Hulao Gate.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 100%.** Seen from above: the three brothers circling
Lü Bu like the spokes of a turning lantern, green blade, snake spear, and two
swords coming at him from three sides at once, Red Hare rearing at the
centre, Lü Bu twisting in the saddle. Dust in a ring around them. Both
armies watching from the edges of the page.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left)*
> "For the first time in his life, Lü Bu could not see all of his enemies at once."

**Why you turn the page:** Lü Bu is surrounded, and something has to break.

---

## PAGE 45 · *dramatic*

**Location:** two — the plain, and the doors of Hulao Gate.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Lü Bu feinting a thrust straight at Liu Bei's face; Liu
Bei jerking back; a gap opening in the ring.

**Panel 2 — 25%.** Lü Bu wheeling Red Hare out through the gap and
galloping for the gate. Zhang Fei, spurring after him.

> **ZHANG FEI:** "He's running! Lü Bu is running away from us!"

**Panel 3 — dominant, 50%.** The great doors of Hulao Gate slamming shut
behind Red Hare's tail just as the three brothers reach them. Arrows coming
down from the wall. Zhang Fei pounding on the doors with the butt of his
spear; Guan Yu pulling at his arm; Liu Bei reining in, staring up.

> **SOUND** *(across the doors)*
> "BOOM"
>
> **GUAN YU:** "Third Brother, the arrows! Get back from the gate!"

**Why you turn the page:** Lü Bu ran, and now he's behind the gate.

## PAGE 46 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the foot of Hulao Gate.

**Panel 1 — 20%.** Zhang Fei, out of arrow range, shaking his spear up at
the wall.

> **ZHANG FEI:** "Come back out, you three-father slave! We're not finished!"

**Panel 2 — dominant, 45%.** Looking up the grey wall: Lü Bu leaning on the
battlements high above, breathing hard, his plumes against the sky, looking
down.

> **LÜ BU:** "Who are you? The three of you. Who are you?"

**Panel 3 — 20%.** The three brothers below, side by side, looking up.

> **LIU BEI:** "I am Liu Bei. This is Guan Yu. This is Zhang Fei. We are brothers."

**Panel 4 — 15%.** Lü Bu on the wall, and Guan Yu below, stroking his
beard.

> **LÜ BU:** "Next time, come at me one at a time."
>
> **GUAN YU:** "No."

**Why you turn the page:** Lü Bu knows their names now, and the day is ending.

---

## PAGE 47 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the brothers' fire, at dusk.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** Dusk at the edge of the camp. Guan Yu and Zhang Fei a
little apart by the fire, Zhang Fei eating. Cao Cao, alone, sitting down
beside Liu Bei and pouring two cups of wine. Liu Bei does not take his.

> **LIU BEI:** "You asked us to stand with you when Lü Bu came, Lord Cao. Where were you?"
>
> **CAO CAO:** "On my hill, watching. I wanted to see what the three of you would do. Now I know."

**Panel 2 — 20%.** Cao Cao, amused, nodding toward the great gold banner
across the camp.

> **CAO CAO:** "Yuan Shao is telling the camp that his guards drove Lü Bu off. Yuan Shao has ancestors. Ancestors don't fight Lü Bu."

**Panel 3 — dominant, 35%.** Cao Cao leaning close, smiling, his cup raised,
his narrow eyes glittering in the firelight.

> **CAO CAO:** "There are two heroes in this camp. You, Liu Bei. And me."

**Panel 4 — 15%.** Liu Bei, who would not take the cup, is holding it now,
looking down into it. Cao Cao standing to go, laughing.

> **LIU BEI:** "I am only a man who sells sandals, Lord Cao."
>
> **CAO CAO:** "Of course you are."

**Why you turn the page:** Cao Cao watched them nearly die and called it a test, and he has just said out loud the thing Liu Bei wants most.

## PAGE 48 · *illustrated prose*

**Location:** the edge of the camp, at night.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 70%.** Moonlight. Liu Bei standing alone at the edge
of the camp, his back to us, looking at the dark shape of Hulao Gate filling
the pass. Behind him, by the embers of a fire, Guan Yu and Zhang Fei asleep,
Zhang Fei with his mouth open.

> **PROSE FIELD 1** *(across the top, over the night sky)*
> "Liu Bei could not sleep. He walked out past his sleeping brothers and looked at Hulao Gate in the moonlight.
>
> Behind it were the fat man who had turned his back on him, the warrior who had sold his father for a horse, and a boy of nine on a throne. In front of it were eighteen lords who would not fight, and one who poured wine and might be anything.
>
> He thought about the moment the halberd came at his face, and how he had flinched, and how Lü Bu had gone through the gap he left.
>
> He thought about the peach garden. He thought about what Cao Cao had said, and he was ashamed of how much he wanted it to be true."

**Why you turn the page:** Liu Bei wants the empire, and the gate is still shut.

---

## PAGE 49 · *spectacle*

**Location:** before Hulao Gate, at dawn.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 100%.** Cold blue dawn. The three brothers on
horseback side by side in front of the shut gate: Guan Yu with the Green
Dragon blade, Liu Bei in the middle with his two swords at his back, Zhang
Fei with the snake spear. High on the battlements, small against the sky,
Lü Bu's two pheasant plumes. Behind the brothers, on a ridge in the near
corner, large and clear, Cao Cao on his horse under his black-and-indigo
banners, watching them. Liu Bei faces the gate; he does not look back.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left, over the sky)*
> "At dawn the three brothers rode to the gate to call Lü Bu out again. Behind them on the ridge, Cao Cao was watching. Liu Bei knew it. He did not turn around."

> **TITLE CARD** *(lower edge, small)*
> "END OF VOLUME ONE"

**Why you turn the page:** (the book ends) Liu Bei has chosen his brothers over Cao Cao's praise for today, and the man on the ridge is still watching.
