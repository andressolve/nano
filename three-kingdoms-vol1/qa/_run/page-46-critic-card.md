## Page intent

## PAGE 46

Dramatic, two places: the plain, then the doors of Hulao Gate. Lü Bu feints a thrust straight at Liu Bei's face; Liu Bei jerks back, and a gap opens in the ring. Lü Bu wheels Red Hare out through it and gallops for the gate while Zhang Fei spurs after him, shouting that Lü Bu is running away from them. The dominant turn is the large final panel: the great doors slamming shut behind Red Hare's tail just as the brothers reach them, arrows coming down from the wall, Zhang Fei pounding the doors with the butt of his spear, Guan Yu pulling at his arm and telling him to get back, Liu Bei reined in, staring up at the wall. The feint, the flinch, and the slam are the turn. The reader turns because Lü Bu ran, and now he is behind the gate.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 46 — card

### C1 — Transcription
Every listed string reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in causal order, owned by the right mouth by tail and placement, the sound cue across the doors, with no extra text; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is never measured. Nonblocking: curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing, a missing diaeresis on Lü, the run length of the repeated letter in the sound cue; the words and their order must match.

### C2 — Feint, flinch, slam
The reader must see Lü Bu's thrust make Liu Bei jerk back, Lü Bu breaking away for the gate, and the doors closing behind Red Hare's tail. The slamming doors own the page. If the flinch or the escape cannot be followed, blocking.

### C3 — Attribution
"He's running!…" hangs from Zhang Fei; "Third Brother, the arrows!…" hangs from Guan Yu, who is pulling Zhang Fei back. Either line readable as another's is blocking.

### C4 — Identity
Zhang Fei pounding the doors; Guan Yu red-faced and bearded; Liu Bei reined in with both swords drawn; Lü Bu, plumed on the red horse, the one escaping. A swap of roles is blocking.

### C5 — No death drawn
Arrows fall but strike no one; no blood or wound. Arrow count and wall detail are nonblocking.
