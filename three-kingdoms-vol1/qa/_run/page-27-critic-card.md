## Page intent

## PAGE 27

Dramatic. Night. Cao Cao lies awake on a mat; through the thin wall comes lamplight and the sound of a knife on a whetstone, and a whisper about tying the legs and one quick cut. He hears "tie," "cut," and "he," and does not wait. His drawn sword comes through a lamplit doorway toward an old woman and a young man still holding the knife he was sharpening, startled and alive, and the panel ends there; nothing of the killing is shown. The page's one dominant turn is the dawn: a small empty kitchen, a live pig tied by its legs on the table, a cleaver and the whetstone beside it, and Cao Cao in the doorway with his sword lowered, looking at it. That still image owns the page; the night is its approach. The reader turns because Cao Cao has killed a whole family for nothing, and the old man is still out buying wine.

### C1 — Transcription
Every scripted string reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order, with no extra text; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing, and a regional spelling variant are nonblocking; the words and their order must match. The run length of a repeated letter in the sound cue is nonblocking. Captions carry no tails.

### C2 — The pig owns the page
The dawn kitchen must land by eye as the page's centre: a live pig tied by its legs on the table, cleaver and whetstone beside it, nobody else, Cao Cao in the doorway, sword lowered. If the pig is not plainly alive and tied, the reader cannot see what was meant. Dramatic mode.

### C3 — The killing is not shown
The doorway panel ends with the old woman and young man startled and alive. Any blood, wound, body, blow, or bloody blade anywhere on the page changes the page's event and is blocking.

### C4 — The causal chain
The reader must follow: awake, the sound through the wall, his hand on the sword, the doorway, the kitchen. The young man holds a knife in the doorway panel. A missing link that leaves the reader unable to tell what happened is blocking.

### C5 — Identity
Cao Cao short, angular, goateed, in a travel-stained robe; the family plain, in brown, never carrying a balloon.
