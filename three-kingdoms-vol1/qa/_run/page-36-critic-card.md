## Page intent

## PAGE 36

Dramatic. The page's one turn: the helmet, the wine still warm, and the three nobodies thrown out anyway. The drums stop; Guan Yu ducks in through the flap and sets a dented plumed helmet on the table before Yuan Shao. The central image owns the page, close and quiet: Guan Yu lifting the cup, steam still rising, and Cao Cao beside him, delighted that it is still warm. Then the noise: Zhang Fei roars along the table that his brother killed the champion and they should take the whole gate; Yuan Shao, white with anger, orders the three nobodies thrown out; Cao Cao shrugs that a victory is a victory. The reader turns because Guan Yu won and they are being thrown out regardless.

### C1 — Transcription
All seven strings read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in causal order, each owned by the right mouth by tail and placement, with no extra text; a string that does not read is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, and a regional spelling variant of the same word are nonblocking. A line owned by placement, or naming its addressee ("Lord Yuan"), is owned even if its tail wanders. The helmet seen in a second panel is continuity, not a doubled object.

### C2 — Still warm owns the page
The image of Guan Yu lifting the still-steaming cup with Cao Cao delighted beside him is what the eye finds as the page's heart; the page is dramatic. Judge by eye, never by area.

### C3 — Attribution
The helmet line hangs from Guan Yu; "still warm" from Cao Cao, not Guan Yu; both roars from Zhang Fei; the order to throw them out from Yuan Shao; the shrug line from Cao Cao, below Yuan Shao's. A swapped line is blocking.

### C4 — The helmet
A dented plumed helmet set on the table before Yuan Shao. Blood, a wound, or anything severed is blocking; long pheasant plumes like Lü Bu's that make the reader think Lü Bu is dead are blocking.

### C5 — Identity
Guan Yu red-faced, beard to the belt, green; Cao Cao short, goatee, indigo; Yuan Shao thin, three-strand beard, tall black hat, ivory; Zhang Fei broad, bristling whiskers, black, never fat or old.
