## Page intent

## PAGE 24

Dramatic. The dagger comes out and rises over Dong Zhuo's back, and in the bronze mirror the reader sees what Cao Cao does not: the tyrant's one open eye, and the blade. Dong Zhuo rolls over roaring, Lü Bu's shadow already in the doorway. The page's one dominant turn is the bluff: Cao Cao is on both knees before anyone can move, the dagger lying flat across his two open palms like a present, smiling, offering it as a gift. Dong Zhuo, greedy, turns it in his fat fingers and admires the jewels, calling Lü Bu "my son," and Cao Cao, rising and bowing, asks to try the new horse once around the courtyard. The kneeling offer owns the page; the mirror and the roar are its fuse. The reader turns because Cao Cao has lied his way out and is about to be on a fast horse.

## Exact owner-controlled script

## PAGE 24 · *dramatic*

**Location:** Dong Zhuo's bedchamber.

**Panel 1 — 20%.** The dagger out and raised above Dong Zhuo's back. In the
bronze mirror: Dong Zhuo's one open eye, and the blade.

**Panel 2 — 20%.** Dong Zhuo rolling over, roaring. Lü Bu's shadow falling
across the doorway.

> **DONG ZHUO:** "Cao Cao! What are you doing?"

**Panel 3 — dominant, 40%.** Cao Cao already on both knees, the dagger lying
flat across his two open palms, held up like a present, smiling. Lü Bu in the
doorway behind him.

> **CAO CAO:** "My lord, I have a precious dagger, the finest blade in the capital. I wanted to give it to you with my own hands."

**Panel 4 — 20%.** Dong Zhuo turning the dagger in his fat fingers,
admiring the seven jewels. Cao Cao rising, bowing.

> **DONG ZHUO:** "Seven jewels! Look at this, my son!"
>
> **CAO CAO:** "May I try the horse your son chose for me? Just once around the courtyard."

**Why you turn the page:** Cao Cao lied his way out with the blade in his hands, and now he's on a fast horse.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 24 — card

### C1 — Transcription
Every scripted string reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in causal order, owned by the right mouth by tail and placement, with no extra text; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, and a regional spelling variant are nonblocking; the words and their order must match. A line owned by placement or by naming its addressee is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — The kneeling gift owns the page
Cao Cao on his knees with the dagger flat across both open palms, smiling, must be what the eye finds as the page's centre. If he holds it like a weapon, or the moment is lost among equal panels, the bluff does not land. Dramatic mode.

### C3 — The mirror
The mirror must show Dong Zhuo's open eye and the blade above his back, so the reader understands how he was caught. If the reader cannot see why Dong Zhuo wakes, the causal chain breaks.

### C4 — Attribution
The roar and "Seven jewels!" are Dong Zhuo's; the gift speech and the request to try the horse are Cao Cao's, the request reading after "Seven jewels!". Lü Bu is silent.

### C5 — Identity and integrity
Dong Zhuo obese in purple and gold; Cao Cao short, angular, goateed, in indigo; Lü Bu tall, beardless, plumed. The offering hands are two, open, coherent. No one is cut; any blood is blocking. The jewel count is nonblocking.
