## Page intent

## PAGE 37

Dramatic. The page's one turn: Cao Cao warns that Lü Bu comes tomorrow and asks for the brothers, and Liu Bei will not say yes. The opening image owns the page: the brothers' small tent at night, one lamp, Cao Cao crouched alone with the three of them over a wine jar and a basket of roast meat, his face serious for once, telling them who Lü Bu is and what he did for a red horse. He leans closer and asks Liu Bei to stand with him, not with Yuan Shao; Liu Bei answers that they will fight beside anyone who fights Dong Zhuo. Cao Cao gone, the flap still swinging, Zhang Fei punches his palm and Guan Yu judges the man who went on bowing. The reader turns because Lü Bu comes tomorrow and Liu Bei gave Cao Cao no answer.

## Exact owner-controlled script

## PAGE 37 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the brothers' tent, at night.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 45%.** The brothers' small tent, one lamp. Cao Cao,
alone, crouched by the three of them, a wine jar and a basket of roast meat
set down between them, his face serious for once.

> **CAO CAO:** "Tomorrow they'll send out Lü Bu. He kneels to Dong Zhuo now, and calls that fat man Father."
>
> **CAO CAO:** "I was at court when he killed the man who raised him, for a red horse. Nobody here will last three blows."

**Panel 2 — 25%.** Cao Cao leaning closer to Liu Bei.

> **CAO CAO:** "When he comes, I want you three with me. Not with Yuan Shao."
>
> **LIU BEI:** "We came to fight Dong Zhuo, Lord Cao. We will fight beside anyone who does."

**Panel 3 — 30%.** Cao Cao gone, the tent flap still swinging behind them.
Zhang Fei, left, turned to his brothers, slamming his fist into his palm;
Guan Yu, right, stroking his beard, looking at the flap.

> **ZHANG FEI:** "Lü Bu killed his own father for a horse? Tomorrow I'll call him that to his face, in front of both armies."
>
> **GUAN YU:** "Cao Cao watched a son kill his father, and he went on bowing."

**Why you turn the page:** Lü Bu is coming tomorrow, and Cao Cao asked for an answer Liu Bei didn't give.

### C1 — Transcription
All six strings read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in causal order, each owned by the right mouth by tail and placement, with no extra text; a string that does not read is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, and a regional spelling variant of the same word are nonblocking. ### C2 — Cao Cao's warning owns the page
The lamplit tent with Cao Cao crouched alone among the three brothers over the wine and meat is what the eye finds first; the page is dramatic. Judge by eye, never by area.

### C3 — The ask and the non-answer
Cao Cao's three lines hang from Cao Cao; Liu Bei's reply hangs from Liu Bei and reads after the ask. If Liu Bei's line reads as Cao Cao's, or the ask as Liu Bei's, the turn is lost; blocking.

### C4 — After he goes
In the last panel Cao Cao is gone and the flap swings; the horse line hangs from Zhang Fei and the bowing line from Guan Yu. Cao Cao visible in this panel, or a swapped pair, is blocking.
