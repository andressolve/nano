# Exact script — pages 1–10

Open only after the blind sequence read.

## PAGE 1 · *spectacle*

**Location:** the burning empire.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 100%.** Loess hills under a smoke-brown sky. A walled
town burning on a hill, orange flame and black smoke. Down the road toward
the reader, filling the lower half of the page edge to edge, a river of men
with yellow cloths tied around their heads, spears and hoes and torches held
high, mouths open, singing. Nobody's face is close. In the lower corner, a
farmer's cart overturned at the roadside, its owner gone.

> **TITLE CARD** *(upper third, over the smoke)*
> "THREE KINGDOMS
>
> The Peach Garden Oath"

> **CAPTION** *(lower left, over the dust of the road)*
> "The Han emperors had ruled China for four hundred years. Now the land was starving, and the Yellow Turbans, an army in yellow headbands, were on the march."

**Why you turn the page:** the empire is on fire, and somebody is going to have to stand in front of that road.

---

## PAGE 2 · *illustrated prose*

**Location:** the market of Zhuo County.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 65%.** A crowded dusty market street under a grey tile
gate. In the shade of a wall, a small stall hung with straw sandals. Behind
it, LIU BEI: tall and slim, a faded hemp robe patched at the elbows, a plain
cloth cap, long earlobes, a thin moustache. He is not selling. He is standing
very still, looking at a pale paper notice pasted on the wall beside his
stall, with a red seal at its foot. Shoppers pass behind him without looking.

> **NOTICE** *(the paper on the wall, large, plain)*
> "VOLUNTEERS WANTED
> to fight the Yellow Turbans"

**Panel 2 — 35%.** Close on his long hands, a half-woven sandal in them,
gone still.

> **PROSE FIELD 1** *(across the top of the page, over the pale sky above the gate)*
> "In Zhuo County, far in the north, a man named Liu Bei sold straw sandals in the market. He was twenty-eight, tall and quiet, with long arms and ears so long that strangers stared.
>
> Three hundred years ago his family had been princes, the sons of emperors. Now Liu Bei wove sandals, and sold them, and bowed to everyone who bought a pair.
>
> On the morning the notice went up, he read it eleven times."

**Why you turn the page:** a prince's grandson is standing in a market wanting something badly, and he hasn't moved.

## PAGE 3 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the market of Zhuo County.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 50%.** LIU BEI, left, in front of the notice, his
shoulders dropping in a long sigh. Behind him, right, looming over him, ZHANG
FEI: short and enormously broad, a leather butcher's apron, sleeves rolled
over arms like hams, a square head, round bulging eyes, black whiskers
bristling all round his jaw. Arms folded. Grinning.

> **ZHANG FEI:** "A grown man, sighing at a wall! If you want to fight the rebels, why don't you go and do something about it?"

**Panel 2 — 25%.** Liu Bei turned to face him, embarrassed, his hands
folded into his sleeves.

> **LIU BEI:** "I want to. I have the blood of princes and a stall full of sandals. What would I fight them with?"

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Zhang Fei thumping his own barrel chest, then throwing
one huge arm round Liu Bei's shoulders and steering him off toward a tavern
sign.

> **ZHANG FEI:** "I'm Zhang Fei. I have a butcher's shop, a farm, and more money than sense. Come and drink with me, Prince of Sandals!"
>
> **LIU BEI:** "My name is Liu Bei."

**Why you turn the page:** a stranger with money just grabbed him, and they're going to talk about war.

---

## PAGE 4 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the tavern.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 45%.** A low-beamed tavern, benches, a wine jar,
lamplight. In the doorway, ducking under the lintel, GUAN YU: a head taller
than anyone in the room, a deep red face, long narrow eyes tilted up at the
corners, a glossy black beard falling in one sheet to his belt, a green
headscarf. He has slammed one fist on the nearest table. Liu Bei and Zhang
Fei at a table in the foreground, turning to look.

> **GUAN YU:** "Wine, and quickly! I'm going to enlist before the day is over, and I won't do it thirsty."

**Panel 2 — 25%.** Zhang Fei, left, delighted, waving him over. Guan Yu,
right, sitting down heavily at their table.

> **ZHANG FEI:** "Now that is a soldier! Hey, Red Face! Sit with us. We're enlisting too."
>
> **GUAN YU:** "My name is Guan Yu. In my village a rich man beat the poor and took their land."

**Panel 3 — 30%.** The three at one table. Guan Yu, right, stroking his
beard downward with one hand. Liu Bei, left, leaning in.

> **GUAN YU:** "I killed him. I've been running for five years."
>
> **LIU BEI:** "And now you'll fight for the emperor whose soldiers have been chasing you?"
>
> **GUAN YU:** "The rebels burn villages like mine. I'll fight anyone who does that."

**Why you turn the page:** three men who have nothing in common are at one table, and none of them is leaving.

---

## PAGE 5 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the courtyard of Zhang Fei's farm, at night.

**Panel 1 — 35%.** A rammed-earth courtyard, a fire, a wine jug. Zhang Fei
sprawled laughing, right. Guan Yu upright, centre. Liu Bei, left, staring
into the flames.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left)*
> "They talked all night, at Zhang Fei's farm."
>
> **LIU BEI:** "You two could join any army in the empire. You don't need me. Why would anyone follow a man who sells sandals?"

**Panel 2 — 25%.** Guan Yu, stroking his beard. Zhang Fei holding up
fingers.

> **GUAN YU:** "I did not come for your ancestors, the princes. I came for the man who sighed at that wall."
>
> **ZHANG FEI:** "He read it eleven times. I counted!"

**Panel 3 — dominant, 40%.** Zhang Fei on his feet, pointing past the house
into the dark, where pale shapes of blossoming trees show over a wall. Liu
Bei looking up at him, his face lit and hungry.

> **ZHANG FEI:** "Behind my house is a peach garden, and every tree is in bloom."
>
> **ZHANG FEI:** "Tomorrow morning we go out there and swear to be brothers. Real brothers!"
>
> **LIU BEI:** "Then tomorrow we will be brothers."

**Why you turn the page:** tomorrow, in a garden, three strangers are going to swear.

---

## PAGE 6 · *spectacle*

**Location:** the peach garden.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 100%.** Morning in a walled garden of old peach trees in
full pink-and-white bloom, petals falling through slanting light. Under the
largest tree, a small table with incense smoking and three cups of wine. The
three kneel side by side facing the table, cups raised: GUAN YU left, LIU BEI
centre, ZHANG FEI right. Their faces are solemn; even Zhang Fei's. The only
soft colour in the whole book. Liu Bei speaks the oath for all three.

> **LIU BEI:** "We swear to stand together, to protect the people, and to serve the empire."
>
> **LIU BEI:** "We were not born on the same day. But we swear to die on the same day."

**Why you turn the page:** they have just promised to die together, and now they have to live up to it.

---

## PAGE 7 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the peach garden.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** Under the blossom, the three rising from their knees,
the cups empty on the little table. Zhang Fei, right, counting on his thick
fingers. Liu Bei, left, and Guan Yu, centre, watching him.

> **ZHANG FEI:** "Now, who's oldest? You are, Liu Bei. So you're Big Brother."
>
> **ZHANG FEI:** "Red Face is Second Brother. I'm the youngest, so I'm Third Brother. Third! It sounds terrible."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 45%.** Liu Bei, left, bowing to them both with folded
hands, moved and hungry at once. Guan Yu, right, stroking his beard.

> **LIU BEI:** "Brothers need weapons, and soldiers, and money. I have twenty pairs of sandals."
>
> **GUAN YU:** "And I have a horse and a cart."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Zhang Fei throwing his arms wide toward the farmhouse
behind the garden wall, grinning.

> **ZHANG FEI:** "And I have a shop, fields, and cattle. Tomorrow I sell all of it. Everything but the house and this garden."

**Why you turn the page:** Zhang Fei has just given away everything he owns, and now they can buy weapons.

## PAGE 8 · *illustrated prose*

**Location:** two — the smith's forge, and the peach garden full of men.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 60%.** A smith's forge, red heat, sparks. Laid across
the anvil and a rack: two matching straight swords in black scabbards; a
spear as long as two men with a wavy blade like a snake; and a great curved
blade on a long pole with a dragon worked at its base. Guan Yu lifting the
curved blade in one hand while the smith gapes.

**Panel 2 — 40%.** The peach garden, now in leaf, packed with farmers
holding hoes and pitchforks; the three brothers before them.

> **PROSE FIELD 1** *(across the top, over the dark rafters, never over the weapons)*
> "With the money, the smith made a weapon for each brother. For Liu Bei, two matching swords. For Zhang Fei, a spear as long as two men, with a wavy blade like a snake. For Guan Yu, a great curved blade on a long pole, so heavy two men could hardly lift it. He called it the Green Dragon.
>
> Five hundred farmers came to the peach garden with hoes and pitchforks. In a month they had spears."

**Why you turn the page:** three brothers and five hundred farmers are going to war.

---

## PAGE 9 · *dramatic*

**Location:** a ridge above the rebel army.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 50%.** A bare ridge. The three brothers on horseback in
front, five hundred farmers with spears behind them. Below, a dust-yellow
plain filled to the horizon with yellow headbands. Between the ridge and the
plain, a narrow valley with a scrubby hill on either side.

> **ZHANG FEI:** "Fifty thousand of them and five hundred of us. Good! That's only a hundred each."

**Panel 2 — 20%.** Guan Yu, glancing back at the farmers, who are pale and
shaking.

> **GUAN YU:** "The farmers are shaking, Third Brother. They can count too."

**Panel 3 — 30%.** Liu Bei, centre, pointing down at the two hills. Zhang
Fei, right, staring at him.

> **LIU BEI:** "Then we won't fight fifty thousand. Second Brother, hide behind the left hill. Third Brother, behind the right."
>
> **LIU BEI:** "I'll ride out with the farmers and let them chase us."
>
> **ZHANG FEI:** "You? You're the worst fighter of the three of us!"
>
> **LIU BEI:** "That's why they'll chase me. When they're between the hills, come down."

**Why you turn the page:** the plan is that Liu Bei runs from fifty thousand men.

## PAGE 10 · *spectacle*

**Location:** the valley between the two hills.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 75%.** Down the valley toward the reader: LIU BEI in
front on his horse, the farmers running behind him, and behind them the
yellow army pouring into the valley after them. From the left hill, GUAN YU
crashes down in green with the Green Dragon blade raised; from the right,
ZHANG FEI in black with his snake spear, men streaming behind each of them.
The yellow army is caught between two walls.

> **ZHANG FEI:** "Here I am! Who wants to be first?"

**Panel 2 — 25%.** A rebel captain in a yellow headband on a brown horse,
going over backward out of the bottom of the frame as Zhang Fei's spear
drives past. His men behind him already turning to run.

**Why you turn the page:** five hundred farmers just broke fifty thousand, and people are going to hear about it.
