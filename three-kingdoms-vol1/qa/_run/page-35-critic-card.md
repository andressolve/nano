### C1 — Transcription
The caption and the sound cue read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once each, with no other text; a string that does not read is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured. The run length of a repeated letter in the sound cue, ellipsis against three dots, spacing around punctuation, and curly against straight quotes are nonblocking; the words and their order must match.

### C2 — Spectacle, one image
One image is the page: the steaming cup near, the frozen lords, and through the open flap the distant duel. Panels, balloons, or a close-up of the fight are blocking.

### C3 — The duel swallowed in dust
Two tiny riders, one green and one dark, meet in a wall of dust and no result can be seen. A visible winner, a fallen rider, blood, or a helmet anywhere is blocking: the next page's reveal depends on it.

### C4 — The cup is still steaming
The cup in the foreground plainly steams. If the cup cannot be found or is not steaming, the reason to turn is lost; blocking.

### C5 — The brothers who stayed
