## Page intent

## PAGE 20

Dramatic. In a stable at night, by one lantern, Lü Bu is polishing his halberd when Dong Zhuo leads in a huge blood-red horse, Red Hare, and offers it to him. Lü Bu's cold face changes when his hand touches the horse's neck: he wants it, and that wanting is the page's centre. Dong Zhuo, close and smiling, a fat hand on his shoulder, goes after the old man who raised him, and Lü Bu says he has a father; Dong Zhuo answers, "Do you?" and offers gold armor to match the horse. The dominant turn: Red Hare; "I have a father." "Do you?" The reader turns because Lü Bu has been offered a horse for his father and has not said no. The stable and the lantern stay subordinate.

### C1 — Transcription or ownership fails
Any of the five strings is missing, altered, duplicated, reordered, or not comfortably transcribable from the 600 × 900 proof, or extra text appears. A string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word; the words and their order must match. A line placed directly under its speaker's previous balloon or naming its addressee is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — The turn does not land
The eye does not find Lü Bu's hand on the red horse and his face wanting it as the centre of the page, or the page does not end on Dong Zhuo's hand on his shoulder answering "I have a father."

Nonblocking: Dong Zhuo's shoulder contact occurs in the immediately preceding "He's yours…" beat rather than the final "Do you?" beat, provided the closing exchange still reads as one continuous, close, invasive attempt to replace Lü Bu's father. Exact contact timing alone does not justify risking an otherwise successful full page.

### C3 — Attribution fails
"This is Red Hare…", "He's yours…" and "Do you? A real father…" must hang from Dong Zhuo's mouth; "I have never seen a horse like this." and "He raised me. I have a father." from Lü Bu's, each by tail. A reply readable before its prompt, or any balloon from the horse, blocks.

### C4 — Identity or the horse fails
Lü Bu does not read as the lean, clean-shaven, plumed warrior; Dong Zhuo not as the obese old man in purple and gold; or the horse is not a huge blood-red horse with a darker mane. Nonblocking: tack, the halberd's exact form, the lantern.

### C5 — Consequential generation failure
A duplicated figure or horse, gross anatomy on the focal hand on the neck or the hand on the shoulder, corruption, or text collision. Background texture does not block.
