## Page intent

## PAGE 21

Dramatic. Morning in the throne hall. Lü Bu strides up the black floor, kneels before Dong Zhuo, and lays down a small bronze tiger seal, the old man's seal, while Red Hare waits through the great doors behind him. He says the old man will never say no to anyone again, and he calls Dong Zhuo Father. Dong Zhuo, delighted, heaves himself up to raise him and calls for gold armor. Among the courtiers, Cao Cao, very still, says to nobody what has happened. Lü Bu kneeling over the seal owns the page. The dominant turn: the seal on the floor; "Father"; Cao Cao, "For a horse." The reader turns because the tyrant now owns the warrior nobody can beat, and Cao Cao is watching. The death is never shown; the seal says it. The courtiers stay subordinate.
