# Exact script — pages 31–40

Open only after the blind sequence read.

## PAGE 31 · *illustrated prose*

**Location:** the coalition camp before Hulao Gate.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 100%.** A long mountain valley filled with tents to the
far end, cooking smoke, and banners of eighteen colours and shapes, a great
gold banner over the largest tent. At the head of the valley, filling the
pass between two mountains, a vast grey fortress gate with walls climbing the
slopes on either side and purple banners on its towers. In the near corner,
small, the three brothers riding in at the edge of the camp with their five
hundred behind them, pitching one small tent.

> **PROSE FIELD 1** *(across the top, over the sky above the mountains)*
> "Eighteen lords answered Cao Cao's letter. They came with their armies from every corner of the empire and camped in a valley in the mountains, in front of Hulao Gate.
>
> Hulao Gate was a fortress built across the only pass on the road to the capital. Behind it was Dong Zhuo. Behind Dong Zhuo was the boy emperor.
>
> Liu Bei, Guan Yu, and Zhang Fei came with five hundred men, most of them the same farmers as before, and pitched their one tent at the very edge of the camp."

**Why you turn the page:** eighteen great lords are about to meet three nobodies.

## PAGE 32 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the great tent.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 40%.** A long table down a great tent, lords in bronze
and silk seated in order of rank. At the head, under the gold banner, YUAN
SHAO in ivory and gold, tall hat, three-strand beard. Cao Cao seated halfway
down. At the far end by the entrance, standing, the three brothers.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left)*
> "Yuan Shao, who had defied Dong Zhuo in the throne room, was chosen to lead the eighteen lords."
>
> **YUAN SHAO:** "Who are those three standing at the back, by the door?"

**Panel 2 — 20%.** Cao Cao, turning in his seat, one finger raised.

> **CAO CAO:** "Liu Bei, of the imperial family. He beat the Yellow Turbans with five hundred farmers."

**Panel 3 — 20%.** Yuan Shao smoothing his beard between two fingers. Lords
laughing behind their sleeves.

> **YUAN SHAO:** "The imperial family? I was told he sells sandals."
>
> **CAO CAO:** "He came when we called, Lord Yuan. Give him a seat."

**Panel 4 — 20%.** Liu Bei sitting down on the very end of the last bench,
bowing. Behind him Guan Yu stands like stone, and Zhang Fei leans down,
red-faced, to his ear.

> **ZHANG FEI:** "Say the word, Big Brother, and I'll put his hat in the soup."
>
> **LIU BEI:** "Not a word, Third Brother. Not one."

**Why you turn the page:** Liu Bei is at the table, at the very end of it, and Zhang Fei is about to burst.

## PAGE 33 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the great tent.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** A messenger flinging himself down at the head of the
table. Yuan Shao on his feet.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left)*
> "That afternoon, Dong Zhuo's champion rode out of Hulao Gate and dared the lords to fight him."
>
> **YUAN SHAO:** "Send out our best!"

**Panel 2 — 20%.** The messenger on his knees again, head bowed. The lords
silent.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left)*
> "Their best rode out, and did not come back. Neither did the next."

**Panel 3 — 20%.** Yuan Shao, pale, striking the table with his fist.

> **YUAN SHAO:** "If only my own great generals were here, we would not have to be afraid of one man!"

**Panel 4 — dominant, 35%.** From the back of the tent, Guan Yu stepping
forward, huge, stroking his beard, and every head turning to look at him.

> **GUAN YU:** "I will go out and bring his helmet back to this table."

**Why you turn the page:** a man with no rank has just volunteered in front of eighteen lords.

## PAGE 34 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the great tent.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Yuan Shao, left, at the head of the table, staring
down its length. Liu Bei, right, standing at the end.

> **YUAN SHAO:** "And who is this? What is his rank?"
>
> **LIU BEI:** "My brother, Guan Yu. He rides in my company. He has no rank at all."

**Panel 2 — 20%.** Yuan Shao, outraged, flinging out one silk sleeve.

> **YUAN SHAO:** "A common soldier? Do you want the eighteen lords to look like fools? Throw him out of this tent!"

**Panel 3 — 20%.** Cao Cao on his feet, one hand raised.

> **CAO CAO:** "Wait. Look at the size of him. Dong Zhuo's champion won't know he's a common soldier. If he fails, punish him then."

**Panel 4 — dominant, 35%.** Cao Cao, left, pouring a steaming cup of wine
and holding it out. Guan Yu, right, already turning for the door with the
Green Dragon blade in his hand.

> **CAO CAO:** "Drink this before you go. It's hot."
>
> **GUAN YU:** "Pour it and leave it on the table. I will be back before it is cold."

**Why you turn the page:** Guan Yu has bet his life against a cup of wine.

## PAGE 35 · *spectacle*

**Location:** the great tent, and through its open flap, the plain before Hulao Gate.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 100%.** Inside the great tent. In the near foreground,
on the table, the cup of wine with steam still curling up from it. Behind it,
the lords frozen in their seats, listening, Liu Bei and Zhang Fei at the far
end on their feet. Through the open tent flap, far away on the dusty plain
under the grey wall of Hulao Gate, two tiny riders charging at each other
at full gallop, a green-robed one and a dark one, and at the very moment
they meet, a great wall of dust swallowing them both. No result can be
seen.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left)*
> "Outside, the war drums began to beat."

> **SOUND** *(across the top of the tent)*
> "BOOM… BOOM… BOOM…"

**Why you turn the page:** somebody is going to come back through that flap, and the wine is still steaming.

## PAGE 36 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the great tent.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** Guan Yu ducking in through the tent flap and setting a
dented plumed helmet on the table in front of Yuan Shao.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left)*
> "The drums stopped. Then the tent flap opened."
>
> **GUAN YU:** "His helmet. As I promised."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 35%.** Guan Yu lifting the cup; steam still rising
from it. Cao Cao beside him, delighted.

> **CAO CAO:** "Look at that. It's still warm."

**Panel 3 — 20%.** Zhang Fei bursting forward along the table, roaring.

> **ZHANG FEI:** "My brother just killed Dong Zhuo's champion!"
>
> **ZHANG FEI:** "Now let's all go out and take the whole gate, and drag that fat pig out by his ears!"

**Panel 4 — 20%.** Yuan Shao standing, white with anger. Cao Cao, right,
shrugging.

> **YUAN SHAO:** "Silence! We are lords, not butchers! Throw these three nobodies out!"
>
> **CAO CAO:** "A victory is a victory, Lord Yuan, whoever wins it."

**Why you turn the page:** Guan Yu won, and they're being thrown out anyway.

## PAGE 37 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the brothers' tent, at night.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 45%.** The brothers' small tent, one lamp. Cao Cao,
alone, crouched by the three of them, a wine jar and a basket of roast meat
set down between them, his face serious for once.

> **CAO CAO:** "Tomorrow they'll send out Lü Bu. He kneels to Dong Zhuo now, and calls that fat man Father."
>
> **CAO CAO:** "I was at court when he killed the man who raised him, for a red horse. Nobody here will last three blows."

**Panel 2 — 25%.** Cao Cao leaning closer to Liu Bei.

> **CAO CAO:** "When he comes, I want you three with me. Not with Yuan Shao."
>
> **LIU BEI:** "We came to fight Dong Zhuo, Lord Cao. We will fight beside anyone who does."

**Panel 3 — 30%.** Cao Cao gone, the tent flap still swinging behind them.
Zhang Fei, left, turned to his brothers, slamming his fist into his palm;
Guan Yu, right, stroking his beard, looking at the flap.

> **ZHANG FEI:** "Lü Bu killed his own father for a horse? Tomorrow I'll call him that to his face, in front of both armies."
>
> **GUAN YU:** "Cao Cao watched a son kill his father, and he went on bowing."

**Why you turn the page:** Lü Bu is coming tomorrow, and Cao Cao asked for an answer Liu Bei didn't give.

## PAGE 38 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the top of Hulao Gate, at night.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 50%.** The battlements of Hulao Gate under purple
banners. Dong Zhuo on a heap of cushions, eating. Lü Bu beside him, halberd
on his shoulder, looking down at the thousands of campfires in the valley.

> **DONG ZHUO:** "My champion is dead. They say a red-faced common soldier did it, and then the lords threw him out of their tent!"

**Panel 2 — 20%.** Dong Zhuo laughing, then not laughing, looking out at
the fires.

> **DONG ZHUO:** "Fools. But there are eighteen armies down there, and I have one gate."

**Panel 3 — 30%.** Lü Bu, turning from the parapet, the pheasant plumes
black against the moon.

> **LÜ BU:** "You have me, Father. Open the gate tomorrow, and I will bring you all eighteen."

**Why you turn the page:** tomorrow the gate opens, and Lü Bu comes out.

## PAGE 39 · *spectacle*

**Location:** Hulao Gate.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 100%.** The great studded doors of Hulao Gate thrown
open. Out of them, at full gallop toward the reader, LÜ BU on RED HARE: the
blood-red horse at full stretch, Lü Bu in gold armor and a vermilion cloak,
the two long pheasant plumes streaming back, the halberd levelled. Behind
him, his army pouring out under purple banners.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left, over the gate tower)*
> "Among men, Lü Bu. Among horses, Red Hare."

**Why you turn the page:** the man nobody can beat is riding out, and the eighteen lords have to face him.

## PAGE 40 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the plain before Hulao Gate.

**Panel 1 — 25%.** A lord's champion in bronze charging at Lü Bu; the
champion's spear snapping in two, the pieces spinning in the air.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left)*
> "The lords sent their champions out one at a time."

**Panel 2 — 20%.** Another champion knocked out of his saddle and out of
the frame; his horse running on without him.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left)*
> "Not one of them lasted three blows."

**Panel 3 — dominant, 55%.** The coalition's front line breaking, banners
wavering, men turning to run. On a rise under the great gold banner, Yuan
Shao on his horse, gripping the reins. Far off, Lü Bu turning Red Hare
toward the gold banner. On the same rise, near the gold banner, the three
brothers on horseback with their small company. On a separate rise in the distance, plainly apart
from the gold banner, Cao Cao on his horse under black-and-indigo banners,
his riders held still behind him, watching. He is not riding.

> **YUAN SHAO:** "Is there no one in eighteen armies who can stop one man?"

**Why you turn the page:** Lü Bu has seen the leader's banner, and he's coming for it.
