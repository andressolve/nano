## Page intent

## PAGE 40

Dramatic. The lords send their champions out one at a time, and none lasts: a spear snaps in two in one small panel, a rider is knocked out of his saddle and out of frame in the next, his horse running on alone. Then the large panel: the coalition's front line breaking, banners wavering, men turning to run, and Yuan Shao on his rise under the great gold banner asking whether eighteen armies have no one who can stop one man. Far off, Lü Bu turns Red Hare toward that banner. The three brothers sit their horses on the same rise near it. On a separate rise, plainly apart, Cao Cao holds his riders still under black-and-indigo banners and watches. The dominant turn is the line breaking while Cao Cao holds still on his own hill. The reader turns because Lü Bu has seen the leader's banner and is coming for it.
