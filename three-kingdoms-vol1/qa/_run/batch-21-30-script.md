## PAGE 21 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the throne hall, in the morning.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 55%.** Morning light through the columns. Lü Bu
striding up the hall toward Dong Zhuo, and laying on the black floor in front
of him a small bronze seal in the shape of a crouching tiger. Through the
great doors behind him, a groom holds Red Hare. He is going
down on one knee. Courtiers on both sides drawing back.

> **LÜ BU:** "The old man said no to you. He will never say no to anyone again."
>
> **LÜ BU:** "This was his seal. I have come to call you Father."

**Panel 2 — 25%.** Dong Zhuo, delighted, heaving himself up to raise Lü Bu
by both arms.

> **DONG ZHUO:** "My son! Bring my son his gold armor!"

**Panel 3 — 20%.** Among the courtiers, Cao Cao, very still, watching. He
murmurs it to nobody.

> **CAO CAO:** "He killed the man who raised him. For a horse."

**Why you turn the page:** now the tyrant owns the warrior nobody can beat, and Cao Cao is watching.

## PAGE 22 · *illustrated prose*

**Location:** two — the emperor's bedchamber, and the throne hall.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 60%.** The emperor's bedchamber: Dong Zhuo asleep
across the great imperial bed among silk curtains, a plate of half-eaten food
on his belly. In the doorway, Lü Bu on guard.

**Panel 2 — 40%.** The throne hall at morning audience. Rows of courtiers
bowing. In the front row, bowing lowest of all, Cao Cao, and just showing at
his sleeve, the jeweled hilt of a dagger.

> **PROSE FIELD 1** *(across the top, over the silk curtains, never over faces)*
> "Now Dong Zhuo had the capital, the boy emperor, and the one warrior nobody could beat.
>
> He slept in the emperor's bed and ate from the emperor's dishes. His soldiers robbed the city. The boy on the throne said what he was told to say. Every lord who could run away had run, and the ones who stayed bowed.
>
> Cao Cao bowed lowest of all. He came to court every morning and smiled at everything Dong Zhuo said. In his sleeve he carried a dagger with seven jewels in the hilt."

**Why you turn the page:** Cao Cao has a dagger, and he is close enough to use it.

# MOVEMENT IV — THE DAGGER · pp 23–30

## PAGE 23 · *dramatic*

**Location:** Dong Zhuo's bedchamber.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** Dong Zhuo lying on a wide couch, too fat to sit up for
long. Lü Bu standing beside him. In the doorway, left, Cao Cao bowing.

> **DONG ZHUO:** "You're late, Cao Cao."
>
> **CAO CAO:** "Forgive me, my lord. My horse is old and slow."

**Panel 2 — 20%.** Dong Zhuo waving a fat hand. Lü Bu, halberd on his
shoulder, already turning for the door.

> **DONG ZHUO:** "Lü Bu, go down to my stable and choose a fast horse for Cao Cao."

**Panel 3 — dominant, 50%.** Dong Zhuo has rolled his great body over to
face the wall, his back to the room. Cao Cao stands behind him, alone, one
hand on the jeweled hilt inside his sleeve. In the corner, a round bronze
mirror on a stand faces the couch.

> **DONG ZHUO:** "Wake me when the horse comes."

**Why you turn the page:** the tyrant has turned his back, and Cao Cao's hand is on the dagger.

## PAGE 24 · *dramatic*

**Location:** Dong Zhuo's bedchamber.

**Panel 1 — 20%.** The dagger out and raised above Dong Zhuo's back. In the
bronze mirror: Dong Zhuo's one open eye, and the blade.

**Panel 2 — 20%.** Dong Zhuo rolling over, roaring. Lü Bu's shadow falling
across the doorway.

> **DONG ZHUO:** "Cao Cao! What are you doing?"

**Panel 3 — dominant, 40%.** Cao Cao already on both knees, the dagger lying
flat across his two open palms, held up like a present, smiling. Lü Bu in the
doorway behind him.

> **CAO CAO:** "My lord, I have a precious dagger, the finest blade in the capital. I wanted to give it to you with my own hands."

**Panel 4 — 20%.** Dong Zhuo turning the dagger in his fat fingers,
admiring the seven jewels. Cao Cao rising, bowing.

> **DONG ZHUO:** "Seven jewels! Look at this, my son!"
>
> **CAO CAO:** "May I try the horse your son chose for me? Just once around the courtyard."

**Why you turn the page:** Cao Cao lied his way out with the blade in his hands, and now he's on a fast horse.

## PAGE 25 · *illustrated prose*

**Location:** two — the city gate, and Dong Zhuo's bedchamber.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 60%.** Cao Cao galloping out through the great city
gate in a cloud of dust, crouched low over the horse's neck, guards turning
too late.

**Panel 2 — 40%.** Dong Zhuo sitting up on his couch, the jeweled dagger in
his hand, frowning at it. Lü Bu beside him.

> **PROSE FIELD 1** *(across the top, over the dust and sky, never over the rider)*
> "Cao Cao rode once around the courtyard, and out of the gate, and out of the city, and he did not stop.
>
> It was an hour before Dong Zhuo turned the dagger over in his hands and asked why a man would kneel to give a gift with the blade already out of its sheath.
>
> 'Because he was going to kill you, Father,' said Lü Bu.
>
> By nightfall Cao Cao's face was painted on every gate in the empire, with a reward for his head."

**Why you turn the page:** the whole empire is hunting Cao Cao.

## PAGE 26 · *dramatic*

**Location:** a farmhouse at dusk.

**Panel 1 — 35%.** Dusk. A modest farm gate in yellow fields. Cao Cao,
dusty and hollow-eyed, on a tired horse. The gate opening: UNCLE, a thin
little old man, nearly bald with a white topknot and a sparse white beard,
his arms flung wide.

> **UNCLE:** "Who's there? Cao Cao? Little Cao Cao, at my gate! Your father and I swore to be brothers when we were young men!"

**Panel 2 — 25%.** Cao Cao, low, glancing back down the road.

> **CAO CAO:** "Uncle, there is a price on my head. If they find me here—"
>
> **UNCLE:** "Then they won't find you. Not in this house! Come in, come in."

**Panel 3 — dominant, 40%.** The courtyard. The old man climbing onto a
grey donkey with an empty wine jar under his arm, beaming. Cao Cao alone in
the courtyard, watching him go, one hand on his sword.

> **UNCLE:** "The wine in this house isn't good enough for my brother's son."
>
> **UNCLE:** "I'll ride to the town for the good wine, and be back by morning. Rest! My family will look after you."

**Why you turn the page:** a kind old man has left a hunted man alone in his house.

## PAGE 27 · *dramatic*

**Location:** inside the farmhouse.

**Panel 1 — 20%.** Night. Cao Cao on a sleeping mat in the dark, eyes open.
Through the thin wall, lamplight in the crack, and a sound.

> **SOUND** *(along the wall)*
> "SHHK… SHHK…"

**Panel 2 — 20%.** Close on Cao Cao's face, listening, his hand closing on
the sword beside him.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left)*
> "Through the wall he heard a knife on a whetstone, and a whisper: 'Is he asleep? Good. Tie the legs tight first, so there's no struggling. Then one quick cut, so he never hears a thing.'"

> **CAPTION** *(lower left)*
> "Cao Cao heard 'tie,' and 'cut,' and 'he.' He did not wait to hear more."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** A lamplit doorway. Cao Cao's drawn sword coming through
it from the dark. Inside the little room, an old woman and a young man
with the knife he was sharpening still in his hand, turning toward the door, startled,
alive. The panel ends there.

**Panel 4 — dominant, 35%.** Grey dawn in a small kitchen. On the table, a
live pig tied by its legs, a cleaver and a whetstone beside it. Nobody else
in the room. Cao Cao in the doorway, sword lowered, looking at the pig.

> **CAPTION** *(upper left)*
> "He killed every person in that house. Then he found the kitchen."

> **CAPTION** *(lower right)*
> "They had been getting a pig ready. For him."

**Why you turn the page:** Cao Cao has killed a whole family for nothing, and the old man is still out buying wine.

## PAGE 28 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the road outside the farm.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** Morning. Cao Cao riding out fast between willows.
Coming up the road toward him, the old man on his donkey, a full wine jar
and a basket of fruit, waving.

> **UNCLE:** "Little Cao Cao! Leaving already? I told them to kill a pig for you! Turn around, we'll eat in an hour!"

**Panel 2 — 20%.** Cao Cao reined in, looking down at the old man.

> **CAO CAO:** "Uncle, I'm sorry."
>
> **UNCLE:** "Sorry? Sorry for what?"

**Panel 3 — dominant, 35%.** Cao Cao above the old man, his sword drawn,
his face calm. The old man's open, puzzled face.

> **CAO CAO:** "When you get home you'll see what I did, and you'll tell the world."
>
> **CAO CAO:** "I would rather betray the whole world than let the world betray me."

**Panel 4 — 15%.** The empty road. The donkey wandering away. A wine jar
rolling in the dust, fruit scattered behind it.

> **CAPTION** *(lower right)*
> "The old man never came home."

**Why you turn the page:** the man who tried to kill the tyrant has just killed an innocent old man, and only you saw.

## PAGE 29 · *illustrated prose*

**Location:** two — Cao Cao's camp, and the market of Zhuo County.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 60%.** A camp under black-and-indigo banners, riders
galloping out of it in every direction with letters in their coats. Cao
Cao on a rise, watching them go.

**Panel 2 — 40%.** The market of Zhuo County, five years older and just as
dusty. Liu Bei at his sandal stall under the same wall, taking a sealed
letter from a dust-covered rider.

> **PROSE FIELD 1** *(across the top, over the sky above the camp)*
> "Cao Cao reached his home country, sold his father's land, and raised an army. Then he wrote to every lord in the empire: 'Dong Zhuo holds the emperor like a puppet. Every man who is loyal to the throne, rise and march against him.'
>
> One of the letters came all the way to Zhuo County, where a man was still selling straw sandals in the market.
>
> Nobody there knew what Cao Cao had done on the road."

**Why you turn the page:** the letter has reached Liu Bei, and he has waited five years for it.

## PAGE 30 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the peach garden.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** The peach garden, the trees in full green leaf. Liu Bei,
centre, reading the letter aloud. Guan Yu, left, and Zhang Fei, right,
leaning in on either side of him.

> **LIU BEI:** "'Every man who is loyal to the throne, rise and march against Dong Zhuo.'"
>
> **ZHANG FEI:** "Dong Zhuo? That's the fat pig we saved! The one who gave us rice!"
>
> **GUAN YU:** "And it is Cao Cao's letter. The man who smiles too quickly."

**Panel 2 — dominant, 45%.** Liu Bei on his feet, the letter crushed in his
long hand, his face fierce and hungry.

> **LIU BEI:** "Five years I have sold sandals and waited for someone to send for us. Nobody sent for us. Cao Cao sent for everybody."
>
> **LIU BEI:** "I don't care who sent it. We go."

**Panel 3 — 25%.** Guan Yu, rising, taking the Green Dragon blade from where
it leans against a peach tree. Zhang Fei, behind him, whooping, already
running for his spear.

> **GUAN YU:** "Then we will go and fix the mistake we made when we saved Dong Zhuo."

**Why you turn the page:** the brothers are going to war against the man they saved, at the call of a man they don't know.
