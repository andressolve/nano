## Page intent

## PAGE 12

Spectacle. The whole plain is on fire: flames taller than men, black smoke, tents going up, rebels running out of the blaze toward us, and dark horsemen under black-and-indigo banners sweeping in from both sides to cut them off. In the foreground the three brothers rein in, black silhouettes against the orange, too late to do anything. The dominant turn: the plain burning, and Zhang Fei's outraged cry that somebody stole their battle. Zhang Fei owns the only line, in a small close panel of his orange-lit face, mouth wide; the outrage is funny and real. The reader turns wanting to know who got here first. The riders stay anonymous: no leader is singled out yet, and no one falls in view.

### C1 — Transcription or ownership fails
Zhang Fei's line is missing, altered, duplicated, not comfortably transcribable from the 600 × 900 proof, or not owned by his mouth; or any extra text appears. A string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue; the words and their order are what must match.

### C2 — The burning plain does not own the page
One great image of the plain on fire must own the page: flames, smoke, rebels fleeing toward us, and riders under dark banners cutting them off from both sides, with the brothers in silhouette in the foreground, too late. If the fire or the strangers' riders do not read, the question "who got here first?" is lost.

### C3 — Zhang Fei's outrage fails
The small close face must be recognisably Zhang Fei (round bulging eyes, bristling whiskers, broad head), lit by fire, mouth open in outrage. Another face, or a calm one, is blocking.

### C4 — The stranger is revealed or a death is drawn
No single rider may be singled out as the leader; the riders stay anonymous. Any blood, wound, body, or burning person is blocking. Crowd counts and background anatomy are nonblocking.
