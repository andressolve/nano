## Page intent

## PAGE 47

A dramatic page at the foot of Hulao Gate, late in the day. The gate has slammed and Lü Bu is safe behind it, but the page belongs to him looking down from the battlements, out of breath for the first time in the book, asking the three men below who they are. The dominant turn is the exchange itself: "Who are you?", Liu Bei answering with three names and the word "brothers," and Guan Yu's one-word refusal. Zhang Fei's shouted insult opens the page and stays a small beat; the brothers below are small against the grey wall. What changes is that the best warrior in the empire has had to learn their names. Guan Yu's "No." closes the page with the oath answering Lü Bu's bargain. The reader turns because Lü Bu knows who they are now and the light is going.

### C1 — Transcription
Every scripted string reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in causal order, owned by the right mouth by tail and placement, with no extra text anywhere: Zhang Fei's shout, Lü Bu's question, Liu Bei's names, Lü Bu's bargain, Guan Yu's "No." A string that does not read is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, and a regional spelling variant of the same word are nonblocking; the words and their order are what must match. A line owned by placement, directly beside or under its speaker, is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — Lü Bu on the wall does not own the page
The eye must find first Lü Bu leaning on the battlements high above, out of breath, looking down; the brothers below are the smaller beats. If Zhang Fei's shout or the three brothers' panel takes the page, the turn has not landed. Judge by what the eye finds, never by area.

### C3 — Attribution of the closing exchange
"Who are you?" and "Next time, come at me one at a time." must hang from Lü Bu on the wall; "I am Liu Bei…" from Liu Bei, not from Guan Yu or Zhang Fei; "No." from Guan Yu, read after Lü Bu's bargain. "No." owned by Liu Bei or Zhang Fei, or read before the bargain, is blocking.

### C4 — Identity and the giants pair
Lü Bu must be pale, clean-shaven, with two long plumes; Guan Yu red-faced with a beard to his belt; Liu Bei broad-faced and moustached with long earlobes; Zhang Fei broad with bristling whiskers. A bearded Lü Bu, a pale or beardless Guan Yu, or any two of them blurred into one face is blocking.

### C5 — Continuity and integrity
The gate doors are shut and Liu Bei's swords are sheathed at his back. A body, a wound, or blood is blocking, as are duplicated figures or a balloon on a soldier. Background soldiers' faces and banner detail are nonblocking.
