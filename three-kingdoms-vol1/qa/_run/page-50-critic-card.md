## Page intent

## PAGE 50

A spectacle page before Hulao Gate at cold blue dawn, the last page of the volume. The three brothers sit their horses side by side before the shut gate, Liu Bei in the middle facing it, Guan Yu and Zhang Fei on either side. High on the battlements, small against the sky, Lü Bu's two pheasant plumes. Behind them, large and clear on a ridge in the near corner, Cao Cao on his horse under his black-and-indigo banners, watching. The dominant turn is that Liu Bei does not turn round: he has chosen his brothers over Cao Cao's praise, for today. One caption tells it, and a small title card ends the volume. Nothing else is said. The book ends with the gate still shut and the man on the ridge still watching.

### C1 — Transcription
The caption and the title card read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once each, the caption first, with no other text anywhere on the page. A string that does not read is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, and a regional spelling variant of the same word are nonblocking; the words and their order are what must match. ### C2 — The turn does not land
The eye must find the three brothers facing the shut gate together and, large in the near corner, Cao Cao watching their backs. Liu Bei must face the gate. A Liu Bei turned toward Cao Cao, or a Cao Cao too small or hidden to read as watching, loses the book's last turn and is blocking. Judge by what the eye finds, never by area.

### C3 — Identity
Cao Cao must be short, narrow-faced, goateed, in indigo under black-and-indigo banners, and never mistakable for Liu Bei; the three brothers must read as three different men (green giant with the crescent blade, white-cloaked Liu Bei in the middle with crossed sword hilts, broad black-armored Zhang Fei with the wavy spear). Lü Bu reads only as two small plumes on the wall. A swapped order with Liu Bei not in the middle, or a blurred brother, is blocking.

### C4 — Continuity and integrity
The gate is shut; Liu Bei's swords are sheathed. A balloon, a fight, a body, duplicated riders, or extra lettering on banners is blocking. Dawn colour, banner count, and wall detail are nonblocking.
