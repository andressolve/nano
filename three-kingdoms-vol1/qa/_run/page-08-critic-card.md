## Page intent

## PAGE 8

Illustrated prose, two places. At a smith's forge in red heat and sparks, the new weapons lie ready: two matching swords in black scabbards, a spear as long as two men with a wavy snake blade, and a great curved blade on a long pole with a dragon at its base, which Guan Yu lifts in one hand while the smith gapes. Below, the peach garden, now in green leaf, is packed with farmers holding hoes and pitchforks, the three brothers before them. The prose tells how the money became a weapon for each brother, names the Green Dragon, and brings five hundred farmers who in a month have spears. The dominant turn is the brothers becoming an armed company: the forge owns the page, the gathering is its consequence. The reader turns because three brothers and five hundred farmers are going to war.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 8 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
Both prose paragraphs must read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order, every sentence present, with no balloons and no other text; a dropped sentence or clause is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant.

### C2 — The arming does not land
The page must read as illustrated prose: the forge, with the weapons and a red-faced giant lifting the great curved blade one-handed, plainly owns the page, and the garden full of farmers reads as its consequence. Prose covering a weapon, a hand, or a face is blocking.

### C3 — The weapons do not read
A pair of matching straight swords in black scabbards, a very long spear with a wavy blade, and a great curved blade on a long pole must each be identifiable, because the prose names each one. Swords drawn or held bare is blocking; the dragon's detail and hardware are not.

### C4 — Identity fails
The man lifting the blade has a red face, a long loose beard, and a green headscarf; the smith must not look like a brother, and the small brothers in the garden must stay distinct from the farmers. Farmers' faces and the farmer count are nonblocking.
