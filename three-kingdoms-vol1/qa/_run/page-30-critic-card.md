## Page intent

## PAGE 30

Dramatic. In the peach garden, now in full green leaf, Liu Bei reads the letter aloud with Guan Yu and Zhang Fei leaning in on either side. Zhang Fei is outraged that Dong Zhuo is the fat man they saved; Guan Yu notes the letter is from the man who smiles too quickly. The page's one dominant turn belongs to Liu Bei: on his feet, the letter crushed in his hand, his face fierce and hungry, saying that nobody ever sent for them, Cao Cao sent for everybody, and he does not care who sent it: they go. That close, hungry face owns the page; it is the first time his want shows. Then Guan Yu rises and takes his Green Dragon blade from a peach tree, and Zhang Fei whoops and runs for his spear. The reader turns because the brothers are marching against the man they saved, at the call of a man they don't know.

## Materiality threshold

The critic is a release gate, not a defect collector. A visible issue is
blocking only when it materially harms the reader event, exact text or
attribution, named or focal identity, consequential continuity or object state,
focal generation integrity, or the dominant dramatic relationship. Every
correction replaces the complete page. Minor background, cosmetic, tiny-prop,
geometry, colour, texture, and prompt-fidelity observations are nonblocking.
Omit them. Numeric panel shares and lettering sizes are builder construction
guidance, never critic measurements.

Each numbered criterion is also its stable retry signature. Cite it only when
the reader harm and redraw justification are concrete.

A lettered speaker name, label, or tag anywhere on the page is extra text and
blocks under the transcription criterion, whatever else reads correctly:
finished pages carry dialogue, captions, prose, and sound cues only.
A sound cue whose letters and order match the script reads correctly; the run
length of a repeated letter in it is nonblocking and never counted.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 30 — card

### C1 — Transcription
Every scripted string reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in causal order, owned by the right mouth by tail and placement, with no extra text; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, and a regional spelling variant are nonblocking; the words and their order must match. A line owned by placement or by naming its addressee is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — Liu Bei's hunger owns the page
Liu Bei on his feet, the letter crushed in his hand, face fierce, must be what the eye finds. If he looks mild or the image is one of several equal panels, his first open want is lost. Dramatic mode.

### C3 — Attribution of the three-way reading
The quoted letter line is Liu Bei's; "the fat pig we saved" is Zhang Fei's; "smiles too quickly" is Guan Yu's, in that order. Both lines in the large image are Liu Bei's; the closing line is Guan Yu's. Zhang Fei's whoop carries no balloon.

### C4 — Three brothers, three silhouettes
Liu Bei slim with a moustache only and long earlobes; Guan Yu a red-faced giant with a belt-length black beard; Zhang Fei short and broad with bristling whiskers. Liu Bei's swords stay sheathed on his back.

### C5 — Place and objects
The garden is in green leaf. Guan Yu takes the Green Dragon blade from a tree. Blossom is nonblocking unless it confuses the season's story.
