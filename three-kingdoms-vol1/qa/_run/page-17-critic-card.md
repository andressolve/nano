## Page 17 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
The one caption does not read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, or other text appears (a speech balloon, lettering on a banner, a label). A string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing, a regional spelling variant of the same word; the words and their order are what must match.

### C2 — The turn does not land
The eye does not go first to a fat man in gold riding a horse up the palace stair, or the page reads as a city view with no event, or he is on foot. Nonblocking: the exact size of the rider, the number of soldiers, the city's layout.

### C3 — Dong Zhuo not recognisable
The rider does not read as the obese, jowled general in gold and purple with food in his fist. Nonblocking: whether the drumstick is legible as a drumstick when the fist plainly holds food.

### C4 — A death is drawn
Blood, a wound, or a body appears on the stair. Courtiers falling aside unhurt are correct.

### C5 — Consequential generation failure
A duplicated rider, gross anatomy on the rider or horse, or corruption at the focal point. Anonymous crowd repetition, background hands, and texture do not block.
