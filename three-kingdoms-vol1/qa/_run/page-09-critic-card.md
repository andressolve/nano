## Page intent

## PAGE 9

Dramatic. On a bare ridge, the three brothers on horseback, five hundred farmers with spears behind them; below, a dust-yellow plain full of yellow headbands to the horizon, and between ridge and plain a narrow valley between two scrubby hills. Zhang Fei jokes that it is only a hundred each; Guan Yu notes the farmers are shaking because they can count too. Then Liu Bei points at the two hills and makes the plan: his brothers hide behind them, he rides out with the farmers as bait. Zhang Fei protests that he is the worst fighter; Liu Bei says that is why they will chase him. The dominant turn is fifty thousand below and the plan making Liu Bei the bait. The reader turns on the plan. The individual farmers and rebels stay small.

## Exact owner-controlled script

## PAGE 9 · *dramatic*

**Location:** a ridge above the rebel army.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 50%.** A bare ridge. The three brothers on horseback in
front, five hundred farmers with spears behind them. Below, a dust-yellow
plain filled to the horizon with yellow headbands. Between the ridge and the
plain, a narrow valley with a scrubby hill on either side.

> **ZHANG FEI:** "Fifty thousand of them and five hundred of us. Good! That's only a hundred each."

**Panel 2 — 20%.** Guan Yu, glancing back at the farmers, who are pale and
shaking.

> **GUAN YU:** "The farmers are shaking, Third Brother. They can count too."

**Panel 3 — 30%.** Liu Bei, centre, pointing down at the two hills. Zhang
Fei, right, staring at him.

> **LIU BEI:** "Then we won't fight fifty thousand. Second Brother, hide behind the left hill. Third Brother, behind the right."
>
> **LIU BEI:** "I'll ride out with the farmers and let them chase us."
>
> **ZHANG FEI:** "You? You're the worst fighter of the three of us!"
>
> **LIU BEI:** "That's why they'll chase me. When they're between the hills, come down."

**Why you turn the page:** the plan is that Liu Bei runs from fifty thousand men.

## Page 9 — card

### C1 — Transcription or ownership fails
The six lines must each read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in causal order, each owned by the right mouth by tail and placement, with no extra text; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing, a regional spelling variant. A line placed directly under its speaker's previous balloon or naming its addressee is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — The odds and the plan do not land
The first and largest image must show the few on the ridge above a plain full of yellow headbands, with two hills framing a valley between, so the plan later makes sense; the page must end on Liu Bei making himself the bait.

### C3 — Attribution across the exchange
Zhang Fei owns "a hundred each" and "You? You're the worst fighter"; Guan Yu owns the farmers shaking; Liu Bei owns both plan lines and "That's why they'll chase me." Zhang Fei's protest must read before Liu Bei's reply, each hanging from its own mouth.

### C4 — Identity and armed state fail
Liu Bei pale and long-eared in iron and a white cloak, two swords sheathed crossed on his back; Guan Yu red-faced and long-bearded in green; Zhang Fei broad and whiskered in black. Swords drawn, or brothers confused, is blocking.

### C5 — Focal generation integrity
A duplicated brother or a garbled speaker's face is blocking. Farmer and rebel counts, horse anatomy at distance, and background faces are not.
