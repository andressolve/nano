## Page intent

## PAGE 22

Illustrated prose. A quiet page of aftermath across two places. Above, the emperor's bedchamber: Dong Zhuo asleep across the great imperial bed among silk curtains, a plate of half-eaten food on his belly, and Lü Bu standing guard in the doorway. Below, the throne hall at morning audience: rows of courtiers bowing, and in the front row, bowing lowest of all, Cao Cao, with the jeweled hilt of a dagger just showing at his sleeve. The prose tells what Dong Zhuo now has, how the city suffers, and that Cao Cao smiles at everything and carries a seven-jewelled dagger. The dominant turn: the tyrant in the emperor's bed; Cao Cao bows lowest, a dagger in his sleeve. The reader turns because Cao Cao has a dagger and is close enough to use it. The rows of courtiers stay subordinate; the dagger hilt is small but must be found.

The critic is a release gate, not a defect collector. A visible issue is
blocking only when it materially harms the reader event, exact text or
attribution, named or focal identity, consequential continuity or object state,
focal generation integrity, or the dominant dramatic relationship. Every

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 22 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
Any of the three prose paragraphs is missing, altered, duplicated, reordered, or not comfortably transcribable from the 600 × 900 proof, or any other text appears (a balloon, a caption, a label). A string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, line breaks within a paragraph; the words and their order must match.

### C2 — The turn does not land
The bedchamber with Dong Zhuo asleep across the imperial bed, food on his belly, and Lü Bu guarding the door does not own the page; or the lower panel does not show Cao Cao bowing lowest in the front row. Prose lying over a face or a focal hand blocks.

### C3 — The dagger cannot be found
A reader cannot find the jeweled dagger hilt at Cao Cao's sleeve. Nonblocking: the number of jewels; the dagger stays in the sleeve.

### C4 — Identity fails
Dong Zhuo, Lü Bu (tall, clean-shaven, plumed, in the doorway), or Cao Cao (short, sharp-faced, pointed goatee, small black cap, indigo) cannot be recognised, or a courtier wears plumes, indigo, or purple.

### C5 — Consequential generation failure
A duplicated named figure, gross anatomy on a focal figure, corruption, or prose colliding with art. Courtiers' repeated faces and hands do not block.
