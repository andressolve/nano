## Page intent

## PAGE 38

Dramatic. The page's one turn: on the wall, Lü Bu asks Dong Zhuo for the gate. The opening image owns the page: the battlements of Hulao Gate at night under purple banners, Dong Zhuo eating on a heap of cushions, gleeful that a red-faced common soldier killed his champion and was then thrown out by the lords; Lü Bu beside him, halberd on his shoulder, looking down at thousands of campfires in the valley.
