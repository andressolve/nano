## Page intent

## PAGE 19

Dramatic. At the night banquet an old lord in grey stands and says what Yuan Shao said, louder, and Dong Zhuo reaches for his sword. Then the page's real event: behind the old lord, standing perfectly still, is a giant with two long pheasant plumes and a halberd on his shoulder, Lü Bu, looking straight at Dong Zhuo, and Dong Zhuo's hand stops. Dong Zhuo laughs it off. Later he asks the young officer at his elbow, Cao Cao, who the giant was, and decides he wants him. Lü Bu's silent stare owns the page. The feast and the old lord stay subordinate.
