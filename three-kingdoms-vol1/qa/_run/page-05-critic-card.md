## Page intent

## PAGE 5

Dramatic. Night at Zhang Fei's farm, round a courtyard fire, after talking all night. Liu Bei says what he really fears: these two could join any army, so why would anyone follow a man who sells sandals? Guan Yu answers that he came for the man who sighed at the wall, not for the princes, and Zhang Fei crows that he counted the eleven readings. Then Zhang Fei stands and points into the dark at the blossoming trees over his wall: tomorrow, in the peach garden, they swear to be brothers. The dominant turn moves from the doubt to the answer, and lands on Zhang Fei pointing and Liu Bei's face lit and hungry as he accepts. The reader turns because tomorrow three strangers are going to swear. The fire and the wine jug stay subordinate.

**Panel 1 — 35%.** A rammed-earth courtyard, a fire, a wine jug. Zhang Fei

> **GUAN YU:** "I did not come for your ancestors, the princes. I came for the man who sighed at that wall."
>

**Panel 3 — dominant, 40%.** Zhang Fei on his feet, pointing past the house
into the dark, where pale shapes of blossoming trees show over a wall. Liu
Bei looking up at him, his face lit and hungry.

> **ZHANG FEI:** "Behind my house is a peach garden, and every tree is in bloom."
>
> **ZHANG FEI:** "Tomorrow morning we go out there and swear to be brothers. Real brothers!"
>
> **LIU BEI:** "Then tomorrow we will be brothers."

**Why you turn the page:** tomorrow, in a garden, three strangers are going to swear.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 5 — card

### C1 — Transcription or ownership fails
The caption and six lines must each read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in causal order, each owned by the right mouth by tail and placement, the caption tail-free, with no extra text; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing, a regional spelling variant. A line placed directly under its speaker's previous balloon or naming its addressee is owned even if its tail wanders.

### C2 — The turn to tomorrow does not land
The page must move from Liu Bei's doubt by the fire to Zhang Fei on his feet pointing at blossoming trees in the dark, and end on Liu Bei's lit face accepting; that pointing image must be what the eye finds as the page's peak.

### C3 — Attribution across the exchanges
Liu Bei owns the doubt and "Then tomorrow we will be brothers"; Guan Yu owns "I did not come for your ancestors"; Zhang Fei owns "I counted!" and both peach-garden lines. Liu Bei's acceptance must read last and hang from his mouth, never from Zhang Fei's.

### C4 — Three brothers collide
Pale long-eared Liu Bei, red-faced long-bearded Guan Yu, broad whiskered Zhang Fei must stay distinct by face and build in firelight.

### C5 — Focal generation integrity
A duplicated named figure or a garbled speaker's face is blocking. The count of Zhang Fei's raised fingers is nonblocking.
