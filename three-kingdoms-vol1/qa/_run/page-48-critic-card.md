## Page intent

## PAGE 48

A dramatic page at the brothers' fire at the edge of the camp, at dusk. Cao Cao sits down beside Liu Bei with two cups of wine; Liu Bei will not take his and asks where Cao Cao was when Lü Bu came. Cao Cao answers without shame: on his hill, watching, testing them. He mocks Yuan Shao's claim to the victory, then leans close in the firelight and says the thing the page turns on: there are two heroes in this camp, Liu Bei and himself. That close, glittering face owns the page. Liu Bei, who refused the cup, ends the page holding it and looking into it, answering that he is only a man who sells sandals, while Cao Cao laughs and goes. Guan Yu and Zhang Fei stay subordinate, apart by the fire. The reader turns because Liu Bei has just been told what he most wants to hear by the man he trusts least.

## Exact owner-controlled script

## PAGE 48 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the brothers' fire, at dusk.

**Panel 1 — 30%.** Dusk at the edge of the camp. Guan Yu and Zhang Fei a
little apart by the fire, Zhang Fei eating. Cao Cao, alone, sitting down
beside Liu Bei and pouring two cups of wine. Liu Bei does not take his.

> **LIU BEI:** "You asked us to stand with you when Lü Bu came, Lord Cao. Where were you?"
>
> **CAO CAO:** "On my hill, watching. I wanted to see what the three of you would do. Now I know."

**Panel 2 — 20%.** Cao Cao, amused, nodding toward the great gold banner
across the camp.

> **CAO CAO:** "Yuan Shao is telling the camp that his guards drove Lü Bu off. Yuan Shao has ancestors. Ancestors don't fight Lü Bu."

**Panel 3 — dominant, 35%.** Cao Cao leaning close, smiling, his cup raised,
his narrow eyes glittering in the firelight.

> **CAO CAO:** "There are two heroes in this camp. You, Liu Bei. And me."

**Panel 4 — 15%.** Liu Bei, who would not take the cup, is holding it now,
looking down into it. Cao Cao standing to go, laughing.

> **LIU BEI:** "I am only a man who sells sandals, Lord Cao."
>
> **CAO CAO:** "Of course you are."

**Why you turn the page:** Cao Cao watched them nearly die and called it a test, and he has just said out loud the thing Liu Bei wants most.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 48 — card

### C1 — Transcription
Every scripted string reads exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in causal order, owned by the right mouth by tail and placement, with no extra text anywhere: Liu Bei's question, Cao Cao's three speeches, Liu Bei's answer, Cao Cao's "Of course you are." A string that does not read is blocking; a string that reads is not to be measured. Spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, and a regional spelling variant of the same word are nonblocking; the words and their order are what must match. A line owned by placement, beside its speaker or naming its addressee, is owned even if its tail wanders. The gold banner carries no words.

### C2 — "Two heroes" does not own the page
The eye must find first Cao Cao leaning close in firelight, cup raised, glittering. If the opening fireside panel or the banner panel takes the page, the turn has not landed. Judge by what the eye finds, never by area.

### C3 — Attribution across the fireside exchange
"Where were you?" and "I am only a man who sells sandals" must hang from Liu Bei; the other four lines from Cao Cao. A reply readable before its question, or any line hanging from Guan Yu or Zhang Fei, is blocking.

### C4 — Liu Bei and Cao Cao blur
Liu Bei must be tall, broad-faced, sad-eyed, long-earlobed, with a moustache only, in white; Cao Cao short, narrow-faced, goateed, narrow-eyed, in indigo. If either face could be mistaken for the other in any panel, it is blocking.

### C5 — The cup does not change hands
In the first panel Liu Bei's cup sits untaken and his hands are not on it; in the last he is holding it and looking into it. A Liu Bei drinking or holding the cup at the start, or empty-handed at the end, loses the turn and is blocking. The exact number of jars or bowls around the fire is nonblocking.

### C6 — Focal integrity
Gross anatomy on Liu Bei's hands with the cup or Cao Cao's cup hand, duplicated figures, a balloon on Guan Yu or Zhang Fei, or Yuan Shao appearing is blocking. Background tents and fire detail are not.
