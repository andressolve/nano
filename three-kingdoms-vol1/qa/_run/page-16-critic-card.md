## Page intent

## PAGE 16

Illustrated prose, two places, because the page's turn is the passage of time. Above, three riders very small on a long empty road under a huge winter sky, going home with nothing. Below, far away and five years later, a great army marches on a walled city of grey roofs under purple banners, a fat general in gold at its head. The narration crosses between them: the rebellion broken by winter, titles and gold for the great men, nothing for three brothers and five hundred farmers; then the old emperor dead, murder in the palace, and a general from the west marching in. The dominant turn: nothing for heroes, five years, and the fat general marching on the capital. The empty road and the size of the army carry it; no face is close. The reader turns because the man who turned his back on them is marching into the capital.

blocking only when it materially harms the reader event, exact text or

## Page 16 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
Any of the four prose paragraphs is missing, altered, duplicated, reordered, or not comfortably transcribable from the 600 × 900 proof, or any other text appears. A string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue; the words and their order are what must match.

### C2 — Time does not pass
The page must read as illustrated prose across two places: three tiny riders alone on an empty winter road, then a great army marching on a grey-roofed walled city under purple banners. If the two images read as one moment, or the prose covers the riders or the army, the passage of five years is lost.

### C3 — The fat general is not there
The reader must find a fat general in gold at the army's head and connect him to the man the brothers saved; if he is absent or unreadable, the reason to turn fails. His small scale is expected.

### C4 — The riders are not the brothers
The three riders must read as the brothers by colour and build (green, white, black). Their small scale is expected.
