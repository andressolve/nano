## Page intent

## PAGE 1

Spectacle. The book opens on the empire coming apart: a walled town burning on a hill under a smoke-brown sky, and down the road toward the reader a river of men in yellow headbands, singing, torches and hoes held high, filling the lower half of the page. The page's one dominant turn is the empire burning with the Yellow Turbans on the road. The title sits over the smoke and one caption tells the reader who these men are and why the land is starving. No one is close enough to have a face; the army is a force, not a set of people. The reader turns because somebody is going to have to stand in front of that road. The overturned farmer's cart at the roadside is a small grace note and must stay small; the march owns the page.

## Exact owner-controlled script

## PAGE 1 · *spectacle*

**Location:** the burning empire.

**Panel 1 — dominant, 100%.** Loess hills under a smoke-brown sky. A walled
town burning on a hill, orange flame and black smoke. Down the road toward
the reader, filling the lower half of the page edge to edge, a river of men
with yellow cloths tied around their heads, spears and hoes and torches held
high, mouths open, singing. Nobody's face is close. In the lower corner, a
farmer's cart overturned at the roadside, its owner gone.

> **TITLE CARD** *(upper third, over the smoke)*
> "THREE KINGDOMS
>
> The Peach Garden Oath"

> **CAPTION** *(lower left, over the dust of the road)*
> "The Han emperors had ruled China for four hundred years. Now the land was starving, and the Yellow Turbans, an army in yellow headbands, were on the march."

**Why you turn the page:** the empire is on fire, and somebody is going to have to stand in front of that road.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 1 — card

### C1 — Transcription fails
The two title lines and the caption must each read exactly from the 600 × 900 proof, once, in order, with no other text on the page; a string that does not read is blocking, a string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word; the words and their order are what must match.

### C2 — The empire is not burning
The page must read at a glance as a burning town and an army in yellow headbands filling the road toward the reader, one image owning the page as spectacle. A page where the march is a thin background band, or where the eye lands on a single figure or the cart, fails the turn.

### C3 — Someone is singled out
No face is close and no marcher reads as a character: a red face, a long beard, plumes, or any balloon on a marcher is blocking. Repeated anonymous faces and rough background anatomy are nonblocking.

### C4 — A death is drawn
Any blood, wound, body, or fallen figure is blocking. Fire and smoke are not.
