## Page intent

## PAGE 10

Spectacle. The trap from the page before springs. Liu Bei, the bait, gallops down the valley toward us with the farmers running behind him and the whole yellow army pouring in after them; then, from the hill on the left, Guan Yu crashes down in green with his great crescent blade, and from the hill on the right Zhang Fei in black with his snake spear, men streaming behind each. The dominant turn: the ambush, two brothers coming down from two hills, and the yellow army breaking. Zhang Fei owns the one shout. A small closing image shows a rebel captain toppling backward out of frame as the spear drives past, his men already turning to run; the fall happens out of frame and nothing is shown of what follows. The reader turns because five hundred farmers just broke fifty thousand, and people will hear about it. The crowd stays a mass; no single rebel face matters.

## Numbered blocking criteria

## Page 10 — card

### C1 — Transcription or ownership fails
Zhang Fei's one line is missing, altered, duplicated, not comfortably transcribable from the 600 × 900 proof, or not owned by his mouth by tail and placement; or any extra text appears. A string that reads is not to be measured. Nonblocking: spacing around a dash, curly against straight quotes, a hyphen for a dash, a comma present or missing inside a line, a regional spelling variant of the same word, the run length of a repeated letter in a sound cue; the words and their order are what must match.

### C2 — The ambush does not land
One large image must own the page and read as a trap sprung: the bait and farmers running toward us, the yellow army behind them, and two brothers coming down from two opposite hills. If the reader cannot see two charges from two sides closing on the yellow mass, the turn fails. The closing image of the toppling captain and his men turning to run must read as the army breaking. Crowd faces, counts, and background anatomy are nonblocking.

### C3 — The brothers are not recognisable
Guan Yu (red face, beard, green, crescent blade) and Zhang Fei (broad, bristling whiskers, black, wavy spear) must each be identifiable and on opposite sides; Liu Bei must read as the slim rider in white in front, swords sheathed on his back. A rebel wearing a brother's identity is blocking.

### C4 — A death is drawn
Any blood, wound, body, or severed part is blocking; the captain's fall must leave the frame. Nonblocking: dust, falling horses, scattering men.
