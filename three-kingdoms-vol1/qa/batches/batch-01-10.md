# Sequence gate — pages 1–10

## Blind sequence read

The Han realm is starving as the Yellow Turbans march through a burning
landscape. In Zhuo County, the impoverished sandal seller Liu Bei studies a
call for volunteers but cannot act because he has no weapons or money. Zhang
Fei challenges his sighing, hears the reason, and immediately offers money,
drink, and companionship. In the tavern they meet Guan Yu, a fugitive who
killed an abusive landowner and now means to fight the rebels because they
burn villages like his own.

At Zhang Fei's farm, Guan Yu and Zhang Fei recognize the resolve behind Liu
Bei's hesitation. Zhang Fei proposes an oath in his flowering peach garden,
and the next morning the three swear to protect the people, serve the empire,
and die together. They name Liu Bei eldest brother, Guan Yu second, and Zhang
Fei third, then pool what they own. Zhang Fei promises to sell everything but
his house and garden.

Their sacrifice becomes weapons and an army: Liu Bei receives twin swords,
Zhang Fei a serpent-bladed spear, Guan Yu the Green Dragon, and five hundred
farmers become spearmen. Facing fifty thousand Yellow Turbans, Liu Bei avoids
a direct fight. He sends Guan Yu and Zhang Fei behind opposite hills, rides
out with the farmers as bait, and draws the rebels into the valley. The two
brothers descend from the flanks, springing the trap as Zhang Fei brings down
the enemy captain.
