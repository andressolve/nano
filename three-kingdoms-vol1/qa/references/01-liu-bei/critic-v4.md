## Mandatory findings

1. **The face is still too long and tapered, and the beard reads too close to a pointed goatee.** In the three-quarter and strict-profile views, the jaw narrows below the cheekbones into a small pointed chin; the dark beard mass also continues to the lower chin instead of remaining an isolated tuft under the lip. This weakens the required soft oval and enters both Cao Cao's pointed-goatee space and Yuan Shao's long-narrow-face space in a costume-free crop. **Redesign requirement:** in all four views, shorten the mouth-to-chin distance by about ten percent, widen the jaw immediately below both cheekbones, and round the lower jaw and chin into one continuous oval curve. Remove every beard stroke from the lower chin and jaw; retain one small vertical tuft directly under the lower lip that ends clearly above the chin's lower edge.

2. **The long-arm primary marker is not structurally legible at thumbnail.** The low hand position is suggested, but the wide hanging sleeves conceal the elbow locations, so the figure reads as a tall man in long sleeves rather than a man whose arms are unusually long. The same ambiguity persists in the profile and market states and would disappear when the costume changes. **Redesign requirement:** lengthen both upper arms and forearms so the released fingertips would reach mid-thigh; place the folded hands below the belt at upper-thigh level; and redraw the sleeve bends in the front, profile, and market views so both elbow points sit visibly below an ordinary man's waist. Preserve the tall, slim torso rather than gaining the reach through sleeve length alone.
