## Nonblocking observations

- The candidate has no lettering, labels, seals, watermarks, borders, figures, scenery, faction banners, poles, fortifications, smoke, hills, dust clouds, or ground plane.
- The blood-red coat, darker red-black mane and tail, massive build, and high arched neck form a durable re-entry lock. The bare rearing state supplies the reaction/action test, while the standing state keeps the full body readable at thumbnail.
- The bronze-and-vermilion tack is confined to the saddled state and matches the specified state; the bare state contains no consequential object in the wrong state.
- In grayscale, the massive body, dark mane and tail, black lower legs, short ears, and powerful neck retain identification independently of the red coat. The nearest approved equid, the uncle's donkey, remains unmistakably smaller, lighter-built, and long-eared.
- The warm mulberry-paper ground, visible brush-and-ink line, matte earth/mineral washes, cinnabar body colour, and restrained bronze tack carry the Lacquer and Dust register. Neutral plate lighting does not impose a false stable-night or battle-day state and therefore remains usable across Red Hare's palace-stable, daylight battle, dusk, and dawn appearances.
