## Mandatory findings

1. **The setting plate contains human figures.** Multiple armored guards are visible on both sides of the head position. A setting plate must contain no figures, and these helmeted, face-obscured bodies also fail the costume-free face, strict-profile, reaction, and long-absence tests because they have no stable structural identity to test. **Structural redesign requirement:** remove every human body, face, helmeted head, and human silhouette from the canvas. Preserve the empty guard posts, spear racks, chairs, and entrance architecture as environmental structure only; do not replace the guards with alternate people.

2. **The tabletop is dominated by an untracked campaign-map and desk-prop state.** The large laid-out map, numerous tied scrolls, brush pots, ink pieces, and bronze desk vessels establish a specific working-command configuration that is absent from the setting manifest and consequential-object continuity. As a generation input across repeated tent scenes, this would encourage those objects to recur, move, or disappear without a governed state, and it leaves no clean staging surface for the scripted cup and champion's helmet. **Structural redesign requirement:** retain the long red-edged table and rank-ordered chairs, but clear the full central tabletop of the campaign map, loose and tied scrolls, brushes, ink pieces, and bronze desk vessels. Leave a broad, mostly bare dark tabletop with an unobstructed head-of-table area so the separately governed hot-wine cup and champion's helmet can be introduced only in their scripted states.

## Nonblocking observations

- The long table, ranked seating, head position, and entrance flap open onto the plain communicate the tent's hierarchy and geography clearly.
- The oversized gold dragon banner supplies a strong gold command accent, but it currently competes with the specified in-tent dramatic accent, the hot-wine cup. Do not add a permanent cup to this general setting plate: the cup's appearance and warmth are consequential page states and should come from the approved object reference and page-specific staging.
- The image has no four-view requirement: it is a setting plate, not a character sheet. The character-sheet profile and reaction tests apply only to the prohibited embedded guards, which finding 1 removes.
