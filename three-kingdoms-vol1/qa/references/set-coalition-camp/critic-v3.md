## Mandatory findings

1. The required edge-camp view is not a distinct dusk study: the small tent and fire are embedded in the foreground of the same daylight valley panorama, so the plate supplies one continuous camera view rather than the specified two-view location lock. Recompose the sheet as two unbordered environmental studies on one continuous warm mulberry-paper ground: retain a daylight valley-wide study with the fortress at its head, and redraw the small plain tent and adjacent fire as a separate dusk study. Separate the studies with open paper and irregular brush-faded edges, with no rectangular inset, rule, or broad framed gutter.

2. The consequential command marker does not read as Yuan Shao's great gold banner at thumbnail or in grayscale: the dominant gold shape resolves as a tent roof or canopy. Redesign this primary marker as one unmistakable wind-stretched matte-gold banner on a tall pole directly above the largest tent, with a free cloth edge and a silhouette distinct from every tent roof. Shift the large canopy beneath it to neutral loess canvas so the banner alone carries the gold command accent; keep the banner wholly blank, without emblem, seal, pictogram, or character-like stroke.
