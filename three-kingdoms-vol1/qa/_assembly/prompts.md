## PAGE 1 — The burning empire

### Reader turn
The empire is on fire and an army in yellow headbands fills the road toward the reader.

### Ordered moments
1. One full-bleed image, the whole page. Loess hills under a smoke-brown sky; upper middle distance, a walled town of rammed earth and grey tile burning on a hill, orange flame and black smoke rolling across the sky. Down the road toward the reader, filling the lower half of the page edge to edge, a river of men with yellow cloths tied round their heads, spears, hoes and torches held high, mouths open, singing. Nobody's face is close; the nearest figures are mid-size, the rest a mass. In the lower right corner, small, a farmer's two-wheeled cart overturned at the roadside, its owner gone. The title is set large in the upper third over the smoke, two lines, centered, clear of the town's flames. The caption sits lower left in a matte parchment box over the dust of the road, never over faces.

### Exact strings
TITLE CARD: `THREE KINGDOMS`
TITLE CARD: `The Peach Garden Oath`
CAPTION: `The Han emperors had ruled China for four hundred years. Now the land was starving, and the Yellow Turbans, an army in yellow headbands, were on the march.`

Render exactly these 3 strings once each, in this order, with clear ownership; no other text anywhere on the page. The two title lines are the scripted title card, the one exception to the no-title rule; the first larger than the second, both in the restrained editorial family, never a display or distressed face.

### Character locks
No named character appears. Yellow Turbans: lean, sun-dark farmers in plain brown and grey hemp, yellow headbands, bareheaded under them, generic faces at distance; never a red face, never a long beard, never plumes, never armor beyond scraps of leather.

### Consequential exclusions
Liu Bei, Guan Yu, Zhang Fei and every other named character are absent. No balloons; the marchers sing silently. No bodies, no blood, no wounded or fallen figure anywhere; the town burns but no one is shown dying. The cart is empty and small, never focal. No banners with writing, no readable characters on anything.
