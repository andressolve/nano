VERDICT: REVISE

## Blind read
The sworn brothers turn resources into military force: Zhang Fei sells nearly everything, a smith makes each brother a distinct weapon, and five hundred farmers become spear-bearing recruits. Guan Yu owns the page visually, standing monumental in the forge with the Green Dragon while the smith looks up at him; the lower scene widens the result to the three brothers before the gathered farmers. The turn is the conversion of private wealth and farm labor into weapons and an army.

## Visible text
Narrative prose on the top parchment (no speaker):

Liu Bei was the eldest, so he became Big Brother. Guan Yu was Second Brother. Zhang Fei,
the youngest, and that same week he sold his shop, his fields, and his cattle: everything but
the house and the peach garden behind it.

With the money, the smith made a weapon for each brother. For Liu Bei, two matching swords.
For Zhang Fei, a spear as long as two men, with a wavy blade like a snake. For Guan Yu, a great
curved blade on a long pole, so heavy two men could hardly lift it. He called it the Green Dragon.

Five hundred farmers came to the peach garden with hoes and pitchforks. In a month they had spears.

Names are lettered only as part of the narrative prose. No speaker name, label, tag, dialogue, caption, or sound cue is lettered elsewhere on the page.

## Findings

### Finding C1
- Observation: The first prose paragraph reads “Zhang Fei, the youngest, and that same week...” The required clause “was Third Brother,” is absent between “youngest,” and “and that same week.”
- Material reader harm: The omission breaks the exact owner-controlled prose and removes Zhang Fei’s stated place in the order of brotherhood, which the page is required to name.
- Redraw justification: This is a missing narrative clause carrying consequential named relationship information, not a cosmetic or punctuation variation; the released page would tell an incomplete version of the brotherhood order.
