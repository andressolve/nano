## Blind read
An old man riding a donkey calls after the departing armored horseman, Little Cao Cao, and warmly urges him to return for a meal. Cao Cao apologizes; the old man does not understand why. The large central panel unmistakably gives Cao Cao ownership of the page as he looms over the old man with a drawn sword and turns the apology into a declaration that he has done something the old man could expose, followed by his credo about betraying the world first. The closing panel changes the encounter into its fatal aftermath: the donkey departs alone past a spilled jar and fruit, while the caption confirms that the old man never came home.
