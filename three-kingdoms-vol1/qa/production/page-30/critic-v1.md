VERDICT: REVISE

## Blind read
Three sworn companions receive and read a letter calling loyal men to march against Dong Zhuo. The white-robed man at center owns the page: after the group identifies the summons as Cao Cao’s and recognizes Dong Zhuo as the man they once saved, the large middle panel isolates him as he crushes the letter, turns five years of waiting into a decision, and declares that they will go. The bottom panel confirms the turn from frustrated passivity to united action, with the green-robed companion framing the march as a chance to correct their old mistake.

## Findings

### Finding C1
- Observation: Liu Bei’s proclamation balloon omits the scripted opening and closing single quotation marks around the words he reads from the letter.
- Material reader harm: The exact scripted string is not present, and the missing marks erase the required textual distinction between a written proclamation being quoted aloud and Liu Bei’s own speech.
- Redraw justification: Exact dialogue punctuation is an owner-controlled release requirement; approving the page would preserve a visibly incomplete required string.

### Finding C3
- Observation: In the first panel, Guan Yu’s lower-left “smiles too quickly” balloon is encountered before Zhang Fei’s lower-right “fat pig we saved” balloon in normal left-to-right reading, reversing the required order after Liu Bei’s quoted letter line.
- Material reader harm: Zhang Fei is required to recognize the saved enemy first and Guan Yu to follow by identifying the distrusted sender; reversing them breaks the specified three-way causal rhythm and separates Guan Yu’s warning about who sent the letter from Liu Bei’s later answer that he does not care who sent it.
- Redraw justification: The wrong order is built into the first panel’s balloon composition and contradicts the card’s explicit attribution sequence, so the scripted reader event cannot be released as drawn.
