## Findings

### Finding C2
- Observation: The dominant panel isolates Lü Bu and the old lord; Dong Zhuo and his sword hand are absent, while the opening panel does not show a clear reach-to-stopped-hand transition. Lü Bu's gaze therefore reads as an imposing pose, not as the stare that stops Dong Zhuo's hand.
- Material reader harm: The identification and closing exchange read, but the page's central event—that Lü Bu's silent stare checks Dong Zhuo—is not visible, so the dominant dramatic relationship and the cause of Dong Zhuo's reversal are lost.
- Redraw justification: This missing action is the named dramatic turn and page-turn logic, not a cosmetic or geometric detail; its absence materially weakens the complete reader event enough to justify the risk of replacing the page.
