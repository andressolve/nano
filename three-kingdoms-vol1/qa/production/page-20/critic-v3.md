## Blind read
In a stable at night, an obese older nobleman brings a huge red horse to a lean, plumed warrior who is polishing his halberd. The warrior's hand on the horse and the horse's scale own the page: his reserve gives way to visible interest. ## Findings

### Finding C2
- Observation: Dong Zhuo's hand rests on Lü Bu's shoulder in the earlier strip carrying “He's yours…,” before Lü Bu says “I have a father.” In the final answering strip, Dong Zhuo's hand is extended between them rather than resting on Lü Bu's shoulder.
- Material reader harm: The decisive answer to “I have a father” loses the required invasive shoulder contact, so the closing adoption offer reads as a conversational bargain rather than Dong Zhuo physically pressing his claim over Lü Bu at the moment he attacks that filial bond.
- Redraw justification: That separation materially weakens the dominant closing relationship and the scripted turn the page is built to land; restoring the shoulder contact to the answer is substantial enough to risk replacing the complete page.
