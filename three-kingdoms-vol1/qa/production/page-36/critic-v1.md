## Findings

### Finding C1
- Observation: None of the seven required strings appears. Instead, the page carries seven unrelated numbered prose captions beginning `1. After the fall of the Han dynasty...` and ending `7. This is the story of how a divided empire gave birth to legends.`, plus lettered names on banners and additional unreadable banner text.
- Material reader harm: The required causal sequence—Guan Yu's helmet proof, the still-warm wine, Zhang Fei's challenge, Yuan Shao's expulsion order, and Cao Cao's reply—is wholly absent, so the scripted page cannot be read.
- Redraw justification: This is a complete page-level transcription and scene mismatch, not a local lettering flaw; preserving the page would replace the authored event with a different passage.

### Finding C2
- Observation: The page's principal image is a posed introduction of Cao Cao, Guan Yu, and Sun Quan beneath faction banners; Guan Yu never lifts a steaming cup and Cao Cao is never shown delighted beside him.
- Material reader harm: The still-warm-wine revelation, which is the specified dramatic heart and proof of Guan Yu's astonishing speed, does not exist on the page.
- Redraw justification: The missing dominant relationship and action require a fundamentally different composition, making a complete redraw necessary.
