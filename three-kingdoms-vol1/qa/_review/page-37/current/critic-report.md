## Blind read
Inside a war-camp tent, Cao Cao speaks while holding a cup to Liu Bei, Guan Yu, and Zhang Fei. Cao Cao owns the page: he recounts Lü Bu’s betrayal, tests the three men’s allegiance, and asks them to join him rather than Yuan Shao. Liu Bei commits the three to fight alongside anyone opposing Dong Zhuo; Zhang Fei turns the account into a public challenge; Guan Yu’s final reply turns the accusation back on Cao Cao by observing that Cao Cao witnessed the betrayal and continued bowing.
