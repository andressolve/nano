## Blind read
At night above a vast enemy camp, an older, richly dressed father reports that his champion is dead and that a red-faced common soldier killed him before being expelled by the lords. He then voices the impossible defensive imbalance: eighteen armies against his one gate. The younger armored warrior turns that fear into a plan, promising his father that if the gate is opened tomorrow, he will bring him all eighteen. The younger warrior owns the page through the decisive final panel and its reversal from fear to audacious confidence.
