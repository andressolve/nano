# Visual-continuity gate — canonical pages 1–10

## Verdict

APPROVED

## Findings

No revision findings.

Pages 1–10 remain within the approved **Lacquer and Dust** register and read as one book. Warm mulberry-paper grounds, earth and mineral color, firm lamp-black structure, visible painted texture, and hard daylight/firelight logic remain coherent across the sequence. The brighter peach-blossom pages and the cooler ridge pages are motivated scene changes rather than palette drift; black level, contrast, and overall finish remain compatible with the approved locks.

Lettering remains coherent: serif display/narration and the hand-lettered dialogue face retain stable weight and readability, while ivory balloons use the same rounded, black-edged construction and tails. Variations in panel count and narration treatment are functional and do not break the visual system.

Liu Bei remains recognizable from market clothes through armor by his slim bearing, gentle moustached face, long earlobes, white outer clothing, and paired swords. Guan Yu retains the red face, green robe and headscarf, belt-length beard, massive scale, and Green Dragon blade. Zhang Fei retains his heavy build, wild-eyed bearded face, black clothing/armor, butcher's apron state, and serpent spear. Their costume transitions preserve story state and do not create identity collisions.

The Zhuo market, tavern, peach-garden courtyard, and ridge/valley retain the architecture, terrain, materials, palette, and lighting logic established by their approved setting locks. Liu Bei's paired swords, Guan Yu's crescent blade, and Zhang Fei's wavy spear remain consequential and legible without recognition-breaking redesign.

No page in the reviewed range falls outside the book's established visual continuity.
