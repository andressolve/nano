# Visual-continuity gate — canonical pages 31–40

**Verdict: APPROVED**

## Findings

NONE.

Pages 31–40 remain in the **Lacquer and Dust** register. Warm paper, visible
brush line and tooth, matte earth-and-mineral washes, strong lamp-black, and
the controlled ivory, green, indigo, purple, and vermilion accents hold across
the sequence. The shift from hard dusty daylight to firelit tent interiors,
cold moonlit Hulao, and back to hard daylight is motivated by place and time;
it does not read as a change of book. Contrast, black level, paint treatment,
balloon construction, and overall finish remain coherent.

Liu Bei, Guan Yu, Zhang Fei, Cao Cao, Yuan Shao, Dong Zhuo, and Lü Bu retain
their locked facial structures, silhouettes, costumes, and color lanes without
colliding. The coalition camp, great tent, and Hulao Gate remain recognizable
across scale and lighting changes. Red Hare, Guan Yu's blade, Liu Bei's paired
swords, Lü Bu's halberd, and the returned helmet retain the consequential
identity needed by the story.

Page 40 uses a serif face for Yuan Shao's spoken balloon where nearby dialogue
usually uses the hand-lettered face. This is cosmetic variation, not a revision
finding: the balloon and tail make the speaker and speech function immediately
clear, and no story state or reading is confused. Page 39's brush-script boast
and the serif prose/caption treatments on pages 31 and 40 remain purposeful
display and narration treatments.

No page in the batch falls outside the visual identity of the book.
