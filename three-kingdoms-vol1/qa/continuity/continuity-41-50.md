## Continuity assessment

All ten pages belong to the same book. They sustain the **Lacquer and Dust** register: warm toothed paper, bold wet brush-and-ink contours, matte earth-and-mineral washes, weathered texture, and deep but readable blacks. The brighter battle pages and the darker camp pages vary by scene lighting without changing the underlying line, paint, contrast, or finish language.

Lü Bu remains immediately recognizable by his young angular face, vermilion-and-gold armor, long pheasant plumes, red horse, and halberd. Zhang Fei remains broad, black-clad, round-eyed, and bristle-bearded; Guan Yu keeps his deep-red face, green headscarf and robes, long beard, and Green Dragon blade; Liu Bei keeps his white cloak, headband, long earlobes, slim silhouette, and paired swords; and Cao Cao keeps his narrow angular face, pointed goatee, black armor, and dark cloak. No named character drifts enough to create identity confusion.

Hulao Gate, its mountain wall, purple banners, dusty battlefield, and the coalition camp remain coherent recurring environments. The weapons and Red Hare retain their consequential identifying forms. Pages 48–50 also carry a legible dusk-to-moonlit-night-to-dawn progression.

Lettering weight, prose treatment, balloon fill, outline, tails, and rounded balloon shapes remain consistent across the sequence. Differences in page architecture—full-page spectacle, bordered panels, prose-led night tableau, and closing splash—read as intentional pacing rather than finish drift.
