# Page Contract — 50 pages

One row per page, generated from the approved script (`08-FULL-SCRIPT.md`,
script gate cleared 2026-10-04 at six rounds and again at round 7 after the owner split page 7), never transcribed from it. **Mode**
is blocking at the page gate. **Dominant turn** is the one thing that changed; a
page whose turn will not fit one sentence goes back to
`03-VOLUME-ARCHITECTURE.md`, not to the prompt.

**Panel size is not a rule and blocks nothing.** The share in each row is a
construction target for the page prompt and nothing else. **Locations ≤ 2,
always** — two only when the page's turn *is* the transition.

Exact text lives in `08-FULL-SCRIPT.md` and nowhere else. Words are an
approximate count of rendered strings, for the prompt-writer's lane planning.

Legend — **D** dramatic · **P** illustrated prose · **S** spectacle/silence.
