# Characters - Foundation, Part One

## Identity Policy

Every recurring character or recurring uniformed group gets a locked visual reference before dependent pages. Existing shipped identities remain canon unless full-resolution inspection proves them unusable.

## Gaal Dornick

**Pages:** Cover, 1-16, 19-24.

**Existing identity ref:** `../foundation/refs/ref_gaal.png`; copy to `refs/ref_gaal.png` for a self-contained production folder.

**Lock:**

> Young man in his early twenties, deep brown skin, short tight black curls, large dark earnest eyes, smooth boyish face, slender build, plain charcoal-grey high-collared tunic and matching trousers with a narrow black belt. Wide-eyed provincial newcomer's energy. Realistic young-adult anatomy, never cute or mascot-like.

Gaal begins openly curious and physically drawn upward toward scale. After Page 9, wonder gives way to troubled concentration. In detention and trial he remains young and frightened but intellectually honest.

## Hari Seldon

**Pages:** Cover, 6-9, 12-24.

**Existing identity ref:** `../foundation/refs/ref_seldon.png`; copy to `refs/ref_seldon.png`.

**Lock:**

> Elderly man in his late seventies, thin and upright, balding crown with wispy white hair at the temples, deeply lined gentle face, calm knowing faint smile, plain pale-grey high-collared scholar's robe, austere and serene. Realistic elderly anatomy.

Seldon never performs mystical gestures. His authority comes from stillness, clarity, and preparation. He may hold a calculator pad or indicate a projection, but never wave his hands like a sorcerer.

## Jerril

**Pages:** 2 background, 4-6.

**New ref:** `refs/ref_jerril.png`.

**Lock:**

> Imperial security agent presenting himself as a friendly civilian, man in his early forties, light olive skin, narrow observant hazel eyes, tidy dark-brown hair brushed back, clean-shaven angular face, lean build. Wears an immaculate but deliberately ordinary bronze-brown travel coat over a dark teal tunic. Polite smile that never reaches his eyes. No uniform, no visible weapon.

He must remain visually distinct from Gaal and the navy-uniformed Advocate.

## The Commission Advocate

**Pages:** 14, 16-19.

**New ref:** `refs/ref_advocate.png`, derived from the shipped trial page.

**Lock:**

> Middle-aged Imperial advocate, light skin, slicked-back black hair with a sharp widow's peak, gaunt angular face, hooked nose, heavy dark brows, controlled severe expression. Ornate navy-blue high-collared Imperial uniform with silver-gold braid, starburst chest emblem, broad decorated cuffs, and no helmet.

He represents the case against Seldon. He must never drift into Jerril, Chen, or a soldier.

## Linge Chen

**Pages:** 20-22.

**New ref:** `refs/ref_linge_chen.png`, anchored on the central commissioner in the shipped trial page.

**Lock:**

> Chief Commissioner in his eighties, lean ancient East Asian face, hooded unreadable dark eyes, thin silver hair beneath a fitted dark judicial cap, narrow mouth, perfectly still posture. Scarlet-and-gold judicial robe over black, with a restrained Imperial starship clasp. He projects quiet final authority rather than anger.

Chen is the central commissioner and must remain distinct from the other four elderly men.

## The Five Commissioners

**Pages:** 16-22.

**New group ref:** `refs/ref_commissioners.png`, derived from `../foundation/pages/page-05.png` and `../foundation/refs/ref_bench_commissioners.png`.

**Lock:**

> Five clearly different elderly commissioners seated together: distinct faces, hairlines, noses, and expressions; coordinated scarlet-and-black robes with varied gold trim and fitted dark judicial caps. Linge Chen sits at the center and is the leanest and stillest. Never clone or repeat a face.

## Commission Guards

**Pages:** 13-16, 23.

**New group ref:** `refs/ref_commission_guards.png`.

**Lock:**

> Two disciplined Commission security officers in practical charcoal-grey uniforms with narrow scarlet piping, high collars, black gloves and boots, small gold starship badges, and holstered sidearms. One is a broad-shouldered Black man with a shaved head; the other is a pale compact woman with short dark hair. Serious, restrained, no helmets unless outdoors.

The same pair may recur as a continuity device. They never speak except the lead officer's single arrest line on Page 13.

## Non-Recurring Figures

No separate identity refs required when they remain confined to one page:

- view-room officer on Page 1;
- customs supervisor and taxi driver on Pages 2-3;
- observation-platform visitors on Page 4;
- University workers and Foundation families on Pages 23-24.

These one-page figures must not resemble Gaal, Seldon, Jerril, the Advocate, or Chen.
