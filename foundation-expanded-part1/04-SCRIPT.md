## Cover

Single full-bleed poster image, no panels. Trantor fills the lower half: a bronze-teal city covering a planet, still brilliant and intact. Above it, Hari Seldon stands calm at left and Gaal Dornick stands troubled at right, both matching their refs. Between them floats a luminous analytical projection of the same world centuries later: dark, cracked towers and broken routes, clearly framed by geometric probability lines rather than supernatural mist. Chrome colony ships arc toward one amber point on the galactic rim. Preserve generous title space.

- Large chrome science-fiction title at top: `FOUNDATION`
- Amber serif subtitle beneath: `PART ONE · THE MAN WHO SAW THE END`
