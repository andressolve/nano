## What This Volume Accomplishes

The volume stays with Gaal long enough to make the opening causal chain legible:

1. Gaal arrives at apparently permanent Trantor.
2. Jerril quietly establishes government surveillance.
3. Seldon defines psychohistory as probability applied to vast populations, not individual prophecy.
4. Gaal independently verifies a 92.5 percent probability of Trantor's destruction within three centuries.
5. Pages 10-11 visualize the causal chain from lost routes and taxes to a future ruined Trantor.
6. The ruin is explicitly framed as a calculator-generated probability projection, not a current event or supernatural vision.
7. The Commission treats the Project's knowledge and scale as a political threat.
8. Seldon reveals that the fall cannot be stopped, but the dark age can be shortened from thirty thousand years to one thousand.
9. Chen chooses exile to Terminus, believing he has neutralized the Project.
10. Gaal realizes Seldon prepared the migration more than two years earlier and required exile.
11. The Second Foundation at Star's End and Seldon's remaining year are revealed.
12. Colony ships rise from an intact Trantor: nothing has fallen yet, which is why the warning is difficult to believe.

It also identifies the genetic dynasty, Demerzel's television role, the Star Bridge, and major continuous character arcs as screen additions or recombinations.

Use the series only as evidence that physical scale, human continuity, and visible consequence matter.
