# Cogito — Full Script (19 Pages)

## Page Structure

19 pages total: 1 cover + 18 story pages. Each page is a single generated image (2:3 vertical). Pages combine panels, captions, and dialogue.

**Narrative arc:** A sickly boy who was allowed to stay in bed → discovered that thinking was his gift → questioned everything → had three dreams that changed his life → traveled the world → found solitude → loved and lost a daughter → invented coordinate geometry → pushed doubt to its absolute limit → published his ideas in a language everyone could read → was killed by the cold and early mornings of Stockholm → left behind a grid that contains the universe.

**Emotional arc:** Stillness → curiosity → restlessness → revelation → searching → solitude → joy → triumph → grief → the deepest question → death → legacy.
