# Cogito — Characters

## René Descartes — Philosopher (Primary, Pages 10–16)

The main look. This is Descartes in his Netherlands years and beyond: 32–53, the mature thinker. Appears in the most pages.

### Character Lock Block (COPY VERBATIM)

```
CHARACTER RENÉ DESCARTES — PHILOSOPHER (must appear identical in all panels):
- Age/build: Mid-30s to early 40s, small stature, slight build, notably large head in proportion to body
- Face: Broad forehead, prominent aquiline nose, full lower lip, pale complexion with slight flush, composed expression
- Hair: Long dark brown hair falling past ears and nearly to eyebrows, center-parted or swept back, sometimes under a dark cap
- Eyes: Large, dark brown, intelligent and searching — often gazing slightly upward or inward
- Facial hair: Thin dark mustache with small pointed goatee/vandyke beard
- Outfit: Black wool doublet with wide flat white linen collar (falling band collar, characteristic 1630s-40s Dutch style), black breeches, dark stockings
- Accessories: Quill pen or compass when at desk; no jewelry
```

## Notes on René's Aging

René appears across four distinct life stages. Key visual continuity markers across all ages:

1. **The broad forehead** — present from childhood, increasingly prominent
2. **The large head** — proportionally large at every age
3. **The dark brown eyes** — same intensity from child to philosopher
4. **The prominent nose** — develops from small (child) to aquiline (adult)
5. **The pale complexion** — lifelong
6. **The slight build** — always small, never physically imposing

The transition should feel natural: the child's watchful stillness becomes the student's focused intensity becomes the young man's restless searching becomes the philosopher's composed certainty.
