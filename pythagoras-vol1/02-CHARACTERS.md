# Ratio: The Seeker — Characters (Vol 1)

Vol 1 has a modest cast. The protagonist at three ages, three foreign teachers, plus a handful of incidentals. The elder Pythagoras, Theano, Hippasus, and Cylon are all Vol 2.

## Pythagoras of Samos — three ages in Vol 1

Dedicated reference sheet for each age. Generate all three before any page generation.

### Pythagoras — Boy (~age 10, Samos, pages 1–2)
- Olive skin, tanned
- Dark brown hair, tousled, shoulder-length
- Thick straight brows (SIGNATURE — keep at every age)
- Large dark brown eyes, serious for his age
- Broad high forehead (SIGNATURE)
- Aquiline strong nose (SIGNATURE — subtle at this age)
- Slim wiry build, typical 10-year-old
- Simple cream wool short tunic (exomis), leather sandals or barefoot
- No jewelry, no ornament
- Expression: curious, attentive, listening

**Prompt anchor:** "Greek boy, age 10, olive skin, dark brown tousled hair, thick straight eyebrows, broad forehead, serious curious expression, cream wool short tunic, barefoot. NOT a cartoon child. Realistic proportions. Serious mature graphic novel."

**File:** `refs/pythagoras-boy.png`

### Pythagoras — Teen (~age 14, Samos, page 3)
Same face as the boy, slightly older. Hair a touch longer, same clean-shaven face. No separate ref needed — use `pythagoras-boy.png` with the prompt modifier "age 14, slightly taller, same face."

### Pythagoras — Young Man (~age 20, pages 4–7 approximately)
- Olive skin, slightly weathered from travel
- Dark brown hair, longer, tied back or loose to shoulders
- Short dark beard forming (age 20 — sparse, clean-cut edges, NOT a full mustached beard)
- Same thick straight brows, broad forehead, aquiline nose
- Wiry but stronger build
- Travel clothes: dark himation (cloak) over cream tunic, leather travel sandals, simple rope belt
- Carries a wooden walking staff on travel pages
- Expression: alert, absorbing, respectful in foreign settings

**Prompt anchor:** "Greek young man, age 20, olive skin, dark brown shoulder-length hair, short dark beard, thick straight eyebrows, broad forehead, aquiline nose, cream wool tunic, dark travel himation, wooden staff. NOT a children's book."

**File:** `refs/pythagoras-young-man.png`

**Note:** Einstein-retrospective lesson — make sure this ref reads as age 20, not age 30+. Beard is SPARSE and EDGED CLEAN, not full-mustached. Body is lean, not bulked.

### Pythagoras — Mature-young (~age 40, pages 11–17)
- Olive skin
- Dark brown hair threaded with grey at the temples, shoulder-length, sometimes tied back
- Full dark beard, well-kept, subtle grey streaks
- Same thick brows, broad forehead, aquiline nose
- Stronger, settled build; upright teacherly presence
- Ivory himation draped over ivory chiton
- Expression: grave, settled, certain. A man about to begin.

**Prompt anchor:** "Greek man, age 40, olive skin, dark brown hair with grey at temples, full dark beard with grey streaks, thick straight eyebrows, broad forehead, aquiline nose, ivory himation over ivory chiton. NOT a children's book. Serious mature graphic novel."

**File:** `refs/pythagoras-mature.png`

**Shared with Vol 2:** YES. This same ref carries into Vol 2's Brotherhood pages. Generate once; use in both volumes.

## Pherecydes of Syros (page 3 only)

Historical. One of the last Ionian pre-Socratic philosophers. Old when Pythagoras is young.

- Old Ionian Greek man, age ~70
- Olive skin, weathered
- Long white beard, long white hair
- Sharp eyes under heavy white brows
- Grey or ochre himation draped over his body
- Seated, usually with a scroll or wax tablet in hand
- Expression: watchful, approving, teacherly

**Ref needed?** Optional for a single-page appearance. If drift occurs in the generation, make a quick ref. Otherwise prompt in-page with the anchor string above.

## The Egyptian priest / rope-stretcher (page 6 only)

Composite historical figure. The *harpedonaptai* were the Egyptian surveyors who used knotted ropes for right angles.

- Egyptian man, age ~40
- Dark brown skin
- Shaved head (priestly)
- Clean-shaven, dignified face
- White linen kilt (shendyt), bare chest with a simple sash, broad collar necklace of gold and lapis
- Bronze armbands
- Kohl around the eyes
- Holds a knotted rope (12 knots, laid out in a 3-4-5 triangle on the sand)
- Expression: patient, teacherly, confident in his craft

**Ref needed?** Optional. Single appearance with supporting role. Prompt anchor above should be sufficient.

**File (if made):** `refs/egyptian-priest.png`

## The Babylonian Magus (pages 8–10)

Composite historical figure representing the Magi — the priestly caste with mathematical and astronomical knowledge.

- Older man, age ~60
- Lighter skin than the Egyptian, long grey-black beard
- Hair covered by a tall conical hat (blue or white, sometimes star-embroidered)
- Long robes of deep blue and ochre, embroidered
- Holds either a clay tablet with cuneiform or a bronze star-chart disc
- Stands on a rooftop under stars on his primary page
- Expression: grave, hospitable, weighing the young Greek who has come to learn

**Ref needed?** YES — he appears across three pages (8, 9, 10). Make a dedicated ref.

**File:** `refs/babylonian-magus.png`

## The face-lock discipline (SERIES)

Einstein-retrospective lesson: age-specific refs alone aren't enough. Signature features drift. For Pythagoras, the three features that must repeat in every prompt are:

- **Thick straight dark brows** (white when old in Vol 2; dark across all of Vol 1)
- **Broad high forehead**
- **Aquiline strong nose**

If any page generation produces a handsome generic Greek face, regenerate composing TWO refs: the age-specific one AND the mature ref, with the instruction: "Keep the distinctive features — thick straight brows, broad forehead, strong aquiline nose — but render him at age X."
