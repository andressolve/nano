# Relativity — Full Script (20 Pages)

## Page Structure

21 pages total: 1 cover + 20 story/legacy pages (special relativity is split across two pages, 12 and 12b). Each page is a single generated image (2:3 vertical). Pages combine panels, captions, and dialogue.

**Narrative arc:** A dreamy Munich child transfixed by a compass needle → a teenager who refuses to be schooled by rote → a college student who meets his equal in Mileva → a patent clerk who rewrites physics in a single year → a middle-aged professor who spends eight years wrestling gravity into curved spacetime → a global celebrity confirmed by an eclipse → a Jewish refugee who signs the letter that starts the atomic age → an elder who refuses a country, plays the violin, and quietly insists the universe is not a dice game.

**Emotional arc:** Wonder → rebellion → love → quiet labor → revelation → fame → exile → regret → humility → legacy.

## PAGE 12b — MOVING CLOCKS TICK SLOWER (June 1905, part 2)

**Narrative beat:** The second half of special relativity. The light-clock thought experiment. A pulse of light bouncing between two mirrors — straight up-and-down from inside the train, but a longer zig-zag when seen from outside. Same speed of light + longer path = more time per tick. Moving clocks literally run slower. Later confirmed with atomic clocks in airplanes.
