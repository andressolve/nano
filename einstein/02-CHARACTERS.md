# Relativity — Characters

## Albert Einstein — Young Man / Patent Clerk (Primary A, Pages 7–14)

The first "hero look." Einstein at 23–28, during the Bern patent office years and the 1905 miracle year. Trim mustache, dark hair, alert eyes. This is the Einstein most pages feature.

### Character Lock Block (COPY VERBATIM)

```
CHARACTER ALBERT EINSTEIN — YOUNG MAN (must appear identical in all panels):
- Age/build: 23-28, medium height, slight but wiry build
- Face: Broad heavy nose, full lips, softly rounded jaw, Mediterranean-Jewish features, slightly mournful alert eyes
- Hair: Dark brown, thick and curly, combed back but unruly, parted slightly left
- Eyes: Deep-set, dark brown, intelligent, with a soft humorous spark
- Facial hair: Dark full mustache, neatly trimmed, covers upper lip
- Ears: Slightly prominent, stick out a touch
- Outfit: Rumpled dark wool suit, white shirt with rounded collar, dark loose tie, sometimes a dark vest. Clothes never quite pressed — a thinker's carelessness
- Accessories: Pipe occasionally; fountain pen or pencil at desk
```

## Notes on Albert's Aging

Albert appears across five distinct life stages. Key visual continuity markers across all ages:

1. **The broad heavy nose** — present from childhood, grows more prominent with age
2. **The deep-set dark eyes** — same intense, slightly mournful quality from child to elder
3. **The slightly prominent ears** — subtle but consistent
4. **The mustache** — appears in adolescence, stays for life (darkens, thickens, whitens)
5. **The full lips, softly rounded** — never thin or hard
6. **The hair journey** — dark combed (child) → curly dark (teen/young man) → greying and lifting (middle) → wild white halo (elder). Hair is the clock.

The transition should feel natural: the solemn child's watchful quiet becomes the teenager's rebellious alertness becomes the patent clerk's sharp-eyed curiosity becomes the middle-aged professor's tired brilliance becomes the elder sage's gentle, time-worn warmth.
