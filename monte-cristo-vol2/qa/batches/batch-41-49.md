# Canonical sequence gate — Pages 41–49

## Uninterrupted sequence

Mercédès tells Albert that Fernand helped destroy Edmond Dantès in 1815 and
that Albert is due to face that same man at dawn. Albert publicly withdraws the
duel, names his father's betrayal, renounces the Morcerf name, and leaves with
his mother. The Count, denied the death he had prepared for, decides Fernand's
debt remains and prepares to receive him. Fernand arrives armed; the Count
explains the enemy-roof rule, reconstructs the betrayal, and reveals himself as
Edmond Dantès. Fernand returns to the home his wife and son have emptied of
themselves, finds that they took none of his wealth, and shoots himself.
Mercédès prevents Albert from returning after the shot and takes him away with
only one bag. The Count watches their carriage leave, counts Fernand as one,
and turns his attention to Villefort's lit window on the next roof.
