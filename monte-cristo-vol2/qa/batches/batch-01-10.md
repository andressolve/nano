## Register

Pages 1 and 10 share the same tactile, matte Velvet Cinema system when compared directly. The batch does not progressively become warmer, glossier, smoother or more detailed. Architectural spectacle and close dialogue change subject scale without changing rendering language.

## Palette logic

Pages 1–5 preserve the Count-house logic of lacquer black, ivory, cold grey and restrained new gold, with the three lit roofs carrying the exterior warmth. Page 6 makes a clearly motivated transition to the Morcerf house, and Pages 7–10 sustain its burgundy, polished walnut, old gold and dense candle amber. The strong warmth beginning on Page 6 is the intended location contrast, not palette drift. Janina and the later institutional palettes have not yet entered the sequence.

2. **Page 2:** The Count and Haydée identify the men behind those roofs, choose the banker first, and set the dismantling for spring.
3. **Page 3:** Haydée makes the distracted Count hear that her father’s head was carried through Janina and that the man who betrayed him is dining only three streets away.

6. **Page 6:** Morcerf’s engraved invitation brings the Count by carriage to the brilliantly lit Morcerf house on Thursday night.
