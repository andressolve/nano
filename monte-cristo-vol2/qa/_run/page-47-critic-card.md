## Page intent

## PAGE 47

Fernand climbs his own empty staircase carrying the case and finds a bedroom
stripped only of Mercédès and Albert: their valuables remain because they took
nothing of his. Outside, the dead house has one white upstairs window, then a
single crack.

The suicide is conveyed through architecture, absence, and sound. No body,
impact, gore, muzzle flash, or interior shooting image is needed.
