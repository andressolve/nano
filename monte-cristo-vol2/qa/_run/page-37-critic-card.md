## Page intent

## PAGE 37

Haydée returns triumphant from Fernand's humiliation and discovers that the
Count cannot enjoy the victory he wanted. She is warm, moving, and certain; he
is motionless before an untouched meal, looking at a wall because Albert is
twenty-two and Haydée was eleven.
