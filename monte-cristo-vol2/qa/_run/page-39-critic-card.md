## Page intent

## PAGE 39

Mercédès asks Edmond not to kill Albert, defeats each practical objection, and
names the private motive beneath his revenge: he needed her to see it. Once she
says she has seen it, his closed hand opens and he agrees to stand still and
die rather than fire on her son.

The dominant relationship is two wrecked people looking directly at one
another; everything else serves that surrender.

grossly broken, or corruption disrupts the scene. Room detail, pose precision,
