## Page intent

## PAGE 38

Mercédès comes alone at night, strips away the Count's title with one word, and
speaks the name the whole volume has withheld: Edmond. In the vast black room,
their final close view collapses twenty-three years of distance.

The page is about recognition and history becoming intimate. It is not about
furnishing, etiquette, or decorative accuracy.

## Exact owner-controlled script

## PAGE 38 · *dramatic*

**Location:** the Count's house, the black room. Night.

**Panel 1 — 13%.** A servantless hall. Mercédès alone in the doorway in
travelling black, having come herself, at night, with no card and nobody to
announce her.

> **COUNT:** "Madame la Comtesse. At this hour."
>
> **MERCÉDÈS:** "Don't."

**Panel 2 — 20%.** She comes into the room. She does not sit.

> **MERCÉDÈS:** "I have sat in that house since the newspaper, while men I have
> known twenty years found reasons not to look at me."
>
> **MERCÉDÈS:** "I have not once had to wonder who was doing it."

**Panel 3 — 12%.** The enormous black underfurnished room, and Mercédès small in
the middle of it. The room makes her small and it does not make her less.

**Panel 4 — dominant, 55%.** Two faces, and nothing else in the frame. This is the
sentence the whole volume has been holding.

> **MERCÉDÈS:** "Edmond."
>
> **COUNT:** "Nobody has said that name to me in twenty-three years."
>
> **MERCÉDÈS:** "I have said it every day."

**Why you turn the page:** the secret the volume is built on has just been said
out loud, and there are ten pages left.
