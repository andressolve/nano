# Promoted Pages 11–20 — visual continuity gate

## Contact-sheet review

The deterministic promoted contact sheet was inspected as one image, followed by the promoted pages and approved identity locks where thumbnail questions required confirmation.

## Brightness, gloss, detail, and saturation

No page jumps out as a different rendering system. Pages 11–14 and 16 maintain the Morcerf house's dense candle-amber, burgundy, walnut, and old-gold field; Pages 15 and 17 shift coherently to the Count house's cold grey morning; Page 18 returns that house to tight lamp-yellow against lacquer black. Page 19's bright blue/white opening and Page 20's night-blue/fire field are the intended Janina rupture and continuation. Their brighter sky and hotter fire are location/story accents, not gloss or saturation drift. All ten retain matte painted surfaces, visible brush structure, and compatible detail density.

## Principal distinction at thumbnail scale

- Mercédès remains mature, burgundy-black, grey-threaded, and coiffed on Pages 11–13 and 16.
- Haydée remains younger, long-unbound-haired, olive-gold, and vertically crimson-and-gold on Pages 14, 17, and 18; she does not collapse into Mercédès.
- The Count remains a clean-shaven pallid black vertical across the batch. Fernand remains heavier, moustached, receding-haired, decorated, and warm-toned on Page 16.
- Albert does not appear in Pages 11–20, so this batch introduces no Albert/Count collision; the Count's unrelieved-black lane remains intact.
- The Page 20 mother/child shapes are deliberately faceless silhouettes and do not create a principal collision.

## Whole-book register test

No page reads as another book. The apparent jumps on Pages 15, 19, and 20 are precisely the designed changes of time, place, and dramatic temperature, while line, material handling, shadow structure, prose/balloon treatment, and figure construction remain continuous. There is no unmotivated brightness, smoothness, gloss, flattening, detail, or saturation outlier.
