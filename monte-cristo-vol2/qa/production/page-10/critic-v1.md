## Reader read

The page reads smoothly and the dominant panel establishes the intimacy and danger of Albert’s easy disclosure. The value contrast makes the two men effortless to track, and Panel 4 lands Albert’s invitation against the Count’s emptied face. I would turn the page. However, the two locked physical beats that give the conversation its subtext—the untouched plate remaining where it was set and the Count’s answer arriving visibly late—are not both present in the rendered action, so the page cannot pass unchanged.
