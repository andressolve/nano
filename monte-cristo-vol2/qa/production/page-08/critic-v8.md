## Reader response

The redesigned dialogue works: each pressure point now has its own clean beat, `In Greece.` lands on Fernand alone, the glass refusal reads as a separate silent action, and the Count’s courtesy closes the page with a sharp forward pull. The eye path is effortless. But the page visually treats the staircase setup as a medium panel rather than the declared dominant authority, and the raised glass lacks the conspicuous empty vertical interval that is supposed to make the first refusal physically unmistakable. Those are story-bearing construction failures, not cosmetic preferences.
