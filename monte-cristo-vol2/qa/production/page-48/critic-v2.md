## Blind read
Albert suggests that they could have taken family plate, but Mercédès reveals that it was purchased and that the supposed inheritance was false. Albert realizes she knows the corrupt origin of everything, and she confirms it. An off-panel shot stops them; both turn toward the dark house, and Mercédès immediately understands what has happened. She then grips Albert's arm, refuses to return after twenty-three years in the house, and orders him into the carriage. Mercédès owns the page through her final refusal of the house and her control of their departure.

## Findings

### Finding C2
- Observation: No small bag is visible with Mercédès and Albert in any panel; the page shows them beside the carriage without any clearly identifiable luggage.
- Material reader harm: The consequential object state that they have left the Morcerf house with only one small bag is absent, so the image does not establish the extremity of their refusal of Fernand's possessions at the moment of departure.
- Redraw justification: Their drastically reduced belongings are a central visual fact of the page's final refusal, not a cosmetic prop detail. Because the sole bag is not identifiable even in the full candidate or reading proofs, that story fact cannot be recovered from the art and is substantial enough to warrant replacement of the complete page.
