## Page 1 appendix

- **Object and set state — PASS.** The dominant room is enormous, cold, unlit, and deliberately underfurnished, with no family objects, clutter, fire, warmth, or comfortable drawing-room furniture.
- **The three roofs — PASS.** The lower band establishes three individually countable foreground roofs: the left roof has the copper gutter, the centre roof has the flagpole, and every visible window on the right-hand roof is lit.
- **Motif — PASS.** Warm illumination is confined to the windows of other people's houses; the room itself remains cold and unlit.
- **Text architecture — REVISE.** The page has exactly two prose fields, four exact text blocks, no balloons, and no speaker. The fields clear the visible window glass and the Count, but both fail the mandatory 40 px source-lettering floor described above.
- **Lookalike lanes — PASS.** There is one small rear-view figure only, recognizably the Count by his tall slim columnar silhouette and unrelieved black costume; there is no second identity to confuse with him.
- **Likely failure — PASS.** The model did not furnish the room, and the Count remains small enough for the room and window to retain their intended scale.

## Reader response

The page works as a reader-facing opening. The dominant panel carries the turn: the vast empty room reduces the solitary man to a deliberate black mark at the glass, then the lower band answers his gaze with three concrete targets. The room, window, and downward eye path are effortless to parse, and the last sentence creates a clean reason to turn the page. The redraw is required only because the source lettering violates the independent 40 px gate.
