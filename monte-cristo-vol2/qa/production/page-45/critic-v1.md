VERDICT: REVISE

## Blind read
Fernand arrives at the Count's dark house ruined, carrying a closed pistol case and demanding a duel. The Count refuses, keeps control of the encounter, makes Fernand sit, drinks in front of him, and explains his earlier refusals to eat or drink before declaring that Fernand is now beneath the Count's own roof. The Count owns the page, changing Fernand's armed challenge into a forced reckoning whose reason is still withheld.

FERNAND by the visible balloon tail, though the scene context conflicts: "Sit down. You are going to be told why first."

## Findings

### Finding C1
- Observation: Two crucial Count balloons do not have clear Count ownership. In Panel 2, the tail of "Sit down. You are going to be told why first." points up-left toward Fernand's back. In Panel 4, "It is a rule of mine. I don't eat or drink under the roof of an enemy." has no clear tail to the Count and visually groups with Fernand's adjacent "What?" balloon.
- Material reader harm: The page assigns or leaves unclear the Count's command and the explanatory rule that drives the entire reveal, reversing or obscuring who controls the encounter and who declares Fernand an enemy.
- Redraw justification: Exact ownership of these two pivotal strings cannot be established from the art without relying on outside script knowledge; that materially compromises the dominant dramatic relationship and the final roof/enemy meaning enough to justify replacing the complete page.
