## Reader response

The page works. The engraved invitation arrives as a cold fact, the central panel erupts into the Morcerfs’ conspicuous warmth and purchased grandeur, and the carriage coda withdraws to darkness while preserving the house at the far end of the same street. The staircase is immediately legible through the doors, the eye path is effortless, and the Count’s private timing of the invitation turns the spectacle into proof that his plan is already moving. I would turn the page to see him enter.
