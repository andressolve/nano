## Blind read
Haydée owns the page. In the Chamber, she calmly names herself and her father, then identifies the decorated officer across the full hall as the man who betrayed her family. The President raises and reads the signed sale document aloud, turning her testimony into public proof. In the final panel Fernand stands frozen below while the Count leans forward from the gallery above him, watching the destruction he arranged reach its conclusion.
