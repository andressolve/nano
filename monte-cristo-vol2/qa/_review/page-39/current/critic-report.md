## Blind read
Mercédès asks Edmond to spare her son. Edmond answers that her son issued the challenge publicly and is an expert shot; she strips away each practical objection until she identifies the revenge's private purpose: Edmond needed her to witness it. She says she has seen it, and Edmond yields, opening his hand and agreeing to stand for the shot without firing back.
