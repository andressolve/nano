## Blind read
In Albert's room, Mercédès finds her son handling the pieces of a pistol and makes him put them down. She owns the page, telling him directly that Edmond Dantès was the nineteen-year-old she was going to marry, that Danglars wrote the letter that caused his arrest, and that Albert's father posted it. Albert realizes that she has always known. The disclosure changes his coming duel: the man he is due to shoot at eight is Edmond.
