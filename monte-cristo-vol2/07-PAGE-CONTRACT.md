# Page Contract — 49 pages

One row per page. **Mode** is blocking at the page gate. **Dominant turn** is the
one thing that changed; a page whose turn will not fit one sentence goes back to
`03-VOLUME-ARCHITECTURE.md`, not to the prompt.

**Panel size is not a rule and blocks nothing** — see the note below the blocking
checks. The share in each row is a construction target for the page prompt and
nothing else. **Locations** ≤ 2, always. **Every row below was generated from the
script, not transcribed from it.**

Exact text lives in `08-FULL-SCRIPT.md` and nowhere else. This table does not
duplicate it.

Legend — **D** dramatic · **P** illustrated prose · **S** spectacle/silence.

## Two-location pages

Only 3 pages carry two locations, and each is a deliberate cut across
distance: **6, 25, 28** — the Count's hall / the Morcerf house (6), his black room at
dawn / the Morcerf breakfast tray (25), and a Janina street / the Count's black
room (28). Every other page is single-location, **including 29**, where the carriage,
the front steps and the side stair are all one address.

## Acceptance questions — asked of every page at the script gate

1. **What does each character in this room want from the other person, right
   now?** "To inform them" is not a want and fails the page.
2. **Does anybody say something other than exactly what they mean?**
3. **With the speaker names stripped, can the lines be assigned correctly?**
4. **Is the event dramatized and the meaning left to be inferred** — never the
   reverse?
5. **Is the Count's appetite visible,** or has he gone serene? Serenity is
   permitted only where the script names the flinch it is covering. **He is
   absent from at most two consecutive pages anywhere in the volume**, and he is
   never confined to the smallest panel on a page that belongs to his own plan.
6. **Why does the reader turn this page?** One sentence, in the script, under
   every page — and it must be true of what is actually on the page.
7. **Can this page physically hold its own text** at the numbers in
   `06-TYPOGRAPHY-SYSTEM.md`? **No speech balloon over 24 words; no panel under
   18% of the page carrying more than 20 words.** Both are verified mechanically
   against the script, not asserted. If a page fails, restage or split it. Never
   shrink the type.

## Blocking checks derived from this contract

- Declared mode is not the mode rendered → **blocking**.
- More than two locations → **blocking**.
- More than one turn, or a turn that cannot be stated in a sentence →
  **blocking**.
- Any string that fails transcription from the 600 × 900 proof → **blocking**.

**Panel size blocks nothing, at any gate (owner instruction, 2026-08-16).** There
is no share floor, no ceiling, no ratio and no sum check. The `Dominant` column
below is a construction target for the page prompt, because the model will not
build hierarchy from a list of equal-sounding panels — it is an instruction to a
generator, never a test of a result. **A finding whose content is a panel size is
void at every gate; strike it.** The story rule that actually matters is *one
dominant turn*, which is checked directly two lines above, and whether a page
reads is settled by transcription. This criterion was cut after it blocked page 1
at 73% and page 8 at 42% — both needing owner overrides — while the one real
hierarchy defect it is credited with, page 5, was seen by eye on the rendered
page and only afterwards argued in percentages.

**† Page 8, as shipped.** Page 8 was planned at five panels and reached nine
through six executor redesigns before v14 was approved. The 62% dominant share
this row once carried was never a design intent — it was a number invented to
compensate for the generator and written back into this table by mistake. The
row now records what actually rendered: a staircase field of roughly 42%, the
sole dominant field at more than three times any other panel. That was a floor
miss under the old band and needed an owner override; panel size no longer blocks
anything, so the question does not arise. **Page 8 is the only page in this volume
outside the 2–5 panel range and it is not a precedent.**
  This is the *only* text gate. Lettering size is never blocking on its own and
  is never measured.
